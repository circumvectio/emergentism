## 1. Four different constraints

| Type | Form | What violation means |
|---|---|---|
| analytic | a conclusion follows from definitions and premises | contradiction or a false premise |
| physical | lower-level laws bound admissible trajectories | the proposed event is physically unavailable under the model |
| strategic | a specified payoff/game makes an action worse or unstable | a model-relative incentive result, not impossibility |
| normative/constitutional | authorized participants declare an action inadmissible | wrongdoing or rule breach may remain physically possible and privately profitable |

Confusing these types creates the is/ought error. A prohibition is not weak
because it can be broken. It exists precisely because the forbidden act may be
possible.

## 2. What the reciprocal chart proves `[A]`

For the declared chart,

```text
φ = cot(θ/2),  ν = tan(θ/2),  θ in (0,π),
φν = 1,        B = sin(θ) = 2ν/(1+ν²).
```

`B` has its unique maximizing **colatitude** at `θ=π/2`, equivalently the
unique scalar choice `ν=1`. On full `S²`, every longitude at that colatitude is
equatorial. The chart does not prohibit another colatitude, transfer resources,
create a tax, or derive a moral verdict.

In the deliberately balance-only game `Γ(N,λ)`, players are paid only through
their own `B` and its population mean. There, `ν_i=1` is strictly dominant and
the Price of Anarchy is 1. This is a payoff-construction theorem. When a private
side-payment is admitted, zero extraction can cease to be a best response.

## 3. Why norms and enforcement remain necessary `[I]`

Extraction can be:

- physically possible;
- score-lowering under one diagnostic;
- privately profitable under another payoff;
- unauthorized or unjust; and
- reversible, reparable, or catastrophic in different circumstances.

No one of those statements entails the others. `η=0` is therefore a declared
and enforceable non-extraction fence, not a law of arithmetic or automatic
equilibrium. A real mechanism must specify actions, observations, penalties,
exit, timing, payer/beneficiary flows, and every profitable deviation.

## 4. The Justice envelope

```text

```

The selector emits a commitment receipt; the world or an independent channel
emits the outcome receipt. Justice then evaluates every materially affected

Morals run `individual → whole`; ethics run `whole → individual`. Strict
