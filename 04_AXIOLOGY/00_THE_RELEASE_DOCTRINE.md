# The Release Doctrine

> **Calibration rule:** be bold when discovering, exact when deriving, and
> ruthless only when promoting a claim as externally established.

Ratification records a claim at its existing tier. It authenticates an
authorized corpus action; it does not make the claim true.

## 1. The release claim `[I]`

**Emergentism releases as an openly inspectable, forkable, versioned research
corpus and a candidate grammar for unification by organization.** It offers a
common typed vocabulary for comparing constraints, option fields, commitments,
outcomes, learning, and persistent traces across sciences, while each science
retains its own methods and laws.

This is translation and research coordination. It is not **universal**
unification by derivation from one chart, proof of a necessary D0–D6 ontology, an externally calibrated
theory of everything, or evidence that every domain obeys one aggregation law.

The source tree is an inspectable model of the proposed organization. It is
still a map, not the territory. A directory placement remains `[I/C]` until its
bridge variables, measurements, rivals, predictions, and kill criterion are
declared and tested.

## 2. The medium supports the constitution; it does not equal it `[I]`

Repository mechanics create useful affordances. They do not automatically
enforce truth, identity, consent, custody, non-extraction, or justice.

| Affordance | What it can support | What is additionally required |
|---|---|---|
| version history | K3-style visible correction | protected refs, retention, append-only release ledgers, and independent mirrors |
| licensed forks | K4-style portability | a positive export manifest containing the required assets and dependencies, plus explicit trademark/data/service exclusions |
| pull-request review | A7-style challenge | required review, visible objections, meaningful falsifiers, and the ability to change canon |
| cryptographic signatures | accountable authorization | verified key custody plus principal, mandate, scope, consent, actor, consequence bearers, expiry/revocation, and contest path |
| open licensing | copying, study, modification, and redistribution within the license | separate economic and governance evidence before any `η=0` claim |
| issue tracking | a working interface for falsifiers | committed, hashed, exportable snapshots for durable custody |

A signature proves control of a key under a verification policy. It does not by
itself prove natural-person identity, authority, truth, morality, or informed
consent. Open licensing permits reuse; it does not prove non-extraction.

## 3. Unification by organization `[I/C]`

The research grammar is:

```text
lawful possibilities
  → organizational constraint
  → modeled alternatives
  → commitment
  → world outcome
  → model and selector revision
  → persistent collective trace
```

Candidate science placements ask which part of this grammar a domain studies
and whether the proposed bridge adds intervention-sensitive information beyond
domain-native rivals. Historical classification systems may illustrate how
organization can coordinate inquiry before mechanisms are complete; they do
not validate this framework. The periodic table, Linnaean taxonomy, and
Anderson's emergence hierarchy may be cited only with sources and at the tier
their histories support.

The live research question is not “how do we force every science into seven
folders?” It is:

> **Which constraints, option structures, aggregation laws, receipts, and
> persistent traces are operationally useful in this bounded domain, and what
> evidence would kill the proposed translation?**

### The positive kernel that releases with the wager

The audit cut false necessity claims; it did not erase bounded results. The
release front door must carry the positive and negative ledgers together:

| Result | Honest force |
|---|---|

| Balance-Game theorem | structural `[S]` inside its restrictive payoff model |

| mirror-pair and sign-square grammar | real algebraic shape; no forced centre or census seven |

The complete calibrated overview is `../00_WHAT_IS_EMERGENTISM.md`. None of
these entries establishes novelty, priority, universal empirical fit, or a
theory of everything.

## 4. Release phases

### P0 — Reconcile

Close the Receipt-126 propagation manifest in source-authority order. Active
owners must no longer assert retracted derivations, while historical receipts
and explicit tombstones remain visible.

### P1 — Extract

Build a fail-closed public export from a positive allowlist. Review every
included path for secrets, personal data, partner material, private governance,
third-party rights, attribution, and license compatibility. New paths default
to excluded.

### P2 — Instrument

Add the fourth script: a typed claim manifest and deterministic `canon-lint`.
The machine may validate metadata, transitions, references, retention, and
declared authorization. It may never report that a substantive claim is true or
proved merely because its schema passes.

### P3 — Orient

Provide one human front door, a tier key, a reader path, a contribution
constitution, and a small science scaffold whose placements carry explicit
tiers, measurements, rival models, predictions, and kill criteria.

### P4 — Challenge

Invite independent reviewers for mathematics, physics, emergence science,
comparative religion, ethics, security, privacy, and licensing. Publish their
reports and the framework's responses in full, subject to consent and lawful
redaction. “Externally reviewed” applies only to a surface with a dated report.

### P5 — Release candidate

Produce a reproducible export manifest, checksums, release notes, verified tag,
independent mirror, withdrawal/incident procedure, and named contact. A clean
checkout must reproduce the released bytes.

### P6 — Open governance

Move from named genesis custody to shared, contestable canonical authority.
Until a dated governance-adoption receipt exists, describe the corpus as
stewarded and open to contribution—not as governed by an activated public
council.

## 5. Launch blockers

Prime-time release is blocked until all of the following are true:

1. The Receipt-126 propagation manifest is closed with exact counts and
   source-negative checks.
2. The public export passes allowlist, secrets, privacy, provenance, license,
   and human-eyes review.
3. Licensing distinguishes original doctrine/content, software, third-party
   works, trademarks, data, and excluded material.
4. Epistemic CI passes adversarial fixtures and cannot upgrade its own evidence
   tier or pronounce truth.
5. A stranger can find the core claim, tier, falsifier, owner, and trial record
   without agent-only routing knowledge.
6. Every load-bearing release surface has the external review actually claimed
   for it.
7. Governance names authorization, mandate, custody, consent, contest,
   revocation, succession, and consequence bearers.
8. Released bytes reproduce from a clean checkout and at least one independent
   mirror verifies their hashes.

## 6. Governance transition `[D]`

- **Genesis custody:** historical signatures retain their provenance. A
  signature authenticates an authorized corpus act, not truth.
- **Open contribution:** anyone may fork, test, file a falsifier, or propose a
  change. Contribution does not itself confer merge authority.
- **Shared custody:** consequential canon changes require a complete accountable
  authorization envelope and review by more than one independent natural
  person.
- **Succession:** authority becomes role-based, revocable, time-bounded, and
  contestable. Founder custody does not silently become permanent public
  sovereignty.
- **Activation:** public governance exists only after a dated adoption receipt
  verifies its actual members, mandate, procedures, and custody.

## 7. Epistemic-CI minimum contract `[D]`

Every machine-governed claim record must declare:

```yaml
schemaVersion: 1
claimId: CC-0001
statement: "..."
tier: A|B|S|I|C|D
status: draft|active|retracted|superseded
register: analytic|empirical|structural|interpretive|conjectural
ownerPath: "..."
evidenceRefs: []
killCriterion: "..."          # or an explicit notApplicableReason
promotionRule: "..."
survivorIds: []
supersedes: []
authorizationRef: "..."       # required for a canonical status change
```

Authorization is a separate typed record. It must not be inferred from a name,
commit signature, repository role, or claim metadata.

The minimum adversarial fixtures reject:

- `[C]` presented as `[A]`;
- promotion without new evidence and valid authorization;
- deletion of a retracted claim rather than retained provenance;
- missing owner, source, kill criterion, or contest path;
- open-license-as-`η=0`, fork-as-complete-exit, or signature-as-personhood;
- rewriting of the canonical trial record without ledger detection;
- nondeterministic machine outputs; and
- any linter verdict that says a schema-valid claim is “proved.”

## 8. Non-goals

This doctrine does not authorize or claim:

- universal unification by derivation from the reciprocal chart, or a theory of everything;
- a forced, exhaustive, or native sevenfold ontology of nature;
- “first in history,” “only medium on Earth,” or “no other project” priority;
- religion-building, believer/member optimization, or human caste ranking;
- a packaged spiritual practice, foundation filing, production deployment,
  public-site mutation, or product launch;
- repository mechanics as substitutes for law, economics, identity
  verification, governance, or moral judgment; or
- deletion, refolding, or relocation of the sovereign working corpus merely to
  make the public export simpler.

## 9. Kill criterion

This release doctrine fails if its public medium makes the corpus less
contestable, less portable, less honest about evidence, or more dependent on an
unaccountable center than the private working corpus. In that case release
pauses; the failure is recorded rather than explained away.
