# RCAB-01

Requesting reason-based uptake from equally situated co-agents commits the
requester to: bearer visibility, reason access, contest, correction, and
feasible Exit. Arbitrarily denying those conditions while demanding them is
performatively inconsistent.

**Type:** constitutive inside reciprocal public justification. Not an ought
derived from value-free facts. Does not prove GEX-01. Does not establish
stance-independent moral realism.

Objectivity levels: DEFINITION_STABLE (this file) · PROCEDURALLY_REPRODUCIBLE
(testable) · EMPIRICALLY_ADEQUATE (conjectural) · STANCE_INDEPENDENT (unclaimed).
