# GEX-01 — The Guardianship Extension

Reciprocity is never a condition of worth, rights, protection or care. Infants,
non-speaking or incapacitated people, nonhuman animals, ecosystems and future
people may be materially affected even when they cannot enter RCAB-01's
reason-giving practice. GEX-01 is the separately disclosed Emergentist extension
for those cases.

## 1. Status and rule

GEX-01 is a chosen Justice commitment `[I]`, not a consequence of RCAB-01 and
not a proof of moral realism:

> A present actor who can materially alter a dependent or absent bearer's
> viability, rights, habitat or durable options must keep that bearer visible
> and use only a bounded, reviewable and least-restrictive protective mandate.

Guardianship does not create ownership, rank, substituted personhood, blanket
power, consent, authority or a right to command the bearer.

## 2. Required guardianship record

```text
bearer                  the actual or future interest-bearer
mandate_source          lawful or publicly declared basis for representation
protected_interest      the specific interest at stake
scope_and_duration      exact limits and expiry
evidence_and_uncertainty what is known, inferred and open
least_restrictive_test  why a less intrusive act is inadequate
conflicts               guardian and third-party interests
alternatives            including no-action and independent advocate
review_and_appeal       who can challenge and when
revocation              how the mandate ends or changes
exit                    the bearer's feasible exit, restoration or successor
outcome_and_repair      independent consequence evidence and correction path
```

Protection is a duty under a mandate, not authority manufactured by concern.
An AI, model, repository, organ or benchmark may assist with a proposal but may
not sign, authorize or appoint itself guardian.

## 3. Conflict and aggregation

The collective's benefit cannot launder a dependent bearer's destruction. A
proxy score may not stand in for the bearer when direct evidence is available.
When interests conflict, the record must expose the conflict, uncertainty,
irreversibility and distribution of burdens. An empty admissible set returns a
tragic residue; it does not turn coercion into syntropy.

Future and ecological bearers require explicit temporal and boundary choices.
“The ecosystem,” “humanity” or “future generations” cannot be used as an
unbounded abstract whole that makes present individuals invisible.

## 4. Rivals, discriminator, kill and survivor

Rivals include best-interests standards, supported decision-making, fiduciary
duty, capabilities, rights-based approaches, precaution, stewardship and the
null that no distinctive Emergentist extension is needed.

**Discriminator:** compare GEX-01 with those rivals on hidden-bearer detection,
conflict disclosure, least-restrictive choice, error correction, proxy capture,
restoration and practical Exit.

**Kill or narrow GEX-01** if it treats reciprocity as worth, makes protection
self-authorizing, hides a conflict, fuses guardian and bearer, permits
collective-benefit laundering, blocks independent review, or performs worse
than a simpler established safeguard.

**Survivor:** absent or dependent bearers still require explicit representation,
conflict disclosure and protection against proxy capture.
