# Objective Ethics Bridge Docket

Emergentism currently chooses Justice as a constitutional orientation. That
choice guides action but does not establish that moral facts are objective.
The moral-realist question therefore runs in parallel and may end in support,
narrowing, error theory, pluralism or deferment.

## The bridge question

Can any normative conclusion be warranted without hiding a normative premise
inside words such as *potential*, *balance*, *emergence*, *health*, *function*
or *flourishing*?

Every candidate bridge must expose:

```text
descriptive premises
normative premises
bridge rule
bearers and scope
counterexample class
rival theory
discriminator
kill or narrowing condition
survivor
```

No reciprocal-chart result, Titan frame, D-level, μ crossing, node product,
evolutionary pattern or aesthetic response supplies an ought by itself.

## Rival families

| Family | What could ground normativity | Main burden for this program |
|---|---|---|
| naturalist realism | natural facts such as welfare, needs, reasons or flourishing | avoid defining the normative conclusion into the natural property; answer open-question and disagreement challenges |
| non-naturalist realism | irreducible normative facts or reasons | explain epistemic access, supervenience and practical authority without special pleading |
| constructivism | norms generated by a valid practical standpoint or procedure | justify the standpoint and show why excluded bearers should accept it |
| contractualism | principles no one could reasonably reject | specify reasonableness, bargaining asymmetry, future bearers and nonparticipants |
| consequentialism | outcomes such as welfare or capability | declare aggregation, distribution, uncertainty, rights, thresholds and tragic residue |
| virtue ethics | excellent character and practical wisdom | avoid circular exemplars, status hierarchy and culture-bound authority |
| theological ethics | divine nature, command or sacred order | no tradition, revelation or contemplative report is assumed as a public premise |
| capabilities approach | real opportunities to be and do what matters | select the capabilities, bearers, thresholds and trade-offs transparently |
| error theory | ordinary moral assertions systematically fail to refer to objective facts | explain what chosen constitutional practice can still warrant without pretending realism |

## Candidate Emergentist bridge `[C]`

A bounded proposal may test whether bearer-complete option protection,
correction capacity and practical exit provide public reasons that survive
multiple rival theories. This is deliberately weaker than “maximize
potential.” It names actual persons and wholes, distinguishes capability from
preference, and records when options conflict.

The discriminator is not elegance. It is whether the proposal handles hard
cases—irreversible harm, aggregation, disability, future people, nonhuman
animals, coercion, deception and tragic conflict—more coherently and with
fewer hidden premises than serious rivals.

## Status rules

- **Component-supported** may apply to one bridge in one problem class; it does
  not establish global moral realism.
- Convergence among rival theories may support a practical overlap while their
  metaethical grounds remain incompatible.
- Persistent underdetermination keeps Justice chosen and contestable.
- A counterexample that defeats a universal bridge narrows the claim; it is not
  dismissed as a lower perspective.

## Kill and survivor

Kill an objective-Emergentist ethics claim if its conclusion depends on a
hidden value premise, geometry-to-duty transfer, aggregate sacrifice, human
rank, compelled teleology or circular definition of “good potential.”

The survivor may be:

> Justice is a declared, revisable constitutional commitment to visible
> bearers, accountable authorization, correction, repair and exit. It can be
> defended by public reasons even if no unique objective moral ontology has
> been established.
