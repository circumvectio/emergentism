# Justice over Goodness — a selection, defended `[D]`

`01_TRANSCENDENTALS.md` already chooses Beauty, Truth, and Justice `[I]`. `00_THE_GOOD_THE_EVIL_AND_THE_TRANSCENDENTALS.md` already declares the good as their AND-class coupling, not as a scalar. `52` :467 already fences BTJ as **not** the classical Beauty/Truth/Goodness; the analogy is `[I]`.

This file does not discover that substitution. It states **why the selection is the stronger one**, as a defence, not a finding. DF-21 stays closed: no ought from the chart. *"Geometry locates the constraint; the finite being chooses the vow."*

Say it as a **selection you defend**, not a discovery. “Better transcendental of the good” would be a comparative on a fenced analogy.

## 1 · Three structural reasons `[I]`

**Goodness is scalar; Justice is relational.** A thing “is good.” Justice holds *between* bearers — it names parties. The ethics after DF-21 is relational: bearers visible, nothing extracted without return, correction in public. Those are justice predicates. Goodness was never the term the surviving ethics used.

**Goodness aggregates; Justice under `min` cannot.** “Sum of the good” invites the compensation the AND-law forbids — one bearer is not made whole by making another better off. Justice under `min` *is* the no-compensation law read morally. Goodness would smuggle the product form back in through the ethics door after it was killed in the mathematics. The mathematics is an index here, not a warrant (DF-21).

**Goodness is the term that gets captured.** “Good for the race” is a sentence Rosenberg could write. “Just toward this bearer” is one he could not, because it demands you name who.

## 2 · Prior art, never first `[B]` attribution

Plato makes justice the master virtue of the *Republic* while keeping the Good above it. Aristotle calls justice “the complete virtue, toward another” — the only one essentially relational and essentially *done* (*NE* V). Rawls makes it the first virtue of institutions. Company, not solitude, not derivation.
