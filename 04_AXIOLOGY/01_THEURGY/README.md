## Claim Boundaries

- Strategic Implementation is `[S]` where it preserves the typed sequence
  `D5 option model -> authorization -> D4 commitment -> environmental outcome -> correction`.
- Strategic Implementation is [I] where it maps onto contemplative or esoteric practice traditions.
- Strategic Implementation is [C] where it claims direct F5 manipulation, reverse-time effect, or occult causality.
- An AI may route, witness, draft, lint, and warn. It may act only within a
  valid mandate and remains an actor whose consequences require custody and
  contest; it cannot invent its own authorization.
- No human, AI, council, or office becomes infallible by signing.
