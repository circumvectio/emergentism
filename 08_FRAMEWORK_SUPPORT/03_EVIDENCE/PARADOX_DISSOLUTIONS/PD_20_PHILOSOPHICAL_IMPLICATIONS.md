# PD-20 — Philosophy After a Bounded Reframe

Emergentism does not solve or dissolve philosophy in toto. It offers a
correction-governed lens for separating:

- map from territory;
- actual event from possible content;
- formal consequence from empirical contact;
- individual gain from bearer-complete Justice;
- useful analogy from proof transfer.

Some old puzzles become easier when a category error is exposed. That does not
show that all paradoxes are flat projections onto `S²`, that Descartes merely
chose the wrong geometry, or that the D0–D6 scaffold closes 2,500 years of
inquiry. Each proposed dissolution owes a local argument and remains at its own
tier.

The reciprocal identity `P∞=φν=1` holds by definition on the open chart; it is
not a state a practitioner can “maintain under perturbation.” The selected
balance score is not practical wisdom. Justice, judgment, evidence, and action
remain separate disciplines.

First-person experience may orient inquiry and disclose salience. Quiet sitting
does not grant direct public access to `Φ`, the Ground, or a higher evidence
tier. A report becomes publicly testable only through explicit boundary,
method, alternatives, custody, and possible defeat.

## What remains for philosophy

1. Clarify the categories and assumptions in which a question is posed.
2. Compare live rival accounts rather than translate them into one table.
3. Ask which consequences follow formally and which require contact with the
   world.
4. Make normative commitments visible instead of laundering them through
   nature or mathematics.
5. Preserve revision and exit when a worldview becomes a cage.

The sphere may help visualize a relation `[I]`; removing it leaves this work
intact.

**Kill criterion.** Drop the special Emergentist framing wherever ordinary
philosophical distinctions explain the case as well or better. A counterexample
to one proposed dissolution changes that case, not philosophy as a whole.
