# PD-04 — Zeno's Motion Paradoxes

The standard resolution needs no Burri sphere. A convergent geometric series
can contain infinitely many terms while having a finite sum, and a continuous
trajectory can traverse a bounded interval without performing a completed list
of discrete stopping acts. In ordinary analysis, Achilles reaches the target
at the finite limit time `[A]`.

Compactness of `S²` does **not** make an arbitrary trajectory or partition
converge; in metric spaces it guarantees convergent subsequences under the
relevant hypotheses. Nor does Zeno force a continuous topology onto a discrete
lattice. Those were false upgrades.

points, intervals, events, and an agent's imagined sequence of tasks? The D
registers can help keep these types apart, but add no mathematical solution
unless they yield a new discriminator.
