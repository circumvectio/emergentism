# PD_08: THE LIAR PARADOX

**Canonical Number:** PD_08 (see [PD_00_INDEX](PD_00_INDEX.md))

> **[金] Kintsugi seam.** The former page assigned meaning to D4, truth
> evaluation to D5, and called the Liar dimensionally impossible. That inverted
> the active D4/D5 contract and overstated an analogy as a logical result. This
> successor keeps the useful warning about type confusion without claiming that
> sphere geometry solves the paradox.

## 1. The logical problem `[B]`

The sentence “This sentence is false” creates trouble for an unrestricted
truth predicate in a language capable of referring to its own sentences.
Different formal treatments respond differently: object-language/meta-language
hierarchies, partial truth predicates, fixed-point semantics, type theories,
and paraconsistent logics are among the established options.

Gödel incompleteness and Tarski undefinability are precise theorems with their
own hypotheses. The resemblance to semantic self-reference is important, but
the Emergentist scaffold neither proves nor replaces those theorems.

## 2. The typed Emergentist crosswalk `[I]`

The canonical modality distinction is:

```text
D4 = causal actuality
D5 = merely possible counterfactual content
```

An utterance, inscription, parser state, truth-assignment attempt, ranking
event, and evaluation receipt are all **D4 actual**. A proposition can have
counterfactual alternatives—what would follow if it were true or false—and
those represented alternatives can be modeled as **D5 possible content**.
Self-reference by itself does not make a sentence D5.

```text
SentenceToken:D4(actual)
  ├─ refers-to ─▶ SentenceToken:D4(actual)
  └─ represents ─▶ {true-case,false-case}:D5(possible)

EvaluationEvent:D4(actual) ── attempts ──▶ TruthAssignment:D4(actual)
```

The useful Emergentist lesson is therefore modest: do not collapse a token,
its represented content, the language that evaluates it, and the evaluation
event into one untyped object. That discipline resembles familiar logical
stratification. It does not derive stratification from `S²`.

## 3. What the sphere may and may not do

The Burri sphere may serve as a **Rosetta projection** for displaying distinct
roles and relations. It may not transfer a topological fact into a theorem of
semantics. In particular:

- no point on `S²` is “truth” or “falsehood” by mathematical necessity;
- `φν=1` is a reciprocal-chart identity, not a truth-consistency law;
- D4 and D5 are not two truth values or two logical moments;
- no `μ` crossing is needed to utter, parse, or evaluate the Liar;
- the Liar is not physically or dimensionally impossible.

Removing the sphere analogy leaves the operational type warning intact.

## 4. What survives

The paradox remains a valuable audit of self-sealing systems. A model becomes
epistemically dangerous when it both generates a claim and declares itself the
only valid judge of that claim. The Soul Loop therefore keeps model, selector,
commitment receipt, outcome receipt, and correction rule separately
inspectable.

That is a governance and model-design lesson `[I]`, not a new solution to the
Liar.

## 5. Kill criteria

This crosswalk loses its value if:

1. typing token, content, evaluator, and evaluation adds no clarity to a
   concrete formalization;
2. an established logical account handles the target case without any
   analogous separation and with lower complexity;
3. the Rosetta picture is used to override, rather than summarize, the formal
   semantics of the chosen logic.
