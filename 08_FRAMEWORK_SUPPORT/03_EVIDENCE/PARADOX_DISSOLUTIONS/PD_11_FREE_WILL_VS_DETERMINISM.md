# PD-11: FREE WILL AND DETERMINISM

## A typed reframing, not a dissolution by geometry

> **[金] Kintsugi seam — options do not escape causation.** The predecessor
> mapped determination and freedom onto a sphere, treated balance as lawful
> selection, and partly grounded responsibility in equatorial position. The
> repair keeps the useful distinction between actual causes and represented
> alternatives without claiming to settle metaphysical free will.

## 1. The dilemma

Determinism asks whether an action follows from prior conditions and laws.
Libertarian freedom asks whether an agent could genuinely have done otherwise.
Randomness alone supplies neither authorship nor responsibility. Compatibilist
accounts instead locate freedom in kinds of causally realized control.

Emergentism does not prove one of these positions. It offers a typed model for
examining a narrower question: when does an actual system represent
alternatives, select among them for reasons, attempt an action, and revise
itself from the outcome?

## 2. One modality, two contents

```text
D4 = causal actuality
D5 = merely possible counterfactual content
```

A present model token, comparison, selection event, body, means, attempted
action, and receipt are D4 actual. The alternative represented by a token is
D5 merely possible. Possibility is not a second substance and does not suspend
physical law.

The candidate crossing `μ₄:D4→D5` asks whether counterfactual modeling adds a
discriminable capacity at a chosen scale. Missing reduction is
`currently_unreduced`, never proof of irreducibility. A successful reduction
makes the crossing weak emergence without erasing the operational distinction.

## 3. Agency in the Soul Loop

An agent's D4 model can represent and rank D5 alternatives. A commitment then
references one alternative and combines it with actual means and an
authorization assessment to attempt a D4 action. The environment—not the
selector—returns the outcome. Receipts update model and selector.

Changing a represented future may therefore change present action
distributions. Emergentism calls this model-mediated retrocausality; standard
causal language calls it anticipatory or future-guided control. Every physical
carrier in the chain remains present or past-directed in the ordinary causal
order; no future event propagates backward in time.

## 4. What the model can and cannot say

The model can operationalize degrees of control:

- quality and breadth of represented alternatives;
- accuracy of prediction;
- usable means and physical reachability;
- sensitivity of choice to reasons and interventions;
- freedom from coercion or manipulation;
- ability to learn from commitment and outcome receipts.

These variables may support a compatibilist account of agency `[I/C]`. They do
not prove libertarian freedom, show that D4-only descriptions are impossible,
or make every counterfactual-capable system morally responsible.

## 5. Responsibility requires an independent norm

Responsibility is not distance from an equator. It depends on evidence about
knowledge, foresight, means, control, coercion, authorization, affected
bearers, and receipted consequence under a declared legal or moral standard.

The Emergentist Justice envelope supplies one explicit normative construction.
It is not derived from D4, D5, `P_node`, balance, or `η_observed`. Diminished
capacity can mitigate responsibility in a specific practice only under the
practice's separately justified rules.

## 6. Falsifiers and survivors

The counterfactual-agency hypothesis loses empirical force if represented
alternatives, reason-sensitive intervention, and feedback memory add no
predictive discrimination over a lower-level null model. The strong-emergence
reading fails if the capacity is successfully reduced.

What survives either result is the type discipline: actual tokens and events
must not be confused with their merely possible contents, and intended actions
must not be confused with outcomes.

## Verdict

Emergentism reframes the free-will problem as an empirical study of
counterfactual control plus an independently declared account of
responsibility. It does not dissolve the metaphysical debate by placing freedom
at D5 or by converting geometry into morality.
