## 1. The Live Problem

Functional accounts can explain discrimination, report, integration, memory,
attention, control, and behavior to varying degrees. The hard problem asks why
or whether such processes are accompanied by phenomenal experience. Showing a
function does not by itself entail a phenomenology; asserting irreducibility
does not prove it either.

Emergentism records this as an open explanatory gap, not a completed paradox
dissolution.

## 2. Two Evidence Channels

- **First-person:** experience and disciplined reports are data available to
  the subject. Sitting or contemplative practice can improve observation, but
  does not create public proof by immediacy alone.
- **Third-person:** behavior, reports, physiology, neural activity, and
  interventions support public comparison. They can correlate with experience
  without settling its ontology.

The channels constrain one another. Neither is entitled to erase the other.

## 3. The Soul-Loop Contribution

The Soul Loop gives a functional architecture:

```text
present D4 state and model -> represented D5 alternatives
-> D4 ranking/selection -> commitment receipt -> action
-> world-grounded outcome receipt -> revised model/selector
```

This architecture separates cognitive, execution, and outcome gaps and makes
future-directed content causally tractable through present models. It may help
study report, metacognition, agency, and self-model revision. It does not explain
why any of this feels like something.

`φ`, `ν`, `B`, `P_node`, and the sphere are optional model coordinates. They do
not prove phenomenal/physical identity, duality, conservation of experience,
or a refutation of philosophical zombies. D5 is possible content, not
consciousness.

## 4. Candidate Emergence, Honestly Typed

A consciousness-related `μ` crossing may be recorded as `currently_unreduced`
only when its saturation evidence, proposed new freedom, lower-register
recovery, rivals, prediction, and kill criterion are explicit. Lack of a
reduction is not evidence of strong emergence. A successful reduction
reclassifies the candidate without destroying the scaffold.

## 5. Research Questions

1. Do receipt-separated self-model loops predict report or control better than
   simpler feed-forward, recurrent, predictive-processing, global-workspace,
   higher-order, or other rivals?
2. Do interventions on represented futures change present choice and
   metacognitive report as predicted?
3. Which first-person protocols add reliable signal beyond expectancy,
   suggestion, and third-person measures?
4. Can `Φ/V` variables be operationalized reliably, and do they outperform
   domain-native measures?
5. Does any result discriminate phenomenal theories rather than merely fit
   behavior?

## 6. Defeat Conditions

The Emergentist consciousness program contracts if its variables cannot be
measured, its interventions add no discriminating signal, simpler rivals win,
or its practice reports fail reliability and bias controls. Even a successful
functional model must not be advertised as closure of the hard problem without
an argument that reaches phenomenology.

## Compression

> **Experience is real as disclosed data; its mechanism and ontology remain
> open. The Soul Loop is a testable functional lens, not the missing proof.**
