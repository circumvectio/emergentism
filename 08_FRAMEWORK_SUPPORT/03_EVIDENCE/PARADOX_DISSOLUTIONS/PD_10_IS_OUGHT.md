## 1. The Gap Survives

A set of descriptive premises does not entail a normative conclusion unless a
normative bridge premise is supplied. Neither `φν=1`, `B≤1`, `P_node:=ΦV`, a
physical law, an evolutionary outcome, nor a payoff maximum proves what anyone
ought to do.

Emergentism therefore does not solve Hume's gap by deduction. It exposes the
extra premise and makes its application auditable.

## 2. The Declared Bridge

The framework posits `[I]`:

> A consequential act is admissible only when it preserves the separately
> visible durable potentials of individual and sustaining whole under
> bearer-complete Justice.

Justice includes principal, mandate, scope, consent, custody,
expiry/revocation, contest path, actor, consequence bearer, payer,
beneficiary, reversibility, and visible cost. Authorization and Justice are
distinct: valid authority does not make an act just, and a just aspiration does
not grant authority.

Reject this postulate and the classical gap remains. Accept it and the problem
becomes a transparent constrained-decision problem.

## 3. Typed Decision Interface

```text
D4 actual:
  state + present model token + ranking/selection event + embodied means
  + authorization + commitment receipt + action + outcome receipt

D5 possible:
  counterfactual alternatives and future-directed contents represented
  by the D4 model
```

The selector interface is:

```text
chi_t : (X_t, Omega_t, M_t, V_t, U_t) -> (a_t, q_t)
(X_(t+1), r_(t+1)) ~ K_t(. | X_t, a_t, E_t)
(M_(t+1), G_(t+1)) = Loop(M_t, G_t, q_t, r_(t+1))
```

`q_t` records the commitment/attempt. `r_(t+1)` records independently
world-grounded outcome evidence. They are distinct typed records and
epistemic roles; the outcome cannot be circularly self-certified.

Anticipated futures can influence the present because their content is encoded
in a present D4 model that changes selection. This is ordinary model-mediated
causation, not a physical signal from the future.

## 4. Power-Max Does Not Derive the Norm

For a declared horizon `T`:

```text
W_x(T) = integral_0^T P_x(t) dt

A_J = {a : J(a; all consequence bearers)
           and Delta_T W_i(a) >= 0
           and Delta_T W_H(a) >= 0}

a* in argmax_(a in A_J) E_Mt[W_i(T) | a]
```

Justice defines the admissible field; Power-Max chooses within it. The
extraction counterexample matters precisely because unconstrained or
side-payment games can reward one-shot extraction. Coupling and a long horizon
may change incentives in a fully specified game, but they do not universally
align self and whole.

The scalar Balance Game proves only that its declared balance payoff is
maximized at its center. It contains no extraction reward and therefore cannot
serve as proof that real extraction is dominated.

## 5. What the Compass Adds

The useful compression is procedural:

```text
facts constrain -> models represent alternatives -> values rank them
-> authorization licenses an attempt -> the world returns consequence
-> receipts revise map and selector
```

This does not erase the difference between facts and values. It prevents the
difference from becoming an excuse for opaque values, unaccountable action, or
unreceipted consequences.

Prospective labels use predicted deltas. Moral, ethical, syntropic, sacrifice,
and failure labels are awarded retrospectively from receipted deltas under
Justice. Aggregate gain cannot launder an omitted or destroyed bearer.

## 6. What Would Defeat the Proposal

The operational bridge should be revised or retired if:

1. a simpler decision architecture matches or exceeds its prediction,
   contestability, and error-correction performance;
2. separating commitment from outcome does not reduce cognitive, execution,
   or outcome-gap errors;
3. the Justice envelope systematically hides rather than exposes materially
   affected bearers;
4. interventions on represented futures do not change present choices where
   the model predicts they should; or
5. domain-specific rival objectives outperform `P_node` and Power-Max under
   preregistered criteria.

Analytic identities remain analytic; changing a definition creates a rival
model rather than empirically falsifying an identity.

## 7. Compression

> **The “ought” is declared, the option field is modeled, the act is
> authorized, and the consequence is receipted.**

That is not a derivation of value from geometry. It is a compass for making the
value premise visible and holding its use accountable.
