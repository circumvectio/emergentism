# PD_10: THE IS-OUGHT GAP

**Canonical Number:** PD_10 (see [PD_00_INDEX](PD_00_INDEX.md))

> **[金] Kintsugi seam.** The former page made “Is” a D4 witness, “Ought” a
> D5 agent, and a D4→D5 transition into normative mechanics. That both inverted
> modality and smuggled a value premise into geometry. The repair preserves the
> classical gap and states Emergentism's bridge as a chosen, auditable vow.

## 1. What cannot be derived `[B]`

A description of facts does not, without a normative premise, entail what an
agent ought to do. Neither the reciprocal chart, `S²`, the D-registers,
`P_node=ΦV`, nor a `μ` record supplies that missing premise.

The selected finite-node product is a declared normalized conjunctive model.
It can describe trade-offs after variables and goals are chosen; it cannot
choose the good by algebra alone.

## 2. The modality contract

```text
D4 = actual state, actual means, present model tokens, actual ranking and
     selection events, attempted action, commitment receipt, outcome receipt

D5 = merely possible alternatives and modeled-future contents
```

An ought is not “located in D5.” A norm, promise, law, or vow has a D4-actual
carrier when written, remembered, spoken, or enforced. It can refer to D5
possible outcomes. The actual choice and action remain D4 events.

```text
NormToken:D4(actual) ── evaluates ──▶ Alternative:D5(possible)
SelectionEvent:D4(actual) + Means:D4(actual) ── attempts ──▶ Action:D4(actual)
```

No future event physically causes the present. Present models of possible
futures can reweight present action.

## 3. Emergentism's chosen bridge `[I]`

Emergentism adopts a Justice envelope rather than pretending to discover an
unconditional command in nature. Let `i` be an individual bearer, `H` the
coupled whole, `W_x(T)` durable potential over horizon `T`, and `J` the
accountable-authorization, consent, custody, contest, reversibility, and exit
conditions.

```text
Moral(a)    iff Δ_T W_H(a) > 0 and Δ_T W_i(a) ≥ 0 and J(a;i,H)
Ethical(a)  iff Δ_T W_i(a) > 0 and Δ_T W_H(a) ≥ 0 and J(a;i,H)
Syntropic(a) iff Δ_T W_i(a) > 0 and Δ_T W_H(a) > 0 and J(a;i,H)
```

These are **definitions and commitments**, not theorems forced by the chart.
Aggregate benefit cannot launder the destruction of either bearer. A voluntary
sacrifice is recorded as a distinct costly class; it is not mislabeled strict
syntropy and cannot be demanded as proof of virtue.

## 4. The bounded Power-Max result `[S/I]`

For a declared model:

```text
W_x(T) = ∫₀ᵀ P_x(t) dt

A_J = {a : J(a;i,H) and Δ_T W_i(a) ≥ 0 and Δ_T W_H(a) ≥ 0}

a* ∈ argmax_(a∈A_J) E_Mt[W_i(T) | a]
```

This is a transparent constrained objective. It does **not** show that
extraction is dominated in every game. Without enforced non-extraction,
adequate horizon, real coupling, and visible side payments, one-shot
extraction can benefit the extractor. The Justice envelope excludes such a
move normatively; geometry does not make it impossible.

## 5. What is genuinely gained

The framework does not close Hume's gap. It makes the bridge inspectable:

1. state the actual conditions and affected bearers;
2. represent possible consequences;
3. disclose the value premise and authorization;
4. select and attempt an action;
5. separate commitment from outcome;
6. update the model and selector from receipts.

That is a compass for accountable action. It is stronger than a hidden ought
because the vow can be contested, revised, or refused.

## 6. Kill criteria

The Emergentist bridge fails in a scope if:

- the Justice conditions cannot be operationalized or audited;
- an action classified as syntropic destroys either individual or whole;
- hidden payers or beneficiaries reverse the verdict;
- the model cannot distinguish intended, attempted, and observed outcomes;
- the framework presents its chosen vow as an empirically discovered command.
