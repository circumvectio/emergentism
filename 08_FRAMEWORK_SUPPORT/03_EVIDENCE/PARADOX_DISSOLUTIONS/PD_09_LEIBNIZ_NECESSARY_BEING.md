# PD-09 · LEIBNIZ'S NECESSARY BEING

**Status:** ACTIVE boundary clarification · repaired 2026-07-20
**Verdict:** Emergentism reframes the question but does not prove a necessary
being, necessary topology, or necessary actuality.

> **[金] Kintsugi replacement.** The predecessor correctly cautioned that the

## 1. Three questions that must not be fused

1. **Logical necessity:** what follows in a specified formal system?
2. **Explanatory necessity:** what premises a model requires to explain a class
   of observations?
3. **Ontological necessity:** what must exist in every possible reality?

An analytic identity can answer the first relative to definitions. It cannot by
itself answer the third. Calling a topology "necessary" does not remove the need
to justify why that topology applies to actuality.

## 2. What the framework actually has

On the declared open reciprocal chart,

```text
phi = cot(theta/2)
nu  = tan(theta/2)
phi*nu = 1
```

This is `[A]` mathematics. `S²`, `{0,1,infinity}`, the Titan roles, the product
node model, and D0-D6 are selected modeling commitments at their own tiers. None
is a forced generator of reality. Ordinary mathematical use of the empty set
also cannot be promoted into a physical event called "nothing generating
something."

The Crown Wager—that logically coherent necessary structure is somehow
instantiated as das All—remains `[C]`. It is a wager precisely because no
derivation inside the selected algebra certifies its own physical instantiation.

## 3. The modest reframing

Leibniz's question can be sharpened from "which supreme entity explains every
fact?" to "which explanatory dependencies, if any, are unavoidable in our best
models?" This shift may reduce personification and expose assumptions. It does
not dissolve the regress, establish a final ground, or refute theological
answers.

The D4/D5 distinction contributes only this: actual arguments, models, and
records are D4; the possible worlds and alternatives they represent are D5
content. A represented necessary being is not actualized by being represented.

## 4. What would move the claim

The Crown Wager would gain empirical relevance if a precisely stated structural
constraint produced a novel discriminator that independently outperformed rival
models across observations. It would still not become logical necessity without
a valid modal argument.

This reframing fails if it merely renames Leibniz's being as "topology," if an
independent ontological argument succeeds, or if the selected structure adds no
explanatory or predictive gain. The honest remainder is an open question, not a
completed proof.
