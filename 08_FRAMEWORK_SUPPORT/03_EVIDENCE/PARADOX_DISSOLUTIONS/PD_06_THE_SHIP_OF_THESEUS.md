# PD_06: THE SHIP OF THESEUS

**Status:** Active Kintsugi reframe
**Evidence tier:** `[I]` philosophical model; `[C/B]` for domain applications

> **[金] Kintsugi seam (2026-07-19).** The prior text identified the ship with
> an equatorial position, assumed the rebuilt planks were nonfunctional, and
> tied identity to `η`. Those claims do not follow. The repair preserves the
> useful distinction between carrier turnover and persistent trace while
> keeping rival identity criteria explicit. The prior body is preserved in Git
> blob `c2cc5440e76155312e398d28ed7d687d3d63fe44`.

## 1. Why the Paradox Persists

“Same ship” can mean several things:

- same material components;
- continuous spatiotemporal object;
- continuous causal and maintenance history;
- same function or design;
- same name, custody, legal title, or social recognition;
- same narrative or institutional trace.

The original vessel and the vessel rebuilt from removed planks can each satisfy
different subsets. Either can be seaworthy. No contradiction follows until one
criterion is silently treated as the only meaning of identity.

## 2. Emergentist Compression

The Compass proposes a trace criterion for systems whose carriers change:

```text
IdentityCandidate(x_t, x_(t+1)) requires:
  declared continuity criterion
  + persistent causal/operational trace
  + custody and provenance
  + measurable conditioning of later behavior
  + explicit rival criteria and contest path
```

This is close to the operational Egregoreotype test: a pattern may persist
across carrier turnover when a shared trace survives and measurably reweights
later selections. It does not imply consciousness, personhood, or metaphysical
identity.

Material reduction is compatible with this view. A continuity relation can be
fully physically realized while still being the criterion users care about.
The issue is which relation the word “same” denotes, not whether continuity is
made of nonphysical stuff.

## 3. D4/D5 Type Boundary

The planks, repair events, custody records, functions, models, and judgments are
D4 actual. A D4 model may represent D5 possible continuations (“repair this
ship,” “reassemble those planks”). D5 does not carry identity or act.

`P_node`, `B`, the equator, and `η` are optional diagnostics only after a
domain mapping is declared. None defines identity.

## 4. What the Reframe Solves

It dissolves the demand for one context-free answer. Before deciding ownership,
conservation, responsibility, or naming, state the operative criterion and its
bearers. Two claimants can then disagree substantively rather than equivocate
over “same.”

This is a compass, not a metaphysical proof. Law, engineering, heritage, and
ordinary speech may legitimately use different criteria.

## 5. Defeat and Revision Conditions

Revise or retire the trace model if:

1. it cannot distinguish cases that domain experts and affected bearers
   reliably distinguish;
2. a simpler material, causal, functional, legal, or narrative criterion
   predicts judgments and consequences better;
3. the declared trace cannot be measured or intervened on; or
4. custody and contest rules let one claimant manufacture continuity merely by
   naming it.

## Compression

> **The paradox is not “which object owns identity?” but “which continuity
> relation is relevant here, to whom, and with what receipts?”**
