# PD-06 — The Ship of Theseus

The paradox persists because “the same” can name different criteria:

- material continuity;
- spatiotemporal and causal continuity;
- functional or organizational continuity;
- historical provenance;
- legal, social, or narrative recognition.

No single criterion is forced in every context. Replacing planks gradually may
preserve some criteria while the reassembled planks preserve others. The
reassembled ship is not assumed functionless. Which answer matters depends on
the question—navigation, ownership, conservation, memory, or history.

Emergentism can ask which D1 distinctions, D2 relations, D3 persistence
conditions, and D4 records a chosen identity rule preserves `[I]`. Identity is
not “position on `S²`,” equatorial balance does not cause persistence, and `η`
does not settle numerical identity. `Φ` is a present D4-evaluated score
concerning D5 option content, not an identity substance.
