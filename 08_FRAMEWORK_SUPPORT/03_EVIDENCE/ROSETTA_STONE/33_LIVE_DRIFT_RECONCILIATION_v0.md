# Live Drift Reconciliation v0

> Four repairs the Rosetta refinements require. Each one was identified in
> 30_ROSETTA_VNEXT_REFINEMENT_2026_07_31 §6 and §8.6; each one lands here as a
> concrete correction with the smallest possible change.

---

## Drift 1 · KSC-02 product form still surfaces in worldview artefacts

**The drift.** The 2026-07-29 ruling (HR-D1) selected `min(Φ̂₄, V₄)` as the
working ordinal AND-class score and explicitly retired the product `Φ̂₄ · V₄`
as a ranking. Yet several worldview artefacts still foreground the product
form: the Weltanschauung, the Goal, the Lived Compass, the Master Rosetta,
the G7 owner, and the regime-table equations.

**The correction.** The product form is the **historical conjunction name**,
not a ranking. The Master Rosetta is updated to state this directly (see
§3 of `00_THE_MASTER_ROSETTA.md`). Cells in any pack that still use the
product form as a working score are marked `[D]` for downstream review; the
K-1 owner of each cell decides whether to re-tier to `[I]` (historical
naming) or to retire.

**The non-transfer rule.** "The product was retired; min is the score"
**does not** mean "min is a Rosetta geometry." Min is a **selection policy
for an AND-class combination**. Rosetta does not select the policy; the
owner does. Rosetta records the selection and the discriminator.

## Drift 2 · "The base" — unqualified, after the 2026-07-31 ruling

**The drift.** The 2026-07-31 ruling said neither mathematical base has
primacy and banned the unqualified phrase "the base" in cross-base
discussion. Yet `00_THE_FOUNDATION.md` and the 5+1 Constitution still
narrate one base producing the sphere.

**The correction.** A base is a **downstream projection** of a foundation
notion; the foundation notion itself is upstream of any base. The phrase
"the base" is replaced with "this base" or "the selected base" wherever it
appears unqualified in the Foundation and the 5+1.

**The rule.** *A Rosetta cell does not promote a chosen base to the
foundation.* If two bases disagree, the cell records both, the
discriminator, and the kill criterion; the disagreement is owned by the
foundation, not resolved by Rosetta.

## Drift 3 · Equal dignity on geometry — a recurring failure

**The drift.** Parts of the replicator projection (`REP7@1`) still lean on
`φν = 1` to support equal dignity across castes. The 2026-07-30 council
caught the same error class elsewhere: an η=0 conclusion derived from an
aesthetic property of the golden ratio. The rationale was struck; the
distribution kept.

**The correction.** Equal dignity is a **Justice and anti-reification
commitment**, not a derivation from geometry. Geometry cannot manufacture
ethics. The replicator projection is amended: equal dignity is recorded as
a `PHIL7@1:value` precondition, not a `G7@1` or `GEN7@1` consequence.

**The standing failure mode.** This is the second instance of the same
error class. It is named here as a **standing failure mode** rather than two
isolated corrections:

> **Elegance mistaken for warrant.** When a derivation produces an η=0 or
> Justice-shaped conclusion and the derivation rides on an aesthetic
> property of a chosen object (golden ratio, symmetry, fixed point,
> convergence, "natural" structure), the rationale is suspect. Audit
> every such derivation for this pattern.

The auditor checklist now includes: *does the η=0 conclusion follow from a
necessity of structure, or from a chosen object?* The latter is not
warrant; the former is — and only after the necessity is itself audited.

## Drift 4 · Soul Loop not updating U

**The drift.** The formal Soul Loop updates `M` and `G` (model and selection
policy) from the outcome receipt `r_{t+1}`, but does not update `U`
(values / authorisation). If outcomes are meant to revise axiology, the
model must explicitly place revisable value weights inside `G` or add a
separately governed `U_{t+1}`. Authorisation must not silently rewrite
itself.

**The correction (v0, partial).** Two admissible options:

- **Option A.** Treat `U` as **non-revisable from receipts** and document
  the reason. U is a *constitutional* parameter; it changes by owner
  ruling, not by outcome. This is the conservative read and the default
  for `SOUL4@0`.
- **Option B.** Add an explicit `U_{t+1}` revision step, with its own
  guardrail (e.g., "values revision requires an explicit owner signal or
  a K-1/K-4 ruling, never a silent outcome-driven update"). The
  guardrail is the load-bearing piece: it must be impossible for an
  outcome receipt to silently rewrite the values that authorised the
  act that produced it.

`SOUL4@0` adopts **Option A** for v0: U is constitutional; outcomes do
not silently update it. A future v1 may add an explicit `U_{t+1}` step with
a guardrail; that v1 is not in scope today.

**The rule.** A Soul Loop that lets an outcome receipt silently rewrite
its own values is not corrigible. Corrigibility requires the values to be
*available* for revision by owner, not by receipt.

## Why these four together

These are not four unrelated corrections. They are the same shape:

- **Drift 1** is "elegance as warrant" applied to a selection policy.
- **Drift 2** is "elegance as warrant" applied to a base choice.
- **Drift 3** is the same pattern stated explicitly.
- **Drift 4** is the runtime consequence of unstated values revision — the
  *one place* the loop could silently promote elegance into warrant, and
  the rule that prevents it.

The four corrections together say: *every place the corpus has let a chosen
object do the work of a chosen rule, the rule must be made explicit, owned,
and auditable.*
