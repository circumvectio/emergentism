# Rosetta vNext — refinement proposal

**Governing conclusion (owner):** *refine the Rosetta inward before expanding it outward.*

The kill criterion fired: a universal seven-level pattern was **not** established. What
survived is more valuable — a disciplined translation instrument for comparing relational
structures **without transferring proof or authority**.

---

## 1. Why the Rosetta seems mysterious

Four distinct effects can each produce apparent correspondence:

1. A genuinely recurring functional problem.
2. Common constraints of human cognition and organisation.
3. Historical diffusion or shared ancestry between traditions.
4. **The interpreter's freedom to merge, reorder, rename, or omit categories.**

**vNext must distinguish these four rather than treating every resemblance as convergence.**
Effect 4 is the one that manufactures agreement for free.

## 2. Core + Packs + Ledger

## 5. Three separate evidential questions

- **`fact_tier`** — is the source-domain fact established?
- **`mapping_tier`** — is this placement within Rosetta warranted?
- **`outcome_status`** — did the translated use actually work?

An established neuroscience fact placed speculatively at L6 is **still a speculative cell**.
A beautiful translation producing no external benefit is **still an interpretation**.

## 6. Mirrors, regimes, drift

- **Mirrors** (L1↔L7, L2↔L6, L3↔L5, L4 self) default to **`unknown`**, never `true`. Each
  pack names what is inverted, what survives, and what would break it.
- **Regimes** become **dynamic capture signatures**, not row outcomes. The question is:
  *what happens when one function monopolises evidence, alternatives, authorisation, means,
  receipts and correction?* A healthy organism rotates through functions; a captured one
  freezes one into sovereignty.
- **Two drift repairs are urgent.** KSC-02 selects `min(Φ̂₄,V₄)` as the working ordinal
  AND-class score and retires the product as a ranking, while several Rosetta surfaces still
  foreground the product. And **equal dignity rests on the Justice and anti-reification
  commitment — not on `φν=1`. Geometry cannot manufacture ethics.**

## 7. Expansions worth making

**A. A 7 × phase Soul-Loop matrix** — apply every philosophical question to every phase
rather than forcing one question into one temporal stage.

**B. A formal `SOUL4` pack**, recorded as a downstream application, with fences: Circle does
not make information true by collecting it; ReFu does not make a possibility feasible by
generating it; Agentz does not acquire signing authority by evaluating values; Skyzai does
not manufacture the outcome by coordinating commitment; the world returns the receipt.
Cosmology, ontology and horizon are **cross-cutting contracts over all four organs**, not
three missing products.

**C. A Counter-Rosetta library** — strongest 5-, 6-, 8-row and domain-native rivals for every
serious projection.

**D. Cultural dependency mapping** — `independent | common ancestry | direct diffusion |
modern synthesis | framework-derived | unknown`. **Ten correspondences descending from one
Indo-European lineage are not ten independent observations.**

**E. Egregoreotype testing** — persistent trace across carrier turnover, reweighting after
intervention, recurrent objective-like bias, resistance to individual replacement, visible
substrate cost, and **a result distinguishable from ordinary institutional incentives**.

**F. Translation-utility experiments** — blind coders against native and n-row grammars;
teams using Rosetta versus an ordinary glossary; bounded teams using the matrix versus a
standard decision protocol.

> **The deepest invariant may not be "everything has seven levels." It may be: different
> domains repeatedly confront boundary, knowledge, inference, value, world-model, being and
> ultimate-horizon problems; a disciplined translation can reveal their relational
> similarities without erasing their native differences.**

## 8. Agent pushes — `[D]`, staged, unsigned

Four objections and one convergence. These are the agent's, not the owner's.

### 8.1 The `SOUL4` diagram breaches a fence it lists

The draft assigns Skyzai *"purpose, means, coordination."* But Skyzai's own route card states
it **"does not observe, predict, or decide. It executes and remembers."** Purpose is
L1/teleology — upstream of all four organs. As drawn, the diagram licenses F4 to originate
ends, which is the merge the fences exist to prevent.

Skyzai's slot is narrower: **receipt-bound execution and memory.** Means-selection sits with
F3. Purpose sits above the loop — which *supports* §7B's own conclusion: it belongs with
cosmology, ontology and horizon as a cross-cutting contract, not inside an organ.

### 8.2 The fences guard overreach but not disconnection

All four fences constrain an organ from claiming too much. The failure observed in the
running system on 2026-07-30 was the **opposite**: the loop had no return leg. RealityFutures
had no way to acknowledge TheCircle — no route, no schema, no storage — and it went unnoticed
for months **while every fence held.** Four organs in a row is not a loop.

**Proposed fifth fence, dual to the others:** *each organ must be able to receive from the
next, or the arrow is decorative.* The measure is **closure**, not compliance.

### 8.3 Counter-Rosetta is missing its harshest rival

§7C proposes 5-, 6-, 8-row and domain-native rivals. The real null is **no mapping at all**:
does translation beat reading both domains in their own terms? An 8-row rival still concedes
that rows are the right instrument. **The null does not, and it is the rival that could
actually kill the thing.**

### 8.4 §7F is listed last and should be first

§§1–6 and A–E are architecture. **F is the only item that could show the instrument works.**
A vNext that ships Core + Packs + Ledger and not F is a more disciplined instrument carrying
the same unmeasured claim — the estate's recurring pattern of building Φ and deferring V.

### 8.5 Convergence — the dependency scorer already has a twin

§7D's cultural-dependency genealogy is **structurally identical** to TheCircle's
source-independence problem: two sources syndicating one wire are not two sources.

Same question in both domains: **are these N observations, or one observation N times?**

Circle's `independence_chain_id` and `directness` fields are declared and unscored, and
building that scorer is the largest unblocked item on the commercial path. **One build serves
both:** the same enum shape, different edge types. Recommended as the first concrete vNext
step precisely because it is not only a Rosetta step.

### 8.6 A recurring failure mode, named

§6's ruling that `φν=1` cannot ground equal dignity is the same error class a council caught
in agent work on 2026-07-30: an η=0 conclusion derived from an aesthetic property of the
golden ratio. The rationale was struck and the distribution kept.

**Elegance mistaken for warrant.** Worth naming as a standing failure mode rather than two
isolated corrections, because it recurs and it is persuasive each time.
