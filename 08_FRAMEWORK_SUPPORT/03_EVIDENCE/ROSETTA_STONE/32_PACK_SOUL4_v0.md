# SOUL4@0 — the Soul-Loop Mission Engine as a Rosetta pack

> The Soul-Loop Mission Engine is a **downstream application** of the Soul Loop
> grammar, not a replacement for it. This pack records that application as a
> namespaced Rosetta cell. It is not the runtime; it is the audit document for
> the runtime.

---

## 1. The four organs

| Slot | Organ | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SOUL4@0:circle` | Circle | Epistemic interface — observation, model, evidence, and audit. The receipt of the world into a fallible model. | It is not a knowledge oracle. Collecting does not make information true. |
| `SOUL4@0:refu` | ReFu | Methodological possibility — alternatives, tests, generated options. The expansion of D5. | It is not feasibility. Generating a possibility does not make it feasible. |
| `SOUL4@0:agentz` | Agentz | Axiological deliberation — values, constraints, objections, proposals. The L4/L7 axis. | It is not signing authority. Evaluating values does not grant signing authority. |
| `SOUL4@0:skyzai` | Skyzai | Receipt-bound execution and memory — D4 means, attempted action, custody, and the actual commitment receipt. | It is not purpose, prediction, or decision. It executes and remembers; the loop returns the answer. |

The four organs are **concentrations of function**, not permanent owners of
entire philosophical faculties. They are roles in a repeated Soul Loop, not
the Soul Loop itself.

## 2. The four fences (each organ cannot claim too much)

| Fence | What it forbids |
|---|---|
| `F1` | Circle does not make information true merely by collecting it. |
| `F2` | ReFu does not make a possibility feasible merely by generating it. |
| `F3` | Agentz does not acquire signing authority merely by evaluating values. |
| `F4` | Skyzai does not manufacture the outcome merely by coordinating commitment. |

The fences are anti-usurpation. They stop one organ from promoting itself into
the role of another. They do not, by themselves, guarantee that the loop
actually closes — see §3.

## 3. The fifth fence (each organ must be able to receive from the next)

> *Each organ must be able to receive from the next, or the arrow is
> decorative.*

The original four fences constrain **overreach**. The 2026-07-30 incident in
the running system was the **opposite failure**: RealityFutures (ReFu) had no
way to acknowledge TheCircle (Circle) — no route, no schema, no storage — and
the disconnection went unnoticed for **months while every original fence
held**. Four organs in a row is not a loop.

**The fifth fence** is the closure check:

```text
for each organ O_i, there exists an organ O_{i+1} such that
   O_i can publish to O_{i+1}  AND
   O_{i+1} can acknowledge O_i.
```

**The measure is closure, not compliance.** A runtime that satisfies F1–F4
and violates F5 is not Rosetta-compliant; it is a row of organs.

## 4. Skyzai's slot — narrower than the vNext draft

The vNext draft (§7B, 2026-07-31) assigned Skyzai *"purpose, means,
coordination."* That breaches F4. Skyzai's own route card states it **"does
not observe, predict, or decide. It executes and remembers."** Purpose is
L1/teleology — upstream of all four organs.

**Skyzai's slot is narrower than the draft:** receipt-bound execution and
memory. Means-selection sits with Agentz (L4). Purpose sits above the loop
— which is exactly where §7B's own conclusion places it: with cosmology,
ontology, and horizon as a **cross-cutting contract**, not inside an organ.

## 5. The three cross-cutting contracts (not products)

These three are not additional organs. They are contracts that apply across
all four organs simultaneously.

| Contract | What it asserts | What it forbids |
|---|---|---|
| **Cosmology** | All four organs share a world model (or honestly disagree about one). | A private cosmology inside one organ. |
| **Ontology** | All four organs share a type schema for entities, bearers, and boundaries. | A private ontology inside one organ. |
| **Horizon** | A non-negotiable set of refusals and ultimate questions governs the whole loop. | An unquestionable purpose inside one organ. |

The user's intuition that the four products (Circle, ReFu, Agentz, Skyzai)
concentrate philosophical functions is **correct**, but only as a downstream
projection of the Soul Loop grammar. Cosmology, ontology, and horizon are
**not three missing products**; they are the cross-cutting governance of the
loop.

## 6. Skyzai as teleology AND as enactment

Skyzai has two distinct roles in the loop, and they must not be fused:

1. **Skyzai-as-teleology** — the *what end does the organism pursue* question,
   asked at L1/PHIL7. This is upstream.
2. **Skyzai-as-enactment** — D4 means, attempted action, custody, and the
   receipt-bound execution of the commitment. This is downstream.

Acting does not itself establish purpose. A loop's enactment is its execution
and memory; the purpose is what the loop orients toward and is asked of at
L1. The two are different claims, both useful, and they should not be
collapsed.

## 7. The Soul Loop grammar (upstream)

The Soul Loop is the **temporal grammar of corrigible agency**. It is not a
seven-stage realisation of `PHIL7@1`. All seven questions may be asked of every
loop phase. The `SOUL4@0` pack is one downstream application; the Soul Loop
itself is upstream of any pack.

```text
X_t (actual territory)
  → M_t (actual fallible model)
  → Ω_t (represented D5 alternatives)
  → U_t, G_t, V_t (values/authorisation, selection policy, actual means)
  → χ_t (selection)
  → q_t (commitment receipt)
  → K_t (independent world transition)
  → r_{t+1} (outcome receipt)
  → revision of M and G
```

Three gaps can never be collapsed:

- territory versus model;
- intention versus performed attempt;
- expectation versus observed consequence.

The operational "soul" is therefore not a substance. It is **corrigibility
maintained across these three asymmetries**.

## 8. The kill criterion for SOUL4@0

The pack is killed (or contracted) if:

- any of the five fences is structurally unenforceable in a candidate
  runtime;
- the four organs fail to close into a loop (F5 violation) for more than
  one cycle without an external exception;
- the cross-cutting contracts (cosmology, ontology, horizon) become
  internalised by an organ, merging the row with its governance;
- the upstream Soul Loop grammar is amended in a way that contradicts
  SOUL4@0's structural claims.

- `PHIL7@1:knowledge` is upstream of `SOUL4@0:circle`.
