# Balance of Houses — Trophic Cascade, Coadaptation, and Convergence

## 1. Canonical statement

> **The Rosetta rows represent mobile functional positions in a trophic-cascade
> model `[I]`: each row transforms typed inputs and changes the conditions under
> which other rows operate, so capture, starvation, removal, or overgrowth at
> one row can propagate through the whole dependency web. They do not represent
> seven biological trophic levels, hereditary human castes, species, ranks of
> dignity, or permissions to consume another row.**

This is a projection from ecological process into a cognitive and institutional
ecology. The biological source and the Rosetta target remain different objects.
The projection transfers a set of questions and predictions; it transfers no
matter, energy, causal proof, moral authority, or evidence tier.

## 2. What “following biological laws” means here

The phrase means that the analogy must respect empirically constrained biological
mechanisms before it adds philosophical interpretation. It does not mean every
ecological regularity is a universal exceptionless law, or that an institution
becomes an organism merely because both can be drawn as networks.

Four distinctions are load-bearing:

1. **Trophic cascade `[B]`.** A perturbation at one part of a food web can have
   indirect effects elsewhere in the web. A direct handoff between two nodes is
   not yet a cascade; the claim needs a path, lag, predicted effects and
   non-effects, observations, a boundary, and a counterfactual or rival.
2. **Biological coevolution `[B]`.** Interacting populations undergo reciprocal
   heritable evolutionary change across generations. One side adapting to a fixed
   backdrop is not coevolution.
3. **Convergent evolution `[B]`.** Focal trait states can evolve independently
   in distinct lineages under comparable constraints. Similarity,
   independence, and the constraint set must be evidenced. Shared ancestry is
   compatible with convergence; inheritance or copying of the **focal trait**
   defeats the claim that that trait originated independently.
4. **Normative convergence `[I/C]`.** Persons and institutions may intentionally
   learn toward a declared ideal. Biology does not supply or authorize that
   ideal. Its bearer, metric, costs, contest path, exit, revision rule, and
   authorization remain separately owned.

Within-life change by a person is ordinarily learning or development. Change in
an organization is ordinarily **coadaptation** or organizational learning.
Reserve *biological coevolution* for a claim with heritable population variation,
differential reproductive persistence, and reciprocal evolutionary change across
generations.

## 4. Individuals and functional castes

The Sanskrit varṇa and English *caste* labels remain historical-functional
analogies. In this contract a caste means a **functional guild expressed for a
bounded task and time**, never a biological essence.

An individual may:

- express zero, one, or several row functions at once;
- change functions across contexts and life stages;
- retrain and relinquish a role;
- refuse a role and retain Grace Exit;
- never acquire dignity, authority, entitlement, or hereditary status from a
  row assignment.

A valid role-expression record names the actor, function, scope, observation
window, capability evidence, consent, revocation and exit paths, and any
separately applicable authorization. There is no valid scalar `person.row`.

Individuals may coadapt through learning, imitation, contest, cooperation, and
division of labour. Functional guilds qualify as coadapted when a retained change
in one alters the selection environment of another and the second produces a
retained response. Calling that process biological coevolution requires the
additional biological warrants above.

## 6. Four typed research objects

### `ECO.WEB@1` — arbitrary-cardinality biological source

An ecological source graph contains time-bounded nodes and edges:

```yaml
nodes: [taxon, functional guild, detrital pool, abiotic input, ...]
edges:
  - source_ref: exact source or pool
    sink_ref: exact sink or recipient
    interaction_or_resource_kind: typed relation
    unit: physical or declared observational unit
    magnitude_and_uncertainty: measured value
    observation_window: bounded interval

```

Untyped “energy” is invalid. Matter, metabolic energy, information, attention,
labour, money, legitimacy, and authorization retain different units and bearers.
Ecological transfer efficiency `η_ratio` is a descriptive ratio; constitutional
extraction `η_move` is a bearer-and-authority judgment. Neither may substitute
for the other, and ordinary ecological loss is not automatically a relational
violation.

### `RS.CASCADE@1` — perturbation and indirect response

A cascade claim records the graph snapshot, perturbation, path, lags,
preregistered effects and non-effects, observations, counterfactual or control,
receipts, strongest rival, discriminator, and kill criterion. In the House graph,
at least three distinct nodes or control surfaces and one indirect response are
required before the word *cascade* is used.

### `RS.ADAPTATION@1` — mechanism-typed change

Every adaptation claim chooses one mechanism:

- `BIOLOGICAL_COEVOLUTION`;
- `ORGANIZATIONAL_COADAPTATION`;
- `BIOLOGICAL_CONVERGENCE`;
- `NORMATIVE_TARGET_CONVERGENCE`.

The mechanism determines the required evidence. Feedback alone cannot be relabeled
coevolution; similarity alone cannot be relabeled independent convergence.

### `RS.NORMATIVE_TARGET@1` — the separately authored ideal

A normative target records its author, scope, affected bearers, consent basis,
constraints, distance or dominance metric, costs and externalities, contest and
exit paths, revision trigger, expiry, and authorization. It always carries:

```yaml

preordained_evolutionary_destination: false
```

The “ideal” is normally an admissible region or revisable attractor, not a single
perfect point. A candidate approach is evidence only when a preregistered metric
moves toward that region across repeated, independently receipted cycles without
hiding bearer costs, coercion, or loss of exit.

## 7. Coevolution and convergence tests

### Reciprocal adaptation

For functions `A` and `B`, evidence must show successive retained responses:

```text
A_t changes the selection environment of B
B_(t+1) changes in a retained way
B_(t+1) changes the selection environment of A
A_(t+2) changes in a retained way
```

Same-window correlation, one-way adaptation, or two outputs from one controller
do not satisfy reciprocity.

### Biological convergence

Require at least two independence-chain identifiers, comparable constraint
descriptions, a preregistered trait-distance metric, and evidence that the focal
trait states arose independently rather than by inheritance or copying. Shared
deeper ancestry alone does not defeat convergence. `ideal_target_ref` must be
`null`.

### Normative-target convergence

Require an explicit `RS.NORMATIVE_TARGET@1`, independent outcome receipts,
cost/externality accounting, contestability, revocation, and exit. The result may
show institutional learning toward a chosen target; it is not evidence that
natural selection chose the target.

## 8. Discriminators and kill criteria

1. **Cascade discriminator.** Does the cascade model predict intervention effects
   and non-effects better than a plain dependency, diffusion, or queueing graph?
   If not, the simpler model wins and the trophic projection contracts.
2. **Specialization discriminator.** Do mobile Rosetta-specialized groups beat
   matched generalist or arbitrary-role groups on coverage, correction,
   coordination, and switching cost without greater coercion, hidden cost, or
   role lock-in? If not, withdraw the specialization claim.
3. **Coevolution discriminator.** Are there reciprocal retained changes across
   successive windows? If not, call the result one-way adaptation, feedback, or
   coadaptation-unproven.
4. **Convergence discriminator.** Do independent lineages approach a similar
   operational solution under comparable constraints without inheriting or
   copying the focal trait? If not, use common inheritance, copying, coincidence,
   or flexible interpretation as the rival.
5. **Ideal-convergence discriminator.** Do repeated cycles approach the declared
   target while preserving all affected bearers, correction, contest, and exit?
   If not, the target or learning rule is revised or rejected.

Any mapping is killed if it makes row order causal by itself, forces an ecological
graph to seven nodes, treats a person as a hereditary row, assigns political or
moral rank from biology, converts ecological predation into human authorization,
or claims that evolution guarantees the framework's ideal.

These sources warrant their biological findings. They do not warrant the Rosetta
projection, its seven seats, the Balance of Houses, or a normative ideal.
