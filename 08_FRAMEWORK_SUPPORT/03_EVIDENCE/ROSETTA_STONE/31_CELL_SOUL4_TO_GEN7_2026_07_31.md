## 1. The correction that came out of filing it

An earlier reading held that two independent surfaces — the sevenfold mirror in
`01_NOOSPHERE/CLAUDE.md` and the `soul_loop` in TheCircle's VMOSK-A — agreed on
Circle=L2, RF=L3, Agentz=L4, and that this constituted convergence.

**They are not independent.** TheCircle's VMOSK states the organ *is* "Skyzai's
**Epistemology** organ"; RealityFutures is defined through **Methodology**; Agentz through
**Axiology**. GEN7 places epistemology at L2, methodology at L3, axiology at L4.

**The organs were named after the rows.** The agreement is a definition being read back, not
a discovery. Two surfaces descending from one design decision are **one observation counted
twice** — the identical error the refinement note warns about for Indo-European correspondences.

`dependency_status: framework-derived`. This caps what the three FIT rows are worth,
and the cap is severe: they are near-tautological.

## 3. Why Skyzai's MULTIROW is the only row carrying information

Nobody designed Skyzai to straddle two rows. The corpus's sevenfold mirror places its
*execution hand* at **L4 alongside Agentz**, and assigns **L5 to Cortex** — Skyzai's memory
subsystem. That falls out of the organ's own definition: *"it executes and remembers."*
Execute is L4-adjacent; remember is L5.

**A mapping that disagrees with the tidy design is evidence in a way agreement is not.** The
three FIT rows tell us the corpus is internally consistent. The MULTIROW row tells us
something the design did not already assert — and forcing it to a single row would be exactly
the Procrustean normalisation the anti-normalisation rule forbids.
