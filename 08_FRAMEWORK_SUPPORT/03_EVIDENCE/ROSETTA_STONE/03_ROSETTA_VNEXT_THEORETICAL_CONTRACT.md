# Rosetta vNext — Namespaced Projection Registry and Research Contract

This document is the pure-method disposition of the dated vNext refinement
proposal. The proposal remains provenance at `[D]`; its application-specific
examples do not become premises, owners, or evidence here.

## 0. What survived the mystery

The old Rosetta appeared to work because many domains could be arranged into
the same seven visible rows. Its universal-seven interpretation failed: source
traditions have different native counts, several fits require selection or
normalization, and one interpreter can generate correlated correspondences.
That kill is already settled. It must remain visible.

What survived is more modest and more useful. Rosetta can function as a
**typed translation protocol** when it preserves relations, exposes losses,
and keeps source truth separate from the mapping. Three non-exclusive
hypotheses may explain why some projections feel unexpectedly generative:

1. **Relational recurrence `[I]`:** distinct domains may share local patterns
   such as boundary/interior, selection, inversion, mediation, or feedback.
2. **Interface compression `[I]`:** a small, memorable question set may help a
   finite reasoner inspect a complex system without claiming to exhaust it.
3. **Interpreter selection `[C]`:** the apparent fit may be produced by chosen
   examples, flexible coding, inherited categories, or aesthetic preference.

Rosetta vNext does not choose among these explanations by elegance. It records
their different predictions and lets discriminating observations decide.

## 1. Core, packs, projections, and applications

The Rosetta is not one giant table. It is a registry governed by one small
core, but the registry contains two different object types:

- a **pack** owns a native vocabulary and source snapshot;
- a **projection** makes one directional, revisable claim between packs.

Changing a mapping must not silently change either vocabulary. The split is
load-bearing.

| Identifier | Object type | Native object or function | Native cardinality | Standing |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| `RS.CORE@1` | core contract | partial-map and non-transfer laws | not a row set | `[S]` method inside this corpus |
| `PHIL7@1` | pack | seven selected philosophical questions | 7 | `[B]` names; `[I]` selection and order |
| `G7@1` | pack | four named D5 moves plus three named Titan-frame projections | 7 by declared construction | source-owner tier; not universal |
| `GEN7@1` | pack | selected generative interface seats | 7 | `[I]` construction and cross-domain uses |
| `S2BOUNDARY@1` | pack | the reciprocal chart's two distinct pole markers | 2 | `[A]` chart boundary distinction; no person or stage semantics |
| `REP6@1` | pack | six replicator layers | 6 | `[S]` source facts where cited; `[I/C]` ordering hypothesis |
| `SOUL@1` | pack | typed Soul Loop feedback graph | 11 named nodes | `[S]` typed contract; `[I/C]` interpretation and effects |
| `RS.REGIME@1` | analysis contract | dynamic capture analysis over declared control surfaces | declared per analyzed system | `[I]` diagnostic; political claims need independent evidence |
| `RS.APPLICATION@1` | return contract | generic downstream experiment and feedback envelope | not a row set | `[S]` separation protocol; outcomes require receipts |
| `ECO.WEB@1` | source-graph contract | time-bounded ecological nodes and typed interaction or resource-flow edges | source-native and arbitrary; never forced to 7 | `[B]` only where externally measured and cited |
| `RS.CASCADE@1` | analysis contract | perturbation path, indirect responses, lags, controls, receipts, rival, and kill criterion | graph-relative; not a row set | `[S]` record type; each cascade claim keeps its source tier |
| `RS.ADAPTATION@1` | mechanism contract | biological coevolution, organizational coadaptation, biological convergence, or normative-target convergence | not a row set | `[S]` type split; uses remain `[B/I/C]` |
| `RS.NORMATIVE_TARGET@1` | target contract | separately authored, contestable and revisable institutional ideal | not supplied by biology | `[I/C]`; transfers no biological warrant or authority |

`SOUL@1` uses the native keys `state`, `possibilities`, `model`, `means`,
`authorization`, `policy`, `selector`, `commitment_receipt`, `environment`,
`outcome_receipt`, and `revision`. A downstream application may group these
into fewer operational phases, but it must record that normalization.

`G7@1` uses semantic keys rather than borrowed L-seats:
`kali_take_phi`, `kali_take_v`, `krishna_give_v`, `arjuna_give_phi`,
`brahma_create`, `shiva_dissolve`, and `vishnu_preserve`. A projection may map
`GEN7@1:L4` to `G7@1:arjuna_give_phi`; the two terms do not become one object.

The catalogue is extensible. A new pack is admissible only when it names an
owner, native vocabulary, native count, and reproducible source snapshot. A new
projection separately names its normalization, invariant, losses,
dependencies, rival, discriminator, and kill criterion. Reusing the numeral
seven is never sufficient.

The ecology-facing objects are specified by the
[Balance of Houses contract](04_BALANCE_OF_HOUSES_TROPHIC_COEVOLUTION_CONTRACT.md).
They preserve three separations. First, a biological food web keeps its native
node count and cannot be normalized into `GEN7@1`. Second, the claim that a
perturbation cascades through a row or House graph depends on typed interfaces
and observations, not row order. Third, biological convergence and intentional
movement toward a normative target are different mechanisms; biology neither
authors nor authorizes the target. A person may express several mobile functions
but may never be assigned a biological or hereditary Rosetta caste.

Repository route-card fields such as `primary_level: L3` belong to the agent
dispatch grammar, not automatically to `G7@1`, `GEN7@1`, or `PHIL7@1`. They
carry routing authority only at the scope declared by the applicable
`AGENTS.md`; they neither evidence nor redefine a theoretical projection.

## 2. Stable reference grammar

Every native term reference has the form:

```text
pack_id@version:term_key
```

Examples:

```text
PHIL7@1:purpose
G7@1:arjuna_give_phi
GEN7@1:L4
S2BOUNDARY@1:zero_pole
REP6@1:phenotype
SOUL@1:commitment_receipt
```

The rules are strict:

- Bare `L4` is valid only as local shorthand inside a document that has
  declared one active pack. It is invalid in a cross-pack claim.
- Equal or related labels do not imply identity. `G7@1:arjuna_give_phi` and
  `GEN7@1:L4` are distinct objects even when a projection relates them.
- A version is immutable. A semantic change creates a new version and a
  supersession edge; it does not rewrite the old meaning.
- A term key is native to its pack. Projecting `phenotype` to a target `L4`
  does not rename the biological source term.
- A projection is directional. Its inverse is a new audited claim.

This grammar removes the most persistent source of false coherence: one bare
`Lx` silently carrying several incompatible senses.

## 3. Pack and projection contracts

### 3.1 Pack manifest

A pack declares native vocabulary and source custody only:

```yaml
schema_version: rs.pack_manifest.v2
pack_id: PHIL7
version: 1
owner_ref: path or named source owner
native_domain: philosophical-question index
native_terms: [purpose, knowledge, inference, value, world, being, horizon]
native_term_definitions:
  - term_key: purpose
    label: Teleology
    description: What end or purpose is being proposed?
native_cardinality: 7
source_version: immutable version or dated snapshot
digest_algorithm: sha256
source_exact_digest: lowercase digest of the exact source bytes
native_expression_digest_algorithm: sha256
native_expression_digest_canonicalization: rs.native_term_definitions.v1
native_expression_digest: lowercase digest of ordered key-label-description records
semantic_digest_status: PROVISIONAL
semantic_canonicalization_id: null
semantic_canonicalization_version: null
digest_normalization_operations: []
lifecycle_status: ACTIVE
```

`source_exact_digest` is required and is SHA-256 over the exact source byte
stream. A semantic digest is provisional until a named, versioned canonical
serialization exists. Mapping operations such as grouping or reordering are
never digest normalization. `rs.pack_manifest.v2` adds the ordered native term
key, label, and description records because a key-only list cannot bind semantic
distinctions. Their `rs.native_term_definitions.v1` digest is part of every pack-
model digest. Neither digest grants truth or authority. PackManifest v1 remains
historical/read-only; new active records use v2.

### 3.2 Projection manifest

A projection owns the directional interpretation between pack snapshots. A
pack reference alone is an address, not a lock. Projection v2 therefore binds
the source **and every target** to the exact PackManifest model and source
snapshot that were interpreted:

```yaml
schema_version: rs.projection_manifest.v2
projection_id: PHIL7_TO_SOUL
projection_revision: 1
parent_revision: null
supersedes_ref: null
source_pack_ref: PHIL7@1
source_pack_binding:
  binding_schema: rs.pack_snapshot_binding.v1
  pack_ref: PHIL7@1
  owner_ref: controlling source owner
  source_version: immutable version or dated snapshot
  source_exact_digest: lowercase SHA-256 of exact source bytes
  pack_manifest_digest_algorithm: sha256
  pack_manifest_digest_canonicalization: rs.pack_manifest_digest.v1
  pack_manifest_digest: lowercase SHA-256 of the canonical PackManifest model
target_pack_refs: [SOUL@1]
target_pack_bindings:
  - binding_schema: rs.pack_snapshot_binding.v1
    pack_ref: SOUL@1
    owner_ref: controlling source owner
    source_version: immutable version or dated snapshot
    source_exact_digest: lowercase SHA-256 of exact source bytes
    pack_manifest_digest_algorithm: sha256
    pack_manifest_digest_canonicalization: rs.pack_manifest_digest.v1
    pack_manifest_digest: lowercase SHA-256 of the canonical PackManifest model
normalization_operations: []
proposed_invariant: relation the projection claims to preserve
discarded_information: distinctions the projection removes
dependency_refs:
  - binding_schema: rs.dependency_binding.v1
    kind: CONTRACT
    ref: RS.CORE@1
    binding_status: BOUND
    owner_ref: exact source owner or path
    version: immutable dependency version
    digest_algorithm: sha256
    digest_canonicalization: exact-bytes or named canonical model
    digest: lowercase SHA-256
    deferred_reason: null
strongest_rival: alternative map or null model
discriminator: observation that separates them
kill_criterion: condition that kills or contracts the claim
round_trip_test: what must survive a return projection
claim_digest_algorithm: sha256
claim_digest_canonicalization: rs.projection_claim_digest.v1
claim_semantic_digest: digest of bound packs plus mapping semantics
resolution_status: UNDERDETERMINED
resolution_dispositions: []
predecessor_comparisons: []
rejected_claim_digests: []
lifecycle_status: ACTIVE
```

`rs.pack_manifest_digest.v1` is canonical JSON over the native model fields:
schema version, pack ID and version, owner, native domain, ordered native term
keys and their stable labels and descriptions, native-expression digest,
native cardinality, source version, exact-source digest declaration, and
semantic-digest declaration. It excludes only mutable presentation,
availability, runtime health, and lifecycle metadata. A string saying that a pack is “available,”
“current,” or “loaded” can never substitute for this binding. Each binding
repeats the owner, source version, and exact source digest so it remains
inspectable even when the registry is unavailable.

`rs.projection_claim_digest.v1` fingerprints the two-sided pack bindings and
the directional mapping semantics, including normalization, cells, invariant,
loss, dependencies, rival, discriminator, kill criterion, and round-trip test.
It excludes mutable application statuses. Reintroducing the same fingerprint
under a new ID is therefore the same rejected claim, not a fresh hypothesis.

Every `dependency_refs` entry is an `rs.dependency_binding.v1`, not a
`kind + ref` availability assertion. `BOUND` requires owner, immutable version,
SHA-256 algorithm, named canonicalization, and digest. A pack dependency may
use `kind: PACK` and reuse the owner, source version, and
`rs.pack_manifest_digest.v1` values from `rs.pack_snapshot_binding.v1`; a
projection dependency binds its `rs.projection_claim_digest.v1`. When custody
is genuinely unavailable the entry is `DEFERRED`: all binding fields are null
and `deferred_reason` is non-empty. A projection with a deferred dependency
cannot be `RESOLVED`.

`resolution_status` is a run declaration, not its own warrant. Changing only
that field to `RESOLVED` resolves nothing. A resolved projection requires a
separate, append-only `rs.resolution_disposition.v1` that binds the exact
projection ref, claim digest, and non-circular full audit-state digest; declares
the limited resolution scopes and explicit limitations; names the accountable
decision authority, decision record, custody, and trust boundary; cites
non-empty discriminator and evidence result refs; retains the complete lineage;
and carries an independent external verifier attestation. The disposition must
set `world_efficacy_claim: false`: theoretical resolution of a scoped audit
question is not evidence that an intervention works in the world.

Normalization operations are an ordered list, not a footnote. Examples include
`group`, `split`, `insert_external_ground`, `drop`, `reorder`, `translate`, and
`choose_representative`. Every operation names what information it destroys.
The projection revision changes when any mapping semantics change; the pack
version changes only when its native vocabulary or source snapshot changes.
Any changed source or target binding starts a new projection revision or
branch. It may not be silently accepted as the object previously tested.

## 4. Cell contract and orthogonal statuses

A projection cell is a claim about a relation, not a synonym. Its stable ID is
the source reference, target reference, and projection revision together. It
records:

- its owning `projection_id@revision` plus fully namespaced source and target
  term references;
- relation type: `ONE_TO_ONE`, `ONE_TO_MANY`, `MANY_TO_ONE`, `ANALOGY`,
  `INVERSION`, `BOUNDARY`, `UNMAPPED_SOURCE`, `UNFILLED_TARGET`, or `EXTRA`;
- proposed invariant and discarded information;
- source basis, independence, dependencies, scale, and time horizon;
- strongest rival, discriminator, kill criterion, and downgrade path;
- receipt references, when a use or outcome is claimed.

Five semantic status families must remain separate. They use six recorded
fields because the mapping family separates warrant (`mapping_tier`) from
disposition (`fit_status`). Authorization is not a use state; this keeps an
unauthorized but causally real attempt representable:

| Axis | Examples | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| source fact | `[A]`, `[B]`, `[S]`, `[I]`, `[C]`, `[D]` | which kind of warrant supports the native-domain statement |
| mapping | `mapping_tier` from the same vocabulary plus `FIT`, `PARTIAL`, `MULTIROW`, `UNMAPPED_SOURCE`, `UNFILLED_TARGET`, `EXTRA`, `DISPUTED`, `KILLED`, `ARCHIVED` | which kind of warrant supports the mapping and how the proposed correspondence stands |
| authorization | `NOT_REQUESTED`, `PENDING`, `AUTHORIZED`, `REFUSED`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`, `INVALID`, `ABSENT`, `NOT_REQUIRED` | what authority disposition applies independently of causal action |
| use | `UNUSED`, `PROPOSED`, `ATTEMPTED`, `ABORTED` | what was proposed or causally attempted |
| outcome | `NOT_OBSERVED`, `OBSERVED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `REPLICATED`, `NON_INFORMATIVE` | what the world receipt supports |

`AUTHORIZED` is not a mapping or use tier. `ATTEMPTED` does not imply
authorization or outcome. An unauthorized attempt is recorded as
`authorization_status: ABSENT|INVALID|REFUSED` plus `use_status: ATTEMPTED` and
may still have externally observed consequences. A successful outcome is not
proof of the Rosetta interpretation without a discriminator and a rival.

`[A]`, `[B]`, `[S]`, `[I]`, `[C]`, and `[D]` are a vocabulary of warrant
**kinds**, not a total strength order. List position is serialization only;
there is no generic rule such as `[A] > [B] > [S] > [I] > [C] > [D]`, and a
mapping kind is not computed by comparing it with a fact kind. Each axis must
name the kind appropriate to its own basis. Every changed `fact_tier` or
`mapping_tier` requires a receipt-backed transition regardless of which symbol
comes before the other in a displayed list, as well as the external verifier
attestation required for every status transition.

Every recorded status transition carries a typed
`rs.external_verifier_attestation.v1` made outside the actor whose status is
being changed. It names the verifier and its evidence authority, independence
basis, subject and digest, verification method, issue time, custody, and
signature or attestation. It repeats the status axis and old/new states and
binds the canonical transition subject, so it cannot be detached and replayed
against another transition:

```yaml
axis: authorization_status
from: PENDING
to: AUTHORIZED
recorded_at: immutable timestamp
reason: bounded reason
receipt_refs: []
external_verifier_attestation:
  schema_version: rs.external_verifier_attestation.v1
  attestation_ref: stable external reference
  verifier_ref: verifier identity
  verifier_authority_ref: evidence authority
  independence_basis: why verifier is outside the actor and effective controller
  subject_ref: owning cell or application claim
  status_axis: authorization_status
  from_status: PENDING
  to_status: AUTHORIZED
  subject_digest_algorithm: sha256
  subject_digest_canonicalization: rs.status_transition_subject.v1
  subject_digest: lowercase SHA-256
  verification_method_ref: versioned method
  issued_at: immutable timestamp
  custody_ref: external custody chain
  signature_or_attestation_ref: inspectable signature or attestation
```

`rs.status_transition_subject.v1` is canonical JSON over schema version,
subject ref, axis, old state, new state, record time, reason, and ordered
receipt refs. A receipt reference or an application self-report is not an
external attestation.

An authority-bearing `authorization_status: AUTHORIZED` additionally binds a
complete `rs.authorization_envelope_ref.v1`: stable envelope reference and
digest, principal, mandate, scope, consent, custody, validity start, expiry or
revocation control and last check, contest path, actor, and every known
consequence bearer. Missing or stale fields yield `PENDING`, `INVALID`,
`EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`, or `ABSENT`; they cannot be inferred from an attempted or
successful act. `NOT_REQUIRED` itself needs an externally attested rule showing
why no mandate is required.

The complete authority reference is:

```yaml
schema_version: rs.authorization_envelope_ref.v1
envelope_ref: stable external reference
envelope_digest_algorithm: sha256
envelope_digest: lowercase SHA-256
principal_ref: accountable principal
mandate_ref: exact mandate
scope: bounded action and consequence scope
consent_ref: inspectable consent or lawful basis
custody_ref: envelope custody
valid_from: immutable timestamp
expires_at: timestamp or null
revocation_ref: registry or revocation record, or null
revocation_checked_at: immutable last check
contest_path_ref: usable contest path
actor_ref: proposed or actual actor
consequence_bearer_refs: []
```

At least one of `expires_at` or `revocation_ref` is present. A verifier attests
the envelope state; it does not become the principal or manufacture consent.

## 5. Cardinality, composition, and round trips

Native cardinality is evidence about the source representation, not a nuisance
to hide. `REP6_TO_GEN7@1` keeps `REP6@1` at six biological terms when projecting
into seven `GEN7@1` target seats. The unmatched target seat is recorded as
`UNFILLED_TARGET` or a boundary on the
target side; it does not become a seventh source term. If a projection inserts
an external context term, that term must itself have a named source pack and
reference. An eight-part source may likewise produce an `EXTRA` term rather
than a forced merge. A partial map is often the honest result.

Projection composition is not automatically transitive:

```text
A --rho_1--> B --rho_2--> C
```

does not license `A --rho_3--> C`. `rho_3` is a fresh projection with its own
source snapshot, invariant, normalization, dependency graph, rivals, losses,
and tests. Otherwise error and interpretation disappear inside the composition.

Likewise, a round trip is not an identity proof. It reports a loss vector:

```text
loss = source distinctions not recoverable after A -> B -> A
```

Loss may be lexical, ordinal, causal, temporal, normative, geometric, or
authority-bearing. A low-loss round trip establishes only the declared
representational property.

## 6. The Soul Loop adapter is many-to-many

`SOUL@1` is a feedback graph, not another seven-rung ladder. The philosophical
questions can interrogate every part of it:

| Question pack key | Especially visible in `SOUL@1` | Non-exclusive question |
|---|---|---|
| `PHIL7@1:purpose` | possibilities, policy, selector, revision | which proposed end shapes selection, and should it persist? |
| `PHIL7@1:knowledge` | model, commitment receipt, outcome receipt | what is known, predicted, attempted, and independently observed? |
| `PHIL7@1:inference` | model, policy, selector, revision | by what rule are options ranked and the model updated? |
| `PHIL7@1:value` | authorization, policy, commitment receipt | what matters, to whom, under which exposure and contest path? |
| `PHIL7@1:world` | state, means, environment, outcome receipt | what actual causal field constrains the attempt? |
| `PHIL7@1:being` | all typed boundaries | which entities and relations are being posited or confused? |
| `PHIL7@1:horizon` | purpose, nonclosure, revision | what limit, ultimate commitment, or refusal frames the loop? |

This table is an adapter, not seven identities. Teleology does not belong to
only one phase, epistemology to only one observer, methodology to only one
modeler, or axiology to only one selector. Those can be useful **primary
emphases** in an application, but every phase remains answerable to all seven
questions. Any one-to-one allocation must survive comparison with this
many-to-many rival.

The commitment/outcome split is load-bearing. A selector issues a commitment
receipt; the environment issues the outcome receipt. The same provenance or
evidence authority may not author both and call the loop validated. One
transport component may carry both records only when their distinct issuers,
signatures or attestations, custody, and verification remain inspectable.

Every commitment and outcome receipt is therefore paired with a typed
provenance record naming its issuer, evidence authority, custody, digest, and
signature or attestation. The commitment issuer set and commitment evidence-
authority set must each be disjoint from their outcome counterparts for the
same application claim. Distinct filenames, hashes, transport routes, or
references from one issuer or aliased authority do not establish independence.

```yaml
commitment_provenance:
  schema_version: rs.commitment_provenance.v1
  receipt_ref: stable commitment receipt
  issuer_ref: commitment issuer
  evidence_authority_ref: commitment evidence authority
  custody_ref: commitment custody chain
  record_digest: lowercase SHA-256
  signature_or_attestation_ref: inspectable signature or attestation
outcome_provenance:
  schema_version: rs.outcome_provenance.v1
  receipt_ref: stable outcome receipt
  issuer_ref: outcome issuer
  evidence_authority_ref: outcome evidence authority
  custody_ref: outcome custody chain
  observation_ref: independently identified observation
  observation_digest: lowercase SHA-256
  method_ref: versioned observation method
  independence_basis: why this observation is independent
  signature_or_attestation_ref: inspectable signature or attestation
```

`REPLICATED` requires at least two outcome provenance records with independently
identified issuers, evidence authorities, observations, and custody chains,
plus a stated independence basis. Two references to one observation, two
signatures controlled by one evidence authority, or a copied receipt can support
custody redundancy but cannot support replication.

### Regimes emerge as control distributions, not row destinies

The political regime names in `GEN7@1` are historical-social analogies. No row
causes tyranny, democracy, oligarchy, timocracy, aristocracy, anarchy, or
theocracy. A serious regime analysis instead uses `RS.REGIME@1` to inspect who
controls each declared surface: evidence, modeled possibilities, inference,
valuation, authorization, means, execution, outcome receipts, correction, and
exit.

For every surface `RS.REGIME@1` records a typed control record:

```yaml
schema_ref: RS.REGIME@1
surface_ref: exact analyzed control surface
observation_window:
  start: bounded start
  end: bounded end
  inclusion_rule: observations admitted to the record
controller_refs: []
controller_plurality:
  count: integer
  inclusion_rule: how effective controllers and aliases were counted
concentration_measure:
  metric: named metric
  denominator: declared opportunity or control set
  value: observed value
  uncertainty: uncertainty or limitation
alias_coalitions: []
contestability:
  eligible_challengers: []
  path_ref: exact challenge path
  cost: observed or bounded cost
  latency: observed or bounded latency
  observed_disposition: result or not yet observed
revocability:
  revoker_refs: []
  scope: revocable surface
  latency: observed or bounded latency
  test_evidence_refs: []
  observed_effectiveness: result or not yet observed
dependency_refs: []
receipt_independence:
  commitment_issuer_refs: []
  outcome_issuer_refs: []
  evidence_authority_refs: []
  custody_refs: []
  verifier_refs: []
observed_consequences: []
longitudinal_capture_signatures: []
political_label: null
```

A **capture signature** appears only from longitudinal evidence that one
function, alias coalition, or effective controller repeatedly concentrates
several surfaces and can suppress adverse receipts, contest, revocation, or
correction. Point-in-time plurality is insufficient when nominally separate
controllers share a signer, beneficial owner, evidence authority, custody
provider, mandate, or failure domain. A healthier signature keeps functions
distinguishable, makes outcome evidence independent of the actor, and preserves
effective correction, revocation, contest, and exit.

This is a dynamic diagnostic, not a deterministic political label, moral label,
or typology of persons. `RS.REGIME@1` never computes a named regime from a row,
score, threshold, or capture signature. A named political regime requires its
own historical and institutional evidence and an explicitly separate claim.
The discriminator is whether the declared control distribution predicts
observable suppression, correction, error persistence, revocation, contest, or
exit better than a simpler institutional account.

## 7. Recursive disambiguation

Disambiguation is a bounded research process, not infinite self-approval:

```text
snapshot -> detect ambiguity -> generate rival parses -> select discriminator
         -> test or defer -> append revision -> compare residual ambiguity
```

Each run records:

1. immutable source and target pack bindings, including PackManifest-model and
   exact-source digests, plus projection revision and claim digest;
2. the exact ambiguity class: namespace, type, count, order, scale, causality,
   normativity, authority, provenance, or outcome;
3. at least one rival interpretation, including a null map where meaningful;
4. the discriminator and evidence required;
5. changes, surviving ambiguity, and newly introduced loss;
6. parent run, recursion depth, budget, and termination status;
7. a typed comparison against **every** predecessor in the lineage, recording
   predecessor reference and claim digest, `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED`, reason, and
   external-verifier attestation;
8. the monotonic set of rejected claim digests inherited from all predecessors.

Permitted terminal states are:

```text
RESOLVED
UNDERDETERMINED
DEFERRED
KILLED
CYCLE_BLOCKED
BUDGET_EXHAUSTED
AUTHORITY_REQUIRED
SOURCE_UNVERIFIABLE
```

The run graph must be acyclic. A child may not modify an ancestor, erase a
failed interpretation, or silently retest a changed source. Optimistic locking
requires every expected source and target PackManifest binding to match before a
revision is accepted. Changed inputs start a new branch.

`RESOLVED` is permitted only when every predecessor comparison in the complete
ancestral path is present and `ACCEPTED`. A rejected intermediary remains a
rejected fact about that lineage. Removing it from a list and re-adding the same
claim under another ID, revision, order, or transport does not heal it: the
stable `claim_semantic_digest` inherits the rejection. A renewed claim must
change its semantics or evidence, receive a new digest, retain the rejected
predecessor, and state the discriminator that justifies reconsideration.
Skipped comparisons, missing ancestors, a non-monotonic rejection set, or a
reintroduced rejected digest force `UNDERDETERMINED` or `KILLED`, never
`RESOLVED`.

Even a complete accepted lineage is necessary but insufficient. `RESOLVED`
also requires the last entry in the append-only disposition history to match
the current projection:

```yaml
schema_version: rs.resolution_disposition.v1
disposition_ref: stable disposition record
predecessor_disposition_ref: prior disposition ref or null
projection_ref: exact projection_id@revision
claim_semantic_digest: exact rs.projection_claim_digest.v1
audit_state_digest_algorithm: sha256
audit_state_digest_canonicalization: rs.projection_resolution_audit_state.v1
audit_state_digest: digest of the full manifest audit state and prior dispositions
from_status: a non-RESOLVED terminal state
to_status: RESOLVED
lineage_projection_refs: [root projection, ..., current projection]
lineage_comparison_attestation_refs: [one external attestation per lineage edge]
resolution_scopes: [SCHEMA_CONFORMANCE]
scope_limitations: [what remains unresolved or outside the disposition]
world_efficacy_claim: false
disposition_authority_ref: accountable decision authority
decision_ref: externally inspectable decision record
decision_custody_ref: custody outside the projection assertion
trust_boundary_ref: declared separation of decision, verification, and custody
decided_at: immutable timestamp
reason: scoped reason for disposition
discriminator_result_refs: [nonempty discriminator receipts]
evidence_result_refs: [nonempty evidence receipts]
external_verifier_attestation:
  schema_version: rs.external_verifier_attestation.v1
  subject_ref: projection_ref#resolution-disposition/disposition_ref
  status_axis: resolution_disposition
  from_status: same as disposition from_status
  to_status: RESOLVED
  subject_digest_algorithm: sha256
  subject_digest_canonicalization: rs.resolution_disposition_subject.v1
  subject_digest: digest of every disposition field except this attestation
  # plus verifier, independent evidence authority, independence basis, method,
  # issue time, external custody, attestation ref, and signature/attestation ref
```

The permitted `resolution_scopes` are `SCHEMA_CONFORMANCE`,
`SOURCE_AND_CUSTODY`, `MAPPING_DISAMBIGUATION`, `APPLICATION_EVIDENCE`, and
`REGIME_CONTROL_ANALYSIS`. They may be combined, but the limitations list may
not be empty. The audit-state canonicalization includes the complete projection
manifest except the circular `resolution_status` declaration and current
`resolution_dispositions` list, then adds every prior disposition verbatim.
Thus the current disposition cannot hash itself, while history remains
append-only and any other audited-field change invalidates its digest.

The verifier identity, verifier evidence authority, and verifier custody must
not overlap the disposition authority, decision custody, or source/target owner
custody. The decision ref and attestation ref are distinct. A current
disposition hidden behind a non-resolved status is invalid, and a resolved
status without a matching current disposition is invalid. These constraints
separate proposal, accountable disposition, and external verification; none
alone constitutes world truth.

Every comparison attestation named by `lineage_comparison_attestation_refs`
must also be recovered as the exact comparison object for that ancestor edge,
its canonical subject and digest must be recomputed, and the external trust
boundary must validate it. A list of ancestor attestation references is not
lineage evidence and cannot qualify `RESOLVED`.

The corpus checker can validate this type, digest binding, lineage, and declared
separation. It cannot manufacture trust in a signature or external custodian.
A consumer may treat the disposition as trusted only after a verifier outside
the candidate projection has validated the attestation against the named trust
boundary and verification method. A caller-provided attestation object is not,
by itself, that validation.

```yaml
comparison_schema: rs.predecessor_comparison.v1
predecessor_ref: projection_id@revision
predecessor_claim_digest: bound rs.projection_claim_digest.v1
comparison_status: ACCEPTED or REJECTED
comparison_reason: discriminating reason
external_verifier_attestation:
  schema_version: rs.external_verifier_attestation.v1
  subject_ref: current_projection_ref::predecessor_ref
  status_axis: predecessor_comparison
  from_status: UNREVIEWED
  to_status: ACCEPTED or REJECTED
  subject_digest_algorithm: sha256
  subject_digest_canonicalization: rs.predecessor_comparison_subject.v1
  subject_digest: lowercase SHA-256
  # plus verifier, authority, independence, method, issue time, custody,
  # attestation ref, and signature_or_attestation_ref from the full type above
```

The predecessor subject digest binds the current/predecessor pair, predecessor
claim digest, result, and reason. A later revision may add evidence, but it may
not rewrite an earlier comparison or shrink the inherited rejection set.

Recursion can improve explicitness and internal consistency. It cannot create
independent evidence by repeatedly inspecting its own output. AI or reviewer
agreement is search signal until a discriminating receipt exists.

## 8. Generic application and return contract

The theoretical owner and any downstream implementation operate at two speeds:

```text
inner loop: application observes -> proposes -> authorizes or refuses
            -> attempts -> receives world outcome -> revises its local mirror

outer loop: application packages anomaly -> theory owner audits provenance,
            rival, discriminator, and replication -> accepts, defers, or kills
            a theory change proposal
```

The inner loop may update its local projection registry continuously. It may
not mutate this corpus, promote a mapping, or manufacture an outcome receipt.
The outer loop changes theory only through ordinary corpus review and source
ownership.

`RS.APPLICATION@1` carries:

```yaml
theory_ref: pack_id@version:term_key
projection_claim_id: stable application-side identifier
application_snapshot_digest: immutable tested state
experiment_protocol_ref: preregistered procedure or test
authorization_status: status independent of proposal or attempt
authorization_envelope_ref: complete typed external envelope when authority is claimed
status_transitions: typed external-verifier attestations for every recorded change
commitment_receipt_ref: what was proposed, authorized, refused, or attempted
commitment_provenance_records: typed issuer, evidence-authority, custody, digest, signature
outcome_receipt_ref: independently produced observation, if any
outcome_provenance_records: typed issuer, evidence-authority, observation, custody, method, independence
application_observation: result and residuals
strongest_rival: competing explanation
discriminator_result: pass, fail, or underdetermined
theory_change_proposal: add, split, merge, downgrade, kill, or no-change
proposer: accountable source of the proposal
review_status: proposed, checked, disputed, accepted, deferred, or killed
```

The referenced `AuthorizationEnvelope` must name principal, mandate, scope,
consent, custody, validity start, expiry or revocation control and check,
contest path, actor, and consequence bearers. A commitment receipt may carry
that envelope, but a bare `AUTHORIZED` label never substitutes for it. The
external verifier attests the envelope and status transition; it does not
become the principal or grant the mandate. An unauthorized attempt remains
recordable and observable under a non-authorized disposition.

An application receipt may justify improving the application. A replicated,
well-discriminated family of independently provenanced outcome records may
motivate a theory proposal. Multiple references without distinct issuers,
evidence authorities, observations, and custody do not establish replication.
Neither route makes an application an owner of Emergentist truth.

## 9. Research hypotheses and kill criteria

Rosetta vNext makes testable method claims:

| Hypothesis `[C]` | Discriminator | Failure response |
|---|---|---|
| namespaced references reduce cross-pack equivocation | blinded audits find fewer wrong-row interpretations than bare `Lx` controls | simplify or replace the identifier grammar |
| explicit native counts and losses expose forced fits | independent coders identify hidden normalization earlier than with prose-only tables | revise required fields and coding guide |
| rivals and preregistered discriminators reduce flexible rescue | held-out mappings are killed or retained more consistently across raters | contract the audit claim |
| recursive disambiguation lowers unresolved ambiguity without tier inflation | successive bounded runs reduce a declared ambiguity metric while evidence-kind changes and other receipt-required status changes remain receipt-backed | cap recursion or retire the process for that scope |
| many-to-many Soul Loop interrogation outperforms fixed one-to-one ownership | it finds more preregistered omissions without unacceptable false positives | retain only the clearer adapter |
| two-sided PackManifest bindings prevent source/target substitution | changed target snapshots fail optimistic locking even when their IDs or availability labels remain unchanged | revise or retire the binding contract |
| typed verifier and evidence provenance prevent self-certifying status inflation | adversarial tests cannot promote authorization, outcome, or replication with actor-issued or aliased evidence | block application-derived promotion |

The vNext architecture is killed or substantially revised for a scope if:

- namespacing adds ceremony without reducing observed ambiguity;
- flexible normalization can still rescue every mapping;
- revisions erase failed mappings or cannot reproduce their source snapshots;
- a projection can substitute either source or target snapshot without changing
  its bound model and source digests;
- recursion loops, self-promotes, or converges only because rivals are excluded;
- a rejected claim can become resolved by remove/re-add, aliasing, or skipping a
  predecessor comparison;
- one provenance authority can fabricate both commitment and outcome evidence;
- two receipts under aliased issuers or one custody chain can be called
  replication;
- independent raters cannot apply the schema with useful agreement;
- a simpler rival protocol yields equal clarity, lower loss, and better
  out-of-sample discrimination.
