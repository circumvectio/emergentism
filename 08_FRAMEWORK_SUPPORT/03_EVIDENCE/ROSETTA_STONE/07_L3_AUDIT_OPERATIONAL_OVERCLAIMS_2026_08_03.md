# L3 Audit — operational layer vs doctrine registers

> with the Emergentist doctrine on the load-bearing points (Rosetta-as-method,
> no ontology transfer, demon/god-as-move-direction, Justice-as-chosen). Four
> **DRIFT** findings remain — all of them **headline overclaims** where a strong

## Finding 1 — DRIFT: "thermodynamic certainty" headline overclaims (D5)

**Where (verbatim):**
- `syntropic-dyadism.md:77` — *"it is a thermodynamic certainty."*
- `AGENTS.md:169` — *"thermodynamically defeated"* (η=0 row, biological-law column)
- `virtue-vice-biology-ethics.md:33` — *"vice is not only wrong; it is **thermodynamically stupid**"*

**Why DRIFT, not ALIGNED:** Lindeman's 10% rule is real biology `[B]` — at the
*ecological* trophic-transfer level. The application-to-castes step is hedged
`[I]` in the body text of all three files. But the headline noun
("certainty" / "defeated" / "stupid") reads as if the no-extraction rule is
*proved* by thermodynamics. D5 forbids this exactly: no evolutionary pattern
"supplies an ought by itself," and the bridge docket holds Justice as chosen
and contestable. A reader could mistake the headline for a natural-law proof of
the fence — the precise geometry-to-duty transfer D5 kills.

**Why DRIFT, not BROKEN:** the body text carries the `[I]` hedge; the violation
is the *register of the noun*, not an identity promotion. The fence itself
stands on its chosen-constitutional warrant (`AGENTS.md` table), independent of
the thermodynamic framing.

**Repair:** soften the headline nouns. "thermodynamic certainty" →
"thermodynamic *image of cost* `[I]`"; "thermodynamically defeated" →
"costly even when it succeeds (10% image `[B/I]`)"; "thermodynamically stupid"
→ "costly in the 10%-rule image `[B/I]`". The 10% rule stays as floor image; it
stops being a proof claim.

## Finding 2 — DRIFT: "derivation" framing reads as proof (D4, D5)

**Where (verbatim):**
- `AGENTS.md:152` — section header *"Standing Constitutional Fences (5+7 = 12 layer, **derived from** Rosetta + Syntropic Dyadism + biological laws)"*
- `AGENTS.md:154` — *"The 12 fences are **derivations** of this root, not ad-hoc additions."*
- `syntropic-dyadism.md:17` — *"It **derives** from three root pieces"*
- `syntropic-dyadism.md:25` — *"**Derivation sketch**"*
- `SKILL.md:48` — *"root + **derivation** in `references/syntropic-dyadism.md`"*

**Why DRIFT:** D4 states Justice "is not derived from `φν=1`, `P_node=ΦV`,
natural selection, efficiency, popularity, or authority." The fences are
*chosen constitutional constraints* `[I/S]`. Calling them "derivations" of a
root that includes "biological laws" risks reading as if the 10% rule *derives*
η=0 — which would be the naturalistic-bridge claim D5 holds open but does not
grant. "Derivation" in mathematics/logic connotes proof; the doctrine's posture
is selection + declared safeguards (Constitution-on-Burrisphere: *"The sphere
does not derive a constitution"*).

**Why DRIFT, not BROKEN:** `syntropic-dyadism.md:23` (root-note) and the body
hedge the bio piece as `[B/I]` image, and `§6` carries the falsification
criteria. The fences stand on their own chosen warrant. The overclaim is the
word "derivation," not the fence substance.

**Repair:** retitle as "Standing Constitutional Fences (5+7 = 12 layer,
**rooted in** Rosetta + Syntropic Dyadism + biological-floor *images*)" —
"rooted in" preserves the intellectual genealogy without the proof connotation.
In the body, prefer "the 12 fences are *consistent with* / *drawn from* this
root" over "are derivations of." A reader should hear "chosen safeguards that
cohere with a root," not "theorems proved from axioms."

## Finding 3 — DRIFT: 10%-rule applied uniformly to all 7 castes (D1, D5)

**Where (verbatim):**
- `AGENTS.md:181-187` — the row-fence table's biological-image column applies
  "~90% loss" to **every** caste (L1 autoimmune, L2 dysbiosis, L3 over-claim,
  L4 rogue-act, L5 homeostasis-blind, L6 memory-chain, L7 attentional-narrow).
- `virtue-vice-biology-ethics.md:42-48` — same 7-row pattern with bio image per row.
- `syntropic-dyadism.md:91-138` — §4 applies the 10% floor to each of the 7.

**Why DRIFT:** D1 forbids treating the seven rows as anything more than a
selected reading order; D5 forbids evolutionary patterns from supplying oughts.
Mapping a *distinct* biological pathology to each of the seven castes (and a
uniform ~90% loss to each) risks reading as if the seven rows *are* seven
biological failure modes — i.e., that the biology evidences the seven-ness.
That is the "seven-row fit proves the source natively has seven stages" claim
the Master Rosetta §2 non-transfer laws forbid. The biology is genuinely
evocative per-row; the uniformity is the tell.

**Why DRIFT, not BROKEN:** three of the seven have real empirical anchors
(L1 ROOT_POLLUTION `[B]`, L3 lazy-tier, L4 the 2026-07-28 amendment — per
`syntropic-dyadism.md §6`), and the body tags the rest `[I]`. The mapping is
explicitly framed as image, not identity, in the synthesis's "Biology does not
supply" table. The drift is the *uniformity* implying evidence where the body
admits only 3/7 are anchored.

**Repair:** in the row-fence table bio column, mark only the 3 anchored rows
with `[B]`; mark the other 4 `[I] image only, not evidence of the row`. Add a
one-line footnote: *"The 10%-rule image is applied per-row as orientation; it
is not evidence that seven rows are biologically privileged (3 of 7 are
empirically anchored; 4 are images)."* The image stays; the false-uniformity
is named.

## Finding 5 — UNDERCLAIM: demon/god as noun-class when the doctrine is move-direction (D6)

**Where (verbatim):**
- `SKILL.md:19-22` — caste table class column: *"L1 … Demon (ego-axis)"*,
  *"L2 … God/Demon (taking axis)"*, *"L3 … God (giving)"*, *"L4 … God (giving)"*
- `L1-stone-brief.md:8` — *"Geometry: Tier **Demon**"*
- `L3-stone-brief.md:8-9` — *"God tier"*
- `L4-stone-brief.md:8` — *"Arjuna ⚔ · **God** (deployable, mixed-sign)"*

**Why UNDERCLAIM (not overclaim):** this is the *opposite* drift. The doctrine
(D6) is that demon/god is "the **direction of a move**, not a permanent kind of
person." The operational layer *says exactly this* in its prose hedges
(`SKILL.md:27-33` defines demon/god as ego- vs collective-potential-maxing;
`SKILL.md:167-168` and `L2:21` call it a "retrospective predicate, never
identity"). **But the caste tables present "Demon" / "God" as a noun-class
column header (`Tier`/`Class`)**, which is the exact reification D6 forbids —
even though the prose two lines below un-reifies it. The result underclaims the
doctrine's own discipline: the table format fights the prose.

**Why UNDERCLAIM, not DRIFT:** the *prose* is correct; the *table format* fails
to carry the doctrine's anti-reification into the most-screenshotted surface.
The doctrine is more severe than the table lets on.

**Repair:** rename the column from "Class"/"Tier" to **"Polarity (move-direction)"**
and suffix entries with "(predicate, not identity)" or a footnote pointing at
the prose. The nouns stay (they're useful mnemonics); the column header stops
naming an essence.

measured against) without carrying the "chosen, not derived" register into the

orientation `[I/S]`, not a theorem. The synthesis's "Provenance" section gets
this right; the per-caste "Moral content" column doesn't carry it inline.

Justice as proved — it's *under*-carrying the doctrine's "chosen" register
into the places Justice is named. The doctrine is more epistemically humble
than the operational use.

the declared Emergentist orientation (chosen `[I/S]`, bridge-to-realism open
per the Objective Ethics Bridge Docket), not a discovered moral fact."* No

## Non-transfer laws restated (Master Rosetta §2)

1. A theorem in domain A does not become a theorem in domain B.
2. A seven-row fit does **not** prove a source domain natively has seven stages.
3. Rosetta cannot promote `[I]`/`[C]` to `[S]`/`[A]`.

**Net assessment:** the operational layer's *body text* obeys all three. The
*headlines and table formats* (Findings 1–4, 6) drift where a strong noun or an
untagged glance could be misread as promotion. No actual `[I]`→`[S]`
promotion survives the parallel register-corrections. The repair scope is
headline-softening + register-clarification, ~6 small edits across 4 files.
