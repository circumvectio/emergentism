# Rosetta Stone: neuroscience comparison boundary

## Verdict

The former seven-row brain-region, hemisphere, brainwave, and cognitive-mode
table is **not an established neuroscientific result**. It bundled broad neural
findings into unsupported one-to-one assignments to the Rosetta levels. Those
assignments are withdrawn from active evidentiary use.

The historical text is preserved in Git at blob
`f0fbaea1ec045aa7369b2c9f1cfcd1e676c376c2`. This document is its Kintsugi
successor: the comparison may suggest experiments, but it cannot transfer proof
from neuroscience to Emergentism or from Emergentism to neuroscience.

## What outside neuroscience can establish

Neuroscience can investigate separately measurable phenomena such as threat
processing, social cognition, executive control, episodic future simulation,
attention, self-referential processing, and large-scale network integration.
Those phenomena are distributed, task-dependent, and context-sensitive. A
brain region, hemisphere, or frequency band is not by itself a Rosetta level,
an operator, a moral rank, or a stage of spiritual attainment.

Accordingly:

```text
observed neural result [A/B]
  + declared comparison rule [I]
  != proof of a Rosetta identity
```

Removing every Rosetta label must leave each cited neural result unchanged.
Removing the neuroscience comparison must leave the Emergentist operational
calculus unchanged.

## Permitted research questions

The following are candidate questions `[C]`, not predictions already earned:

1. Does a preregistered measure of counterfactual breadth predict a distinct
   present-choice distribution after controlling for memory, intelligence,
   income, age, and task difficulty?
2. Do interventions on represented future alternatives change present action
   while leaving the physical causal cone unchanged?
3. Can commitment, performed action, and receipted outcome be decoded as
   empirically separable stages without treating D5 content as a causal token?
4. Does greater functional integration improve a declared task outcome, and
   under which task, population, measurement, and time horizon?

Each study must preregister its operational variables, comparison model,
exclusions, sample, analysis, and kill criterion. Results must be reported at
their own tier even when they resemble a Rosetta row.

## Forbidden inferences

The comparison does not license:

- `L1...L7 =` seven brain regions, hemispheric modes, EEG bands, developmental
  stages, moral ranks, or states of consciousness;
- left hemisphere = analytic person or right hemisphere = intuitive person;
- a unique neural equator, a neurologically proven seven-count, or a necessary
  ascent through the rows;
- a Global Workspace = L7 identity, non-dual awareness as a measured terminal
  state, or any claim that neural integration proves D6;
- promotion of a suggestive correlation into a mechanism or an ontology.

## Kill criterion

If a preregistered Rosetta-coded model fails to outperform serious simpler
neural and behavioral baselines on held-out data, retire that mapping. Even a
successful discriminator would calibrate only the tested mapping in the tested
domain; it would not validate the whole Weltanschauung.
