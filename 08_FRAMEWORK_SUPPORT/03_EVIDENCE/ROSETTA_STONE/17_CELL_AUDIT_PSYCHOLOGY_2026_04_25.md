# Cell Audit: Psychology — reversible prototype

> **[金] Kintsugi seam — 2026-07-20.** The original audit assigned exact
> Rosetta coordinates to developmental constructs and then treated selected
> similarities as validation of a four-level core with a seven-level halo. It
> also attached mythic roles, caste labels, virtues, shadows, and a privileged
> center to people. The cited literatures do not warrant those moves. This
> repair preserves the research prompt while withdrawing the coordinate claims.

## Corrected verdict

Developmental and motivational psychologies contain patterns that may be
compared with a Rosetta layout `[I]`. They do **not** independently establish
the Rosetta's row count, order, symmetry, center, operators, values, or moral
typology. No person receives an `L` coordinate from this document.

The surviving artifact is a prototype for a comparative study. Its role is to
ask whether a declared translation predicts or compresses observations better
than alternatives. It is not a diagnostic instrument, developmental scale, or
classification of human worth.

## Source families and dependency cautions

| Source family | Native subject | What may be compared | What may not be inferred here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piaget | Performance on cognitive-development tasks | Changes in kinds of reasoning across tasks and development | A universal Rosetta coordinate, fixed age boundary, moral status, or terminal human rank |
| Kohlberg | Forms of moral reasoning elicited by dilemmas | Differences in stated justification | Independence from Piaget, moral behavior, moral worth, or a universal final stage |
| Maslow | Motivational needs and later growth language | Co-occurrence and relative salience of reported needs | A compulsory sequence, exact alignment with cognitive stages, or a privileged center |
| Post-formal models | Several proposed extensions beyond formal operations | Rival hypotheses about adult reasoning | One settled continuation, much less three required Rosetta rows |
| Sanskrit virtues and shadow labels | Framework-selected interpretive vocabulary | Optional qualitative comparison | Empirical psychological variables, diagnoses, person-types, or proof of the row structure |

Piaget and Kohlberg are historically and conceptually dependent; placing them
in adjacent table columns does not create independent convergence. Maslow is a
different source family, but that independence does not establish the same
number, sequence, or boundaries. Framework-added `+` stages cannot validate the
framework that supplied them.

## Unit of analysis

The admissible unit is a **declared observation under a declared task and
context**. It is not the essence of a person. A participant may show different
responses across tasks, times, relationships, cultures, languages, and material
conditions. Any study must preserve those differences instead of compressing a
person into a single row.

The following are therefore prohibited outputs of this audit:

- `person p is L_n`;
- `L_n` is intrinsically higher, purer, more moral, or more human than `L_m`;
- a mythic, caste, god, demon, or Titan label diagnoses a person;
- an unobserved inner journey rescues an otherwise unobservable category;
- a geometric midpoint establishes psychological integration or Justice.

## Reversible study design

A future empirical study may test a Rosetta projection only through a
pre-registered comparison:

1. define the domain-native variables and scoring rules before adding Rosetta
   labels;
2. state the proposed projection and preserve every source's own categories;
3. compare at least a continuous model, each source-native model, and the
   selected Rosetta partition;
4. separate training from held-out cases and report missingness, uncertainty,
   culture, language, age, task, and sampling limits;
5. test measurement invariance before comparing groups;
6. report whether the Rosetta mapping adds out-of-sample discrimination rather
   than post-hoc narrative fit; and
7. permit a null result to remove the mapping without changing upstream canon.

### Candidate discriminator `[C]`

After all labels are hidden, an independently coded Rosetta partition should
predict held-out task responses better than both the source-native baselines
and simpler continuous alternatives. The prediction, sample, metrics, and
minimum improvement must be fixed in advance.

### Kill criteria

Withdraw the psychological projection if any of the following occurs:

- the selected row count or order changes materially across valid tasks or
  populations;
- a simpler or domain-native model matches or exceeds held-out performance;
- coders cannot assign observations reliably without reading Rosetta labels;
- apparent fit depends on framework-generated extensions or duplicated source
  lineages; or
- the mapping cannot produce an observable difference from a label-free
  description.

Failure withdraws this crosswalk; it does not refute or amend the
[Emergent Axioms](../../../06_ONTOLOGY/03_THE_EMERGENT_AXIOMS.md), the
[Canonical Formula Block](../../../05_COSMOLOGY/00_CANONICAL_FORMULA_BLOCK.md),
or the independently declared
[Justice model](../../../04_AXIOLOGY/02_VALUE_THEORY/00_OBJECTIVE_MORALS_AND_ETHICS.md).
