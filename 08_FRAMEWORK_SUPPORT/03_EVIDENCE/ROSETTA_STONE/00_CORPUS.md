# Rosetta Stone corpus map

The Rosetta is a translation instrument. It projects a selected topology across
domains so that similarities, inversions, and missing cells become visible. It
does not transfer proof, upgrade evidence, or establish that every tradition
natively contains seven levels.

## Owners

- [Master Rosetta](00_THE_MASTER_ROSETTA.md) — the governing partial-map and
  non-transfer laws, not a principal universal table.
- [Rosetta vNext](03_ROSETTA_VNEXT_THEORETICAL_CONTRACT.md) — projection-pack
  namespaces, cardinality and loss discipline, recursive disambiguation, and
  the generic application-return boundary.
- [Rosetta protocol](00_THE_ROSETTA_PROTOCOL.md) — translation discipline.
- [Seven philosophical questions](00_THE_SEVEN_PHILOSOPHICAL_DISCIPLINES.md)
  — `PHIL7@1`, a reversible question interface.
- [Replicator crosswalk](ROSETTA_REPLICATOR.md) — genotype through
  Egregoreotype, held at explicit tier.
- [Computation](ROSETTA_COMPUTATION.md), [psychology](ROSETTA_PSYCHOLOGY.md),
  [music](ROSETTA_MUSIC.md), [mythology](ROSETTA_MYTHOLOGY.md), and other
  family columns — domain-specific projections.
- [Row studies](D_SERIES_ROWS/) and [domain studies](D_SERIES_DOMAINS/) — local
  elaborations that remain downstream of the Master and source disciplines.

## Translation contract

For a projection `rho_domain`, report:

1. the `projection_id@version` and immutable source snapshot;
2. the source relation, native vocabulary, and native cardinality;
3. the target-domain evidence and fully namespaced target references;
4. every normalization operation, mismatch, dependency, and omitted degree of
   freedom;
5. separate fact, mapping, use, and outcome statuses;
6. the strongest rival, discriminator, round-trip loss, and kill criterion.

A round trip may preserve topology while losing semantics. That loss must be
declared. A fit discovered after choosing the rows is not independent evidence
for the row count.

Projection composition is non-transitive by default. Every composed map is a
fresh audited claim. Failed and superseded revisions remain visible.

## Boundary

The active Rosetta contains Emergentist correspondences only. Former product,
organization, financial, and agent-runtime projections remain available as
historical applications in the
[pure-boundary archive](../../../90_ARCHIVE/pure_emergentism_boundary_2026_07_20/08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE).
Active downstream implementations live with their own owners. They may return
`RS.APPLICATION@1` observation and theory-change proposals, but neither their
runtime state nor their outcomes automatically alter this corpus.
