# Rosetta Stone corpus map

The Rosetta is a translation instrument. It projects explicitly named
relations across separately owned packs so that similarities, inversions, and
missing cells become visible; some packs additionally test a selected
topology. It does not transfer proof, upgrade evidence, or establish that every
tradition natively contains seven levels.

## Translation contract

For a projection `rho_domain`, report:

1. the source and target `pack_id@version` manifests and immutable snapshots;
2. the directional `projection_id@revision`;
3. both native vocabularies, cardinalities, and fully namespaced term references;
4. every projection normalization, mismatch, dependency, and omitted degree of
   freedom;
5. separate fact, mapping, authorization, use, and outcome statuses;
6. the strongest rival, discriminator, round-trip loss, and kill criterion.

A round trip may preserve topology while losing semantics. That loss must be
declared. A fit discovered after choosing the rows is not independent evidence
for the row count.

Projection composition is non-transitive by default. Every composed map is a
fresh audited claim. Failed and superseded revisions remain visible.

## Boundary

The active Rosetta contains Emergentist correspondences only. Former product,
organization, financial, and agent-runtime projections remain available as
historical applications in the
[pure-boundary archive](../../../90_ARCHIVE/pure_emergentism_boundary_2026_07_20/08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE).
Active downstream implementations live with their own owners. They may return
`RS.APPLICATION@1` observation and theory-change proposals, but neither their
runtime state nor their outcomes automatically alter this corpus.
