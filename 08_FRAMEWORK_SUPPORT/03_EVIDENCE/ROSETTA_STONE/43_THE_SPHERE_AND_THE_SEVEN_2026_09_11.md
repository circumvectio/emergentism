## 0 · Why this file exists

together:

1. **G7's count has an owner-attested origin that lives nowhere in this tree.**
   The seven came from the Burrisphere — two demons, two gods, three Titans.
   Until now that sentence appeared only on the public site, in one line of
   `/burrisphere/`, with **no owner in the Documents tree.** A count this

   off-by-one is not a coincidence.** It is stated in `38` §1 and has never
   been read as a statement.

Neither is a new result. Both are recordings. **Nothing here is promoted.**

## 1 · The provenance `[I]` — owner-attested, not derived

**Owner attestation, 2026-09-11:** the Burrisphere was G7's inspiration — the
figure on which two demons, two gods and three Titans were separated. The
partition came off the sphere; it was not counted up from a list.

This is recorded as `[I]`, at the tier that attestation carries. It is **not**
evidence that seven is forced. `DF-03` — seven-as-forced — is
`EMPIRICALLY-REFUTED` and stays refuted; `42` §4 and `KSC-04` both record that
the compactified-ray forcing supplies *"no angular structure, no fourfold, and
no count of seven."* Knowing where a selection came from is not a derivation of
it. **Provenance explains a choice; it never upgrades one.**

Recorded as a property, **not as a second derivation:** the same seven admit a
`3 + 3 + 1` reading (§2) as well as the `4 + 3` reading (`M4 ⊎ F3`). Two cuts of
one set are two cuts, not two proofs.
