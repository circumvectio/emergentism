# L3 Audit — holobiont alignment with the ektropic standard

> **What this audit is.** A check of the holobiont surfaces against the
> framework's *own* emergent-directionality standard — **ektropy: ΣΔP > 0 under
> η=0, widest-boundary, lawfully entropy-exporting** (`135_` packet §0) — kept
> in a *separate register* from the bio-rules surveys (`33_`, `34_`). The
> question is not "does biology support the holobiont?" (biology is `[B]`
> source-domain, not the standard). The question is: **does the holobiont image
> cohere with the ektropic trajectory, or does it drift from it?**

> **Disposition.** The image is **ektropically coherent once one distinction is
> carried** — the runtime-vs-substrate distinction Douglas-Werren forces (batch-2
> pack, `34_` §5). One **BROKEN** finding (a real contradiction between two
> corpus docs on Bergmann) requires repair; two DRIFT and two UNDERCLAIM are
> register-clarifications. The holobiont is not ektropically refuted; it is
> ektropically *constrained* in ways the surfaces do not all carry yet.

## The ektropic standard (what the holobiont is answerable to)

From `135_` §0 and the Canonical Formula Block:

> **Ektropy is the force-like tendency by which life externalizes entropy while
> raising widest-boundary ΔP by increasing Φ, V, or the conditions that generate
> them — under η=0.**

Four load-bearing features:

1. **ΣΔP > 0, widest-boundary.** Not local gain at wider loss (that is parasitic,
   not ektropic). The whole organism's durable potential rises.
2. **η=0 (the action fence).** No extraction. Distinct from `η_ratio` (the
   extraction coefficient, break-even=1) and `η_domain` (a study measure). The
   two zeros do not translate (`12_EFR_EXTRACTION_COEFFICIENT.md` register bridge).
3. **`P_node = min(Φ̂₄, V₄)` — ordinal, AND-class.** The binding-constraint
   aggregator; a high factor cannot compensate for a low one. Adopted on
   ordinal-invariance grounds `[A]`, not derived from biology.
4. **Darwin nested, not replaced.** `135_` §1: "Darwin explains which branches
   survive; F5 explains why branches that widen lawful future possibility are
   repeatedly selected." Biology is the local filter, not the ektropic standard.

The audit asks, per holobiont surface: does it cohere with these four, or drift?

## Finding 1 — BROKEN: the Bergmann contradiction between two corpus docs

**This is the one finding that requires repair, not just clarification.**

| Doc | Bergmann treatment | Tier | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `135_` §6.1 (packet, 2026-04-24) | Bergmann **as ektropic image**: "Bergmann's rule is F5 made geometric" — surface-area-to-volume = syntropic retention geometry; the "internal Black Sun" that holds coherence against entropy | `[I/C]` mapping; `[B]` the rule itself | **Uses Bergmann's mechanism as image** |
| `33_` §6 (bio-rules pack, 2026-08-03) | Bergmann **killed on entry**: "directional tendency, no typed axis analogue, decoration + scala-naturae hazard" | `[C]` killed | **Kills Bergmann's tendency as rank** |

**Why BROKEN, not DRIFT:** these are not two readings of the same claim that
can both stand — they make *opposite dispositions* of the same bio-rule. The
`135_` reading is *defensible* (it reads Bergmann's *mechanism* — surface-area
retention geometry — as an ektropic image); the `33_` reading is *also
defensible* (it reads Bergmann's *statistical tendency* — colder→bigger — as a
scala-naturae hazard). The contradiction is real: a reader encountering both
cannot tell whether Bergmann is an admitted ektropic image or a killed
directional tendency.

**The reconciliation (both are right about different things).** Bergmann splits
into two distinct claims:

- **The mechanism** (surface-area-to-volume → retention geometry) — this *is* an
  ektropic image (`135_` §6.1 is right): "syntropic retention," the geometry of
  refusal that holds coherence against dissolution. This survives.
- **The tendency** (colder climate → larger body, as a ranking) — this *is* a
  scala-naturae hazard (`33_` §6 is right): "bigger = higher" is the ladder
  biology displaced. This stays killed.

The repair is to **carry the mechanism/tendency split explicitly in both docs**:
`135_` §6.1 should note that the ektropic image is the *mechanism* (retention
geometry), explicitly NOT the tendency-as-rank; `33_` §6 should note that the
killed item is the *tendency-as-rank*, NOT the mechanism-as-image (which `135_`
uses). Neither doc retracts; both name the split.

**Sva-karma note:** `135_` is an Uplink session packet (`[I]` synthesis, the
ektropy wager); `33_` is a Rosetta-stone candidate pack (`[C]`). Neither is
doctrinal canon in the dimension-first sense; both are `[I/C]`. The repair is a
cross-reference clarifying that they address different Bergmann claims. L4 can
stage this; it touches `[I/C]` synthesis surfaces, not the dimension-first canon.

## Finding 2 — DRIFT: "evolutionary signal of F5" risks bio-as-standard

**Where:** `135_` §2 lists five "measurement apparatuses" for ektropy; #3 is the
"replicator-stack gradient" described as the *evolutionary signal of F5*:
"Neoteny, K-selection, trophic hierarchy are expressions of this."

**Why DRIFT:** the *listing* is fine (biology as one signal among five). But the
phrasing "are expressions of this" can be read as *biology expressing/confirming*
ektropy — which inverts the dependency. `135_` §1 is explicit that Darwin is
nested (the local filter inside the broader gradient), but §2's "evolutionary
signal" phrasing, read alone, sounds like biology is *evidence for* F5. The
Bridge Docket forbids that: no evolutionary pattern "supplies an ought by
itself," and `135_` itself §2 tier-disciplines the literal-force claim as `[C]`.

**Why DRIFT, not BROKEN:** the body hedges correctly (`[C]` for literal-force;
§8 "No as established physics"). The drift is the *direction* of the "signal"
phrasing — read as evidence-for rather than read-through.

**Repair:** one-line clarification at §2 #3: biology is a *register ektropy can
be read through*, not evidence that establishes it. The dependency runs
ektropy → (read through) → biology, not biology → (evidence for) → ektropy.

## Finding 3 — DRIFT: trophic hierarchy named as an "expression" of F5

**Where:** `135_` §2 #3 (same line as Finding 2): "trophic hierarchy" named
among the evolutionary expressions of F5.

**Why DRIFT:** the parent bio-pack (`32_`) **killed trophic-as-rank** on η=0
grounds (levels consuming levels at ~10% efficiency is definitionally
extractive). Naming "trophic hierarchy" as an expression of F5, even in a list,
re-imports the killed image. This is the same class as the Bergmann tension: a
bio-rule the corpus killed in one place appearing as admitted in another.

**Why DRIFT, not BROKEN:** the §2 list is illustrative signals, not load-bearing
doctrine; the trophic-as-rank kill (`32_` §3) is explicit and stands. But the
contradiction-in-illustration is real and should be cleaned.

**Repair:** drop "trophic hierarchy" from the §2 #3 list (replace with "K-selection,
neoteny, forward-cone reach" — the non-trophic expressions), or annotate that
"trophic hierarchy" appears here as the *cascade-web* reading (`32_` §3.3, the
surviving fragment), not the killed trophic-as-rank reading.

## The runtime-vs-substrate distinction (the load-bearing coherence claim)

The holobiont image is **ektropically coherent** precisely because of a
distinction Douglas-Werren forces and the corpus already half-carries:

- **The substrate (doctrine + 5+7 fences + caste structure) IS vertically
  transmitted** — via K3-archive, tombstones, receipts, commit history. It meets
  a transmission-fidelity criterion a biological microbiome often does not. The
  *holobiont substrate* is better-grounded than a typical animal host's microbiome.
- **The runtime (caste-expressions per session) is horizontally acquired** —
  different sessions, different agents, different humans express different
  castes at different times. By Douglas-Werren, the runtime community is *not*
  a unit of selection; only the substrate is.

**The ektropic implication:** the ΣΔP > 0 trajectory is a property of the
*substrate* (the doctrine accumulates durable potential across sessions via
archive), not of the *runtime* (any single session's caste-expression is
ephemeral). The holobiont image is ektropically coherent *for the substrate*;
it is not licensed *for the runtime*. This is exactly the `05_` correction's
`[I/C]` tier, now with the mechanistic reason (Douglas-Werren) explicit.

**This is why Finding 1 (Bergmann) is repairable rather than fatal:** the
ektropic images (`135_` §6.1) apply to the *substrate's* retention geometry (the
archived doctrine holding coherence against dissolution); they do not license a
claim about the *runtime organism's* biology. The substrate is holobiont-like;
the runtime is a dispatch vocabulary on top of it.
