# Rosetta Stone: Psychology Comparison Prototype

> **[金] Crack.** The predecessor treated selected developmental constructs as
> discovery of one seven-position geometry, then attached capacities, virtues,
> shadows, and a privileged center to those rows.
>
> **Repair.** Source theories keep their own evidence and boundaries. The table
> below is a reversible comparison `[I]`; person-level or predictive claims are
> `[C]` and require instruments and independent tests that do not presuppose the
> Rosetta.

## Standing

This document is a **selected comparison surface** `[I]`. It places terms from
different psychological traditions beside seven Rosetta prompts so that
similarities and failures can be inspected. Adjacency in the table is not
evidence that the source theories discovered positions on `S²`, share one
latent scale, or independently confirm Emergentism.

The source theories retain their own constructs, instruments, stage counts,
populations, limitations, and evidentiary standing. Piaget and Kohlberg are
historically related. Maslow supplies a different motivational vocabulary, and
the sequential hierarchy attributed to it is not established as a universal
order. The L5–L7 entries below are framework-selected extrapolations, not stages
validated by those theorists.

No row assigns a coordinate, moral worth, diagnosis, caste, operator, or fixed
developmental rank to a person. No capacity is caused or made available by an
operator position. In particular, the equator is a property of the chosen
chart; it does not establish that moral autonomy, self-actualisation, or any
human trait peaks at L4.

## Reversible crosswalk

Every cell in this table is `[I]` unless separately cited as a description of
its source theory. The final column states what the comparison must not imply.

| Rosetta prompt | Piaget vocabulary compared | Kohlberg vocabulary compared | Maslow vocabulary compared | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 · immediate embodied constraint | sensorimotor activity | punishment-and-consequence reasoning | physiological and safety needs | a primitive person-type or moral deficit |
| L2 · social relation | pre-operational symbolic activity | interpersonal or rule-facing reasoning | belonging and love needs | a universal age, caste, or social rank |
| L3 · stable operations and production | concrete operations | conventional justification | esteem needs | a single latent psychological coordinate |
| L4 · integration prompt | formal operations | post-conventional reasoning | self-actualisation language | maximum morality, maturity, balance, or worth |
| L5 · systematic reflection | no source-native exact counterpart | no source-native exact counterpart | later growth language | a validated fifth Rosetta stage |
| L6 · reflexive or post-formal prompt | post-formal literature outside Piaget's canonical stages | universal-principle language, where used | self-transcendence language | a universal sixth stage or clinical state |
| L7 · boundary or whole-field prompt | no source-native exact counterpart | no source-native exact counterpart | no source-native exact counterpart | transcendence as a measured terminal rank |

Optional Sanskrit virtue and shadow terms may be used as literary annotations
in a separately marked interpretation. They are not psychological variables or
diagnoses and are intentionally excluded from this evidence table.

## What would make the comparison informative

The crosswalk earns more than illustrative status only through an independently
specified study. At minimum it must:

1. define each source construct without using Rosetta labels;
2. preregister the row-assignment rule and alternatives with different row
   counts or orderings;
3. use blinded, independent coders and report disagreement;
4. test out-of-sample predictions that differ from rival models; and
5. report null and adverse results without converting them into a new mapping.

A person-level hypothesis additionally needs a validated instrument, a target
population, uncertainty bounds, and an explicit prohibition on dignity,
eligibility, treatment, or authority decisions. Until then, person-coordinate
language is out of scope.

## Kill and downgrade criteria

Downgrade or withdraw the crosswalk if blinded coders cannot reproduce its row
assignments, if rival alignments fit equally well, if the result depends on
framework-authored L5–L7 extensions, or if the table invites ranking or clinical
use despite the boundary above. Failure removes the comparison; it does not
count against the source theories.
