# Rosetta R14: Proto-Indo-European Comparative-Linguistics Crosswalk

> **[金] Crack.** The predecessor treated reconstructed roots as ancient names
> for a Teleological Force, forced them into `S²` and a seven-level table, then
> counted other cultural mappings as independent confirmation.
>
> **Repair.** Proto-Indo-European forms are scholarly reconstructions inferred
> from regular correspondences. Their forms, semantic ranges, dates, and
> daughter relations belong to comparative linguistics. An Emergentist
> projection remains `[I]` and cannot upgrade that scholarship—or be upgraded
> by it.

## Two records, never one

| Reconstructed item | Linguistic research question `[D/B]` | Optional comparison `[I]` | Not licensed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `*h₂r̥tó-` and related forms | Which reconstruction, formation, semantic range, and daughter cognates are supported? | fitting or ordered relation may prompt a constraint comparison | an objectively existing “ordering force,” F5, or universal ethic |
| `*dyeu-` and related forms | Which brightness, daylight, sky, divine, or theonymic senses are reconstructible, and at what stages? | luminosity may prompt a disclosure metaphor | perception of one hidden geometry or proof of agency/order |
| daughter traditions | Which forms are regular cognates, borrowings, innovations, or later reinterpretations? | compare how relations are narrated across traditions | exact L-level placement or a forced sevenfold census |

Named handbooks or lexica are leads, not citations by mention. A `[B]` row must
identify the edition/page or stable scholarly source and represent competing
reconstructions where material.

## Rosetta boundary

`ρ_PIE` can ask whether an attested relation is usefully compared with a named
Emergentist relation. It cannot claim that:

- a reconstructed root denotes a physical or teleological force;
- sound correspondences encode `φ`, `ν`, `η`, D-registers, or the Burri sphere;
- a linguistic tree supplies a causal ladder of nature;
- a word for order makes Justice objective; or
- Egyptian, megalithic, Greek, German, and Indo-European rows are statistically
  independent confirmations after one framework selected and coded them.

## Round-trip and rival test

A comparison must preserve the linguistic paraphrase when mapped back. It must
also state at least one rival explanation: broad semantic analogy, translation
bias, cherry-picking, inherited Indo-European material, borrowing, or ordinary
human salience of order/light metaphors.

Kill the mapping if it depends on a disputed reconstruction without disclosure,
if contradictory roots fit the same target equally well, or if it adds no
discriminating prediction beyond those rivals.

## Public wording

> Comparative linguistics reconstructs several Indo-European roots associated
> with fitting/order and brightness/sky. Emergentism may compare those semantic
> fields with its own vocabulary, but the roots do not name or prove its
> geometry, ladder, force, or ethic.
