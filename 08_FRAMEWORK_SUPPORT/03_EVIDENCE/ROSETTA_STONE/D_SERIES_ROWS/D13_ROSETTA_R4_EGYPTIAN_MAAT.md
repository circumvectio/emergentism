# ROSETTA R4: AN EGYPTIAN MA'AT CORRESPONDENCE

> **[金] Kintsugi seam — a translation does not run backward as proof.** The
> predecessor identified Egyptian figures with sphere coordinates, guaranteed
> collapse from imbalance, and called the Duat a literal D4/D5 transition. This
> successor preserves the evocative comparison and removes every evidential
> upgrade.

## 1. Source and projection are different surfaces

The cited Egyptian material supplies historical motifs `[B]`: the weighing of
the heart against a feather associated with Ma'at, Anubis attending the
weighing, Ammit as a devouring figure, declarations made by the deceased, the
Duat, and an afterlife destination often translated as the Field of Reeds.

The Emergentist reading is a modern projection `[I]`:

| Egyptian motif | Optional Emergentist reading |
|---|---|
| heart and feather weighed | compare two named factors rather than collapsing them |
| scale | a visible balance test |
| Anubis | accountable witness or custody of the record |
| Ammit | irreversible consequence when a test fails |
| declarations | claims exposed to an audit |
| Duat | passage through uncertainty or transformation |
| Field of Reeds | an image of restored relation |

This table says what the framework can notice in the motif. It does not say
what ancient Egyptians “really meant,” that they encoded the Burri Sphere, or
that the mythology independently confirms Emergentism.

## 2. Geometry boundary

The reciprocal chart may be laid over the weighing image:

```text
two factors       ↔ heart and feather
φ=ν=1             ↔ balanced scale
B<1               ↔ modeled displacement from balance
```

Every arrow is `ρ_domain`, a Rosetta projection. It neither transfers proof
nor supplies a causal mechanism. An unbalanced coordinate does not guarantee
personal, institutional, thermodynamic, or civilizational collapse.

The Duat is **not** D4 or D5. At most it can illustrate passage. Canonically,
D4 is causal actuality and D5 is merely possible counterfactual content; those
registers are not ancient Egyptian categories, an underworld, or extra
spacetime dimensions.

## 3. Value boundary

The weighing motif is normatively resonant, but no pole, equator, symbol, or
`η_observed` sign decides good and evil. Emergentist moral classification uses
the independently declared, bearer-complete Justice envelope and checks
authorization, consent, payers, beneficiaries, affected bearers, consequence,
contest, and exit.

The sitting practice may use the image as a contemplative prompt `[I]`. A
first-person experience does not validate the historical mapping or upgrade a
public empirical claim.

## 4. What survives

The correspondence has a legitimate use: it helps readers compare an ancient
judgment image with a modern discipline of visible factors, witnessed claims,
and receipted consequences. Its beauty does not depend on literal identity.

## Kill criteria

Withdraw or rewrite the mapping if it:

1. misstates its cited Egyptian sources;
2. claims historical influence or independent confirmation without evidence;
3. treats a Rosetta arrow as derivation, mechanism, or identity;
4. assigns D4/D5 meanings other than actuality/possibility; or
5. derives a moral verdict or inevitable collapse from geometry.
