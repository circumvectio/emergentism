# Rosetta R7 — Brain Networks

> **[金] Kintsugi seam.** The predecessor identified right hemisphere with
> `phi`, left hemisphere with `nu`, corpus callosum with multiplication and the
> equator, and split-brain effects with collapse of a conserved product. It also
> assigned emotions, thoughts, practices, and mental illness to coordinates.
> Those identities are withdrawn. What survives is a network-level research
> analogy with a strict medical boundary.

## 1. Type boundary

Brains are actual biological systems studied through anatomy, physiology,
behavior, and reported experience. `phi`, `nu`, `Phi`, `V`, balance, and G7 are
framework variables or labels. A Rosetta projection may compare relations
between them, but it cannot identify a variable with an organ, hemisphere,
tract, network, emotion, diagnosis, or person.

In particular:

- lateralization is graded, task-dependent, and distributed; no hemisphere is
  a pure coherence or capability axis;
- the corpus callosum is neural tissue supporting interhemispheric
  communication, not an arithmetic operator or geometric equator;
- the chart identity `phi*nu=1` is not a conserved neural quantity;
- split-brain findings do not show that an Emergentist product collapsed;
- default-mode and task-related activity cannot be assigned fixed moral or G7
  valence; and
- McGilchrist's master/emissary image is an interpretation, not anatomical
  confirmation of the Burri Sphere.

## 2. Testable network hypotheses

Define neural quantities independently of the Emergentist chart. For example,
let `I_t` be a preregistered measure of task-relevant network integration and
`S_t` a preregistered measure of task-relevant specialization or segregation.
Only after measurement may a study compare a selected conjunctive score
`C(I_t,S_t)` with additive, minimum, harmonic, and domain-standard baselines.

Candidate hypotheses `[C]`:

1. For a specified task and population, a model using both independently
   measured integration and specialization predicts held-out performance or
   adaptation better than either alone and better than fair baselines.
2. A controlled perturbation of inter-network communication changes both the
   measured network relation and a preregistered behavioral outcome; the effect
   need not be hemispheric or multiplicative.
3. A contemplative practice, compared with an active control, may alter
   specified network dynamics and behavior. Direction, persistence, individual
   variation, null effects, and adverse effects must all be reported.
4. Multivariate models of affect and cognition may be compared with a
   two-factor projection. The projection survives only if it adds reproducible
   predictive or intervention value; it cannot define what an emotion or
   thought "really is."

No result may be manufactured by defining one variable as the reciprocal of
the other. A product fit is one candidate among non-interchangeable models, not
evidence for a universal neural conservation law.

## 3. Kill criteria

The network correspondence narrows or dies if:

- independently defined measures fail reliability or construct validity;
- additive or domain-standard models match or outperform it;
- effects fail held-out prediction, perturbation, or independent replication;
- the proposed balance optimum shifts arbitrarily across tasks without a
  predictive account;
- hemisphere labels do the explanatory work after network variables are
  controlled; or
- clinical or moral conclusions appear without a complete independent bridge.

## 4. Medical and Justice boundary

This note is not medical advice, a diagnostic model, or a treatment protocol.
It does not explain, locate, rank, or cure mental illness. Sitting practice is
not represented here as a neurological cure and should not replace qualified
care. Any human study requires appropriate ethics review, informed consent,
privacy and data custody, adverse-event monitoring, contest, withdrawal, and
bearer-complete Justice. A harmed participant cannot be offset by an aggregate
effect.
