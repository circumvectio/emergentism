# Rosetta R9: Anmut · Demut · Mut — bounded comparison

> **[金] Crack.** The predecessor segmented several German words into a complete
> Burri geometry, assigned them exact axes and an equator, and treated the match
> as an independent confirmation whose chance occurrence decreased
> exponentially.
>
> **Repair.** Etymology is historical linguistics, not symbolic free association.
> Wordplay may inspire a reversible comparison `[I]`; it cannot establish a
> coordinate system, ontology, ethic, or probability of truth.

## Source and projection are different records

| Item | Source question `[D/B]` | Optional comparison `[I]` | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Mut* | What are its documented historical forms and meanings? | courage or disposition may prompt a question about capacity | a pre-geometric ground or universal potential |
| *Anmut* | How did its form and senses develop in attested German? | grace may prompt a question about perceived coherence | the prefix mechanically encodes `↑φ` |
| *Demut* | What are its historical morphology and semantic changes? | humility may prompt a question about self/world calibration | the word encodes `→ν` or a moral pole |

The lexical column requires dictionaries, historical corpora, dates, and rival
analyses. Until those are attached term by term, it remains a research surface,
not `[B]` evidence. Even perfect lexical sourcing would not upgrade the Burri
projection above `[I]`.

## No independence multiplication

German, Greek, Sanskrit, Egyptian, archaeological, and philosophical materials
were selected and aligned by the same Rosetta. Their recurrence is therefore
not a set of statistically independent trials. At most it reports that the
chosen table is expressive across several externally sourced corpora.

No probability of coincidence is defined without a preregistered corpus,
coding scheme, null distribution, selection rule, and independent coders.

## Round-trip and kill

A row survives only if translating it to an Emergentist relation and back does
not overwrite the word's attested history or contested meanings. Kill the row
if:

- its segmentation is folk etymology;
- incompatible meanings fit the same coordinate equally well;
- the mapping depends on English glosses rather than historical German;
- a moral or psychological property is inferred from a word shape; or
- removing the mapping changes no prediction and clarifies no comparison.
