# Rosetta R10: Greek Philological Correspondence

> **[金] Crack.** The predecessor said Greek vocabulary encoded the complete
> Burri geometry “not metaphorically” and treated virtue, Nemesis, time, and
> practice as exact coordinates or conservation dynamics.
>
> **Repair.** Greek terms are historically varied words embedded in distinct
> authors, periods, and schools. The rows below are optional prompts for
> comparison. They transfer neither proof nor evidence to the reciprocal chart,
> the node model, the D-registers, or Emergentist value theory.

## Type boundary

The comparison has three independently priced parts:

1. **Lexical and historical claims** require explicit texts, editions, dates,
   translations, and authoritative scholarship `[B]`. This note does not yet
   supply that term-by-term source apparatus, so its lexical detail remains
   `[D]` pending audit.
2. **Rosetta placement** is an Emergentist interpretation `[I]`.
3. **Chart arithmetic** such as `φν=1` is `[A]` only inside the stipulated
   reciprocal chart. It is not a conservation law acting through Greek history.

`ρ_Greek` may translate a relation for inspection. It may not conclude that
Greek thinkers anticipated the Burri sphere, that several traditions are
independent confirmations, or that a shared word-field makes the geometry
true.

## Candidate comparison table

| Greek term | Broad comparison prompt | Optional Emergentist reading `[I]` | What is not licensed |
|---|---|---|---|
| *logos* | account, reason, proportion, discourse | coherence or articulated relation | `logos = φ`; proof of a cosmic reciprocal law |
| *technē* / *dynamis* | craft, capacity, potential | usable means or capacity | `technē = ν`; a single timeless Greek axis |
| *aretē* / *sōphrosynē* | excellence, temperance or sound-mindedness in named contexts | a prompt for discussing skilled balance | exact placement at `φ=ν=1`; universal optimization |
| *eudaimonia* | flourishing in a specified ethical tradition | a comparison with durable potential | `P∞=1` “lived”; derivation of flourishing from the chart |
| *phronēsis* | practical wisdom | situated judgment before commitment | a guaranteed equatorial action or infallible selector |
| *hubris* / *pleonexia* | overreach or acquisitiveness in named contexts | a warning about excess or extraction | a literal south pole, `ν→∞`, or automatic moral score |
| *nemesis* | distribution, indignation, retribution, or corrective consequence depending on context | feedback after consequential action | a geometric force restoring `φν=1` |
| *metanoia* | change of mind or orientation in a named usage | revision inside a Soul Loop | mathematical reduction, narrator removal, or a privileged contemplative state |
| *chronos* / *kairos* / *aiōn* | differing temporal vocabularies | prompts for duration, occasion, and age | torus coordinates or proof that one moment is the only real time |

These glosses are deliberately broad. A publishable philological claim must
split terms by author, genre, period, and contested translation rather than
treating “the Greeks” as one speaker.

## What convergence can mean

If Greek, Egyptian, Hindu, or German materials can all be projected onto the
same table, the result is one translation instrument applied several times.
That may show the instrument is expressive `[I]`; it is not a probability
calculation and does not create independent observations. “Four encodings” is
therefore **one mapping exercise with four corpora**, not four confirmations of
one geometry.

## Kill and upgrade criteria

- **Kill the mapping** if careful source work shows that it erases distinctions
  essential to the compared traditions or permits incompatible placements with
  equal ease.
- **Keep it `[I]`** if it remains a useful, reversible comparison that makes no
  identity claim.
- **Upgrade lexical rows to `[B]`** only after each gloss has a checkable source
  and rival translation.
- No amount of lexical sourcing upgrades the Burri mapping above `[I]` without
  an independent discriminator.
