# ROSETTA R10: GREEK PHILOLOGY CROSSWALK

## Attested vocabulary, an interpretive mapping, and its limits

> **[金] Kintsugi seam (2026-07-19).** Original blob
> `75fbbb529768e13482f6a191310b4581e5ca1c11` claimed that Ancient Greek
> encoded every coordinate of the Burri Sphere, that four cultures supplied
> independent confirmations, and that coincidence was statistically
> negligible. No sampling model, independence proof, or historical diffusion
> control supported those claims. The repair keeps the valuable result: Greek
> philosophy offers a rich, internally differentiated vocabulary for reason,
> capability, flourishing, excess, correction, transformation, and time. The
> Rosetta can project those distinctions onto the framework at `[I]`. The
> projection is a translation aid, not evidence that Greeks possessed the
> equation or that one geometry caused the vocabulary.

---

## 1. Evidence contract

This row separates three things:

1. **Lexical attestation `[B]`:** a term and a historically supportable range of
   meanings, cited to primary texts or standard lexica.
2. **Rosetta projection `[I]`:** a proposed correspondence to a Burri variable,
   latitude, or move.
3. **Historical claim `[C]`:** any claim that Greek thinkers encoded, intended,
   or derived the framework's equation.

`rho_Greek` translates a selected relation. It does not transfer proof or
upgrade the target claim.

## 3. What the mapping can do

The crosswalk can:

- give a Greek-trained reader entry points into model, means, judgment,
  flourishing, overreach, feedback, and timing;
- distinguish D4 actual craft/action (`techne`, commitment, receipt) from D5
  possible capacity and modeled alternatives (`dynamis` as a partial analogy);
- test whether the same relational topology can be expressed in another
  vocabulary without claiming identical meanings;
- expose where the framework's compression erases historically important
  differences.

The crosswalk cannot:

- show that Greek thinkers knew `phi=cot(theta/2)` or `nu=tan(theta/2)`;
- infer `phi*nu=1`, `P_node:=Phi*V`, the D-ladder, or an ethic from the words;
- turn etymology into causal history;
- count multiple terms from one textual tradition as independent statistical
  confirmations;
- establish that reality must have seven levels or three Titan generators.

## 4. A responsible philosophical reading `[I]`

Greek traditions repeatedly explore tensions among reason, appetite,
spiritedness, craft, judgment, flourishing, excess, and correction. Emergentism
can read that field through a conjunction lens:

```text
model/foresight must meet embodied means before action
action returns a consequence that can correct the model
flourishing is evaluated under bearer-complete Justice
```

This is a Rosetta projection, not a claim that Greek ethics was secretly a
product equation. Product, minimum, harmonic, and Cobb-Douglas aggregators can
all model different versions of "both matter" and may rank cases differently.
The framework selects `P_node:=Phi*V`; Greek vocabulary does not derive that
selection.

Likewise, `sophrosyne` can illuminate balance and `hubris` can illuminate
overreach, but geometry alone supplies no moral verdict. A receipted act is
classified by the Justice envelope: authorization, consent, custody,
reversibility, exit, payer, beneficiary, and consequences for every materially
affected bearer.

## 5. Independence and diffusion discipline

Rosetta correspondences frequently share historical channels, translations,
astronomical mnemonics, philosophical inheritance, or later comparative
selection. They therefore cannot be multiplied as independent observations
without a diffusion model.

The wider Rosetta audit's planetary finding is a standing warning: an elegant
sevenfold match may reflect the seven classical planets carried through a
historical family rather than an independently rediscovered universal. This
does not make the mirror grammar unreal. It separates the genuine algebraic
shape from a culturally transmitted census.

## 6. Falsifiers and upgrades

Demote or revise this row if:

1. a cited lexical range is not supported by primary texts or a standard
   lexicon;
2. a proposed mapping requires suppressing a term's central historical senses;
3. a Rosetta correspondence is presented as proof or independent confirmation;
4. historical diffusion is ignored when independence is claimed;
5. `metanoia`, `nemesis`, `kairos`, or another term is made into a literal
   operator, equation, or coordinate without separate evidence.

An upgrade would require a preregistered corpus, coding rules fixed before
scoring, rival mappings, blinded evaluators, diffusion controls, and a result
that discriminates the Burri topology out of sample. Until then, `[I]` is the
right tier.
