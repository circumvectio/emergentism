# The Ladder and the Two Partitions

> **Read this first.** A session on 2026-08-05 re-derived the `B`-per-level
> ladder from the sphere and briefly treated it as a finding. **It is not.**
> `L1_L7_REFINEMENT_AUDIT.md` already carries the per-level `sin θ` table and
> already confirms the mirror identity. `07_MIRROR_SYMMETRY_FALSIFICATION_TEST`
> already declares the `B` values in its protocol. `100_ROSETTA_DRIFT_AND_
> OPERATOR_REGISTER_AUDIT_2026_07_02` already derives the count a second way.
>
> This document exists to put those three side by side, because **together they
> say something none of them says alone** — and to record the one question that
> falls out, which is open.

## 2 · The geometric derivation of the ladder `[A]` given `[S]`

Divide the meridian `θ ∈ [0, π]` into **six equal arcs of 30°**, giving seven
stations. Evaluate `B = sin θ`:

```text
level      θ         B = sin θ      value declared in 07 §1
  L7      0°           0            ~0   (near-pole)      ✓
  L6     30°           0.5           0.5                  ✓
  L5     60°           0.8660254     √3/2                 ✓
  L4     90°           1.0           1    (max)           ✓
  L3    120°           0.8660254     √3/2                 ✓
  L2    150°           0.5           0.5                  ✓
  L1    180°           0            ~0   (near-pole)      ✓
```

Seven for seven. The three values are the 30–60–90 sines.

**This is not a derivation of seven.** It is the observation that *given* seven
equal stations, the declared values are forced — they are what `sin` does
there. The count is imported, not produced. See §5.

## 4 · The observation this document exists for `[I]`

**Seven is overdetermined. The two derivations agree on the count and
disagree on the partition.**

```text
GEOMETRIC   3 + 1 + 3     palindromic, centred on L4
            L1↔L7 · L2↔L6 · L3↔L5 · L4 self
            the partition B respects

COMBINATORIAL   4 + 3     linear, split between L4 and L5
                L1..L4 transfers · L5..L7 frames
                the partition operational access respects
```

Both are live in the corpus. Both are cited. They are **not the same cut**, and
no document reconciles them. `B` is symmetric about L4 and puts L4 *inside* the
first block; deployability is asymmetric about L4 and puts L4 *at the end* of
the first block.

**The open question, and it is genuinely open:**

> Are `3+1+3` and `4+3` two true structures over one ladder — a balance
> structure and an access structure, independent by nature — or is the
> coincidence of the count evidence that one of them is a forced fit?

Seven arising twice is either a convergence worth taking seriously or an
artifact of having chosen seven first and found two ways to reach it. **This
document does not resolve it and no argument here should be read as resolving
it.** Recorded as open.

## 6 · The deployability asymmetry is NOT an open finding

The 2026-08-05 session predicted, from `B(L1) = B(L7) = 0`, that L1 should
carry the same non-deployable status as L7, and treated the linear rule
(`L1–L4` deployable, `L5–L7` Executive witness) as failing the corpus's own
mirror test.

**That prediction is refuted by an existing ruling with a stated reason.**
`L1_L7_REFINEMENT_AUDIT.md`:

> *"The balance symmetry `B(L2) = B(L6)` is exact, but the tier asymmetry is
> deliberate… The balance function is symmetric; the operational access is not.
> You can use Kālī. You can only experience Śiva. This is why the ascent
> (L1→L4) is under active control and the descent (L4→L7) is surrender."*

Confirmed in the runtime binding at
`08_AGENTS/MANAGED_AGENTS/agents/01_candala_firewall.agent.yaml:75` —
`"operational (L1-L4 deployable; L5-L7 Executive witness)"` — and audited as
earned `[S]` in the
[dated L2 claim-vs-evidence audit](../../../00_HANDOFF/2026_07_20_k2_audit_trio_l1_l7/L2_CLAIM_VS_EVIDENCE_AUDIT_2026_07_20.md),
row 21.

So the asymmetry is **documented, reasoned, and deliberate**, and it is exactly
the `4+3` partition of §4 asserting itself against the `3+1+3` one. It is not a
defect and must not be raised as one again. What §4 records is the *unreconciled
coexistence* of the two partitions — a different and smaller question.
