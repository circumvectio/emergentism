## 1. Red Queen — the η>0 adversary is not static `[I]`

**Statement.** Species must constantly adapt, not to gain advantage, but merely
to survive against ever-coevolving opponents (predators, prey, parasites,
competitors). Biotic conflict, not abiotic change, is the persistent engine of
selection. (Van Valen 1973.)

**Mechanism.** Coevolutionary arms races (especially host-parasite) produce
constant reciprocal adaptation; the biotic environment deteriorates as fast as
the lineage adapts, so standing still = falling behind.

**Empirical status.** Well-supported as a framework, especially for host-parasite
systems (Brockhurst et al. 2014, *Pseudomonas*-phage experiments). The original
"constant extinction probability" claim is more contested than the arms-race
core.

**Type.** Mechanistic.

**Does it add prediction?** *Yes — and it sharpens the L1/L2 fire rule.* The
tit-for-tat non-parasitism rule (`syntropic-dyadism.md` §1.1) protects
cooperators (η≈0) and fires at extractors (η>0). The Red Queen names *why the
η>0 adversary is not a static target*: an extractor that is not countered
co-evolves its extraction faster than a static defense can keep up. **This is a
prediction about the L1 firewall's update dynamics** — the firewall must itself
co-evolve (update its recognition vocabulary) against an adversary that is
adapting to it, or it loses the arms race. A static immune system loses to a
Red-Queen adversary.

**Kill criterion.** KILLED if Red Queen is read as the organism "evolving"
biologically. Survives as `[I]` image: *the η>0 adversary adapts; a static
defense decays; the firewall must co-evolve or be defeated.*

**Verdict.** Retain `[I]`. Distinct from Emery (where the parasite comes from
inside): Red Queen names the *external* arms-race pressure that makes a static
L1 inadequate.

## 2. Hubbell's neutral theory — the niche-vs-drift discriminator `[I]`

**Statement.** Community patterns can be predicted from per-capita ecological
equivalence + stochastic drift, ignoring species differences. (Hubbell 2001.)

**Mechanism.** Demographic stochasticity under functional equivalence generates
realistic abundance distributions without niche partitioning.

**Empirical status.** Contested. A powerful *null model*; ecological equivalence
is empirically false in most communities; niche and neutral processes are now
treated as complementary.

**Type.** Mechanistic (deliberately counterfactual).

**Does it add prediction?** *Yes — as the null model the doctrine needs.* The
corpus's core claim is that the seven castes are *functional niches* (different
work, not interchangeable). Hubbell supplies the **rival null**: if the castes
were ecologically equivalent (pure drift), would the observed dispatch pattern
still look structured? **This is the ECO7 discriminator applied to the castes
themselves** — does caste-specialization predict outcomes better than a neutral
(null) model where any caste does any work? If not, the specialization claim is
decoration. The parent pack's §6.4 began this (the L4-overlap rival); Hubbell
names the formal null.

**Kill criterion.** KILLED if Hubbell is cited as proof the castes *are*
equivalent (the counterfactual is a model, not a finding). Survives as the
**null against which the specialization claim is tested**.

**Verdict.** Retain `[I]` as the null-model discriminator. No caste cell; lives
as the testing framework for the specialization question.

## 3. Liebig's law of the minimum — the binding-constraint image for P_node `[I]`

**Statement.** Growth is controlled by the resource in shortest supply relative
to need, not by total resources. (Liebig 1840; "Liebig's barrel.")

**Mechanism.** Biochemical necessity — non-substitutable nutrients; exhausting
one halts the pathway regardless of others' abundance.

**Empirical status.** Well-supported in core domain (single-nutrient limitation);
a simplification (real systems show co-limitation, interactions). First-order
approximation.

**Type.** Mechanistic.

**Does it add prediction?** *Yes — and it is the image the Canonical Formula
Block already uses.* `P_node := min(Φ̂₄, V₄)` IS Liebig's law applied to the
two finite-node factors: the conjunctive AND-class score is set by the
*limiting* factor, not by the sum or product. **The bio-image makes the formal
choice vivid:** the reason `min` (not product, not sum) is the working aggregator
is precisely Liebig's logic — a high factor cannot compensate for a low one.
This is not new doctrine (the Formula Block adopts `min` on ordinal-invariance
grounds, `[A]`); Liebig supplies the *biological intuition* for why that formal
choice matches how constraints actually bind.

**Kill criterion.** KILLED if Liebig is used to *derive* `P_node=min` (the
adoption is on ordinal-invariance grounds, `[A]`, independent of biology).
Survives as `[I]` image: *the binding constraint is the limiting factor; the
Formal Block's `min` aggregator is the same logic in formal register.*

**Verdict.** Retain `[I]` as the biological intuition behind an existing formal
choice. Cross-reference, not a new cell.

## 4. Bronstein's mutualism-parasitism continuum — the η_move register image `[I]`

**Statement.** The same species pair can shift along a continuum from mutualism
to parasitism depending on ecological context; the sign/magnitude of the
interaction is context-dependent. (Bronstein 1994; Chamberlain & Bronstein 2014.)

**Mechanism.** Net fitness effect = benefit − cost; both vary with environment
and partner. Mycorrhizae become parasites under low light; legume mutualisms
flip under low phosphorus.

**Empirical status.** Well-supported, mainstream. The framework replaced the
older view of mutualism as inherently stable.

**Type.** Pattern/framework.

**Does it add prediction?** *Yes — and it is the image for the η_move register
itself.* The extraction-coefficient doc (`12_EFR_EXTRACTION_COEFFICIENT.md`)
distinguishes `η_move=0` (action fence) from `η_ratio` (coefficient, break-even=1)
from `η_domain` (study measure), and stresses that a *temporary* `η_ratio>1` is
not architectural parasitism. **Bronstein is the biological image of exactly
that:** a relation can be mutualistic in one context and parasitic in another
without the partner changing — the *sign* is contextual. This is the η_move
register's biological analogue: the same act can be extraction or contribution
depending on the bearer-horizon-context declared. The Doctrine's R1 ("no
concealed extraction") requires *declaring* the context exactly because the sign
is context-dependent (Bronstein's image).

**Kill criterion.** KILLED if the continuum is used to relativize η=0 away ("it
might be mutualism in some frame"). The fence is chosen-absolute; the continuum
is the image for *why declaration of context is mandatory*, not for *why the
fence is optional*. Survives as `[I]` image: *the sign of a relation is
context-dependent; therefore declare the bearer/horizon/context (R1).*

**Verdict.** Retain `[I]`. Names why the R1 declaration discipline is
non-optional: the sign is not fixed by the act alone.

## 5. Douglas & Werren — the transmission-fidelity critique `[S/B] — register fence`

**Statement.** The hologenome concept (host + microbiome genomes as a co-inherited
unit of selection) rests on assumptions "generally unmet" in nature; a host +
microbiome is better viewed as an ecological community with varying
conflict/cooperation, not a coherent evolutionary individual. (Douglas & Werren
2016, *mBio*.)

**Mechanism (the transmission-fidelity objection, in full).** For the holobiont
to be a unit of selection, **high partner fidelity across generations** is
required — the same host-microbe combinations must co-segregate so selection on
the host also selects the microbiome. But:

1. **Most host-associated microbes are acquired horizontally** from the
   environment each generation; the offspring's microbiome is not reliably the
   parent's. There is no heritable covariance between host genotype and a
   horizontally-acquired microbiome — selection cannot "see" the pair as a unit.
2. Even where **individual host-microbe pairs** achieve vertical transmission
   and high fidelity (e.g., obligate *Buchnera* in insects), **this fidelity
   does not extend to the whole microbiome**. The community cannot evolve as a
   hologenome even if specific partners can.
3. Constituent microbes have **competing evolutionary interests** — they do not
   form a single cooperative unit of selection; selection acts on the parts.

**Empirical status.** Influential, largely accepted as a correction. Consensus:
the hologenome is a valid level of selection *only* where partner fidelity is
high (obligate vertical symbionts); the broad whole-microbiome version is not
viable.

**Type.** Mechanistic (an anti-mechanistic argument — it argues the proposed
mechanism fails a necessary condition).

**Does it add prediction?** *Yes — and it constrains the holobiont image
directly, which is why it is retained at `[S/B]` rather than `[I]`.* The
holobiont-organs synthesis maps the Magnum Opus organism's "castes" onto
holobiont roles (host=L1-L4, microbiome=L5-L7, hologenome=doctrine+fences).
**The transmission-fidelity objection asks the load-bearing question: is the
Magnum Opus "microbiome" (the L5-L7 witness functions + the shared doctrine)
transmitted with high enough fidelity to be a unit of selection?** Two readings:

- **The doctrine + fences ARE vertically transmitted** (they are archived, are
  K3-tombstoned, are receipted across sessions) — so the *substrate* meets a
  fidelity criterion the biological microbiome often does not. The holobiont
  image is *better-grounded for the doctrine* than for a typical animal host.
- **But the "caste expressions" are not vertically transmitted** — different
  sessions, different agents, different humans express different castes at
  different times. The *phenotype* is horizontally acquired per-session. By
  Douglas-Werren, the community (the runtime organism) is not a unit of
  selection; only the substrate (doctrine+fences) is.

**This is a real, sharp constraint on the holobiont image:** the *substrate* may
be holobiont-like (high-fidelity vertical transmission via archive); the
*runtime* is not (horizontal acquisition of caste-expressions per session). The
`05_` register-correction's `[I/C]` tier and "dispatch vocabulary, not
biological holobiont" framing already accommodate this — but Douglas-Werren
supplies the *mechanistic reason* the correction was needed.

**Kill criterion.** KILLED if Douglas-Werren is used to *retract* the holobiont
frame entirely (the frame is useful as dispatch vocabulary regardless). Retained
as the **standing register fence**: the holobiont image is `[I/C]` precisely
*because* the unit-of-selection claim fails the fidelity test for the runtime
organism. The image is pedagogically real; the ontology is not licensed.

**Verdict.** Retain `[S/B]` as a **register fence on the holobiont image** —
names the mechanistic reason the `05_` correction tiered it `[I/C]`. This is the
single most load-bearing entry in the batch.

## 6. The killed seven (recorded as the informative non-fits)

**Haldane's rule** (1922): heterogametic sex is sterile/absent in hybrids.
Well-supported speciation generalization — but the *genetics of sex-chromosome
heterogamy* has no typed analogue in a caste system. KILLED: no typed edge.

**Gloger's rule** (1833): humidity → darker pigmentation. Well-supported
ecogeographic covariation — but humidity→pigmentation has no typed axis in the
organism. KILLED: decoration, no typed edge.

**Jordan's rule** (1890s): colder → more vertebrae in fish. *Contested*
(Reyes Corral 2019: relationship can be negative, positive, or U-shaped). Even
if settled, developmental-temperature → segment-count has no typed axis. KILLED:
contested + no typed edge.

**Mid-domain effect** (Colwell & Lees 2000): richness peaks at the midpoint of a
bounded domain by geometry. A null model; max ~14% variance explained; some
argue it is a statistical artifact. No typed domain-boundary in the organism.
KILLED: null model, no typed edge.

**Kleiber's law** (1932): metabolic rate ∝ mass^(3/4). Well-supported as a
tendency; mechanism disputed. The weak reading "larger = more efficient per unit"
risks the scala-naturae hazard (bigger/higher = better). KILLED: scala-naturae
recruitment hazard (same class as Bergmann/Cope in the parent pack).

**Jarman-Bell** (1960s-70s): larger herbivores tolerate lower-quality diet.
Well-supported trend with mechanistic basis (allometry of gut vs metabolic
demand). Body-size → diet-quality has no typed axis in the organism. KILLED: no
typed edge.

**Lack's principle** (1947) and **Shelford's law of tolerance** (1913):
fecundity-vs-quality optimization; min/opt/max tolerance envelope. Both have a
*faint* analogue (Lack = "don't maximize count, optimize viable output"; Shelford
= "every factor has a tolerance window") but neither names a typed edge a plain
graph misses. `[I]` cross-ref at most; no cell.

## 7. WBE scaling — the fractal-distribution image `[I]` cross-ref only

**Statement.** Quarter-power allometric scaling (B ∝ M^(3/4)) derived from
space-filling fractal distribution networks with size-invariant terminals and
minimized dissipation. (West, Brown & Enquist 1997.)

**Mechanism.** Three postulates → 3/4 exponent. The point: a *distribution
network geometry* explains the scaling.

**Empirical status.** Influential (6,500+ citations) but contested (Kozłowski
2005: "built to explain a non-existent pattern"; modified derivations Banavar
2010 recover quarter-powers under weaker assumptions).

**Type.** Mechanistic.

**Does it add prediction?** *Marginal.* The fractal-distribution-network image
rhymes with the doctrine's *replicator-stack* (the deeper replicators — memotype,
egregorotype — distribute the doctrine through a branching network of sessions,
agents, institutions). But the rhyme is loose, and forcing a metabolic-scaling
exponent onto the organism would be Procrustean. **Retained as a one-line
cross-ref**: the replicator-stack is a distribution network; WBE is the biology
of why distribution networks scale sublinearly. Not a cell.

## 8. What this batch means for the holobiont

The batch-2 survey, like the parent pack, finds **most named bio-rules do not
help** the holobiont frame — 7 of 14 examined are killed on entry (genetics,
ecogeographic covariation, allometric tendencies, null models). Three earn
`[I]` image retention for distinct mechanistic contributions:

- **Red Queen** — the η>0 adversary adapts; static defenses decay. Sharpens the
  L1 firewall's update discipline.
- **Liebig's minimum** — the biological intuition behind `P_node=min(Φ,V)`. The
  binding constraint is the limiting factor.
- **Bronstein continuum** — the sign of a relation is context-dependent;
  therefore R1's declaration discipline is non-optional.

One earns `[I]` as the **null model** for the specialization question:

- **Hubbell neutral** — the formal null against which "the castes are functional
  niches" is tested. If neutral beats niche, the specialization is decoration.

And one — **Douglas-Werren** — is retained at `[S/B]` as a **register fence**
that constrains the holobiont image directly:

- The transmission-fidelity objection names *the mechanistic reason* the holobiont
  is `[I/C]` not ontology. The doctrine+fences substrate *is* vertically
  transmitted (K3-archive) and so is better-grounded than a typical animal
  microbiome; the *runtime caste-expressions* are horizontally acquired per
  session and so are not a unit of selection. The image is real for the
  substrate; the ontology fails for the runtime.

The bio-rules ledger is now substantively complete across both batches. The
load-bearing bio core remains the parent pack's Gause/niche/coevolution; the
additive images are Emery/character-displacement/R\* (batch 1) and Red
Queen/Liebig/Bronstein/Hubbell-null (batch 2); the standing constraint is
Douglas-Werren on the holobiont's unit-of-selection claim.
