# Dissolution Formal Verification — Historical Prototype

> **[金] Kintsugi seam.** The predecessor presented sketches containing
> `Axiom`, `Admitted`, placeholders, and type errors as a formal-verification
> framework for A1-A7, the sphere, the Trinity, and the Great Work. No completed
> Coq, Lean, or TLA+ artifact accompanied those claims. E1-E10 has since
> superseded A1-A7, and selected symbolic correspondences are not theorem
> targets merely because they can be encoded.

## Standing

This path is a historical proof-plan receipt. It is **not**:

- a machine-checked proof;
- evidence that the Burri chart describes reality;
- a derivation of E1-E10, ethics, G7, or dimensional closure;
- an executable specification; or
- authority to treat an assumption introduced with `Axiom` as a result.

A proof assistant can verify consequences of declared definitions and axioms.
It cannot establish that those premises instantiate in the world, transfer
evidence between Rosetta domains, or convert a chosen norm into a theorem.

## Reusable verification contract

A future formalization may proceed only claim by claim:

1. name a current owner and evidence tier;
2. type every primitive and distinguish definition, assumption, conjecture,
   theorem, and empirical bridge;
3. provide compiling source with no unclosed proof obligations;
4. state the exact theorem and dependencies in ordinary language;
5. run a pinned, reproducible checker and retain its receipt; and
6. state what the proof does **not** establish about reality or value.

The analytic reciprocal-chart identity is a suitable local exercise. The
identification of that chart with reality, candidate mu-crossings, strong
emergence, morality, and operator completeness remain outside what such an
exercise proves.
