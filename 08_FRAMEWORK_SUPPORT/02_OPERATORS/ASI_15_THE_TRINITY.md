# THE TRINITY

## The selected `{0,1,∞}` Titan frame

> **[金] Kintsugi seam — symbol survives; forced ontology does not.** The former
> macro said the Titans were prior to number, that three was uniquely stable,
> that all arithmetic derived from the triad, and that every information system
> instantiated it. Those claims were not proved. The original remains at Git
> blob `3eeeec3e9a29ab4074645f770da90971c1fc3b17`.

## 1. Type boundary

The glyphs name selected symbolic roles:

| Glyph | Mathematical locus in the declared chart | Interpretive role `[I]` |
|---|---|---|
| `•` | ordinary `0` / ground-limit | absence, boundary, subtraction |
| `⊙` | ordinary `1` / finite reciprocal point | unit, balance, mediation |
| `○` | projective `∞` | horizon, unbounded limit |

Ordinary `0` and `1` remain numbers and operands. `∞` is a projective point in
the extended setting. The Titan labels do not precede arithmetic, act as causal
particles, or generate the D-registers.

## 2. Why use this triad? `[I]`

`{0,1,∞}` is a standard and useful projective normalization. Emergentism
selects it because ground, finite unit, and horizon make a compact visual frame.
Selection is not necessity. The retracted Triadic Stability proof did not show
that monism is sterile, dualism collapses, or every `N≥4` structure is redundant.

The frame remains useful only where it clarifies a declared comparison better
than two-part, four-part, continuous, or domain-native alternatives.

## 3. The analytic chart `[A]`

For `θ∈(0,π)`, define

\[
\phi=\cot(\theta/2),\qquad \nu=\tan(\theta/2).
\]

Then `φν=1`, `φ+ν≥2`, and the selected balance coordinate satisfies `B≤1`.
These are analytic facts inside the open reciprocal chart. The poles are limits,
not locations at which ordinary field arithmetic evaluates `0·∞`.

The glyph equation

\[
⊙=•\times○
\]

is therefore an emblem. It is not the arithmetic theorem `1=0×∞`, a
conservation law of nature, an ontology, or an ethic.

## 4. Rosetta use `[I/C]`

The same three labels may be projected into mythology, computation, cognition,
or institutions as a comparison lens. Such a projection inherits no proof from
the chart and does not establish that the target domain literally has three
components or one shared mechanism.

Traditional parallels are historical or interpretive claims requiring their
own sources. They must not be presented as identical structures merely because
three labels can be aligned in a table.

## 5. Kill criteria

This macro fails wherever it is used to claim:

- a unique triadic ontology;
- arithmetic derivation from the Titans;
- `1/0=∞` in ordinary field arithmetic;
- universal substrate independence without a discriminator; or
- ethics, governance, or product design proved by the emblem.

The local correspondence also fails when a rival partition explains or
predicts the target domain better.
