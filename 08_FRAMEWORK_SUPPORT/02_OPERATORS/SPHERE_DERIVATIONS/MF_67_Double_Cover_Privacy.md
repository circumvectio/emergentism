# MF-67 · Double cover and privacy correspondence

> **[金] Crack.** The former paper treated `SU(2)→SO(3)`, spinor sign, Bloch and
> Riemann spheres, privacy, and subjectivity as one identity. The group theory
> does not prove a private mental dimension or a law of measurement.

`SU(2)` is a double cover of `SO(3)`; two elements of `SU(2)` map to one spatial
rotation. Spinorial phase and its observability depend on the physical setup.
These facts may suggest a limited analogy: a public projection can omit
distinctions present in its source. That is not a derivation of consciousness,
privacy, personhood, or D5.
