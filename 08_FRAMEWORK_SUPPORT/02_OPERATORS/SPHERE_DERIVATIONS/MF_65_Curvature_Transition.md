# MF-65 · Curvature transition correspondence

> singular torus-to-sphere transition, a D4-spacetime/D5-consciousness identity,
> and claims of inevitable reconvergence. Those inferences are not theorems.

curvature is zero. A round sphere has constant positive Gaussian curvature and
total curvature `4π`. A change of genus cannot occur through one smooth family
of regular embedded surfaces without a singularity or surgery. These geometric
facts do not prove a transition in physics or consciousness.

divergent versus reconvergent model trajectories. D4 nevertheless remains
causal actuality and D5 counterfactual possibility; neither is identified with
a surface, genus, mind, or spacetime dimension. An agent’s physical light cone
is still bounded by spacetime and `c`.
