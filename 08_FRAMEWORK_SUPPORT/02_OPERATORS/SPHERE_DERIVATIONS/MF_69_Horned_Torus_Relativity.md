# MF-69: Horn-torus / relativity correspondence

> **[金] Kintsugi seam — resemblance survives; causal-horizon inflation does
> not.** The prior document identified the horn torus with the relativistic
> light cone, treated the horn point as `c`, and placed D5 beyond the causal
> horizon. Those claims were not derived. The original remains available at
> Git blob `b839bec513eb087802c9ed59181e2f97fd6a7118`.

## 1. The bounded observation `[I]`

A horn-torus morph can visually resemble several qualitative features often
used to teach relativistic limits:

- a narrowing scale can suggest contraction;
- a closing cycle can suggest decreasing proper-time rate;
- a singular transition can suggest a limiting boundary; and
- a divergent deformation cost can suggest an energy asymptote.

This is a visual correspondence. Similar-looking curves or transitions do not
establish equality of metrics, causal structures, dynamics, or physical
mechanisms.

## 2. What special relativity actually supplies `[A/B]`

For a massive one-dimensional state with rapidity `w`, normalized null
four-momentum coordinates may be written `e^w` and `e^{-w}`. Their product is
one and their mean is `cosh(w)=γ`. That exact change of variables can be drawn
using the same reciprocal chart used elsewhere in Emergentism.

The physical light cone remains a spacetime causal structure bounded by `c`.
Nothing in this document changes that bound, creates a route “beyond” it, or
turns a topological animation into a Lorentzian metric.

## 3. D4/D5 type boundary `[S/I]`

- `D4` contains actual physical carriers, actions, measurements, and records.
- `D5` contains merely possible modeled contents and ranked alternatives.
- Actual model tokens representing D5 contents remain D4 physical events.

An agent's **option cone** can widen when it models, coordinates, and reaches
more admissible histories. Its **physical light cone** does not widen. The
transition from a physical drawing to an option-field drawing is a change of
modeling register, not travel outside causal horizons and not a μ-crossing
proved by relativity.

## 4. Photon boundary `[I/C]`

The horn point may be used as an image for a limiting case. It is not a
residence occupied by photons, and no genus transition explains why photons
are massless or experience zero proper time along a null path. Any such reading
is removable conjectural imagery.

## 5. Kill criteria

The correspondence is rejected wherever:

1. it is presented as a derivation or replacement of Minkowski geometry;
2. a quantitative comparison fails against standard relativistic equations;
3. it implies a physical region beyond `c` or a D5 causal substance; or
4. a simpler diagram communicates the bounded teaching point more faithfully.

Removing this document changes no operational Burri Rule, D-register type,
Soul Loop equation, or external-calibration claim.
