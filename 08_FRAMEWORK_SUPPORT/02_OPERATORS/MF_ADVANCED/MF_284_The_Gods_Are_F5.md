# MF-284: KINTSUGI TOMBSTONE

## Mythic operators are a reading, not a force derivation

The former essay at this path claimed that four mythic figures were forced
instances of a fifth physical force and that social action was quantum
measurement. Those claims are withdrawn. The pre-repair text remains
recoverable through Git history; this stable path records the surviving result.

## What broke

1. A `2 x 2` table can exhaust the cells of a chosen partition without proving
   that reality contains exactly four agents, gods, forces, or moral moves.
2. The labels "give/take" and "actual/possible" are model choices. Their cross
   product is complete only relative to those definitions.
3. D4 action is causal actuality and record; D5 content is modeled possibility.
   Commitment is not wave-function collapse, and deliberation is not a quantum
   measurement operator.
4. Complex conjugation does not establish a social, psychological, or mythic
   operation. Similar diagrams transfer no proof or evidence tier.
5. Mythology may inspire a memorable Rosetta projection, but mathematics does
   not confirm a tradition merely because the two can be arranged in the same
   table.

## What survives

`[S]` Given two declared binary predicates, their cross product has four cells.

`[I]` Emergentism uses four operator names as a mnemonic partition of finite
moves along two declared questions:

1. Does the move widen or contract another bearer’s durable potential?
2. Does it primarily alter embodied means or modeled options?

This is a graphical grammar for inquiry. It is not a census of nature and does
not assign supernatural agency, physical force, moral worth, or human rank.
Mixed moves may occupy more than one cell at different times or scales.

The demon/god polarity is likewise an ethical analogy: ego-maxing extraction
versus durable mutual potential-widening. It is evaluated through visible
consequences and the Justice envelope, never through mythic identity.

## Kill criterion

If the four-cell partition fails to classify consequential moves without hiding
material payer, beneficiary, time-horizon, or mixed-effect information, it must
be refined or abandoned. Mythic elegance is not a defense.
