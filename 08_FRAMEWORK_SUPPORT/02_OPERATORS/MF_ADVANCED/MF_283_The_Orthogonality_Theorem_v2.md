## 1. The positive mathematical residue

In a declared inner-product space, vectors can be orthogonal. Over the complex
numbers, a value may be written `z=a+ib`, with real and imaginary components;
complex conjugation gives

```text
z z* = a² + b².
```

These are genuine mathematical facts under their definitions. They do **not**
imply that actuality is the real axis, possibility is the imaginary axis, or
conjugation physically destroys unreal worlds. Algebra supplies a language of
components and transformations, not an ontology by itself.

## 2. The typed Emergentist distinction

The operational distinction is modality, not a physics-axis identity:

```text
D4 actual   = causal state, embodied means, performed act, factual record
D5 possible = counterfactual alternatives, modeled futures, ranking, selection
```

Every present model token, computation, and selection event is physically
carried in D4 even when its content concerns D5 possibilities. Commitment turns
one selected option into an attempted D4 action; the environment separately
returns an outcome receipt. No imaginary-to-real transduction is required.

This is the useful “orthogonality” claim: actual and possible statements obey
different types and cannot be substituted for one another. It is a logical and
modeling discipline, not a theorem that they are perpendicular physical axes.

## 3. Quantum quarantine

Everett's relative-state formulation has no fundamental collapse. A
Copenhagen-family actualization story is interpretation-specific. Neither is an
extra spacetime dimension, and neither is stacked on the D-registers.

A removable `[C]` correspondence may compare:

```text
D5 alternative-content  ↔ structured alternatives or branch relations
D4 factual record       ↔ observer-relative or interpretation-specific record
```

The arrow means “compare,” never “is.” Removing this paragraph leaves the Soul
Loop, D4/D5 typing, Power-Max, Justice, and the Burri Rules unchanged.
