## 1. Established physics `[A]`

In SI units,

\[
\alpha=\frac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\hbar c},
\]

and its low-energy value is approximately `1/137.036`. It is dimensionless
and controls perturbative electromagnetic interaction strengths. In the usual
QED Feynman rules an electron–photon vertex carries the electric charge
coupling `e` (with convention-dependent normalization); probabilities or
cross sections acquire powers related to `e²` and hence `α`. It is therefore
incorrect to say that each vertex simply carries a factor `α`, or that `α`
is itself a probability amplitude.

The effective electromagnetic coupling runs with energy. That established
running does not imply convergence of D-registers, consciousness, will, or a
Titan equation. Grand-unification scenarios are model-dependent and do not
entail `α→1` or a D4/D5 merger.

## 3. What survives as correspondence `[I/C]`

The useful question is general:

> When a higher-level model is claimed to constrain or reweight lower-level
> trajectories, what operational coupling variable measures the intervention
> effect, with what units, baseline, rivals, and costs?

`α` is an instructive example of a precisely defined, independently measured
physical coupling. It can discipline the analogy by showing what a real
coupling claim would need:

1. typed state variables and domains;
2. an intervention that changes the proposed coupling while controlling
   alternatives;
3. a quantitative response law;
4. independent measurement and uncertainty;
5. rival mechanisms;
6. a preregistered discriminator and kill criterion.

The Emergentist model-mediated-future claim currently has external construct
anchors, but no universal scalar coupling constant. Its carrier is a present
model token; its content may concern a future; its effect is measured by a
change in present action distribution.

on the physical model. This paper makes no fixed numerical habitability window
and no inference from observed habitability to purpose, necessity, or the
Emergentist chart.
