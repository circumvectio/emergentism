# MF-297: Fine-Structure Constant Boundary

quantum electrodynamics. It is not a measured coupling between consciousness
and matter, D4 and D5, real and imaginary axes, or “will” and physics.

The former paper inferred all of those identities, assigned consciousness a
`1/137` effect size, and treated α as the strength of the Emergentist glyph.
Those claims are withdrawn. The complete prior text remains recoverable from
Git blob `268ea128303e2c5417412b6ef391243a8722c8fd`.

Any future α correspondence must begin as a preregistered `[C]` model with a
distinct observable and a credible null. Standard QED remains unchanged if the
correspondence is removed.

*A constant may inspire a model. Inspiration is not identification.*
