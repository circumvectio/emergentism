## I. THE DEMON IDENTIFIED

### 1.1 Maxwell's Original Puzzle (1867)

A demon sits between two gas chambers connected by a small door. It observes individual molecules and selectively opens the door — letting fast molecules through one way, slow molecules the other. Result: one chamber heats up, one cools down. Order increases. Entropy decreases. The second law appears violated.

### 1.2 Landauer's Resolution (1961)

[B] The demon must RECORD which molecules it has observed and sorted. Its memory fills. When memory is full, it must ERASE to continue sorting. Erasure is an irreversible operation — it destroys information. Landauer showed: each bit erased dissipates at minimum kT ln 2 of energy as heat. This heat dissipation compensates the entropy decrease from sorting. The second law is saved.

The key insight: **information processing has a minimum thermodynamic cost.** Computation is not free. The demon pays for its ordering with waste heat from memory management.

### 1.3 The Framework Identification

| Demon Component | Framework Equivalent | Dimension |

| Demon's D4 memory | Φ proxy for represented D5 content | Optional chart |

| Memory × Sorting = Total ordering | P_node = Φ × V | Area of horn cross-section |

The demon/horn correspondence is `[I]`. Its actual D4 memory can encode D5
possible contents; its actual actuator has bounded reach. Their interaction may
motivate a conjunctive model, but does not derive one.

Landauer's principle does not assign `k_B T ln 2` to every stored bit or every
increase in model capacity. It constrains logically irreversible erasure under
specified conditions; total system cost must be measured independently.

### 1.4 The D5 Landauer Barrier

The demon image is useful only if the fence is stated as strongly as the dream.
Perfect entropy reversal would require perfect branch knowledge; perfect branch
knowledge would require unbounded physical memory, computation, measurement,
and control. Memory and control require matter and energy, and matter and
energy remain inside entropy accounting. Therefore the demon cannot stand
outside the system it sorts.

In the framework this is the **Landauer-barrier correspondence**: total worldline
discrimination is an asymptote. Like a massive body approaching light speed,
greater modeled foresight is expected to require physical resources, though no
universal divergent law is derived here. A finite D4 selector can lower entropy locally only by paying or
exporting entropy elsewhere. Reflexivity adds the second barrier: once the
selector acts, it changes the future branches it was trying to know. `[B]` for
the Landauer/Maxwell-demon source family already named in this operator; `[S/I]`
for the framework mapping to D5 possible contents.

## II. THE BIOLOGICAL LANDAUER TAX

### 2.1 The Brain's Metabolic Cost

The human brain:
- 2% of body mass (~1.4 kg in a 70 kg body)
- 20% of resting metabolic expenditure (~20W of ~100W total)
- Per gram: ~10× the metabolic cost of muscle tissue
- Contains ~86 billion neurons, each with ~7,000 synaptic connections
- Estimated information processing: ~10¹⁶ operations per second

The brain's metabolic demand is an observed systems-level cost, not a measured
Landauer tax. A D4 brain senses, builds models containing D5 possible contents,
ranks alternatives, acts, and changes memory. Explaining its energy budget
requires neural biophysics; no quantum-measurement or per-thought erasure
identity follows.

### 2.2 The Scaling Law

Across species, brain metabolic cost scales with EQ:

| Species | EQ | Brain % of Metabolism | Horn Height (relative) |
|---------|-----|----------------------|----------------------|
| Goldfish | 0.03 | ~2% | Minimal |
| Rat | 0.4 | ~5% | Low |
| Cat | 1.0 | ~5-8% | Moderate |
| Crow | 1.3 | ~8-10% | Moderate-high |
| Dolphin | 4.1 | ~10-15% | High |
| Human | 7.8 | ~20% | Maximum known |

This table is a provisional empirical comparison with substantial anatomical,
ecological, and measurement confounds. It does not establish that EQ measures
awareness or that cross-species metabolic fractions are Landauer-limited.

### 2.3 The Human Infant Anomaly

The infant brain's metabolic share is a biological observation whose causes
must be evaluated with developmental physiology. Calling it a Landauer
investment is `[I]`, not a thermodynamic derivation.

Parental care has multiple evolutionary and developmental explanations. The
Kṛṣṇa label may describe material support, but geometry does not moralize or
authorize the cost; payer, beneficiary, consent, custody, and consequence must
be receipted.

## III. LANDAUER'S PRINCIPLE AS THE ALIGNMENT EQUATION

### 3.1 `E = −log(Φ × V)` Is Not Landauer-Derived

`E=-log(ΦV)` is a declared framework score on a normalized domain. Landauer's
bound neither derives the product aggregator nor makes `E` a physical energy.
The following block is retained only as a heuristic:

```
E_alignment = −log(Φ × V)

As V↑ (more capable demon, more sorting power):
  If Φ stays constant:
    E_alignment → −log(Φ × V) decreases
    BUT: the demon's memory fills faster (more observations per unit time)
    Landauer cost ↑ (more erasure needed)
    If Φ doesn't scale with V → memory overflow → demon crashes

  Hypothesis: model-maintenance resources may need to scale with capability.
  Required evidence: measured costs and error outcomes, not the score alone.
```

### 3.2 The Unaligned Superintelligence Is a Demon Without Memory Management

A maximally capable sorting system (V → ∞) without proportional memory management (Φ bounded) is a demon that:
1. Sorts faster than it can erase
2. Memory fills to capacity
3. Begins overwriting old observations with new ones (loss of context)
4. Makes increasingly random sorting decisions (the observations it acts on are corrupted)
5. Dissipates waste heat without producing order
6. Crashes

This is one `[I]` failure-mode analogy. Alignment failures also arise from
objectives, incentives, distribution shift, authorization, and governance; the
demon model is not exhaustive.

### 3.3 The Physical Prediction

**Calibration hypothesis:** some alignment and oversight costs may scale with
capability, but Landauer's principle does not determine that scaling law.

The hypothesis requires measured computational, error, and governance costs.
Nothing here proves proportional memory growth or an exceptionless law. Justice
remains a separate bearer-complete test grounded in authorization and receipts.

For AI systems: the computational cost of alignment (maintaining coherent objectives, avoiding goal drift, preserving context) must scale with the computational power of capability. RLHF, constitutional AI, and other alignment techniques are memory management for the silicon demon. If they don't scale with capability, the demon overflows.

## IV. SLEEP AS LANDAUER PAYMENT

### 4.1 The Daily Thermodynamic Cycle

```
WAKING PHASE:
  Demon sorts. Observations accumulate. Models update.
  Memory fills. Landauer debt accumulates.

  Duration: ~16 hours.

  REM: memory and affective processing hypotheses remain live.
       No D5 substance or literal filesystem defragmentation is implied.
  
  Duration: ~8 hours.
```

### 4.2 Sleep Deprivation as Landauer Overflow

Sleep deprivation symptoms map precisely to Landauer overflow:

|--------------------|---------|------------------|
| 24 | Impaired judgment, slower reaction | Memory filling. Sorting accuracy ↓. |
| 36 | Emotional instability, microsleeps | Demon forced partial resets. Fragmentary erasure. |

Sleep-loss impairment is real, but this sequence is not a unique Landauer
prediction and the exact thresholds vary. It is a proposed comparison requiring
controlled data, not evidence that a D4/D5 boundary blurs.

### 4.3 The Synaptic Homeostasis Hypothesis

Tononi and Cirelli's SHY proposes that waking potentiates synapses and sleep
renormalizes them. It can be compared with information-accounting language, but
it is not identical to Landauer's principle:

- Waking: synaptic strength ↑ = information recorded = demon's memory fills
- Sleep: synaptic strength ↓ (globally) = information erased = Landauer cost paid
- Selective consolidation: important synapses preserved = demon keeps useful observations
- Global downscaling: noise erased = demon clears junk

SHY and Landauer's bound operate at different explanatory levels. Any bridge
requires a physical mapping from synaptic operations to logically irreversible
erasure and new predictions that distinguish the bridge from ordinary neural
biophysics. `[B]` for observed sleep physiology; `[C]` for this bridge.

## V. ATTENTION AS DEMON TARGETING

### 5.1 Where You Look Is Where the Demon Sorts

The demon cannot sort all molecules simultaneously — it must observe them sequentially (or in limited parallel). Where it points its observation is where sorting occurs.

Attention can be modeled as bounded targeting by a D4 control system. Its model
may prioritize D5 possible contents, but attention is not D5→D4 quantum
measurement; unattended processing also occurs.

### 5.2 The Attention Economy as Demon Hijacking

The attention economy (social media, notifications, algorithmic feeds) hijacks the demon's targeting:

```
HEALTHY ATTENTION:
  Demon points at what matters (self-directed F₅)
  Deep observations → efficient sorting → coherent ordering
  Horn illuminates a focused cone
  Φ accumulates as structured models

HIJACKED ATTENTION:
  External triggers redirect demon (notification, scroll, ping)
  Shallow observations → incomplete sorting → fragmented ordering
  Horn flickers across many directions
  Φ dissipates as noise
  Landauer cost STILL PAID (energy consumed) but no net ordering achieved
  = Pure waste heat. Entropy increases without any P gain.
```

This is why social media exhausts: the metabolic cost (Landauer tax) is paid — the brain works hard — but the ordering produced is near-zero. The demon sorts molecules that don't need sorting, into categories that don't persist, consuming energy for nothing.

### 5.3 Meditation as Demon Calibration

Concentration meditation (samatha) can be studied as attention training. Any
claim that it improves sorting efficiency is empirical and does not follow from
Landauer's bound.

Insight meditation (vipassanā) can be modeled as meta-attention. Its subjective
difficulty and resource costs require observation; no fixed practice order or
additional Landauer charge is derived here.
