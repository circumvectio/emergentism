# MF-295: `c` BOUNDS CAUSAL SIGNALING, NOT THE MORAL CIRCLE

## Physical cone, evidence reach, and option cone are different types

**Evidence tiers:** `[A]` for the special-relativistic signal bound; `[I]` for
the Emergentist distinction among causal access, evidence reach, and option
cones; `[C]` for historical claims about communication and moral inclusion.

The former version of this essay said that communication technology widened a
communication-dependent cone and treated aggregate change in node power as an ethical
test. Both formulations are superseded. The physical light cone is fixed by
spacetime and `c`; information systems alter what evidence an agent receives
and what admissible futures it can model or coordinate. Ethics remains subject
to the Justice envelope and cannot be laundered through an aggregate gain.

## I. Three typed sets

For an event `e=(t0,x0)`, special relativity bounds future causal influence by:

```text
C+(e) = {(t,x) : t >= t0 and |x-x0| <= c(t-t0)}.
```

This is a physical relation. An agent, institution, message, or model does not
enlarge it.

For an agent `x` at time `t`, define an **evidence-reach set**:

```text
E_x(t) = records and signals physically available to x by t.
```

`E_x(t)` is limited by causal history but also by sensors, memory, bandwidth,
language, access, censorship, attention, and error. Better communication may
increase or improve `E_x(t)` without changing the physical cone.

Define the agent’s **option cone**:

```text
O_x(t) = admissible future histories x can model, rank, coordinate, and
         plausibly reach from its present means and constraints.
```

`O_x(t)` is a model-relative subset of physically admissible histories, not a
spacetime dimension. Two agents at the same event therefore have the same
physical cone while possessing different evidence reach and option cones.

## II. What communication can change

Writing, printing, telecommunication, and networked media can:

- transmit records sooner or with greater fidelity;
- preserve evidence across time;
- expose consequences that were previously hidden;
- coordinate action among distant agents;
- add modeled options or make an existing option reachable;
- amplify falsehood, overload attention, or narrow a model through filtering.

These are changes in `E_x(t)`, model quality, coordination, and reachable
options. Do not describe them as expansion of the physical causal cone.

Historical correlations between a medium and moral inclusion remain `[C]`.
They require comparison against rival explanations such as political struggle,
institutions, material surplus, literacy, coercion, and selection effects.
Visibility can enable care, spectacle, manipulation, fatigue, or repression;
it does not mechanically produce moral progress.

## III. Consequence and responsibility

Causal unknowability, actual ignorance, reasonable foreseeability, and willful
blindness are not equivalent. A just evaluation asks at least:

- What consequences were represented or reasonably representable?
- Which evidence was available, withheld, distorted, or deliberately avoided?
- Who authorized and performed the act?
- Who paid and who benefited?
- What was irreversible?
- Who held custody and could contest, revoke, or exit?
- What outcome was actually receipted?

An unavailable fact may limit prediction. It does not automatically erase a
duty of care, especially where an actor created the uncertainty, refused
ordinary inquiry, or imposed risk on another bearer.

The moral test is not `sum(delta P) > 0`. Aggregate benefit may never compensate
for destroying one side of a coupled relation. Using the current value owner:

```text
Moral(a)     iff delta W_H > 0 and delta W_i >= 0 and J(a;i,H)
Ethical(a)   iff delta W_i > 0 and delta W_H >= 0 and J(a;i,H)
Syntropic(a) iff delta W_i > 0 and delta W_H > 0 and J(a;i,H)
```

The Justice predicate `J` keeps consent, custody, payer, beneficiary,
reversibility, consequence bearer, contest, and exit visible.

## IV. Model-mediated future influence

When new evidence changes a modeled future, it may change present selection:

```text
represented alternatives -> ranking -> commitment -> D4 action -> outcome receipt
```

This is the Soul Loop’s defensible future-directed relation. Present model
tokens represent possible future content; the future does not send a physical
signal backward. Communication can enlarge the represented or reachable option
set while all physical influence remains inside ordinary causal structure.

## V. Tests and kill criteria

1. **Type test:** hold spacetime location fixed and vary information access. If
   represented alternatives change while the physical cone does not, the
   evidence/option distinction earns operational use.
2. **Intervention test:** provide a verified consequence record while holding
   preferences and means as stable as possible. If present action distributions
   do not change, the proposed model-mediated effect fails in that domain.
3. **Coordination test:** add a communication channel. Distinguish added options
   from options merely made cheaper or more reachable.
4. **Historical test:** compare moral-circle change against rival causal models.
   A loose chronology does not establish the communication hypothesis.
5. **Justice test:** reject any evaluation that hides a harmed bearer behind a
   positive aggregate.

If evidence reach and option-cone measures cannot be operationalized distinctly,
the distinction remains pedagogical `[I]`, not an empirical theory.
