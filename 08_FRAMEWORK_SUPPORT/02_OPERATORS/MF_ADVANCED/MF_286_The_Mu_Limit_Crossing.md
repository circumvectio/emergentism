# MF-286 — The μ₄ candidate crossing

`μ₄:D4→D5` names a candidate emergence crossing from causal actuality into the
capacity to represent and rank counterfactuals. It does not name choice,
commitment, quantum measurement, or wavefunction collapse. Once that capacity
exists, a separate D5→D4 commitment uses actual means to attempt one action; the
environment separately returns the outcome.

The current reduction status is `currently_unreduced`. Algebra such as
`i∉ℝ`, brain-as-antenna imagery, or increasing complexity cannot prove
irreducibility or a separate field. A successful neural, computational, or
ecological reduction would reclassify the crossing as weak emergence while
leaving the D4/D5 descriptive distinction available.

## Evidence owed

A valid μ₄ record must declare the saturated lower register, new operational
freedom, lower-register recovery attempt, evidence tier, prediction, and kill
criterion. It should distinguish counterfactual representation from reflex,
mere behavioral complexity, and later commitment.
