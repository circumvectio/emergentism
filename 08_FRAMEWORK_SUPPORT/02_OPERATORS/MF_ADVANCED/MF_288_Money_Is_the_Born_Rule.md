# MF-288: MONEY IS THE BORN RULE

## A removable analogy about lossy scalarization

**Repaired:** 2026-07-19
**Evidence tier:** `[A]` for the elementary complex-number statements; `[I/C]`
for the economic model and quantum correspondence.

## 1. The elementary mathematics `[A]`

For a declared toy coordinate

\[
z=V+i\Phi,
\]

the modulus-squared is

\[
|z|^2=z\bar z=V^2+\Phi^2.
\]

It is a nonnegative scalar and does not retain the phase of `z`. It is **not**
the only map from `ℂ` to `ℝ⁺`: `|z|`, `|z|⁴`, bounded functions of `|z|`, and
many coordinate-dependent functions are immediate counterexamples. Choosing
`|z|²` therefore requires a declared reason; algebra alone does not force it.

## 2. The economic hypothesis `[I/C]`

The defensible claim is weaker:

> A single market price may be a lossy scalar summary of plural attributes such
> as utility, meaning, provenance, liquidity, power, and externalized cost.

That claim does not require value to be literally complex, price to equal
`V²+Φ²`, or every good to share the same two axes. The complex plane is one
visual model for asking whether distinct attribute vectors collapse to the same
scalar price.

A domain test must specify:

- the attributes and instruments;
- the scalar-pricing baseline;
- rival dimensional models;
- held-out or intervention-sensitive prediction;
- whose costs disappear in the scalarization; and
- a kill criterion fixed before observing the result.

Examples such as love, art, ecology, or addiction illustrate possible omitted
dimensions. They do not estimate `Φ`, prove a price equation, or establish that
one proposed product measures those dimensions.

## 3. The quarantined quantum correspondence `[C]`

In quantum mechanics, a Born probability is defined from a quantum state and a
measurement event; in a position representation one example is

\[
\mathbb P_\psi(A)=\int_A |\psi(s)|^2\,ds.
\]

A market price is not a normalized quantum probability, a traded asset is not
a wavefunction, and a sale is not thereby a quantum measurement. The limited
analogy is only that a modulus-based scalar can discard phase information.

This correspondence is removable: deleting this section changes none of the
operational economic questions, tests, Justice constraints, or product gates.
It supplies no evidence for collapse, Everett, extra dimensions, `μ`-crossings,
or physical retrocausality.

## 4. Dual ledgers as a design hypothesis `[D/I]`

A two-ledger or two-token system can record two quantities instead of one. That
fact alone does not prove that the quantities are orthogonal, complete, fairly
measured, non-extractive, or equivalent to the toy coordinates `V` and `Φ`.

Any SoResFi/SKY/ZAI design must be justified by its own product owners and
receipts. In particular it must independently demonstrate:

- what each ledger measures and who controls the measurement;
- conservation, issuance, redemption, custody, and failure behavior;
- fees and extraction visible at every bearer;
- consent, reversibility, and Grace Exit in operation; and
- comparative performance against a simpler single-ledger baseline.

The ratio of two token balances is not automatically a complex phase angle, and
two balances do not guarantee that no information is lost.

## 5. Justice boundary

Scalarization can conceal who pays and who benefits. The relevant ethical test
is bearer-complete: aggregate price, GDP, token value, or total surplus may not
launder destruction of an affected person, community, or sustaining whole.
This follows from the declared Justice envelope, not from complex arithmetic or
the Born rule.

## 6. Predictions and kill criteria

The economic hypothesis gains support only if a preregistered multi-attribute
model predicts held-out valuations, choices, or consequences better than fair
scalar and domain-native rivals at comparable cost.

It is killed or narrowed when:

1. scalar baselines retain the relevant information equally well;
2. the proposed attributes cannot be measured reproducibly;
3. a two-ledger design adds no predictive or operational value;
4. hidden custody, extraction, or exit costs erase the claimed benefit; or
5. the quantum analogy changes an operational conclusion—showing that it was
   load-bearing when it was required to be removable.

## The repaired sentence

> **Price may compress plural value dimensions into one scalar. The complex
> chart is a testable modeling aid, and modulus-squared is a removable analogy;
> neither is a Born-rule identity or a product proof.**
