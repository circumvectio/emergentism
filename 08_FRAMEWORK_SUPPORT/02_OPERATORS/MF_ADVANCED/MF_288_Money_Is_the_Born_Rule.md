# MF-288: KINTSUGI TOMBSTONE

## Price is not the Born rule

The former essay at this path treated market price as if it were the quantum
Born rule and then treated a particular financial-product design as the
correction. That correspondence is withdrawn from active Emergentist doctrine.
The pre-repair text remains recoverable through Git history; this stable path
records why it cannot remain load-bearing.

## What broke

1. A market price is not a quantum probability measure. Writing a modeled value
   as a complex number does not make a transaction a quantum measurement.
2. The map `z -> |z|^2` is only one many-to-one scalar map. It is not the unique
   algebraically consistent projection from a multidimensional value space to a
   non-negative price.
3. The loss of information under a scalar summary does not establish that the
   discarded variables are complex amplitudes, physical phases, or D5 entities.
4. A product, token, exit gate, or protocol cannot serve as evidence for the
   worldview that was used to design it.
5. Price, gross output, and aggregate gain cannot by themselves establish moral
   worth. Distribution, consent, custody, reversibility, payer, beneficiary, and
   consequence bearer remain visible under the Justice envelope.

## What survives

`[I]` A scalar price can be an incomplete proxy for plural value. That modest
claim can be tested without quantum vocabulary. Let a richer state be `x` and a
reported scalar be `p = g(x)`. Information is lost whenever distinct admissible
states `x1 != x2` satisfy `g(x1) = g(x2)`. Which distinctions matter is an
empirical and normative question, not an algebraic revelation.

Any proposed two-axis or higher-dimensional value model must therefore declare:

- the operational meaning and measurement procedure for each coordinate;
- rival aggregators and ranking counterexamples;
- predictive tests against simpler scalar models;
- the people who pay and benefit;
- consent, custody, reversibility, contest, and exit;
- a kill criterion under which the added coordinates are removed.
