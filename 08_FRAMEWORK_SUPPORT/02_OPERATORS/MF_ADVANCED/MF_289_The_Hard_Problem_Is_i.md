it cannot prove that experience is physically irreducible, that matter is a
subset of consciousness, that qualia are complex phase, or that minds perform
quantum measurement. Representation inside `ℂ` is not ontological containment.

counterfactual content. First-person and third-person descriptions can remain
methodologically distinct while researchers test whether and how one recovers
the other. Until a fair recovery succeeds or fails, the status is
`currently_unreduced`, not a theorem of dualism, idealism, or panpsychism.

operation and no D4/D5 type.
