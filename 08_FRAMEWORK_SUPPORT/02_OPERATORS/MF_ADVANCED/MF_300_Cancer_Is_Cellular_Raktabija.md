# Cancer and Raktabīja — Analogy Boundary

> **[金] Crack:** the former note called cancer a D5 standing wave and treated a
> mythic capture pattern as biological mechanism. **Repair:** cancer is an
> actual biological process; the analogy transfers no mechanism or treatment.

Tumors, cells, mutations, tissues, immune responses, measurements, and clinical
outcomes are `D4 = actual`. Possible disease courses and treatment alternatives
represented by actual models are `D5 = merely_possible`.

The Raktabīja story may illuminate an `[I]` pattern: an intervention can select
for resistant variants or be absorbed into the process it opposes. In oncology,
that intuition is already addressed by precise concepts such as clonal
selection, heterogeneity, resistance, microenvironment, and evolutionary
dynamics. Those established mechanisms—not the myth—must carry any claim.

No Egregoreotype, consciousness, moral evil, or autonomous objective function
is inferred. A predictive crosswalk must specify variables, comparator,
intervention, clinical/experimental endpoint, source evidence, and a result
that differs from standard evolutionary-oncology models. Without that
discriminator, the correspondence is pedagogical only and must not guide care.
