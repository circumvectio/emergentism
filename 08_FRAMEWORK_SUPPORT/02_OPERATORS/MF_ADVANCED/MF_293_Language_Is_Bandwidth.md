# MF-293 — Language as a Costly Channel

**Status:** Complex-axis identity retired on 2026-07-20.

The former paper called meanings D5 complex phases, physical signals D4 real
carriers, mathematics lossless, and culture a phase-reconstruction engine.
Those are not results of information theory or linguistics.

Original Git blob: `d9a6862386df8249bda85a30469d46038ca5db03`.

What survives `[B/I]`: communication uses physical carriers; shared codes and
context influence reconstruction; every channel has capacity, noise, cost, and
failure modes. A Rosetta translation may compare those variables with model and
receipt gaps, but no language transmits meaning or “phase” perfectly.

*A channel can carry a message without carrying the sender's mind.*
