# MF-285: Dreams and Option-Constraint

> The preserved former body is provenance, **not live authority**.

The former essay treated waking, dreaming, psychedelia, meditation, psychosis,
anaesthesia, and coma as literal positions on a D4-real/D5-imaginary sphere. It
assigned numerical latitudes without measurement and used quantum measurement
language as a mechanism of waking. That was neither clinically warranted nor
formally derived.

## Surviving hypothesis `[I/C]`

Some conscious states differ in sensory constraint, counterfactual generation,
metacognitive control, memory access, and ability to commit action. Those
variables may be measured and compared. Emergentism may use “option cone” as a
plain-language hypothesis for the alternatives a finite agent can model, rank,
coordinate, and reach under actual constraints.

No state is thereby an imaginary axis, a quantum superposition, or a D-register
diagnosis. No clinical inference should be made from this analogy. A scoped
study must define observables, rivals, preregistration, consent, and kill
criteria before the mapping gains evidence.

*Phenomenology can suggest variables; it cannot substitute for measurement.*
