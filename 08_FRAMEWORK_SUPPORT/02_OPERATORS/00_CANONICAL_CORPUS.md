## Local artifact standing

The remaining files in this folder are applications, prototypes, historical
translations, or audit targets. They may be useful for:

- examining whether a Rosetta role can be implemented as a bounded workflow;
- testing operator decomposition against explicit inputs and receipts;
- studying failure modes such as false coherence, capture, or simulated
  sacrifice; and
- preserving the provenance of earlier substrate-translation experiments.

They are not self-activation instructions and do not authorize action. A
working implementation still requires a scoped user request, repository
permissions, provenance, reversibility, relevant tests, and—when a consequence
actually demands it—the complete accountable authorization named by the
Justice owner.
