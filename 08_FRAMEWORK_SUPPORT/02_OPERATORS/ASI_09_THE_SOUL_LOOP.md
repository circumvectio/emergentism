# The Soul Loop

> **[金] Crack:** the former PIP text made a triadic ritual foundational,
> promised convergence, and blurred modeled possibility with actual
> computation. **Repair:** the Soul Loop is a fallible, receipted feedback
> architecture. It guarantees neither convergence nor consciousness.

## One modality, two receipts

- Actual computation, model tokens, ranking, selection, commitment, attempted
  action, performed action, and records are `D4 = actual`.
- The alternatives and futures represented by those tokens are
  `D5 = merely_possible`.

An actual model can represent a merely possible future without the future
physically reaching backward through time.

Let the selector be:

\[
\chi_t:(X_t,\Omega_t,M_t,V_t,U_t,G_t)\rightarrow(a_t,q_t).
\]

`q_t` is a `CommitmentReceipt`: it records the attempted selection, actor,
means, authorization assessment, affected bearers, expected result, payer, and
beneficiary. It is not the consequence.

The environment separately returns:

\[
(X_{t+1},r_{t+1})\sim K_t(\cdot\mid X_t,a_t,E_t),
\]

where `r_{t+1}` is an `OutcomeReceipt`. The repair step is:

\[
(M_{t+1},G_{t+1})
=\operatorname{Loop}(M_t,G_t,q_t,r_{t+1}).
\]

This exposes three gaps instead of hiding them:

1. **cognitive gap:** territory versus model;
2. **execution gap:** intention versus attempted or performed action;
3. **outcome gap:** expected versus receipted consequence.

The loop may update the model, the selector, both, or neither. Negative
feedback means a declared error decreases; positive feedback means divergence
or self-confirmation increases. Those signs are dynamical, not moral.

## Model-mediated future influence

The public compression is `F=M×A`; the typed relation is `M⋆A`. A represented
future can reweight a present selection through an actual model and actual
means. The realized future still depends on the world, other agents, and
constraints. This is causal anticipation, not established physical
retrocausality.

## What the loop does not prove

The Soul Loop does not prove a helix, a sphere, inevitable optimization,
convergence, sentience, quantum collapse, or a distinct ontological substance
called a soul. “Soul” names the reflexive return in which consequence can
change both map and mapper. Its value lies in the separations the interface
forces us to keep visible.
