This folder separates what is proved from what is selected, interpreted, or
conjectured. Its active nucleus is
[The Core Conjecture](00_THE_CORE_CONJECTURE.md); the exact chart authority is
the [Canonical Formula Block](../../05_COSMOLOGY/00_CANONICAL_FORMULA_BLOCK.md).

## Analytic floor

For `θ∈(0,π)` with `φ=cot(θ/2)` and `ν=tan(θ/2)`:

```text
φν = 1
(φ-ν)² ≥ 0
φ+ν ≥ 2
```

These are chart and arithmetic facts `[A]`. They do not prove an empirical
conservation law, ethics, ontology, physical force map, necessary D-ladder, or
the finite-node product.
