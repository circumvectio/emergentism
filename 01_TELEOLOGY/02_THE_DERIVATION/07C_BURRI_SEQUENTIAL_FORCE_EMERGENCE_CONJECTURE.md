# Burri Sequential Force-Emergence Conjecture

> **Provenance boundary.** [B] This records Yves R. Burri’s dated first-party
> proposal of this four-row test protocol within the Emergentism corpus. It
> asserts no global historical priority, first conception, discovery, ownership
> of physics, or evidential support.

## The question

The motivating question is simple and serious:

> If gravity can be an emergent macroscopic law, emergent from what?

Emergentism’s proposed answer is not an announced microphysical substrate. It
is a four-part research wager: reality may become intelligible through a
selected sequence of freedoms, and each physical interaction may have one
non-arbitrary role-affinity with one such freedom. The proposal has content
only if it recovers the relevant native physics and loses against a better
placement or an ordinary no-placement account.

The conjecture begins with four selected registers:

| Register | Native Emergentist interface | Candidate question |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | distinction and integrated duality | Does one interaction require a D1-specific distinction map that recovers its native theory? |
| D2 | relation and configuration | Does one interaction require a D2-specific configuration map that recovers its native theory? |
| D3 | probability-bearing quantum state | Does one interaction require a D3-specific quantum-state map that recovers its native theory? |
| D4 | causal spacetime actuality | Does one interaction require a D4-specific actuality/spacetime map that recovers its native theory? |

The conjecture therefore names a possible answer to “emergent from what?”:
from prior typed freedoms plus a newly declared, independently testable
interface at each declared
crossing. It does not identify those interfaces with particles, fields,
entropy, information, or a pre-existing force by assertion.

## What “sequential” means here

The proposal now separates two independently killable claims.

### SEWG-MAP — structural assignment

[C] At the first layer, sequential means an ordered candidate assignment:

~~~
π = (π(D1), π(D2), π(D3), π(D4)).
~~~

This layer does **not** say that a D-register is a physical substance or that
it caused the next register. The current 24-permutation protocol tests this
structural map.

### SEWG-SERIAL — joint emergence, one freedom at a time

[B] On 2026-08-21, Yves R. Burri stated the stronger founder conjecture in this
exact corpus form:

> Reality emerges one freedom after another, and a distinct interaction mode
> emerges with each new freedom: strong, electromagnetic, weak, then gravity.

This is a named `[C]` extension, not a conclusion smuggled into SEWG-MAP. Let
`Σ_i` name an ordinary candidate stage description and `𝔣_i` the interaction
mode newly available at its transition:

~~~
StageStep_i : Σ_(i-1) → (Σ_i, D_i, 𝔣_i) or NoStage

π_SEWG = (
  StageStep_1 ↦ (D1,S),
  StageStep_2 ↦ (D2,E),
  StageStep_3 ↦ (D3,W),
  StageStep_4 ↦ (D4,G)
)
~~~

“With” is load-bearing. The force neither manufactures the new freedom nor is
identical to it. The candidate description jointly specifies a register-role
annotation and a distinct interaction grammar; it asserts neither ontic
creation nor causal production. `D_i` is an annotation on the proposed stage,
not an object produced by `StageStep_i`, and `StageStep_i` is not yet a
time-evolution law. A later stage must recover the valid lower-stage theory in
its accepted domain.

The corresponding founder display is:

~~~
Σ_0  ⇝  [D1 ∥ S]  ⇝  [D2 ∥ E]  ⇝  [D3 ∥ W]  ⇝  [D4 ∥ G]
~~~

`Σ_0` is not silently identified with numeric zero, a quantum vacuum, or the
Titan ground mark. It is a placeholder whose carrier remains to be specified.
The [Titan Limit-Crossing Process Conjecture](../../05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/58_TITAN_LIMIT_CROSSING_PROCESS_CONJECTURE.md)
is a research neighbor, not a derivation. Connecting them would require a
record-based admission packet:

~~~
AdmitSeedBridge : {
  finity_witness_ref,
  ordinary_carrier,
  transition_law,
  recovery_map,
  kill
} → StageDescription[Σ_0] or NoBridge
~~~

The `FinityWitness` neither instantiates nor causes the ordinary carrier. Until
every field is supplied, `NoBridge` is the honest default.

SEWG-SERIAL can be read first as an **ontological dependency order**. A literal
cosmic-time reading is a stronger subcase and must additionally state an
initial condition, energy or temperature regime, order parameter, scale
history, recovery of accepted cosmology and particle physics, and a held-out
observable. Electroweak unification and the role of gravity in the earliest
standard cosmological descriptions are direct counterpressures, not details to
explain away after the fact.

### SEWG-MECH — horizon-debt pre-mechanism admission template

[C] The founder mnemonic is:

> Every new freedom incurs a new consistency debt. A physical force is the
> candidate law by which reality pays that debt.

The testable admission claim is narrower: for one specified candidate row, a
newly declared freedom may expose a predeclared closure problem; a native
interaction law may then be tested as one candidate resolution. This is not yet
a physical mechanism.

For stage `i`, freeze an unresolved boundary or obstruction `O_i` using only
the lower-stage language. The transition must be a minimal extension that both
opens the new freedom and supplies a consistency witness:

~~~
O_i := obstruction(Σ_(i-1))

StageStep_i : (Σ_(i-1),O_i)
            → (Σ_i,D_i,𝔣_i,C_i) or NoStage

C_i : consistency witness for composition, transport, transition, or
      conservation in the newly opened freedom
~~~

`𝔣_i` earns the word “force” only when its native physical law implements
`C_i` without being inserted into `O_i` by definition. The mechanism predicts
a provisional four-row debt pattern:

| Stage | Newly exposed freedom | Candidate consistency debt | Candidate physical payer | Native recovery owed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | distinction and bounded sectors | what preserves lawful sector identity and closure? | strong interaction / confinement structure | QCD color and non-Abelian gauge structure |
| D2 | relation and configuration | what makes local relational or phase comparisons coherent? | electromagnetic connection | U(1), Maxwell/QED, and EM-specific observables |
| D3 | probability-bearing quantum state | what lawfully changes chiral/flavor alternatives? | weak transition structure | electroweak theory and weak-specific observables |
| D4 | causal spacetime actuality | what makes localized energy, causal history, and geometry mutually consistent? | gravity | GR and valid low-energy EFT |

The phrases in the middle columns are questions, not derivations. Confinement
does not follow from the word distinction; electromagnetism does not follow
from relation; the weak interaction does not follow from probability; gravity
does not follow from actuality. The required bridge is the native-theory
recovery, obstruction calculation, and held-out discriminator.

This mechanism gives “emergent from what?” a sharper conjectural answer:
from a lower description reaching a reproducible closure failure, followed by
the minimal new freedom and its consistency law. At the agent-scale D4/D5 seam,
an unresolved boundary may be represented as the local `○̂` of TLC-01a. Earlier
physical stages need no mind or representation; their `O_i` must be stated as
an ordinary mathematical or physical obstruction.

The local `○̂` is neither an `O_i` in the four physical rows nor evidence for
SEWG-MECH. If `O_i` cannot be stated without the target interaction's own
objects or laws, `StageStep_i(Σ_(i-1),O_i) = NoStage` and the row fails
admission.

SEWG-MECH fails if no lower-stage obstruction can be specified independently,
if the same generic consistency story fits all 24 permutations, if a smaller
native theory resolves the obstruction without the D-map, or if the alleged
new force law is merely copied into the bridge assumptions.

## The four physical candidates

Let the physical interaction labels be:

~~~
S = strong interaction / QCD
E = electromagnetic interaction
W = weak interaction within electroweak theory
G = gravity / general relativity with valid low-energy EFT
~~~

Every permutation of S, E, W, and G across D1–D4 is initially admissible.
The historically motivating assignment is:

~~~
D1 → S     D2 → E     D3 → W     D4 → G
~~~

It is called **SEWG** in the companion preregistration. It is an
origin-reference only, not a default, leading candidate, or result.

This matters because the prior universal force-bijection was formally
refuted. [S] The active W7 owner permits only independently killable
role-affinity legs; it forbids identity, exclusive residence, causal
generation, and derivation. The refuted result DF-10 remains refuted. A
successful row would at most earn a narrower crosswalk under its declared
native-theory and measurement conditions.

## D3 is the quantum decision point

D3 is the probability-bearing quantum-state register. That makes it a natural
place to ask whether one force has a distinctive quantum role. It does **not**
make the weak interaction the winner merely because all Standard Model gauge
interactions use quantum field theory.

[C] A serious D3-to-weak proposal must predeclare a weak-specific package,
such as:

1. left-chiral and parity-violating electroweak coupling;
2. charged-current flavor transition; and
3. a precise held-out asymmetry, selection rule, decay branching feature, or
   other observable.

It must then show why a matched QCD or QED map does not recover that same
feature. General references to amplitudes, superposition, state vectors,
gauge fields, quantum measurement, or “the quantum realm” are insufficient.
They earn D3-Q = FAIL because they do not discriminate weak interaction from
the other quantum theories.

Gravity also cannot be excluded from D3 by definition: low-energy
quantum-gravity effective field theory remains a live native-theory boundary.
Likewise, the low-energy distinction between weak and electromagnetic behavior
cannot be treated as proof of two primitive cosmic stages; electroweak
unification is a fixed rival.

## Gravity and the current entropy result

[B] Dorau and Much’s paper, [From Quantum Relative Entropy to the Semiclassical
Einstein Equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24491), provides a precise
component result relevant to the question. In its stated setting of quantum
fields on a bifurcate Killing horizon and with the Bekenstein–Hawking
entropy–area law assumed, the paper relates quantum relative entropy to energy
flux and recovers the semiclassical Einstein equations. The published
[Physical Review Letters record](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/lmq8-nsty)
is the authoritative publication record.

[A/B] That is a valuable thermodynamic and quantum-information route into
semiclassical gravity under named assumptions. [I] It is a possible D3-to-D4
mechanism neighbor: quantum-state information and energy flow are connected to
geometric response. It does **not** identify the microscopic degrees of
freedom, prove that gravity emerges from a named substrate, select G as the
D4 row, establish the four-row order, or explain the origin of the other
interactions. The conjecture must not borrow more than the paper supplies.

## What would count as an answer

The companion [24-permutation preregistration](../../03_METHODOLOGY/03_PREREGISTRATIONS/05_W7_D1_D4_FORCE_ASSIGNMENT_PREREG.md)
requires every candidate to supply:

1. a declared D-source interface;
2. native physical objects and equations;
3. a bridge map not defined by the desired outcome;
4. accepted-domain recovery with a stated tolerance;
5. a comparison against all other permutations and a no-placement rival;
6. one held-out, force-specific observable; and
7. independent replication.

Until then, “emergent from what?” has an honest answer at two levels:

- **Conceptual answer [C]:** from a serially typed emergence of freedoms, with a
  distinct interaction mode becoming available at each transition, if the
  required carriers and maps are non-arbitrary.
- **Scientific answer [D]:** unknown. No candidate has yet earned a mechanism,
  recovery, prediction, or independent result.

SEWG-SERIAL earns consideration only if it adds something the unordered map
does not. At minimum it must yield all three:

1. **stage novelty:** a later interaction mode cannot be reconstructed in the
   frozen earlier-stage language without adding the claimed new freedom;
2. **downward recovery:** removing a later stage preserves the accepted valid
   domain of every earlier stage; and
3. **order discrimination:** at least one held-out result differs between SEWG,
   another permutation, a many-to-many map, and the native no-ladder account.

predict a held-out discriminator, that candidate fails. If every candidate
