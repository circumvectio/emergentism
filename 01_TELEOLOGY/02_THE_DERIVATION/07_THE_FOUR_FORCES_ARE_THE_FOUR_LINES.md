# Four Forces / Four Lines — Superseded Correspondence

The former body mapped four algebraic lines to the four accepted physical
interactions and treated F5 as a corresponding D5 force slot. It is preserved
in Git history at the blob above and is no longer active doctrine.

## What broke

- The assignments were internally inconsistent: the same line received more
  than one force across the argument.
- Four-to-four cardinality and symbolic resemblance supplied no novel physical
  discriminator.
- `(φ-ν)²≥0` is an inequality, not a time evolution or weak-interaction
  mechanism.
- `-log(φν)=0` in the reciprocal chart is constant and does not derive gravity.
- A bare `S²` construction does not recover the Standard Model gauge structure.
- A force↔dimension bijection is neither required by the D-register scaffold nor
  evidenced by the existence of four accepted interactions.

## What survives

The correspondence can remain historical hypothesis-generation `[C]`. The
analytic reciprocal chart remains `[A]`; the D0–D6 scaffold remains `[I]`; each
candidate `μ`-crossing keeps its own prediction and kill criterion; F5 remains an
optional teleological/ordering name `[I/C]`, not a physical interaction.

Any future physics revival requires a novel quantitative discriminator,
comparison with established theory, and independent reproduction. Numerical or
mythic fit cannot reopen the claim by itself.
