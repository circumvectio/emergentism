## Premises and result `[A]`

For positive real `phi` and `nu`,

```text
(phi-nu)^2 >= 0
phi^2 + nu^2 >= 2*phi*nu
```

On the separately declared reciprocal chart where `phi*nu=1`, AM-GM gives

```text
phi + nu >= 2
```

with equality if and only if `phi=nu=1`. Equivalently, the selected balance
score `B:=2/(phi+nu)` has maximum one at the equality case.

The derivation contains no time variable, state-transition law, probability,
physical unit, Hamiltonian, causal mechanism, agent, transfer, or bearer.

- movement or attraction toward the equality case;
- physical stability, energy, conservation, or cost of existence;
- that reality instantiates the chart or sphere;
- `P_node:=Phi*V` or any other empirical aggregator;
- that extraction is irrational, unstable, or immoral;

Calling `phi+nu` a Hamiltonian or cost creates a new model and requires units,
dynamics, data, rivals, and kill criteria. Calling a transfer just or unjust
requires the independent bearer-complete Justice envelope.

can be reported transparently. That makes the result a useful analytic ruler.
It is a compass only after users state what the coordinates measure and test
whether the ruler predicts anything in the target domain.
