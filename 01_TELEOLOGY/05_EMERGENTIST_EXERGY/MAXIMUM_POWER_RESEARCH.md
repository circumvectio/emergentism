# Maximum power, ektropism, and machine capability

## Executive finding

[I] Emergentism has entered an existing, substantial research field: **how
energy availability, organization, control, and selection interact to sustain
and extend a system's capabilities**. The field contains related questions,
competing answers, and several incompatible meanings of “power.” Finding this
neighborhood is valuable; calling every neighbor confirmation would discard
the distinctions that make the neighborhood useful.

Five findings carry this review:

1. [B/I] Lotka and Howard T. Odum supply a serious energetic selection
   hypothesis, not an established universal fourth law. Conditions on resources,
   constraints, and the unit of selection are load-bearing. See
   [Lotka 1922a](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.8.6.147) and
   [Odum and Pinkerton 1955](https://canadiancor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Odum-Pinkerton-MPP2.pdf).
2. [B] **Schertel explicitly invokes Felix Auerbach's ektropism.** The inspected
   English text connects energy, imagination, and magical agency. That is a
   documented textual connection, not evidence that magic works or that
   Schertel derived MPP. See the [book, printed pp. 73, 79–81](https://tradition.st/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Ernst-Schertel-Magic.pdf).
3. [B] A nearby philosophical comparison already exists: Timothy McWhirter's
   2025 book explicitly compares **Odum and Nietzsche**. Its existence is
   verified; this review inspected the publisher's description and contents,
   not the entire argument. [Publisher record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-031-80622-3).
4. [I] For Emergentism's concern with **retaining reachable alternatives**, the
   closest formal neighbors include empowerment, requisite variety, and
   conditional AI power-seeking results—not just metabolic power. They count
   control or options, not joules. See
   [Klyubin, Polani and Nehaniv 2005](https://doi.org/10.1109/CEC.2005.1554676) and
   [Turner et al. 2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/c26820b8a4c1b3c2aa868d6d57e14a79-Paper.pdf).
5. [C] The machine research opportunity is **copyable policies coupled to
   material service-and-renewal networks**. Copying a policy, delivering a
   useful task, and reproducing the infrastructure that supports it are three
   different achievements. This is a testable extension, not a new law already
   established by the literature.

critical readings, and unresolved bibliography. This dossier does not claim

## 2. The historical map

### Lotka: selection among physically permitted processes

[B] Lotka's first 1922 paper connects selection to available-energy use,
material circulation, and organic mass while explicitly conditioning the
proposal on unused resources and constraints. Resource scarcity changes the
case for economical use. The claim cannot be reduced to “the largest burner
always wins.” [Primary text](https://www.sustainable.soltechdesigns.com/lotka1922.pdf).

[B] The second paper asks what additional information selection supplies when
thermodynamic prohibitions alone do not specify the course of events. Its
opening discusses rates and guidance, including Ostwald. This is not itself
evidence for a new interaction. [Lotka 1922b](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.8.6.151).

[I] Three questions must remain separate: **what is permitted**, **what dynamics
produce**, and **which variants persist**. A selection rule need not be an
extra physical force. Neither paper, as inspected here, establishes
Emergentism's D5-force conjecture.

### Howard T. Odum: converters, feedback, ecosystems, accounting

[B] Odum and Pinkerton's 1955 analysis connects rate, loading, efficiency and
self-maintenance. Importantly, the linear irreversible-thermodynamic argument
on printed p. 335 is near equilibrium; its extension to living and social
systems is a further proposal. Their model-specific intermediate optimum is
not a universal 50% efficiency theorem. The paper also discusses slow,
efficient operation when resources are limited. [Primary text](https://canadiancor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Odum-Pinkerton-MPP2.pdf).

[I] The larger Odum program must be read as several projects, not one sentence:
measurement of ecological flows; models of self-organization; accounting for
upstream support; and interpretations of social futures. The
[1957 Silver Springs study](https://doi.org/10.2307/1948571),
[1988 transformity paper](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.242.4882.1132), and
[Environmental Accounting](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en?isbn=9780471114420&option=com_eshop&title=Environmental+Accounting&view=product)
require different tests. The catalogue flags the books and studies not yet
fully read.

[B/I] **Howard T. Odum and Eugene P. Odum are not interchangeable authors.**
Eugene's 1969 ecosystem-development paper is a related succession framework;
Howard and Elisabeth C. Odum's later descent proposals form another branch.
Neither is simply the 1955 converter calculation enlarged.
[Eugene Odum 1969](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.164.3877.262),
[Howard and Elisabeth Odum 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2004.05.012).

### Auerbach and Schertel: a documented neighboring branch

[B] The digitized-book record identifies Felix Auerbach's 1910
*Ektropismus oder die physikalische Theorie des Lebens*. Metadata is verified;
its full argument has not been read in this review.
[Digitized edition record](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/Felix_Auerbach_Ektropismus_oder_die_physikalische?id=N1M_AAAAIAAJ).

[B] The inspected English **Schertel** names Auerbach (pp. 73, 79–81) while
attributing agency to imagination (pp. 69–73). Translation metadata was not
recoverable. Collation with the German original remains owed.
[Inspected book](https://tradition.st/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Ernst-Schertel-Magic.pdf).

[I] The justified connection is **Schertel's uptake of an energetic vocabulary
in a metaphysical account of imagination and action**. It is not a demonstration
of maximum useful power, measured exergy, psychokinesis, or a physical coupling
between possibility and actuality. The same word can do different work in a
metaphysical argument and an engineering calculation.

[B] Exact Lotka/Odum searches returned no hits in that extraction—not proof
of historical independence. No direct Auerbach–Lotka influence chain
was established. Auerbach's appearance elsewhere in Lotka's bibliography is
not sufficient unless it concerns the relevant energetic claim.

## 3. The quantities that must not collapse

[I] This is the central disambiguation table. Each row needs its own boundary,
unit, measurement method, and horizon; no common numerical exchange rate has
been established.

| Quantity | Unit or representation | Question answered |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | J | What energy is transferred or stored? |
| Thermodynamic exergy | J, relative to a reference environment | How much useful work is physically available? |
| Useful power | W = J/s | At what rate is the defined useful work delivered? |
| Thermodynamic entropy production | W/K | At what rate do irreversible processes produce entropy? |
| Emergy / empower | Emergy: solar emjoules (sej); empower: sej/s, under a stated reference basis | What upstream energetic support is attributed under this accounting? |
| Path information entropy | bits or nats; k_B-scaled constructions have J/K, with physical interpretation dependent on the model | How diverse or uncertain are modeled histories? |
| Empowerment | bits | How much control can actions exert over future sensed outcomes? |
| Variational free energy | nats under the usual log convention | What bound on model surprise is minimized? |
| Reproductive success / growth | Defined descendants, surviving offspring, or rate | Which entities reproduce and persist under selection? |
| Task effectiveness | Goal-specific result | Did the intended task succeed? |
| Emergentist Exergy | Default: typed VMO/SKA profile, no scalar unit | Which intended trajectories have supported means and correction? |
| Justice / legitimacy | Explicit constraints and judgments | Which actions are acceptable to affected bearers? |

## 4. Competing explanations, not a choir

[I] The catalogue contains the source-level details. The following distinctions
are the comparisons that matter for Emergentism.

| Family | Representative sources | Difference from MPP |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum entropy production | Callen; Prigogine | Restricted near-equilibrium stationary result, not a universal far-from-equilibrium selector |
| Maximum entropy production / climate extrema | Paltridge; Dewar | Dissipation or path-probability claims; not inherently useful work or reproductive success |
| Driven adaptation | England; Perunov and colleagues | Transition probabilities also depend on accessibility and fluctuations, not average heat alone |
| Gradient degradation / metabolic organization | Schneider–Kay; Morowitz–Smith | Accounts of organization under energy flow; no established universal maximizer follows |
| Constructal flow access | Bejan–Lorente | Proposed evolution of flow architecture under application-specific constraints |
| Earth-system power limits | Kleidon | Quantitative limits and approximations for linked physical fluxes, not a theorem of biological fitness |
| Ecological/economic network robustness | Ulanowicz and colleagues | Throughput must be compared with redundancy and resilience; a specific network theory, not universal midpoint proof |
| Bioeconomics / useful-work growth | Georgescu-Roegen; Ayres–Warr | Material constraints and production models, not identity of price, profit and exergy |
| Control and adaptive options | Ashby; empowerment; causal entropic forces | Regulation capacity or reachable trajectories, not raw energetic intake |
| Inference and portable learning | Friston; Hinton and colleagues | Generative inference, copying or distillation mechanisms; no general power-selection theorem |

[B/I] Dewar's derivational claims have published criticism; minimum and maximum
entropy principles cannot simply be combined into one law. Prigogine explicitly
limits the minimum result's generality. The relevant dispute must travel with
the supporting citation.
[Prigogine lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/prigogine-lecture.pdf),
[Grinstein and Linsker critique](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/40/31/N01).

[I] Two especially close neighbors deserve full replication work. Empowerment
quantifies an action-to-sensor channel over a declared horizon. Causal entropic
forces introduce a finite-horizon trajectory-entropy objective and demonstrate
behaviors when that forcing is applied. The latter is not evidence that nature
spontaneously implements an additional universal force.
[Empowerment](https://doi.org/10.1109/CEC.2005.1554676),

## 5. What the empirical and mathematical evidence actually earns

[B/I] These studies are not interchangeable replications. They use different
organisms, selection rules, outputs, and measures. An aggregate positive score
would be misleading.

| Study | Contact with evidence | Earned conclusion and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Cai, Montague and Davis 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2005.04.022) | Feedback-controlled light in plankton microcosms | Increased resource capture; does not establish the global maximum over every possible configuration |
| [DeLong 2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0030-1299.2008.16832.x) | Ten trials from three classic protist competition experiments; power estimated using allometry | Higher estimated monoculture power predicted winners in the selected cases; narrow inclusion and estimated metabolism limit generality |
| [DeLong et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1007783107) | Comparative scaling across major transitions | Metabolic and efficiency relationships differ; not an MPP selection experiment |
| [DeLong 2012](https://johnpauldelong.weebly.com/uploads/2/2/9/4/22944738/delong_2012_eer_rate_size_trade_off.pdf) | Temperature manipulation of one protist | Supports a particular rate–size model, not universal evolutionary maximization |
| [DeLong and Vasseur 2012](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0030081) | Two protists partition bacterial resources; oxygen consumption measured | No detectable increase in community function; resource partitioning alone does not establish a gain |
| [Glazier 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/sci6020028) | Review of biological power/efficiency relationships | Both negative and positive associations occur; universal opposition is untenable |
| [Burger and Brown 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/biae105) | Critical review, not a new experiment | Metabolic rate alone omits generation time, mortality and surviving offspring |
| [Gianinetti 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/ecologies7010029) | Theoretical reappraisal; no new dataset | Proposes multiscale alignment; explicitly leaves falsifiable testing unfinished |
| [Saavedra et al., 2025 v2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.14.664758v2.full.pdf) | Consumer–resource model with specified costs | Consumption optimization and assimilation optimization yield different locations of the power optimum; evolutionary alignment needs extra conditions |

[B] The Saavedra version inspected is a preprint. The author's current
[publication list](https://sites.google.com/site/sergueisaavedra/publications)
says a PLOS Complex Systems article is in press; the final publisher version
was not located. In the inspected equations, a consumption-rate optimum can
precede the Hopf boundary; an assimilation-efficiency optimum can coincide
with it when feasible. “Maximum power always occurs at instability” is not
what the model establishes. Its output measure is biomass times specific
growth rate; conversion into physical watts requires an energy-density assumption.

[I] The resulting ranking is:

1. **Strongest bounded use:** explicitly defined physical converter limits and
   testable resource-capture predictions in specified systems.
2. **Plausible but conditional:** persistent feedback can favor investment in
   future capture, with resource regime and selection mechanism stated.
3. **Open:** a general multilevel criterion connecting individual competition,
   network renewal and long-run system power.
4. **Not established:** universal optimum efficiency, necessary occupation of
   every niche, biological/machine efficiency exclusivity, moral obligation,
   or a D5 physical interaction.

[S] A reported failure must name the prediction it defeats. The absence of a
community-function gain challenges a simple niche-packing prediction; it does
not refute every possible constrained MPP. Conversely, adding a new constraint
after each failure cannot count as an independently successful prediction.

## 6. A mathematical hygiene check

[S] For a declared physical converter, let input exergy rate be
`B_in_dot(u)` and exergy efficiency be `eta_x(u)` at control setting `u`:

`P_useful(u) = eta_x(u) × B_in_dot(u)`.

Both factors require measurement and a system boundary. At an interior smooth
optimum, if both are positive:

`d(log eta_x)/du = -d(log B_in_dot)/du`.

[I] This elementary derivative condition says that proportional efficiency
loss balances proportional input-rate gain at a stationary point. It does
**not** ensure the point exists, is a maximum, or is evolutionarily selected.
The [existing toy-model tests](test_power_models.py) and
[power-and-capability note](POWER_AND_CAPABILITY.md) include boundary cases
where a middle maximum fails. Reproduction, maintenance, and comparative
fitness require additional models.

[S] If `u` means throughput, its units and relation to the physical input must
be declared. A generic expression such as `eta(v) G v` is under-specified if
`G` could mean watts, concentration, voltage, or a potential difference. Symbols
cannot replace the dimensional audit.

## 7. What changes for machines

[I] The useful distinction is **where capabilities are stored, how they are
transferred, and what reproduces their support**—not “biology has efficiency,
machines have effectiveness.” Both living and engineered systems can trade
off speed, yield, robustness, and task quality. The categorical split is not
supported by the reviewed biology. [Glazier 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/sci6020028).

[B/I] Hinton distinguishes portable digital parameters from hardware-specific
computation and discusses the transfer limitations of each. Distillation can
transfer behavior without an identical implementation. These are mechanisms
and engineering proposals, not a proof that machine populations maximize a
particular physical quantity.
[Hinton 2022, section 9](https://www.cs.utoronto.ca/~hinton/absps/FFXfinal.pdf),
[Hinton, Vinyals and Dean 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531).

[C] For the owner's “I, Pencil” intuition, treat industrial interdependence as
the hypothesis to operationalize. Do not assume a software copy is a working
descendant. Record separate clocks:

| Clock | What must complete | Possible limiting dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Policy transfer | A recipient acquires usable behavior | Data, bandwidth, compatibility, distillation examples |
| Task delivery | A verified result is produced | Energy, sensors, actuators, latency, permissions, task quality |
| Maintenance | Existing capability remains available | Repair, spare parts, cooling, operator labor, security |
| Physical expansion | Another complete supported instance becomes operational | Fabrication, materials, power delivery, supply-chain lead time |
| Renewal | The support network can replace losses over the horizon | Suppliers, skills, ecological inputs, institutional continuity |

[I] This framing neither denies biological cooperation nor declares all
machines incapable of self-assembly. Current policy-copying and industrial
reproduction are distinct mechanisms; any future autonomous manufacturing
claim must specify the entire process and its external inputs.

[C] Candidate thesis: **cheap policy transfer can make specialization valuable
across a network, while physical bottlenecks and repair determine whether that
specialization persists**. In a fixed, independent-bottleneck throughput model,
additional power increases throughput only when power is the binding constraint;
outside that model it can also affect reserves, repair or coupled constraints.
More watts cannot by themselves supply a missing chip,
a valid permission, or a competent repair process.

[S] Measure a profile, not one triumphant number: verified tasks per second;
joules per verified task; transfer time and energy; available replacement
capacity; dependency concentration; recovery after outage; achieved versus
advertised options; and separately the burdens on every affected bearer.
Profit and willingness to pay remain different measurements from useful work.

## 8. Five discriminating studies

[S/C] These are proposed studies, not completed experiments or preregistrations.
Use existing simple baselines before building a new architecture.

1. **Objective separation.** Run agents with matched budgets under objectives
   of immediate task throughput, useful power, option preservation, and
   prediction quality. Include environments where these disagree. Define
   survival/task success independently; do not reward energy capture and then
   announce that the winner proves energy capture is selected.
2. **Copy versus reproduction.** Compare exact weight copying, distillation,
   and retraining on matched and changed hardware. Count working recipients,
   quality retention, time, energy and material support—not files copied.
3. **Bottleneck intervention.** Vary electricity and spare-part availability
   independently. Predict when additional power stops increasing delivered
   capability. Failure to model the non-energy bottleneck defeats the proposed
   energy-only explanation.
4. **Partition versus coordination overhead.** Compare a generalist, several
   specialists, and redundant specialists on the same task distribution. Vary
   communication, verification and handoff costs. A specialist network must
   outperform after its support costs, not only within isolated subtasks.
5. **Horizon and disruption.** Compare maximum immediate throughput with
   maintenance, slack, multiple suppliers and graceful shutdown under outages.
   Measure both short-run output and retained service capability over time.
   A long-run benefit from slack would reject naive instantaneous maximization,
   not thermodynamics.

[S] A feasible first case is a simulated maintenance-and-service network with
two independently varied resources and controlled outages. Predefine the
unit of selection, resource boundary, time horizon, success metrics, candidate
models, random seeds, exclusions, and failure criteria. Then repeat with an
independent implementation or dataset. Simulation success alone does not
validate a theory about economies, organisms, or machine civilization.

## 9. What Emergentism can take forward

[I] The scientific neighborhood strengthens the *research question* more than
the proposed universal answer. An operational synthesis is:

**Represent worthwhile reachable alternatives; use actual means to test and
realize them; renew the capabilities and dependencies that keep alternatives
available; revise the representation when the world disagrees.**

[S] The parent's six-field instrument can organize this research: Vision declares the desired capability;
Mission identifies candidate routes; Objectives specify tests; Strategies
specify interventions; KPIs record results and failures; Agents implement only
within their separately granted scope. At group scale, functional roles can
coordinate these activities without ranking people's worth or presuming that
a selected seven-row map has been established by the energetic literature.

[S] The cooperative/Justice condition remains an explicit normative choice.
Neither available energy, maximum power, emergy nor empowerment proves that a
bearer must preserve someone else's options. Ecological dependence can make
harm costly without guaranteeing restraint. The source owner remains
[The Goal](../00_THE_GOAL.md), not a new thermodynamic commandment.

[C] F5 retains an independent burden: a new physical account needs a distinct
observable, explicit coupling or boundary mechanism, units, rivals, and tests.
Present models influencing present actions are already a different, weaker
claim. Similar vocabulary in Lotka, Schertel and an AI objective does not pay
the stronger claim's empirical bill.
