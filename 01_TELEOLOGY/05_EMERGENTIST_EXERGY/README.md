# Emergentist Exergy

**Imagine a worthwhile future. Find a feasible way toward it. Act within your
scope. Let what happens change your mind.**

The owner-selected reader expression is:

**Emergentist Exergy = VMO × SKA**

Here **VMO gives direction; SKA makes it actionable and revisable**. The six
letters name structured fields, not six numbers. In the default reading, `×`
means their supported composition: which intended trajectories can actually
be connected to means, agents, checks, and correction? A folder full of plans
multiplied by a fleet of agents is not, by itself, usable capability.

This is reader vocabulary intended to make the instrument clearer, not a replacement for
thermodynamics or a silent restoration of the retired numerical `ΦV` ranking.
The quantitative interpretation has its own experimental gate below.

## The six fields

| Field | Meaning in this instrument | What must remain inspectable |
|---|---|---|
| **Vision** | The future the bearer can imagine and values exploring: an imagined **option cone** | Purpose, exclusions, blind spots; imagination does not certify feasibility |
| **Mission** | A family of candidate trajectories toward that vision under declared constraints | Alternatives, dependencies, uncertainty, and ruled-out routes; not one predestined worldline |
| **Objectives** | Testable waypoints on those trajectories | Which trajectory, success criterion, deadline/horizon, and evidence would count |
| **Strategies** | Policies for choosing present actions toward the waypoints | Which agent can perform which action, within what scope, using which dependencies |
| **KPIs** | Specified observations that test progress and expose failure | Metric, unit, threshold, source, time, expiry, uncertainty, and adverse effects |
| **Agents** | Situated people, machines, or coordinated participants that implement actions | Actual capabilities, resource limits, role, and separately granted scope; `A` is not Authority |

Vision's imagined cone, Mission's candidate routes, supported feasibility, and
authorized action are **different sets**. Here *imagined option cone* includes
unverified possibilities; *supported option cone* means the feasibility-qualified
subset, consistent with the parent's admissible/reachable use of “option cone.”
An impossible route may be retained
as `ruled_out`; an unsupported route remains `imagined`. Keeping it visible is
not counting it as achievable. The metaphorical cone is not a relativistic
light cone, and a family of modeled histories does not prove many-worlds
actuality.

The D4/D5 distinction survives the new names: intended future contents are
possible; their present documents, calculations, policies, implementations,
measurements, and revision events are actual carriers. Neither a Vision nor a
Strategy acts without an actual bearer and actual means.

## The loop, not a frozen plan

```text
Observe the present → imagine alternatives → constrain a Mission
     → choose testable Objectives → propose a Strategy
     → separately authorized Agents act → observe outcomes through KPIs
     → retain, repair, replace, stop, or revise any of the six fields
```

There is no mandatory return to an old trajectory. A failed KPI may indict a
strategy; repeated failures may indict an objective, mission, vision, metric,
or agent-capability assumption. A met KPI may expose a bad proxy. Revision is
an explicit new record with a parent, changed fields, evidence, and reason—not
retroactive editing of the target to make the old attempt appear successful.

Long direction and short correction remain separate: `review_at` is the next
informative check; `horizon_end` includes delayed consequences. Both are finite
and revisable. Evidence outside its declared validity window cannot silently
support current progress.

The validator below only checks records. It neither performs an action nor
verifies that an external authorization, source, capability, or outcome is
genuine. There is no execution adapter or autonomous permission expansion.

## What the equation can and cannot calculate

Two explicit modes prevent a name change from concealing a mathematical change.

1. **`composition_only` — default.** Returns a traceable profile. There is no
   scalar score. Unknown capability is not zero capability; missing evidence
   is not evidence of success. Supported connections require records and still
   await source/outcome verification.
2. **`experimental_product` — candidate only.** Separately declared evaluators
   map the two bundles to cardinal factors `c_VMO` and `c_SKA` for one context:

   `q_E(c) = c_VMO(c) × c_SKA(c)`.

   The contract must bind the exact context and VMO/SKA content by hash and name
   evaluators, calibration evidence, meaningful zeros,
   units, permitted transformations, uncertainty, and the comparison domain.
   The prototype uses dimensionless factors in `[0,1]`. That normalization is
   a model convention, not calibration. It computes a candidate only when both
   inputs are known; otherwise the candidate remains `null`, even if the other
   factor is zero. It never reports a scientific or ethical validation.

The old formal owners remain intact. Their `min` convention requires a shared
coding of the two axes; merely ordinal types permit independent recodings, for
which the componentwise Pareto relation—not a universal scalar—is invariant.
The product's common-recoding counterexample remains a regression test.
Renaming the factors does not defeat it. See the
[Canonical Formula Block](../../05_COSMOLOGY/00_CANONICAL_FORMULA_BLOCK.md) and
[Power-Max lemma](../../05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/08_EFR_POWER_MAX_LEMMA.md).

No theorem here says every action stops when a bundle scores zero: a body may
move without an articulated mission, and a battery has physical exergy without
any vision. Zero is meaningful only for the specified purposeful-capability
model and its declared measurement protocol. No reciprocal identity forces
the new factors to multiply to one or to peak at the Burrisphere's equator.

## Exergy in physics stays a separate ledger

Thermodynamic exergy is maximum useful work relative to a specified reference
environment. It has energy units and depends on physical state and boundary
conditions, not on an agent having a mission. The NBS report explains this
availability concept and distinguishes task-oriented effectiveness from
process efficiency. It does not derive the VMO/SKA expression.
[NBS Technical Note 1115, Appendix A.2–A.3](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/TN/nbstechnicalnote1115.pdf).

For an actual physical study, record system boundary, reference environment,
interval, exergy input/output/storage/destruction, units, method and evidence.
Do not convert `q_E` into joules. Energy, exergy, entropy production/export,
history entropy, viability cost, and agent/affected-whole options are separate
ledgers. Missing physical measurements remain unmeasured.

This makes the geo–bio–noosphere comparison more precise: ask what the task
achieves, what energy and materials it costs, what is transferable, and which
dependencies can fail. “Biology efficient / machines effective” is a research
contrast, not an exclusive partition. Copying a policy does not reproduce its
chips, power supply, cooling, maintenance, or social permission. A cooperating
supply network can increase supported capabilities; dependency failure and
coordination overhead can also reduce them. No new maximum-power law or fifth
interaction follows from this vocabulary. The related conjecture remains at
its own tier in the
[power-regime note](../../07_THEOLOGY/09_GEOSPHERE_BIOSPHERE_NOOSPHERE_POWER_REGIMES_2026_09_15.md).

The [power-and-capability refinement](POWER_AND_CAPABILITY.md) develops this
comparison: physical useful power, current task delivery, and supported future
capability stay distinct. It adds source-checked MPP qualifications, explicit
counterexamples to a universal middle optimum, and a service-and-renewal
network lens for machines. Those toy calculations are not empirical validation.

## Justice is not another multiplier

Keep each affected bearer visible, including the sustaining whole. Record
costs, consent, remedy and exit separately. A high candidate score cannot
compensate for absent authority or concealed harm. An action can meet its
output KPI and still fail its purpose. Positive effects on all recorded
bearers do not prove the bearer census complete. Voluntary sacrifice is not
automatically strict syntropy.

The declared normative owner remains [The Goal](../00_THE_GOAL.md). This
package does not infer moral obligation from useful work, evolution, geometry,
or the ability to write a product.

## Worked example: clinic water, without a success story invented in advance

The [synthetic record](example.json) imagines reliable clinic water. It keeps
two routes: repair a pump or replace it. The initial waypoint is 100 litres
per hour by the declared review horizon. A synthetic observation of 60 L/h
fails that KPI. Backup power and water safety remain unknown; clinic patients
and the supporting watershed remain visible. Nobody is authorized by the file.

The appropriate next proposal might inspect leakage before replacing the
pump. That changes Strategy, not the old flow observation. If the water source
cannot sustainably supply the objective, revise Objectives or Mission. If
flow reaches 100 L/h but contamination appears, do not report “mission
accomplished”: the Vision was dependable water, not merely pump throughput.
Tests exercise this as a linked revision and reject concealed changes.

## Offline use

From this directory, with Python 3.10 or later:

```sh
python3 -B validate.py --check
python3 -B -m unittest -v test_contract.py
python3 -B -m unittest -v test_power_models.py
python3 -B validate.py example.json
```

For a linked record use `validate.py new.json --previous old.json`. Paths are
read-only. Source strings are inert: they are never fetched or executed.
`contract.json` is a **custom contract vocabulary**, not JSON Schema. Exact
field shapes and cross-reference rules are implemented in `validate.py` and
tested in `test_contract.py`. Validity means only that those checks passed.

The result always says `may_execute=false` and `empirical_validation=false`.
Source pins detect drift, not truth. The [evaluation plan](EVALUATION.md)
defines the tests needed before claiming practical advantage. The
[migration and receipt](MIGRATION.md) distinguishes what changed here from
what still needs separate source/public integration.

**The instrument's promise is not “the plan is right.” It is that purpose,
means, evidence, and revision can be inspected together.**
