# Maximum-power research: source catalogue

[S] Companion to the [research dossier](MAXIMUM_POWER_RESEARCH.md). Research
date: 15 September 2026. The identifiers below are local reading references,
not public claim IDs. All summaries attribute the author's position, not our
endorsement. Access may have been through the main researcher or one of three
read-only research lanes.

**TEXT** = relevant sections of a full-text source inspected, not an entire
book/derivation audited. **EXCERPT** = partial text, including indexed passages.
**ABSTRACT** = abstract/summary only. **METADATA** = bibliographic record,
publisher description or contents; argument remains unread. An author-hosted
manuscript and final publisher version are distinguished where material.

## A. Lotka and the Odum programs

### A01 — Lotka 1922a

Alfred J. Lotka, *Contribution to the Energetics of Evolution*, PNAS 8:147–151.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.8.6.147);
[primary scan](https://www.sustainable.soltechdesigns.com/lotka1922.pdf).
**TEXT.** Available-energy selection conditioned on resources and constraints;
important qualifications about scarcity. Foundational selection hypothesis,
not a measured universal maximum.

### A02 — Lotka 1922b

Alfred J. Lotka, *Natural Selection as a Physical Principle*, PNAS 8:151–154.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.8.6.151);
[scan](https://storage.e.jimdo.com/file/c4cfa0e7-c939-448d-aa8b-1d93308f379d/Lotka.pdf).
**EXCERPT / opening primary passages.** Selection is proposed as information
beyond thermodynamic constraints; rates and guidance discussed. Whole-paper
and philosophical interpretation still need close reading.

### A03 — Lotka 1925

*Elements of Physical Biology*, Williams & Wilkins.
[University catalogue](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupid?key=olbp93007).
**METADATA.** Central book-length reading debt. The later retitled mathematical
biology edition should be collated rather than assumed textually identical.

### A04 — Odum and Pinkerton 1955

Howard T. Odum and Richard C. Pinkerton, *Time's Speed Regulator: The Optimum
Efficiency for Maximum Power Output in Physical and Biological Systems*,
American Scientist 43:331–343.
[Primary scan](https://canadiancor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Odum-Pinkerton-MPP2.pdf).
**TEXT.** Converter loading, speed, self-repair and optimum output. Printed
p. 335 states the near-equilibrium scope of the linear argument. Intermediate
efficiency and scarcity discussions do not establish universal 50% operation.

### A05 — Howard T. Odum 1957

*Trophic Structure and Productivity of Silver Springs, Florida*, Ecological
Monographs 27:55–112. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/1948571).
**METADATA / opening text.** Foundational ecological-flow study; detailed
methods and original tables remain to be audited before using it as a test
of an optimization claim.

### A06 — Eugene P. Odum 1969

*The Strategy of Ecosystem Development*, Science 164:262–270.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.164.3877.262);
[primary scan](https://biophilosophy.ca/Teaching/2070papers/odum.pdf).
**EXCERPT.** Succession and ecosystem maintenance/yield trade-offs. Related to
the energetic program; distinguish Eugene from Howard, and theoretical
succession patterns from direct MPP tests.

### A07 — Howard T. Odum 1971

*Environment, Power, and Society*.
[Located book scan](https://naturocracy.org/EnvironmentPowerAndSociety-HowardT.Odum.pdf).
**BIBLIOGRAPHIC LEAD; book not read.** Priority for tracing how the ecological
program extends into social interpretation. Edition and pagination need
direct confirmation before passage-level claims.

### A08 — Howard T. Odum 1983

*Systems Ecology: An Introduction*.
[Book record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Systems_Ecology.html?hl=en&id=hWvwAAAAMAAJ&output=html_text).
**METADATA.** Systems notation and energy-network modeling; full-book study
owed, especially accounting boundaries and higher-level selection claims.

### A09 — Howard T. Odum 1983 rebuttal

*Maximum power and efficiency: A rebuttal*, Ecological Modelling 20:71–82.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3800(83)90032-7).
**ABSTRACT.** Replies to Silvert's efficiency criticism. Must be read as one
side of an exchange, not a settled resolution. Publisher and Crossref render
the title as “A rebutttal”; some bibliographies normalize that spelling.

### A10 — Howard T. Odum 1988

*Self-Organization, Transformity, and Information*, Science 242:1132–1139.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.242.4882.1132).
**ABSTRACT via [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17799729/).** Published
25 November 1988. Describes reinforcement, pulsed production/consumption,
transformity, and extensions to information and socioeconomic valuation.
Those valuation claims are not empirically established by an abstract.
Do not conflate transformity with thermodynamic exergy efficiency.

### A11 — Howard T. Odum, Environmental Accounting

*Environmental Accounting: Emergy and Environmental Decision Making*, Wiley,
ISBN 9780471114420.
[Publisher contents](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en?isbn=9780471114420&option=com_eshop&title=Environmental+Accounting&view=product).
**METADATA / contents.** Publisher record dates December 1995; scholarly
references commonly cite 1996. This edition-date discrepancy is retained.
Emergy algebra, support networks and information are full-text reading debts.

### A12 — Howard T. and Elisabeth C. Odum 2006

*The prosperous way down*, Energy 31:21–32.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2004.05.012).
**ABSTRACT / excerpts.** Resource descent, pulsing and social adaptation; a
physical/social proposal, not proof of an inevitable forecast. Their 2001 book
of related title remains a separate reading task.

## B. Empirical tests, disputes, and recent reappraisals

### B00 — Silvert 1982

William Silvert, *The theory of power and efficiency in ecology*, Ecological
Modelling 15:159–164. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3800(82)90059-X).
**ABSTRACT / publisher-deposited metadata.** Challenges the standard Atwood
analysis and a universal optimum efficiency. Aquatic-foraging models differ
with flow regime. Theoretical critique, not a general empirical refutation.
Full article and the Odum reply need paired reading. The publisher record
dates this March 1982, not 1981 as an encountered secondary description does.

### B01 — Cai, Olsen and Campbell 2004

*Maximum (em)power: a foundational principle linking man and nature*, Ecological
Modelling 178:115–119. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2003.12.009).
**ABSTRACT / sections.** Explains capture-reinforcement and the move toward
emergy-based evaluation. The extension from power to empower is substantive,
not a unit-label substitution.

### B02 — Hall 2004

Charles A. S. Hall, *The continuing importance of maximum power*, Ecological
Modelling 178:107–113. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2004.03.003).
**ABSTRACT.** Advocacy and retrospective significance. Full argument and
underlying examples remain to be checked independently.

### B03 — Cai, Montague and Davis 2006

*The maximum power principle: An empirical investigation*, Ecological
Modelling. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2005.04.022).
**TEXT: methods/discussion.** Plankton microcosms developed with pH-controlled
light versus fixed photoperiods; the latter were assayed under pH control on
the final day. Mean daily light-duration **increases** were 506 and 412 minutes
respectively, not those absolute photoperiods. Increased access does not
establish a maximum over all configurations. Controller effects warrant scrutiny.

### B04 — DeLong 2008

John P. DeLong, *The maximum power principle predicts the outcomes of two-species
competition experiments*, Oikos.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0030-1299.2008.16832.x).
**TEXT.** Ten trials selected from three classic protist studies; abundance
and metabolic allometry estimated population power. Supports a bounded
competitive prediction; metabolism was not directly measured and coexistence
was not itself predicted beforehand.

### B05 — DeLong and colleagues 2010

*Shifts in metabolic scaling, production, and efficiency across major
evolutionary transitions of life*, PNAS.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1007783107).
**TEXT.** Comparative prokaryote, protist and small-metazoan data. Scaling and
efficiency change across groups. Not an experimental demonstration of selection
for MPP; some metabolic values were estimated by regression.

### B06 — DeLong 2012, rate–size

*Experimental demonstration of a rate–size trade-off governing body size
optimization*, Evolutionary Ecology Research.
[Author PDF](https://johnpauldelong.weebly.com/uploads/2/2/9/4/22944738/delong_2012_eer_rate_size_trade_off.pdf).
**ABSTRACT.** One protist at three temperatures; observed slope −0.91 versus
predicted −0.94. Supports the specified supply–demand relation, not universal
evolutionary optimization.

### B07 — DeLong and Vasseur 2012, coexistence countercase

*Coexistence via Resource Partitioning Fails to Generate an Increase in
Community Function*, PLOS ONE 7:e30081.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0030081).
**TEXT.** Two protists partitioned bacterial prey sizes; direct oxygen
consumption showed no increase in community function (reported P=.49).
Interference is a proposed explanation. No significant increase is not proof
of an exactly zero effect, but it defeats treating this study as confirmation
of a necessary gain from niche partitioning.

### B08 — Sciubba 2011

Enrico Sciubba, *What did Lotka really say? A critical reassessment of the
“maximum power principle”*, Ecological Modelling 222:1347–1353.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2011.02.002).
**ABSTRACT / excerpts.** Critiques substitutions among exergy, energy, emergy
and entropy-extremization principles. Important conceptual adversary; not
an empirical refutation of every constrained MPP.

### B09 — Yen and colleagues 2014

*Thermodynamic extremization principles and their relevance to ecology*,
Austral Ecology. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/aec.12130).
**TEXT: selected sections.** Surveys confusion and quantitative prospects;
distinguishes statistical MaxEnt and physical energetic principles. Useful
starting point for a systematic, rather than supportive-only, review.

### B10 — Hall and McWhirter 2023

Charles A. S. Hall and Timothy McWhirter, *Maximum power in evolution, ecology
and economics*, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2022.0290).
**TEXT: selected sections.** Historical review and defense, including forest
correlations. Not authored by DeLong; not a new universal selection experiment.

### B11 — Glazier 2024

Douglas S. Glazier, *Power and Efficiency in Living Systems*, Sci 6:28.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/sci6020028).
**TEXT, including section 7.** Negative power–efficiency relationships appear
especially in resource supply; positive relationships are common in resource
use. Both patterns matter. His proposed terminology for fitness/adaptiveness
must remain attributed rather than treated as consensus.

### B12 — Burger and Brown, 2025 issue / 2024 online

*Does nature select to maximize power?*, BioScience 75:502–504.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/biae105).
**TEXT.** Critical book review: generation time, offspring survival and
mortality complicate energetic fitness claims. Conceptual criticism with cited
evidence, not a newly conducted experiment. Online publication was 6 November
2024; issue date is 2025.

### B13 — Gianinetti 2026

Alberto Gianinetti, *A Reappraisal of the Principle of Maximum Power*, Ecologies
7:29. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ecologies7010029).
**TEXT.** Proposes cross-scale alignment through interdependence while calling
for falsifiable multiscale tests. No new dataset. A dependency argument does
not establish that local selection optimizes whole-ecosystem performance.

### B14 — Saavedra and colleagues, 2025 v2

*Linking power, efficiency, and bifurcations in consumer–resource systems*.
[Inspected preprint v2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.14.664758v2.full.pdf);
[author publication list](https://sites.google.com/site/sergueisaavedra/publications).
**TEXT: equations/model/discussion.** Output power is modeled as biomass times
specific growth rate; physical watts need an energy-density assumption.
Consumption optimization can maximize
power before the Hopf boundary; assimilation optimization can place it at that
boundary when feasible, otherwise at a parameter boundary. Evolutionary
alignment requires explicit marginal-cost conditions. Author list says PLOS
Complex Systems “in press”; a final publisher article was not located.

## C. Neighboring physical and ecological frameworks

### C01 — Callen 1957 and Prigogine 1977

Herbert B. Callen, *Principle of Minimum Entropy Production*.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.105.360). **ABSTRACT.**
Ilya Prigogine, *Time, Structure and Fluctuations*.
[Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/prigogine-lecture.pdf).
**EXCERPT.** Restricted stationary/linear, near-equilibrium setting; entropy
production has units W/K. Not an unrestricted rule for far-from-equilibrium life.

### C02 — Paltridge 1975 and 1979

Garth W. Paltridge, *Global dynamics and climate—a system of minimum entropy
exchange*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.49710142906); *Climate and thermodynamic
systems of maximum dissipation*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/279630a0).
**ABSTRACTS.** Different climate variational formulations under energy-balance
constraints. Do not silently equate the 1975 exchange expression with maximum
total planetary entropy production.

### C03 — Dewar 2003, with a published criticism

Roderick C. Dewar, *Information theory explanation of the fluctuation theorem,
maximum entropy production and self-organized criticality in non-equilibrium
stationary states*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/36/3/303);
[author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0005382);
[inspected author upload](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/1820430_Information_theory_explanation_of_the_fluctuation_theorem_maximum_entropy_production_and_self-organized_criticality_in_non-equilibrium_stationary_states).
**TEXT.** Path-distribution
entropy and specified constraints are used to argue for physical MEP.

G. Grinstein and R. Linsker, *Comments on a derivation and application of the
“maximum entropy production” principle* (2007).
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/40/31/N01). **ABSTRACT.** Criticizes a step
in Dewar's 2005 far-from-equilibrium derivation and his 2003 criticality claim.
Not a refutation of every empirical MEP model.

### C04 — England 2015; Perunov, Marsland and England 2016

*Dissipative adaptation in driven self-assembly*,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nnano.2015.250), **TEXT**;
*Statistical Physics of Adaptation*,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.6.021036), **ABSTRACT / manuscript passages**.
Driven matter, a thermal bath and microscopic reversibility; transition
accessibility and work fluctuations matter. Not simply “dissipate most” and
not a theorem of maximum reproductive power.

### C05 — Schneider and Kay 1994

Eric D. Schneider and James J. Kay, *Life as a manifestation of the second law
of thermodynamics*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0895-7177(94)90188-0).
**EXCERPT.** Organization and gradient degradation; authors retain survival
and do not make thermodynamics sufficient for life. Direct PDF access failed;
indexed primary-text passages supplied the bounded reading.

### C06 — Bejan and Lorente 2010

Adrian Bejan and Sylvie Lorente, *The constructal law of design and evolution
in nature*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2009.0302).
**ABSTRACT.** Evolution of access for flows in finite configurations.
Application-specific resistance/conductance, not a common universal unit or
one entropy maximum.

### C07 — Schrödinger 1944

Erwin Schrödinger, *What Is Life?*
[Primary-text copy](https://www.physik.uni-kl.de/eggert/statmech/what-is-life.pdf).
**EXCERPT: chapter 6 and later chapter note.** Explains maintenance through
exchange; the note discusses free energy as technical wording. A maintenance
requirement is not a proof that the intake rate is maximized. Edition must be
specified when quoting the later note.

### C08 — Morowitz and Smith 2007

Harold J. Morowitz and Eric Smith, *Energy flow and the organization of life*.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cplx.20191);
[2006 working paper](https://sfi-edu.s3.amazonaws.com/sfi-edu/production/uploads/sfi-com/dev/uploads/filer/43/7a/437a1774-ec12-446e-ba17-5a01341bba3a/06-08-029.pdf).
**TEXT of working paper; published abstract.** Chemical flux organization
under geochemical driving. The manuscript acknowledges missing quantitative
application; it does not establish inevitable life or universal MPP.

### C09 — Kleidon 2023

Axel Kleidon, *Working at the limit: a review of thermodynamics and optimality
of the Earth system*, Earth System Dynamics 14:861–896.
[Primary article](https://esd.copernicus.org/articles/14/861/2023/).
**TEXT: introduction and selected sections.** Linked work-generating fluxes
and constrained Earth-system estimates. Important physical comparison;
observational agreement in climate models is not evidence of a biological
selection mechanism.

### C10 — Ulanowicz and colleagues 2009

*Quantifying sustainability: Resilience, efficiency and the return of
information theory*, Ecological Complexity 6:27–36.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecocom.2008.10.005).
**ABSTRACT.** Network efficiency/reserve trade-offs; a candidate framework,
not proof of a universal balance point. A located
[2016 critical manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.05056) remains unread
and must enter any deeper assessment of the proposed measure.

### C11 — Goerner, Lietaer and Ulanowicz 2009

*Quantifying economic sustainability: Implications for free-enterprise theory,
policy and practice*, Ecological Economics 69:76–81.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2009.07.018).
**ABSTRACT / sections.** Extends a network framework toward economic robustness.
Its policy interpretation is a further claim; neither GDP nor throughput alone
is asserted to establish sustainability.

## D. Exergy accounting and economics

### D01 — NBS Technical Note 1115

[Primary report](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/TN/nbstechnicalnote1115.pdf),
Appendix A.2–A.3. **Previously inspected physical-definition anchor in this
package; not re-audited in full for this dossier.** Availability relative to a
reference environment; distinguishes task effectiveness from process efficiency.
Does not derive VMO/SKA or a moral objective.

### D02 — Brown and Herendeen 1996

Mark T. Brown and Robert A. Herendeen, *Embodied energy analysis and EMERGY
analysis: a comparative view*, Ecological Economics 19:219–235.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0921-8009(96)00046-8).
**ABSTRACT.** Parallel accounting on the same model systems, with continuing
disagreement between the authors. A useful warning against declaring all
energy-quality accounting equivalent.

### D03 — Herendeen 2004

Robert A. Herendeen, *Energy analysis and EMERGY analysis—a comparison*,
Ecological Modelling 178:227–237.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2003.12.017);
[institutional PDF](https://www.uvm.edu/giee/pubpdfs/Herendeen_2004b_Ecological_Modelling.pdf).
**EXCERPT / introduction.** Continues the disagreement and questions ambitious
prescriptive uses while recognizing the ecological dependence displayed by
emergy accounting. Full worked comparisons remain to be audited.

### D04 — Georgescu-Roegen 1971

Nicholas Georgescu-Roegen, *The Entropy Law and the Economic Process*, Harvard
University Press. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.4159/harvard.9780674281653).
**METADATA / contents.** Essential bioeconomics reading debt. No claim here
that this review audited the entire book or accepts every later material-entropy
extension associated with the program.

### D05 — Ayres and Warr 2005

Robert U. Ayres and Benjamin Warr, *Accounting for growth: the role of physical
work*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.strueco.2003.10.003);
[2002 working paper](https://flora.insead.edu/fichiersti_wp/inseadwp2002/2002-70.pdf).
**TEXT of working paper; published abstract/excerpts.** Useful work, capital
and labor in a historical growth model. The authors distinguish correlation
from causal direction. Money and exergy do not become one quantity.

## E. Control, future options, and machine learning

### E01 — Ashby 1956

W. Ross Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics*.
[Author archive](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf).
**TEXT: especially chapter 11.** Regulation bounds using variety and channels;
not a prescription to maximize every kind of variety or energy consumption.

### E02 — Klyubin, Polani and Nehaniv 2005

*Empowerment: A Universal Agent-Centric Measure of Control*.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/CEC.2005.1554676);
[institutional copy](https://uhra.herts.ac.uk/id/eprint/282/1/901241.pdf).
[Inspected author upload](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/4201381_Empowerment_A_Universal_Agent-Centric_Measure_of_Control).
**TEXT through author-uploaded manuscript; institutional PDF intermittently
timed out.** Action-to-future-sensor channel
capacity, in bits. Sensor/actuator scope and horizon matter; not every imagined
possibility is controllable or perceptible.

### E03 — Wissner-Gross and Freer 2013

*Causal Entropic Forces*, Physical Review Letters 110:168702.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.168702);
[author PDF](https://cfreer.org/papers/PhysRevLett_110-168702.pdf).
**TEXT.** Finite-horizon trajectory entropy with proposed forcing and
illustrative simulations. An imposed force producing behavior does not
establish a new force acting spontaneously in nature.

### E04 — Friston 2010

Karl Friston, *The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?*
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2787);
[full text](https://activeinference.github.io/papers/unified.pdf).
**TEXT: especially pp. 1–3.** Variational free energy is information-theoretic,
not physical exergy. Particular models connect inference, perception and
action; a universal watt-equivalence is not supplied.

### E05 — Hinton, Vinyals and Dean 2015

*Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network*.
[Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531).
**TEXT: especially section 2.** Teacher-to-student transfer through training
targets; task behavior can move without identical hardware or weights. Quality,
data and student capacity remain conditions.

### E06 — Hinton 2022

*The Forward-Forward Algorithm: Some Preliminary Investigations*.
[Author manuscript](https://www.cs.utoronto.ca/~hinton/absps/FFXfinal.pdf).
**TEXT: especially section 9.** Hardware-specific “mortal” computation versus
portable parameters and high-bandwidth sharing. Engineering hypothesis, not a
universal efficiency ordering of biology and machines. A restored file still
needs working hardware, energy and compatible execution.

### E07 — Turner and colleagues 2021

*Optimal Policies Tend To Seek Power*, NeurIPS 34:23063–23074.
[Primary paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/c26820b8a4c1b3c2aa868d6d57e14a79-Paper.pdf).
**TEXT: abstract/introduction.** Conditional MDP results involving symmetries,
reward distributions and options. Not every agent, not a timing forecast, and
not physical power in watts. Full theorem-assumption audit remains to be done
before importing a result into an Emergentism model.

## G. Gaps to close before a systematic-review claim

[S] Priority reading debts are the full Lotka/Odum books, original German
Auerbach and Schertel, the complete McWhirter comparison, the Silvert–Odum
exchange, original forest-gradient data, and adverse evidence outside the
retrieved MPP citation network. Expand the search to Boltzmann/Ostwald,
Georg Hirth, energy-return studies, ecological network evolution, kinetic
stability/origins of life, and standard fitness/resource-competition theory.
These names are a search agenda, not a list of already corroborating witnesses.

[S] Before synthesizing effect sizes, record: organism/system; randomization;
selection mechanism; exact dependent quantity and unit; input and output
boundary; treatment; comparator; time horizon; uncertainty; exclusions;
prediction fixed before outcomes; and independent replication. Reviews must
not be counted as new experiments, or several papers on one dataset as several
independent discoveries.

[S] Full-text access failures, snippets, missing metadata, and unsuccessful
queries are retained as such. No paywall was bypassed, no books purchased, and
no absence of a search result is promoted into a claim that the literature
contains no rival.
