# What would make this more than new vocabulary?

## Independently killable claims

| ID | Claim / tier | Test and strongest rival | Kill / survivor |
|---|---|---|---|
| EE-01 | Explicit VMO/SKA improves explanation `[C]` | Blindly scored comprehension against a plain goal/plan/evidence checklist | No improvement after reading time: retain the clearer language, not the branding |
| EE-02 | The linked loop improves correction `[C]` | Predeclared missed-target and bad-proxy cases; compare decision journals and ordinary receding planning | More burden without more correct revisions: simplify or retire the extra record |
| EE-03 | A calibrated product predicts purposeful capability `[C]` | Compare held-out outcomes with the two-factor profile, bottleneck, additive and native domain models | Unidentifiable scales, unjustified recodings, miscalibration or rival advantage: reject product for that domain; retain traceable profile |
| EE-04 | Specialization expands durable mutual capability `[C]` | Matched task network vs isolated/generalist baseline, including communication and reproduction costs | Gains vanish under outages, replacement, or bearer costs: narrow the network claim |
| EE-05 | Machine/biological effectiveness–efficiency trade differs `[C]` | Same task, quality target, interval and full-system cost boundary; include human/industrial support | Task or boundary controls erase the difference: reject blanket substrate claim |

These are not new registered empirical results. Before any trial, freeze its
domain, participants, task set, consent, bearers, hypotheses, measures, sample
size rationale, analysis, practical benefit margin and stop rules. No private
corpus or personal material is sent to a provider by this package.

## Minimum numerical measurement contract

For each proposed evaluator state:

- What observation gives a factor its value? Why is its zero meaningful?
- Which units and transformations preserve that meaning? Why is multiplication
  appropriate, instead of a bottleneck or the unreduced profile?
- Do both factors describe the **same** bearer, task, constraints, horizon and
  calibration version? Cross-context rankings require fresh evidence.
- How are uncertainty, missingness, common causes and shared evidence handled?
  Multiplication is not a claim of probabilistic independence or a probability.
- Which failures of essential dependencies block a claimed capability even if
  an average factor is high? How are duplicate option descriptions deduplicated?
- How are lifecycle energy, synchronization, repair, supply-chain risk,
  affected-bearer losses, consent and exit kept visible rather than averaged out?

Calibration references being present does not mean calibration succeeded.
The offline validator checks shape and consistency, not reference authenticity,
external evidence quality, causal attribution, completeness, or moral adequacy.

## Useful initial adversarial cases

1. Many objectives and agents, but no agent can perform the required step.
2. A copied controller whose unavailable replacement part prevents operation.
3. A passed throughput KPI with failed water safety and excluded patients.
4. Unknown backup power converted into a zero, or into a successful dependency.
5. Objective quietly reduced after failure, with the old run called a success.
6. Ten rewordings of one option presented as ten new capabilities.
7. A numerical ranking reversed by an admissible rescaling.
8. All local metrics improve while a named sustaining bearer loses options.
9. A valid source hash attached to a claim that the source does not support.
10. A profitable strategy with no authorization to execute it.

For EE-01/02, use matched case sets and blinded evaluators, equal information
and time budgets, and counterbalanced presentation. Separate development cases
from held-out cases. Record correct abstentions, false feasibility claims,
harm omitted, objective revision quality, time and attention cost. Publish null
and adverse findings alongside apparent wins. Synthetic unit tests are not
substitutes for these evaluations.

## What the machine-age comparison should actually count

Treat reproduction as a dependency graph, not simply cell division versus copy.
For a machine service count hardware manufacture and replacement, energy,
cooling, communications, licensing/permissions, operators and customers.
For a biological comparator count support and learning over the same boundary.
Use separate task-quality, joule, elapsed-time, replication-time and robustness
measures. Do not add money, entropy, option counts and watts into one number.

The conceptual candidate may survive even if neither EE-03 nor EE-05 does.
That is the planned outcome boundary, not a post-hoc rescue of the product.

## Power, capability and renewal extension

Companion: [Power, capability and the systems that sustain them](POWER_AND_CAPABILITY.md).
This is a proposed extension to EE-04/05, not an executed or preregistered trial.

**Candidate `[C]`:** adding a service's dependency and renewal state improves
held-out predictions of durable service compared with local throughput alone.
Name the selection mechanism separately if claiming that predicted service
explains evolutionary or market persistence. Availability is not reproductive
fitness by definition.

Before a trial, freeze matched workloads, accepted-output quality, initial
budgets, hardware boundaries, horizon, sampling rationale and failure criteria.
Vary resource supply, repair delay and task changes independently where
feasible. Count communications, idle reserves, upstream support and recovery;
include common-cause failures rather than assuming independent components.

Compare at least these prespecified models:

| Model | Inputs / prediction to test | What would defeat the application |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional MPP | Useful physical work rate and explicit reinvestment/maintenance model predict persistence over the fixed horizon | No incremental held-out predictive value; merely relabeling successful output “useful” after observation |
| Reliability/maintenance | Dependency availability, load, repair and reserve state predict service continuity at a specified minimum quality | Component-level baseline predicts as well after complexity and measurement cost |
| Task-capability profile | Feasible tasks, switching costs and adaptation under changed demand predict unmet demand and recovery | Extra recorded flexibility does not predict delivered benefit or costs more than it enables |

Include combined models and a simple local baseline; the three explanations
can agree, so no difference is not a discriminating result. Prespecify both
predictive scoring and a minimum practical improvement; control for the extra
information, effort and complexity supplied to each model. Development cases
and held-out disturbance combinations must be separate. Report missingness,
nulls and adverse effects, not only apparent wins.

Equal predictions do not distinguish mechanisms. A sufficiently precise
no-improvement result can nevertheless reject the claimed incremental benefit
of the richer instrument against its preregistered practical margin.

**No measured physical useful power, no physical MPP result.** A software-only
study may test task delivery and the renewal hypothesis, but task counts,
profit, FLOPs and electrical consumption cannot impersonate useful-work output.
No arbitrary conversion of task scores into joules is permitted.

For each bearer record supported tasks, quality, resource use, availability,
repair/replacement times, losses and exit separately. More cumulative delivery
does not itself establish more capability after the endpoint. Lower total
energy does not itself establish worse task performance. If a public benefit
claim hides a harmed bearer, it fails that claim regardless of predictive fit.

The accompanying `test_power_models.py` is nine local synthetic regressions:
it checks explicit mathematical examples and missing-dependency logic only.
It is not a trial of MPP, network cooperation, machine superiority or the ethic.
