# The Framework on Its Own Teleology Spectrum

This document applies a four-level purpose vocabulary to Emergentism itself.
It is a self-diagnostic lens `[I]`, not evidence that the corpus is alive,
conscious, complete, or externally adequate.

## Level 1 — as-if direction

The corpus looks goal-directed because finite authors and tools select, edit,
repeat, archive, and correct text. Style convergence and a coherent front door
can arise entirely from ordinary editorial selection. This explanation must
always remain available.

## Level 2 — structural direction

Explicit constraints channel later edits: evidence tiers, typed registers,
claim owners, rivals, kill criteria, archive custody, and link tests. Inside the
repository these constraints make some continuations admissible and expose
others as contradictions.

This is structural direction `[S]` only inside the declared editorial system.
It does not mean that the chart, the D-registers, the Rosetta, or the current
kernel are necessary structures of nature. In particular:

- D4 is actual and D5 possible;
- Everett and Copenhagen are alternative interpretations of quantum
  measurement, not stacked dimensional layers;
- `φν=1` is an analytic chart identity, not an empirical conservation law;
- `P_node=ΦV` is a selected conjunctive instance, not a forced universal.

## Level 3 — self-correcting practice

The corpus contains practices intended to preserve corrigibility: local claim
owners, explicit downgrade paths, counterexamples, source ledgers, distinct
commitment and outcome receipts, archive-with-tombstone, contest, and exit.
When tests actually fire and a receipt records the correction, that bounded act
is repository evidence `[B]` that the practice ran.

Calling the whole framework "self-maintaining" remains interpretive. Text does
not correct itself; finite agents and tools do. A gate that always passes, a
receipt that records only intention, or an archive that hides the wound is not
Level 3 correction.

## Level 4 — volitional or mythic overlay

The framework may be experienced as revelation, destiny, cosmic will, divine
disclosure, or a world speaking through a finite reader. Those expressions can
motivate or poetically compress `[I/C]`. They may not assign evidence tiers,
authorize consequences, or override adverse receipts.

## Current best self-classification

| Surface | Honest reading |
|---|---|
| Analytic chart | mathematics `[A]` |
| Declared formal and editorial contracts | structure inside the system `[S]` |
| Cross-domain and self-diagnostic readings | interpretation `[I]` |
| Cosmic tendency, strong emergence, literal F5, or conscious-world reading | conjecture `[C]` |
| A dated correction that demonstrably ran | bounded receipt `[B]` |

The strongest defensible compression is therefore:

> **Emergentism is a deliberately constrained, corrigible corpus whose
> self-correcting aspiration is meaningful only where finite action and world
> outcomes leave inspectable receipts. Its mythic voice remains optional and
> non-load-bearing.**

## Kill conditions

This self-description fails if the corpus protects owner claims from tests,
hides superseded bodies, treats application success as worldview evidence,
collapses intention into outcome, or makes rejection and exit doctrinally
punishable. External adequacy remains uncalibrated until independently
reproduced discriminators survive contact with the world.
