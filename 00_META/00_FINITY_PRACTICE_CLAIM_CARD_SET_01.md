The strongest ordinary rivals include a decision journal, OODA/PDCA loop,
premortem, implementation intention, and a shorter checklist. A fair comparison
must equalize instruction, components, time, measurement, domain, and follow-up;
measure burden and harm; and allow the ordinary method to win.

practical value after burden and cost, retire it. Preserve the better-performing
receipt practice and the map/action/outcome/correction distinction.
