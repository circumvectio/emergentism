# Finity Practice Claim-Card Set 01

## Decision

The public **Finity Card** now has an active source owner in the Lived Compass.
The name denotes a selected seven-prompt decision worksheet. It does not denote
the formal F0 Finity protocol, create an algebra, establish an ontology, supply
authorization, or prove better decisions.

## Test obligation

A future comparison must predeclare its decision domain, comparator, instruction
budget, outcomes, delayed-harm window, bearer set, burden, null route, custody,
and kill criterion. It must measure more than completion or user enthusiasm.

action follow-through, delayed-harm detection, and time cost.

This internal review establishes routing and wording only. It is not an
independent review, field result, replication, or validation of Emergentism.
