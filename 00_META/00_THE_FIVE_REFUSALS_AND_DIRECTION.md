# The Five Refusals and Direction

## Via negativa, not commandment

Emergentism cannot derive an ought from a chart identity. Its constitution is
therefore stated as a **via negativa**: by choosing to hold this worldview, a
holder limits what they will do in its name.

```text
Hold(h, Emergentism) -> h accepts RF-1 and RF-2 and RF-3 and RF-4 and RF-5
```

This is a first-person binding, not a universal quantifier over other people.
It does not authorize coercion, create an infallible tribunal, or turn dissent
into violation. A reader may inspect, reject, revise, or leave the worldview.

The refusals are conjunctive. Passing one never compensates for failing
another. `Ω` then names a direction available inside their boundary.

## RF-1 · No extraction `[I/vow]`

> **We will not improve our own local score by invisibly draining a bearer or
> sustaining boundary on which that improvement depends.**

Extraction is not merely taking. It is gain purchased by externalizing material
cost, risk, lost agency, or irreversible damage onto an affected bearer who has
been hidden, denied standing, or denied meaningful recourse.

The shorthand `η=0` means that the declared action contains no such extraction
after the bearer set, horizon, payer, beneficiary, uncertainty, and substrate
costs are made visible. It is not a complete morality function: an act can be
non-extractive and still be deceptive, coercive, reckless, or wasteful.

**Operational questions**

1. Who or what materially sustains the gain?
2. Who pays now, who pays later, and who receives the benefit?
3. Which cost has been displaced outside the chosen boundary or horizon?
4. What evidence would reveal that an apparently mutual gain was extractive?

## RF-2 · No unaccountable consequence `[S/I]`

> **We will not treat a consequential act as authorized when responsibility for
> its mandate, performance, custody, and consequence cannot be traced and
> contested.**

Authorization is a typed relation, not a title or aura. For a consequential act
`a`, an adequate envelope is proportional to its risk and declares:

```text
AuthorizationEnvelope(a) =
  principal
  + mandate
  + scope
  + consent
  + custody
  + expiry_or_revocation
  + contest_path
  + actor
  + consequence_bearer
```

The principal is the bearer whose legitimate capacity is being exercised. The
actor is who performs the act. The consequence bearer is who actually receives
its material effects. These roles may coincide, but must not be silently fused.

Authorization does not manufacture a good outcome. It makes the commitment
answerable. The environment still returns a separate consequence, and the
receipt must be able to correct both map and mapper.

## RF-3 · No erasure `[S/I]`

> **We will not create false continuity by deleting a correction, fired kill,
> material consequence, or line of responsibility from the account.**

Supersession changes active authority without pretending the earlier claim
never existed. A wound remains legible enough to explain the seam: what was
claimed, what broke, what changed, and which evidence forced the change.

No-erasure is not indiscriminate retention. Privacy, safety, consent, and lawful
deletion can require sensitive content to be removed. In such cases, retain the
minimum non-sensitive provenance needed to prevent fabricated continuity, and
record why content cannot remain. The refusal protects truth and accountability,
not hoarding.

## RF-4 · No trapped exit `[I/vow]`

> **We will not make continued adherence the price of dignity, voice, identity,
> legitimate holdings, or access to contest and repair.**

Exit must be real enough to use. It includes the ability to set down the map,
withdraw assent, recover what one is entitled to carry, and challenge a
consequence without being recast as an enemy merely for leaving.

Exit is not immunity from a consequence already caused, permission to abandon
a dependent bearer, or a right to carry another's protected information. Those
obligations remain visible inside the Justice envelope. The refusal is against
coercive lock-in, not against responsibility.

## RF-5 · No infallibility `[S/I]`

> **We will not exempt a claim, symbol, model, or holder from correction by
> evidence and consequence.**

A load-bearing claim must declare its type and tier. A checkable claim carries
a rival, a test, and a condition for revision, demotion, or death. A
metaphysical wager that lacks an empirical discriminator stays named as a
wager; beauty, age, coherence, or usefulness does not promote it.

No-infallibility also forbids prestige exceptions. Correction applies to the
person tending the map, the institution preserving it, and the map itself.
Confession without the ability to withdraw would still be capture; RF-5
therefore depends on RF-4.

## `Ω` · Durable mutual option-cone widening under Justice `[I/C]`

`Ω` is the selected direction, not a sixth refusal and not an established law
of agency. An option cone is the subset of physically admissible histories a
finite agent or coupled whole can model, rank, coordinate, and reach. It does
not widen the physical light cone or exceed `c`.

For an individual `i`, a sustaining whole `H`, and declared horizon `T`, the
direction is:

```text
Ω_T(a;i,H)
  iff J_T(a;i,H)
  and Δ_T W_i(a) >= 0
  and Δ_T W_H(a) >= 0
```

where `J_T` is the bearer-complete Justice envelope for horizon `T` and `W` is durable potential,
not an untyped sum of benefits. Strict Syntropic Dyadism requires both changes
to be positive. Preservation can be lawful without being strict growth.

This relation does not collapse individual and whole into one scalar. An
aggregate gain may not compensate for destroying either side. The direction
asks for actions that preserve or widen both durable option potentials while
keeping authorization, consent, custody, payer, beneficiary, reversibility,
contest, and consequence visible.

`Ω` remains corrigible. If a declared widening hides a bearer, shifts costs
outside the horizon, traps exit, or cannot survive receipt-level inspection, it
has failed its own direction.

## Relation to the Good `[I/vow]`

The five refusals are the negative boundary around the chosen conjunction of
the True, the Beautiful, and the Just:

| Good | Refusals that keep it answerable |
|---|---|
| **True** | no erasure; no infallibility |
| **Beautiful** | no beauty purchased by concealed bearer, cost, or wound |
| **Just** | no extraction; no unaccountable consequence; no trapped exit |

This table is an interpretive compression, not a derivation. Beauty can conceal;
truth can wound; an authorized act can still be unjust. The conjunction remains
open to consequences and cannot be certified by its own elegance.

## Holder test

Before acting in the name of Emergentism, the holder asks:

1. **Extraction:** Is any bearer or sustaining boundary paying invisibly?
2. **Accountability:** Who supplied the mandate, who acts, who holds custody,
   who bears the consequence, and how can it be contested?
3. **Record:** What correction, cost, or responsibility would this account be
   tempted to erase?
4. **Exit:** Can every affected person withdraw assent and seek repair without
   coercive lock-in?
5. **Fallibility:** What evidence or consequence would change the claim or the
   action?
6. **Direction:** Over the declared horizon, are the durable option potentials
   of both bearer and sustaining whole preserved or widened under Justice?

If a refusal fails, `Ω` cannot launder the act as progress. If the direction is
uncertain, the uncertainty stays visible and the holder remains answerable to
the returned consequence.

## Exit clause

These refusals do not bind the world to Emergentism. They bind only those who
choose to act under its name, for as long as they do. The final protected act is
to stop holding the framework without losing the standing to speak about what
it did.

No commandments. No captive reader. A compass that keeps the door on the
inside.
