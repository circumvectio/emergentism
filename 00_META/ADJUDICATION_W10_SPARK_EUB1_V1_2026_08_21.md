# W10-SPARK × EUB-1 v1.0

## Additive ruling

The 2026-08-13 adjudication remains preserved. Its phrase
“cross-architecture agreement” is narrowed for EUB-1 v1.0:

> Agreement between systems is a **robustness, correlation, and disagreement
> diagnostic**. It is never independent evidence that a proposition is true.

Architecturally different systems may share training data, benchmark exposure,
human sources, elicitation artifacts, and correlated priors. Their agreement
can expose or weaken a claim about robustness; their disagreement can refute
the idea that internal coherence alone tracks truth. Neither outcome supplies
ground truth.

## Truth-discriminating tests

Where a fixture permits truth discrimination, EUB-1 uses predeclared:

1. identifiable causal targets or admissible equivalence classes;
2. held-out interventions with custodied outcomes;
3. held-out predictions with frozen scoring rules; and
4. explicit abstention targets where the evidence underdetermines the answer.

Cross-architecture agreement may appear in `EUBRunReceipt.v2` only under a
diagnostic disagreement/robustness field. It is excluded from the primary score
vector, pass conditions, and ontology claims.

## Framework comparison boundary

The Emergentist/Rosetta elicitation arm is one falsifiable condition among
matched controls. The first controlled dispatch-text pilot was negative
(-4.7%; 4/24 wins). EUB-1 therefore precommits to publish null effects or harms,
and the framework receives no scoring bonus, tie-break, or truth presumption.

## Kill

This compatibility ruling fails if any EUB-1 scorer, paper, receipt, or public
page converts model agreement into truth evidence or suppresses a null/harm
result from the Emergentist arm.
