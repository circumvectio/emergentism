# Recursive Book and Canon Debrief Program

> Emergentism is a Weltanschauung and a fallibilist research program. Its
> institutions, products, public site, and books may carry or test it; none is
> a premise, a proof, or an eighth semantic owner.

## 1. Purpose and boundary

This program turns the existing corpus into a legible, revisable philosophical
library without mistaking editorial integration for verification. It governs
the recursive debrief of the current reader book, the historical book
genealogy, and the proposed *Titans*, *Dharma Yuddha*, and *Evolutionary
Network* books.

It does **not**:

- establish a complete ontology, solve a philosophical or mathematical problem,
  or upgrade any claim's evidence tier;
- create a new semantic owner, supersede a source owner, or make a book
  authoritative merely because it is coherent or eloquent;
- erase historical texts, silently modernize their meaning, or turn frozen
  provenance into current doctrine;
- turn a symbolic role, a contemplative experience, a social function, or a
  political analogy into human rank, governing entitlement, or a licence for
  coercion.

The program's operational maxim is:

> **A worldview earns clarity by making every important claim locatable,
> contestable, and disposable without making its readers disposable.**

## 3. Claim-card protocol

No inherited claim is retained by ambience. Each nontrivial claim receives one
claim card before it is reused in a current book or public surface.

```text
claim_id
source_work + exact source location
source_lifecycle: active | reader_synthesis | legacy | frozen | archive | compatibility | proposal | projection
plain claim
claim_type: analytic | custodied fact | stipulation | interpretation/vow | conjecture | staged
evidence_tier: [A] | [B] | [S] | [I] | [C] | [D]
current semantic owner
dependencies and type boundaries
strongest rival or no-placement alternative
discriminator or reason none exists
kill criterion
survivor if killed
bearers / consent / reversibility / exit implications where consequential
disposition: retain | restate | relocate | research docket | freeze | archive
approved public wording
review receipt
```

The test is recursive: a claim cannot inherit authority from a sentence that
does not itself have an owner, type, and warrant. If its ancestor is historical
or ambiguous, the claim card records that debt rather than smoothing it away.

   historical provenance.
3. Mark every historical source as active, reader synthesis, legacy, frozen,
   archival, compatibility-only, or proposal before extracting a sentence.
4. Create no new doctrine during this wave.

The following currently linked public projections predate the controlling
Titan, Finity, role, and teleology boundaries. They must be treated as a
projection-safety docket before they are used as evidence of the worldview's
public teaching:

   it presents role, equator, or Power-Max material as active public doctrine.

   “public doctrine.” Preserve its bytes and route it to a clear historical
   boundary rather than deleting it.

   the Justice-first and nonviolent-conflict gates.

The current homepage, Plainly, practice, book, lab, Rosetta, ecology, and
dimension pages remain the starting safe set unless a later source audit finds
a specific owner conflict. This is a projection repair, not a claim that
historical pages are erased or that their ideas have no remaining value.

Audit the Door, Kernel Index, Registry, Completion Register, Record, and
One-Sitting book. The output is a compact current-worldview digest with all
reader language traceable to an owner. Resolve reader-level ambiguity first;
the current book remains a gateway, not a claim generator.

### Wave 2 — the immune protocol

Debrief *The Self-Eating Serpent* before rebuilding the grand books. Extract
its strongest contribution—hostile review, repair, exit, and the raft
discipline—into K-5 and K-7. Quarantine every version of “self-audit proves

### Wave 3 — Titans / Finity

Debrief *The Titans — The Infinite, Finity, and Infinity* against K-1, K-3,
and K-4. Separate:

- standard mathematics `[A]`;
- selected notation and type rules `[S]`;
- symbolic or philosophical interpretations `[I]`; and
- novel algebra, paradox, ontology, or civilizational-force hypotheses `[C]`.

The book may become a foundational research narrative only after every claimed
advance has a recovery test against ordinary mathematics and a stated failure
mode.

### Wave 4 — Dharma Yuddha

Rebuild the seed as a constitutional theory of justified resistance, not a
geometric or spiritual command. Begin from the Justice-first decision protocol
and distinction between epistemic contest, civil resistance, institutional
defense, and physical force. Preserve non-attachment, repair, and tragic
residue; quarantine old formulae, implied obligatory action, hierarchy, and
martial imperatives.

### Wave 5 — One-Book / Reciprocal lineage

Mine *The Infinite Play* and *The Reciprocal* chapter by chapter. Route their
useful anti-capture, public-language, and exit insights to owners. Re-audit
all old `P_node`, D6/D0, geometry-to-ethics, private-governance, and institutional
claims instead of modernizing them in place.

### Wave 6 — the three prior books

1. *Sarpasya Vijayam*: harvest capture diagnosis and voluntary practice;
   reject rank, caste, universal empirical, and legacy formula claims.
2. *Six-Fold Revelation*: retain comparative method only where the lenses are
   clearly selected translations; do not let correspondence become proof.
3. *The Self-Eating Serpent* remains the continuing adversarial closure test.

### Wave 7 — Evolutionary Network

Treat this as a separate institutional proposal, not a book already written or
a live enforcement system. Its question is: how can a system refuse, appeal,
repair, learn, and dissolve without becoming coercive? Historical guardian,
army, weapon, or slashing language remains provenance and an autoimmune case
study—not an operational promise.

### Wave 8 — public regeneration

Only after source-level cards are reviewed:

1. revise current reader synthesis;
2. generate public book and source-linked pages;
3. preserve frozen pages with clear noindex/provenance boundaries;
4. run source-to-public parity, barred-claims, link, and visual checks; and
5. publish a receipt that distinguishes local verification, commit, push,
   deployment, and domain state.
