# Emergentism Ontology Decision Matrix

> **Purpose.** This is a decision-support profile of what the current corpus
> can honestly claim. It does not decide truth, amend doctrine, create a new
> semantic owner, or rank human beings or traditions. A score records a claim's
> present maturity on one named axis; it is never a total score for reality.

## 1. The question this matrix can answer

The useful question is not *"Has Emergentism won?"* It is:

> **At what strength can a particular Emergentist claim currently be used—as a
> formal result, a typed ontology, a paradox diagnostic, a practical compass, a
> scientific research program, or a world-account?**

The answer must remain a **profile**, not an average. Formal elegance cannot
compensate for absent world contact; a useful ethical vow cannot prove an
ontology; component evidence cannot establish an integrated scientific theory.

The active ontology owner gives the decisive ceiling: as presently assembled,
the bare ontology is a method, posture, vow, and geometry—not yet a
world-picture accounting for matter, life, and mind. The science relation is a
candidate translation grammar, not a unification theory, until it derives
common dynamics, constants, or a novel replicated discriminator. See
[Degrees of Freedom Ontology](../06_ONTOLOGY/02_THE_DEGREES_OF_FREEDOM_ONTOLOGY.md)
§5 and the [Grand Puzzle Assembly Ledger](00_THE_GRAND_PUZZLE_ASSEMBLY_LEDGER.md)
§§6–7.

## 2. Shared assessment scale

The score is not an evidence tier. A formal `[A]` result may score highly for
its stated mathematics while scoring zero for world-fit; an empirical `[B]`
component may support one narrow bridge while leaving the whole synthesis open.

| Score | Meaning on the assessed axis |
|---:|---|
| **0** | Absent, retired, known broken, or not currently admissible as a claim. |
| **1** | Named and scoped, but only a proposal, interpretation, or research route. |
| **2** | Typed, owned, rival-aware, and conditional or falsifiable; no decisive external adjudication. |
| **3** | Internally audited or established in its proper native domain; boundaries and survivors are explicit. |
| **4** | Strongest available result: a formal proof inside named assumptions, or a preregistered independently replicated empirical discriminator. |

### Non-compensatory rules

1. **No world-account score from internal coherence.** A stable D-register does
   not establish the contents of reality.
2. **No derived ethic score.** Justice is a chosen, bearer-complete constraint;
   it is not geometry's conclusion.
3. **No unification score from a crosswalk.** Each science row owes native
   recovery, a non-permutation challenge, incremental discrimination, and
   independent replication.
4. **No universal paradox score from a diagnostic.** A category correction may
   dissolve one formulation while leaving the underlying question open.
5. **No total.** Do not add rows. A zero on a required gate remains visible.

### Current ontology decision

**Use now:** an internally coherent, type-disciplined Weltanschauung and
research program. It is especially strong at preventing category errors and
keeping ontology, science, ethics, and practice from borrowing each other's
warrant.

**Do not use now:** a complete account of reality, a formally proven global
ontology, or evidence that the selected world-picture is true.

The [Internal Completion Register](00_EMERGENTISM_INTERNAL_COMPLETION_REGISTER.md)
defines “complete” as stable ownership, type, tier, and no live contradiction;
it explicitly does not mean empirical truth or metaphysical completeness.

## 4. Paradox-adjudication profile `[I]`

The matrix uses four distinct verdicts:

- **formal correction** — a conclusion follows in a named formal domain;
- **diagnostic** — a type or frame error is exposed;
- **reframe** — the question receives a more precise formulation but remains
  substantively open;
- **resolution** — a rival-aware answer survives its named criterion.

No present category earns a cross-rival, independently replicated resolution
score. Historical paradox papers must not be treated as current authority when
their successor or tombstone says otherwise.

## 5. Science-hierarchy and unification profile `[I]`

The D-register may organize scientific questions **without** reducing every
science to a rung or treating the ordering as a hierarchy of worth. It is a
recurrence-not-reduction address grammar:

```text
D0–D1  boundary, distinction, mathematics, and formal constraints
D2     configuration, relation, model structure
D3     probability-bearing quantum-state description
D4     actual systems, instruments, records, and domain sciences
D5     represented counterfactual content studied through D4 models and methods
D6     exit/nonclosure; explicitly not a science
```

Biology, neuroscience, cognitive science, social science, physics, and
mathematics retain their native objects, methods, and warrants. D4 is not one
discipline that supersedes them; it marks actuality and record across them.

| ID | Requirement | Current score | Current status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **H1** | Type-preserving organization | **2/4** | Stable internal address map; native scientific warrant is meant to remain intact. |
| **H2** | Native-science recovery | **1/4** | Some rows have external neighbours and recovery obligations, but no force/freedom leg is calibrated and no Standard-Model-grade bridge exists. |
| **H3** | Cross-disciplinary compression | **2/4** | The map usefully aligns level-specific concepts, macro-constraint questions, teleonomy, and model-mediated future effects without claiming transferred proof. |
| **H4** | Novel held-out discriminator | **1/4** | GP-01 through GP-11 provide routes; the integrated-adequacy test is deferred and no whole-system discriminator has fired. |
| **H5** | Independent replication | **0/4** | Framework-designed simulations and local artifacts do not establish independent replication. |
| **H6** | Scientific unification | **0/4 / not yet admissible** | No shared dynamics, constants, recovered native theory, non-permutation result, novel replicated discriminator, or integrated theory result exists. |

### Promotion sequence for a science row

```text
native objects and equations
  → type-preserving D-placement
  → accepted-domain recovery
  → permutation and serious-rival challenge
  → preregistered incremental prediction or intervention
  → independent replication
  → only then: joint unification assessment
```

This sequence follows the active [Known Unknowns Program](00_KNOWN_UNKNOWNS_PROGRAM.md),
[External-neighbor crosswalk](../03_METHODOLOGY/00_EXTERNAL_COMPONENT_CALIBRATION_2026_07_20.md),
and [Empirical Program Board](../03_METHODOLOGY/00_EMPIRICAL_PROGRAM_BOARD.md).

## 7. Current public-safe summary

> **Emergentism currently offers a coherent, type-preserving way to organize
> philosophical and cross-disciplinary questions. Its formal components retain
> their native proofs; its broader ontology, paradox readings, ethical
> orientation, and science map remain tiered research claims. It is not yet a
> complete account of reality or an empirically unified science.**

## 8. Change discipline

- Update a row only when its cited owner, test receipt, or independent review
  changes.
- Retain prior scores and reasons in a dated change log; do not rewrite a past
  verdict as though it was always current.
- Do not promote an `[I]` or `[C]` bridge because an inherited component is
  `[A]` or `[B]`.
- Do not use this matrix to decide doctrine. Source owners and the evidence
  contract remain controlling.

*The matrix is strongest when it makes an attractive conclusion wait for the
world.*
