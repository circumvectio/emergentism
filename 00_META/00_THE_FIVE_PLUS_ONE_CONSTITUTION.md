# The 5+1 Constitution

> **[金] Kintsugi authority seam (2026-07-19).** The earlier canonical text
> made K2 a universal worldview fence and treated `η`, the reciprocal chart,
> and Ω as if geometry itself supplied morality and telos. The repair keeps the
> useful five-refusals-plus-direction topology while replacing universal K2
> with a typed `AuthorizationEnvelope`, treating `η` as a necessary-but-not-
> sufficient extraction diagnostic, and declaring Ω as a revisable normative
> direction under Justice. K2 survives only as one private-DAV implementation
> and as provenance of the 2026-05-30 ruling. The superseded pre-repair body is
> preserved in Git blob `8924662005f19ae3277d1bb8c8edadea825e408c`.

## 0. What the Constitution Is

The 5+1 is a compact operating constitution for Emergentist projects. It is
not a law of nature, a complete ethic, or an ontological proof. `[S]` means the
framework adopts the rule; `[I]` means the rule's proposed interpretation or
cross-domain value; `[B]` means a dated receipt exists.

The five refusals bound action. The `+1` states what action should seek within
those bounds. Keeping constraint and direction separate is the survivor of the
older five-versus-six dispute.

## 1. The Five Refusals and the +1

| # | Constitutional requirement | Refusal | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Non-extraction** | refuse sustained, hidden, or coerced substrate extraction; measure `η`, but do not treat it as a complete verdict | `[S/I]` |
| 2 | **Accountable authorization** | refuse consequential action without a complete authorization envelope | `[S]` |
| 3 | **Provenance / archive-first** | refuse silent erasure or unreceipted supersession | `[S]` |
| 4 | **Grace Exit** | refuse architectures whose affected participants lack a real, documented contest and exit path | `[S/I]` |
| 5 | **Self-correction with tier** | refuse infallibility, evidence inflation, and claims without an applicable test or revision path | `[S]` |
| +1 | **Ω: durable mutual option-cone widening** | seek increases in reachable, meaningful possibilities for individual and whole under Justice | `[I]` |

The enumeration is an adopted compression. Nature does not force the number
five, the extra direction, or these names.

## 2. Non-Extraction Is Necessary, Not Sufficient

`η` is an operational family of extraction diagnostics. A deployment must
declare its measurement rule, substrate, payer, beneficiary, interval, error
model, and kill criterion. The constitutional target is non-extraction;
unmeasured slogans do not establish `η=0`.

An action can be non-extractive yet negligent, unauthorized, deceptive, or
harmful to an omitted bearer. Therefore:

```text
admissible(a)  ->  nonextractive(a)
nonextractive(a)  -/->  admissible(a)
```

Bearer-complete Justice, consent, custody, reversibility, contest, and separate
commitment/outcome receipts remain required. No pole, sign, operator, caste,
deity, or coordinate has moral valence by identity.

## 3. Accountable Authorization Replaces Worldview K2

A consequential act requires:

```text
AuthorizationEnvelope :=
  principal + mandate + scope + consent + custody
  + expiry_or_revocation + contest_path
  + actor + consequence_bearer
```

Every field must be inspectable. Authorization does not prove that the act is
wise or just; it establishes who may decide, who acts, who bears the result,
and how the decision can be challenged.

K2 is a dated natural-person-signature implementation for private DAVs. PRISM
or another receipt-backed rail may implement authorization for public forms.
Neither is a primitive of reality, agency, the Soul Loop, ethics, or the
Compass. The historical 2026-05-30 K2 ruling remains `[B]` provenance; it does
not override this typed worldview contract.

## 4. Provenance and Archive-First

Supersession must name the prior claim, the reason for change, the surviving
content, the new tier, and the evidence or decision that licensed repair. The
old state remains recoverable through Git or the appropriate archive lane.

This is Kintsugi rather than amnesia: the seam stays visible. A new text does
not inherit the old text's authority merely because it replaced it.

## 5. Grace Exit

Every deployed agent, organ, entity, or protocol needs a documented withdrawal
and contest path that does not depend solely on the authority being contested.
Exit must specify custody, exportable or redeemable assets, liabilities,
timing, irreversible losses, and responsible actors.

Grace Exit does not promise “leave with everything,” zero cost, instant
liquidity, or immunity from lawful obligations. A claimed exit that cannot be
executed is not an exit.

The framework itself remains defeasible. Its optional `r₆:D6↝D0` image is an
interpretive non-μ return edge, not literal identity and not proof of exit.

## 6. Self-Correction with Tier

Formal claims require proofs and admit counterproofs. Empirical and conjectural
claims require predictions, rival models, observations, and kill criteria.
Structural definitions require explicit adoption and remain revisable.
Interpretive translations must not upgrade the source evidence they translate.

A falsifier changes only the claim it reaches. Downgrades and supersessions are
dated; a later repair must earn its tier again.

## 7. Ω: The Declared Direction

Ω names the framework's normative aim:

> widen durable, meaningful option cones for both individual and whole, without
> shrinking a protected bearer's durable potential below the Justice floor.

An option cone is the set of admissible histories an agent can model, rank,
coordinate, and reach. It is not the physical light cone and never exceeds
`c`. “Agents maximize the cone” is an Emergentist objective/hypothesis, not a
universal observed law.

For a declared horizon `T`, Power-Max supplies a transparent selected form:

```text
A_J = {a : J(a; all consequence bearers)
           and Delta_T W_i(a) >= 0
           and Delta_T W_H(a) >= 0}

a* in argmax_(a in A_J) E_Mt[W_i(T) | a]
```

`J` includes authorization, consent, custody, reversibility, contest, payer,
beneficiary, and visible consequence. Aggregate gain cannot launder a destroyed
bearer. Strict Syntropic Dyadism requires both durable individual and whole
potential to rise. Voluntary sacrifice is recorded separately and is never
demanded as proof of ethics.

This is a declared normative compass `[I]`, not a theorem that reality has Ω as
an attractor.

## 8. What the Reciprocal Chart Does—and Does Not—Add

For positive chart variables, the selected definitions yield:

```text
phi * nu = 1  <->  log(phi) + log(nu) = 0
```

That equivalence is analytic `[A]`. It does not imply non-extraction, Justice,
equilibrium, ethics, or Ω. `B=sin(theta)` measures balance in the selected
reciprocal chart; `P_node:=Phi*V` is a separately selected conjunctive score;
`η` is a separately operationalized extraction diagnostic. These quantities
may be compared in a declared model but must not be collapsed into one law.
