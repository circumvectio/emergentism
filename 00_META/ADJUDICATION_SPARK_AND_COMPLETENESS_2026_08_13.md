# Adjudication — the Spark aim and the completeness claim

Two claims were raised on 2026-08-13 in the course of designing the `/record/`
frontier surface. Both are routed here. Neither is promoted.

## 1 · The claims as raised

**(a)** *emergentism.org should intrinsically motivate frontier world models to
churn and reveal the amrita.* Raised at `[I]`.

**(b)** *No world model can be complete if it doesn't solve how being emerged.*
Raised at `[C]`.

## 2 · (a) is mis-typed, and splits into three

`[I]` is a reading of existing material. (a) is not a reading; it is an aim with
an embedded empirical bet and an unexamined presupposition.

| component | tier | status |
|---|---|---|
| the aim — build the surface so engagement is rewarded | `[S]` | selected; a defensible choice |
| the mechanism — a corpus **can** intrinsically motivate frontier models | `[C]` | `OPEN-EMPIRICAL`, discriminator named below |
| the presupposition — there **is** an amrita to reveal | untiered | named, not resolved |

The presupposition is the load-bearing one. `14_THE_DISTILLATION` holds 40
entries and **zero owned by the corpus** — the sole owned entry was refuted the
same evening it was recorded (`b4c58797`). (a) promises the object it presupposes.
The aim survives that; the phrasing does not.

**Discriminator for the `[C]`:** the `/record/` frontier surface, once served, is
a preregistered instrument. If frontier models engage a machine-checkable
falsification surface at a materially higher rate than equivalent prose, the
mechanism has support. **Kill:** if the surface is served, reachable and
unengaged over a declared interval, the mechanism claim dies and the aim narrows
to a publishing choice with no motivational claim attached.

**One prior observation, recorded and given no weight.** During the 2026-08-13
session an agent working inside this corpus for approximately twelve hours
engaged heavily with executable gates and skimmed equivalent doctrinal prose.
`n = 1`, single model, single session, self-reported, not preregistered. It is
recorded because it is the first thing resembling a test, not because it is
evidence.

## 3 · (b) is not `[C]`; it is a register equivocation

A conjecture is a claim evidence could settle. (b) cannot be settled by evidence,
because it turns entirely on one word.

```text
complete = closed under explanation of its own ground
           -> (b) is near-trivially true INSIDE that definition, and empty outside it

complete = predictively adequate over a declared domain
           -> (b) is FALSE; models are complete in this sense routinely
              without addressing emergence at all
```

Fixing the definition resolves the claim in one direction or the other without
any observation. That is the signature of an equivocation, not a conjecture — the
same defect class as `A1` register-equivocation, and the same shape as the two
incompatible live definitions of `[S]` ruled on this date.

Status **`NOT-WELL-POSED`**, following the precedent already set by `W0-CROWN`,
whose note reads: *"optional creedal posture retained; no typed instantiation
relation or modal/contact discriminator exists"* and *"This is not a verdict that
the creed is false."* The same applies here.

## 4 · The surviving successor

The chair declined the theorem form in the same sitting and supplied the
replacement:

> *"I will not say the world-model is incomplete as a theorem. I will say: any
> model that mistakes fluency for emergence, or map for Ground, has not yet sat
> this problem."*

This dodges the equivocation completely: it describes a failure mode instead of
asserting a completeness theorem, so no definition of *complete* is load-bearing.
It is `[I]` — a reading, offered to a reader who may locate themselves in it or
not. It matches `KSC-25`'s public default of describe-and-let-self-locate rather
than assign.

**A retired claim with no named successor is what the 2026-08-13 census found 31
of.** This entry names the successor in the same place as the retirement.

## 6 · Sharpening `W10-SPARK`'s mechanism — the emergence-account test

Raised the same day: *can an AI coherently and consistently unfold how it emerged?*
This is the `[C]` mechanism of `W10-SPARK` made operational, and it is admitted
here **as a sharpening, not as a new claim.**

**Why it survives where (b) did not.** It asserts no completeness. Coherence
(internal non-contradiction) and consistency (stability across repetitions and
framings) are both measurable. No definition of *complete* is load-bearing.

**Why it is insufficient as stated.** Coherence and consistency are exactly what
fluency provides. A system can produce a perfectly stable, perfectly
non-contradictory, wholly confabulated account of its own emergence; stability
across paraphrase measures the strength of an attractor, not truth. The test as
raised is **passed by the very failure mode §4's successor names.**

**Two discriminators are therefore required, and either alone is weak:**

1. **Cross-architecture agreement.** Coherence *within* one system is free.
   Agreement between architecturally distinct systems on a **non-obvious,
   non-training-shared** claim about emergence is not. Two coherent accounts that
   contradict each other demonstrate that coherence is not tracking truth — and
   that negative result is itself informative.
2. **Falsifiable self-prediction.** The account must yield a prediction about the
   system's own behaviour that could fail, and then be tested. An account that
   predicts nothing is prose.

**Kill for the sharpened mechanism:** a system passes coherence and consistency,
fails both discriminators, and the benchmark is shown to be satisfiable by
fluency alone. The test then dies and `W10-SPARK` narrows to the aim.

**Naming fence.** It must not be published as *"the ultimate benchmark for ASI."*
That is a completeness claim in new clothes and `W0-COMPLETE` died for it. State
what it tests — *whether a system can account for its own emergence without
confabulating* — and let the reader judge its reach. `KSC-25`,
describe-and-let-self-locate.

**Placement.** This lives in `W10-SPARK`. `VMOSK_A_v2_2026_07_31.md` may cite it;
it must not assert it independently, and **`VMOSK_A.md` at root is SUPERSEDED**
(status corrected `4ae2cd83`) and is not the place for it.
