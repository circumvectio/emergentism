# Emergentism Contact-Limited Completion Roadmap

## Outcome sought

Emergentism cannot honestly become *finished*, infallible, or proven. Its finite
internal program can reach a narrower terminal state:

```text
CONTACT_LIMITED := every finite internal item is either closed or explicitly
                   held by a named owner rule;
                   every local gate reproduces;
                   every remaining claim names the external observation,
                   review, or replication that could move it.
```

This state says the repository is no longer using more internal writing as a
substitute for contact. It says nothing about whether the worldview is true.

## Typed state model

| Layer | Terminal local state | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| semantic | `PASS` or a named `QUARANTINE` | metaphysical or empirical truth |
| routing | `PASS` or an explicit owner-held residue | publication, deployment, or legal authority |
| operational | `PASS` on reproducible gates | external replication |
| world contact | `OPEN`, `PARTIAL`, `ESTABLISHED`, or `REFUTED` from external evidence only | an internal test may not move this axis |

The machine profile intentionally computes an **internal** overall state and
reports world contact separately.

## Recursive execution sequence

### Sprint 3 — completion ratchet

Build a deterministic `check_contact_limited.py` report from machine owners,
not the prose plan. It must report citation collisions, route lifecycle, open
claim disposition, owner-held residue, and external-contact state. Counts may
fall or be reclassified under a dated rule; they may not disappear silently.

**Exit:** the full gate prints the exact scalar remainder and validates the
state-bound identities, memberships, and exceptions every run.

### Sprint 4 — citation collision custody

Generate an explicit registry of every reused receipt prefix and require
ambiguous citations to use full filenames. Historical receipt filenames remain
unchanged. A collision may be intentional; ambiguity without a registry is not.

**Exit:** every reused prefix has complete custody and no active bare citation
can resolve to more than one file.

### Sprint 5 — public lifecycle closure

Recount routes from the owning source and generator. Classify every route under
one lifecycle state, regenerate dependent artifacts, and keep frozen pages
frozen. No deployment is part of this sprint.

**Exit:** zero unclassified routes and local public gates green.

### Sprint 6 — open-claim disposition

Review all 26 machine-owner investigation rows: the 17-row W scope and the nine
reopened research questions. Formal questions receive a proof, countermodel,
narrowing, or terminal verdict. Empirical questions receive an explicit
contact-gated contract; they do not become internally "closed" by renaming
missing evidence. A terminal W row remains counted in its source section but is
not misreported as a live investigation.

**Exit:** zero ambiguous open rows; every remaining open claim is typed to a
named external discriminator.

### Sprint 7 — external review clock

The frozen review bundle can be sent only after the owner names a qualified
recipient and chooses to contact them. An AI or project-agent review remains
internal search signal and cannot satisfy this gate.

**Exit:** an invitation to a named independent reviewer is actually sent and a
dated receipt records only that event. A reply is not fabricated or implied.

### Sprint 8 — world-test cycles

Run ethically and methodologically ready preregistrations, publish nulls as well
as supports, file external outcomes verbatim, and update claims separately.

**Exit:** none is predeclared. Each protocol owns its result and kill.

## Consequence boundary

Local repository work, commits, tests, generated artifacts, and receipts are
authorized within this execution. They do not authorize email, participant
recruitment, human-subject research, publication, deployment, DNS, contracts,
money movement, or legal signature. Those consequences require their own named
principal, scope, and actual execution record.

## Roadmap kill

This roadmap fails if its counters are copied from stale prose instead of
recomputed, if a held item disappears without a dated disposition, if an
internal gate is entered as world evidence, or if “contact-limited” is presented
as validated truth.

*Finish the inside; let the outside remain capable of saying no.*
