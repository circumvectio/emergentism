## Comparative worldview questions

Emergentism should compete with religious, naturalist, idealist, existential,
pragmatist, scientific-realist and pluralist worldviews on separable questions:

- Does it state its primitives and type changes more clearly?
- Does it explain more with fewer protected assumptions?
- Does it recover what successful native theories already explain?
- Does it produce a novel, risky and replicated discriminator?
- Does it guide action while keeping bearers, uncertainty and tragic residue
  visible?
- Does it correct publicly, preserve failed claims and permit practical exit?
- Can non-adherents use its tools without accepting its metaphysics?

Religions and other durable traditions cannot be dismissed as incoherent or
extractive by label. Each must be evaluated at the level of actual claims,
institutions, practices, costs, benefits, correction and exit. The same rule
applies to Emergentism.

## Meaning of “win”

Winning means that, for a declared problem, the framework demonstrates greater
clarity, explanatory economy, prediction, practical consequence, correction
or voluntary usability than serious rivals. A local win stays local. It never
means adherents, rank, suppression, sacred authority or domination of persons.

Research completion may be replication, narrowing, death, deferment or freeze.
The matrix is successful when it makes those outcomes visible without letting
one attractive profile buy forgiveness for another profile's failure.
