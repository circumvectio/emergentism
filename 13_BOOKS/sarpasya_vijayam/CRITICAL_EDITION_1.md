# Sarpasya Vijayam

## 1. Diagnosis with rivals

Ask whether an institution preserves its label while its observed function,
cost distribution, correction or exit changes. Compare adaptation, scarcity,
specialization, capacity, preference and measurement error before naming
capture.

## 2. Mathematics under quarantine

The reciprocal chart retains its declared mathematics. Symbolic triads,
geometry, D-levels and operator names transfer no ontology, ethics, human rank
or proof. Historical failures remain visible rather than modernized.

## 3. Historical precedent

History can nominate mechanisms and counterexamples. It cannot establish a
present diagnosis by analogy. Every comparison names sources, causal rivals,
differences and what would defeat the mapping.

## 4. Nonviolent repair

Strategy contracts to voluntary inquiry, connection, refusal, record and
small-scale repair. No recruitment, enemy taxonomy, sovereignty or field
command survives.

## 5. Voluntary practice

Reflection and connection are optional. No fixed dose, health benefit,
privileged perception or spiritual class is promised. A user may stop without
loyalty cost.

## 6. Institution as proposal

A mesh is only a proposal with consent, custody, affected bearers, dissent,
cost, appeal, repair, sunset and exit. Ordinary institutions remain serious
rivals.

## 7. Dissolution and record

Stop, rename, fork and leave when a method fails. Dissolution never erases
outcomes, harm, ownership or repair. What remains is a testable practice, not a
self-validating worldview.
