# The Self-Eating Serpent

## A critical edition of the immune protocol

This edition is reconstructed from a preserved historical book. It is neither
the historical text nor a silent correction of it. Each chapter keeps one
procedural survivor and one refusal.

## 1. Review

A framework should invite a hostile reader to state what is overstated,
missing, unfalsifiable or harmful. The reviewer need not adopt the framework's
terms. A completed review is evidence that a review occurred—not that the
system is safe, true or corrected.

## 2. Application

Illustrative applications nominate tests. They do not demonstrate benefit.
Every claim needs a fair baseline, affected-bearer record, outcome measure and
case in which the framework could perform worse. A corpus with only favorable
examples has not passed world contact.

## 3. Turn

Founder charisma, special vocabulary, AI fluency, metrics and the pleasure of
diagnosis can capture a correction process. Naming these mechanisms does not
neutralize them. Outside review and observed correction remain necessary.

## 4. Trap

A movement, consultancy, foundation, application or dashboard may preserve the
language of correction while shifting cost, blocking dissent or rewarding the
metric. These are scenarios to test, not accusations inferred from a label.

## 5. Dissolution

A map can be narrowed, forked or put down. But symbolic self-dissolution does
not erase affected people, records, ownership, liability or repair. Failure
remains visible after the vocabulary is gone.

## 6. Seed

What survives is optional practice: make the claim explicit, compare a rival,
act at a reversible scale, observe the outcome, repair harm and protect exit.
No loyalty test, health promise, privileged perception or enforcement organism
is inherited with it.

> The serpent is not safe because it can bite itself. It is corrigible only to
> the extent that criticism, consequence, repair and exit work outside the
> story it tells about itself.
