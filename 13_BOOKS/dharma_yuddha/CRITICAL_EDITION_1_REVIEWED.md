# Dharma Yuddha

## Accountable resistance under Justice

This critical edition contains no sacred command, human rank or authorization
for violence. *Dharma* is an optional comparative vocabulary. The plain
constitutional protocol remains fully usable without it.

## 1. Justice first

Justice is declared rather than derived from geometry. A move names facts,
uncertainty, authority, affected bearers, consent, rivals, consequence, contest,
repair and exit. The objective-morality question remains a separate research
program.

## 2. Two horizons

`T_S` is the shortest interval that can honestly distinguish the move from a
serious rival. `T_L` is the longest interval for which bearers, causal pathways,
costs, uncertainty and revision remain nameable. Orient by `T_L`; commit only
to the next reversible receipt at `T_S`.

## 3. Nonviolent resistance

Inquiry, counter-speech, replication, refusal, witness, association, lawful
protest, mutual aid and voluntary exit are distinct forms. Their material and
retaliation costs remain visible. Shared conviction never validates a claim or
turns a person into an enemy class.

## 4. Institutional defence

Due process, lawful boundaries, audit, appeal, whistleblower protection,
temporary containment, repair and dissolution protect the possibility of
correction. Physical force remains outside the framework's authority; no
symbol, role, emergency narrative or civilizational goal supplies permission.

## 5. Repair, exit and residue

Prefer protection from irreversible harm, correction capacity, smaller
reversible action, dissent protection and visible cost. Keep the values that
could not be jointly satisfied, the people who paid and the repair still owed.
Any holder may reject the Dharma vocabulary and retain the accountable record.
