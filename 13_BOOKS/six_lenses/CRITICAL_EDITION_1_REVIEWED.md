# The Six Lenses

## Preface — selected comparisons

The lenses are questions a reader may apply, not a complete analytical genome.
No correspondence supplies proof and no first-person report outranks public
evidence.

## I. Ontological

What exists, depends, persists and disappears under intervention? Keep service,
dependency, harm and exit distinct; do not infer Ground or evil essence.

## II. Epistemological

Which warrant supports the claim? First-person reports are data with scope,
not privileged ontological access. Correction and public replication remain
separate.

## III. Axiological

Who benefits, pays, consents, contests, repairs and exits? No geometry or score
settles value; difficult trade-offs preserve actual bearers.

## IV. Teleological

Compare stated purpose with incentives and observed consequence across declared
horizons. Reject prophecy and existential pressure.

## V. Methodological

Use rivals, negative controls, receipts, whistleblower protection, fork and
repair. A method supplies no governing authority.

## VI. Comparative sacred action

Sacred vocabularies may be studied as interpretations. Consent, ordinary
authority, consequence and AI non-sovereignty remain primary. No ritual or
symbol commands action.

## Synthesis

The six lenses may reveal different aspects of one case while remaining
incompatible or incomplete. Convergence nominates a question; it never proves
one total ontology.
