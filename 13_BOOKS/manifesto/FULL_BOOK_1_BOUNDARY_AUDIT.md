# Full Book 1 — Boundary Audit

## Purpose and non-authority

This record specifies what may enter a full staged manifesto book, in which
editorial layer, and under which release ceiling. It is a firewall, not a
source of doctrine, evidence, authorization, or publication approval. The
book must remain a projection of its named claim-card owners. `[S]`

The proposed editorial move is to make the full manifesto the rebuilt reader
output of `COMP-ACTIVE-01-WELTANSCHAUUNG`, rather than a fourth active book or
an eighth owner. The composition remains a staged, unconfirmed proposal. Its
former “entrance layer” description has now been replaced by a Book I
Preamble-and-Quickstart role in the staged architecture. `[S/I]`

## Non-negotiable classification

| Class | May contain | Mandatory label and treatment | May enter a current/public body? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `current_body` | Claim cards whose `public.state` is `bounded_current`, pinned to their reviewed source revision. | Card IDs, source work, tier, and source revision must be visible to the contract. Conjectural cards retain their conditional wording. | Yes, after all book and public gates pass. |
| `research_record` | `candidate` cards, or `source_only` cards rendered from their approved critical/research editions. | Mark the chapter and every claim box as research/staged; retain rival, discriminator, kill/narrow route, and no-current-body disposition. | No. It may exist only in the private staged full-book artifact. |
| `historical_provenance` | Read-only legacy/archive source identity, edition history, debrief disposition, and an unstrengthened bibliographic pointer. | State that it is historical and non-current; use the critical edition/debrief rather than silently modernizing source text. | No substantive historical claim enters the body. |
| `custody_only` | Preservation metadata, hash, route, and reason for quarantine. | No regenerated explanatory prose or claim reuse. | No. |
| `barred` | Withheld artifacts, uncaptured/uncarded source modules, and retired claims. | Exclude from generated prose; at most list the route and withholding reason in a non-substantive custody table. | No. |

`legacy`, `frozen`, `archive`, and `withheld` material cannot become current
through quotation, generation, or publication. Reader prose must remain no
stronger than its source owners. `[S]` [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

## Full-book contract requirements

The full-book contract must replace the short-draft assumption that every
paragraph is a `current_body` paragraph. It must contain, for every chapter
and marked prose unit: `[S/I]`

1. `classification` from the five classes above;
2. unique `claim_card_ids`, source work ID, pinned source SHA, owner IDs, and
   evidence tier(s);
3. a paragraph/line coverage map proving 100% substantive coverage;
4. for `research_record`, the rival, discriminator, kill/narrow condition, and
   conspicuous no-public-body label;
5. for historical/custody material, only provenance metadata plus a route to
   its debrief or withholding record;
6. a single primary projection home per card; reference-only appearances may
   link but do not obtain explanatory ownership; and
7. deterministic generation and an index that excludes non-current material
   from public RAG, search, sitemap, service-worker, and route manifests.

## Review gates

1. **L5 source map:** reconcile the full-book designation with the staged
   three-book topology and resolve primary-home conflicts before prose grows.
2. **L1 lifecycle gate:** reject every paragraph that violates the table above.
3. **L2 rival gate:** reconstruct the strongest ordinary rival for every
   research claim and every practical claim of incremental benefit.
4. **L3 claim gate:** verify card IDs, owner, source SHA, tier, lifecycle,
   line coverage, citations, barred-claim scan, and no claim strengthening.
5. **L4 writing gate:** write only after the applicable source/card packet is
   complete; no source-owner edits occur in this projection audit.
6. **L6 custody gate:** retain historical text, debriefs, freezes, and kills;
   never delete or overwrite them.
7. **L7 reader gate:** compress only after the classification is intact; do not
   make a research chapter sound like an earned world claim.
8. **Release gate:** public consideration requires source parity, fresh-reader
   tier comprehension, hostile review, deterministic build, public barred-claim
   checks, predeploy, immutable-artifact audit, and separately verified hosting.

## Status after the first full-book routing pass

- **B1 — topology conflict — resolved in the staged map:** the composition now
  names the full manifesto as its Book I output, and the short text is its
  Preamble and Quickstart. The editorial architecture remains unconfirmed and
  private; resolution is not publication approval. `[S/I]`
- **B2 — lifecycle conflict — resolved in the private full build:** Self-Eating
  and Sarpasya are restricted to research/historical apparatus; all frozen
  Reciprocal cards route to custody only. The deterministic builder and
  paragraph-ledger test now enforce those labels for the assembled manuscript.
  A separate public generator must still preserve the same exclusion. `[S]`
- **B3 — coverage boundary — controlled:** a full import of *The Lived Compass*
  beyond the Finity Card, or of uncarded Titans modules, remains excluded from
  this book because it fails the 100% card-coverage rule. Card source owners
  must be extended before any such chapter is written. `[S]`
- **B4 — delivery boundary — open:** no public route may expose `research_record`,
  historical, custody, or barred material. The staged full book remains private
  until a separately reviewed public edition is built. `[S]`

## Firewall conclusion

Full Book 1 can be substantial without becoming totalizing: current reader
chapters carry the bounded core; research chapters carry their own uncertainty;
critical genealogy preserves how earlier claims were narrowed; and custody
records keep barred material visible without reviving it. `[I]`
