# The Emergentist Manifesto

## A worldview for finite beings

This is the full-reader architecture for Book I of the proposed three-book
corpus. It rebuilds `COMP-ACTIVE-01-WELTANSCHAUUNG`; it does not create a fourth
book, a new doctrine, or a new semantic owner. The existing One-Sitting edition
remains the source-reader spine. The Preamble and Quickstart remains preserved
at [`MANIFESTO_DRAFT_0.md`](MANIFESTO_DRAFT_0.md). The assembled private
manuscript is [`MANIFESTO_BOOK_1.md`](MANIFESTO_BOOK_1.md): it is complete for
the local source-mapping and lifecycle gate, but it is neither public nor a
deployment.

The editorial target is a 60–85k-word private reader plus evidence and
genealogy appendices. That range is a build target, not a claim about the
present manuscript and not a publication promise. Depth must come from
argument, examples, rivals, sources, and failure conditions—not from reviving
withdrawn claims or repeating the same book under new headings.

## Reader path

1. **One sentence** — the map must be able to lose.
2. **One image** — map, alternatives, means, action, consequence, correction,
   and exit remain distinct.
3. **The short argument** — finite beings must act without a completed view.
4. **A ninety-minute core** — Parts I–III and the exit chapter, using only
   `bounded_current` cards.
5. **The full reader** — current core plus clearly labelled research and
   critical-genealogy apparatus.
6. **The record** — cards, owners, source revisions, dockets, rivals, kills,
   withheld boundaries, and historical custody.

This uses a compression-to-depth reading form only. It does not import a
state-building, sovereignty, territorial, membership, founder-entitlement,
alignment-test, census, cryptoeconomic, or growth-validation programme.

## Four editorial layers

| Layer | What it does | Public ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| **Current core** | Develops the finite-condition argument from reviewed `bounded_current` cards. | May be considered only after full source and public gates pass. |
| **Research record** | States live conjectures, formal work, practical proposals, and their rival/failure routes. | Private staged manuscript only. |
| **Critical genealogy** | Shows what earlier work retained, narrowed, or killed. | Historical apparatus only; no current authority. |
| **Custody** | Identifies frozen provenance without restating its claims. | No regenerated prose. |

The layer is part of the claim. A source-only or frozen module does not become
current because it appears late in a large book.

## What stays separate

*The Titans* remains the technical research companion. Its formal programme
requires definitions, standard recovery, countermodels, and independent review;
the manifesto cannot turn it into a completed algebra through rhetoric.

*The Lived Compass* remains the practical companion. Book I may introduce the
carded Finity worksheet, but only a future source-card wave can import its
broader practices as current reader chapters.

*Dharma Yuddha*, *Six Lenses*, *The Self-Eating Serpent*, *Sarpasya Vijayam*,
and *The Evolutionary Network* enter only in the labelled research or critical
layers specified above. They retain their own owners and release states.

*The Reciprocal / Infinite Play* is frozen. It receives a custody appendix and
historical route only; this book does not regenerate its claims.

## Build record and release boundary

1. **Completed locally:** the current core is assembled from
   `bounded_current` cards; every rendered substantive unit has a unique marker
   and source receipt.
2. **Completed locally:** research and historical material remains in labelled
   private layers with a card-atlas rival, discriminator, kill/narrow route,
   source revision, and public ceiling.
3. **Completed locally:** critical genealogy is drawn from the corrected
   editions/debriefs, and Reciprocal material is custody-only.
4. **Completed locally:** the deterministic assembly emits the manuscript,
   build receipt, paragraph ledger, card atlas, dockets, and custody note.
5. **Still open beyond local completion:** fresh-reader comprehension,
   independent hostile review, and a controlled component-matched Finity
   comparison; these are not satisfied by the build or its authors.
6. **Separate public decision:** a public edition must exclude non-current
   layers and pass the public boundary. No staged manuscript is a deployment.

The book’s closing discipline is its governing constraint: it may be criticized,
narrowed, forked, or put down. Its size does not make it an ontology; its
readership does not make it true.
