# Three books, one genealogy

The proposed architecture gives the corpus three active reader promises rather
than eight overlapping books. This is an editorial proposal about sequence and reuse. It creates no doctrine,
changes no evidence tier, and does not erase any historical edition.

| Active book | Unique job | Material absorbed without duplicate ownership |
|---|---|---|
| *Emergentism: A Worldview for Finite Beings* | worldview, finite agency, collective traces, criticism and exit | One-Sitting; Self-Eating immune protocol; selected Reciprocal and Sarpasya survivors |
| *The Titans: The Infinite, Finity & Infinity* | formal programme, standard recovery, Finity, paradox adjudication and failure | Titans; Foundation modules; formal Reciprocal and Sarpasya survivors |
| *The Lived Compass: Justice, Action & Institutions* | decisions, horizons, resistance, institutions, repair and exit | Finity practice; Dharma; Six Lenses; institutional and historical survivors |

*The Serpent Cycle* preserves *Sarpasya Vijayam*, *Six Lenses*, *The
Self-Eating Serpent*, and *Reciprocal / Infinite Play* as a critical genealogy.
It is a historical reader, not a fourth source of doctrine. The Evolutionary
Network remains a research dossier because its institutional advantage has not
been established. `RIP01-05` remains custody-only.

The machine contract in [`book-manifest.json`](book-manifest.json) assigns all
72 cards exactly one primary book, dossier, or custody route. A reference-only
appearance may supply context but cannot create a second owner or a second
primary home.

The overlap boundary is functional: Book I is the proposed primary projection
home for why the worldview must remain corrigible; Book II for what the formal
programme warrants; Book III for how consequential decisions and institutions
operate; and the historical reader for how the corpus arrived here. This staged
map remains unconfirmed and creates no semantic ownership. Outside a confirmed
primary home, a claim receives only a link or short bridge, never a second
explanatory chapter.

## Transition rule

Existing editions remain in place until their named successor exists and passes
claim coverage, source revision, parity, purity, link and public-boundary gates.
Only then may an edition be marked superseded. Superseded means preserved and
routed to genealogy or module use; it never means deleted.
