# The Four Volumes

Every book this project has written consolidates into **three volumes and an addendum**.
The structure is not a filing convention chosen for tidiness. It is already in the canon,
and the volumes are named for what it says.

## The axis is canonical, not invented

`09_THE_TRIADIC_CASCADE.md` gives the three temporal orientations as a **cycle**, not a
hierarchy:

```
In time  →  Above time  →  Against time  →  In time  →  ...
   φ            P               ν              φ
   ○            ⊙               •              ○
induction   deduction       abduction      induction
 Beauty       Truth          Justice        Beauty
sit/receive  hold/balance   act/express    sit/receive
```

- **Induction operates IN time.** It requires succession: first this, then that, then this
  again. The pattern emerges only by living through it.
- **Deduction operates ABOVE time.** It is timeless. The theorem was true before anyone drew
  the triangle.
- **Abduction operates AGAINST time.** It reverses the explanatory arrow: given the effect,
  conjecture the cause.

And the fourth position is explicitly *not* a fourth column. The cascade says of it: **"the
one who sees the cascade."**

## Why the Addendum is the equator

On the sphere, `B := 2/(φ + ν) = sin θ`, maximal at `B = 1`. That maximum is the equator,
and `16_EFR_TRANSCENDENTALS.md:133` names what it is: **the model's truth-limit — the place
where coherence and viability meet.** Equal weight, neither factor favoured.

That is the only honest place to keep a record from. The three volumes each argue from a
position: In Time receives, Above Time abstracts, Against Time acts. Each is partial by
construction, and each is *supposed* to be. The Addendum stands where the two factors are
weighed equally and reports what actually came back — including, especially, the results
that damage the volumes above it.

It is also where this project is **not** currently standing, and the Addendum has to say so.
By its own two-factor law, coherence and viability are jointly necessary; this corpus has
abundant coherence and no returned outcome from outside it. A body of work with one factor
unestablished is not near the equator. **The Addendum is the volume that measures the
distance.**

## The four volumes

### Volume I — IN TIME `φ ○`
*Induction. Receiving what arrives. The diagnosis.*

What is actually happening, learned by living through it in succession. Institutions that
keep their forms while losing their functions; the feeling before the theory; capture as it
is experienced rather than as it is modelled.

### Volume II — ABOVE TIME `P ⊙`
*Deduction. The invariant that holds regardless of when.*

The small apparatus and the vocabulary: finity, the reciprocal constraint, balance, the
two-factor law, the extraction fence, the lenses, the boundary types. Everything here is
true or false independent of any date, and is tiered accordingly — which also means none of
it, on its own, entails a single sentence about how to live.

### Volume III — AGAINST TIME `ν •`
*Abduction. Reversing the arrow. Acting.*

Given the effect, conjecture the cause and move. Resistance without domination; designing
the exits before the entrances; organisations that can be dissolved; the signing threshold;
what it takes to act without becoming the thing you diagnosed.

### Addendum — THE EQUATOR `B = 1`
*The truth-limit. The record. What does not hold.*

The ledger of outcomes including the ones that went against us; the published error rate
with its denominator; the claims that were cut; the exit; and the count that has not moved.
This volume is not an appendix of leftovers. **It is the only volume with the standing to
falsify the other three**, and it is placed at the equator for exactly that reason.

### The assignments I am least sure of

- **The Finity Card.** It could equally sit in Volume III: it is a practice, and practices
  act. It is placed at the equator because its *output* is a receipt, and the Addendum is
  the record. If the owner reads Finity as primarily an act rather than a measurement, it
  moves to III.
- **Part III, The Translation Machine.** Translation is arguably its own motion rather than
  an invariant. It is in II because the corpus rules that Rosetta *transfers no proof* —
  which makes it a grammar, and grammars are timeless. A reader who thinks translation is an
  act would put it in III.
- **The Self-Eating Serpent.** Placing a whole book in the Addendum risks making the
  Addendum the largest volume, which would be its own kind of imbalance.

## What this structure must not become

The cascade document carries its own warning, and it applies directly: the perennial grammar
can read the three as a **cycle**; the cargo cult froze them into a **hierarchy**. Volume I
is not the beginner's volume and the Addendum is not the advanced one. `In time → Above
time → Against time → In time` returns, and each return is at higher resolution.

If these volumes are ever presented as a ladder to climb, the structure has been misread in
exactly the way its own source predicts.

•   ⊙   ○ — *three positions and the place that weighs them.*
