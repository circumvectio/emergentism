# The Reciprocal / Infinite Play

## 1. Diagnosis without a villain

Compare stated and observed function, benefit, cost, correction and exit.
Adaptation, scarcity, specialization and heterogeneous preference remain live
rivals to capture.

## 2. Formal boundaries

The reciprocal chart is mathematics in its declared domain. Finity and the
Titans supply selected boundary language. No formula proves ontology, value,
human rank or social law.

## 3. Translation without proof

The lenses and Rosetta may compare questions across domains only while native
objects, mechanisms, equations and evidence remain primary.

## 4. Collective traces

Living ideas and Egregoreotypes are carrier-and-trace hypotheses. A five-marker
pattern is not consciousness, personhood, intention or Justice.

## 5. Institutional firewall

Historical governance and AI examples are provenance only. Any present
institution begins again as a voluntary, appealable, repairable and dissolvable
proposal.

## 6. Immune protocol

Loyalty, founder, vocabulary, metrics and self-audit can capture a framework.
Outside criticism, observed correction and practical exit remain necessary.

## 7. Seed and record

Retain the smallest testable practice, failed claims, affected bearers and
unfinished repair. A holder may fork or leave; dissolution never validates the
map.
