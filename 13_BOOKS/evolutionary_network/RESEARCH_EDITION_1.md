# The Evolutionary Network

This is a voluntary institutional proposal, not an enforcement organism. Its
question is whether specialization can remain corrigible when entry, dissent,
repair and dissolution are designed first.

## 1. Voluntary entry

Entry has a bounded mandate, custody, expiry, costs, revocation and practical
exit. No worldview, ritual, score or human type is required.

## 2. Appeal

Proposal, authorization and execution remain separate. Affected people may
contest in ordinary language. No actor is the sole reviewer of its own
irreversible consequence.

## 3. Repair

Outcome receipts, bearer challenge, correction and funded repair decide
learning. Self-issued success is never enough.

## 4. Dissolution

Sunset, data export, property return, record custody and unfinished obligation
are declared before activation. Closing a unit never erases liability.

## 5. Institutional-failure rivals

Compare ordinary management failure, scarcity, incentives, information,
heterogeneous preferences, law, security, measurement and concentration. If a
simpler institution protects delivery, correction, dissent and exit better,
use it instead.
