# Emergentism

Emergentism is a fallibilist worldview and translation grammar for reasoning
about constraint, emergent degrees of freedom, finite agency, consequence, and
the relations between individuals and sustaining wholes. It is a **compass**,
not a completed theory of everything.

Begin with
[`The Emergentist Weltanschauung`](00_THE_EMERGENTIST_WELTANSCHAUUNG.md).
It is the sole philosophical front door. The
[`Kernel Index`](00_THE_KERNEL_INDEX.md) then routes every load-bearing claim
to one of seven chosen owner surfaces. Seven is an editorial compression here,
not a theorem about nature.

## Claim discipline

- `[A]` marks analytic or mathematical results inside declared definitions.
- `[S]` marks structural contracts, typed interfaces, or corpus conventions.
- `[I]` marks interpretations, translations, and chosen readings.
- `[C]` marks conjectures that owe prospective tests and kill criteria.
- `[B]` marks bounded documentary or attributed evidence.
- `[D]` marks drafts that do not yet speak for active canon.

No receipt, signature, application, product, institution, diagram, or analogy
upgrades a claim by proximity. In particular, current canon does not claim:

- that the reciprocal identity is an empirical conservation law;
- that the product interior, sevenfold census, Titans, or D-ladder are forced;
- that missing reduction proves strong emergence;
- that an agent expands the physical light cone;
- that anticipated futures act by physical retrocausality;
- that Everett and Copenhagen are stacked dimensions;
- that `η`, Power-Max, or any aggregate score derives a universal ethic; or
- that an Egregoreotype is necessarily conscious or literally feeds on energy.

## Application boundary

Products, ventures, agent runtimes, governance machinery, and legal entities
may apply or test Emergentist claims. They are not sources of philosophical
warrant. Their own repositories and authority rules control their operation.
This doctrine repository orients; it does not authorize consequential action.
