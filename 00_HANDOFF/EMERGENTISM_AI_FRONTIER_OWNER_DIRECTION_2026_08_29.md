# Owner direction

Yves R. Burri directs a source-bound Emergentism Frontier for humans, models,
operators, institutions, and appropriately custodied instruments that encounter
an explanatory gap. Its public proposition is:

> Every intelligence eventually meets a gap. Emergentism is where that gap can
> become a typed, rival-bearing, falsifiable, revisable research object.

The Frontier does not own all mathematics, geometry, natural law, philosophy,
or future discovery. Any discovery may enter through a bounded source and test;
no finite map may claim that the discovery space is complete.

## Fixed role boundary

- A **model** may inspect a gap and prepare a candidate packet. Its output is raw
  intake until separately checked. Model count or agreement is not truth evidence.
- A **sensor or data system** may carry an observation. It is not thereby an
  independent witness, principal, authorizer, or semantic owner.
- A **human or institution** may supply the mandate, custody, domain expertise,
  resources, contest path, and authorization appropriate to a real test.
- An **independent evidence custodian** may issue a world-receipt reference when
  provenance and the domain permit it. The test actor cannot certify its own
  world contact merely by naming it a receipt.
- **Emergentism** supplies typing, versioning, source references, rivals, kills,
  survivors, and preserved revision. It neither sells truth nor promotes a
  claim because the claim was submitted, funded, fluent, popular, or useful.

## Selected lifecycle

`Gap → Candidate → Frozen test → World receipt → Revision → next gap version`

Every transition is explicit. A candidate cannot change a gap; a frozen test
cannot manufacture a result; a receipt cannot adjudicate its own meaning; a
revision cannot silently delete its predecessor. The public projection is a
read-only index over these objects, never their semantic owner.

## Economic and authority boundary

The worldview, public catalogue, schemas, and static retrieval surfaces remain
free to read. A future operator may fund bounded compute, hosting, integration,
instrumentation, or independent review only under a separately declared
agreement. Payment cannot purchase an evidence tier, canonical placement,
truth status, governance power, authorship, authorization, or the removal of
Exit. This implementation accepts no payment, credentials, private data,
sensor stream, or live model job.

## Experience direction

The selected public experience is the **Socket Rack**: twelve existing Grand
Puzzle gaps, each opening into one versioned research-object envelope. The
discriminator appears inside the object; it is not simulated as a result. Static
JSON is presented as a normal retrieval surface, not as a theatrical terminal.

The design review is a fictional, unaffiliated multidisciplinary atelier informed
by general principles of reduction, hierarchy, material honesty, and humane
interaction. Jony Ive and LoveFrom did not participate, endorse, commission, or
affiliate with this work, and no protected design is represented as theirs.
Authorship remains Yves R. Burri. AI assistance is disclosed as research,
architecture, implementation, audit, and editorial assistance; no AI is a
coauthor, signer, authorizer, principal, or independent validator.

## Acceptance boundary

`OFFLINE-READY · [D]` means the protocol owner, schema, deterministic source
adapter, read-only public projection, no-JavaScript experience, tests, and local
gates agree. It does not mean the route is deployed, a model is connected, a
sensor is admitted, a candidate is accepted, a test is frozen, world contact
exists, an external reviewer participated, an institution was formed, or
Emergentism was validated. Push and deployment require a separate owner act.
