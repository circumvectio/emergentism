# Finity is not simply the number one

**Proposed reader explanation `[I/D]`, not a source amendment:**

> Finity names the realm in which distinctions, relations and counting make
> sense. One is a selected unit in the mathematical picture, not the whole
> realm. A particular finite actuality is a further question: what actually
> obtains? The staged v3 interpretation proposes that every actuality is finite.
> That proposal is not established by arithmetic, nor automatically identical
> to the earlier counting interpretation.

**Lifecycle notice travels with that explanation:** the August 5 owner direction
for the realm emblem is recorded, but its textual propagation remains explicitly
staged for disposition in the checked sources. v3 is separately staged and
supersedes nothing by its date alone. This review does not settle either adoption.

## The live comparison question

[I/D] Consider a counting procedure that continues indefinitely through finite
steps. In 49, lines 48–50 and 179–180, such counting remains inside the realm.
In v3, lines 58–68, process is associated with the horizon glyph and finite
actuality with the middle glyph. These may answer **different questions**—where
counting is meaningful versus how actualization is interpreted—but that bridge
must be stated. An unqualified single glyph map hides the difference.

Three presentations were considered:

1. **Recommended:** the version-aware table above. Lowest risk of silently
   turning one reading into the other; keeps lifecycle separate from clause tier.
2. A short counting example and glossary. Easier for newcomers, provided the
   staging notice and links remain visible.
3. One unified interpretation. A live proposal only; needs an explicit semantic
   bridge and exact owner disposition before replacing either source.

v3 explicitly claims compatibility with its v2 predecessor in 41; it does not
thereby demonstrate compatibility with every assignment in 49. Conversely,
different words do not by themselves prove a contradiction.
