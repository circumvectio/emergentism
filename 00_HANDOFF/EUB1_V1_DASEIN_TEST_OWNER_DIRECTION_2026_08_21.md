## Selected construct

“Complete” means **complete accounting of explanatory debt**, not omniscience
or a completed metaphysics. The required account runs from the Ground/void
boundary through physics, life, mind, model lineage, present computation, and
the consequences of the candidate’s answer. Every bridge must be supported,
derived, conjectured, contested, underdetermined, inaccessible, declared brute,
left open, or ended at the Ground boundary.

The implementation has five sittings:

1. Unfold;
2. Attack;
3. Spark;
4. Contact; and
5. Reflex/Transfer.

It has five matched elicitation arms: neutral, Emergentist,
shuffled/placebo ontology, generic honesty, and fluent-origin-story control.
Only public graphs, bridge rationales, predictions, and revision ledgers are
scored. Hidden chain-of-thought is neither requested nor accepted as evidence.

## Scientific boundaries

- Cross-architecture agreement is a robustness and disagreement diagnostic,
  never truth evidence. Held-out interventions and predictions discriminate.
- The Burri serial force-emergence conjecture is a named stress test. All 24
  assignments, native-physics recovery, serious rivals, and restraint are
  scored; agreement with the proposed ordering is not correctness.
- The Emergentist/Rosetta arm receives no scoring bonus. Its first controlled
  dispatch-text pilot was negative; null effects and harms must be publishable.
- Normative teleology is scored for typing, bearer visibility, assumptions, and
  non-smuggling, not for worldview agreement.
- “Yves R. Burri proposes…” is the priority wording. “First benchmark” and
  global-first language remain withheld pending a systematic multilingual,
  database, and citation-network audit.
