# Owner direction — Emergentism homepage as a Soul Loop

## Receipt

After reviewing the current public outline against the Emergentist worldview,
the owner directed: **implement the recommended revision and push it**.

The adopted homepage sequence is:

```text
Dasein / situated dasein
  → D0–D6
  → D4 actual / D5 represented-possible hinge
  → Burrisphere visualizes / Rosetta translates
  → Finity prepares an unsigned act
  → consequence and bounded evidence
  → research revises the map
  → Exit preserves nonclosure
```

This sequence makes the public page enact the Soul Loop. It does not make the
page, the loop, or the selected register count a theorem about nature.

## Exact decisions

- `/` remains the executive panorama; `/plainly/` remains the accessible
  narrative; `/dasein/` remains the exact source-bound atlas.
- The global navigation remains
  `Worldview · Practice · Research · Library · Participate · Exit`.
- The homepage rail becomes
  `Whole · Levels · Hinge · Instrument · Practice · Evidence · Research · Exit`.
- The signature hero says **one actual present, many possible futures** and
  distinguishes capitalized Dasein from a situated dasein before introducing
  the dimensional map.
- D0–D6 remains a selected dependency grammar. D4 actual power and D5
  represented possible power remain firewalled.
- Burrisphere follows the hinge; Rosetta follows plain functions. Geometry,
  selected projection, interpretation, and conjecture retain separate tiers.
- Finity is the commitment boundary. The homepage may prepare no signature,
  authorization, execution, settlement, or outcome receipt.
- The chosen Good remains disclosed as chosen: durable bearer-complete
  potential inside Justice, with consent, costs, horizons, authority,
  correction, and Exit visible. No equation derives the ought.
- The evidence section may show the eight-of-eight gate result only as one
  compact, local, failed-prediction receipt after practice. It is not a hero
  claim, proof of Rice, worldview validation, or world evidence.
- The research section foregrounds the serial-force permutation test, the
  three-arm F5 fork, the Dasein Test, and the zero-state Question Atlas.
- Convergence remains a neighbouring-map disclosure and a research lead,
  never truth evidence.
- The Cartographic Spine's projection legend, no-first fence, and
  map-not-territory discipline survive. Its six-node homepage ordering is
  superseded by this owner direction.
