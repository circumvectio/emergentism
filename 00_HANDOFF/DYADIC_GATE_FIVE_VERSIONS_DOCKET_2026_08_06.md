# DOCKET — the dyadic gate, five versions

The discovery pass (2026-08-06) found that the dyadic gate — the corpus's most
distinctive ethical content — exists in five versions across five ACTIVE
documents, at four distinct logical strengths. All five verified on disk; each
quoted below with its strength. They do not agree.

## The five versions

**V1 — the inference-block version** `[A]`-structural
`04_AXIOLOGY/02_VALUE_THEORY/00_OBJECTIVE_MORALS_AND_ETHICS.md §4`:
the inference `Σ_(x∈B) ΔW_x > 0 therefore action a is just` is INVALID.
Aggregates may be reported descriptively; the Justice verdict keeps every
bearer visible; unresolvable conflict returns **no admissible action**, not
permission to choose the least visible victim.
*Strength: an invalidity claim about an inference form — the weakest and most
structural version; compatible with many resolutions.*

**V2 — the envelope-requirement version** `[I]` operational through `[S]` tests
`04_AXIOLOGY/00_THE_EXTRACTION_LAW.md`: "no aggregate laundering" as one of
nine conjunctive Justice-envelope requirements (alongside consent, custody,
authorization, contest, receipts), over the complete affected-bearer set `B(a)`.
*Strength: a conjunctive requirement inside an envelope — operational, not
structural; it binds acts, not inferences.*

**V3 — the both-rise version** `[I/S]`
`10_SEED/01_THE_SEED_LADDER/D5_THE_GAME.md`: "strict syntropy requires both
the bearer and the sustaining whole to rise… no aggregate laundering — a
positive group sum never licenses extraction from one member."
*Strength: the strongest positive form — a two-party rise requirement PLUS a
no-license clause. Note it is two claims in one sentence, and the first
(both-rise) is strictly stronger than the second (no-license).*

**V4 — the coordination-rule version** `[C]`
`00_THE_WELTANSCHAUUNG_ONE_SITTING.md:481`: "bearer-horizon vectors are
compared without aggregate laundering; reversible and informative moves break
unresolved ties. The real-world adequacy of this coordination rule remains [C]."
*Strength: a defeasible comparative heuristic, explicitly conjectural.*

**V5 — the separation version** (navigational compression)
`00_META/00_THE_COMPASS.md:227`: "aggregate laundering | keep each bearer,
payer, and beneficiary separate."
*Strength: a fence row — a compression of one of the above, unspecified which.*

## The non-equivalences

1. **No-license ≠ both-rise.** V3's second clause (a positive group sum never
   licenses extraction) forbids a *justification*; V3's first clause (both
   bearer and whole must rise) imposes a *positive outcome requirement*. A
   situation where one bearer falls — while nothing is licensed by the sum —
   satisfies no-license and violates both-rise. The two are not the same gate.
2. **Invalidity ≠ requirement.** V1 says the aggregate inference is invalid
   (structural, about reasoning); V2 says laundered acts are inadmissible
   (operational, about acts). An agent can reason validly (V1 satisfied) and
   still commit a laundered act (V2 violated).
3. **Requirement ≠ heuristic.** V2 binds; V4 is explicitly `[C]` and defeasible.
   Where they conflict, V2 governs acts and V4 governs comparison of remaining
   options — but no document says so.
4. **V5 compresses without saying which.** The Compass row is a compression of
   one of V1–V4; it does not state which, so it inherits the ambiguity.

## What the owner is choosing between

- **Option A — V1 as canonical, the rest as derivatives:** the invalidity claim
  is the gate; V2–V5 are its operational/compressed forms. Cheapest; loses the
  both-rise requirement (the strongest content).
- **Option B — V3 as canonical:** the both-rise requirement is the gate; V1 is
  its structural floor, V2 its envelope form, V4 its heuristic, V5 its
  compression. Richest; but both-rise is the strongest claim and needs its own
  kill, which no version currently carries.
- **Option C — split the gate:** no-license (V3 clause 2 / V1 / V2) is the
  gate; both-rise (V3 clause 1) is a separate, named, stronger commitment with
  its own tier and kill. Most precise; most edits.

The docket stages the choice; it does not make it. Whichever option is chosen,
the four unchosen forms must be marked as derivatives or struck, so the gate
says one thing. Until then the corpus's most distinctive ethical content says
four different things, all live.
