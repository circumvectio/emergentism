# Owner direction — Emergentotype hierarchy and strategic Egregoreotype

## 1. The insight received

The owner directed the corpus to make explicit the variation-and-carrier logic
of the existing six-layer Replicator Stack:

```text
Genotype
< Epigenotype
< Phenotype
< Extended Phenotype
< Memotype
< Egregoreotype
```

The originating examples were clones that diverge epigenetically and
phenotypically; a beaver-mediated change outside the beaver's body; the word
and concept *chair* spanning many actual instances; and an egregore represented
as one strategic or conscious-seeming actor even though no single organismal
body carries or dies as that actor.

The game-theoretic insight is that people can include the attributed response
of such a represented actor in present choice—for example, a believer may
choose an action partly because the believer expects a deity to reward or
punish it. The bounded claim concerns the represented actor-model and its
effects on actual carriers. It neither proves nor denies the represented
being's independent metaphysical reality or consciousness.

## 2. Adopted typing corrections

The owner direction is implemented with these corrections rather than copied
literally:

- a **gene is not an individual or clone**; a genotype is a declared heritable
  pattern instantiated by actual carriers;
- a **word token is not a meme**; word token, word type, concept record,
  memotype candidate, and carrier instance remain distinct;
- an extended phenotype is not every environmental consequence; attribution
  requires a declared organism-mediated causal chain and boundary;
- the vector-database comparison is an implementation analogy, not the
  ontology of meaning;
- a general Egregoreotype remains consciousness- and personhood-neutral under
  `KSC-11`;
- the new subtype records a pattern being represented and used as a strategic
  actor. It does not inherit consciousness, personhood, metaphysical
  independence, Justice, legitimacy, or authority.

## 3. Architecture ruling

The update is substantial but does **not** create a second semantic-owner
folder or a seventh layer. The audit selected an additive split:

1. Cosmology owns the carrier-variation profile and the
   `StrategicEgregoreotypeCandidate` contract.
2. Memetics owns the word/token/concept/memotype carrier distinctions.
3. The existing Egregoreotype owner retains the complete five-marker parent
   test unchanged.
4. A JSON Schema makes the strategic subtype fail closed and keeps
   consciousness, personhood, Justice, and authority in separate records.
5. `KSC-33` records the selected terminology and its non-entailments without
   upgrading the empirical conjecture.

The name **Emergentotype hierarchy** is retained as owner provenance and
reader compression. Its technical form is a **six-layer carrier-profile map**:
an analytic instrument over the existing stack, not a natural-kind census,
social rank, D-register, Rosetta row, or proof that the same mechanism operates
at every layer.
