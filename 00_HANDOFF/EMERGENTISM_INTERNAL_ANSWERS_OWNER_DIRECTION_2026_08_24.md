# Owner direction — the ten internal answers

Yves R. Burri directs Emergentism to stop describing the ten named questions
as questions for which it has **no answer**. The worldview is to state its
answers clearly, under stable identifiers, while keeping each answer's native
physics, philosophy, empirical, and review debt visible.

The controlling distinction is:

```text
owner-adopted internal answer
  != analytic proof
  != empirical validation
  != PQA-54 resolution
  != publication
  != compulsory belief.
```

Belief may motivate inquiry. It is not an evidence tier. The system remains
fallibilist because every answer has a rival, discriminator, kill, and survivor.

## Fixed answer decisions

1. `EA-01 COHERENT-PLENITUDE` — Dasein is the world-tagged plenitude of all
   coherent-consistent determination. Opposites are included without becoming
   jointly actual. Balance is counterpart-completeness, not automatic numeric
   cancellation and not permission to erase local harm.
2. `EA-02 SEQUENTIAL-ACTUALITY` — actuality is the one ordered,
   consequence-bearing D4 history. Perception discloses actuality to a situated
   bearer through a record; it does not retroactively create every unperceived
   event.
3. `EA-03 PHENOMENAL-ACTUALIZATION` — phenomenal experience is adopted as the
   bearer-side aspect of integrated, action-available actualization in a
   self-maintaining system. The stronger claim that bare physical collapse is
   universally proto-phenomenal remains a separately killable `[C]` branch.
4. `EA-04 NOETIC-FREEDOM` — F5's selected internal reading acts on represented
   thought-options through an actual D4 model token, reweighting attention,
   intention, and commitment before ordinary means act on the world. This is
   real bounded agency. Its `F5-W0` ordinary anticipation floor is contained
   within the `F5-N` null; any `F5-W+` increment and irreducibility from lower
   descriptions remain `[C]`.
5. `EA-05 DEATH-CONTINUITY` — death ends one organism's new embodied actions,
   but cannot make an actual life never have been actual. Personal continuation
   exists only where an explicit bearer-continuity relation is satisfied; a
   trace or duplicate alone is not the person.
6. `EA-06 CONSTITUTIVE-ETHICS` — effects on Contribution, Support, option sets,
   consent, hidden cost, Justice, and Exit are objective relational facts.
   Equal bearer standing is adopted as a constitutive condition of Dasein's
   many-sidedness, not inferred from value-free physics. Stance-independent
   moral realism is an owner-adopted `[C]` commitment whose world adequacy can
   fail.
7. `EA-07 SERIAL-ARTICULATION` — D0–D6 is adopted as Emergentism's proposed
   decomposition of articulation: boundary, distinction, relation, quantum
   state, actual history, represented possibility, and nonclosure. The nature
   claim is `[C]`; the scaffold is not a temporal Big-Bang chronology. D0 and D6
   are distinct poles with a declared boundary-role self-resemblance.
8. `EA-08 RELATIVE-EMERGENCE` — a type absent from a frozen lower grammar cannot
   be denoted inside that grammar; a declared forgetful map may recover the
   lower description from the higher one. This supplies a conditional formal
   strong-up/weak-down asymmetry, not proof that a physical crossing satisfies
   the premises. Each natural crossing remains independently `[C]`.
9. `EA-09 MODULAR-FORCE-F5` — strong↔D1, electromagnetic↔D2, weak↔D3,
   gravity↔D4, and F5↔D5 are the selected role affinities. The first four remain
   one of 24 testable assignments. F5-W is the selected internal noetic reading;
   F5-N contains its ordinary floor and contests its incremental reading, while
   F5-R remains the separate physical rival. No fifth physical interaction is
   claimed established.
10. `EA-10 DEFINITE-OUTCOME` — actualization is selection in the ontological
    register: a D3 probability-bearing state gives way to one stable,
    consequence-bearing D4 record. Bearer-integrated actualization is the
    phenomenal face proposed by EA-03. Native physics still owes Born weights,
    interpretation, and any unique dynamical selection law.

## Supersession boundary

The answer set supersedes only the blanket reader claim that Emergentism has no
internal answer to these questions. It does not reopen graves, promote W0/W2/W7,
change `CLAIM_STATUS.yaml`, turn CCC into evidence, establish a quantum
interpretation, validate strong emergence, prove a force map, or convert an
owner commitment into a fact for non-adherents.

## Release boundary

This turn authorizes local source changes, deterministic machine contracts,
checks, and path-limited commits. It does not authorize push, deployment, DNS,
external deposit, live model calls, a new PQA score, or a claim that any answer
has received independent validation.
