## One concrete correction proposal surfaced

[B] In the returned Power-Max section, lines 107–108 refer to a "first"
derivative and a "second" cross-partial, but the displayed block has only one
derivative. That derivative also needs the cross-agent index condition stated.
This was noticed by the reviewing agent in retrieved context, not automatically
diagnosed by APU.

[D] **Unadopted source-owner proposal:** keep the cardinal toy model, declare
`N≥2`, fixed `0<λ≤1`, arithmetic network mean, `j≠i`, and fixed other coordinates.
Then display the two distinct quantities explicitly:

```text
∂C×_eff(i)/∂V_j = λΦ_i/N      (positive if Φ_i>0; zero if Φ_i=0)
∂²C×_eff(i)/(∂Φ_i ∂V_j) = λ/N > 0.
```

For `j=i` the first derivative instead is
`Φ_i(1-λ+λ/N)`; do not apply the cross-agent formula to that index. These are
ordinary derivatives of the declared cardinal toy expression, not derivatives
of the framework's ordinal `min` score and not new mathematical discoveries.
The existing zero-sum extraction counterexample and Justice-admissibility fence
must remain unchanged. Verify the source's intended domain before adoption;
this packet does not silently amend its formula or public projections.
