# Owner direction

Yves R. Burri directs the v2.2 refinement of Emergentism as a source-bound,
fallibilist research release. The implementation may change canonical sources,
formal contracts, local fixtures, deterministic public projections, tests and
generated artifacts. It may make local path-limited commits. It may not push,
deploy, alter DNS, submit a paper, call a live model, publish a scored benchmark,
or claim external validation without a separate owner act.

## Fixed semantic decisions

1. **Plain names lead.** `Contribution(a;i,H)` names the part-to-whole
   direction and `Support(a;H,i)` names the whole-to-part direction. The older
   `Moral_E(a)` and `Ethical_E(a)` terms remain explicit Emergentist technical
   aliases. Neither direction is good merely because of its sign.
2. **The is–ought bridge is conditional.** `RCAB-01` tests whether reciprocal
   public justification constitutively commits reason-givers to bearer
   visibility, contest, correction and feasible Exit. It does not derive an
   ought from value-free facts or establish moral realism.
3. **Guardianship is separate.** Infants, non-speaking or incapacitated people,
   nonhuman animals, ecosystems and future people do not lose worth or care
   because reciprocal reason-giving is unavailable. `GEX-01` is a disclosed,
   separately chosen extension with mandate, conflict and review requirements.
4. **Objectivity is typed.** The release distinguishes definition stability,
   procedural reproducibility, empirical adequacy and stance-independent moral
   realism. Only the first is presently established inside the selected model.
5. **Philosophical resolution is earned question by question.** PQA-54 freezes
   nine domains with six public question families each. Inventory, clarification,
   formal correction, type dissolution, conditional resolution and remaining
   debt are different result types. Launch state is `54 selected · 0 evaluated ·
   0 independently reviewed · 0 resolved`.
6. **“Most” has a denominator.** A bounded PQA-54 majority requires at least
   28 of 54 earned type-dissolution or conditional-resolution results, at least
   three in each domain, two independent native-domain reviews per result, no
   live kill, and incremental benefit over native-frame and generic-decomposition
   controls. This never licenses an unbounded “most philosophy” claim.
7. **The Dasein Test stays frozen.** PQA-54 is a companion construct. It may
   bind EUB-1 hashes but may not change EUB-1 v1.0 schemas, fixtures, scores or
   truth status.
8. **Earlier scientific wagers remain modular.** SLWP, M4 compression, the
   24 force assignments and the three-arm F5 fork retain their own tests and
   tiers. Agreement with the founder's preferred mapping is never correctness.

## Public thesis

> Emergentism does not end philosophy. It makes philosophical debt legible—and
> tests where exact type distinctions dissolve a problem, conditionally resolve
> it, reframe it, or leave it open.

Authorship remains Yves R. Burri. AI assistance is disclosed as research,
formalization, implementation and editorial assistance; no AI is a coauthor,
signer, authorizer or independent validator.

## Acceptance boundary

`OFFLINE-READY · [D]` means the source owners, schemas, deterministic fixtures,
validators, public projections and local gates pass. It does not mean strong
emergence, M4 maximality, a force assignment, F5, objective moral realism, a
universal is–ought closure, a philosophical resolution, model performance or
the worldview itself has been empirically established.
