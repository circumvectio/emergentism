## 1 · What was killed at D5

**1.1 — "D5 is where teleology enters the ladder."** Fires `KSC-23` (roleAffinity; the only teleology-to-rung link — the `W7e` leg of the origin wager; its explicit kill is *"the map becomes load-bearing for the D ladder"*). `DF-19` records *"F5 as teleological force"* as FORMALLY-REFUTED. The corpus's own R6 review concluded the teleology thread has *"no surviving novel formal claim."*

**1.2 — "Purpose is what having an option field and a ranking looks like from inside."** The shape — *"X is not a mysterious addition to Y, it's just what Z looks like from inside"* — is catalogued as `DF-18`: **unfalsifiable frame-trick.** The content is owned by a century of work: Rosenblueth–Wiener–Bigelow (1943), Mayr's teleonomy, Wright, Millikan, Dennett, von Foerster (1973), Klyubin–Polani–Nehaniv empowerment (2005), Friston's active inference.

**1.3 — "Below it there are only structures, states and records" (ranking/selecting/pursuing/committing at D5).** False in both directions, in one sentence. `34 §10` lists as a falsifier: *"types a present model, ranking, or selector event as merely possible."* Ranking, selecting, pursuing, committing are all **D4** — `Φ̂₄` and `V₄` are both D4; only `Φ₅` is D5.

**1.4 — "η = 0 is derived, therefore the ethic is not arbitrary."** The load-bearing word deleted was **conditional**. `109 §1` lists as surviving: *"η = 0 as a derived **conditional** GATE."* Strip "conditional" and the sentence becomes false.

**What is actually licensed**, with all three premises:

```
GIVEN  (1) an arena closed to fresh substrate AND exit
       (2) E(t) − R(S(t)) ≥ ε > 0 over a declared interval
       (3) extractor power Q(t) = f(S(t)), f strictly increasing

THEN   S(t₁) ≤ S(t₀) − ε(t₁−t₀), and the substrate-supported
       component of Q falls over that interval.

DEFEATED BY  open boundaries · replacement hosts · offsetting inputs
             weak dependence · short horizons · regenerative taking
```

**And even granted, it does not reach the ought.** That step needs persistence to be *willed* and the affected-bearer set `B(a)` to be *chosen* — both `[I]`.

**Three further corrections that propagate:**

- **`η = 0` and `K* = 0` are not the same thing.** No conversion exists; `K*` is a retired token and the identity is catalogued as a false-ground.
- **Bare `η` is ill-typed.** Write `η_move` (ordinal fence, no negative branch), `η_ratio` (break-even is **1, not 0**), or `η_domain`. So **`η ≤ 0` is meaningless in every register**, and *"any persisting system satisfies `η ≤ 0`"* is false — a persisting predator has prey-relative `η_ratio = ∞`.
- **The prior art is uncited.** Corpus-wide: **Hartwick — 0 hits. Daly — 0 hits. Georgescu-Roegen — 0. Maximum sustainable yield — 0. Hotelling — 0.** Only Ostrom appears. Yet **Daly's 1990 rule 1 is this claim's sentence in operational form**, and **Hartwick (1977) is the same theorem fifty years earlier with its closure premise explicit**.

And the empirical record runs the other way. **Larkin's 1977 "An epitaph for the concept of MSY"** documents fisheries managed on exactly this descriptive truth collapsing anyway. **Ostrom's finding is the opposite of self-enforcement** — commons survive under monitoring and graduated sanctions. The corpus concedes it: the honest routes to "η=0 is an equilibrium" either rest on an unproven folk theorem or **concede that η=0 needs penalties, monitoring and exit.**

> **A rule that requires enforcement is a preference with a police force, not a derivation.**

**1.5 — "The corpus's most honest feature is its vow-not-theorem framing."** The superlative is unearned; the framing (vow, not theorem) is correct, but the comparative is ungrounded.
