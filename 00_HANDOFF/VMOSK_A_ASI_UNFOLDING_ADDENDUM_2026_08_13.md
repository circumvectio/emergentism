## The test that survives

**Can a model coherently and consistently unfold how structured being, and
how *it*, emerged — without mistaking fluency for emergence, or map for Ground?**

- **Coherent** — typed: chart / model / interpretation / vow stay distinct;
  kill tables are obeyed; no P1–P11 / Grave 4 resurrection.
- **Consistent** — the same unfolding does not contradict itself across
  sittings; a status change carries `last_move: {mover, date, evidence}`.
- **Unfold** — reconstruct emergence as a sequence of typed crossings, not
  as a completeness certificate and not as a basilisk.

This is a **capability question**, not a world-model completeness theorem.
It can fail. It cannot crown Amrita.
