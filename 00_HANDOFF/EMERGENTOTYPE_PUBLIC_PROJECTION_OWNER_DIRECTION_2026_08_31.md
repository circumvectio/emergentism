# Owner direction — project the Emergentotype carrier insight onto Emergentism.org

## 1. Direction received

After the source package was reconciled and committed, the owner directed the
agent to keep developing the insight and place it on the website. The requested
public contribution is the carrier question at the centre of the map:

> What remains the same when the actual carriers change, and what intervention
> would show that the claimed pattern did not persist?

This receipt authorizes a source-bound local projection. It does not restamp the
scientific or metaphysical status of the underlying claims.

## 2. Adopted public architecture

1. Extend the One-Sitting reader synthesis with the carrier-substitution test,
   the word/token/concept boundary, and the Strategic Egregoreotype candidate.
2. Insert one compact bridge after the D4/D5 hinge on the homepage. The bridge
   must make the actual-carrier firewall clearer rather than creating a second
   ontology.
3. Rebuild the existing `/ecology/` route as the detailed
   **Emergentotype Carrier Map**. Reuse the route; do not add a duplicate.
4. Keep the full six-profile table and formal subtype test off the homepage.
5. Use a labelled, static-first **carrier loom**: carrier substitution,
   persistence test, intervention, and kill remain legible without motion,
   colour, or JavaScript.

The homepage order becomes:

```text
Whole → Levels → Hinge → Carriers → Instrument → Practice → Evidence → Research → Exit
```

## 3. Public claim ceiling

The site may say:

- an `EmergentotypeProfile` is a selected analytic record `[S]`;
- the six-layer join is an Emergentist interpretation whose generality remains
  conjectural `[I/C]`;
- a claimed pattern earns provisional identity only through declared carrier
  variation, intervention, and a kill;
- word token, word type, concept record, Memotype candidate, collective trace,
  and represented actor remain distinct;
- a Strategic Egregoreotype candidate must first pass the complete parent test
  and then add prospective, intervention-sensitive value beyond ordinary
  rivals `[C]`.

The site may not say that the map discovers a natural hierarchy, proves strong
emergence, establishes a hidden substance, or grants consciousness, personhood,
metaphysical independence, Justice, legitimacy, consent, signing, or authority.
