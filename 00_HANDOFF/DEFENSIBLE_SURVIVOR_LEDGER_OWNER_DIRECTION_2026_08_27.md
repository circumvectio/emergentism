# Defensible survivor ledger

## 1. Owner direction received

The homepage should stop presenting a shelf of discoveries. The current
negative-space census assigns every theorem it contains to prior owners. The
force bijection, unification-by-derivation, Titan arithmetic, universal
sevenfold, ektropy-as-force, and kernel-to-ethics warrant remain in the graves
or behind narrower surviving types. The public front door should state what
survives this concession rather than compensate for it with a novelty claim.

## 2. The four defensible classes

### 2.1 Selections `[S]`

The D-register ladder, one sphere with two reciprocal charts, the selected
`{0,1,infinity}` frame, `G7 = M4 disjoint-union F3`, and
`P_node = min(Phi-hat_4,V_4)` as a chosen ordinal AND-class score are
instruments. Their burden is usefulness, reconstructability, and comparison
against simpler selections. Selection does not create theorem ownership,
natural necessity, or priority. `G7` closes only its declared language by
construction, and M4 maximal compression remains untested under a draft
comparator protocol.

### 2.2 Typings `[S/I]`

The actual/possible firewall, settled/serviced distinction, Closure Rule, and
the separation of extraction from adjudication constrain how the estate may
speak and test. The extraction/adjudication distinction is established
abstraction discipline in program analysis; the new local fact is that the
2026-08-27 adversary run forced this distinction onto the estate's own gates.
It is not an original theorem.

### 2.3 Measurements of this apparatus `[B/D]`

- Eight frozen gates were tested under a preregistered local adversary run;
  eight admitted a constructed artifact that violated the advertised property,
  including both controls predicted to resist. The prediction failed. This is
  evidence about those gates, not confirmation of Rice, the worldview, or the
  world.
- In one small, same-model, no-tool reconstruction run, the Seven Acts seed
  transmitted the formal spine beyond an unseeded control while several
  philosophical postures were already ambient. The sample is one and is not
  independent validation.
- The current register tally visibly assigns no corpus result to D1 or D4.
- `R2_OLOGY_DIVERGENCE@1` concerns a seven-ology ladder. It does not establish
  that independent coders preserved all four M4 cells while reshuffling all
  three F3 frames. The preregistered double-coding protocol has not run, so that
  repeated M4/F3 formulation is withheld from public result language.

### 2.4 Counterexamples and necessity checks `[A/B]`

The executed S5 model kills the inference from an admissible empty world to
plenitude under the stated inclusive semantics. The five-peak construction
shows that monotone availability and persistence do not buy unimodality. The
two boundary constructions show that both endpoint premises of SAT-01 are
load-bearing. These constructions delimit claims; they do not establish a new
ontology or priority for the pre-empted saturation lemma.

## 3. The living frontier

Four questions presently deserve front-door research sockets:

1. **Butterfield discriminator `[C]`.** Distinguish level crossings where a
   limit erases information enough to yield real underdetermination from those
   where it produces only difficult but recoverable simulation.
2. **Two-mode exhaustiveness `[C]`.** Supply a typing theorem for the selected
   potential/process distinction against Hamkins-Linnebo-style plural mode
   taxonomies, or abandon exhaustiveness.
3. **One complete `L_R` instance at D5 `[C/D]`.** Fill the ledger heuristic's
   eight fields on a register where the estate owns measurements, then compare
   its held-out prediction with native control and verification accounts. This
   is not the already-used `L_D5` name for the separate three-legged gate
   instrument.
4. **Describer-residual control `[C/D]`.** Hold the task domain fixed, define
   both the describer's residual and incremental ledger gain before contact,
   and test the candidate prediction that a fully integrable control has zero
   ledger gain. This is not yet operationalized; until the measures and
   integrable control are frozen, it remains a prompt for a preregistration,
   not a result.

The force permutation and F5 fork remain available in the deeper research
record as stress tests. The killed force bijection and ektropy-as-force claims
do not return through those routes.

## 4. Public disposition

The homepage may compress the surviving estate as:

> A selected instrument, explicit typings, local measurements of its own
> apparatus, and bounded counterexamples. The mathematics stays with its
> named owners; the open questions stay open.

No public surface may call the four classes discoveries, claim that the
127-entry census proves Emergentism, cite the 8-of-8 run as confirmation of
Rice, describe the unrun M4/F3 double-coding as measured evidence, or present
the describer-residual control as an executed experiment.

## 5. Kills

- The four-class ledger is wrong if a listed item cannot be typed to its class
  without borrowing a stronger warrant.
- An apparatus measurement loses `[B]` if its frozen input, run, output, or
  failed prediction cannot be replayed from its receipt.
- A frontier socket closes or narrows when its strongest rival matches the
  proposed discriminator or its own kill fires.
- This receipt fails if it is used to turn honest contraction into a new
  comparative, priority, or totality claim.
