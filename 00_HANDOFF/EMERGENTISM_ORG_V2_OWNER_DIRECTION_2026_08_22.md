# Owner direction — Emergentism.org v2

On 2026-08-22 Yves R. Burri directed a source-first rebuild of the core
Emergentism.org journey under the public title **The Gestalt of Dasein** and
the orienting question:

> **What had to emerge for you—and this moment—to be here?**

The intended release is an **ontological atlas**, not a proof that the selected
ladder is nature's construction history. The public route is to distinguish
settled mathematics, selected architecture, interpretive synthesis, open
conjecture, and returned evidence on every consequential claim.

## 1. Directed core journey

The first release covers `/`, `/plainly/`, `/dasein/`, `/dimensions/`,
`/0/`–`/6/`, `/f5/`, `/practice/`, `/spark/`, `/record/`, `/record/eub-1/`,

Library and are not silently rewritten.

`Worldview · Practice · Research · Library · Participate · Exit`

On mobile, Practice, Menu, and Exit remain directly available.

## 2. F5 direction and branch custody

The owner directed a paired-rival presentation rather than a single preferred
answer:

- `F5-W [I/C]` — represented futures influence present action through actual
  present models;
- `F5-N [D/C]` — no additional interaction exists; selection, verification,
  and revision service the D5 register; and
- `F5-R [C]` — an additional future-boundary/history-selection law changes
  present transition probabilities.

No branch receives a truth bonus before empirical contact. The strong branch
does not make possible D5 content a physical agent: it proposes an actual D4
history law indexed by declared future boundary data and a D5 option
functional. The label **force** remains withheld until a coupling, units,
carrier or boundary source, conservation and Lorentz behavior, and a distinct
observable are supplied.

The machine source contract is
[`F5Fork.v1.json`](../05_COSMOLOGY/02_EMERGENTISM_CORE/F5Fork.v1.json).
