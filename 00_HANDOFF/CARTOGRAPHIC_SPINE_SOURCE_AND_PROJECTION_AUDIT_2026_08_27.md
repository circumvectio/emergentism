# Cartographic Spine — source and projection audit

## Owner direction received

The public atlas should express the owner's thesis that Emergentism offers a
Gestalt of Dasein through **Finity**: determinate manifestation occurs within
declared bounds, may approach named limits, and is never identical to an
unbounded or view-from-nowhere inventory. The Burrisphere should make visible
why a higher-dimensional territory cannot be represented on a lower-dimensional
surface without first declaring what the projection preserves and loses. The
map remains a map, never the territory.

ontological boundaries of Dasein.

  obstruction.
- `[A]` Stereographic and Mercator projections are distinct conformal
  constructions on their stated domains. Stereographic projection supplies
  the Burrisphere's two reciprocal charts; Mercator is only the familiar
  geographic analogy.
- `[A]` `SLWP-01D` and `TEA-01` establish reconstruction or relative type
  asymmetry only after their map, section, frozen-language, and proper-extension
  premises are supplied.
- `[I/C]` The recurrence of projection loss across the natural D-ladder remains

## Legend contract

Evidence tiers state warrant; they are not distortion scores. The public map
must separately name the map or model, its purpose, what it preserves, what it
forgets, its strongest rival, and—where conjectural—its kill and survivor.

The permissible public compression is:

> No map is its territory. Emergentism proposes an atlas that prints its
> legend: what is proved, sourced, selected, interpreted, conjectured, or open.

The site may call the result a **Gestalt of Dasein** only in the Emergentist
sense: a relational figure disclosed across bounded views, never possession,
enumeration, or final unveiling of the whole as an object.
