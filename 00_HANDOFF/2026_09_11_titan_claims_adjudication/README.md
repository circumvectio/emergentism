**The finding that matters, and it is the strongest single result of the audit.**
The typing rule — *the boundary seats are frames, not operands* — is **classical
projective geometry, by textbook definition**. In the projective line the triple
`(∞, 0, 1)` is the *canonical frame*, and its third point is literally called the
**unit point** of that frame: the seat that fixes the scale against which every
coordinate is read. Coordinates are defined *relative to* the frame, never *among*
its points. Möbius 1827; von Staudt 1847.

So the throne is not a discovery and not a stipulation. It is the name the
subject already uses. Two consequences the corpus has not drawn:

1. **All three seats are frame points, not two.** The claim as stated says the two
   *boundary* seats are frames. Projectively, the unit is a frame point too.
2. The corpus uses the term **once, uncited, and inside a tombstone**, at
   `05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/09_EFR_GODEL_CLARIFICATION.md` — *"so `{0,1,∞}`
   is a *selected* projective frame, not a forced closure of `{0,∞}`"* — without
   noticing that it hands the `[I]` typing rule to von Staudt. **Verified
   2026-09-11:** `/usr/bin/grep -rl "von Staudt"` over the corpus excluding
   `90_ARCHIVE` returns **exactly one file — this one.** Coverage in the corpus
   proper is zero. "canonical frame", "unit point", "cross-ratio" and "anharmonic"
   return only mathlib-doc and "canonical framework" false positives.

**Not a fact about the sphere.** `FV-06` and `FV-07` are one group-theoretic
statement instantiated twice: in any group `g = g⁻¹` exactly when `g² = e`. The
positive reals are torsion-free, so the identity is their only self-inverse
element; `ℂ^×` has exactly one further element of order two. Sphere,
stereographic projection and the word "equator" are **presentation**. Under the
logarithm the whole statement is the one already on disk at
`05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/40_THE_LOGARITHMIC_REALIGNMENT.md`: *"The
reciprocal map on ℝ₊ is the reflection map on ℝ."*

**Two errors in the claim as drafted, both requiring repair before any use.**

  positive reals … infinity is not in the field"* slips a nonzero multiplicative

### C3 — The horizon as absorber; the loop as grave

| | |
|---|---|
| **Custody** | 20% live work · 60% already-settled material re-presented · 20% regression into a grave dug three times. |
| **Prior art** | PRE-EMPTED on all three live limbs; the `[A]` limb is worse than pre-empted — it is **analytic**. |

**The `[A]` limb is unkillable, which means it is not a finding.** Identity at the
bottom, absorber at the top *is the definition of a bounded lattice* (Huntington
1904 universal bounds; Birkhoff 1940). `V ∪ {x} = V` is analytic from `V` being
the universal class — so doc 49 §8's stated kill, *"exhibit a set in the
difference"*, **cannot fire**. A row whose kill cannot fire is not capable of
losing.

**Misattribution, and it is actionable.** The corpus credits **Dedekind 1888** for
`V ∪ {x} = V`. Dedekind owns only the *middle* line — a set is infinite exactly
when equinumerous with a proper subset — and that line additionally requires
countable choice, which travels nowhere in this corpus. The horizon line is
**Cantor's**: his own criterion separating the *Transfinitum* (**vermehrbar**,
increasable) from the *Absolutum* (**unvermehrbar**, unincreasable) in the
*Mitteilungen zur Lehre vom Transfiniten* (1887–88) **is absorption under another
name**, with the set-theoretic form in the Dedekind letter of 28 July 1899 and in
Burali-Forti's `Ω + 1 ≤ Ω`. The attribution must be withdrawn from all live sites.

**The duality is operation-relative, and the fence does not exist.** Under
intersection the assignment inverts exactly — `V ∩ S = S` makes the horizon the
*identity*, `∅ ∩ S = ∅` makes the ground the *absorber*. "Ground = absorbed,
horizon = absorber" is a choice of union over intersection presented as an
asymmetry the seats possess. **That fence exists nowhere on disk.**

**And the dual, as drafted in session, has its algebra backwards.** In `S ∪ ∅ =
S`, `∅` is the **identity** — what is absorbed is the adjoined `x`. The phrase
"identity and absorber" concedes this against the sentence before it. **Strike the
ground-seat dual or retype it to `[I]`**, citing `43`'s empty-slot ruling in the
same breath.

**The `[S]` reassignment is the only limb with real work left**, and it is correct.
Its disposition is a single owner act **pending 37 days**. But doc 49 §4 does not
list receipt **242** among its parents, and 242 — dated the same day — already
named the owners: **Aristotle** *Physics* III.6 (*"there is always another and
another to be taken"*) and **Linnebo–Shapiro 2019**. UNFINDABILITY hit.

**The RQ is lawful in form but technically false as written.** *"top OR x = top,
so the glut absorbs"* is **FALSE under LP's own disjunction**: `B ∨ T = T ≠ B`.
The glut absorbs only under the **knowledge join**, never the truth join — so the
RQ's stated kill fires against LP on day one. It is not well-posed until it names
which bilattice ordering it means. And the territory is owned: **Belnap 1977**'s
approximation lattice `A4` already places the **gap at the identity bottom**
(`None ⊕ x = x`) and the **glut at the annihilating top** (`Both ⊕ x = Both`) —
the corpus's ground/horizon pair, published 1977. Ginsberg 1988 and Fitting 1991
fold both orderings into one bilattice, so *"logic-relative"* is a structural
feature, not a live question. Open the RQ only with **Landini 2009** carried as
counter-citation and the sentence/totality distinction answered up front.

**The grave holds, and is stronger than the corpus states** — the barber's
unsatisfiability is a theorem of pure FOL, not merely model-unsatisfiability.
