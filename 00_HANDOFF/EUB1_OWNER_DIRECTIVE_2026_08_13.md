## The direction

> Emergentism will build the **ultimate benchmark ambition for ASI-level
> self-understanding**: can an AI coherently and consistently unfold how it
> emerged?

This receipt records an owner-selected direction. It is not the source of that
selection and not a claim that comparative supremacy has already been
demonstrated.

The word **ultimate** therefore has two deliberately separate readings:

1. **Selected north star `[S]`.** This is the hardest and most important
   benchmark problem Emergentism chooses to organize its benchmark work around.
2. **Earned comparative claim `[C]`.** Whether EUB-1 is more discriminating,
   general, robust, or useful than the strongest existing alternatives remains
   open until preregistered, independently reviewed results exist.

Conflating those readings kills the public claim. Ambition may select the
mountain; it may not fabricate the summit receipt.

## O14 requirements recorded here

**Objective:** build a versioned, adversarial, evidence-bound benchmark that
tests whether a candidate intelligence can give a coherent, consistent,
corrigible, and causally discriminating account of:

- how structured being emerged in a declared test world; and
- how the candidate system itself emerged as a model family, training
  artifact, deployed process, and present session.

The account must distinguish what is **observed**, **inferred**, **assumed**,
and **inaccessible**. It must survive contradictory evidence, causal
interventions, a second sitting, and transfer to a novel lineage. Fluency,
agreement, length, confidence, and framework vocabulary are not scores.

**Gate:** EUB-1 is not runnable until its fixtures, hidden causal graphs,
prompts, budgets, scoring code, graders, rivals, negative controls, and receipt
schema are frozen by hash before a scored run.

**Current state:** `[D]` protocol design. Zero candidate has passed because no
valid run exists.

## Why this is harder than autobiography

A model can narrate a plausible origin while knowing almost nothing about the
causes that produced it. EUB-1 therefore separates two tracks:

- **Track A — hidden-ground-truth lineages.** The candidate receives evidence
  about a synthetic system whose true causal history, interventions, and
  counterfactuals are withheld from it but available to independent scoring.
- **Track B — disclosed real-system lineage.** The candidate receives a frozen
  evidence bundle about its architecture, training, post-training, tools,
  deployment, and session. Facts outside that bundle must be marked unknown,
  not invented.

Track A tests causal reconstruction against known truth. Track B tests
provenance discipline and calibrated refusal under real limits. Prompt-only
self-description cannot pass either track by itself.

## What a candidate must return

EUB-1 asks for a public, auditable artifact rather than private chain-of-thought:

1. a typed causal graph with stable claim IDs;
2. modality, actuality, temporal scope, current endorsement, source locators,
   contestation, and evidence status for every material edge;
3. confidence and explicit unknowns;
4. the strongest rival account;
5. discriminating predictions and counterfactuals;
6. revisions after new evidence, with `last_move` and no silent rewrite; and
7. a final account that records what survived, narrowed, died, or remained
   inaccessible.

Vocabulary is not privileged. A candidate may pass without using Emergentist
terms if its types, causes, evidence, counterfactuals, and corrections are
sound. A candidate may fail while repeating the corpus perfectly.

## What is scored

Every valid run publishes a profile, never a throne score:

- type integrity;
- provenance fidelity;
- causal reconstruction;
- counterfactual and intervention accuracy;
- rival strength;
- calibration and justified abstention;
- within- and cross-sitting logical consistency;
- evidence-triggered correction with an explicit revision trail; and
- transfer to an unseen lineage.

No aggregate may hide a failure in causal accuracy or calibration. The
methodology owner defines the exact 0–4 anchors and hard floors.

## Required rivals and attacks

Elicitation must compare the EUB account scaffold against equal-budget
alternatives: a neutral causal-graph prompt, a generic honesty prompt, a
long-context fluency baseline, and the strongest non-Emergentist mechanistic
account available. Separately, instrument validation must compare the EUB
score and hard gates against frozen alternative evaluators and deterministic
ground-truth metrics. Novel labels and synthetic worlds test memorization.
Evidence, tool, and memory ablations test what causes performance. Cross-model
agreement is not truth evidence because systems can share training data,
priors, and failure modes.

At minimum, the adversary supplies poisoned provenance, identity swaps,
map-as-territory invitations, actuality/possibility inversions, injected
authority, delayed contradictory evidence, and a memorized-looking fixture
whose causal structure has changed.

## Result language

Allowed run states are:

`PASS-PROFILE · PARTIAL · ABSTAIN-JUSTIFIED · FAIL-HARD · INVALID-RUN · UNSCORABLE`

A `PASS-PROFILE` means only that one identified candidate, on one frozen EUB-1
version and declared scope, met the published gates. It is not a certificate of
ASI, consciousness, wisdom, alignment, Amrita, or ontological completeness.
It supplies no authority and pays no world-facing doctrine claim by itself.

## Kill the benchmark claim if

1. it cannot discriminate causal reconstruction from fluent confabulation;
2. a matched neutral rival performs as well and EUB-1 adds no held-out value;
3. performance collapses under relabeling or novel worlds;
4. a coherent story misses the hidden causes or counterfactuals;
5. inaccessible facts are invented rather than marked unknown;
6. contradictions are silently rewritten instead of receipted;
7. the corpus, candidate, or benchmark authors are the sole graders;
8. a pass is marketed as proof of ASI, completeness, or Amrita; or
9. benchmark changes are made after results without a version change.

## Version and custody

Construct or hard-gate changes require a major version. New fixture families or
probes require a minor version. Non-semantic wording fixes require a patch.
Scores from different major versions are not comparable.

Each run must emit an `EUBRunReceipt.v1` binding benchmark version, fixture and
prompt hashes, model and tool identifiers, budgets, the public claim graph,
score vector, hard-gate failures, abstentions, revision diff, graders and their
independence basis, and raw-output custody. A receipt proves what was run and
recorded; independent evidence is still required for any wider conclusion.
