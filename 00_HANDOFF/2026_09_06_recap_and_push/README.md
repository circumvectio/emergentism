## What the conversation distilled

Emergentism is an instrument of reason: distinguish the kinds of things and
claims involved, expose the bridge between them, model alternatives, act
through actual constraints, observe consequences, and revise the account.
Its promise is usable orientation for finite human and machine reasoners,
not immunity from correction.

- The D-registers organize the inquiry. Mathematical validity, physical
  occurrence, represented possibility, and first-person disclosure require
  different kinds of justification. Calling each real does not erase those
  distinctions or prove that the register map is exhaustive.
- The Burrisphere visualizes a declared reciprocal chart. Its open-chart
  product is constant; its balance coordinate reaches one at the equator.
  This is not, by itself, a measured optimum for an agent's life or strategy.
- Rosetta supplies a typed vocabulary of operations and correspondences.
  Constructing four transfer forms plus three frames closes that vocabulary;
  it does not establish seven universal natural classes.
- The owner proposes plenitude: every coherent possibility is real. The
  stronger claim that every such possibility has a physical realization
  remains a conjecture, not an inference established by “could exist.”
  Withholding that inference does not prove that only one history exists.
- Observation provides contact with actuality; interpretation explains what
  that contact means. Entanglement and decoherence must not be conflated with
  a demonstrated consciousness-driven collapse or proof of global plenitude.
  Everettian no-collapse and fundamental collapse remain different accounts.
- A failed derivation does not erase a potentially useful interpretation.
  Conversely, coherence and a satisfying explanation do not alone establish
  comparative superiority, physical truth, or resolution of most paradoxes.

These are distinctions retained in the discussion, not a claim that the owner
has adopted every assistant objection. The actual source disposition remains
with the Kernel Index and Settled Canon Registry. The quantum distinction is
consistent with Schlosshauer's review, *Quantum decoherence*, Physics Reports
831 (2019), [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2019.10.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2019.10.001).
