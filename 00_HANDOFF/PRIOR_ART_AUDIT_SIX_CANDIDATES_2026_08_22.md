# PRIOR-ART AUDIT — FINAL REPORT: THE SIX WEDGE CANDIDATES

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Method:** Two-pass audit (sweep + adversarial refute) per candidate. Where the refuter overturned, the corrected verdict governs. All six sweeps returned PARTIALLY; **all six refutations overturned to PRE-EMPTED.** The refuters read the load-bearing sources first-hand where the sweep had not, and in four of six cases the sweep's "unclaimed residue" was manufactured by under-attribution or mischaracterization of a source the sweep itself had cited.

**Headline:** Zero of six candidates survives as a claimable novelty. The program's giants-first discipline held: every wedge, as stated, exists in the literature — mostly under named giants, twice in single papers the sweeps missed or under-read (Kauffman 2005; Sengupta–Friston et al. 2016).

## 2. Load-bearing citations per candidate

Verification level is stated per citation. "Read in full" means the refuter or sweep extracted and read the actual text, not an abstract.

### THE RULE
1. **Aristotle, *Physics* III.5–6 (c. 350 BC)** — primary translation fetched (MIT classics). III.5 runs an eliminative argument against every actually infinite body; III.6 then grants the infinite BOTH surviving non-actual modes in successive sentences — a potential existence and a process existence (the day/games analogy) — with everything actually taken always finite. That is the rule's full content, exhaustiveness-by-elimination included; the sweep's "Aristotle has one mode" gloss was contradicted by the primary text.
2. **Louis H. Kauffman, "EigenForm", *Kybernetes* 34(1/2), 2005** — read in full. A Spencer-Brown-lineage glyph calculus composing the candidate's entire package in one paper: the {∅}=1 set-theoretic anchor stated twice, number-generation as act applied to the void, infinity existing strictly as instruction/process never as completed entity, the pure unframed world as possibility, and the diagonal via Lawvere fixed points. Falsifies the sweep's "nobody composes them" residue.
3. **Linnebo & Shapiro, "Actual and Potential Infinity", *Noûs* 53(1), 2019** — read pp. 160–175. Two rival construals of the potential infinite (liberal: determinate modal truths, classical logic via mirroring; strict: truth made at stages, intuitionistic). Their formal system deliberately does NOT impose finitude at every world (p. 170 says the opposite of the sweep's gloss), and the paper itself multiplies potentialist positions along two axes — "exactly two" was contested ground, not silence.

### THE DOORS
1. **Dagnachew Jenber, "Indeterminate Forms", *Adv. Pure Math.* 6, 2016** — read in full. Writes the cosmogenesis equation over absolute zero and infinity (0/0 = c and 0·∞ = c, with c glossed as God or Big-bang), then proves all seven catalogue forms indeterminate, each defined-but-not-unique: arithmetic underdetermines the created something and an external completion fixes it. The doors mechanism, published, in one paper.
2. **Michael Berry, "Singular Limits", *Physics Today* 55(5), 2002** — read in full. Inter-level limits are necessarily singular because the connected theories involve qualitatively different concepts, and emergence lives in that singular borderland — the intra/inter-register diagnosis WITH the universality quantifier the sweep under-reported.
3. **Ilija Barukčić, *CAUSATION* 18(8), 2022** — read pp. 1–24; self-edited venue disclosed. Creation-from-nothing is routed through the indeterminate forms as a class, and completion rules are derived at them (0/0 = 1; 1/0 as negation). Fringe, but prior art is prior art.

### DEBT-TYPING OF THE REALS
1. **Hermann Weyl 1921 + F. P. Ramsey 1926** — verified via Toader, "Weyl's Quantifiers" (arXiv:2408.08562, full text extracted) and Scholz (Wuppertal preprint, full text extracted). The monetary-instrument semantics runs across ALL THREE grades: singular judgments deliver value in hand; general propositions are cheques cashed per instance under a rule; bare existentials are uncovered paper announcing a treasure without location — with Weyl's own paper-economy and redemption (Einlösung) framing. This is the debt-typing, 1921/1926.
2. **Tomaso Poggio, MIT blog, Jan 2026** — fetched. The same three tiers (finitely describable / computable / uncomputable, measure 1) as one pyramid crossing logic, computation, and physics. No money, no institutions — confirming the tiers-plus-axis exists without the debt vocabulary too.
3. **Gregory Chaitin, "How real are real numbers?", 2004** — full text. Nameable vs unnameable, computable vs uncomputable, Omega; run through physics via information bounds. (Note preserved: Chaitin states he never obtained Borel 1952 — that neighbour is partly citation-laundered at its own source.)

### EMERGENCE GRADED BY UPWARD DERIVABILITY
1. **Carroll & Parola, "What Emergence Can Possibly Mean", arXiv:2410.15468, 2024** — read in full. One information-discarding coarse-graining mechanism generates the whole ladder: Type-1a direct/closed-form, Type-1b incompressible with explicit Bedau identification (the simulation class), Type-3 augmented — one microstate to many macrostates, new macro ontology (the underdetermined class). Worked instances are limit passages. The sweep had omitted Type-3, which is what kept the "no unifying source" residue alive.
2. **Bishop & Atmanspacher, "Contextual Emergence...", *Found. Phys.* 36, 2006** — read in full. Fourfold grading of inter-level relations by derivability; contextual emergence = lower level necessary but not sufficient, rigorously instantiated at information-discarding singular limits (KMS states, thermodynamic limit); fn. 3 maps class (1) to the British-emergentist resultant class.
3. **Mark Bedau, "Weak Emergence", *Noûs* 31, 1997** — secondary-source check. Macrostates derivable from microdynamics only by simulation — the candidate's middle class verbatim, by the candidate's own construction.

### WITNESS-FRACTION BOUND
1. **Sagawa & Ueda, *PRL* 102, 250602, 2009 (arXiv:0809.4098)** — abstract checked directly. Fundamental lower bounds on the work cost of measurement and erasure: any physical system maintaining order by feedback pays an irreducible nonzero cost for the non-product-producing measure-and-reset cycle. Greater generality than the candidate, as a theorem. Caveat carried: the irreducible bound is on the combined measurement+erasure cycle.
2. **Stafford Beer, *Brain of the Firm* 1972 / *The Heart of Enterprise* 1979** — secondary scholarly verification. Necessary-AND-sufficient conditions for viability of any system (organism, organization, country) including a sporadic independent audit channel (System 3*) that bypasses routine reporting — the substrate-general law packaged WITH the non-acting seat, in one framework.
3. **Fawzi, Müller-Hermes & Shayeghi, 2022 (arXiv:2202.00119)** — abstract checked. Proven space-overhead lower bound for fault-tolerant quantum computation under any non-unitary noise — the irreducible nonzero-overhead theorem in the computational domain.

### COMPENSATION PRINCIPLE
1. **Weyl 1918/1929 → Yang & Mills 1954 → Utiyama 1956 → Kibble 1961** — survey-level verification (Blagojević & Hehl tradition; "compensating field" wording confirmed in Gronwald & Hehl gr-qc/9602013 at snippet level, NOT in SEP). Localizing a rigid symmetry forces a compensating field with coupling fixed by the symmetry; generalized to arbitrary groups including gravity. The entire physics half of the candidate is this, down to the word.
2. **Sengupta, Tozzi, Cooray, Douglas & Friston, "Towards a Neuronal Gauge Theory", *PLoS Biology* 14(3), 2016** — full text checked via PMC. The gauge-compensator schema at the biological/representational register: local symmetry breaking repaired by a gauge field identified as precision-weighted prediction error (cognitive homologue: attention) — an approximate-Bayesian error-correcting inference process, not a force, under the same apparatus, with scale-generality claimed. This kills the type-shift residue; the sweep never searched the free-energy-principle literature.
3. **Noether II, via Brading 2002 and Avery & Schwab 2015** — secondary. Conservation laws attached to local symmetries hold as off-shell identities (improper conservation) — the balances-to-a-tautology clause is a 1918-vintage theorem.

## 3. WHAT WOULD MAKE HISTORY vs WHAT IS CITATION WORK

**Citation work — everything currently written.** All six candidates, as stated in the corpus, are attribution essays waiting for their bibliographies. Each should be rewritten as a named synthesis over its giants with zero novelty asserted beyond vocabulary. The coined names ("infinite zero", "doors", "registers", "settled/serviced/unbacked", "coherence-runaway/viability-runaway") are vocabulary; a name is not a claim. The acts of synthesis are real intellectual work and honest contributions — but under the program's own discipline they are citation work, not priority claims.

**What would make history — open problems nobody (including us) has solved.** Three genuine conjecture slots fell out of the audit, none of which the corpus currently possesses as a result:

1. **A two-mode exhaustiveness TYPING THEOREM.** No source states the potential/process exhaustiveness as a modern theorem (honest negative, recorded twice). But the Hamkins–Linnebo set-theoretic-potentialism taxonomy actively multiplies modes — so the theorem must be argued AGAINST that literature, not into silence. Aristotle's eliminative structure is the prior art for the claim itself; only a formal proof would be new.
2. **The Butterfield discriminator.** Why does information erasure in a limit yield genuine underdetermination in some level-crossings but mere simulation-hardness in others? Butterfield 2011 shows rigorous limit behaviour can be fully deducible; Carroll–Parola grade but do not derive the boundary. A mechanism-level answer would be a real result in the emergence literature.
3. **The pole-consumption clause as a general law.** Three targeted searches found no source stating "at its pole, every compensator consumes the freedom it services" in general form. Its entire instance base is published physics (confinement, holography); the verification-register pole is unworked. Stating it, working the verification instance, and surviving a sweep that includes Dumbrava ch. 23 in full would be the only path back to a priority claim anywhere in these six.

A fourth, weaker slot: deriving the verification bound in budget-FRACTION form rigorously across substrates — but the refuter's judgment stands that under any finite budget this is a re-parameterization of the per-cycle cost theorem, so treat it as expository, not historic.

## 4. LAUNDERING AND UNCERTAINTY

### Laundering and mischaracterization found in the sweeps (corrected in the verdicts above)

- **THE RULE, two findings.** (1) The sweep called Linnebo–Shapiro "a formal system in which finitude is the sole mode of actuality at every world"; the paper (p. 170, read first-hand) says finitude-at-worlds is the traditional potentialist's optional assumption, explicitly not built into their framework. (2) The sweep's Aristotle row ("ONE non-actual mode... precisely what Aristotle lacks") is contradicted by *Physics* III.6 primary text, which gives both a potential and a process existence. Both errors jointly manufactured the residue that made the sweep's PARTIALLY possible.
- **DEBT-TYPING.** The sweep's Weyl row carried a false rider ("no three-tier typing, no cross-domain axis") contradicted by Ramsey's cheque semantics, Weyl's own treasure/cheque/paper-money grading, and SEP's documentation of Weyl's being/becoming/judgment-abstract tiering. Under-attribution rather than laundering — but it is what created the "unclaimed residue".
- **GRADING.** No false attribution; material under-attribution: Carroll–Parola's Type-3 class and limit-passage instances omitted; Bishop–Atmanspacher's fn. 3 resultant-mapping and coarse-graining character omitted. One missed source: Bar-Yam 2004 (mathematical strong-emergence existence argument via underdetermination), closer to the candidate's warrant than the sweep's Gu et al. citation.
- **THE DOORS.** None substantive; two shadings for the record: Jenber applies the creation question explicitly only at 0/0 and 0·∞ (the other five forms are proven indeterminate under that framing, not re-interrogated door-by-door); and the sweep UNDER-claimed Berry by omitting his necessity sentence — material to the verdict.
- **WITNESS-FRACTION.** None. Beer spot-check found the sweep's gloss accurate and understated (Beer claims necessary AND sufficient).
- **COMPENSATION.** None confirmed. Precision note: the "compensating field"/"principle of compensation" wording is not in SEP; it is confirmed in the Weyl–Utiyama–Hehl tradition at snippet level only. Standing caution against a refuter-introduced source: Harlow–Ooguri 2018 proves no-bulk-global-symmetries via CFT consistency, NOT via black holes eating charges — never cite it as the charge-eating argument.
