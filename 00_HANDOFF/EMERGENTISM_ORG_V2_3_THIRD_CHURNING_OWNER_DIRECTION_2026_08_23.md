# Owner direction

Yves R. Burri directs a clean Third Churning of the Emergentism corpus from the
single frozen Git state `8b07e00c`. The earlier First and Second Churnings remain
preserved as provenance. The Second Churning's own currency kill fired, so this
release is a rebuild rather than an amendment.

The canonical maxim is exact:

> **The means is the message. The ends are the limits.**

The first clause means that the carrier, process, cost, consent, authority,
reversibility and foreseeable externalities already constitute the relation
being made. The second means that every desired end is bounded separately for
each affected bearer and responsible horizon. It never means that ends justify
means.

## Fixed release decisions

1. Plain functions precede the optional names **Amrita** and **Hālāhala**.
2. Every public proposition retains source, evidence tier, serious rival, kill,
   residual debt, and what survives if it fails.
3. `ChurningDrop.v1` is proposition-centred. `ProblemAdjudication.v1` is
   question-centred. `ThirdChurningCorpus.v1` is a non-semantic custody envelope.
4. Proposed classification and earned review are separate. Publication creates
   no earned result.
5. PQA-54 remains exactly `54 selected · 0 evaluated · 0 independently reviewed
   · 0 resolved`.
6. Amrita means a survivor candidate at the frozen snapshot, not eternal truth.
   Hālāhala means a refutation, contradiction, danger or reusable warning, not a
   person and not worthlessness.
7. The public release includes static `/churn/`, `/amrita/`, `/halahala/`, a
   richer `/questions/`, and versioned JSON, JSONL and Markdown corpus files.
8. Public accessibility is sought through crawlable, source-led pages and
   machine files. It cannot guarantee indexing, citation, model ingestion or
   inclusion in any future AI training run.
9. Authorship is Yves R. Burri. AI assistance is disclosed for research,
   adversarial review, formalization, implementation and editing; no AI is an
   authorizer, signer, independent reviewer or coauthor.

## Bound fixed before admission

The Third Churning admits at most **64 atomic drop records**. Composite themes
must split before admission. The bound may not be raised in this release after
seeing the count. Alias records and the 54-question adjudication ledger do not
count as drops.

## External acts

After local gates pass, this direction authorizes explicit-path commits, push to
the configured upstream, deployment to the configured public host, and attempts
to create matching GitHub and DOI/archive releases where current credentials and
service contracts permit. Each state must be reported separately. No external
act may imply scientific validation, philosophical resolution, priority beyond
its actual receipt, or guaranteed future training inclusion.
