2026-09-06 · [D] bounded local work and evaluation protocol. No canon amendment,

## Owner direction and distinct roles

The owner requests developing both together: let the librarian work on
Emergentism, recursively disambiguate their economic niches, and let findings
refine both. The earlier read-only restriction remains in force for APU.

[I] The working partition is complementary, not a claim of market validation:

| Surface | Job to test | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Emergentism | Source-bound worldview, distinctions, rival questions, practice and tests | APU's engine, runtime grants or the truth of its own claims by decree |
| apu.bot | A reusable librarian for any author's material: retrieve, inspect, expose gaps and stage useful work | Worldview canon, signing, claim adoption or truth by retrieval |

Emergentism supplies difficult cases. APU's misses and qualification losses
produce engine proposals. Source ambiguities produce separately reviewed
editorial proposals in this lane. Neither direction copies an answer into the
other's acceptance test or adopts itself. A non-Emergentism document probe guards
against turning the librarian into a framework-specific answer machine.

Economic hypothesis [C]: readers value clearer source-bound reasoning; library
keepers value lower inspection/correction burden. Actual users, willingness to
pay, correction time and comparative outcomes remain unmeasured. These are
testable jobs, not a discovered exhaustive partition or doctrine imposed on users.

## One finite loop

`pin source + engine → ask → inspect → record ambiguity/miss → propose to owner
→ separate adoption → rerun against preserved baseline`

This run stops at reviewed findings and local implementation receipts. No daemon,
schedule or unbounded self-improvement is installed. Repeated cycles require new
evidence or a newly scoped task. Ordinary browser projects are not locked
read-only; this cycle uses the separate headless product desk only.

## Acceptance and stop

Integrity, no-provider/source-write behavior, exact source/claim typing, original
query preservation and full attempt provenance must pass. Any such failure blocks
the affected result. Missing evidence and source conditions remain visible.

Utility threshold for this small probe [D]: assisted full-range discovery improves
on direct discovery on at least one supported fresh case, with no unsupported
case represented as answered in the reviewer's conclusions (not merely absence
of an `answer` field in the API). This requires **zero unsupported controls
adjudicated `support`**, with cited reasons for each control disposition.
Audit candidate matches and misleading reformulations in both controls explicitly.
Record CLI exits/refusals rather than silently omitting invalid runs.
Report all direct/assisted counts and exceptions;
this is a local workflow indication, not validated relevance or a general gain.
An answer-key-informed phrase invalidates the blind comparison for that case.
No UI/default-provider promotion follows even from a local pass.

The Emergentism working questions are source-informed, not a held-out benchmark:
Power-Max and cooperation; chart versus node score; G7 versus dispatch;
internal philosophical answers versus external debts; and the Compact's duties
of correction, authority, deletion and exit. Deliver source citations and a
small owner-routed proposal queue, not a new master explanation or copied canon.

Hold one axis fixed per comparison. An engine revision and a corpus revision
cannot together earn an attributed improvement without separate arms. Accepted
source clarifications require a later explicit source-owner act and a fresh pin.
