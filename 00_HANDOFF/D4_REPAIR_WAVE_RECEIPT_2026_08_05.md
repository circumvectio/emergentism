## 4 · The three fences that make D4 honest

**A token can be actual while its content is not.**

```
ModeledFutureToken : D4 (actual)  ──represents──▶  AlternativeContent : D5 (possible)
RankEvent          : D4 (actual)  ──ranks──────▶   …
SelectionEvent     : D4 (actual)  ──selects─────▶  …
```

The thinking is real. What it is about need not be. **A `[C]` claim card is a D4 carrier bearing D5 content** — and collapsing carrier into content is a defect the Burri Rules record as *already repaired once*.

**Representation does not demote the referent.** A present memory is an actual D4 token; it may represent a historical claim which, *if independently warranted*, refers to a past D4 event. Only the imagined variants are D5.

**Vividness is not modality.** *"Vividness is a present D4 salience property. It does not change modality, register role, dependency priority, or evidence tier."* Three orders that must never collapse: `DependencyPriority ≠ Actuality ≠ PsychologicalSalience`. The owner's motto: *"The remembered sunset can be the first thing I know without being the first thing it depends on."*
