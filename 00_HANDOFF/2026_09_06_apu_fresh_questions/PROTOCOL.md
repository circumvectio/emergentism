# Boundary

The owner accepted a supervised, read-only trial on ten fresh Emergentism
questions. This evaluates an existing lexical reader, not autonomous reasoning,
worldview truth, independent human usability, or the connected Soul Loop.
Original corpus and product code stay untouched. No provider calls, network
access, publication, policy promotion, scheduling, signing or deployment.
The standing A3 frozen-record HOLD remains; no dispatch-stream write is attempted.

# Question authorship

After this protocol and freeze are committed, a separately briefed agent reads
only the copied sources and authors ten new questions: seven supported questions
and three unsupported evidence requests. Across the seven include at least two
definitions, two apparent contradictions, two qualification-sensitive questions,
and two ordinary-language paraphrases (categories may overlap). The three
unsupported requests include at least one plausible near-topic request, not just
invented vocabulary. Unsupported means the selected corpus does not supply the
requested evidence, not that the proposition is false or absent everywhere.

Each supported question has 1–4 required answer parts. Each part names an exact
source span (or explicitly equivalent alternative spans), with at least one part
marked qualification. Spans should be minimal sufficient passages, not whole
documents. Source-informed authorship and agent-only review are disclosed;
this is neither a blind human trial nor an independently authored field test.

A second agent sees only the ten question texts, never sources, keys, engine or
results, and provides one question-only rephrase and 2–4 literal search terms
per question. It must preserve conditions and negations. Freeze questions, keys
and query aids before running. No result-informed repair is counted this trial.

# Arms and inspection budgets

1. **APU original:** unchanged strict results for the original question.
2. **APU assisted:** union of original strict results, the one disclosed rephrase
   and their section-navigation candidates. Navigation is only a reading lead,
   never an answer or automatic support classification. Preserve subchannels.
3. **Ordinary search:** case-insensitive literal OR of the 2–4 question-only
   terms, sorted by source path then line; first 20 matching lines with three
   surrounding lines on each side, merged when overlapping. No ranking,
   synonyms or answer-key access. This is an explicit simple search workflow,
   not a claim to beat all search engines. Report the unequal returned-text
   budgets; no controlled speed or efficiency claim follows.

No full-document oracle inspection is included in any scored arm. An auditor
may inspect sources to grade results, but those inspections are not APU hits.

# Measures (no single score)

- Exact source/revision/line/excerpt hashes for every returned APU passage.
- Required-span coverage: a part passes only if an entire frozen span is inside
  the union of returned line ranges for its source (or an admitted alternative).
  All-parts coverage and qualification coverage are separate counts. They are
  retrieval diagnostics, not semantic answer accuracy or proof completeness.
- Unsupported questions: empty results versus distracting nonempty candidates;
  empty results mean abstention-like retrieval behavior, not established absence.
- Human/agent review of misleading or contradictory leads, and lost conditions.
- Returned unique line count per arm as reading burden proxy; no invented time
  saved or human productivity measures.
- Repeat the unchanged CLI in fresh processes and compare output bytes; verify
  source and engine hashes before/after. Preserve all failures and raw outputs.
