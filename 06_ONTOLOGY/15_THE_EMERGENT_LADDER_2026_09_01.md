# The Emergent Ladder

> **Objects climb. People and agents do not.**

The Emergent Ladder is a selected instrument for deciding what may persist,
what may guide action, what may become reusable policy, and what may be
deliberately illuminated to others.

Its invariant is:

> Every promotion creates a new addressable object or event, preserves real
> ancestry, requires a typed grant, exposes affected bearers where consequence
> enters, and increases the burden of review. No object, agent, level,
> signature, popularity, or system promotes itself into truth or authority.
> Consequence returns as independent evidence.

The vertical direction means increasing **semantic commitment, persistence,
reuse, consequence, and required authority**. It does not mean increasing
human worth, automatic truth, metaphysical height, or organizational rank.

## 1. The seven selected rungs

| Rung | What newly appears | Canonical test | Default fate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signal** | A bounded occurrence: sensor sample, voice fragment, click, stream packet | Can its source, clock, scope, and integrity be named without pretending it already means something? | Transient unless lawfully captured |
| **Data** | Addressable bytes with identity, size, time, and provenance | Can the bytes be retrieved or referenced exactly? | Expires under its rights and retention policy unless promoted |
| **Information** | Data plus declared structure: schema, transcript, table, relation, or tag | Is the transformation inspectable, and is the structure distinguished from the source? | Ephemeral or hot until used, rejected, or promoted |
| **Knowledge** | A contestable claim with evidence, confidence/support state, rivals, and expiry or review | Does the claim say what supports it, what could defeat it, and what remains unknown? | Versioned; supported, contested, degraded, corrected, or superseded |
| **Judgment** | Knowledge plus a bounded decision whose affected bearers and consequences are visible | Is the choice distinct from the proposal, externally authorized where required, and reversible where possible? | Preserved with the decision and authority trail |
| **Wisdom** | A Judgment made into a scoped, reusable policy with review, revocation, and consequence return | May another actor lawfully reuse this exact rule without asking again because authority is already bound? | Versioned and revisable, never timeless by signature |
| **Public** | A deliberately lit, durable public projection of Knowledge or Wisdom | Are the exact public bytes minimized, authorized, receipted, correctable, and linked to their private ancestry without exposing it? | Public exposure cannot honestly be made never to have occurred |

These are semantic classes in this instrument. They are **not** seven storage
folders, seven database domain types, seven Rosetta functions, seven kinds of
people, or a proof that nature itself has exactly seven such stages. The
exhaustiveness claim remains `[C]`.

## 2. The graph is branched, not a prestige escalator

```text
Signal → Data → Information → Knowledge → Judgment → Wisdom
                                  │                    │
                                  └──────→ Public ←────┘
```

`Knowledge → Public` and `Wisdom → Public` are both legal lighting paths.
Publishing Knowledge does not pretend it became Judgment or Wisdom. Public is
a real seventh object class in this selected contract, but it is not a higher
grade of truth.

“No skipping” means no missing **real** ancestry, warrant, grant, or receipt.
It does not require a compiler to fabricate empty intermediate objects.
Already structured external material may enter at Information if its actual
source, transformation, actor, clock, rights, and disposition are retained.
An atomic transaction may create several logical successors efficiently, but
it must still preserve every real transition it performed.

Every accepted promotion is append-only. A changed or derived representation
gets a new identity; a transition is separately addressable. Rejection,
expiry, revocation, correction, contradiction, quarantine, and supersession
remain distinct. Last-write-wins is not a philosophy of memory.

## 3. Four axes that must not collapse

| Axis | It types | It does not supply |
|---|---|---|
| **D-register** | actuality, represented possibility, and the framework's ontological stopping points | a product class or grant |
| **L1–L7** | work functions and boundary counsel | an object rung, person rank, or authority |
| **Emergent rung** | the semantic condition of an object or projection | permission to create the next object |
| **Grant** | the exact verb, purpose, scope, bearer, expiry, and revocation under which a transition may occur | truth, wisdom, or authority beyond its source |

Therefore:

```text
L-level ≠ object rung ≠ actor grant
GEN7 ≠ G7
```

The D4/D5 firewall also remains: a modeled option, forecast, policy candidate,
or proposed future is D5 content; the actual modeling, promotion, signature,
publication, execution, and observation events are D4 occurrences.

## 4. Proposals, authority, execution, and outcomes

The ladder composes with—but does not replace—the consequence spine:

```text
Knowledge
  → unsigned Judgment proposal
  → external authority decision
  → Judgment record
  → exact-bound execution rail
  → ExecutionReceipt
  → independent OutcomeEvidence
  → revised Knowledge or Wisdom
```

A proposal is not a Judgment. A Judgment is not execution. An execution
receipt is not an outcome. A schema, model consensus, product seat, Rosetta
level, AI, swarm, repository, or UI creates no authority.

Agents may capture, structure, claim, and propose only under an exact grant.
`bind:judgment`, `ratify:wisdom`, and `light:public` require the applicable
external human or legal-person mandate, or a valid previously signed delegation
covering the exact act. A child grant is a strict subset of its parent.

## 5. How Wisdom emerges

Wisdom is not “being smart.” It is a reusable rule whose scope has been bound
so another actor may lawfully apply it without asking again each time.

That definition has two independent layers:

1. **Authorization:** an applicable external authority ratifies a versioned,
   scoped, expiring or reviewable, revocable policy. This creates a Wisdom
   object and only the exact reuse permission named in it.
2. **Maturity:** contact with consequences marks the policy
   `PROVISIONAL`, `SUPPORTED`, `CONTESTED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.

A signature authorizes bounded reuse. It does not make the policy true, good,
successful, universal, or permanently wise. `SUPPORTED` requires the policy's
own declared evidence threshold and independent outcome contact. Counterexamples
remain visible. Every maturity change creates a successor and review receipt;
history is not edited in place.

A Wisdom object must expose at least its domain, affected bearers, horizon,
source Knowledge and Judgment, policy version, authority scope, expiry/review,
revocation path, evidence state, counterexamples, kill criterion, correction,
supersession, and Exit.

## 6. Creation, destruction, and preservation

Every consequential Judgment and every reviewed policy carries three separate
ledgers:

| Ledger | Question |
|---|---|
| **Creation** | Which capabilities, options, relations, knowledge, or value were added—and for whom? |
| **Destruction** | Which capabilities, options, trust, resources, continuity, or value were removed—and from whom? |
| **Preservation** | Which accumulated capacities, histories, relations, or conditions were maintained, repaired, transmitted, or rescued? |

Each entry names the bearer, baseline, horizon, consent, reversibility,
externalities, evidence, and uncertainty. No unsupported net scalar may hide
concentrated harm or incomparable bearers.

“Creative destruction,” “destructive creation,” “regenerative creation,”
“wise preservation,” and “pathological preservation” are defeasible readings
of these ledgers—not primitive moral labels. Destruction is not redeemed merely
because creation is visible; preservation is not good merely because an
incumbent survives.

The working conjecture `[I/C]` is:

> Wisdom is the learned capacity to create what should exist, destroy only
> what must end, and preserve what future creation still needs.

## 7. Public begins another loop

Public is a lighting event, not a backup, a truth upgrade, or the end of the
epistemic process:

```text
Public
  → encountered
  → comprehended
  → tested
  → adopted
  → adapted
  → consequence
  → evidence
  → revised Knowledge or Wisdom
```

Reach, fidelity, comprehension, trialability, adoption, adaptation, outcome,
externality, retention, and generativity stay distinct. Virality, repetition,
search rank, citation count, polished presentation, and model association
create neither warrant nor value.

The anti-capture rule is:

> Compress enough to travel; preserve enough ancestry to verify; expose enough
> uncertainty to contest; keep enough Exit to refuse; and return enough
> consequence to revise.

## 8. Justice, bearers, and Exit

At the point where a promotion can affect lives, it must expose at least the
source or data subject, rights bearer, proposer, authorizer, directly affected
people, beneficiaries, burden-bearers, downstream recipients, future
maintainers, people omitted or misclassified, and non-users bearing
externalities—where each is applicable.

Justice requires consent, purpose limitation, reversibility, continuity,
remedy, affected-whole visibility, and public disagreement. The selected
teleology does not authorize means: **the means are the message; the end is the
limit.** No desirable endpoint excuses an illegal promotion, coercive
diffusion, silent extraction, hidden harm, or trapped exit.

Exit includes refusal of capture where applicable, lawful expiry, grant
revocation, correction of structure and attribution, contradiction and
supersession without erasure, withdrawal before irreversible action where
possible, policy sunset, and public correction or retraction. Valid deletion
or expiry propagates to derived views and caches. Any surviving receipt is the
minimum lawful non-content-bearing record unless an independent duty requires
more.

## 9. Application readings are adapters, not identities

The following mapping is `[I]` and non-exclusive:

| Application function | Legitimate relation to the ladder | Refusal |
|---|---|---|
| **Boundary intake** | admits or quarantines raw Signal/Data | admission is not truth |
| **Sourced observation** | structures attributed observations as Information | a sourced observation packet is not raw Signal and repetition is not corroboration |
| **Forecasting** | produces uncertainty-bearing Knowledge candidates | forecast is not fact, choice, or execution |
| **Proposal** | emits unsigned Judgment proposals | proposal is not authorization |
| **External authority** | may bind Judgment, ratify scoped Wisdom, or authorize lighting | signature is not truth or outcome |
| **Field practice** | may embody an authorized act and return receipts and independent outcome requests | it may not authorize or certify itself |
| **Memory system** | preserves typed lineage and refuses illegal persistence transitions | acceptance is not domain truth and a system name creates no legal-person authority |
| **Constraint interface** | may request/display promotion and return a refusal, correction, or non-authorizing pass | presentation is not approval or publication |
| **Worldview instrument** | tests meaning, purpose, Justice, limits, kills, and survivors | orientation is not runtime or authority |

Named products may later bind themselves to one or more of these functions in
their own source lanes. This ontology neither assigns their residence nor
imports their implementation as a premise.

## 10. What would kill or downgrade the instrument

Return the machine-contract projection to `HOLD` if it:

1. turns rungs into kinds of people, Rosetta levels, ranks, or exclusive
   product residences;
2. turns the seven labels into seven application domain types or folders;
3. fabricates intermediate provenance to satisfy “no skipping”;
4. permits raw conversation, embeddings, tags, or generated prose to become
   durable Knowledge automatically;
5. treats a proposal as Judgment, receipt as outcome, signature as truth, or
   Public as a greater truth state;
6. lets any software identity sign, authorize, publish, spend, or widen its own
   grant;
7. hides affected bearers or collapses creation, destruction, and preservation
   into one unsupported score;
8. retains a claim while missing evidence leaves its support state unchanged;
9. optimizes spread while omitting warrant, uncertainty, correction, and Exit;
10. cannot reject any illegal semantic promotion beyond what ordinary access
    control and the existing consequence spine already reject.

If the last discriminator fails, the ladder survives as an explanatory
instrument but does not earn a new protocol.

## 11. Current claim ceiling

The selected ladder is internally coherent enough to specify and test. It is
not evidence that seven rungs exhaust epistemology, that every transition is
strong emergence, that any product implements it, that a policy is wise in
outcome, that value was created, or that anything was published or deployed.

The instrument earns confidence only by refusing illegal promotions,
preserving real provenance, enabling correction, and surviving independent
consequence contact.
