# The Ring and the Ground — Devotional Boundary

> **[金] Crack:** the predecessor identified Tolkien's Ring with `η>0`, a pole,
> and evil, then opposed it to an `η=0` ring of goodness. **Repair:** preserve
> the literary warning without turning a measurement into ontology or ethics.

Tolkien's Ring can be read as an `[I]` image of concentrated power that alters
the bearer, narrows contest, hides costs, and converts means into ends. That
reading is illuminating because it makes custody, temptation, irreversibility,
and consequence vivid. It is not a mathematical identity, an empirical
analysis of Tolkien's intent, or a theorem about every technology.

The Ground is not a ring, artifact, coordinate, person, possession, or
institution. Ordinary `0`, `1`, and the projective `∞` do not encode Sauron,
good, evil, or moral rank. `etaObserved` may record scoped extraction, but zero
observed extraction is necessary and insufficient for Justice.

The practical question licensed by the story is:

> Who controls this power, who pays, who benefits, what becomes irreversible,
> how can the act be contested, and can every bearer leave?

Answer with an authorization envelope and outcome receipt, not a mythic label.
If the analogy conceals evidence, predetermines guilt, or ranks a bearer by a
symbolic pole, put it down.
