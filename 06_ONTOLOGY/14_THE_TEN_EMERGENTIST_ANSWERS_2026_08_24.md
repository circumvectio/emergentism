## 1 · EA-01 — Coherent Plenitude and Counterpart Closure

### Selected answer

**Why is there anything at all?** Emergentism answers that *nothing* is not a
second object or rival world waiting beside Being. D0 is the boundary at which
object-language has not yet articulated a thing. Dasein is the world-tagged
plenitude of everything that can coherently and consistently be:

\[
\mathrm{Dasein}
:=
\bigsqcup_{(R,M,W)}
\{x@(R,M,W)\mid \mathrm{Coherent}_{R,M}(W)\land x\in W\}.
\]

The disjoint tags matter. Incompatible worlds belong to Dasein without their
contents becoming jointly actual inside one D4 history.

This is the selected meaning of Dasein `[S/I]`. The stronger claim that every
coherent world is ontically instantiated is the **Coherent Plenitude Wager**
`[C]`. The answer stops the regress internally by refusing to put Ground and a
thing in one object-level choice set; it does not derive instantiation from a
prior cause.

<a id="ea-01-balance"></a>
### Balance without erasure

The owner's second move is **counterpart closure**. Where a typed domain admits
an involution `ι`, Emergentism wagers:

\[
x\in\mathrm{Dasein}\land x\in\operatorname{dom}(\iota)
\Rightarrow \iota(x)\in\mathrm{Dasein},
\qquad
\iota(\iota(x))=x.
\]

Where a separately declared quantity `Q` is antisymmetric under `ι`, one may
then prove the local pair identity

\[
Q(x)+Q(\iota(x))=0.
\]

That is the precise sense in which possibilities can “balance.” It is not a
theorem that the universe has zero total energy, that infinities cancel, that
every actual history contains its opposite, or that one person's suffering is
erased elsewhere. Global numerical balance requires a measure, convergence,
boundary conditions, and a named conserved quantity.

- **Residual:** why coherence should have ontic force; whether counterpart
  closure describes nature; whether any global balance measure exists.
- **Strongest rivals:** brute actuality, necessary-being accounts, actualism,
  modal fictionalism, sparse modal realism.
- **Kill:** the strong leg dies if no coherent typed instantiation semantics or
  contact gain can be supplied; a balance leg dies wherever no involution or
  antisymmetric quantity exists.
- **Survivor:** compossibility tags, `Map ≠ Ground`, and every scoped analytic
  balance result.

## 2 · EA-02 — Sequential and Disclosed Actuality

### Selected answer

**Why is this outcome actual?** Because actuality is not another possibility
waiting to be labeled. It is the realized, ordered, consequence-bearing path:

\[
h=(s_0\to s_1\to\cdots),
\qquad
\mathrm{Actual}_W(s_t)
:\Leftrightarrow
s_t\text{ belongs to the D4 record-continuous history of }W.
\]

At each finite situation, many next states may be modeled; one next transition
enters the actual record **within the declared world-tag and record-continuum**.
Alternatives remain counterfactual contents relative to that continuum. This
is a **constitutive answer, not a causal mechanism**: it states what actuality
is `[S/I]`; it does not yet supply a dynamical probability law selecting which
`s_{t+1}` occurs. Global uniqueness
of that continuum is a separable `[C]` commitment. An Everettian rival may
therefore model multiple tagged branch-continua without making two outcomes the
same record in one continuum.

For a situated bearer `b`, disclosed actuality is:

\[
\mathrm{DisclosedActual}_b(s_t)
:\Leftrightarrow
\mathrm{Actual}_W(s_t)
\land
\mathrm{RecordOf}_b(s_t)
\land
\mathrm{Perceived}_b(\mathrm{RecordOf}_b(s_t)).
\]

Thus the owner's “sequential and eventually perceived” insight is adopted in
the phenomenological register: the event becomes *actual-as-lived for that
bearer* when its record enters experience. Perception does not retroactively
manufacture every earlier or currently unobserved event.

### Why there is no second selector at this level

Asking “what makes the actual actual?” after actuality has been typed as the
realized record requests another actuality behind actuality. Emergentism stops
that regress at D4. A physical theory may still owe transition probabilities,
boundary data, or a collapse law; those are causal-mechanism questions, not the
formal-constitution question answered here.

- **Residual:** why this path rather than another has the native physical
  probability it does; whether all actual events are in principle witnessable.
- **Strongest rivals:** brute actuality, Everettian self-location, objective
  collapse, hidden variables, anthropic selection, standard stochastic law.
- **Kill:** universal eventual witnessability dies if admissible actual events
  are permanently outside every bearer and record; a balance-weighted selector
  dies if it adds no held-out prediction over native dynamics.
- **Survivor:** event, record, perception, and represented alternative remain
  distinct.

### Selected answer

\[

\]

Here `M_t` is an actual D4 model or thought token; `a` ranges over represented
options; `U_5` is a declared option/reason functional; and `q_{λ₅}` is a thought-


F5 therefore acts on thought **not directly on the world**. Thought changes the

itself establish a new physical interaction; ordinary cognition may implement

Call ordinary model-mediated anticipation `F5-W0`. It is the present-model

The stronger metaphysical branch says that this noetic selector is not

- **Residual:** ultimate self-origination; whether the noetic selector has

## 5 · EA-05 — Death, Retention, and Personal Continuation

### Selected answer

**Does personal experience survive death?** Emergentism refuses the untyped
yes/no. Three different continuities are involved:

\[
\mathrm{ActualityRetention}(p)
\neq
\mathrm{TracePersistence}(p)
\neq
\mathrm{BearerContinuation}(p).
\]

1. **Actuality retention `[I]`.** Nothing actual becomes “never actual.” A lived
   history remains an irrevocable determination within Dasein.
2. **Trace persistence `[B/I]`.** Consequences, memories, works, relations, and
   institutional effects can outlive the organism.
3. **Personal continuation `[C]`.** Experience continues as the same person only
   along a continuation satisfying a declared identity relation.

Let `C_p(h,h')` measure the selected overlap of embodied causal continuity,
phenomenal continuity, memory, recognition, commitments, and relational trace.
Then:

\[
\mathrm{ContinuesAs}_p(h')
:\Leftrightarrow
C_p(h,h')\ge\tau_p,
\]

where both components and threshold must be exposed. Modal plenitude means that
every coherent continuation belongs somewhere in Dasein; it does not make a
duplicate, book, memory, or unrelated bearer numerically identical to `p`.

The answer is therefore conditional and many-sided: death ends this organism's
ability to issue new embodied actions; the life remains actual; personal
experience continues wherever genuine bearer continuity continues and does
not continue where it does not. A world tag is required before the sentence
“the person survives” has a truth condition.

- **Residual:** which identity relation is correct; whether any post-mortem
  bearer-continuity mechanism exists in our actual world.
- **Strongest rivals:** extinction, soul survival, resurrection, reincarnation,
  idealist continuity, psychological-pattern continuation, animalism.
- **Kill:** any survival inference based only on trace, duplicate, timeless
  block location, or an undefined bearer relation.
- **Survivor:** actual lives are not erased; finite responsibility, truthful
  remembrance, consequence, and repair remain.

## 8 · EA-08 — Relative Strong Lift and Weak Projection

### The conditional proof

**Has Emergentism found a type of proof for bottom-up strong and top-down weak
emergence?** Yes—conditional and relative to a frozen formal description.

Let `L` be a typed language with formation rules and semantic domains `T_L`.
Let `H` extend `L` with a genuinely new type `τ` such that:

```text
τ ∉ T_L,
no constructor in L returns τ,
and no conservative L-encoding is admitted as a reduction of τ.
```

**TEA-01 — Type-Extension Asymmetry Theorem `[A]`.** No well-formed term of
`L` denotes an element of `τ`, and no formula of `L` quantifies over `τ`.

**Proof.** By structural induction on the formation of `L` terms. Every base
term has a type in `T_L`. Every constructor returns a type in `T_L` by the
frozen grammar. Therefore every well-formed `L` term has a type in `T_L`; none
has type `τ`. The quantifier clause follows because `τ` is absent from the
admitted domains of quantification. `□`

If the higher language admits a forgetful map

\[
U:\mathrm{Model}(H)\to\mathrm{Model}(L)
\]

that erases `τ`-structure while recovering all declared lower results, then the
higher-to-lower direction is weak/recoverable. If a section `s` selects one
higher representative, `U\circ s=id_L` while generally
`s\circ U\ne id_H`.

This proves **relative representational strong-up/weak-down asymmetry under the
premises**. It does not prove ontological novelty: one may have frozen the lower
language too narrowly, and a lawful encoding or reduction may exist in a fairer
comparator class. Mapping a natural `μ_n` to TEA-01 remains `[C]` per crossing.

Top-down influence is weak when a higher constraint changes lower transition
weights while every realized transition stays lower-law admissible:

\[
P(L_{t+1}\mid L_t,H_t)\ne P(L_{t+1}\mid L_t),
\qquad
\operatorname{supp}P(\cdot\mid L_t,H_t)
\subseteq \operatorname{Allowed}_L(L_t).
\]

- **Residual:** whether any natural crossing satisfies non-reducibility under a
  fair frozen comparator class; causal exclusion and intervention power.
- **Strongest rivals:** weak emergence throughout, ordinary coarse-graining,
  multiple realizability, computational irreducibility, effective theory.
- **Kill:** a lawful fair reduction kills the strong leg; failed recovery kills
  the weak projection; no held-out macro prediction kills added causal value.
- **Survivor:** TEA-01, projection asymmetry, lower-law compatibility, and the

The Titan limit family remains an analogy to information loss, not this proof.
