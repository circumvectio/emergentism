# The Debt-Service Conjectures — W14–W18 (staged)

## 0 · The axis, stated once

07C wagers that each opened freedom incurs a consistency debt and that a
physical interaction is the law that pays it. 09 §5 established that D5's debt
is the one debt that *cannot* be paid in full without collapsing the register
(the gapless self-account falls to D4). Put together, one axis appears:

> **A consistency debt is either SETTLED — paid once, by a law — or SERVICED —
> carried, by an ongoing practice. Which of the two a register's debt admits
> types the register.**

D1–D4 debts are settled. D5's is serviceable only. Everything below is that
one distinction driven through physics, biology, institutions, language, and
self-modeling. Each conjecture can die alone; the axis dies only if all five do.

## W14-SETTLEMENT · the refusal-prediction `[I]` reading · `[C]` wager

**Statement.** The F5 slot in W7 differs in kind from F1–F4 because D5's debt
admits no settlement: **no fifth fundamental interaction coupling to
representation-as-such will be found.** Whatever occupies the slot is a
practice — selection, error-correction, self-revision — not a force. The 07C
ladder stops at gravity *necessarily*, not contingently.

**Emerges from / bridge.** 07C's debt mechanism + 09 §5.3 (both exits from D5
land in D4). The bridge is typed: settlement of the D5 debt ≡ the gapless
self-account ≡ leaving D5.

**Prior art / rival.** W7 already holds F5 at weak form (model-mediated
teleonomy). Rival: any fifth-force programme. Note the standing ones
(quintessence-class) couple to stress-energy, not to representational content
as such — they would not kill this even if found.

**Discriminator.** This is a *refusal*-prediction: the framework forecasts
what physics will not find. Its value is asymmetric and slow, and that is
declared rather than hidden.

**Kill.** Discovery of an interaction whose coupling is a function of
representational content as such. **Survivor if killed:** the 07C ladder gains
a rung; the settlement/service axis dies at the physics register only.

## W15-REFINANCE · maintenance refinancing at the apertures `[C]`

**Statement.** Crossing each `μ` aperture converts part of a system's
maintenance from **settled** (equilibrium, paid by law) to **serviced**
(feedback, paid by correction). At `μ₄` the conversion turns reflexive: the
system carries a distinct, irreducible **error-correction budget about its own
model**, scaling with representational scope rather than with mass or
metabolism alone — and the correction fraction should *jump* at the aperture,
not vary smoothly.

**Prior art, named first.** Friston's free-energy principle (already in this
corpus: `PD_13_HARD_PROBLEM.md`, `50_FINITY_L_THE_HELD_POSITION.md`);
Landauer. **Difference:** FEP claims a universal gradient; W15 claims a
**register-typed discontinuity located at the apertures.** That difference is
the discriminator, so W15 is not FEP restated.

**Discriminator.** Budget decomposition across matched systems straddling an
aperture (with/without anticipatory models): correction fraction as a step
function vs smooth scaling.

**Kill.** No correction component separable from equilibrium maintenance in
full-D5 systems, or smooth scaling with no register structure. **Survivor:**
the settled/serviced distinction survives qualitatively even if the jump does
not.

## W16-WITNESS · the witness-fraction bound `[I]` reading · `[C]` bound

**Statement.** Any durable self-correcting system must spend a nonzero,
irreducible fraction of its budget on **non-acting verification** — witness
work: gates, audits, probes that produce no product — and below a
domain-specific threshold the system drifts poleward: coherence-runaway
(instruments that pass while testing nothing) or viability-runaway (action
without account). **The deployability split is this bound in institutional
form**: the seat that maintains the optimum must not be the seat that acts.

**Evidence already in custody `[B]`.** The 2026-08-13 census: five instruments
publishing properties they did not test, in a corpus whose witness work had
lapsed; every repair consisted of *paying* witness cost (planted negatives,
mutation probes) — and the gates then caught their own author twice within
hours.

**Siblings, cited not absorbed.** `10_PARASITE_LOAD_WAGER` — verification is
the layer-*n+1* defense of the claim layer, so W16 is its D5-register case.
The 2026-08-15 hologenome council's warrant-economics `[C]` (*dies if
verification is free*) is the complementary direction, relayed `[R]`: warrants
have value because verification costs; W16 says systems die when it goes
unpaid.

**Rival.** Mechanism-design substitution: incentives alone yield durability
with zero audit.

**Discriminator.** Matched systems (repos, organisations, ML training runs)
varying only audit fraction; measure defect half-life and drift. ML form:
eval-compute fraction vs collapse under self-training.

**Kill.** A durable self-correcting system with zero witness fraction, or
drift independent of audit budget. **Fence:** the bound governs instruments
and budgets, never persons; no witness-fraction reading of a human is
admissible (`KSC-24/25`).

## W17-TRANSLATE · translation-degradation as ill-posedness detector `[I/C]`

**Statement.** A claim survives register translation (`fable ↔ plainly ↔
record`) with tier intact **iff it is well-posed**; systematic content-loss on
rendering into the machine-checkable register predicts `NOT-WELL-POSED` or
terminal status. **The three scripts are thereby an instrument, not
outreach** — record-renderability is a cheap pre-filter for the graves.

**Evidence in custody `[B]`.** `W0-COMPLETE`: the completeness claim could not
be rendered as a check (the equivocation on *complete*) and died
NOT-WELL-POSED, while its successor — a failure-mode description — renders
cleanly. That is one blind trial already run by history.

**Rival / confound.** Translation-loss may measure formalisation *effort*, not
ill-posedness.

**Discriminator — and this is the cheapest contact available to the corpus
today.** Retrodictive: blind-code every historical grave and survivor for
record-renderability *at time of entry*, then unblind against outcome.
Prospective: W13–W18 themselves are the first preregistered cohort.

**Kill.** Well-posed survivors degrade as often as graves under blinded
coding.

## W18-FIXPOINT · the reflexive convergence band `[C]`

**Statement.** Iterated self-accounts (the Dasein Test run repeatedly, each
pass ingesting its predecessor) admit three regimes: **divergence**
(incoherence), **freezing** (a fixed point with zero novel prediction — the
pole, the gapless claim wearing a halo), and a **convergence band** —
successive accounts stabilise in core commitments while retaining falsifiable
novelty. Health is the band: the equator, circled.

**Prior art.** Löb-style fixed points; MacKay; and the corpus's own kintsugi
practice is itself an exhibit of the band — stable identity through visible
revision.

**Discriminator.** Cross-architecture, per `W10-SPARK`'s existing fence:
inter-iteration distance under a declared metric plus novel-prediction rate,
band boundaries declared before running. Within-architecture convergence
counts for nothing.

**Kill.** No reproducible three-regime structure, or fluent freezing
indistinguishable from honest convergence even across architectures.
