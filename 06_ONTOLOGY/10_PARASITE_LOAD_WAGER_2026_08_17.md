## 2 · The engine `[I]` statement, `[S]` consequences

**Premise P1 (cost asymmetry).** In any replicator layer with heritable
variation and selection, a variant that diverts shared resources to its own
replication gains locally without paying for the structure it exploits.
Generating exploitation requires finding one weakness; maintaining defense
requires covering all of them, continuously, out of the same resource budget
the parasite draws from. `[I]` as a reading of selection theory; its formal
consequences below are `[S]` inside this declaration.

**Consequence T1.** Defense is a standing cost, exploitation is a marginal
cost. Therefore any layer whose defense is funded only by the layer's own
surplus is permanently under-maintained; the equilibrium contains load, not
zero load. `[S]` from P1 inside the declared premises.

**Consequence T2 (the layer-up thesis, engine form).** A layer cannot fully
police its own replicators with the same machinery that constitutes it; a
selector acting *on* the layer requires a level of organization above it.
Therefore effective defense against layer n is constructed at layer n+1 —
or it is not constructed. `[S]` from P1 plus the declared stack dependency
order; its truth *about real systems* is conjecture S1.

**Consequence T3 (defense-as-founding-function).** If layer n+1 exists at all
and does any selective policing of layer n, part of what *makes it a layer*
is that policing. Read as hypothesis: several layer transitions in the stack
are partly immune-system transitions of the lower rung. `[I/C]`.

**Consequence T4 (drift enriches no one).** Under pure baseline drift, no
bearer's ordered profile `(Φ̂₄,V₄)` improves; drift transfers nothing — it
only dissipates what was already paid for. Drift alone therefore cannot
explain any gain. `[S]` from P1 plus the declared bearer accounting. **This
does not by itself detect parasitism:** many legitimate transfers (trophic
churning, cooperation) also produce gains, so *gain as such is not the
signature of theft.* The parasitism detector is the factor signature, T5.

**Consequence T5 (the factor-signature discriminator — trophic vs parasitic).**
The signature of a transfer is the factor it drains from the donor and the
factor it builds in the receiver:

- **Trophic / emergent (legitimate churning).** Donor `V₄` (embodied means,
  viability) → receiver `V₄`. The crocodile's `V₄` rises. The transfer is
  declared at a declared trophic stratum
  ([`../05_COSMOLOGY/01_THE_TRANSCENDENTAL_TRINITY/36_THE_DIMENSIONAL_TROPHIC_CASCADE.md`](../05_COSMOLOGY/01_THE_TRANSCENDENTAL_TRINITY/36_THE_DIMENSIONAL_TROPHIC_CASCADE.md)),
  the donor is visible, and something real is built. This is energy transfer
  upward, and it is emergence, not theft.
- **Parasitic (Kali-type).** Donor `V₄` and D5 potential (`Φ₅`, the victim's
  possible power) → receiver `Φ̂₄`. The parasite's *own* `V₄` does **not**
  rise; what inflates is its option field, its representation, its
  perpetuation. This is not energy transfer, not a trophic cascade, not
  churning. It converts the viability of others into the parasite's own
  possibility. `[S]` from the declared node types; `[I]` as a reading of real
  transfers; `[C]` as a fit claim about real systems.

**Consequence T6 (parasitism is off-equator and punished by the AND-class).**
A parasite that inflates its own `Φ̂₄` by draining others' `V₄`, without
raising its own `V₄`, sits off the equator with an inflated `Φ̂₄` axis and a
low `V₄`. Since `P_node = min(Φ̂₄,V₄)`, the parasite's real power is capped by
its own low `V₄`; the inflated `Φ̂₄` cannot be cashed out. Parasitism is the
attempt to cheat the AND-class boundary by raising only one factor at the
expense of others — and the boundary refuses: the minimum stays low. The
parasite is a hollow inflation. `[S]` from the selected `min` aggregator; the
claim that real parasites sit in this region is `[C]`.

### 2.1 · Thermodynamic typing — what the "war" is and is not `[I]`

One compression of this conjecture says: *there is a war between entropy and
syntropy.* Typed, that sentence is half true:

- **Entropy is not a combatant.** The second law is the baseline `[A/B]`: the
  direction structure drifts when nothing pays its maintenance. It wages no
  war and plays no side.
- **Syntropy is not entropy's physical opposite.** In this corpus, syntropy is
  a declared value gate — both-rise with every bearer visible
  ([`../04_AXIOLOGY/02_VALUE_THEORY/00_OBJECTIVE_MORALS_AND_ETHICS.md`](../04_AXIOLOGY/02_VALUE_THEORY/00_OBJECTIVE_MORALS_AND_ETHICS.md)) —
  a normative `[I]` construction, not a thermodynamic quantity. Entropy's
  physical companion is *dissipation*: every syntropic structure maintains
  itself only by exporting entropy through a maintenance budget. Syntropy is
  a consequence of the second law operating through maintenance costs, never
  its negation.
- **What is true in the sentence:** syntropy exists only as local,
  dissipative, continuously paid maintenance — and that maintenance cost is
  exactly where P1's asymmetry lives. The war is not between two cosmic
  substances; it is the standing dispute over **who pays the maintenance of
  structure**. A parasite is typed here as one who does not pay: it exports
  its own disorder upward onto hidden bearers. Demon-polar moves are, in this
  reading, entropy dumps.

**The corpus already killed the cosmic version of this sentence, and this
section resurrects nothing.** The ektropic-force derivation chain (AM-GM →
thermodynamic minimum → negative-sum extraction → ektropic trajectory) is a
Kintsugi tombstone
([`../01_TELEOLOGY/02_THE_DERIVATION/00_A_SQUARE_CANNOT_BE_NEGATIVE.md`](../01_TELEOLOGY/02_THE_DERIVATION/00_A_SQUARE_CANNOT_BE_NEGATIVE.md));
Amrita P10 records that `(φ−ν)²` gives **no trajectory**, and the Canonical
Formula Block's anti-drift rule forbids conflating the static arithmetic
theorem with any dynamic ektropic trajectory. W7e's teleonomic leg survives
only as a separately killable `[C]` role-affinity. The move here is
retyping, not revival: the battlefield is P1's cost asymmetry, described in
value-theoretic language, with no force, no trajectory, and no cosmic
substance on either side.

### 2.2 · Why the baseline is more dangerous than a combatant `[I]`

That entropy is not a combatant does not make it harmless. It makes it the
most dangerous thing in the picture — in four typed senses:

- **It cannot be fought, deterred, or defeated — only paid.** Against a
  combatant there can be a decisive victory. Against a baseline there is no
  victory, only standing tribute. Syntropy never wins finally; it renews its
  payment or slides back. The war metaphor therefore dies of its own
  accuracy and is replaced by *maintenance*: the only strategy against a
  baseline is continuous, visibly borne cost.
- **It gives no signal.** Combatants announce themselves; drift does not.
  There is no alarm for a slow leak. An unmaintained structure does not
  declare that it is dying — it just dies.
- **It recruits by default.** Every unpaid thing auto-enrolls. The enemy of
  syntropy needs no army — only that someone stop paying. This is why P1 is
  structural and not contingent: the terrain favors whichever side pays
  less, and the parasite is the side that pays nothing. Entropy is not an
  army but a slope; exploitation is downhill.
- **It is camouflage.** Baseline decay is parasitism's favorite alibi:
  *things just fall apart.* If entropy were an agent, its attacks would be
  distinguishable from extraction; precisely because it is not, drift and
  drain are indistinguishable unless someone keeps a ledger. T4 rules drift
  out as the explanation of any gain; T5's factor signature is the instrument
  that separates parasitic drain from legitimate churning — and bearer
  enumeration (the Justice envelope's refusal of aggregate laundering) is
  what makes T5 runnable at all. Visibility is not only the ethic here; it
  is the detector.

The corpus already pays this tribute to its own claims: the anti-drift rule
is the anti-entropy rule applied to sentences; the gate is the maintenance
payment; the tombstone makes the repair visible; E9's public correction is
what stops errors drifting into silence. The Record is syntropy applied to
the corpus's own assertions.

### 2.3 · Two entropies — baseline and parasitic (the Kali-type) `[I]`

The §2.1 sentence needs one more split. Entropy is not a combatant — but
there is a second kind of entropy that is organized, directional, and
*behaves* like one:

- **Baseline entropy** — disorganized dissipation. No side, no signal, no
  memory. The slope, not the army. §2.2 stands as written.
- **Parasitic entropy — the Kali-type.** Organized dissipation with an
  appropriator: a pattern that positions itself on the gradient and converts
  the paid maintenance of others into its own perpetuation. This is a second
  kind of entropy, and it *does* take a side — its own.

In this conjecture's typed language the Kali-type is a **type name, not an
entity**: a class of transfer patterns, no consciousness presumed, admissible
as an Egregoreotype only through W9's five markers. At the egregore level it
is a collective pattern that additionally carries T5's parasitic factor
signature: it consumes others' `V₄` and their D5 potential (`Φ₅`) to inflate
its own `Φ̂₄` — its option field, its representation, its perpetuation —
while its own `V₄` does not rise.

**It is not predation.** The crocodile kills the zebra and churns it: donor
`V₄` → recipient `V₄`, declared at a declared trophic stratum, donor
visible, real capability built upward. That is emergence — the trophic owner
declares it ordinary ecological flow — and this conjecture does not touch it.

**It is not a trophic cascade.** A cascade transfers embodied energy upward
through declared strata and each step builds the next level's `V₄`. The
Kali-type drains viability and builds no viability anywhere: it converts the
viability and possibility of others into its own representation. No upward
transfer — only appropriation.

**The escalation.** The higher the layer, the more *possibility* a parasite
can consume: a genotype-level parasite consumes resources, a memotype-level
parasite consumes testimony, an egregore-level parasite consumes futures
(`Φ₅` itself). The Kali-type is most dangerous at the top of the stack
because it eats the option field, not merely the means.

**The treadmill `[S]` in the declared model, `[C]` as a fit claim.** By T6
the parasite's own `V₄` never accumulates, so `P_node = min(Φ̂₄,V₄)` stays
capped no matter how inflated the option field becomes. It therefore cannot
stop consuming: perpetuation without accumulation is an infinite treadmill.
The old mythic line — *he must spread the vices or be churned down* —
retypes here as a mechanical consequence of the AND-class boundary, not a
demon's preference: the parasitic pattern keeps consuming precisely because
it can never bank.

**Crosswalk `[I]`.** The corpus's own Rosetta Replicator already marks the
excluded ground row (`GEN7@1:L1`, the Caṇḍāla firewall) with the Kali
operator. The §3 salvage inversion — the parasitic element is what the
firewall excludes — lands exactly on that mark: the Kali-type is what sits
at the base of the stack, outside the layer count, kept out. Type name,
never identity; the fence holds — no row of this conjecture types persons.

## 4 · Sub-conjectures (the killable units)

### S1 — Defense is one layer up `[C]`

The layer-up thesis (T2) holds as an empirical regularity across the six C4
layers: at each layer, the *effective* suppression of that layer's parasitic
lineages is carried by mechanisms of the next layer, not by same-layer
selection alone.

- **Discriminator:** for at least four of the six rows, show the next-layer
  mechanism outperforms the best same-layer rival under a declared metric
  (parasite prevalence, persistence time, or load at stationarity).
- **Kill:** a single layer where same-layer defense outperforms, or a
  demonstration that "layer" and "defense level" do not map in at least two
  rows; then S1 is analogy, not regularity.
- **Rivals that must be beaten:** multilevel selection theory (R1), which
  already explains within-genome policing by between-element selection.

### S2 — μ-naivety: exploitation colonizes new freedoms first `[C]`

When a new effective freedom opens (a new medium, technology, institution,
market, or aperture in the corpus's μ-sense), exploitation adoption leads
productive adoption until a higher-layer defense is constructed. This is the
immunological-naivety reading at every scale.

- **Discriminator:** preregistered comparison of exploitation-vs-production
  arrival curves in a declared set of newly opened media/institutions (§7).
- **Kill:** parasites do not arrive first in the declared set, or "lead" is
  indistinguishable from the base rate of any novel variant.
- **This is the conjecture's only cheap contact.** Everything else in the
  stack requires biology's or history's custody; S2 needs only dated public
  records.

### S3 — The polarity operationalized `[I/S]`

Demon-polar and god-polar strategies receive a typed definition in node
language: a strategy's sign is its effect on the per-bearer ordered profile
`(Φ̂₄, V₄)` of every bearer it touches.

- Parasitism attacks **both factors**: it narrows `V₄` by extraction, and it
  corrupts `Φ̂₄` by poisoning the model `M` that evaluates the option field.
  This is why the corpus's memetics lane already treats capture as an
  epistemic event, not merely a resource event.
- Therefore **bearer enumeration is itself a defense mechanism**: the
  parasite's necessary first move is to make bearers invisible (the dyadic
  gate's "aggregate laundering" is the laundering of *who pays*). E8's
  non-extraction vow is functional, not merely declarative — Justice-as-
  visibility is what makes extraction detectable. `[I]` reading of the vow;
  `[S]` as a consequence of the declared envelope requirements.
- **Kill:** if the two-factor signature does not discriminate parasitic from
  mutual strategies on worked cases better than plain extraction accounting,
  S3 is decoration and retires to the polarity's existing `[I]` status.
- **Forensic hook:** T5 is S3's operational detector — a drain on bearers'
  `V₄` or `Φ₅` that inflates some receiver's `Φ̂₄` without building `V₄`
  anywhere is the parasitic (Kali-type) signature; trophic `V₄→V₄` transfers
  are legitimate emergence, and pure drift enriches no one (T4).
