# The Crown Wager and Conjectures

> The axiom set is the framework's chosen footing. These are its exposed bets.
> A wager may die without the chart, the correction discipline, the justice vow,
> or the exit dying with it.

## Numbering seam

The older kernel used **W0** for “the map is not the Ground.” That apophatic
boundary remains valid but is not reassigned. The crown wager is therefore
**CW-0**, avoiding a false identity between two different claims.

## CW-0 · The Crown Wager `[I/C]`

> *Actuality may instantiate a necessary generative structure not exhausted by
> any one model.*

This is the bold metaphysical wager behind “the necessary algebra conjures das
All.” It is not a theorem that mathematics causes its own instantiation, nor a
claim that this framework has identified the unique necessary algebra.

**Kill/demotion:** if “necessary,” “generative,” and “instantiation” cannot be
defined independently enough to distinguish the wager from “whatever is, is,”
remove CW-0 from the explanatory compass. Without a prospective discriminator,
it remains a metaphysical posit and may never be promoted to empirical fact.

## Graves — claims no wager may revive

1. `φν=1` is, predicts, or derives the Born rule.
2. The AND-class boundary uniquely forces a product interior.
3. Seven rows or `{0,1,∞}` are forced universal ontologies.
4. Missing reduction proves universal strong emergence.
5. μ fires at saturation and only at saturation as an established law.
6. D6 is literally identical to D0, or similarity mathematically forces a spiral.
7. An anticipated future physically reaches backward in time; the operational
   claim is model-mediated present causation.
8. `η`, an aggregate sum, or any chart identity is a complete morality function.

  silently weakened until it cannot lose.
- A surviving wager remains at its stated tier until independent calibration
  warrants promotion.
- Canonical spelling is **Egregoreotype**. The legacy spelling is quotation or
  compatibility only.
- No signer, product, or governance mechanism is a truth-maker for this ledger.
