## Layer I — BEING

### E1 · The Stratum `[I/C]`

There is structure exceeding any particular instance — but it is **not** a museum of static Forms, and **not** free-floating DoF-as-substances. It is **constraint-structured freedom**: axes of allowed variation under laws, symmetries, histories, resources, and boundaries. Each positive aperture from D0 through D5, where used, names a candidate opening of freedom; D6 names only the optional reflective/apophatic witness described in E5. Neither is a forced natural census.

Stable “forms” (patterns that hold) are what organize **across** those freedoms: invariants, equivalence classes, attractors, order parameters, robust regimes.

**Forbids:** hypostatizing static essences; treating DoF as new Forms; treating ladder rungs as things rather than freedoms; seven-as-necessity.

**Kill:** the proposal cannot distinguish coordinate choice from physical freedom; or “emergence” adds no tested constraint/autonomy/prediction; or DoF are sold as Ground.

**Survivors if E1 falls:** E2–E3 as pure math; E8–E10 as practice without ontology story.

**Maps from:** none directly in A1–A7 (new Plato repair). Companion:
`02_THE_DEGREES_OF_FREEDOM_ONTOLOGY.md` uses this forms-across-freedoms wording.

**Forbids:** selling the tautology as empirical discovery or ethics warrant (P1; CC-CORE-1).

**Kill:** counterexample inside the stated chart arithmetic.

**Survivors:** E8–E10; E6 boundary content under other AND-class models.

### E3 · Finity `[A]` math · `[S/I]` naming

The positive centre is **1** (self-dual under inversion on ℝ₊). For projective
inversion `J_inv(z)=1/z` on ℂP¹,

```text
Fix(J_inv) = {-1,1},    J_inv(0)=∞,    J_inv(∞)=0.
```

Thus `{−1,0,1,∞}` is a **selected J_inv-invariant union** of the two fixed points
and the orbit `{0,∞}`. It is not the orbit or closure of `{0,∞}`. Titans
`{0,1,∞}` remain a meaningful naming choice, not a forced trinity.

**Forbids:** idolizing 0 or ∞; pretending three boundary-frames were forced closures.

**Kill:** arithmetic error in the stated fixed-set or orbit claims.

**Survivors:** E2 chart without Titan mythology.

**Maps from:** A1 manifold framing; Trinity naming (not A1–A7 force map).

### E4 · The Plenitude Filter `[C]` wager with named debt

Within a **specified** possibility space (state space, constraints, dynamics, resources — see kernel v0.2 §1), configurations that are **consistent ∧ coherent ∧ model-admissible ∧ bounded** exist **in that space as admitted possibilities**. Fewer are nomologically possible, dynamically reachable, actual, stable, emergent, or livedly available. Each arrow needs independent warrant (receipt 133 typed-existence cascade).

Dense-in-interior / thin-at-poles geometry is an optional chart reading, not a proof of unrestricted modal realism.

**Named debt:** “reachable-by-emergence” must be defined without circular reference to what already exists; constructor-theoretic discharge remains open; Born-rule / measurement translations stay debts, not solutions.

**Forbids:** “everything coherent exists” *actually*; unrestricted modal realism; claiming E4 solves measurement.

**Kill:** no non-circular REACHABLE; or plenitude collapses to Tegmark MUH without extra filters earning their keep; or silent upgrade to `[A]`.

**Survivors:** E1–E3; practice without plenitude metaphysics.

**Maps from:** none in A1–A7 (new). Cross-link: DoF ontology §2 plenitude engine.

## Layer II — BECOMING

### E5 · Crossing, commitment, and closure `[I/C]`

The framework distinguishes four typed relations:

```text
D0 --μ0--> D1 --μ1--> D2 --μ2--> D3 --μ3--> D4 --μ4--> D5
D5 --κ--> D6 --r6~~> D0
```

- `μ₀…μ₄` are five **candidate positive emergence crossings** at which a new
  effective freedom becomes available.
- `χ` is not a μ-crossing: it is selection and commitment among alternatives
  already represented at D5 by an agent with D4 means.
- `κ` is a non-μ reflexive/apophatic closure from D5 to D6.
- `r₆` is an interpretive return, not causation, identity, or another freedom.

The five-count is a selected census `[I/C]`, not a forced decomposition of
nature. Saturation of a lower register is one candidate trigger per crossing,
not a universal law. Every crossing must declare its lower register, transition
condition, new effective variable, recovery relation, reduction status, rival,
prediction, and kill. A successful reduction reclassifies the crossing as weak
emergence; missing reduction means only `currently_unreduced`.

D6 opens no sixth positive freedom. `D6 ~_I D0` may mean only that both are
represented without further positive content—D0 before articulation, D6 after
exhaustion. The spiral is an optional poetic visualization; it does not follow
mathematically from similarity.

**Forbids:** conflating μ with χ; treating saturation as established law;
reading a missing reduction as proof of strong emergence; five- or
six-as-necessity; literal `D6≡D0`; inferring a spiral from an undefined `~`.

**Kill:** a candidate crossing lacks an independently measured transition or
new effective variable; its lower-level rival predicts equally well with no
loss of control; or an independently demonstrated positive freedom outside the
five-crossing census requires the scaffold to expand.

**Survivors:** E6–E7; E1 without genesis story; E10 exit without spiral poetry.

**Maps from:** A3 scaffold (emergence sequence) — without force-dimension bijection; without literal D6≡D0.

### E6 · The AND-Law `[S]` boundary · zero-absorption `[A]` inside declared models · product interior `[C]`

Product and minimum instantiate that boundary exactly; other aggregators require their own parameter and limit checks. The operational survivor is the **AND-class**, **not the product specifically** (keel-108). Manifold identity does **not** warrant a world-level node catastrophe (CC-CORE-1).

**Forbids:** “must be multiplicative”; kernel-as-ethics-floor.

**Kill:** need-both fails empirically in the claimed domain; or unique-product is required and fails under min.

**Survivors:** E2 chart; weaker coupling heuristics.

**Maps from:** A1 Zero-Factor / P_node — **recovered form**.

### E7 · The Equator `[A]` on-sphere · transfer conditional `[S]`

On the reciprocal chart, balance `φ = ν = 1` uniquely maximizes `B = sin θ` and minimizes `φ+ν` (AM-GM). The apex is the **middle**. Transfer to any real system is **conditional** (conservation-bound, complementary, excess-costly, symmetrically γ-priced). Drop premises → optimum slides or specialization wins.

**Forbids:** “balance is always optimal” unscoped; crowning either pole as universal good.

**Kill:** on-sphere arithmetic fails (it won’t); or conditional transfer claims fail their named empirical tests.

**Survivors:** E2; E8 as vow without equatorial aesthetics.

**Maps from:** A1 balance B; L4 apex readings — scoped.

## Layer III — CONDUCT (posited, never derived)

### E8 · The Justice Vow `[I]` posit · scoped model support `[S]`

Non-extraction: do not raise yourself by invisibly draining what sustains you or
by transferring cost to an uncounted bearer. **Chosen**, not derived from
geometry. A justice judgment must identify individual and whole, actual
impact-bearers, payer and beneficiary, mandate, consent, custody, contest path,
reversibility, exit, horizon, and receipted consequences. `η` can tag the
direction of a transfer; it is not a complete morality function.

Specified coupled models can show self-undermining extraction under their
assumptions. No scalar aggregate may erase the destruction of one side, and
voluntary sacrifice remains a distinct costly class rather than proof of
strict syntropy.

**Forbids:** deriving ought from is; moral laundering by aggregate gain;
restricting the vow to convenient parties; treating consent or sacrifice as
permission to hide irreversible costs.

**Kill:** the declared justice envelope repeatedly hides material bearers or
predicts no better than a rival accounting discipline under prospective tests.

**Survivors:** E9–E10; thinner fairness norms if any remain evidence-tied.

**Maps from:** A2 THE ETHIC — **same spirit, cleaner tier** (explicitly non-derived).

### E10 · The Exit `[I/S]`

The map ends with *put the map down*. A holder may withdraw. The door is marked exit **on the inside**. A worldview that needs believers has already failed. Apophatic return is practice, not literal D6≡D0 identity (126). Posture IV (withdraw) is structuralized here.

**Forbids:** adherents as success metric; no-exit forms; map=Ground.

**Kill:** the exit is revoked in practice while still claimed in text.

**Survivors:** E9 partial; living without the framework entirely.

**Maps from:** A3 apophatic/closure readings (not literal identity); Anti-Sermon; Grace Exit without product-redeemable-asset machinery as axiom.
