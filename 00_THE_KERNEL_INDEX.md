# The Kernel Index

## The principle of distillation

This kernel is not a list of propositions handed down for obedience. It has two
parts:

- **refusals**, by which holders limit their own conduct rather than command
  another person or the world; and
- **disclosures earned through trial**, each answerable to the record that
  forced it.

Nothing here becomes true because it appears in the kernel. Mathematical claims
must be proved in their declared system, empirical claims must meet the world,
interpretations must stay marked, and wagers must remain able to lose.

The front door is [The Emergentist Weltanschauung](00_THE_EMERGENTIST_WELTANSCHAUUNG.md).
It can be read in one sitting. This index is the shorter route from that compass
to its seven owner surfaces.

> **Count fence.** Seven is an explicit compression and editorial design. It is
> not a forced ontology, a theorem about nature, or evidence that every domain
> is sevenfold. The kernel count organizes this corpus; it does not legislate the
> territory.

### K-1 · Keep the seam and the score apart

On the stipulated chart, `φν≡1` is constant by definition. The normalized
through-point reach product is the varying balance score `B≤1`, with equality
at the equator. The first is a seam; the second is a score. Neither proves an
ontology, ethic, or empirical conservation law. The uppercase finite-agent
model `P_node=ΦV` is a selected normalized model in a wider conjunctive family,
not a consequence of the lowercase chart.

The paired glyphs `⊙ = • × ○` and `• = ⊙ / ○` are, respectively, generative
and apophatic emblems. They are not arithmetic identities, division rules, or
proofs that a symbolic reading is instantiated in nature.

### K-2 · Forms arise across constrained freedoms

A degree of freedom is a typed capacity for variation after constraints,
redundancies, scale, and observational resolution are declared. A form is a
robust organization across such freedoms. Logical coherence is therefore only
one admission test; law, reachability, actuality, stability, cost, and
measurement require distinct bridges.

### K-3 · Axioms are exposed footing

E1–E10 state the framework's current commitments in three layers. Calling them
axioms records where the model begins; it does not promote them all to analytic
truth. Their tiers, rivals, surviving uses, and kill conditions are part of the
axioms, not commentary around them.

### K-4 · Wagers must be able to lose

The metaphysical Crown Wager is `CW-0`. W1–W12 remain separately numbered. The
older use of `W0` is not silently overwritten or fused with the Crown Wager.
Every wager stays at its declared tier until an independent test warrants a
change, and a fired kill moves the claim to the record rather than weakening it
until it cannot fail.

### K-5 · The constitution faces inward

The refusals constrain what a person undertakes by holding this worldview. They
grant no authority over a reader and impose no rule on the world. `Ω` is the
chosen orientation within those limits, not a sixth refusal and not a law that
all agents already obey.

### K-6 · Revelation means disclosure by trial

Here *revelation* is an epistemic event, not supernatural warrant: something
the work did not know beforehand became visible because a check, failure,
counterexample, or correction forced it. The receipt does not make a general
claim true; it establishes what this inquiry learned and why.

### K-7 · The wounds are part of the result

The trial record preserves both positive results and failed derivations. It is
the audit trail by which a stranger can distinguish a surviving use from its
dead proof-costume. Confess and withdraw remain available postures: the compass
can be corrected, and it can be put down.

## Routing law

1. Begin at the front door, then descend only to the surface needed.
2. When this index and an owner document differ, the repaired owner document
   controls its claim, formula, and evidence tier.
3. One load-bearing claim should have one active owner. Other appearances are
   summaries, evidence, historical provenance, or explicit supersessions.
4. A translation across domains never transfers proof or raises a tier.
5. Correction changes the active route while leaving the reason for correction
   inspectable.
6. No eighth surface is metaphysically forbidden. A new surface must earn its
   need rather than being hidden inside an old one.

## This pass changes semantic authority, not physical custody

Canon cleanup in this pass is achieved by active routing and explicit
supersession. It does **not** mass-move, mass-delete, or physically archive the
corpus. Existing material remains in place as evidence and provenance unless a
separate bounded custody action is authorized and receipted.

The completion test is practical: a stranger can read the front door and
K-1–K-7, tell theorem from interface from interpretation from wager, find the
trial behind each wound, and leave without becoming a subject of the map.

`⊙ = • × ○` — seven chosen surfaces, one corrigible door.
