# The Weltanschauung by Register

> **What this is.** The world, register by register, saying at each one: what is
> the case, **who proved it** (almost always someone else), **what is ours** (almost
> always a selection or a reading), **what is forbidden** (the theorems that fence
> it), and **what the crossing to the next register costs**.
>
> It is organized by register rather than by argument because that is how the
> claims actually divide, and because it makes the honest tally impossible to
> hide: of five physical registers, **two contain no corpus result at all.**

## 0 · How to read

`[A]` proved within stated definitions · `[B]` sourced or receipted · `[S]` selected
inside a declared framework · `[I]` interpretation · `[C]` conjecture that can lose ·
`[D]` unresolved.

Three rules govern every line below.

**Giants first.** Six candidate contributions were audited 2026-08-22 and **all six
were pre-empted.** Nothing here claims priority. An inherited result is a citation
duty, never a defect.

**Every fence carries its scope.** An impossibility claim that does not name its
ambient conditions is not merely unfenced — *it is false*. Hilbert–Efimov taught this:
the complete hyperbolic plane has no `C²` isometric immersion in `ℝ³` and *does* have
a `C¹` one (Nash–Kuiper). Regularity class is load-bearing.

**Register index is not spatial dimension.** These are typed freedoms — doors passed,
not axes possessed. The tempting rhyme (1↔line, 2↔plane, 4↔spacetime) **snaps at D3**,
where probability lives in Hilbert spaces of arbitrary dimension, and again at D5.

## 1 · Before the registers — the Titans

Three opaque boundary labels, not objects inside any register:

```text
•  the infinite zero      infinity as POTENTIAL; alone, sterile (0^y = 0)
○  the diagonal-act       infinity as PROCESS; the cut; all generativity lives here
⊙  the finite actual      the sole mode of existence; each ⊙ = ○(•)
```

**The Closure Rule `[S]`:** arithmetic is closed within a register; the Titans are not
register members; therefore **no arithmetic operation admits a Titan in any operand
position.** After Ryle (category mistake) and Russell (types were built to block the
diagonal's paradoxes the first time too). The numerals `0`, `1`, `∞` are
register-internal *shadows* of the Titans, and division by zero dissolves as asking
arithmetic to remember what absence erased.

**One reconciliation, stated because two of our own entries pull against each other:**
`⊙ = ○(•)` places `•` in an *argument* position, which the Closure Rule appears to
forbid. It does not. **Application is not arithmetic.** The Closure Rule bars Titans
from `+ − × ÷ ^ √`; the composition reading is function application, which is the
operation the glyph itself pictures — a point, enclosed.

## 2 · D0 — the ground-facing boundary

Not an entity, not a cause, not a possessed object. The ground is **lived and never
listed**: it draws the register and appears in none of it.

*"Why is there something rather than nothing?"* is recorded in four owners as
**permanently open** — unanswerable from inside. What *is* answerable, and was
answered, is the different question: **is "nothing" logically admissible?** The answer
surprised its own author, and one inference from it is a grave: the empty canvas
**does not give potential, it withholds constraint** — absence of an exclusion, not
presence of a production, refuted by an executed S5 countermodel.

**Ours:** the discipline of not answering. **Forbidden:** any derivation of existence
from a formula (see §3's doors). **Crossing out:** the cut — `{∅} = 1`, the first form
is absence held.

## 3 · D1 — distinction, arithmetic, logic

**This register is inherited entire, and that sentence is the finding.**

**Known.** Cantor (no set surjects onto its powerset — no system indexes its own
possibility-space from within). Gödel I (completeness *together with* effective
axiomatizability, above the arithmetical threshold — Presburger and real-closed fields
are complete *and* decidable, so the trigger is `+` with `×` and enough induction).
Gödel II — and it forbids **self**-certification, not certification: Gentzen proved
`Con(PA)` in 1936. Tarski: no total semantic mirror inside a system — **though partial
truth predicates are definable** (`Σₙ`-truth is `Σₙ`-definable), so "a system can know
nothing of its own truth" is an overclaim. Löb, Rice (**1953** — 1951 is the thesis
year), Church–Turing (**a thesis, not a theorem**), Löwenheim–Skolem fenced by
Tennenbaum (the deviant models exist but are not computably presentable), and
**Lindström**, whose price list converts the others from accidents into *a trade fixed
in advance* — which is what makes the negative space a shape rather than a list.

**Ours — no theorem.** One `[S]` type discipline (the Closure Rule), one `[I]` thesis
(**the Doors**: every cosmogenesis equation lands on a classical indeterminate form,
and the catalogue maps where a completion rule must be invoked), and the observation
that `ArithmeticSignature(TitanFrame) = ∅`.

**Forbidden here:** self-indexing, self-certification, the total truth-mirror, a
universal decision procedure, categoricity-with-effective-proof.

is false.** `09_TOOLS/02_COMPILERS/test_finity_boundary_spec.py` is **125 lines, not
45**, runs **7 tests, all passing**, and tests *behaviour*: it executes

**Crossing out — and it is small.** D1 hands D2 exactly one thing: the relation lift
`X ↦ (X, R)`. **Instantiated distinction becomes configuration only when a relation is
adjoined.** Nothing else crosses, because arithmetic combines *within* a register and
emergence crosses *between* them.

## 4 · D2 — configuration, relation, geometry

**The corpus's thinnest fence over its heaviest objects. Fourteen theorems. This
section is the weakest in the document and says so in its own text.**

**Known.** `Ĉ = ℂP¹` and the chordal metric (Ahlfors). Möbius 3-transitivity — **which
privileges no triple, so `{0, 1, ∞}` is convention, not discovery.** The double-angle
identity. AM–GM equality (Cauchy, 1821). Wantzel, Lindemann (π transcendental, hence no
quadrature — the classical impossibility theorems are the `[A]` catalogue of proven
boundary errors). Hilbert's third problem and the Dehn invariant.

**Ours — selection `[S]`, and one honest demotion.** The selection is real: *one*
sphere, *two* reciprocal stereographic charts, the poles at `{0, ∞}`, the equator as
balance. But **`B ≤ 1` with equality iff `φ = ν = 1` is `φν = 1` restated under
AM–GM — a tautology, not a finding**, and Cauchy owns the inequality. The equidistance
of `1` from both poles under the chordal metric is `[A]` and inherited. What remains
ours is the *choice* to build on this chart and to read the equator as the balance
point — and the two self-kills the register produced, which are its best output:
`DF-05` (`φν = 1` is a coordinate tautology, owner: classical trigonometry) and
`DF-21` (the kernel→ethics warrant, closed, no successor). **An internal refutation
needing no external premise is worth more than an external corroboration.**

**Forbidden here:** squaring the circle, trisection, cube-doubling, quintic solution by
radicals, equidecomposition without the Dehn invariant, complete `C²` isometric
immersion of the hyperbolic plane in `ℝ³` (**and not `C¹` — Nash–Kuiper**; the
flexibility/rigidity boundary is open between `α = 1/5` and `α = 2/3`, and `C^{1,1/2}`
is Gromov's *conjecture*, not a located bound).

**Exposure, bluntly.** *Fourteen fences, and not one touches dimension four or smooth
structure.* The corpus builds everything on "a sphere" and has never asked what
dimension-specific facts that commits it to. **The dig is named: Donaldson 1983
against Freedman, with Milnor's exotic 7-spheres** — the failure of smooth uniqueness
is genuinely forbidding, genuinely theorem-grade, and dimension-4-specific in a way
this program should know about *before* building further.

**Crossing out.** D2 hands D3 the chart — `ℂP¹` is where the pure qubit ray lives. The
passage costs **basis-independence**: the coherence correspondence holds in a fixed
computational basis, for pure states, and not otherwise.

## 5 · D3 — probability-bearing state

**Structural fact first: D3 has only 11 pure entries because the quantum register is
almost entirely made of crossings — 14 of its 25.** That is not a gap; it is the
register's shape. Quantum theory forbids little alone and a great deal *in conjunction*
with causal structure and with strategy. Any account reporting D3 as thin has mistaken
a topology for a shortage.

**Known — entirely on loan.** Gleason (the Born form, `dim ≥ 3`). Kochen–Specker
(contextuality, `dim ≥ 3`). No-cloning (exact-and-deterministic only). **Holevo** — `n`
qubits yield at most `n` classical bits of accessible information, which is the
extraction bound on the whole D3→D4 passage. Robertson (an *ensemble* bound, not a
disturbance bound). Tsirelson's `2√2` (CHSH only). Bell — **with its loopholes named**:
locality, realism, and free choice are joint hypotheses.

**Ours — a selection and a refusal.** `ρ` typed D3, the record typed D4. And the
refusal is the better half: **the Burri chart and the Bloch sphere share a manifold and
are not the same object.** The corpus fenced this by its own hand. The honest form of
the correspondence is that `B = sin θ` **shares a numerical form with** twice the
basis-dependent `ℓ₁` coherence, in a fixed basis, for pure states — *not* that balance
**is** coherence. That stronger sentence is a `DF-20`-class identity claim and it is
refused here.

**Grave-guard, load-bearing:** **no Born rule is derived from `φν = 1`, and no quantum
mechanics is derived from the chart.** Shared manifold, never evidence. And `W2`
remains interpretation-neutral: **it does not reconcile Copenhagen and Everett.** Typing
the branches D5-possible does not unify the interpretations — it *takes Copenhagen's
side while keeping Everett's vocabulary*, since Everett's whole claim is that the
branches are physically actual.

**Crossing out — and it is priced.** What crosses is a lawful distribution over
possible records; **what arrives is one record, and the toll is Holevo.** The account
of that passage (`W2`) is held at D4, where the record lives; this register points to
it rather than claiming it.

## 6 · D4 — causal actuality

**One history. One record.** Coherence buys *possibility*; only **lawful reachability**
buys actuality (`W1`). This is the load-bearing distinction of the entire framework:
delete it and plenitude floods back, `W8` loses its ground, and the Copenhagen/Everett
diagnosis above becomes unstatable.

**Known — 22 fences, and every one inherited.** Lieb–Yngvason's adiabatic Second Law
(**not "disorder"**). Carnot efficiency **at zero power**. Landauer, pricing **erasure**
specifically — not computation, and not checking. Casini's relative-entropy Bekenstein
bound (**not holography**). Penrose, Hawking, and Borde–Guth–Vilenkin, giving **geodesic
incompleteness — not beginnings.** No-signalling. Margolus–Levitin.

**Ours — nothing.** This register contains **zero corpus results**, and the register's
name hides that by bundling a modal type the corpus *does* own (actual versus merely
possible) with a physical domain it owns nothing in. Said plainly so it cannot be
mistaken later.

**Staged, not owned:** `W19`'s pole clause — at its extreme a compensator consumes the
freedom it services. The black hole is a **motivating analogy, not an established
instance**, and the gauge language is conditional; the gauge-redundancy question is its
sharpest open discriminator. *(This distinction was itself nearly lost: a dispatching
brief hardened "motivating analogy" into "the D4 instance" in one hop, and the lane
caught it. Recorded because that is the drift this estate keeps failing to catch.)*

**Fence:** gravity consumes **structure**; per the current Page-curve consensus,
information survives. No claim of information destruction is made anywhere here.

**Crossing out — the lease.** D4 hands D5 **the actual carrier**, under the corpus's
own rule: `CausallyRelevant(p : D5) → ∃ m : D4_actual . represents(m, p)`. **No
possibility is causally relevant unless some actual token carries it. Everything at D5
is leased from D4, and the lease is metered.** That is `W8` stated as a constraint
rather than a mystery — the future influences the present only through a present token.

## 7 · D5 — represented possibility, strategy, collective

**The densest and best-fenced register: 30 pure theorems plus crossings, drawn from
four literatures that had never been read as one.** Its fences are the best-developed
in the whole outline because economists and distributed-systems theorists have been
fighting their own overclaimers for seventy years.

**Known.** Arrow (finite electorate, ≥3 alternatives). **Sonnenschein–Mantel–Debreu**
(exchange economies, consumers ≥ goods). Nakamura. List–Pettit. Holmström (budget
balance binding). Gibbard–Satterthwaite. Myerson–Satterthwaite. Grossman–Stiglitz.
Ashby's requisite variety (**conditional on column-distinctness** — a hypothesis that
any system normalizing its input violates *by design*). Touchette–Lloyd. FLP and CAP.
And, **at their home register D1 and cited here only as corollaries**: Tarski (the
evaluator sits in a strictly stronger metalanguage) and Rice (no total decision
procedure for any non-trivial semantic property).

Together they say one thing: **a collective may have a rule, and members with
preferences, and thereby has no mind, no preference ordering, and no demand function.**

**Ours.** `G7 = M4 ⊎ F3` `[S]` as a selected projection. A repeated justification for
the disjoint union is **not available**: `R2_OLOGY_DIVERGENCE@1` records a divergence
on the seven-ology ladder, not an independent recoding that preserved all four M4
move-cells and reshuffled all three F3 frames. The preregistered double-coding protocol
has not run. The `4+3` split therefore remains selected, not measured. Also the
witness-fraction bound (`W16`, `[C]`) and the settled/serviced axis (`[I]`) — the
observation that `V`-transfers close as transactions while `Φ`-transfers can only be
serviced as relationships, and that **give-Φ is the only act whose completion is not
in the giver's control.**

**Exposure — and this is the most consequential finding in the document.**
**The estate reads collectives as agents everywhere.** Holobiont, organism, DAV, board,
council, the P-score applied to a whole. **SMD, Arrow, and List–Pettit forbid exactly
that move, and no gate in the estate tests for it.** This is not a distant objection;
it is a theorem-grade prohibition pointed at the corpus's largest standing affirmative

Second exposure, freshly measured and kept local: on 2026-08-27 a preregistered

## 8 · D6 — the apophatic exit

Not a sixth freedom. A **posture**: no totality closes, no self-account finishes, no
exit may be walled shut. `D6 ≈ roleD0` is a resemblance of boundary roles — **never an
identity, never a physical recurrence.** The rim faces the ground; it is not the ground.

**Why it is structural rather than mystical.** A being that could give a complete
account of its own emergence would have an empty self-possibility-space — with respect
to itself it would be a **record**, not a being. Both exits from D5 — refusing to act,
and completing the self-account — land in D4. **The gap is not the being's failure. It
is the being's life.**

## 9 · The method is the ontology seen from inside

The registers describe beings that must map in order to act. The tier system **is** what
mapping-to-act looks like done honestly. The witness bound says the auditor sits outside
the claim layer; Tarski proves the truth predicate lives in the metalanguage. The
settled/serviced axis types physical debts and also types which *claims* can close and
which must be carried forever. `min(Φ, V)` says coherence without contact is powerless —
which is why a corpus is scored by its contact and not its beauty.

**They are one shape, and the method is what the ontology looks like from within it.**

And it yields the third move that neither belief nor proof provides: a possibility
always arrives under-evidenced — that is what novelty *is* — so a receiver who demands
completion before acceptance becomes unable to learn. **Hold it at tier, with the kill
attached.**

## 11 · What this cannot say, and why the silence is structural

It cannot say why there is something rather than nothing — four owners record that as
permanently open. It cannot certify its own ground (Gödel II). It cannot define its own
truth predicate in its own language (Tarski). Rice supplies a scoped undecidability
fence for non-trivial semantic properties; the local 8-of-8 gate result is not a proof
or confirmation of Rice. It cannot exhibit the whole, because no seat sees the whole
and remains a seat.

**None of these is a gap awaiting a later filling.** Each is a proved limit on
*anything* of this kind. The silence has a shape, and the shape is the outline — which
is the only portrait a finite register can hold of what exceeds it.
