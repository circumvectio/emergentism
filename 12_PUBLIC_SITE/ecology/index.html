Emergentotype Carrier Map · selected instrument [S/I/C]

One pattern.
Changing carriers.

Emergence becomes testable when identity has to survive declared substitutions—and fail under a declared intervention.

01 · The carrier question

Replace the carriers. Does the pattern survive?

Name the pattern, actual carriers, admitted variation, intervention, rivals, and kill. If no change could distinguish persistence from convenient naming, the profile is decoration.

  1. 01Substitutechange actual carriers
  2. 02Predictdeclare what persists
  3. 03Interveneperturb the pattern
  4. 04Comparematch serious rivals
  5. 05Reviseretain · demote · kill

pattern at L ≠ one carrier at L

pattern at L ≠ all carriers without distinction

claimed identity at L = persistence under declared variation + intervention

Persistence names an intervention result, not an extra substance behind the carriers. This operational rule is selected [S], not a theorem of metaphysical identity or a proof of strong emergence.

02 · Six profiles · six native identity tests

The map joins layers without making them identical.

The ordering is an Emergentist selection [S/I]. Its generality remains [C]. It is not D0–D6, G7, GEN7, a trophic ladder, a person type, or a moral rank.

01 · biological inheritance

Genotype

A declared heritable sequence configuration at named loci or genome scope.

Actual carriers
DNA-bearing cells, organisms, sampled genomes
May vary
carrier identity, development, later mutation, mosaicism
Kill the collapse
A gene or genotype is not an organism, individual, or clone.
02 · developmental regulation

Epigenotype

A process-profile linking genotype to phenotype at a declared tissue, time, assay, and environment.

Actual carriers
developing cells, tissues, organisms, regulatory states
May vary
genetically similar carriers can follow different trajectories
Kill the collapse
Not a peptide list, static snapshot, or genome-free essence.
03 · observed expression

03 · An ordinary doorway

The word is not the pattern.

The token chair is one actual occurrence. It is not the chair-concept, one chair, the class of chairs, or a complete cultural pattern.

Many to many · [S]

Different traces can cue related distinctions.

Words, images, demonstrations, artifacts, rituals, interfaces, and habits may carry or reconstruct a pattern.

Context matters · [S/I]

The same token can cue different concepts.

Carrier, history, practice, model, corpus, objective, metric, and query context remain visible.

Implementation ≠ ontology

A vector database is one retrieval surface.

It can encode relations among traces. It does not contain a timeless essence of chair—or of meaning.

04 · Non-collapse

Different questions need different coordinates.

The prior ecology page’s strongest fence remains: correspondence does not collapse trophic flow, carrier persistence, human function, and reproduction into one human hierarchy.

Ecology · [A/B]

Trophic position

Where do matter and energy flow? Trophic position is neither dignity nor moral rank.

Carrier Map · [S/I/C]

Pattern persistence

What recurs across which carrier changes—and which intervention kills that identity?

Rosetta · [I/C]

Human function

Which complementary work function is operating? No row fixes birth, personhood, status, or worth.

Reproductive domain · [B/I]

Selection pressure

Where does mate choice or reproductive competition operate? It licenses no general social predation.

GEN7 ≠ G7. The six carrier profiles plus their boundary are not the seven Rosetta functions. Any crosswalk is optional [I/C], removable, and incapable of transferring proof or rank.

05 · The strategic edge · candidate [C]

When does a shared pattern behave like one represented actor?

Never by naming alone. The parent collective trace must pass first; only then may an actor-policy model face prospective prediction and intervention.

Parent gate · [S]

Five markers before subtype

  1. persistent trace
  2. carrier turnover
  3. intervention-sensitive selection reweighting
  4. recurrent objective-like bias
  5. visible substrate and bearer cost
Strategic test · [S/I/C]

Prospective value beyond rivals

Add an explicit represented actor, named attributors and traces, predicted policy, counterfactuals, intervention-sensitive uptake, cross-context recurrence or adaptation, and incremental value over incentives, commands, memory, common cause, demand, and observer story.

in actual carriersexpected response

A passed strategic test supports only a bounded predictive model. It grants no consciousness, phenomenal unity, personhood, independent existence, moral worth, Justice, legitimacy, consent, signing, authorization, or legal status. Kill the subtype when actor framing adds no prospective or intervention value over ordinary rivals.

06 · Realization and return

Bottom-up carriers. Top-down constraints—only through actual mechanisms.

The map supplies a test programme for emergence, not a proof that any transition is strongly emergent.

Realization

Carriers instantiate the pattern.

No genotype, phenotype, Memotype, or collective trace floats free of actual material, behavioral, institutional, or computational carriers.

Mediated return

Persistent patterns alter which carrier states recur.

Constraints return through actual traces, expectations, artifacts, incentives, practices, environments, and selection—not magic downward reach.

Strong-emergence test · [C]

Make the upper description earn novelty.

Require incremental prospective prediction or intervention beyond matched carrier-only and native-domain rivals. If it adds none, demote autonomy.
