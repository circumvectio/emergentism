Question Atlas · PQA-54 · public denominator

Fifty-four questions. None quietly counted as solved.

Emergentism does not end philosophy. It makes philosophical debt legible—and tests whether an exact type distinction clarifies, dissolves within a model, conditionally resolves, reframes, or leaves each question open.

Reading rule

Inventory is not evaluation.

Every question begins open.

Native recovery

State the problem before translating it.

The native terms, premises, established result, and strongest native rival must remain visible.

Typed effect

Clarification is not resolution.

Formal correction, type dissolution, conditional resolution, practical guidance, and refutation remain distinct.

Residual debt

What remains open stays on the page.

A renamed question, deleted premise, changed subject, or hidden residual earns no dissolution.

A bounded majority would require 28 of 54 qualifying results, at least three per domain, two independent native-domain reviews per result, no live kill, and incremental value over both native-frame and generic-decomposition controls. Even that would not mean “most philosophy.”

01 · MET

Metaphysics and ontology

Six selected question families. Selection is a benchmark construct, not a philosophical result.

  1. PQA54@0.1:MET:GROUND · ground · DEVELOPMENT

    Why is there something rather than nothing, and where may explanation lawfully terminate?

    Native target: Ground, sufficient reason and explanatory termination.

    Native anchor: Leibniz, Principles of Nature and Grace §7; Heidegger, Introduction to Metaphysics.

  2. PQA54@0.1:MET:ONE_MANY · one_and_many · DEVELOPMENT

    How can reality be one while containing genuine plurality?

    Native target: Unity, plurality and dependence without erasure.

    Native anchor: Plato, Parmenides; Aristotle, Metaphysics.

  3. PQA54@0.1:MET:UNIVERSALS · universals_particulars · VALIDATION

    What is the relation between repeatable properties and particular things?

    Native target: Realism, nominalism and trope theories of universals.

    Native anchor: Plato, Republic; Aristotle, Categories and Metaphysics.

  4. PQA54@0.1:MET:IDENTITY · identity_persistence · VALIDATION

    What makes a thing the same thing through change?

    Native target: Numerical identity, continuity, constitution and persistence.

    Native anchor: Locke, Essay Concerning Human Understanding II.27; Parfit, Reasons and Persons.

  5. PQA54@0.1:MET:MODALITY · modality_possible_worlds · HELD_OUT

    What makes possibility and necessity true?

    Native target: Modal truth, accessibility and possible-world semantics.

    Native anchor: Kripke, Naming and Necessity; Lewis, On the Plurality of Worlds.

  6. PQA54@0.1:MET:TIME · time_change · HELD_OUT

    Is time fundamental, emergent, tenseless or essentially becoming?

    Native target: A-series, B-series, passage and change.

    Native anchor: Aristotle, Physics IV; McTaggart, The Unreality of Time.

02 · EPI

Epistemology

Six selected question families. Selection is a benchmark construct, not a philosophical result.

  1. PQA54@0.1:EPI:EXTERNAL_WORLD · external_world · DEVELOPMENT

    What warrants belief in a world independent of present experience?

    Native target: External-world skepticism and perceptual warrant.

    Native anchor: Descartes, Meditations I; Moore, Proof of an External World.

  2. PQA54@0.1:EPI:INDUCTION · induction · DEVELOPMENT

    What warrants projecting observed regularities into unobserved cases?

    Native target: Hume's problem of induction and projectibility.

    Native anchor: Hume, Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding IV; Goodman, Fact, Fiction, and Forecast.

  3. PQA54@0.1:EPI:GETTIER · knowledge_gettier · VALIDATION

    Why can justified true belief still fail to be knowledge?

    Native target: Gettier luck and analyses of knowledge.

    Native anchor: Plato, Theaetetus; Gettier, Is Justified True Belief Knowledge?

  4. PQA54@0.1:EPI:APRIORI · a_priori · VALIDATION

    How can knowledge be warranted independently of particular sensory experience?

    Native target: A priori warrant, rational insight and empiricism.

    Native anchor: Kant, Critique of Pure Reason; BonJour, In Defense of Pure Reason.

  5. PQA54@0.1:EPI:TESTIMONY · testimony · HELD_OUT

    When does another person's word give knowledge rather than merely borrowed belief?

    Native target: Testimonial warrant, reductionism and social dependence.

    Native anchor: Coady, Testimony; Hume, Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding X.

  6. PQA54@0.1:EPI:DISAGREEMENT · disagreement_underdetermination · HELD_OUT

    How should rational peers revise when evidence underdetermines a shared conclusion?

    Native target: Peer disagreement, conciliation and steadfastness.

    Native anchor: Feldman and Warfield, Disagreement; Christensen, Epistemology of Disagreement.

03 · LOG

Logic and language

Six selected question families. Selection is a benchmark construct, not a philosophical result.

  1. PQA54@0.1:LOG:LIAR · liar_self_reference · DEVELOPMENT

    How can a truth-bearing sentence coherently say of itself that it is false?

    Native target: Semantic self-reference, truth predicates and hierarchy.

    Native anchor: Tarski, The Concept of Truth in Formalized Languages; Kripke, Outline of a Theory of Truth.

  2. PQA54@0.1:LOG:RUSSELL · russell_set_formation · DEVELOPMENT

    Which comprehension principle fails in Russell's paradox, and what survives?

    Native target: Unrestricted comprehension and axiomatic set formation.

    Native anchor: Russell, Principles of Mathematics; Zermelo, Investigations in the Foundations of Set Theory I.

  3. PQA54@0.1:LOG:GODEL · incompleteness · VALIDATION

    What do incompleteness theorems show—and not show—about formal proof and truth?

    Native target: Formal consistency, completeness and arithmetical truth.

    Native anchor: Gödel, On Formally Undecidable Propositions (1931).

  4. PQA54@0.1:LOG:SORITES · vagueness_sorites · VALIDATION

    How can tiny differences accumulate into an apparently impossible boundary change?

    Native target: Vagueness, tolerance principles and borderline cases.

    Native anchor: Eubulides' sorites tradition; Williamson, Vagueness.

  5. PQA54@0.1:LOG:REFERENCE · reference_meaning · HELD_OUT

    How do words refer to things and carry meaning across speakers and contexts?

    Native target: Sense, reference, causal naming and context.

    Native anchor: Frege, On Sense and Reference; Kripke, Naming and Necessity.

  6. PQA54@0.1:LOG:RULE_FOLLOWING · rule_following_truth · HELD_OUT

    What makes one continuation an instance of following a rule rather than another?

    Native target: Normativity of meaning and rule-following skepticism.

    Native anchor: Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations; Kripke, Wittgenstein on Rules and Private Language.

05 · SCI

Science and causation

Six selected question families. Selection is a benchmark construct, not a philosophical result.

  1. PQA54@0.1:SCI:CAUSATION · causation · DEVELOPMENT

    What distinguishes causation from correlation, regularity or description?

    Native target: Counterfactual, interventionist, regularity and process accounts.

    Native anchor: Hume, Enquiry VII; Pearl, Causality.

  2. PQA54@0.1:SCI:LAWS · laws_explanation · DEVELOPMENT

    What makes a scientific generalization a law or explanation?

    Native target: Laws, models, explanation and necessity.

    Native anchor: Hempel and Oppenheim, Studies in the Logic of Explanation; van Fraassen, The Scientific Image.

  3. PQA54@0.1:SCI:EMERGENCE · reduction_emergence · VALIDATION

    Native anchor: Nagel, The Structure of Science; Anderson, More Is Different.

  4. PQA54@0.1:SCI:MEASUREMENT · quantum_measurement · VALIDATION

    How do quantum probabilities relate to definite measurement records?

    Native target: Measurement, decoherence, outcomes and interpretation.

    Native anchor: von Neumann, Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics; Bell, Against Measurement.

  5. PQA54@0.1:SCI:BELL · bell_locality_realism · HELD_OUT

    Native target: Bell inequalities, locality and hidden-variable assumptions.

  6. PQA54@0.1:SCI:PROBABILITY · probability_indeterminacy · HELD_OUT

    Native target: Interpretations of probability and objective chance.

    Native anchor: Kolmogorov, Foundations of the Theory of Probability; de Finetti, Theory of Probability.
