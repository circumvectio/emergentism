01 · Promotion

Signal → Data → Information → Knowledge → Judgment → Wisdom

Each crossing creates a new addressable object or receipt. A label cannot perform the crossing.

  1. SignalA sensed, voiced, clicked, or streamed event before an admitted semantic structure.
  2. DataAddressable bytes with content identity, size, time, source boundary, and retention state
  3. InformationData plus an explicit schema, transformation, interpretation boundary, and provenance trail.
  4. KnowledgeA typed proposition linked to admitted Information, explicit warrant, confidence, rivals, and defeaters
  5. JudgmentKnowledge joined to a scoped decision by a named bearer, with authority state, reversibility, cost, and expected consequence visible.
  6. WisdomA reusable, versioned policy distilled from Judgment with scope, outcome evidence, rivals, kills, correction lineage, and authority boundary.
