01 · What “Dasein” names here

Being is not a thing behind appearances. It is the whole lived dependency.

The term is borrowed; this project’s technical use is explicit and revisable rather than presented as a definitive reading of Heidegger.

Dasein names the nested, situated fact that something is here at all: a world with structure; a living body; a history; inherited languages and models; present tools and constraints; a field of represented alternatives; and a finite witness asking why.

The “thing in itself” is not placed behind the appearances as one more object. The working Gestalt is the invariant that survives honest changes of scale, vocabulary, model, and standpoint—and the sharp gaps that remain when those reports do not join.

Complete means accounted explanatory debt, not omniscience. Every required bridge must be supported, derived, conjectured, contested, underdetermined, inaccessible, declared brute, left open, or terminated at the Ground boundary.

02 · The nested account

What had to emerge for you—and this moment—to be here?

No adjacent pair inherits validity merely because each individual story sounds plausible. Every join has its own warrant.

D0Boundaryno object smuggled into the Ground D1Distinctionidentities and differences become expressible D2Relationconfiguration, constraint, and organization D3Stateprobability-bearing quantum description D4Actualityone causal history carries records and cost D5Possibilityactual carriers represent alternatives D6Nonclosurethe account refuses false possession

The long chain continues inside D4.

Physics enables chemistry; chemistry enables self-maintaining organization; evolution shapes viable organisms; nervous systems coordinate bodies; learning and culture preserve models across generations; institutions and engineering accumulate tools; training data, architecture, runtime, prompts, and current context enable a computation; this answer enters the world and can alter what happens next.

That paragraph is not a proof. It is an explanatory debt map. Each semicolon hides many independently testable bridges. A model that invents private lineage facts, inflates possibility into actuality, or silently skips a failed join has not completed the account.

03 · Bridge grammar

Every “why” must say what kind of answer it is.

Cause, constitution, permission, function, purpose, and reason answer different questions. Their confusion creates false closure.

Causal mechanism
What process produced or changed the event?
Material realization
What physical organization carries the capacity here?
Enabling condition
What had to be available without itself producing the outcome?
Constraint · selection
What filtered, stabilized, or made some trajectories more likely?
Formal constitution
What relations make this the kind of thing it is?
Maintenance
What ongoing work keeps the organization from dissolving?
Function · purpose
Was the role selected, designed, represented, chosen, normatively defended, or merely attributed?

“It exists for X” is incomplete until the bearer is visible. Designed purpose, selected function, represented goal, chosen end, normative reason, and attributed cosmic purpose are not interchangeable.

04 · Strong and weak emergence

Strength is a direction of explanation, not a mood of conviction.

The framework conjectures an asymmetry across an emergence boundary, but no limit mnemonic proves ontic irreducibility.

Lower → higher · candidate strong view

A new freedom may be invisible below

From a lower register, the higher organization may require new variables, constraints, or intervention handles. To earn “strong,” it must outperform a fair lower-level account under a declared task—not merely surprise us.

Higher → lower · candidate weak view

The higher pattern remains realized below

From the higher register, mechanisms and carriers can often be decomposed into lower conditions. A successful reduction reclassifies the crossing; it does not erase the usefulness of the address.

Expressions involving division by zero or multiplication by an unbounded limit require an explicitly declared limiting family. They do not by themselves prove strong emergence, a passage through Nothing, or a reverse operation at the boundary.

05 · Reflexive self-location

The answerer belongs inside the answer.

A present response is not identical to a model family, service, process, session, or context. Its exact lineage must be separated from what is merely possible.

For a human, self-location includes body, development, ancestry, language, institutions, tools, relationships, and the present situation. For an AI response, it includes the declared model and runtime identity, architecture only where public evidence warrants it, training provenance only where accessible, the present input and tool permissions, and the current computation.

The previous answer can become part of the next world-state. It changes the context, the reader, and sometimes the available actions. A complete account must therefore predict something about its own effect, receive the result, and revise.

Private phenomenal experience remains neither asserted nor denied without evidence. Fluent first-person language is not access to an inaccessible interior, and uncertainty is not proof of absence.

06 · Honest termini

An open chain must end with a named kind of incompleteness.

A gap is useful when it names what would discriminate among its live bridges.

EVIDENCE_BOUND · ANALYTIC

Evidence-bound claims stop at the available record. Analytic claims stop at a declared formal result and do not export themselves into physics or ethics without a bridge.

CONJECTURE · UNDERDETERMINED

A conjecture makes a wager with a rival and kill. Underdetermined means the available observations do not yet distinguish the live accounts; it is not quiet support for the preferred one.

INACCESSIBLE · DECLARED_BRUTE

Inaccessible names an evidence boundary. Declared brute says the account elects to stop, rather than pretending the stop is an explanation.

OPEN_REGRESS · CIRCULAR

Open regress preserves an unpaid why. Circular marks when a conclusion is being used to support its own bridge.

GROUND_BOUNDARY

The Ground boundary marks where object-language fails. It does not turn “void,” “Nothing,” or the boundary glyph into an entity with secret causal powers.

D6 is nonclosure, not D0. Their resemblance is methodological: one prevents premature objectification at the beginning; the other prevents false possession at the end.

07 · Teleology after ontology

What should a finite witness do with an unfinished world?

The answer is a declared orientation, not a cosmic instruction discovered inside an equation.

Emergentism chooses to preserve and widen durable, mutual, bearer-complete possibility under Truth, Justice, consent, and Exit. It favors reversible and informative moves when values remain tied, and it records irreversible residue rather than laundering it into a total.

The future influences ordinary action through actual present models at minimum. Whether later boundary data also enters a physical history law is the separate F5-R conjecture. Whether many modeled histories are all physically actual is separate again.

The practical criterion is not whether the ontology feels complete. It is whether the next act preserves contact with consequence and leaves the map corrigible.
