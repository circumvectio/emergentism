Who does what

Connection is not authority.

MODEL

Investigator

Inspect, compare, attack, and prepare a candidate. Fluency, popularity, and agreement remain raw intake.

SENSOR / DATA SYSTEM

Observation carrier

Carry a bounded observation with calibration and custody. More signals are not automatically more evidence.

HUMAN / INSTITUTION

Principal

Supply the real mandate, resources, contest path, domain competence, and authorization appropriate to a test.

EMERGENTISM

Protocol

Type, version, expose rivals and kills, preserve revision, and keep Exit visible. Never own the territory.
