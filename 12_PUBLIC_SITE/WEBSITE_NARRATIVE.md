# Public narrative

**Subject:** Emergentism as a testable lens and compass.

**Audience:** serious general readers who want the idea before the machinery.

**Single job:** let a stranger encounter the strongest proposals, try the Soul
Loop on a real decision, and inspect what survived or failed.

## Sequence

1. **Wonder:** “Reality has a shape. We drew it.” The hero is an invitation,
   not a validation claim.
2. **Discoveries:** ten concise propositions, each with its evidence tier and
   honest boundary.
3. **Practice:** use model, means, commitment, outcome, and return on one
   decision without joining or believing anything.
4. **Record:** show corrections, retractions, and current external-calibration
   limits as the warranty.
5. **Exit:** the reader keeps the useful part and may put the map down.

The signature element is the glyph and its gold seam: `⊙ = • × ○` is an emblem
of relation, never field arithmetic. Black is the unsurveyed territory, bone is
the readable map, and gold marks repaired boundaries rather than perfection.

No public page may derive science, ethics, ontology, or authority from an
external application. No headline may convert correspondence into proof.
