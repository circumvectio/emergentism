# VMOSK-A — Emergentism worldview front door

This packet governs the hierarchy, language, and release tests of
`12_PUBLIC_SITE/`. It cannot create doctrine, increase a claim's evidence tier,
or turn internal coherence, AI review, authorship, popularity, or visual force
into world evidence.

The positioning decision is:

> **Emergentism, made usable through Finity.**

Emergentism is the identity. Finity is the first practice. The research record
is the trust mechanism. The Rosetta and formal notation are progressive depth.

## V — Vision

A thoughtful stranger facing a consequential choice encounters a worldview
that is clear enough to use and modest enough to correct. Within five seconds,
the visitor can identify:

1. the category: a fallibilist worldview and practical compass;
2. the problem: reality exceeds every map, but finite beings must still act;
3. the practice: choose a finite, observable, revisable boundary;
4. the next action: frame one decision; and
5. the freedom: no belief is required and Exit remains open.

## M — Mission

Project the current corpus as one coherent public journey:

`worldview → practice → commitment → observed consequence → research → revision`

The site should help a visitor distinguish what is happening, what may happen,
what can actually be done, who bears the consequence, and what the consequence
requires us to revise. It should make ambitious questions inviting without
presenting evidence-open mathematics, ontology, ethics, or science integration
as completed results.

## O — Objectives

| Objective | Observable release condition |
|---|---|
| O1 · Category before mechanism | The hero names Emergentism as a worldview before introducing Finity. |
| O2 · Useful first contact | “Frame one decision” reaches the Finity Card and a local commitment/outcome receipt builder. |
| O3 · One public grammar | Primary routes read `Practice · Worldview · Research · Library · Participate · Exit` in that order. |
| O4 · World contact visible | The homepage shows the receipt loop: fallible map, possible futures, Finity, actual move, consequence, revision. |
| O5 · Plain before formal | First contact uses ordinary language; D4/D5, Phi, V, mu, Titans, and Rosetta symbolism remain optional depth. |
| O6 · Evidence beside ambition | Exact-within-model, interpretation, conjecture, internal audit, and independent review owed remain visibly distinct. |
| O7 · Source-bound projection | Public Finity practice and worldview copy name current source owners and claim cards; projection never becomes semantic owner. |
| O8 · Voluntary relation | No adherence, rank, conversion, or suppression metric appears; refusal and Exit stay easy to find. |
| O9 · Verified release | Source, generation, semantic parity, accessibility, responsive rendering, artifact, promotion, DNS, and served bytes receive separate results. |

## S — Strategies

1. **Identity first, utility immediately.** Lead with Emergentism, then make
   Finity the primary action rather than the whole category.
2. **One human tension.** Build the first screen around incomplete maps and the
   necessity of action, not around proprietary vocabulary.
3. **Show the loop.** Use one signature receipt-loop visual instead of making a
   symbolic triad carry the first-contact explanation.
4. **Ask five durable questions.** Organize the worldview around actuality,
   knowledge, action, value, and direction; route each answer to its source.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Plain language precedes dimensional notation;
   practice precedes formal specification; current work precedes genealogy.
6. **Compress repetition.** Join mechanism with method, and discoveries with
   evidence and accountability, so the homepage advances rather than restarts.
7. **Trust through correction.** Put evidence grades, rivals, failures,
   corrections, and independent validation owed beside the research invitation.
8. **Keep depth navigable.** Use stable doors across primary pages; keep About
   in accountability footers and specialized routes inside their owning hub.

## K — KPIs and kill criteria

These measure comprehension and honest use, never allegiance.

| Measure | Release target | Kill or revise when |
|---|---|---|
| Five-second category test | Four of five fresh readers say “worldview” or “practical compass,” not “math theory” or “productivity app.” | Fewer than four identify the category. |
| Problem recognition | Four of five can restate “the map is incomplete, yet action is required.” | Visitors instead report an infinity puzzle or abstract cosmology. |
| Practice recognition | Four of five identify Finity as a bounded, observable, revisable practice. | Finity is read as a proved algebra, universal law, or synonym for finitude. |
| Action visibility | Primary action begins within the first mobile viewport or immediately after one short scroll at 390 px. | The first actionable control is buried by branding or notation. |
| Route consistency | The six labels and destinations match on every primary page and generated current reader. | A primary page reintroduces a competing global vocabulary. |
| Claim hygiene | Zero scoped barred-claim hits and no public claim stronger than its source/card. | A “complete ontology,” universal paradox resolution, derived ethic, or science-unification conclusion appears. |
| Receipt usefulness | Field reports can record a decision, boundary, consequence, and revision; comparison with ordinary decision practices remains possible. | The Card adds ceremony without observable improvement, or delays action without reducing harm. |
| Release truth | Local, committed, pushed, immutable, promoted, DNS, and served-hash states are reported separately. | Any earlier state is described as deployment or live proof. |

## Front-door structure

### Global route grammar

`Practice · Worldview · Research · Library · Participate · Exit`

- **Practice** owns the Finity Card and worked receipts.
- **Worldview** owns the plain account and optional Rosetta depth.
- **Research** owns claims, open questions, tests, rivals, and corrections.
- **Library** owns the current reader and clearly labelled historical custody.
- **Participate** owns receipt, contradiction, and evidence contributions.
- **Exit** remains an equal door, not a buried legal qualification.
- **About** remains an accountability link in footers and research context.

### Homepage sequence

1. **Hero — the category and tension.** Emergentism; incomplete maps; necessary
   action; primary Practice CTA; secondary Worldview CTA; visible refusal line.
2. **Practice — frame one decision.** Finity Card plus one worked consequence
   receipt. Formal notation is optional, not required to use the practice.
3. **Worldview — five questions.** What is happening? How can we know? How do we
   test and move? What matters? What should we attempt?
4. **Research — ambition at its price.** Current discoveries, open dockets,
   evidence grades, corrections, authorship, and independent review owed.
5. **Choose depth.** Practice, Worldview, Research, Library, Participate, Exit.
6. **Closing return.** “The map stays revisable. The world constrains the reply.”

The hero receipt loop is the sole homepage explanation of the method. A second
standalone method section is repetition and should remain cut.

### Hero language contract

**Eyebrow:** `EMERGENTISM · A WORLDVIEW FOR FINITE BEINGS`

**Headline:**

> Reality exceeds every map.  
> We still have to act.

**Lead:** Emergentism is a fallibilist worldview and practical compass. It
offers a way to distinguish actuality from possibility, match possible futures
to the means truly available, keep everyone affected visible, and let observed
consequences revise the map.

**Practice bridge:** Finity is its first practice: orient by the longest
responsible horizon, then choose the shortest observable review boundary—
reversible where possible—that can return an honest result.

**Actions:** `Frame one decision` · `Read the worldview`

**Boundary:** `Load-bearing claims graded · comparative benefit untested · no belief required · Exit remains open`

retain meaning without motion, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

validation. Its surviving corrections were:

  language from current first-contact routes;
- `[I]` put the strongest ordinary-method rival beside the Finity Card;
- `[S]` treat reversibility as preferred where possible, not universally
  available, and retain the tragic protocol when all options carry harm;
- `[B]` remove the duplicated homepage method section and keep one receipt
  loop as the signature explanation;
- `[B]` make the practice produce a local, non-transmitting commitment/outcome
  receipt rather than only copying instructions; and
- `[S]` keep functional Rosetta language primary while preserving mythic terms
  as optional mnemonics at depth.

## External evaluation program

The next three gates are typed as separately freezable draft packets under
`03_METHODOLOGY/03_PREREGISTRATIONS/finity_practice/`:

1. `FPE-READ-01` tests fresh-reader category, boundary, transfer, and Exit;
2. `FPE-REVIEW-01` preserves conflict-declared external criticism and dissent;
3. `FPE-COMPARE-01` compares brand, prompt organization, a strong ordinary
   worksheet, and heterogeneous usual practice in a four-arm low-stakes design;
   it does not claim to isolate “structure” as a causal mechanism.

The designs are not packet-complete: exact materials, manifests, ethics
determination, analysis assets, and freeze remain outstanding. They create no
evidence. No human contact, external review, ethics
determination, preregistration, data, result, or replication exists. A
comprehension receipt can move public copy only; review can move protocol
quality only; a comparative result can move only its frozen domain and outcomes.
None validates an ontology.

## Verification and release boundary

Before a candidate is called release-ready:

1. verify every first-contact statement against its source owner and claim card;
2. run the compiler, purity, link, register, doctrine, semantic-parity, public
   predeploy, and living-map gates required by the repository;
3. render desktop and 390 px mobile views, inspect navigation, focus, overflow,
   action position, and reduced-motion behavior;
4. run the five-second comprehension test with people who have not seen the
   corpus; and
5. record commit, push, immutable artifact, promotion, DNS, headers, routes,
   and sampled served hashes as separate states.

Local green gates prove only that a candidate satisfies the encoded checks.
They do not prove the worldview, the practice, a deployment, or independent
validation.

## Stop conditions

- A public phrase would become stronger than its named source owner.
- Finity would be presented as a proved algebra, universal ontology, or general
  paradox solver.
- The page would imply that ethics follows from geometry or that symbols cause
  physical outcomes.
- A route would suppress rivals, membership alternatives, correction, or Exit.
- Superseded material would be erased instead of preserved with provenance.
- A failed gate or rendered defect cannot be repaired inside the bounded scope.
