# Direction

After reviewing Skyzai Brand Guide v6.0, the owner directed: **“ok go for it.”**
The authorized work is to make Emergentism's public design language an explicit,
testable projection contract.

## Selected translation

1. Emergentism keeps its own editorial identity: near-black field, bone text,
   Newsreader display type, Roboto explanation, Roboto Mono receipts, and the
   `• ⊙ ○` boundary grammar.
2. Skyzai's marks, Torus/Lattice identity, substrate green, F.I.R.E. sectors,
   terminal density, Game of Life field, and product voice do not enter this
   lane.
3. The site visibly and machine-readably distinguishes boundary, actuality,
   represented possibility, conjecture, returned evidence, and poison/refutation.
4. Motion is finite, optional, state-bound, and semantically redundant. The
   controlled Burrisphere instrument remains a separately declared exception;
   continuous ambient spectacle is refused.
5. One human-readable constitution and one machine contract own the projection
   grammar. Core shells consume the machine contract; checks reject drift.
6. Every current journey route remains source-bound, locally resourced,
   keyboard reachable, reduced-motion safe, and adjacent to Exit.

## Release boundary

This direction authorizes local source edits, deterministic regeneration, focused
tests, and an exact local commit. It does not authorize push, deployment, DNS
change, promotion, external validation, scientific validation, or a claim that
the design improves comprehension before reader contact.
