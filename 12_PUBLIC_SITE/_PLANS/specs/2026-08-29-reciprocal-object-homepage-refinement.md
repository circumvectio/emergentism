# Reciprocal Object

## The page's single job

Let a new reader grasp one structure before anything else: one consequence-bearing
actual present can carry representations of multiple possible futures, act, receive
consequence, and revise. The homepage then unfolds the selected levels, instrument,
practice, evidence, live wagers, and genuine Exit.

## Direction

The existing identity survives. The intervention is hierarchy, not a foreign skin.
The first screen contains the human question, one thesis, one principal route, and
one integrated atlas field. The field prompt moves to Practice. Evidence pricing
remains early and fully visible, but no longer competes with the thesis.

### Tokens

- Void — `#07090f`
- Bone — `#efe8db`
- Actual — `#d7b65a`, solid and consequence-bearing
- Possible — `#78a7e8`, branching and represented
- Evidence — `#76ceb0`, returning and revising
- Conjecture — `#a58be2`, dashed and killable

Poison retains its existing exceptional oxide token; it is not part of the ordinary
decorative palette.

### Type

- Newsreader — the human question, thesis, and section synthesis
- Roboto — explanation and ordinary reading
- Roboto Mono — addresses, tiers, coordinates, receipts, and boundaries

The title performs the ontology: **One actual present.** is the settled line;
**Many possible futures.** is a visibly offset represented branch. The wording,
not colour alone, preserves the distinction.

### Signature

The opening atlas is integrated into the void rather than presented as a second
bordered instrument. The later Burrisphere remains the crisp mathematical object.
They therefore do different jobs: the first orients; the second demonstrates.

### Motion

Actuality settles first; represented branches unfold once; returned evidence draws
last and rests. There is no spin, parallax, ambient loop, or decorative orbit. The
complete settled state exists before JavaScript and under reduced motion.

## Information edits

1. Keep the opening human question; it is the constitutional entry, not decoration.
2. Keep one primary hero action and one quiet secondary route.
3. Move the complete one-minute field prompt to Practice.
4. Keep Dasein, situated dasein, map/territory, and validation boundaries together
   as one bounded definition block beneath the opening object.
5. Keep all six evidence-tier definitions before the first tier-dependent section,
   with no horizontal hiding at narrow widths.
6. Make the page journey rail quiet and non-sticky on mobile.
7. Reduce repeated three-link action clusters to a primary route plus one secondary.
8. Increase essential mono text to a readable information size.

## Why this is not a generic dark editorial site

Near-black, serif display type, and hairline rules are common defaults. They are not
the identity. The identity is the typed relationship among an actual carrier,
represented branches, returned evidence, explicit claim prices, D0–D6 addresses,
and Exit. If those structures were removed, the design would fail even if it still
looked refined.

The deliberate risk is the split title and borderless atlas field. It is justified
because the typography and containment enact the actual/possible distinction rather
than adding spectacle.

## Kill criteria

Revert or revise if the change hides a tier at 320px, makes actuality/possibility
colour-dependent, weakens D4/D5 typing, removes authorship or Exit, introduces
continuous motion, loses no-JavaScript/reduced-motion meaning, creates global
horizontal overflow, makes both atlases perceptual peers, or worsens a fresh reader's
ability to identify the thesis, who may act, what remains conjectural, and how to
leave.
