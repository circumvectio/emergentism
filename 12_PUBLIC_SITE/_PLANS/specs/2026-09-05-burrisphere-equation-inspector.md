## The row-generation research, not a dismissal

`2 mixed-sign patterns × 2 bearer orientations + 3 frames = 7` constructs the
declared vocabulary. The next question is whether a justified equivalence rule
makes those symbols an adequate, disjoint classifier, and whether a separate
dependency relation orders them as L1–L7. These are different proof burdens.

The executable [strict-signature probe](../rosetta_row_probe.py) compares two
labelled bearers and two independently ordered factors each. F3 uses **self**
as its explicitly selected scope. Every comparison is a supplied synthetic
input, not an observed capability change. In its 81 trivalent comparison
records, 32 match no strict signature, 37 match exactly one, and 12 match more
than one. These counts are not a census of feasible actions.

Two sharp examples:

1. Only self’s possible-power estimate rises; all other comparisons hold.
   No strict signature matches. A degenerate-class assignment is still owed.
2. Self and other both have possible-power estimates rise and actual-power
   comparisons fall. Taking-A and Giving-B both match. Partial signatures
   alone do not partition this record.

The 5,040 permutations count labelled orders **when no additional ordering
relation is supplied**. It does not refute an independently justified order.
Unknown and incomparable comparisons never become preservation. Preservation
needs a declared comparison-equivalence rule or a defended measurement
tolerance; its source shorthand `≈0` supplies neither automatically.

**Survivor:** seven may still be a useful generating vocabulary under explicit
domain, composition and equivalence rules. These tests locate those missing
rules; they prove neither universal exhaustiveness nor universal impossibility,
and they establish no strong emergence.
