# Emergentism.org v2 — The Gestalt of Dasein

## Vision

Rebuild the core public journey as an **ontological atlas** organized by one
question:

> **What had to emerge for you—and this moment—to be here?**

The atlas is a source-bound sketch of the Gestalt, not a total explanation or
scientific validation. Its completeness claim is accounting completeness:
every explanatory bridge is typed, contested, killed, survived, or left open.

## Core route

The stable navigation grammar is:

`Worldview · Practice · Research · Library · Participate · Exit`

The first release covers `/`, `/plainly/`, `/dasein/`, `/dimensions/`,
`/0/`–`/6/`, `/f5/`, `/practice/`, `/spark/`, `/record/`, `/record/eub-1/`,
`/lab/`, `/discoveries/`, `/book/`, `/about/`, `/contribute/`, and `/exit/`.
Deeper and historical routes remain accessible and visibly secondary.

The homepage sequence is fixed:

1. the hero question and `•  ⊙  ○` boundary;
2. D0 boundary → D1 distinction → D2 relation → D3 quantum state → D4 actual
   history → D5 represented possibility → D6 nonclosure;
3. the D1–D4 force/debt wager, explicitly one of 24 assignments;
4. one present, three explanations: `F5-W`, `F5-N`, `F5-R`;
5. the dual option ledger and chosen teleology;
6. Soul Loop and Finity practice;
7. the Dasein Test and open research sockets; and
8. authorship, evidence, participation, and genuine Exit.

## Claim contract

The F5 public surface consumes
[`F5Fork.v1`](../../05_COSMOLOGY/02_EMERGENTISM_CORE/F5Fork.v1.json). It must:

- keep `F5-W [I/C]`, `F5-N [D/C]`, and `F5-R [C]` visible together;
- award no truth bonus for elegance, founder preference, AI agreement, or
  cross-architecture convergence;
- keep D5 possible content from acting by itself;
- call the strong branch a candidate history-selection law until the physical
  force admission gate is paid;
- separate future-boundary dependence, new interaction, multiverse actuality,
  option bias, and normative teleology;
- distinguish subsystem entropy, total entropy production/export, path
  entropy, empowerment, viability, consent, Justice, and Exit; and
- show each branch's rival, discriminator, kill, and survivor.

The gravity neighbor is stated narrowly: Dorau and Much recover semiclassical
Einstein equations from quantum relative entropy under stated horizon and
entropy-area assumptions. They do not prove that gravity is entropy, a gravity
time arrow, a microscopic substrate, the serial-force ordering, or F5.

## Signature figure

One semantic SVG is the memorable device. It contains:

- one D4 present;
- one solid actual history behind it;
- a branching D5 cone of modeled alternatives ahead;
- one solid feedback path from the present model to present action weights;
- one dashed, labelled `[C] UNVALIDATED` path from a declared future boundary
  to present transition weights; and
- no single glowing or predestined endpoint.

The future light cone is never drawn as the source. A history ensemble does not
prove physically coexisting worlds. Visible labels, border styles, and dash
patterns preserve meaning without color.

## Visual system

- near-black field: boundary and unknown;
- bone text: readable account;
- gold: actuality;
- blue: represented possibility;
- violet: conjecture;
- mint: returned evidence;
- self-hosted open editorial display type for theses;
- existing local sans for explanation and mono for claims, sources, and kills.

Motion may unfold the atlas but carries no meaning. The static, no-JavaScript
version is complete. Reduced motion, semantic headings, keyboard operation,
SVG title/description, 48px targets, mobile stacking, and 320px containment are
release requirements.

## Build contract

- `public_semantic_parity.json` v3 owns navigation and `coreJourney` routing,
  never doctrine prose.
- `build_core_shell.py --check` deterministically binds the shared navigation
  and footer.
- `render_dimension_site.py` and `build_book.py` consume the same shell and
  tokens rather than being patched after generation.
- PWA, Atlas, RAG, social cards, and service worker register the new routes and
  assets.
- Historical routes and `xai.css` remain compatibility surfaces; the v2 design
  is scoped and does not silently restyle the archive.

## Adversarial rejects

The release gate must reject affirmative public copy that:

- says untyped “D5 causes” or “D5 pushes”;
- says “gravity is entropy” or assigns gravity a past-to-present arrow;
- uses the future light cone as a causal source;
- presents `F5-R` without `F5-N`, rival, discriminator, kill, and survivor;
- says “least entropy” without typed ledgers;
- hides affected bearers behind agent potential;
- infers ontic multiverse actuality from modeled histories; or
- uses “strange attractor” without state space, flow, invariant set, and
  attraction test.

## Release boundary

Implementation is committed in three explicit path groups:

1. source and claim custody;
2. design system and core pages; and
3. generated artifacts, tests, and gate receipts.

Acceptance is `OFFLINE-READY`: the source fork, redesigned core journey,
deterministic artifacts, and local gates pass. It is not evidence that F5 was
observed, Emergentism was scientifically validated, the site was published, a
domain changed, or any external party reviewed the work.

No push, deployment, DNS mutation, external deposit, model call, or publication
act is authorized by this packet.
