Use it. Test it.
Make it answerable.

Start as a person, not a member: use Finity and share what happened, attack a public claim at its strongest point, or contribute bounded evidence. Each route opens a public GitHub form and needs a GitHub account — that is GitHub's gate, not membership here. This static site has no account, submits nothing itself, and does not treat a GitHub issue as review or a tier upgrade.

Participation boundary

Participate without enrolling.

This page offers public ways to test, criticize, or contribute evidence. It creates no membership, funding relationship, assent obligation, or transfer of authority. You may use a method, submit a public issue, or leave.

No study, funding programme, grant scheme, compute service, or private-data intake is open.

No payment, credential or live inference endpoint exists on this static release.

Optional technical route

Read the research protocol.

Compute envelope

Any future run needs a hard edge.

If a future contribution route is opened, purpose, cost, tools, data, publication, expiry and revocation must be explicit before execution. No such route is open in this release.

AUTHORITY

Who and why

Principal, declared purpose, question identifier, permitted providers and attribution.

  • No donor truth authority
  • No hidden secondary purpose
  • No application outside scope
COST

What may be spent

Maximum currency spend, tokens, runs, rate, models and expiry.

  • Hard server-side limits
  • Visible cost receipt
  • Automatic stop at exhaustion
CUSTODY

What may be seen

Allowed sources, data classification, tools, retention and deletion route.

  • No secrets in prompts or logs
  • No arbitrary public tool execution
  • No undisclosed training use
CONSEQUENCE

What must be published

Prompt and model provenance, outputs, disagreements, failures, costs and review status.

  • Nulls remain visible
  • Sponsor cannot suppress results
  • Revocation stops future runs
