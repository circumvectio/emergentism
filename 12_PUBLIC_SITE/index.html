Emergentism proposes a finite atlas of determinate Being. Dasein names the typed coherent-consistent whole. Finity names that whole under the aspect of determinate, situated manifestation—not a second thing and not necessarily a finite cardinality.

A situated dasein cannot possess the whole from nowhere. It can compare charts, name their limits, act through actual means, receive consequence, and revise.

No map is the territory. This one prints its legend.

Counterexamples with teeth

An executed two-world S5 model refutes “an admissible empty world implies everything is possible” under inclusive semantics. Different maps meet at some edges.

The overlap earns comparison, not belief. These are cited neighbours and a research lead, never truth evidence.
