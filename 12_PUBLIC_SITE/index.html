02 · The debt wager · [C]

Does each new freedom incur a compensating interaction?

The Burri serial force-emergence conjecture proposes one of 24 possible assignments. Agreement with it earns no score; native physics recovery and discriminating predictions decide.

D1 ↔ strong

Distinction held together

The proposed first assignment pairs individuation with the strong interaction. This is an affinity to test, not a derivation from gauge theory.

D2 ↔ electromagnetic

Relation made transmissible

The proposed second assignment pairs relational configuration with electromagnetic coupling. Rival permutations remain live.

D3 ↔ weak

State can transform

The proposed third assignment pairs the quantum-state register with the weak interaction. Standard-model structure remains the native authority.

D4 ↔ gravity

Actual history gravitates

The proposed fourth assignment pairs causal actuality with gravity. Entropic derivations of Einstein dynamics do not establish this ordering.

Kill: failure to recover accepted physics, lack of an invariant mapping, or a rival permutation that predicts as well with less structure. Survivor: the dimensional addresses may remain useful even if every force assignment fails.
