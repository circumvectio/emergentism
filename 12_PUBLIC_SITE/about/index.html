What this is

A worldview kept on trial.

Emergentism is a proposed translation grammar for thinking about constraints, emergence, modeled futures, consequential action, and correction across domains. It combines exact mathematics, inherited evidence, interpretive crosswalks, and conjectures—but marks them differently.

It is not a settled theory of everything, a scientific consensus, a religion, or an instruction to join a group. The operational parts should remain usable if the metaphysical wagers fail.

Established

Typed claims

Analytic identities and inherited results are credited. Interpretations and conjectures keep their own evidence labels and kill criteria.

Still open

External calibration

AI disclosure

Assistance is not peer review.

No external human peer review has yet validated the framework. The named multi-agent audits on this site were AI-assisted internal reviews under human direction. They can expose inconsistencies and test machinery; they are not an independent scholarly credential.

Publication choices, corrections, and unresolved debts remain the responsibility of Yves R. Burri as author and accountable publisher. The public trial record is where material failures and repairs should remain inspectable.

Contact and correction

Submit a correction, counterexample, or proposed falsifier through the project's public GitHub Issues. Name the claim, its evidence tier, the source or reproducible steps, and the expected-versus-observed result. An issue is evidence offered for review—not an automatic correction or endorsement.
