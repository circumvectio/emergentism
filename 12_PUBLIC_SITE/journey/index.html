Seven registers, one selected scaffold, from the ground marker (D0) to the return marker (D6). Walk all seven and notice only a resemblance: D6 and D0 play similar boundary roles, but they are not identical and no recurrence follows. The compass works without the walk; the walk shows where the compass came from. Start anywhere. Leave anywhere.
