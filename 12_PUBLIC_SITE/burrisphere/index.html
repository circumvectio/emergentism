07 · Non-identity fence

G7@1 ≠ GEN7@1

Two sevenfold structures coexist in the corpus. They do not make the same cut.

Burrisphere G7@1

Four plus three

Four quadrant transformations plus three axial Titan stations. It is an action-and-frame projection.

Rosetta GEN7@1

Three plus one plus three

Three mirrored pairs plus a selected centre on the separate L1–L7 meridian ladder.

The mirror ladder’s values 0, 1/2, √3/2, 1, √3/2, 1/2, 0 are analytic only after seven equally spaced stations are selected. Geometry does not force seven; shared count is not truth evidence.

08 · Return to consequence

A position on the sphere never decides whether an act is just.

The diagram describes proposed transformations. Actual judgment begins where the abstraction ends.

Giving may be coercive or destructive; taking may be consensual, reparative, or lawful. No quadrant, alias, sign, pole, or direction carries moral standing by itself.

For a consequential act, name every affected bearer, authority, consent, custody, payer, beneficiary, reversibility, Exit, intention, and outcome receipt. Then return through the Soul Loop and revise.
