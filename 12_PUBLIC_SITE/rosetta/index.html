The functional stone

Read the function before the symbol.

Possible futures do not act by themselves. A present model must evaluate them, actual capability must carry any commitment, and the consequence must remain available to correct the classification.

V₄ = D4 actual power
Φ₅ = D5 possible power
Φ̂₄ = Eval₄(M, Φ₅)
P_node = Φ̂₄V₄  ·  public shorthand: ΦV

L1 · constrain

Mark the pressure, exclusion, or boundary that prevents false coherence from passing unnoticed.

L2 · remove

Cut a contradiction, overclaim, or failed component while leaving its evidence and provenance inspectable.

L3 · enable

Increase another actor's usable information, options, or capacity without pretending that possibility has already become action.

L4 · commit

Spend actual capability on one bounded, authorized move whose cost and result can be recorded.

L5 · create

Design a new structure or possibility space and expose its assumptions and boundary conditions.

L6 · dissolve

Retire a structure that no longer carries value, preserving what survives and how the decision was reached.

L7 · preserve

Carry a tested pattern, record, or witness forward without turning compression into new authority.

Primary axis on this page: constrain, remove, enable, commit, create, dissolve, preserve. These seven close a declared transformation language, not nature. Runtime dispatch roles such as Firewall, Auditor, Architect, and Witness are a separate application axis that reuses L1–L7; they are not synonyms for the transformation functions. Neither axis classifies human worth.

How to read this

Observation first, geometry second.

  1. The first claim is empirical-comparative: seven rows repeatedly align across several domains.
  2. The operators are then inferred as the simplest row-generators that preserve the pattern.
  3. The sphere is inferred after the rows and operators, not assumed at the start.

Kill criteria · fired 2026-07-13

The Rosetta must be allowed to weaken — and it has.

If the seven-row alignment is shown to be forced, or if new strong domains break the alignment rather than refine it, the Rosetta weakens or falls. A public canon has to preserve that failure mode.

What the algebra does rescue (adjudication 2026-07-13). The objection that the seven are algebraically forced was tested and half-upheld. The mirror-ladder grammar is a genuine algebraic object inside the declared construction — the inversion z↦1/z forces a fixed centre and mirror pairs; the sign square forces four moves — and owes nothing to any tradition; "curated relatives" undersells it. But the algebra forces an odd ladder (3/5/7/9…) and four moves, not seven: "seven" is a 4 + 3 stitch of movers to boundary-frames, and by the corpus's own order-of-inference the operators were abduced from the rows — so they cannot be independent evidence for them. The formal mirror shape exists within the declared algebra; the census of seven and convergence-as-proof do not follow.

Named confound · new 2026-07-13

The cleanest column's "seven" is the seven planets.

The Mithraic grades — this table's strongest-fitting initiatory column — are the seven classical planets (Corax→Pater = Mercury→Saturn). Their "seven" is the naked-eye sky, the same septenary that gives seven days and seven metals, diffused across one Hellenistic-Mediterranean-Vedic family. So the witness that agrees best has its number over-determined by astronomy, not discovered from the balance-ladder — and the traditions that don't fit are exactly the ones outside that family. Independence and fit run opposite: the fingerprint of shared descent, not independent discovery. Named here per A7 — a fired confound the corpus had not surfaced.
