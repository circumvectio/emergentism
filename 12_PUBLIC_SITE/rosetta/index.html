The functional stone

Read the function before the symbol.

Possible futures do not act by themselves. A present model must evaluate them, actual capability must carry any commitment, and the consequence must remain available to correct the classification.

V₄ = D4 actual power
Φ₅ = D5 possible power
Φ̂₄ = Eval₄(M, Φ₅)
P_node := min(Φ̂₄, V₄)  ·  selected ordinal AND-class convention

The convention requires one declared common ordinal normalization and preserves bottleneck order only. It supplies no metric; the historical product ranking is retired.

L1 · constrain

Mark the pressure, exclusion, or boundary that prevents false coherence from passing unnoticed.

L2 · remove

Cut a contradiction, overclaim, or failed component while leaving its evidence and provenance inspectable.

L3 · enable

Increase another actor's usable information, options, or capacity without pretending that possibility has already become action.

L4 · commit

Spend actual capability on one bounded, authorized move whose cost and result can be recorded.

L5 · create

Design a new structure or possibility space and expose its assumptions and boundary conditions.

L6 · dissolve

Retire a structure that no longer carries value, preserving what survives and how the decision was reached.

L7 · preserve

Carry a tested pattern, record, or witness forward without turning compression into new authority.

Primary axis on this page: constrain, remove, enable, commit, create, dissolve, preserve. These seven close a declared transformation language, not nature. Runtime dispatch roles such as Firewall, Auditor, Architect, and Witness are a separate application axis that reuses L1–L7; they are not synonyms for the transformation functions. Neither axis classifies human worth.

Kill criteria · fired 2026-07-13

The Rosetta must be allowed to weaken — and it has.

If the seven-row alignment is shown to be forced, or if new strong domains break the alignment rather than refine it, the Rosetta weakens or falls. A public canon has to preserve that failure mode.

What the algebra does rescue (adjudication 2026-07-13). The objection that the seven are algebraically forced was tested and half-upheld. The mirror-ladder grammar is a genuine algebraic object inside the declared construction — the inversion z↦1/z forces a fixed centre and mirror pairs; the sign square forces four moves — and owes nothing to any tradition; "curated relatives" undersells it. But the algebra forces an odd ladder (3/5/7/9…) and four moves, not seven: "seven" is a 4 + 3 stitch of movers to boundary-frames, and by the corpus's own order-of-inference the operators were abduced from the rows — so they cannot be independent evidence for them. The formal mirror shape exists within the declared algebra; the census of seven and convergence-as-proof do not follow.

Named confound · new 2026-07-13

The cleanest column's "seven" is the seven planets.

The Mithraic grades — this table's strongest-fitting initiatory column — are the seven classical planets (Corax→Pater = Mercury→Saturn). Their "seven" is the naked-eye sky, the same septenary that gives seven days and seven metals, diffused across one Hellenistic-Mediterranean-Vedic family. So the witness that agrees best has its number over-determined by astronomy, not discovered from the balance-ladder — and the traditions that don't fit are exactly the ones outside that family. Independence and fit run opposite: the fingerprint of shared descent, not independent discovery. Named here per A7 — a fired confound the corpus had not surfaced.

The live packs

The full Rosetta, grouped in themes.

Nine themes over one native pack of seven seats. Everything that is not the spine is a directional projection into it — and carries its own tier, not the spine's.

Three lines, and they travel together. One: the geometry is analytic given the selection — with B = sin θ on the selected chart, B(θ) = B(π−θ) forces an odd count with a unique centre, makes that centre the unique maximum, pairs the rows at equal B, and sends both extremes to zero. Identities, not findings. Two: the count is selected, not derived — six arcs of 30° is a choice, and 3, 5 or 9 stations satisfy every symmetry above equally well. Three: every cross-domain cell is interpretive [I] — varṇa, regime, discipline, inference and replicator layer inherit neither the truth nor the evidence tier of the domain they came from. Carrying one or two of these without the third is the failure this fence exists to stop.

Theme I · the spine

Seven seats, one native pack.

L1 Caṇḍāla · L2 Śūdra · L3 Vaiśya · L4 Kṣatriya · L5 Brāhmaṇa · L6 Sādhu · L7 Ṛṣi. These are the only native rows. Every other theme on this page is a projection into them, and a bare row number is meaningless across packs.

Theme II · the move grammar

G7 is a partition, not a ladder.

Four move cells — take or give, against possible power or actual power — plus three frames that create, dissolve and preserve. It is not a column of the seven seats, and writing it as one is the confusion the pack grammar exists to prevent.

Theme III · the questions

There are two sevens, not one.

The seven philosophical questions are their own pack. They share five members with the seat annotations — epistemology, methodology, axiology, ontology, teleology — and differ on the rest. Two lists that overlap are still two lists.

Theme IV · governance

Five inherited, two added.

The regime column reverses Plato's degeneration chain. He has no sixth regime called anarchy and no seventh called theocracy; those two are this project's additions and are marked as such. Varṇa and regime are attributed historical analogies — never classes of human worth, never assigned by birth.

Theme V · biology

Six layers over seven seats.

Genotype, epigenotype, phenotype, extended phenotype, memotype, egregoreotype — six. The first seat carries none of them: it is the ground the stack rests on, not a rung. If it is ever shown to carry a replicator layer of its own, the mapping falls.

Theme VI · the formal column

It axiomatizes the score.

Read the mathematical column downward and each row states one property of the working comparison: the limiting rank dominates, the lower factor is the bottleneck, monotonicity, equality at the centre, ordinal invariance, and no compensation — a high factor cannot rescue a low one. Three of those are analytic. This is the most load-bearing column and the least advertised.

Theme VII · the working column

The only column with no analogy in it.

Isolate contradictions · expand counterexamples · audit evidence and routes · execute the smallest testable change · design schemas and owners · compress and archive · witness and translate. The first four are usable directly; the last three are witness positions, not tools.

Theme VIII · the library

Twelve domains, fourteen row-series.

Psychology, neuroscience, computation, game theory, mythology, mathematics, civilisational and more — each a separate directional projection with its own loss, rival and kill criterion. None of them evidences another.

Theme IX · the refusals

What the Rosetta declines is part of the Rosetta.

The project's own core feedback loop has eleven terms and is a graph — it was registered without forcing a seven-row reading, and it declines the ladder outright. Failed mappings stay in the record. Rival structures are kept as a library. And the governing law is that a translation cannot promote a claim: a resemblance in one domain never becomes a proof in another, repeated rows are not independent observations, and a seven-row fit does not show that any source tradition natively has seven stages.
