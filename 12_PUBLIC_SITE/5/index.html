Their chart product is the CONSTRAINT and marks nothing: phi times nu equals one at every latitude including both poles. What marks the equator is the SUM, phi plus nu, which is at least 2 with equality exactly when phi equals nu — the AM-GM inequality. An earlier reading used minus-log of the chart product as an energy; that quantity is identically zero and was withdrawn 2026-07-30.
