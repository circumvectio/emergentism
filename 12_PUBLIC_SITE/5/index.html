Direction and valence. η names extraction valence: value taken from a bearer without warranted return. It runs across every polarity; no pole, gender, caste, operator, or person is morally charged. §2 · The Dissolutions

Is/Ought — Kept Open, Narrowed by a Conditional Test

The framework does not dissolve is/ought. For a policy π, declare the arena boundary, horizon, regeneration rate, host dependence, enforcement, and adoption level; then test what happens under universal adoption. Kant's universalizability is used, not discovered — a diagnostic, not a derived categorical theorem.

Conditional collapse: in a closed arena with no fresh hosts or exit, where extraction exceeds regeneration and extractors depend on host viability, universalized extraction collapses its substrate — [S] inside those premises, not outside them. No automatic ascent: universalized non-extraction avoids that particular self-deletion. It does not by itself prove viability, justice, cooperation's uniqueness, μ-opening, or a D6 ascent; those remain [C] where proposed.

So the "ought" is not derived. The finite being or constitution chooses the horizon, maximand, and enforcement that make the scoped lemma applicable. The ethic remains a vow, not a theorem.

The moralized-operator paradox. If giving is good, is the taker evil? No — cured by typing: modeled extraction valence may be reported by η, never by the operator's name. A lawful cut may be non-extractive; a saccharine gift may hide dependency or cost. Neither the verb nor η supplies the moral verdict: bearer-complete Justice tests boundary, consent, payer, beneficiary, alternatives, reversibility, contest, and receipted consequence.

The altruism-laundering paradox. "The greatest good for the greatest number" can vote a bearer into the furnace. Cured by the dyadic gate: every impact-bearer faces the gate individually; aggregates report, they do not license.

The enforcement paradox. If extraction self-terminates, why forbid it? Because self-termination arrives at scale and at horizon — long after the victims. The lemma is why the vow may be wise; the vow is why the lemma may apply. Neither derives the other.

Burri Window Lemma · exact geometry, bounded inference

The window cannot certify infinity

A sphere-circle through the stereographic projection pole maps exactly to a line in the extended plane. Separately, a large finite circle can become operationally indistinguishable from its chord inside a fixed finite-resolution window. Foundations · game theory before the vow

Axioms, model primitives, and chosen rules

Game theory has no single universal axiom set. A result is only as strong as the declared players, strategies, information, payoffs, timing, and rationality assumptions. This page uses those actual D4 formalisms to describe D5 alternatives.

Game model · inherited formalism

The primitives that must be declared

A game specifies players, available strategies or actions, outcome/payoff functions, information, move order, horizon, and—when relevant—chance. These are model inputs, not facts discovered merely by solving the game.

von Neumann–Morgenstern · inherited [A]

Expected-utility axioms under risk

  • Completeness: the agent can compare any two lotteries.
  • Transitivity: consistent pairwise preferences compose.
  • Continuity: intermediate lotteries can represent intermediate preference.
  • Independence: mixing both options with the same third lottery preserves their ordering.

These axioms yield expected-utility representation under their scope. They are not universal descriptions of human behavior and do not select Emergentism's maximand.

Nash + Kant · inherited, not axioms here

Two tools this rung must not overclaim

A Nash equilibrium is a mutual best-response condition, not a moral optimum and not a promise that η=0 will arise unaided. Kantian universalizability is a philosophical diagnostic used here to stress-test a policy; it is not derived from game theory.
