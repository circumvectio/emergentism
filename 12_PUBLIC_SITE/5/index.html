Possible power and the Rosetta Stone

D5 possibility becomes actionable only through D4 power. Φ names D5 possible power: the structured potential of an option field. V names D4 actual power: embodied capability and usable means. A present model evaluates the first and couples it to the second; an unrealized future never acts by itself.

Inherited structure

Decision theory, control, planning, counterfactual reasoning, and institutional coordination supply comparison classes.

Claim boundary

Typed form: V₄ is D4 actual power; Φ₅ is D5 possible power; Φ̂₄ is its present D4 estimate. P_node = Φ̂₄ times V₄, publicly Φ times V, is a selected model—not a universal law or future-to-past force. Lowercase φ and ν remain chart coordinates. The product is the CONSTRAINT and marks nothing: phi times nu equals one at every latitude including both poles. What marks the equator is the SUM, phi plus nu, which is at least 2 with equality exactly when phi equals nu — the AM-GM inequality. An earlier reading used minus-log of the product as an energy; that quantity is identically zero and was withdrawn 2026-07-30.
