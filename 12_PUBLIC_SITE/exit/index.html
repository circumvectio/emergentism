Exit

[I] chosen practice · [S] release requirement

The exit is real.

You owe this worldview no allegiance. Keep any distinction or practice that proves useful in your circumstances; reject its metaphysics; close the page. A practice receives no support merely because its users believe in it.

What leaving means

  • No penalty for disagreement.
  • No claim that doubt is moral failure.
  • No requirement to retain the vocabulary.
  • No erased record of what the framework got wrong.
  • No private person, institution, or machine owns your judgment.

For dated corrections and retained failures, visit the Trial Record.

Two exits, two different types

Action exit is the boundary between a represented option and an actual commitment: an authorized person may sign the prepared decision packet, revise it, or refuse it. On this static site, any “signature” is only a local acknowledgment. It transmits, executes, authorizes, binds, and settles nothing.

Worldview Exit is this page. Leaving requires no signature, request, approval, contribution, or continued contact. You may leave every prepared transaction unsigned and put the framework down.

If a separate organization later offers a relationship, transaction, or signing process, it must state its own authority, security, terms, and exit path; this page neither creates nor governs one.

Take the portable part

The claim method travels alone: write the claim, label its warrant, name what would kill it, and preserve the dated correction. The Soul Loop travels alone too: distinguish model, action, commitment, consequence, and update.
