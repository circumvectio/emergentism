The Guarantee

What Grace Exit means

[S] Structural

Leave with what is yours

Your relationships, history, reasons, and judgment remain yours. The framework does not claim your identity, loyalty, attention, or assent.

[S] Structural

Refuse extraction

Zero extraction is written η = 0. No collective may enlarge its durable potential by destroying yours or by making departure needlessly costly.

[S] Structural

Make exit inspectable

A real exit names custody, dependencies, reversible steps, irreversible residue, and the route of contest. A slogan is not an exit path.

[I] Interpretive

Grace, not mere permission

Grace Exit means more than "you may leave." It means the system is designed to help you leave well — with continuity, with dignity, with your full history intact.

[S] Grace Exit is one of five refusals: non-extraction, accountable authorization, archive-first repair, exit, and self-correction. Ω is the separate directional +1. See Five + One Constitution.

The Path

How to exit

1

Name what binds you

List commitments, dependencies, custodians, records, and affected people. An unnamed dependency cannot be unwound honestly.

[S] This is a procedural requirement, not a guarantee about any external system.
2

Recover custody

Identify what you can take, what must be transferred, what must remain as provenance, and who bears the transition cost.

[I] Custody is domain-specific and must be verified in the actual arrangement.
3

Withdraw and contest

Revoke consent where possible, contest disputed consequences, preserve receipts, and distinguish reversible withdrawal from irreducible residue.

[S] An accountable envelope names consent, revocation, contest, actor, and consequence bearer.
4

Keep the record

Leaving need not erase what was learned. The history remains legible, and returning later requires fresh consent rather than presumed loyalty.

[I] Grace means continuity without compulsion; it does not promise costless return.

The Constitution

Grace Exit does not stand alone

Grace Exit is one of five refusals. Each reinforces the others; Ω supplies a direction without turning that direction into a command.

η = 0 Zero Extraction No durable gain purchased by destroying another bearer's durable potential. AUTH Accountable Authorization Name principal, mandate, scope, consent, custody, actor, contest, and consequence. TRACE Archive First Superseded content is tombstoned, never erased. Provenance is preserved. EXIT Grace Exit You leave with what is yours. The door is marked on the inside. A7 Self-Correction Keep each claim at its warranted tier: no warrant, no upgrade. Corrections remain visible. Ω Noospheric Direction The directional +1. Not a refusal — a compass bearing.
