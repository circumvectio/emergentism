FPE-COMPARE-01 · TYPED DRAFT · NOT PREREGISTERED

Does Finity add anything?

Four arms separate a Finity-branded worksheet, content-identical brand-masked prompts, a strongest component-matched Ordinary Decision Record, and a person's usual method. Correction, omissions, burden, harm, and attrition remain separate outcomes.

None of these gates validates an ontology. Comprehension can clear the copy; review can improve the search; a controlled result can move only the preregistered low-stakes comparison. The ordinary method is allowed to win.

“Allowed to win” is cheap unless the losing conditions are written down first, so here they are, quoted from the frozen protocol rather than paraphrased:

“Retire or neutralize the brand if F adds pressure/confusion or its apparent advantage over U is only demand, prestige, novelty, or expectancy.”

“If U and F do not beat C by the preregistered meaningful margin after burden, harms, and attrition, preserve the Ordinary Decision Record or other better rival and keep Finity only as an optional selected worksheet if justified.”

“If F/U/C perform alike and beat M, narrow to the supplied structured worksheets in the sampled context; do not call structure itself causal from this contrast alone.”

“Treat non-significance as inconclusive unless equivalence/noninferiority margins were powered and preregistered.”

Those four sentences commit us, in advance, to retiring our own branding, to preferring an ordinary paper form over our worksheet if the ordinary form does better, and to refusing to read a null result as a quiet win. The comparison also cannot begin at all until an ethics determination, a final consent script, a hashed immutable stimulus bundle, and a frozen analysis plan all exist — a no-start boundary, written before any reader is contacted.

The honest status, which is the whole point of saying this now: none of the three has been run. No participant session, no engaged external reviewer, no registered comparison. Publishing the losing conditions before the study is the only part of a preregistration that is worth anything, and it is the only part we have so far done. Full protocols: 03_METHODOLOGY/03_PREREGISTRATIONS/finity_practice/ — 01 fresh-reader comprehension, 02 independent review, 03 controlled comparison.

ADJACENT PROGRAMME · [C] · OUTSIDE GP-01–GP-12

Does shared language coordinate beyond kinship and incentive?

This source-level contract is not yet packet-complete and is not counted among the twelve-question queue. Test whether shared semantic and normative language predicts durable coordination after controlling for genetic relatedness, material incentives, contact frequency, institutions, and coercion. Treat trade as a thin cross-language protocol: bounded exchange can work without a shared worldview.

MULTILEVEL STRESS TEST · [C]

Separate personal stakes from mass mobilization.

At the individual level, test actual-power stakes such as survival, status, resources, and reproduction. At the group level, test whether shared representations, rituals, norms, and teleology add explanatory power beyond security dilemmas, elite strategy, institutions, geography, and material capacity.

SCOPE GUARD

The conjecture is allowed to fail.

This programme does not establish language determinism, reproduction as a universal cause of war, or all wars as cult or egregore wars. Each proposed layer needs its own measure, rivals, discriminator, and kill criterion.
