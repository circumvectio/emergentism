## What this page keeps (and why)

**`[A]` (verified, classical, forced):**
- G2 — the bijection, machine-verified on 10,945 words with 6 mutants
- **KSC-22** — `ℝP¹ = ℝ ∪ {∞_P}`, `dim_ℝ = 1`. **Compactification does not create D2.** Closing the line does not raise it.
- The L1 firewall — the discipline that fences travel with phrases
- The Finity_L theorem — HM is maximal uniquely at a = b under √(ab) = c
- Nash equilibria — classical game theory
- The boundary behaviour — a product of margins dies when either margin dies
- The Dedekind 1888 absorption — V ∪ {x} = V at the horizon
- **Z1** — `0 ∈ ℝ`; `0 ∉ ℝ^×`. Zero is a real number with no multiplicative inverse. Say `ℝ^×`, never `ℝ`.
- **G1** — `√2`, `π`, `e` are numbers in `ℝ`; they are not finite words over `{S, ι}`. The base reaches exactly `ℚ⁺`.
- **FORECLOSED** — `a/0` in a field. The proof: `0·y = N` and `0·y = 0` give `N = 0`. There is no slot, not a forbidden one. "Emergentism forbids nothing here; the field forecloses it."
- **The three failures** — inadmissible term / no such element / indeterminate form. Three different things ordinary speech calls "undefined."
- **The cost of division** — no nontrivial field has a total quotient with `(a/b)·b = a`. Performing the operation doesn't break arithmetic; it costs you the arithmetic you were performing it in.
- **Formation boundaries** — Russell (`R? := {x : x ∉ x}`) is a set-formation boundary, not `∞_P` and not `○`; Cantor (`D_f := {x : x ∉ f(x)}`) is a same-level totalisation boundary.
- **The D3 discriminator** — `|+⟩⟨+|` and `I/2` agree in the Z basis and disagree in the X basis. One fixed classical distribution cannot carry the state. Formal, not metaphysical.
- **The state/measurement contract** — `ρ = ρ†`, `ρ ⪰ 0`, `Tr ρ = 1`; `p(k | ρ, M) = Tr(ρ E_k)` joint in state and declared context. Nothing in the formula is `φν = 1`.
- **The Bloch ∩ Burri reading** — same `ℂP¹`, same `PSL(2,ℂ)`, same stereographic chart, AND the reciprocal involution `z ↦ 1/z` has matrix `[[0,1],[1,0]]` — the Pauli-X gate. The framework's defining involution *is* the qubit NOT.
- **D4 as universal token-bearer** — `D0` "any inscription is a D4 actual token"; `D1` "an actual inscription or discrimination event is D4"; `D2` "an actual configured carrier or observation is D4"; `D6` "any recognition or withdrawal act is a D4 actual token." The ladder is a sequence of structures with exactly one register where anything ever actually happens.
- **D4 = actual, D5 = merely possible** — the two owner tables agree on this and disagree only on D0–D3 and D6. So D4=actual, D5=merely possible is safe; any uniqueness claim spanning the ladder is not.
- **The receipt is for admissibility, not actuality** — an `[S]` convention: a record is not admissible in the claim register until a receipt carries it. A performed act is actual whether or not a receipt carries it. Receipt proves the act was performed; it does not make the act actual.
