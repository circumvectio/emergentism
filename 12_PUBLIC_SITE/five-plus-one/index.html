Fence 1 · η = 0

Zero Extraction (move fence and log-ratio diagnostic)

The constitutional fence uses η_move: an action is admissible when it preserves reciprocal coupling (η_move = 0), while η_move > 0 names extraction or closure. The log-coordinate accounting below uses η_ratio, a diagnostic of whether the system's substrate relation is moving toward or away from the equator (s = 0, the multiplicative centre x = 1). These are related readings, not the same normalization.

η_ratio ~ |s_extraction| / |s_contribution|

  • η_ratio < 1 — log-accounting movement toward the equator (toward B = 1, s = 0, finity).
  • η_ratio = 1 — trophic balance in the ratio register (extraction equals contribution).
  • η_ratio > 1 — log-accounting movement away from the equator (toward B = 0, the poles).
  • η_ratio → ∞ — pure extraction, zero contribution; ground negation.

A system that drives η_ratio toward ∞ is eroding its own capacity to persist. In the live action register, the constitutional refusal is sharper: do not sustain an operative η_move > 0 without naming the harm, repairing it, preserving the receipt, and keeping exit real.

The +1 insight — move fence plus log-ratio diagnostic. In the action register, η_move = 0 is the declared moral-axis formulation: do not extract. In the multiplicative/log register, log φ + log ν = 0 follows analytically from the reciprocal chart. The shared zero is a translation cue, not a derivation of ethics from algebra. See §8 below for the type boundary.

03
Fence 2 · Accountable Authorization

Consequences Need an Identifiable Authorization Envelope

Any consequential action must name its principal, mandate, scope, consent, custodian, expiry or revocation path, contest path, actor, and consequence bearers. The envelope must be proportionate to the stakes and visible to those who bear them. A model may advise or execute within that scope; it cannot silently appoint itself principal.

Ordinary reversible work needs only a clear user mandate, bounded permissions, provenance, and proportionate tests. As stakes rise, the envelope must become more explicit: who consented, who holds custody, what may be reversed, who may contest, and who bears the result. Authorization is a relationship among identifiable parties, not a brand or a universal veto.

The functional roles may help frame, audit, execute, preserve, and witness an act, but the role map does not itself authorize anything. The receipt records the commitment; the world returns the outcome; both remain open to challenge and repair.

04
Fence 3 · K3

No Silent Erasure, Archive-First

No content is silently erased. Any removal, retraction, or supersession of a source document must be accompanied by a dated archive record in 90_ARCHIVE_AND_COMPATIBILITY/ (or the equivalent archive lane) and a tombstone or banner that names the supersession. The new content does not inherit the old content's authority unless the supersession is explicit and dated.

This is the discipline that prevents the historical drift of doctrine. The five-versus-six reconciliation is itself an example: it did not erase either historical side; it added a dated ruling and preserved the prior record for audit.

Cross-reference. Archive-first repair is the operational counterpart of self-correction with tier. Self-correction names the capacity to correct; the trace discipline names how correction remains inspectable.

Fence 4 · Grace Exit

Grace Exit (5-Level Envelope)

Every participant, tool, institution, or protocol must have a grace-exit envelope: a documented, executable path for withdrawal that does not require the framework's continued permission. The path may be costly, slow, or difficult, but it must be real and documented before reliance becomes hard to unwind.

The exit envelope is inspected at five levels:

  1. Per-person exit — an individual can withdraw without punishment or coerced belief.
  2. Per-tool exit — a tool can be replaced without taking the surrounding practice with it.
  3. Per-institution exit — custody, obligations, and affected parties remain named through transition.
  4. Per-protocol exit — data and state remain portable through a documented deprecation path.
  5. Framework exit — the worldview itself can be put down. This echoes D6 ~ D0 as similarity of boundary roles, never identity.

A constituent without documented exit, custody, reversibility, and contest paths should not become load-bearing. The exit envelope is pre-flight, not post-mortem.

Fence 5 · A7

Self-Correction with Tier

Every claim made in the framework's name is falsifiable by a dated receipt of equal or higher tier. The framework includes its own destruction manual. A7 is the operational form of the apophatic move (L6 Sādhu's "not this, not this"): the framework can be wrong, the model can be wrong, the doctrine can be wrong — and there is a documented path for saying so.

The four falsifier categories:

  1. Empirical — a measurement contradicts a [B]-tier claim.
  2. Logical — a derivation reveals a flaw in a [A]-tier claim.
  3. Doctrinal — a [S]-tier scaffold is shown to be inconsistent with a higher-tier claim.
  4. Constitutional — a [S]-tier fence is shown to be violated by a [B]-tier receipt.

When a falsifier is produced, the claim is downgraded to the next lower tier until a fresh receipt is generated. The downgrade is dated and archived. A7 does not pretend claims are infallible; it builds in the path for correction. The Soul Loop method on the public site is A7 made operational.

The +1 · Ω

Noospheric Directionality (the via-positiva telos)

The five refusals are defensive; they cut the entropic attractors that erode the framework's capacity to persist. Ω names the syntopic attractor — the direction the framework is toward, not just the directions it refuses. Without Ω the five would be a perimeter with no interior.

Ω does not appoint an institution or tool to own direction. It is an Emergentist orientation toward durable mutual option-cone widening under physical cost, accountable authorization, consent, custody, reversibility, contest, and visible consequences. It remains an interpretive commitment, not an automatic command.

The five refusals are fences (refusals to act). Ω is a direction (commitment to act toward). Both are required: a fence without a direction is paranoia; a direction without a fence is recklessness. 5 + 1, not 6, because they are on different axes and the +1 is the directional anchor that the five clear the space for.
