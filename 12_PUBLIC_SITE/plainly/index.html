01 · Whole and viewpoint

Dasein is the whole that can be. Situated dasein is where some of it becomes actual.

The capital and lowercase forms keep two questions apart.

Dasein is the selected name for the coherent whole: everything that can exist together without contradiction, including actual worlds, possible worlds, finite viewpoints, and the limits of our accounts. It is not another object hiding behind the world.

A situated dasein is one finite bearer here: this body, history, place, set of relationships, means, models, and open decisions. It can encounter only part of the whole, from one actual present.

The dimensional atlas is one way of articulating Dasein. It cannot inventory or exhaust the whole it describes.

02 · What had to become possible

Boundary. Difference. Relation. State. History. Possibility. Nonclosure.

Plain functions come first; the D-names are addresses for revisiting them.

D0 · boundary
A disciplined stop before we smuggle an object, number, or cause into the Ground.
D1 · distinction
Something can be told apart from something else. Identity and difference become expressible.
D2 · relation
Differences can form distance, order, configuration, and constraint.
D3 · state
A probability-bearing description can encode alternatives within a formal physical state.
D4 · actual history
One causal history carries records, bodies, work, loss, and consequences.
D5 · represented possibility
Actual beings can model what is not actual now, compare alternatives, and coordinate around them.
D6 · nonclosure
The map keeps its own edge visible. It permits correction, extension, and Exit.

03 · Possible is not actual

A future can matter now only because something present represents it.

This is the firewall between D5 content and D4 causation.

possible power · Φ₅

What alternatives can be distinguished?

A route not taken, a design not built, a promise, a threat, a plan, and a scientific hypothesis can all be represented without already being actual outcomes.

actual power · V₄

What can be carried into consequence?

Bodies, time, energy, tools, access, authority, institutions, and material capability belong to the actual present.

P_node := min(Φ̂₄, V₄)

After a declared common ordering, this chosen AND-class convention keeps the bottleneck visible: a good map without means cannot act; means without a discriminating map cannot aim. It is not a measurable product or a ranking of people.
