Exit

Axiology · reciprocal bridge · guardianship fence

From what happens to what we can justify—without hiding the bridge.

Emergentism separates directional definitions, a conditional duty inside reciprocal public reason, and a separately chosen guardianship extension. It does not derive an ought from value-free facts or establish stance-independent moral realism.

01 · Two directions

Contribution goes part→whole. Support goes whole→part.

Definitions describe a direction; Justice evaluates the act.

Contribution(a;i,H)

Part to whole

An actual action by agent a changes the declared whole H relative to part i. Moral_E(a) remains a technical alias.

Support(a;H,i)

Whole to part

An actual action or institution changes the options and conditions of part i within whole H. Ethical_E(a) remains a technical alias.

A positive sign is not automatically good. Bearers, payer and beneficiary, baseline, horizon, consent, reversibility, uncertainty, Justice, Exit, and observed consequence remain separate fields.

02 · RCAB-01 · conditional constitutive bridge

If we ask one another for public reasons, arbitrary exemption defeats the practice.

The premise is explicit: reciprocal public justification is being undertaken.

Inside that declared practice, equally situated co-agents cannot coherently demand reason-governed uptake while arbitrarily denying the other bearer visibility, access to the reason, a contest path, correction and repair, or feasible lawful Exit. A relevant difference may justify different treatment, but the difference must be declared and contestable.

What follows inside the practice · [S]

Reciprocal reason-giving carries procedural duties of visibility, contest, correction, and feasible Exit.

What does not follow

No shared personhood, shared consent, merged will, signing power, external authority, complete Justice, cosmic purpose, or stance-independent moral truth is created.

The adoption of reciprocal public reason remains a disclosed interpretive premise `[I]`. Hume’s general boundary remains open.

03 · GEX-01 · separate extension

Worth and care do not depend on reciprocal speech.

Dependent, absent, nonhuman, ecological, and future bearers require a different bridge.

Guardianship is a separately chosen `[I/C]` duty with a named bearer, an actual mandate source, a protected interest, narrow scope, duration, least-restrictive test, disclosed conflicts, review and appeal, revocation, restoration, and Exit. Representation is not consent. Protection is not ownership or blanket power.

Bearer
Who can be helped, burdened, silenced, or irreversibly harmed?
Mandate
Which actual person, law, relationship, or legitimate process authorizes this bounded role?
Least restrictive
Which admissible action preserves the most supported agency and future correction?
Conflict
What does the guardian gain, and who independently reviews capture?
Exit and restoration
How is the role challenged, revoked, replaced, or returned to the bearer?

No AI, framework, title, model output, or declaration makes itself a guardian, signer, or authorizer.

04 · Four meanings of objective

Stable definitions are not yet moral realism.

The qualifier travels with every result.

1 · definition-stable · [S]

Same complete inputs, same classification

The framework’s terms and rules are explicit enough to reproduce.

2 · procedurally reproducible · [B target]

Independent implementations agree

This requires actual tests, not source coherence.

3 · empirically adequate · [C]

The ledger improves contact with consequences

Comparative prediction, hidden-bearer detection, and correction must outperform rivals.

4 · stance-independent · open

Moral truth independent of adoption

RCAB-01 and GEX-01 neither prove nor disprove this metaethical claim.

Framework-objective means decision-stable after bearers, payer and beneficiary, baseline, horizon, measure, Justice, and uncertainty are declared. Missing inputs return UNDERDETERMINED.

05 · How this can lose

The bridge survives only by outperforming simpler accounts.

Conditional coherence is not independent adequacy.

RCAB-01 is killed or narrowed if reciprocity hides its desired conclusion, if its procedural conditions are not constitutive of the declared practice, or if simpler contractualist, discourse-ethical, constructivist, Humean, realist, pluralist, or error-theoretic accounts perform better. GEX-01 is killed or narrowed if it self-authorizes protection, fuses guardian and bearer, hides conflicts, launders harm through collective benefit, or loses to established supported-decision and fiduciary safeguards.
