Read the Framework — The Complete Public Reading Path

This is a route through the current reader, the practice, the research record, and the dimensional scaffold. It is not a claim that the corpus or the world is complete.

Current reader Practice Worldview Record Research Exit

1 · Begin with the current reader

The One-Sitting Reader is the current twelve-chapter public reader. It is a source-bound synthesis, not a replacement for its source owners. Read it first if you want the compact account; use the worldview page when you want the ordinary-language orientation.

Then try the Finity Card on one bounded decision. It asks for a declared horizon, an actual next move, and an observable review point. It does not require assent to the worldview, and its comparative benefit over simpler decision practices remains open.

2 · Keep the status visible

[S] The kernel is an editorial map with seven named owner surfaces. Internal completeness means an owned, coherent, correctly tiered contract; it does not mean external confirmation. [B] Twelve named world-contact gates remain open. A packet, local run, correction record, or receipt documents work; it is not independent evidence that the worldview is true.

Where the public discussion needs a selected cross-factor convention, it names it plainly: P_node := min(Φ̂₄, V₄); historical product ranking is retired. That convention is not a proof that one scalar captures every decision.

3 · Follow the public scaffold

The dimensional sequence is a typed reading aid, not a census of nature. Each rung has its own source, boundary, alternatives, and kill criteria.

  • D0 · ground-limit and the boundary register
  • D1 · arithmetic and the recentered number line
  • D2 · plane and complementarity
  • D3 · sphere and state assignment
  • D4 · causal actuality and selected models
  • D5 · possible power, affected bearers, and bounded action
  • D6 · nonclosure and the limit of the scaffold

For an interactive overview, use Dimensions or the Living Map. The apertures in those views are candidate structures, not established universal transitions.

4 · Test, correct, or leave

The laboratory names open research work and the conditions under which it could narrow, fail, or remain unresolved. Discoveries exposes propositions on trial. The record preserves corrections, failures, and remaining gaps without converting internal review into independent validation.

You can bring a contradiction or bounded evidence through Participate without enrolling, funding, assenting, or transferring authority. You can also stop at any point: the exit is part of the method, not a penalty.

5 · Historical material stays distinguished

Seven frozen generated-library records remain preserved with provenance, not as a second current reader. The current twelve-chapter One-Sitting Reader is the current public route. Older generated material may remain directly addressable as noindex custody, but it is outside this path until its source and public boundary are re-established.

The point of this distinction is not to erase prior work. It is to prevent an old page, an old formula, or a long catalogue from silently acquiring current authority.
