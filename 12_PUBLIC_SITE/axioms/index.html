Layer I · Being

Freedom under constraint.

The foundation is not a warehouse of perfect Forms. It is a wager about structured room to vary, accompanied by an analytic chart and a deliberately bounded plenitude filter.

Ontology · chart · wager
E1

The Stratum

[I/C]

What persists is read through constraint-structured freedoms. Stable forms are patterns across those freedoms—never free-floating Forms, and never degrees of freedom turned into new substances.

Dies if: the proposal cannot distinguish coordinates from physical freedoms, or emergence adds no tested constraint, autonomy, or prediction.

E2

The Reciprocal Grammar

[A]

On the stipulated positive chart, φ=cot(θ/2) and ν=tan(θ/2), so φν=1 by construction. That is mathematics—not an empirical conservation law or an ethic.

Dies if: the arithmetic fails inside its stated domain.

E3

Finity

[A · S/I]

One is self-dual under inversion on the positive reals; on the projective complex line the fixed points are ±1. The Titan triplet {0,1,∞} is a meaningful naming choice, not a forced ontology.

Dies if: its fixed-point or closure arithmetic is wrong. The mythology survives only as interpretation.

E4

The Plenitude Filter

[C]

Inside a specified model, admit possibilities only when they are consistent, coherent, constraint-compatible, and bounded. Admission is not actuality, reachability, stability, emergence, or lived availability.

Dies if: reachability cannot be defined non-circularly or the extra predicates discriminate nothing beyond consistency.

Layer II · Becoming

Aperture, coupling, conditional balance.

The ladder is a selected scaffold. It earns contact one crossing at a time; missing reduction never proves irreducibility.

Emergence · need-both · optimum
E5

The Two Motions

[I/C]

μ₀…μ₄ name five candidate apertures in which an effective freedom becomes available; χ names selection or deployment among available possibilities. D6 is a non-μ exit marker, not another freedom.

Dies or demotes if: the split remains post-hoc, a single-process rival predicts better, or the named saturation/aperture tests fail.

E6

The AND-Law

[S · A/C]

Where every declared factor is necessary, usable potential reaches zero when any necessary factor reaches zero. Product and minimum are both AND-class members; P_node=ΦV is selected, not uniquely forced.

Dies if: need-both fails in the claimed domain. Product-specific claims die if an alternative predicts better.

E7

The Equator

[A · S]

On the reciprocal chart, φ=ν=1 uniquely maximizes the chosen balance score. Transfer to a real system requires complementarity, a constraint, justified normalization, and the relevant symmetry.

Dies in transfer if: a real target violates the named premises or its optimum moves under unequal weights, prices, or constraints.

Layer III · Conduct

Vow, correction, exit.

The is–ought seam stays visible. Geometry cannot choose Justice; a finite holder can choose it, test its consequences, correct it in public, and leave.

Posited · never derived
E8

The Vow

[I]

Do not raise yourself by draining what sustains you. Apply the vow through bearer-complete Justice: boundary, consent, payer, beneficiary, custody, reversibility, contest, consequence, and exit remain visible.

Dies or revises if: the declared non-extraction test systematically produces worse coupled outcomes than an alternative under fair comparison.

E9

The Correction

[S]

Every load-bearing claim carries a tier, upgrade path, kill criterion, and survivors. Fired kills remain public. The rule is an orientation, not a theorem proving its own infallibility.

Dies if: this apparatus resists error or capture no better than a serious rival, or repeatedly fails under external contact.

E10

The Exit

[I/S]

Put the map down. No adherent count certifies truth; no worldview may make belief compulsory. D6~D0 is boundary-role imagery only, never literal identity or attainment.

Dies if: exit is revoked in practice while remaining painted in the text.

The compression

Constraint-structured freedoms, not Forms; need-both, not unique product; vow, not theorem; correction with an exit—till the limits.

The integrated synthesis remains externally unvalidated. Each world-facing claim must still earn its own discriminator and independent replication.
