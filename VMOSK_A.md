# VMOSK-A — Emergentism

This file coordinates work; it creates no theorem, ontology, axiology,
teleology, public claim, or eighth kernel owner. If it conflicts with K-1…K-7,
the named source owner wins and this projection must be repaired. V/M/O are
chosen project commitments; O/K status claims require dated receipts. VMOSK's
biological correspondences are optional `[I]` Rosetta annotations, never
conformance proof or evidence.

Emergentism is the worldview. Finity is its first practical instrument. The
public site is a projection of both and may never become their semantic owner.

## V — Vision

Build an inhabitable, corrigible worldview for finite beings: one that helps
holders distinguish map from territory, possibility from actuality, and
intention from consequence—and that succeeds when they can revise it, reject
it, or put it down.

This is a selected `[I]` direction. Any claim that Emergentism actually
improves judgment, coordination, or lived outcomes remains `[C]` until fair
comparison and world contact support it.

## M — Mission

Maintain an integrated, typed, fallibilist account of reality, knowledge,
action, value, and direction; connect that account to lived practice; test its
stronger claims against serious rivals and world contact; preserve failed
claims and superseded forms; and keep correction, contest, and Exit inside the
system.

The mission has four inseparable duties:

1. keep one visible source owner for every active claim;
2. state formal result, interpretation, conjecture, and public language at
   their actual evidence tiers;
3. turn worldview claims into bounded tests where possible;
4. make every cleared reader projection weaker than or equal to its sources.

## S — Strategies

1. **Source owner first.** Repair K-1…K-7 before cards, books, indexes, or
   public language.
2. **Worldview first; practice immediately.** Identify Emergentism plainly,
   then let a reader use Finity without requiring assent.
3. **Plain before symbolic.** Give ordinary-language distinctions before D,
   μ, Φ/V, Rosetta, or Titan notation; preserve optional routes into depth.
4. **Practice to consequence.** Use one two-face receipt—commitment before the
   move, observed outcome after it—to connect practice to consequence without
   treating either face as authorization or efficacy evidence.
5. **Evidence beside ambition.** Every stronger promise names its tier, rival,
   discriminator, kill criterion, and survivor.
6. **One route grammar.** Keep Practice, Worldview, Research, Library,
   Participate, and Exit stable across current public surfaces.
7. **Archive without broadcasting.** Preserve frozen genealogy while excluding
   it from current search, RAG, sitemap, and service-worker indexes.
8. **Small reversible releases.** Publish cleared dossiers independently;
   local tests, a commit, a push, an immutable deployment, and production DNS
   are separate states.

## K — KPIs and triggers

These signals change the work; they do not validate the worldview.

| Signal | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Current-surface source/card/revision coverage | Target 100%; block a release on an unowned or stale public claim. |
| Barred-claim and type-leak count | Target zero; narrow or withdraw the offending language. |
| Deterministic register and projection hashes | Two runs must match before release. |
| Five-second first-contact comprehension | Test whether a stranger can identify worldview, problem, Finity, next action, and voluntary Exit. |
| Finity-versus-rival performance | Predeclare outcome quality, calibration, bearer visibility, correction, and overhead; retire the brand if it adds no value. |
| Rival visibility at benefit claims | Put the strongest ordinary-method comparator beside the Card; block unqualified novelty, exclusivity, or efficacy language. |
| Independent review and replication | Required per promoted dossier; internal and AI review never substitute. |
| Correction latency | Shorten time from credible contradiction to visible repair or narrowing. |
| Exit visibility and cost | Keep refusal and departure visible, usable, and non-punitive. |

Followers, applause, products, revenue, institutional adoption, and AI agreement
are never truth or adequacy KPIs.
