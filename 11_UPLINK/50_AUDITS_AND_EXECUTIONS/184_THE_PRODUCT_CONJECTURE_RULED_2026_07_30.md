## §2 · The interior is the convicted move — and the assistant's own test was wrong first

**The correction to this session, recorded before the finding it supports.** The prosecution
was ordered with a suggested counterexample, `f(t) = t²`. **It cannot work, and the reason
is structural:**

```text
A = (0.9, 0.2)   B = (0.4, 0.4)
identity      product  A 0.18000  B 0.16000   A≻B
f(t) = t²     product  A 0.03240  B 0.02560   A≻B    RANKING UNCHANGED
```

The product ranks by `log Φ + log V`; under `f` it ranks by `h(u)+h(v)` with
`h(u) = log f(eᵘ)`. Order of sums is preserved for all pairs **iff `h` is affine**, which
forces `f(t) = t^a` exactly. **The product's invariance group is precisely the power family**
— a measure-zero sliver of the reparametrisations the ordinal ruling admits. A power map was
proposed as the test. Every power map is a non-example.

**The working counterexample, verified two ways.** Piecewise linear through
`(0,0), (0.2,0.01), (0.4,0.50), (0.9,0.90), (1,1)` — slopes `0.05, 2.45, 0.80, 1.00`, all
positive, so strictly increasing and admissible:

```text
              product              min
identity      A 0.18  B 0.16  A≻B  A 0.20  B 0.40  B≻A
after f       A 0.009 B 0.25  B≻A  A 0.01  B 0.50  B≻A
              ^^^^^^^^ FLIPPED     ^^^^^^^^ UNCHANGED
```

And smoothly, so it is not a kink artefact: `f(t) = exp(−(−ln t)^{3/2})` flips it again
(`A 0.12543 < B 0.17305`) while `min` holds. **`min` is invariant for a structural reason:
`min(f(x), f(y)) = f(min(x,y))` for every monotone `f`.**

The corpus already carries the short form at `00_CANONICAL_FORMULA_BLOCK.md:169-176` —
*"The product therefore ranks a presentation rather than the evidence."*
