## §1 · Why it is seated as a refusal, not an axiom `[A]`

The declaration is correct in substance and cannot be carried by an axiom. Doc
46 §4, produced earlier in this same session, proves why:

```text
1. any such claim is a sentence
2. a sentence has truth-conditions only relative to a language L
   and a consequence relation ⊨_L
3. therefore L and ⊨_L are prior to it
4. an axiom of ALL axioms would have to be prior to L and ⊨_L
∴ it is not an axiom of all axioms.                                     ∎
```

Sharper still (doc 46 §3): the admissibility of the null state is **`L`-relative**
— true in inclusive logic, false in classical FOL. A proposition whose
truth-value is decided by a prior selection cannot be the ground of that
selection.

The refusal form carries the entire intent and survives the regress, because it
asserts nothing that needs a prior logic to be true. It binds the holder's hand
instead. **This is not a demotion.** In this framework refusals are the deepest
layer by construction — `E8–E10` are explicitly "posited, never derived," and the
5+1 Constitution is where the framework's non-negotiables already live. Seating
the foundation there puts it in the architecturally correct place, and it is the
only place it survives its own audit.
