## 2 · Method and scope

The audit separated five questions that the first draft had collapsed:

1. Does a named source exist?
2. Does it support the attributed bounded proposition?
3. Is the proposed join logically valid?
4. Did an earlier source occupy the assembly or a subsystem?
5. Does any model make a distinctive prediction that survived contact?

The source search was bounded, English-language, web- and database-directed.
It included primary papers, authoritative reference works, preprints, book
records, citation traces, and adversarial phrase searches. It did **not**
complete a multilingual monograph, dissertation, patent, archive, or full
citation-network audit. Absence from this search would not prove novelty.

The scientific test was stricter than priority. A weak or unreviewed source
can block textual priority while providing no scientific confirmation.
