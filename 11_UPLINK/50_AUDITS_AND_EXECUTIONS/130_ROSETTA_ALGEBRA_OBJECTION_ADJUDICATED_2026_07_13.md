## 1 · What the algebra genuinely forces (owner is RIGHT — concede fully)

A real, non-curated algebraic spine exists, owing nothing to any tradition:

  number of named classes depends on whether hyperbolic maps are treated as a
  special loxodromic case. The corpus may use four declared strata, but must
  state that convention. *Genuine mathematics `[A/S]`; not a seven-count.*
- **The 2×2 sign square** on declared coordinates `(ΔΦ,ΔV)` has four exhaustive
  sign cells. This is exhaustive for that typed square, not for every action in
  nature or society.
- **The involution `z↦1/z`** has fixed points `{−1,+1}` and partitions every
  other admissible point into a mirror pair `{z,1/z}`. A displayed centre at
  `+1` is selected; inversion itself does not privilege it over `−1`.

"It's all curated" is therefore **too strong**; the mirror-pair and sign-square
*grammar* is a real object. This is the owner's genuine, conceded win.

## 2 · What it does NOT force (audit HOLDS)

- **Not the count seven.** The declared Möbius-class convention supplies four
  labels, and the declared two-axis sign square supplies four cells. The
  involution supplies only its fixed points and reciprocal two-cycles; it forces
  no finite ladder, ordering, or odd cardinality. Adjoining `0`, `∞`, and both
  fixed points gives the useful invariant set `{−1,0,1,∞}`, but that set is
  selected—not the closure of `{0,∞}`, which already remains `{0,∞}` under
  inversion. **“Seven” = 4 + 3 is therefore a stitch** joining a sign-square
  move vocabulary to selected boundary frames. The construction may be useful;
  it is not forced by one algebraic decomposition.
- **The algebra cannot corroborate the rows** — because by the framework's own order-of-inference it was abduced *from* them: *"This table was not generated by the equation. The equation was inferred from this table"* (Master Rosetta); *"the operators are then inferred as the simplest row-generators that preserve the pattern"* (public /rosetta/). A generator fitted to a pattern is not independent evidence for the pattern (its *validity* survives; its *witness-standing* is nil — not a genetic fallacy). The one doc inverting the order (Selection Methodology, "math first, search traditions for market fit") self-flags "vulnerable to confirmation bias." **Both orderings defeat independence.**
- **Convergence is not independent.** Fit tracks membership in one Indo-European/Semitic-Mediterranean/Neoplatonic-Vedantic diffusion family; the causally-independent traditions (Buddhist 10, Daoist 3–5, Confucian 8, Sub-Saharan, Polynesian, Mesoamerican) are exactly the ones that **fail**.
