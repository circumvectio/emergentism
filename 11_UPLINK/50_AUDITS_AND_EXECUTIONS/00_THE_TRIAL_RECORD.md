# The Trial Record

## K-7 · No revelation without a receipt; no receipt as revelation

This is the seventh kernel surface: the route through which the worldview
remains answerable to what happened. A receipt attests only to what it directly
records; its provenance, completeness, and method remain contestable. It does
not upgrade a mathematical, empirical, metaphysical, ethical, product, or
public-world claim by proximity.

The record is **append-only in meaning**:

- a failed claim remains visible;
- a correction names what broke and what survived;
- a newer ruling may supersede an older one without rewriting it;
- the next numbered entry remains open to the world;
- no founder, model, institution, or majority may certify its own consequence.

Nothing here is a commandment. Check the source, reproduce what can be
reproduced, contest the inference, and add the outcome.

## How to read a receipt

For every claimed result, ask:

1. **Object:** What exact file, commit, dataset, action, or observation was
   examined?
2. **Method:** Which procedure, baseline, rival, mutation, or test was used?
3. **Result:** What directly happened?
4. **Inference:** Which conclusion was drawn, at which evidence tier?
5. **Boundary:** What was not tested or cannot follow?
6. **Consequence:** What owner changed, and what remained deliberately
   untouched?
7. **Reproduction:** Can another reader reproduce the result from the cited
   material?

A signature may establish decision provenance. It cannot transform an invalid
argument into a valid one or a local test into a universal result.

## The four correction postures `[I]`

The companion posture map is
[`The Three Postures`](../../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/00_META/01_THE_THREE_POSTURES.md),
historically named before the fourth posture was added:

```text
deny       — hide or dissolve the gap;
weaponize  — exploit the gap;
confess    — expose and price the gap;
withdraw   — put the wager down.
```

This is a diagnostic vocabulary, not a proof that every worldview occupies
exactly one posture or that confession must win. The record implements confess
and keeps withdraw available.

## Retelling rule `[金]`

Use four verdicts, not one blunt word:

```text
proof survives
proof dies, use survives
claim remains open at lower tier
claim and use both die
```

“Broken” without this distinction is not a verdict. It is information loss.
Equally, preserving a use never licenses the dead proof that once dressed it.

---

## Record invariant

```text
claim -> test -> receipt -> consequence -> owner repair -> new test
```

The loop stays open. A corpus that records only victories is propaganda; a
corpus that records only funerals is structurally pessimistic. Wins and wounds
travel together at their earned tier.

The final test is external contact. The next entry is not “we now believe
more.” It is a dated observation, failed prediction, reproduced discriminator,
unexpected bearer, or consequence returned by a world the framework does not
control.
