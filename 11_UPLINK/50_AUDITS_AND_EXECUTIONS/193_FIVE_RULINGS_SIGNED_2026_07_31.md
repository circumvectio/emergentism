## Q1 · `§5.1` — the irrationals are register-indexed `[S]`

**RULED.** `√2` **is a number** in `ℝ` — standard, unrevised, nothing here touches it.
`√2` **is not a finite word** over `{S, ι}` — that is `G1`, a theorem. **Neither sentence
may be published without the other in the same proximity window** the site already enforces
on the Titan infix.

Base-side phrasing is *"a non-halting word whose nest denotes a limit"* — never *"is a
limit"*, never *"is a word"*. Bare *"not a number"* is **banned**.

**The cost, accepted.** The owner's own most quotable sentence — *"0 and ∞ are not numbers
and 1 is the only number"* — can never again be published bare. It becomes register-indexed
and stops being a headline. `53_THE_NUMBER_CHART.md:61` re-tiers: `[A]` covers only *not a
finite word*; "limit" drops to `[S]`.

**Kill.** Exhibit a finite word over `{S, ι}` with irrational value. Or a fresh-reader test
where correctly register-tagged copy still reads as *"they say √2 isn't a number"* — then
the register-indexing failed as communication and the plain reading wins.

**Council: 4–0.**

## Q2 · `G-0` — routing, not primacy `[S]`

**RULED.** `G-0` was already decided on 2026-07-29 (exit B-ii, the fourth posit `B4`). What
is signed here is the consequence that never propagated: **neither base is seated over the
other.**

- arithmetic, `D1`, reachability → `05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/52_THE_GENERATIVE_BASE.md`
- Titan-identity questions → `00_THE_FOUNDATION.md` §2 and doc 45

The phrase *"the base"*, unqualified, is **banned corpus-wide**. `KSC-28`'s `S1 sphere
primacy` is restated as a **chart selection**, not primacy. Each chart carries its stated
deficit at the point of use: the generative base **cannot reach `−1`**; §2's identity was
harvested from notation until `B4` was posited.

**The cost, accepted.** The corpus forfeits a one-sentence answer to *"what does this stand
on?"* — which is the stated reason `00_THE_FOUNDATION.md` exists. **This ruling concedes
there are two places.**

**Kill.** Derive `{1, S, ι}` from `B1`–`B4`, or the converse, without routing through
`DF-15`'s forbidden transfer. Then one base does own the seat.

**Council: 3–1**, custody dissenting for the generative base. Dissent preserved in full in
`00_WORK_IN_PROGRESS/README.md`.

## Q4 · The undeclared routes `[S]`

**RULED.** **Passing the parity contract is necessary, not sufficient.** The sixteen
patterns are a *prohibition* filter — they establish a page does not say forbidden things.
Declaration is the corpus **warranting** what it does say.

The grounding is this corpus's own failure of 2026-07-30: `12_PUBLIC_SITE/suda/index.html`
passes all sixteen patterns and still published an unsourced quantitative adverse judgment
about the work of a named living person. **A coherence test is not a capability test.**

- `/amrita/` `/egg/` `/riemann/` `/suda/` — pass the contract → **DECLARED-PROVISIONAL**:
  reachable, indexable, registered, **not warranted**. Not current.
- `/offline/` → **infrastructure**. A service-worker fallback carries no doctrine and must
  not acquire standing by being frozen.
- `/historical-boundary/` → **unchanged**. Already governed by `withheld-routes.json`;
  freezing it double-governs one artifact under two registries that can drift.
- the remainder → **frozen pending disposition** — not "frozen because non-conformant".
  For `/halahala/` the non-conformance *is* the disclosure: it is the page that publishes
  the error-rate denominator.

**Correction adopted:** the population is **~30, not 40**. Ten were already declared
withheld; the earlier count read a second declaration registry as absence.

**Sequencing, binding.** Headers first, `sitemap.xml` second. **Never the reverse.**

**Council: 3–0** on the rule, **2–1** on the disposition, formal dissenting.

## Q6 · The frozen library stays `noindex, follow` `[S]`

**RULED.** Keep it — **and publish the boundary as a stated editorial policy.** The second
clause is not decoration; without it, `noindex` reads as concealment.

**Reason.** The frozen library demonstrably carries claims now known false and not yet
repaired. *"Every displacement from the equator costs energy"* was published at
`[S] Established` and was **false across fifteen pages** until 2026-07-30, while the correct
statement sat machine-checked in the repository and did not propagate. Indexing ~301 such
pages makes search engines the distribution channel for retracted content, and
retraction-after-indexing is not symmetric with never-indexing.

**The cost, accepted.** A launch surfaces roughly 39 pages. The formal system — the most
checkable material the corpus owns — stays out of search.

**Council: 3–1**, reader dissenting: *"my lens is checkability, and this hides the checkable
material. Open the roots whose claims are reproducible by a named script — `formal`,
`papers`, `canon` — and keep the interpretive roots closed."*

**The dissent is not dismissed. It is deferred to evidence:** the page-by-page audit named
in the kill. If the frozen library carries no unrepaired claim contradicted by a later
ruling, the safety ground vanishes and the dissent wins on reach alone.
