# Receipt 173 — The V-axis audit

## Request

Owner instruction, 2026-07-29: run a full logic audit through **our** lens —
the premises we hold and the world does not — rather than through the external
standard.

## Finding 0 — the rescue this audit cannot supply `[B]`

The expected shape of such a request is: *the outside auditor used the wrong
standard; re-judged by our premises, much of the damage is void.*

**That rescue is unavailable, and receipt 126 forecloses it in its own text.**

r126 §18 states the 66 failures were found **"on the framework's own coherence
terms."** §61 calls them **"66 own-currency failures."** The formal audit was
already an internal-lens audit. It did not import an alien standard of proof and
then score us against it; it took our declared tiers, our declared systems, and
our declared hypotheses, and found the machine inconsistent with itself in 66
places.

Therefore: **no claim is restored by this audit.** Not one. Anyone reading this
receipt hoping the graves reopen through a friendlier lens should stop here. The
lens was already ours.

## Finding 1 — the gap r126 named and nobody has filled `[S]`

r126 §61 also records, precisely:

> "Formal logic is a **Φ-instrument** — it measures validity and consistency.
> Lens-vs-law is a **V-question** — contact with the world. By the framework's
> own `P = Φ × V`, a Φ-instrument cannot answer a V-question."

That is the correct diagnosis, and it names an obligation. r126 measured **Φ**
and reported cracks. **No audit has ever measured V.**

Since r126, **54 receipt files** carrying **44 distinct receipt numbers** have
been filed `[B]`. Every one of them is a further Φ-measurement or a Φ-repair.
The kernel's own keystone says the two factors are jointly necessary, and
exactly one of them has ever been instrumented.

This receipt is the first V-axis audit. Its instruments are taken only from our
own kernel — instruments the external analytic lens does not possess and could
never have applied.

## V-1 · Soul Loop closure is zero `[B]`

Counted in `11_UPLINK/50_AUDITS_AND_EXECUTIONS/` on 2026-07-29:

- **155** receipt files
- **2** mention an outcome receipt at all
- **8** mention independent replication — every instance as a *requirement or
  request*, never as a returned result

r158 states it without evasion: *"This is publication and test launch, not
external review"* and *"No data were collected and no preregistration was
accepted by this act."*

The Soul Loop is `territory → model → alternatives → selection → commitment
receipt q_t → outcome receipt r_(t+1) → update`. The corpus has produced 155
instances of `q` and zero instances of `r` from any party outside itself.

**Under our own loop, not one cycle has ever closed.** The corpus runs
`q_t → q_(t+1) → q_(t+2)` — model to model. The Lived Compass §2 names this
exact object: *"A reinforcing loop may amplify accuracy or delusion."* The loop
cannot tell which, because `r` is the only term that discriminates, and `r` is
absent.

## V-2 · The world-contact line has been open while 33 internal receipts were filed `[B]`

K-7 §5 reserves `№-next` for contact: *"the next entry is written by contact
with the world, not by us."* It reads `______ (open — awaiting the world)` and
has since 2026-07-20.

**33 receipts have been written since that date.** All internal.

This is not dishonesty; the record says what it is. It is something our lens can
see and the external lens cannot: under `E9`, a falsifier that is never
*forbidden* but also never *approached* produces the same operational result as
Posture I. Deferral is denial's quiet form. The distinction between "we refuse
the test" and "we have written 33 documents instead of taking the test" is
invisible in the text and identical in the world.

## V-3 · The receipts are D4 tokens whose content is D5 `[S]`

`KSC-03`: D4 is causal actuality; D5 is merely-possible content represented by
actual tokens.

Writing a receipt *is* a D4 event. But the event each receipt records is
"a file was edited," "an owner was routed," "a tier was corrected." Those are
D4 events **inside the corpus**, not D4 transitions **in the world the corpus is
about**.

By our own typing the corpus has been booking **inscription** as **contact**.
The receipt discipline is real and rigorous and it has been measuring the wrong
register — it certifies that we said something carefully, never that anything
answered. A commitment receipt about an editorial act is a D4 token whose entire
content is D5.

## V-4 · `Ω` is plausibly self-violated by volume `[I]`

Refusal +1: prefer actions that preserve or widen the durable option cones of
declared bearers. The Kernel Index sets a **stranger test**: Door plus K-1…K-7,
one sitting, seven distinctions preserved.

Corpus state `[B]`: **2,239 tracked markdown files**, 3,205 tracked files, 402
public HTML pages. The Settled Canon Registry alone is 28 KB; the Door 30 KB.

A stranger's option cone with respect to this corpus is the set of readings they
can actually reach. Past some volume that cone *narrows* — not because the
reader is refused, but because the cost of entry exceeds a finite life's
allocation for one worldview. **`Ω` can be violated by accretion, with every
individual document blameless.** No external auditor would flag this; it is not
a logical defect. Our own lens flags it because `Ω` is one of our instruments
and volume is one of its arguments.

## V-5 · The corpus declares no `η_domain` for itself `[S]`

Refusal 1 requires every `η_domain` to name bearers, payer, beneficiary,
baseline, horizon, consent, reversibility, and regenerative capacity. The
corpus enforces this on every claim it contains.

**It has never declared one for its own production.** Who pays for 2,239 files?
The answer is a specific finite quantity of one person's `V₄` — hours, attention,
and the alternatives foreclosed by spending them here. That transfer is
undeclared, its horizon unnamed, its regenerative capacity untested.

This is not an accusation of extraction; the payer and beneficiary may well be
the same person, freely. It is the observation that **the corpus demands of
every claim an accounting it does not supply about itself** — and Refusal 1's own
living test is that it fails "if a material bearer disappears from the declared
accounting."

## V-6 · The corpus does not pass `BOUNDED_M` `[S]`

`E4`: `ADMITTED_M(S) ⟺ CONSISTENT ∧ COHERENT ∧ CONSTRAINT_COMPATIBLE ∧ BOUNDED`.

`BOUNDED_M` means inside declared domain and resource bounds. The corpus
declares no terminal size, no completion condition, and no resource bound; its

Applied reflexively, **the corpus is not ADMITTED by its own filter.** It is
`CONSISTENT` (mostly), `COHERENT` (r126 says: 66 exceptions), and unbounded.

## V-7 · Exit is textually guaranteed and never tested `[I]`

`E10` and Posture IV: the door is marked on the inside; *"if you cannot leave,

## What survives, and what this audit refuses to say

**Survives, undamaged:** every `FORMALLY-VALID` result in the Claim Status
Register. `FV-01…FV-18` are `[A]`/`[S]` inside their hypotheses and no
V-measurement touches them. The tier discipline itself survives — it is the
reason this audit could be run at all.

**This audit does not say** the worldview is false. `V≈0` is not evidence
against a claim; it is the absence of evidence either way, which is precisely
what the corpus has said about itself all along. The finding is narrower and
sharper: *the framework has been scoring itself on the axis it can control and
has never instrumented the axis that binds.*

**This audit does not say** the work was wasted. `Φ` had to be built before `V`
could be tested; an untyped corpus cannot be falsified. That case is genuinely
strong. It expires, though — and the expiry is measurable: `Φ` work stopped
being the binding constraint no later than r126, and 54 receipt files have been
filed since.
