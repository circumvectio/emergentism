# Active citation custody ratchet

## Outcome

The declared active/current citation scope now binds receipt and packet targets
by exact physical filename or repository-relative path. Every physically reused
numeric prefix remains unsafe when bare. This is local identity custody only: it
does not ratify any dated record, strengthen evidence, rewrite historical files,
publish a surface, deploy a site, or satisfy world contact.

The two canonical machine owners are bound here:

```text
active_receipt_citation_registry_canonical_sha256: 021fc5a1923c7a9504d5ae2324a19440c69dca4142f69b6e124408a478ebc73a
contact_limited_state_canonical_sha256: ed96c0c58ecc8304e853c201c11e5eb36ce4bdca7464be2813ee4f7c28138e00
```

Each digest is SHA-256 over canonical UTF-8 JSON with sorted object keys,
compact separators, and non-ASCII characters retained. A future baseline change
requires a new dated receipt path; this file may not be rewritten after commit.

## 1 · Physical namespace and source repair

The live receipt lanes contain 307 citable Markdown targets under 185 numeric
prefixes. Ninety-seven prefixes are physically reused and remain unsafe bare;
the legacy 91-prefix heuristic remains only a status-word diagnostic.

The source repair changed 406 active/provisional owner locators: 379
source-adjudication repairs, 18 machine-owner repairs, and nine final
current/provisional public and ledger repairs. Two generated PWA outputs were
then synchronized from their source generator. Collective ranges and plural
arcs remain collective, the seven-file purification bundle retains its directory
identity, and packet shorthand remains typed to the session-packet lane.

No historical receipt filename was renamed. Dated receipt bodies, handoffs,
archives, books, compatibility surfaces, and frozen or withheld public output
outside active delivery remain report-only.

## 2 · Registry scope

The final machine registry contains 663 custody rows: 24 typed locators and 639
exact target tokens across 123 fixed active owners. Its current/provisional
public closure contains
67 citation-scannable text dependencies. The broad discovery pass additionally
rejects a new active owner that is not in the fixed set.

The scope cut is explicit in the registry. Generated topology inventories are
not prose citations and remain byte-checked by their own compiler; compiler
fixtures are mutation language; vendored libraries are third-party code; binary
public dependencies are delivered but not citation-scannable. Direct lane route
cards, public manifests, modules, workers, service workers, precache entries,
and imported worker scripts remain active text scope.

The registry observed 5,580 report-only historical typed locators. That count is
diagnostic, not a claim that historical prose was rewritten or independently
adjudicated. Exact active custody does not make a bare numeric prefix safe.

## 3 · Fail-closed controls

The checker requires a complete filename token boundary, same-line binding for
typed locators, exact lane selection for packet language, one full path when a
basename maps to multiple physical targets, and a fixed source-set digest.
Content hashes and source/line ordinals bind meaning without pretending that
line numbers are permanent identity.

Twenty-three focused mutation controls cover wrong same-prefix substitutions,
exact-only swaps, line and ledger shielding, filename-extension spoofs,
packet-lane crossing, candidate disappearance, malformed or duplicate-key JSON,
source-set substitution, diagnostic-context drift, public dependency hiding,
and immutable receipt digest custody. Independent adversarial replay also
blocked six custom bypasses spanning multi-argument worker imports, service-
worker precache entries, worker dependencies, active route cards, and spoofed
filename suffixes.

No push, merge, deployment, publication, DNS change, email, participant or
reviewer contact, contract, money movement, legal signature, or external-system
mutation is performed by this receipt.

## Kill criterion

This receipt fails if either canonical digest differs, a reused prefix is called
safe while more than one physical target exists, an active locator can change
target without registry drift, a newly active text owner escapes discovery, a
report-only body is silently rewritten as cleanup, or a local gate is presented
as receipt truth or world evidence.
