## 4 · Mathematical refutations and retained facts

### 4.1 Retain as scoped mathematics

- `0^y=0` for real `y>0`. Separately, `0^0=1` is a useful combinatorial
  convention because there is one function from the empty set to itself;
  `0^0` remains an indeterminate limiting form.
- The customary elementary real-calculus list is
  `0/0, ∞/∞, 0·∞, ∞−∞, 0^0, 1^∞, ∞^0`. These are limiting forms, not real-number
  equations.
- On a declared nontrivial ring or field, `x↦0x` is a constant non-injective
  map and has no inverse. It is not the unique information-erasing operation.
- For fixed `N>0`, `N/x→+∞` as `x→0+`. With `t→∞`,
  `(N/t)·t=N`; projecting all such paths to `(0+,+∞)` erases `N` by
  construction.
- Pure qubit rays form `ℂP¹`, identifiable with the Riemann sphere. In a fixed
  computational basis, `|ρ₀₁|=½sinθ`; this is basis-dependent.
- Under the explicitly normalized chordal metric
  `d(z,w)=2|z−w|/√((1+|z|²)(1+|w|²))`, the unit circle is equidistant from
  `0` and `∞`, with `d(1,0)=d(1,∞)=√2`.
- Inversion `z↦1/z` exchanges `0` and `∞`, has fixed points `±1`, and selects
  `1` uniquely on the positive real axis.

### 4.2 Strike or retype

- Strike unrestricted `0^y=0`.
- Strike “the seven forms are the complete Titan boundary.”
- Strike “multiplication by zero uniquely erases all information.”
- Strike the unqualified two-sided `N/x→∞` statement.
- Strike `0·∞=N`; replace it with path-dependent limit realizations.
- Strike “mathematics proves the strong-emergence class non-empty” from this
  example.
- Strike `π` as a demonstrated Bedau-class emergent phenomenon.
- Retype `ℂP¹ ≅` Riemann sphere as a shared mathematical model, never shared
  physical substrate.
- Keep qubit phase `ϕ_Q` distinct from the corpus's `φ` variable.
- Strike `√(0·∞)` as a geometric mean. Inversion symmetry, not multiplication,
  selects the unit.
- Strike “unstable separatrix” until a dynamical map and stability criterion
  are named.
- Retype `|+⟩`: zero von Neumann entropy as a pure state; maximal one-bit
  outcome entropy only for measurement in the selected computational basis.

These corrections preserve the insight that endpoint descriptions can lose
path information while refusing the invalid step from information loss to
ontological emergence.

## 5 · W19 physics fences

### 5.1 Gauge dilemma

W19 must escape two opposite failures:

- If “freedom” means any physical degree of freedom, the universal claim is
  prima facie false: degrees of freedom need not possess unique opposing
  forces.
- If “freedom” means local gauge freedom, the connection enters through the
  chosen covariant construction and the claim risks becoming analytic rather
  than an empirical law of emergence.

A viable third form must define register-freedom independently, predict a
compensator before observing it, and state what would count as its absence.

### 5.2 D5 admission test

“Verification is a gauge field” is rejected unless W19 supplies:

1. a base space and state/fibre spaces;
2. a non-trivial local transformation group and equivalence relation;
3. a connection with a transformation law;
4. curvature or field strength;
5. dynamics/action and coupling;
6. gauge-invariant observables; and
7. a held-out prediction that ordinary feedback/error-correction does not
   make equally well.

Until then, witness fraction is a scalar control variable and verification is
a controller, maintenance, or error-correction practice `[I/C]`.

### 5.3 Force mapping

The serial-force conjecture is a **24-permutation stress test**. Agreement with
the proposer's ordering is never scored as truth. Each assignment must recover
the native structures of QCD, electroweak theory, electromagnetism, and gravity.
Cross-architecture agreement is a robustness/disagreement diagnostic only;
held-out intervention and prediction are truth-discriminating.
