# Dharma Yuddha — future civic inquiry HOLD

> [!CAUTION]
> **This is an owner-direction HOLD, not a movement declaration.** The owner has
> proposed a future **Dharma Yuddha** movement guided by Emergentism. The
> canonical source permits no such present claim: it frames Dharma Yuddha as
> optional comparative vocabulary for accountable, proportionate, nonviolent
> resistance under a chosen Justice premise, and it authorizes no war.

## Direction preserved `[D]`

The live question is whether a separately owned, voluntary civic or association
inquiry could someday use *Dharma Yuddha* as a **revocable, optional** vocabulary
for evidence, restraint, consent, repair, dissent, and practical exit. That is
not yet a movement, organisation, membership body, recruitment project, office,
leader, campaign, or authority source.

“Guided by Emergentism” can mean only a one-way, versioned source reference to
the canonical Dharma Yuddha text. It cannot mean a sacred command, a chain of
authority, a claim to represent a religious tradition, an identity, or a right
to override evidence, lawful authority, or affected bearers. The plain
constitutional protocol remains usable by anyone who declines the Dharma
vocabulary.

## Invariants `[S]`

Any later inquiry must retain the canonical boundaries:

- no physical-force authorization, targeting, tactical instruction, or
  emergency exception;
- no human rank, caste assignment, symbolic-role translation, or leader
  entitlement;
- consent, lawful authority, necessity, proportionality, visible bearers,
  serious restraint/withdrawal rivals, repair, contest, and practical exit;
- the smallest honest reversible step, with separate commitment and outcome
  receipts; and
- the right to reject the vocabulary without loss of standing.

The label must be retired if it increases coercion, status claims, suppressed
dissent, harm, identity pressure, recruitment, sacred command, or authority or
evidence bypass.

## Source separation `[S/I]`

| Surface | What it may own | What it must not become through this HOLD |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergentism** | The canonical Dharma Yuddha source and its non-authorizing comparative vocabulary. | A venture premise, operational command, political or legal authority, or proof of a movement. |
| **Future civic/association surface** | Only if separately adopted: its own voluntary scope, protections, governance, communications/data custody, and change process. | An automatic doctrine projection, membership hierarchy, recruitment funnel, or force-authorizing body. |
| **Evolutionary.Network** | Its separate staged durable-electronics direction and contact-deferred functional inquiry. | The movement’s commerce, funding, product, customer funnel, membership record, or governance shortcut. |

The existing seven-functional-seat interview program may remain a separate,
contact-deferred inquiry. It cannot establish a Dharma Yuddha movement, recruit
participants, or turn a functional seat into a human classification or authority
record.

## No-start gate `[D/I]`

No public, participatory, organisational, or consequential step may begin from
this HOLD. Before a future inquiry is even staged, a responsible Emergentism
owner must make a separate source-scope disposition. A later project would then
need its own accountable human owner, exact scope and jurisdiction, voluntary
participation and consent path, dissent/complaint/repair/exit process, data and
communications custody, lawful-authority analysis for each consequence, and a
reviewable change process.

Nothing in this HOLD supplies those conditions.

## Successor decision

The responsible source owner may later choose one of three bounded dispositions:

1. **HOLD** — retain this direction without a project or activity.
2. **STAGE** — authorize a separate, source-separated inquiry with its own
   scope, owner, protections, and no-start gates.
3. **REFUSE** — retire the proposed use while preserving this record as
   provenance.

Until a later disposition lands, the accurate state is: **an owner direction
under inquiry; no Dharma Yuddha movement exists.**
