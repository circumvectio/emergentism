## Justice and collective boundary

`η` is an extraction diagnostic inside the full Justice envelope. It does not,
by itself, classify every moral case. Strict Syntropic Dyadism requires durable
potential to rise for both individual and whole; aggregate gain cannot launder
the destruction of either side. Consequential acts require accountable
authorization with principal, mandate, scope, consent, custody, expiry or
revocation, contest path, actor, and consequence bearer. Payer and beneficiary
are separate Justice-boundary fields; they are not silently folded into the
authorization record.

An Egregoreotype is a candidate only where a persistent shared trace survives
carrier turnover, intervention on the trace measurably reweights later
selection, recurrent objective-like bias appears, and substrate costs remain
visible. Stigmergy is one possible mechanism. None of this presumes
consciousness or personhood.
