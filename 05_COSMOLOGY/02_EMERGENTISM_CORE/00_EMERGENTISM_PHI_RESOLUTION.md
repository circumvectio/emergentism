# THE PHI RESOLUTION: KINTSUGI TOMBSTONE

The former resolution at this path made three incompatible moves load-bearing:

1. it treated an aggregate rise in balance or node score as a moral direction;
2. it settled an idealist interpretation by instruction rather than evidence;
3. it required agents to preserve that interpretation as an execution rule.

Those moves are superseded. The preserved pre-tombstone document remains at
[`90_ARCHIVE/pure_emergentism_boundary_2026_07_20/05_COSMOLOGY/02_EMERGENTISM_CORE/00_EMERGENTISM_PHI_RESOLUTION.md`](../../90_ARCHIVE/pure_emergentism_boundary_2026_07_20/05_COSMOLOGY/02_EMERGENTISM_CORE/00_EMERGENTISM_PHI_RESOLUTION.md).

## Current settlement

- D4 is causal actuality, embodied means, performed action, and record.
- D5 is counterfactual possibility: modeled futures, represented rankings, and
  selection candidates. Actual modeling, ranking, and selection events are D4.
- A represented future may reweight present selection through an agent’s model;
  this requires no physical signal from the future.
- D6 opens no new positive freedom. Its return toward D0 is interpretive
  similarity, not identity.
- First-person disclosure may inform a person’s interpretation. It does not
  settle public ontology or compel an agent to preserve idealism.
- Aggregate scores may report balance, output, or distribution. They never
  issue a moral verdict.

## Value settlement

For an individual `i`, sustaining whole `H`, and Justice predicate `J`:

```text
Moral(a)     iff delta W_H > 0 and delta W_i >= 0 and J(a;i,H)
Ethical(a)   iff delta W_i > 0 and delta W_H >= 0 and J(a;i,H)
Syntropic(a) iff delta W_i > 0 and delta W_H > 0 and J(a;i,H)
```

Every materially affected bearer remains visible. A positive total cannot
compensate for a destroyed individual or sustaining whole. Voluntary sacrifice
is a distinct costly class: it requires informed consent, authorization,
custody, reversibility where possible, payer and beneficiary disclosure, and an
exit or contest path. It is never relabeled strict syntropy and never demanded
as proof of goodness.
