# Finity_L C1 — Kill verdict

> **The clean death.** The theorem `[A]` is elementary and survives. The
> conjecture `[C]` is killed: its structural requirement — a real biological
> pair whose bare product `ab = const` is conserved by active regulation — is
> unmet by any system in eight searched domains. The conjecture is not false;
> it is **vacuously untestable**: the hypothesis it attaches to has no known
> instance.

## The structural requirement, restated

`50_FINITY_L` §4, Claim `[C]`: *"Living systems regulate to `a = b`. Where a
real biological pair is genuinely reciprocal — `ab` conserved to measurement
precision — the observed setpoint will be the balance point."*

The weak joint: *"no real biological pair has been shown to satisfy
`ab = const`."* This kill verifies that joint across eight domains and finds
it unbroken.

## The pattern, stated plainly

**Biological regulation conserves sums, ratios, and single variables — not
products of pairs.**

- **Sums/totals:** mass balance, charge conservation, cell ion content. The
  pump-leak model conserves total Na⁺ (in + out = const).
- **Ratios:** ventilation/perfusion, filtration fraction. The system holds a
  ratio near a setpoint.
- **Single variables:** temperature, pH, glucose, cortisol, MAP, membrane
  potential. One quantity is the regulated variable; everything else adjusts
  to serve it.

**A conserved product of two regulated quantities — `ab = const` where both
`a` and `b` are actively controlled and their product is what stays fixed —
appears nowhere in eight searched domains of physiology and ecology.** The
only conserved product in biology (Hill's equation) is shifted —
`(F+a)(V+b) = const`, not `FV = const` — and its optimum is not at the
balance point.

## The honest limitation

*"Not found in eight searched domains"* is not *"proven nonexistent."* The
search was broad (cardiovascular, muscle, respiratory, endocrine, renal,
membrane, two general surveys) but not exhaustive. A conserved-product pair
could exist in:

- Plant physiology (stomatal conductance × CO₂ assimilation — but this is a
  ratio, WUE)
- Ecological allometry (offspring number × offspring size — but this is a
  trade-off, not a conserved product)
- Bacterial chemotaxis ( CheA × CheY phosphorylation — but this is a ratio)
- A theoretical model not yet published

The pattern across eight domains is strong enough to kill the conjecture
**as stated** — "where a real biological pair is genuinely reciprocal, `ab`
conserved to measurement precision" — because the clause "where a real
biological pair is genuinely reciprocal" has no known referent. But the
search cannot prove the nonexistence of all possible pairs in all possible
systems.

## What this costs and what it buys

**Costs:** one `[C]` conjecture. The corpus's "single live shot at a result
that is not a reading" (as the day's closing synthesis named it) has been
taken and missed. F3 remains open with a narrower (and steeper) requirement.

**Buys:** honesty. The conjecture is no longer an untested claim that reads
as if it might be true. It is a tested claim whose structural requirement
is unmet. The corpus can stop citing `Finity_L` as the path to `V` and start
looking for a different one. **A clean death is worth more than a living
conjecture whose antecedent has no instance.**
