## II. THE EMPIRICAL CORE — SEVEN ESTABLISHED MECHANISMS

### II.a Darwin's Crack [S]

In *The Descent of Man* (1871), Darwin had to introduce sexual selection as a **second force**, separate from natural selection, because natural selection alone predicted the elimination of peacock-tail-style traits. Heavy, conspicuous, metabolically costly traits manifestly *reduce* survival viability — yet they persist and elaborate. Darwin's resolution: differential reproductive success arising from competition for mates, not environmental survival. Two modes:

- **Intersexual** — choice by females (the aesthetic faculty as directional force)
- **Intrasexual** — contests among males (combat, display-of-dominance)

Crucially Darwin treated female choice as *non-random discrimination*. This is a native empirical finding that the framework later reads at `[I]`; it is not an entry of F5 into the empirical record. Darwin and Wallace disagreed about its interpretation; the evidence concerns sexual-selection mechanisms.

### II.b Fisher's Runaway [S]

Fisher (1915; formalised 1930) proved the autocatalytic structure: if a heritable female preference P becomes genetically correlated with a heritable male trait T (because choosy females mate with showy males, producing daughters who prefer *and* sons who display), then dT/dt and dP/dt are positively coupled. The system runs away exponentially until V-cost caps it. The framework may compare this native recursion to its F5 reading at `[I]`; no F5 equation or vital mechanism follows.

### II.c Zahavi's Handicap [S]

Zahavi (1975); ESS proof by Grafen (1990). Costly signals are honest because cost cannot be faked. Only a high-quality male can pay the V-tax of a long tail, loud call, carotenoid plumage, or risky display-from-exposed-perch and still survive. Cheap signals admit mimicry; expensive ones do not. **In this `[I]` translation only,** handicap may be compared to a ν-cost paid to certify φ-quality; the signal remains a native biological signal, not an F5 receipt or causal loop.

### II.d Hamilton-Zuk [S]

Hamilton & Zuk (1982). Bright plumage and elaborate display correlate cross-specifically with parasite load. Pathogens function as a φ-discriminator: they degrade signal in low-quality males, allowing females to read coherence through the parasite-generated S/N ratio. **The display is a real-time φ-readout filtered through biotic noise.**

### II.e The Lek Paradox and Its Red Queen Resolution [B/I]

If females uniformly prefer the same males, additive genetic variance for the preferred trait should be exhausted within generations (Borgia 1979; Kirkpatrick & Ryan 1991). Yet variance persists. Resolution (Hamilton 1980; Eshel & Hamilton 1984): host-parasite co-evolution continuously regenerates variance because the optimal genotype shifts every generation. **Selection never catches its tail; the substrate keeps producing fresh φ-differentials for choice to operate on.** The Red Queen of [Doc 36](36_THE_DIMENSIONAL_TROPHIC_CASCADE.md) is here at the genome's level.

### II.f Dyadic Simultaneity [S]

Intersexual choice and intrasexual combat operate simultaneously, often on the same trait — antlers function in both fights and assessments (Andersson 1994). The dyad is irreducible: φ-display to the chooser and ν-dominance over rivals are co-instantiated in a single morphological investment.

### II.g Modern Layered Synthesis (post-2000) [S]

- **Mate-choice copying** (Dugatkin 1992; Witte 2006): preference itself becomes socially transmitted — a memetic layer riding the genetic
- **Sensory bias / sensory exploitation** (Ryan 1990; Endler 1992): traits evolve to exploit pre-existing perceptual tuning
- **Sexual conflict** (Parker 1979; Arnqvist & Rowe 2005): male and female fitness optima diverge, generating antagonistic co-evolution
- **Sexually antagonistic selection** (Chippindale et al. 2001): same allele beneficial in one sex, costly in the other

[C] The progression — handicap → parasite → copying → conflict → intra-genomic — is a cross-scale comparison, not selection climbing a Replicator Stack or a derivation of D-register order. The listed mechanisms may be compared to candidate coherence/viability evaluations, but neither move nor cause one another through the framework.
