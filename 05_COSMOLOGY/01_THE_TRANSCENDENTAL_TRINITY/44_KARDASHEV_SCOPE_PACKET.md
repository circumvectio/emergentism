# Kardashev Scope Packet

No current Emergentism owner supports a Kardashev crosswalk. If the scale is
cited, it is only externally sourced descriptive vocabulary about energy use.
It does not measure wisdom, Justice, consciousness, survival probability,
moral rank, right to expand or entitlement to govern.

A future crosswalk must predeclare:

- the external definition and data source;
- unit of analysis and uncertainty;
- energy, ecological and security costs assigned to actual bearers;
- comparator metrics for technology, resilience and institutional capacity;
- a held-out prediction that the label adds beyond those comparators; and
- a kill criterion and independent replication route.

Retire the crosswalk if energy-use labels add no preregistered explanatory or
predictive value beyond ordinary technological, ecological, institutional and
security measures. The survivor is historical descriptive vocabulary.

The Great Filter cannot be used as existential pressure to relax these gates.
