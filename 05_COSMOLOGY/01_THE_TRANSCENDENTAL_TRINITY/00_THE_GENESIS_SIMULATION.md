## Purpose

This file specifies a visual explanation of the selected D0–D6 scaffold. It is
an interpretive teaching surface `[D/I]`, not evidence for a sevenfold ontology,
physical retrocausality, quantum collapse, or strong emergence.

## Required scenes

### 1. Selected scaffold

Show `D0…D6` as labeled registers, not spatial dimensions. Titan glyphs may
appear as an optional legend of selected boundary roles; they must never act as
causal particles or ordinary operands.

### 2. Candidate crossings

Show exactly `μ₀…μ₅`. Selecting a crossing must expose five fields: saturated
register, newly observed freedom, lower-register recovery, evidence/reduction
status, and prediction/kill criterion. The default reduction status is
`currently_unreduced`, not "irreducible."

### 3. Actual and possible

Use solid lines for D4 actual relations and dotted lines for D5 possible
alternatives:

```text

                                  │ selection + means + authorization

```

The model token and selection event are actual D4 carriers. Only their
counterfactual content is D5.
