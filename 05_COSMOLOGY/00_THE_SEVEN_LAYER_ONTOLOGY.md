# The Seven-Layer Ontology of Reality — superseded

This path remains only so old links fail legibly. Its former owner text is
preserved byte-for-byte in the archive copy above and in Git history.

The live formulation is the **selected D0–D6 scaffold**, not a theorem that
nature has seven necessary layers:

- D4 is causal actuality, embodied means, performed action, and record.
- D5 is counterfactual possibility, modeled futures, represented rankings, and
  selection candidates; actual modeling, ranking, and selection remain D4.
- `mu_0...mu_4` are five candidate crossings into the selected positive rungs;
  each requires operational evidence and a kill criterion.
- `b6:D5~>D6` and `r6:D6~>D0` are non-mu interpretive edges.
- `D6~D0` means a proposed similarity of boundary roles, never identity.
- Missing reduction laws mean `currently_unreduced`, not strong emergence.
- `{0,1,infinity}` is a selected symbolic/projective frame, not a forced
  generator of reality.
