# The Emergence — What Composition May Add

> **[金] Crack:** the prior synthesis defined emergence as strict
> irreducibility, claimed dishonesty was mathematically impossible, promoted
> market aggregation to truth, and treated portability, non-extraction, and
> self-audit as guaranteed properties of a planned Skyzai composition. Those
> claims outran both reduction records and runtime evidence.
>
> **Repair:** emergence here means an intervention-useful macro-regularity of a
> composition. It may be fully reducible to components and relations. Missing
> reduction is `currently_unreduced`, never proof of irreducibility. The complete
> prior synthesis remains at Git blob
> `3e46ea5b2fe6fdd15cdbfc6ffc30ffcf9ae731b1`.

## 1. Typed emergence contract `[I/C]`

A candidate macro-property `Y` of a composed system qualifies for study when:

1. `Y` has an operational measure not defined merely as “the whole”;
2. a declared composition/intervention changes `Y` predictably;
3. component and simpler relational rivals are specified;
4. lower-level states recover the observed outcome within stated error, or the
   present reduction gap is recorded honestly;
5. a novel held-out discriminator and kill criterion are declared; and
6. substrate costs, payers, beneficiaries, authorization, and custody remain
   visible.

A complete micro-reduction reclassifies the crossing as weak emergence. It does
not make the macro variable useless. A failed macro intervention, superior
simpler rival, or missing measurable effect kills or narrows the candidate.

## 2. Six product-level candidates

The former document's six properties survive only as bounded hypotheses or
design objectives:

| Candidate | Operational question | What would kill or narrow it |
|---|---|---|
| verifiable trust | Do independent users make better calibrated trust decisions when claims, custody, and outcomes are receipted? | receipts add no predictive/audit value, can be forged, or central trust remains load-bearing |
| forecast aggregation | Does the specified market/aggregation rule outperform equal-budget individual and domain-native rivals out of sample? | no gain, manipulation, identity failure, settlement bias, or unpriced selection effects |
| voluntary coordination | Do participants coordinate without hidden command, coercion, or unreceipted dependency? | coordination depends on a central actor, exclusion, or silent subsidy |
| evolutionary portability | Can a clean export preserve the declared data, dependencies, provenance, and usable exit across a real version change? | loss, lock-in, undeclared exclusions, or non-reproducible restoration |
| non-extractive scaling | Do bearer-level gains remain nonnegative under measured growth, with every fee and externality visible? | any hidden payer loss, aggregate laundering, capture, or failure of `η=0` in operation |
| distributed auditability | Do independent checks detect and correct failures better than simpler centralized or hybrid controls? | blind spots, collusion, unverifiable identity, missing custody, or worse detection/repair |

None is “mathematically impossible to violate.” A constitution, repository,
signature, market, or network affords controls; only observed enforcement and
receipted outcomes show whether the property exists.

## 3. Composition without magic

Higher-level organization may constrain or reweight lower-law-admissible
trajectories. It may not manufacture a forbidden one. The relevant question is
not whether a property appears in one isolated part, but whether a macro model
adds intervention-sensitive compression or prediction beyond a fully specified
component/relational rival.

That is compatible with:

- weak emergence and full substrate reducibility;
- distributed traces that survive carrier turnover;
- institutions whose rules change participant incentives; and
- failure: a designed composition may simply not produce its intended macro
  property.

## 4. Authority and Justice boundary

Historical/private-DAV K2 records retain their product-governance provenance;
K2 is not a worldview primitive or evidence of emergence. Public and shared
systems require their own adopted, contestable authorization.

No macro benefit may launder an injured bearer. Every candidate assessment
must separate the commitment receipt from the independently observed outcome
receipt and expose consent, custody, payer, beneficiary, reversibility, and
exit.

## 5. Current verdict

These six candidates are not externally validated as a package. They form a
research and product-test agenda. Their strongest honest use is:

> **Composition can create useful macro-regularities that no isolated component
> exhibits; whether a particular regularity exists, reduces, persists, and
> benefits every bearer is an empirical and intervention-sensitive question.**

**Execution boundary:** no product completion, trust guarantee, market-truth
claim, regulatory exemption, deployment, or moral authority follows from this
