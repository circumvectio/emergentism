# The Bounded Game

## Four movers and the three Titan frames

> **The one line.** The grammar declares four primitive mixed-sign,
> cross-factor transfers between two parties. The Rosetta adds three selected
> boundary frames `{0,1,∞}`. This gives a useful vocabulary of seven positions,
> not a proof that reality, morality, or action in general has only seven forms.

## 1. The domain must be declared `[S]`

Let a dyad contain a focal party `i`, another party `o`, and two modeled factors
`Φ` and `V`. The restricted mover grammar permits only a transfer that:

1. crosses the `i/o` boundary;
2. changes one factor on one side and the complementary factor on the other;
3. gives those changes opposite signs; and
4. records the actual enactment and consequence separately.

Within those commitments there are four signatures:

| Direction | Cross-factor channel | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| toward `i` | `V_o -> Φ_i` | `(+ΔΦ_i, -ΔV_o)` |
| toward `i` | `Φ_o -> V_i` | `(+ΔV_i, -ΔΦ_o)` |
| toward `o` | `Φ_i -> V_o` | `(-ΔΦ_i, +ΔV_o)` |
| toward `o` | `V_i -> Φ_o` | `(-ΔV_i, +ΔΦ_o)` |

The four are complete **for this declared grammar**. They are not the four
cells of all possible action. The following are outside its primitive domain:

- same-factor transfers such as `(-ΔΦ_i,+ΔΦ_o)` or `(-ΔV_i,+ΔV_o)`;
- one-sided changes and axis moves where only one factor changes;
- null actions, delays, refusals, observation, and changes of model;
- same-sign co-growth or co-decline;
- changes in boundary, factor definition, horizon, or authorization;
- multiparty, network, stochastic, continuous, and non-transfer dynamics.

Such actions may be modeled directly or represented as composites only when a
specific decomposition is declared and checked. The grammar never forces the
decomposition.

## 2. The four mythic movers `[I]`

The Rosetta names the four signatures:

| Mythic name | Signature | Narrative function |
|---|---|---|
| **Kali** | `+ΔΦ_i, -ΔV_o` | appropriates means from the other into the focal party's coherence |
| **Kālī** | `+ΔV_i, -ΔΦ_o` | cuts another pattern so the focal party can recover means |
| **Kṛṣṇa** | `-ΔΦ_i, +ΔV_o` | spends attention or coherence to enable the other |
| **Arjuna** | `-ΔV_i, +ΔΦ_o` | spends embodied means to protect or clarify the other |

“God” and “demon” are mythic readings of collective-potential-maxing and
ego-potential-maxing tendencies, not essences of persons. They do not settle
the ethics of an event. A cut can protect; a gift can coerce; a transfer that
looks generous can hide a payer.

`η` is the transfer-direction and extraction diagnostic. It helps reveal who
gains at whose expense; a moral judgment requires the full Justice envelope:
all affected bearers, consent, custody, contest, payer,
beneficiary, reversibility, exit, horizon, and receipted consequence.

## 3. The Titans are selected frames, not moves `[S/I]`

The tokens `{0,1,∞}` are selected symbolic roles:

| Frame | Mythic name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `∞` | **Brahmā ○** | opening or generative boundary |
| `1` | **Viṣṇu ⊙** | normalized seam or preservation witness |
| `0` | **Śiva •** | terminal or dissolutive boundary |

Ordinary `0` and `1` remain numbers; `∞` is the projective point when the
Riemann sphere is in use. The Titan tokens are not causal particles, agents, or
three same-sign actions derived from the four-mover grammar. They frame the
vocabulary. The emblem `⊙ = • × ○` and its numerical gloss `1 = 0 × ∞` are
symbols only; `0×∞` is not ordinary field arithmetic.

The number seven is therefore a ratified assembly `4 movers + 3 frames`. It is
not a discovered constant and not a census of everything a finite being can do.

## 4. D4 and D5 are typed, not stacked `[S/I]`

- **D5 possible:** the agent's fallible option field, represented futures,
  rankings, and candidate mover signatures.
- **D4 actual:** embodied means, performed commitment, action record, and the
  consequence that becomes history.

A D5 model may rank a mover. Only a D4 act instantiates a transfer, and only the
world's later receipt shows what the act did. The Titans do not constitute a
higher physical dimension and do not select an outcome by themselves.

## 5. What the Rosetta can and cannot do `[I]`

`ρ_domain` projects this bounded topology into another domain. A projection may
organize comparison, generate hypotheses, or reveal a missing case. It does not
transfer proof, create evidence, or show that the target domain is sevenfold.

The Rosetta is strongest when the target already has:

- a declared dyadic boundary;
- two interpretable factors;
- cross-factor transfers;
- evidence that the proposed signatures are useful.

Where the target has different axes, more parties, non-transfer dynamics, or a
different topology, the projection must change or fail. Every failure remains
part of the lens's evidence.

## 6. Physics fence `[I/C]`

Source, sink, preservation, potential, and flux occur in many mathematical and
physical models. This is a structural rhyme, not a derivation of a force or a
unification of physics. The game yields no charge, coupling constant, measured
interaction, quantum interpretation, or spacetime dimension. A physical claim
requires its own model, units, prediction, and independent test.

## 7. Kill and revision criteria

The bounded closure fails if, while all four domain commitments remain fixed,
a fifth irreducible cross-factor mixed-sign dyadic transfer signature is
exhibited. The broader worldview must also revise if the four-mover vocabulary
systematically predicts or explains less than a rival partition.

An action outside the declared domain does **not** refute the bounded closure;
it refutes any inflated claim that the closure was universal. That distinction
is the Golden Seam.
