## 1. The base sign interface `[A/S]`

For a stipulated two-party gain/loss interface, the sign pair belongs to
`{−,+}²`, which has four cells. `Φ` and `V` are modeled registers, not empirically
conserved ledgers, and real actions may require zero, uncertainty, more bearers,
more resources, or non-transfer effects.

|              | other **+** | other **−** |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| self **+**   | **`++`** (same-sign) | **`+−` TAKE** |
| self **−**   | **`−+` GIVE** | **`−−`** (same-sign) |

The off-diagonal cells represent zero-sum transfers only when one party's gain is
actually sourced from the other's loss. The diagonal cells represent joint
change in this sign abstraction; they do not prove creation or dissolution.

## 2. Four labels arise after adding the resource register `[S/I]`

The two off-diagonal directions, each labeled once in `Φ` and once in `V`, yield
four named transfer slots. Give/take direction alone does not carry morality:

- **`−self / +other` = GIVE — the Devas (givers):** **Kṛṣṇa (L3)** gives `Φ` (the teacher's transfer); **Arjuna (L4)** gives `V` (the agent's transfer).
- **`+self / −other` = TAKE — the Asuras (takers):** **Kālī (L2)** takes `Φ` from the *counterfeit* (the lawful cut); **Kali (L1)** takes `V` to self (the parasitic pull).

**Two give-named and two take-named slots in the stipulated interface.** Sign
direction is descriptive, never a moral verdict. Any operator use is
Demon-bearing when local/ego durable-potential gain externalizes an affected bearer's loss. It is
God-bearing only when bearer-complete Justice holds, every affected bearer is
nonnegative, and at least one materially affected bearer is strictly positive.
Pure non-harmful stasis is Justice-compliant/preservative, not God-bearing;
strict syntropy requires part and whole to rise. Neither `η` nor a mythic name
alone licenses the act; the labels classify receipted consequences, never
identities.

## 3. The Titans are separately selected frames, not matrix cells `[S/I]`

Rosetta appends `{0,1,∞}` as selected frame tokens; they do not follow from the
two diagonal sign cells. The two pole limits remain distinct and the ouroboros
is an interpretive return image.

- **Brahmā `++`** — both rise: creation, the source.
