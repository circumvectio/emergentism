## Why This Document Exists

In the settled modality, D5 names counterfactual possibility: modeled futures,
admissible options, comparison, and ranking. Actual awareness, model tokens,
commitments, performed actions, interactions, institutions, histories, and
receipts are D4 carriers. They may carry and act on D5 content without becoming
D5 actualities.

Publicly, the nearest established sciences of D5 are:

- game theory
- behavioral economics
- mechanism design
- institutional economics
- coordination science

This document makes that translation canonical.

It does **not** reduce D5 to those disciplines.
It states that these disciplines provide public methods for inferring and testing
D5 option fields through observable D4 choices, interventions, institutions,
and records.

## The Three Public Layers of D5

### Layer A: Established Disciplines [S]

These are the first translation surfaces:

- **Game theory** -- strategic interaction under explicit payoff structures
- **Behavioral economics** -- bounded rationality, heuristics, framing, loss aversion, time inconsistency
- **Mechanism design** -- rule architectures that shape strategic choice
- **Institutional economics** -- how incentives harden into persistent coordination structures
- **Coordination science** -- trust, conventions, equilibrium selection, network effects

These disciplines observe actual D4 behavior and use counterfactual models,
policies, and payoff orderings that the framework reads through D5.

### Layer B: Emergentist Synthesis [I]

The framework selects a shared lens across these otherwise independent
disciplines:

- selection under constraint
- reciprocity vs extraction
- sovereignty vs capture
- alignment vs pathology

This is the D5 translation hypothesis, not a demonstrated unique unification.

### Layer C: Frontier Theory [C]

These stronger claims remain theory:

- that the D5 option-field lens supplies explanatory or predictive gain beyond
  the established local models
- that strategic relation can be reduced to a single scalar moral law
- that all durable institutions can be completely explained by one D5 equation

**Kill criteria:**
- If explicit option-field variables add no held-out predictive or intervention
  gain beyond lower-level descriptions, the first claim contracts to vocabulary.
- If a durable institution is shown to persist while systematically violating
  non-extraction and destroying the durable potential of an individual or its
  sustaining whole, the proposed link between persistence and Justice is falsified.
- If game theory produces a strategic situation where equatorial balance (`B = sin θ`) or node flourishing (`P_node = Φ × V`) is not even conditionally favored, the second claim weakens to a special case.

The framework may test a stronger cross-domain compression. It may not present
that compression as unique, universal, or already settled science.

## The D4 to D5 Shift

This register is easiest to understand through the D4 to D5 transition.

- **D4** contains bounded causal systems, actual model tokens, commitments,
  actions, interactions, institutions, outcomes, and records
- **D5** contains the possible actions, counterfactual policies, modeled futures,
  and rankings represented by those systems

So:

- neuroscience is closest to D4
- game theory and behavioral economics are closest to D5

This is why the transition matters so much for the organism and for AI alignment:

- D4 explains and records how an agent acts and how actual others, incentives,
  and institutions affect it
- D5 models the alternatives the agent represents, compares, and ranks before a
  D4 commitment is enacted

## Reduction Status at the D4-to-D5 Crossing

The candidate crossing is `currently_unreduced`. Present models may be unable to
recover an agent's whole represented option field and ranking from a chosen
lower-register description, while an explicit option model may compactly improve
prediction or intervention. That is evidence for the usefulness of the D5 lens,
not proof of strong or ontological irreducibility.

For each domain, state:

- the lower variables and transition law
- the D5 option or ranking variable being proposed
- the recovery test and fair baseline
- the present reduction status
- the prediction and kill criterion

A successful reduction reclassifies that case as weak emergence without
destroying the scaffold. A failed reduction remains `currently_unreduced` unless
independent evidence warrants the stronger `candidate_strong` status.

D5 lets the framework compare possible actions under its declared ethical
predicates; actual evidence, action, cost, and consequence remain D4.

For individual `i`, sustaining whole `H`, and Justice predicate `J`, the
declared value grammar is:

```text
Moral(a)     iff delta W_H > 0 and delta W_i >= 0 and J(a;i,H)
Ethical(a)   iff delta W_i > 0 and delta W_H >= 0 and J(a;i,H)
Syntropic(a) iff delta W_i > 0 and delta W_H > 0 and J(a;i,H)
```

Aggregate welfare, balance, output, and distribution statistics may inform the
receipt. None supplies the verdict or compensates for a destroyed bearer.

consent, custody, irreversibility, contest, and exit; it is not strict syntropy.

Questions like these can be studied without leaving scientific language:

## Writing Rules for D5-Facing Documents

### Rule 1: Begin with Established Strategic Science

Start with:

- repeated games
- public goods
- principal-agent theory
- bounded rationality
- coordination problems
- mechanism design

Only then introduce the framework's synthesis.

### Rule 2: Do Not Pretend Ethics Is Already Solved by Economics

The framework's claim is stronger than standard economics.
That is acceptable.
But it must be stated honestly as a synthesis:

> strategic science reveals the public structure through which ethical claims can be tested, constrained, and designed

not:

> economics already proves the moral law

### Rule 3: Treat D5 as Public Selection, Not Private Mysticism

Do not lead a D5-facing scientific document with:

- mystical language
- mythic personification
- idealist declarations

Lead with represented options and rankings, then identify the D4 action and
receipt that can test them.

### Rule 4: Keep D6 Distinct

D4 includes the positive, actual carriers of strategic sediment:

- norms
- institutions
- symbolic orders
- civilizational memory as publicly describable pattern

Their possible continuations and ranked alternatives may be modeled in D5.
`D6` does not add one more positive science above this.
`D6` begins where every such positive account is seen as real yet non-exhaustive.

If the D5 lens adds no cross-domain explanatory or predictive value:

- game theory still survives

> that these disciplines benefit from one shared D5 option-field translation

The public disciplines remain.
