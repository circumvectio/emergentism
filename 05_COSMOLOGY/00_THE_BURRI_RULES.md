## 1. Scope and evidence boundary

This document uses the current [`[A/B/S/I/D/C]` evidence ladder](../02_EPISTEMOLOGY/01_EVIDENCE_TIERS/00_THE_HONEST_POSITION.md).
Its draft status does not upgrade any bounded claim inside it, and a stronger
source in one domain does not transfer that tier through an analogy.

A **Burri diagram** is a typed directed graph

\[
\mathcal{B}=(V,E,\tau_V,\tau_E,\lambda,\mathcal{E}),
\]

where `V` is a finite set of typed nodes, `E ⊆ V × V` is a set of directed
edges, `τ_V` and `τ_E` assign allowed node and edge types, `λ` carries explicit
labels and evidence tiers, and `𝓔` is the applicable moral/collective boundary
envelope. The diagram is a semantic model, not proof that its interpretation is
physically or ontologically real. `[S]`

The canonical emblem states:

> **The Titans frame possibility; a finite agent forms a fallible D5 decision state and option field, commits through D4 embodied means and authorization, receives D4 consequences, and recursively corrects both world-model and selector.**

This is the full-text equivalent of the master emblem. The canonical emblem
uses no torus geometry.

## 2. Seven primitive classes

Every well-formed diagram uses only the following primitive classes. Compound
forms are compositions of them, not extra primitives.

1. **Titan frame** — a boundary-frame marker drawn from `{0, 1, ∞}`. It fixes a
   register or boundary relation and never acts as an operand, agent, causal
   particle, or deployable operator.
2. **Typed edge** — a directed relation whose type determines its reading:
   **solid** is actual/enacted, **dotted** is possible/modeled, and **curved**
   is feedback/coupling. In the operational Soul Loop, the enacted D4
   action/receipt worldline is solid and the modeled D5 option fan is dotted;
   the global line grammar remains modality-first. Edge style is semantic, not
   decoration.
3. **`μ`-crossing** — a typed transition `μ_n : D_n → D_(n+1)` satisfying
   [BR-2](#br-2-crossing).
4. **Finite `χ` commitment** — the authorized, fallible selection that turns a
   modeled option into an action through D4 means, as specified by
   [BR-4](#br-4-commitment).
5. **Receipt** — a recorded consequence, observation, or explicit null/failure
   reason returned by an action. A receipt does not become truth merely by
   being recorded; its tier and provenance remain visible.
6. **Persistent trace field** — a carrier-independent-enough record that
   persists across relevant carrier turnover and can reweight later selection.
7. **Boundary envelope** — the visible moral and collective scope governing a
   node, loop, or composition.

### Moral/collective envelope

Every action-bearing or collectively coupled diagram declares, at minimum:

| Field | Required declaration |
|---|---|
| **individual** | Which finite persons or agents can gain, lose, consent, refuse, or exit |
| **whole** | The modeled collective, ecology, institution, or affected system |
| **`η`** | The extraction condition and its measurement register |
| **custody** | Who holds identity, data, capital, keys, records, and outputs |
| **consent** | Who authorized what, at which tier, and how refusal is expressed |
| **reversibility** | Which effects can be undone, at what cost, and by whom |
| **exit** | How a participant leaves with the assets, identity, data, and rights they are entitled to carry |

An omitted field is an unresolved boundary, not permission. The envelope makes
the individual/whole tension and hidden transfer costs auditable; it does not
declare that every such tension has been solved. `[S/I]`

## 3. The six rules

### BR-1 Frame

`{0, 1, ∞}` are **boundary frames**. They are not operands, agents, causal
particles, or deployable operators. The inscription `1 = 0 × ∞` is permitted
only as a **frame-register emblem**; it is not ordinary arithmetic and licenses
no calculation, causal claim, or ontological entity. `[S]`

### BR-2 Crossing

> **Emergence type (canon receipt 106):** μ = **strong emergence** — the genesis
> loop that *wins* a novel degree of freedom (adds a Φ×V factor), irreducible; no
> reducing/threshold law (∅ is its fingerprint, not a gap). `[S]`

For `n = 0, …, 5`, a proposed crossing has the form

\[
\mu_n:D_n\rightarrow D_{n+1}.
\]

```text
mu_n:D_n->D_(n+1), n=0..5
```

For `mu_0` through `mu_4`, it is admissible only when all six fields are
present:

1. the prior register `D_n` and its typing;
2. observable saturation or failure inside that register;
3. the genuinely new positive freedom introduced at `D_(n+1)`;
4. a lower-register recovery or embedding that shows how the prior capacity is
   retained, reconstructed, or explicitly lost;
5. the evidence tier of the crossing claim; and
6. a kill criterion that would reject or downgrade it.

`mu_5` is the closure exception. It is admissible only when it names the typed
D5 register, demonstrates exhaustion of positive option-description, declares
the D6 closure boundary, recovers or explicitly terminates the lower register,
states its tier, and supplies a kill criterion. It introduces **no new positive
freedom**. `D6` is closure of the declared scaffold, not positive science, a
supermind, or an empirically established cosmic agent. Particular crossings
may be useful as `[S]` dependencies or `[I]` mappings; a universal `μ` ontology
remains `[D/I]` pending independent warrant.

### BR-3 Constraint

For system `X` under constraint `C`, admissible constrained possibility obeys

\[
\operatorname{support}(K_X^C)\subseteq \operatorname{support}(K_X).
\]

```text
support(K_X^C) subset support(K_X)
```

The constraint removes or reweights candidates; it does not conjure a physical
future outside the system's causal possibility set. A macro-causal claim also
requires a **macro-causal witness**: an identified mechanism, intervention,
trace, or counterfactual contrast by which the macro constraint changes later
D4 outcomes. Visual narrowing alone is not a witness. `[S]`

### BR-4 Commitment

> **Emergence type (canon receipt 106):** χ = **weak-selective downward
> causation** — the governance/commitment loop that *deploys* a won freedom by
> selecting among reachable futures and emitting a **receipt** `R`; auditable-after
> but with no *governing* law (∅). Not Bedau's no-downward-cause sense. `[S]`

A finite commitment is

\[
\chi_t:(\Omega_t,M_t,V_t,\mathrm{signature})
\rightarrow(a_t,R_{t+1}),
\]

```text
chi_t:(Omega_t,M_t,V_t,signature)->(a_t,R_(t+1))
```

where `Ω_t` is the typed option set; `M_t := (C_t,G_t)` is the fallible D5
decision state; `C_t` is its memory/world-model; `G_t` is its selector,
ranking, and value policy; `V_t` is D4 embodied means / viability (body, tools,
energy, access, timing, and control); `signature` is the authorization
envelope; `a_t` is the selected D4 action; and `R_(t+1)` is its receipt. The
four-argument signature remains exact because `M_t` is explicitly decomposed
into `C_t` and `G_t`. If and only if stochasticity is declared, the action may
instead be written

\[
a_t\sim\pi_t(\cdot\mid M_t,V_t).
\]

```text
a_t~pi_t(.|M_t,V_t)
```

The [Seven Generative Actions](00_D5_THE_SEVEN_GENERATIVE_ACTIONS.md) remain the
move vocabulary. `χ` specifies finite commitment semantics; it does not create
an eighth move, erase authorization, or turn a decision into a quantum
measurement. `[S]`

### BR-5 Reflexive Return

The consequence return is

\[
(C_{t+1},G_{t+1})
=\operatorname{Loop}(C_t,G_t,R_{t+1}),
\]

```text
(C_(t+1),G_(t+1))=Loop(C_t,G_t,R_(t+1))
```

where `C` is the current cognition/world-model state and `G` the current
selector/ranking/value-policy state. The loop must name three separable gaps:

- **cognitive gap** — prediction or representation versus observed receipt;
- **execution gap** — authorized action versus action actually carried out;
- **outcome gap** — carried-out action versus consequence received.

Negative and positive feedback are dynamical signs, not moral verdicts. A
positive loop can amplify harm; a negative loop can suppress a benefit. The
boundary envelope supplies the moral test. `[S/I]`

### BR-6 Composition

Coupled loops become a candidate **Egregoreotype** only when one shared trace:

1. persists across relevant carrier turnover;
2. reweights later selection;
3. reproduces an objective-like bias across more than one cycle; and
4. receives substrate input and pays substrate, maintenance, or sacrifice costs.

This criterion identifies a candidate persistent collective pattern. It makes
no presumption of consciousness, personhood, unitary agency, cosmic cognition,
or moral standing. The Egregoreotype mapping is `[I]` until independently
operationalized and tested.

## 4. Composition operators

Composition preserves every component's types, tiers, receipts, and envelope:

- **Serial composition** — the typed output/receipt of loop `A` becomes a
  declared input to loop `B`; mismatched registers make the composition fail.
- **Parallel composition** — loops operate over the same interval without an
  assumed causal edge; shared context must not be mistaken for interaction.
- **Trace-coupled composition** — loops read from and write to a persistent
  trace field that can reweight later selections; write, custody, and conflict
  rules must be declared.
- **Receipt-recursive composition** — a returned receipt updates the next
  world-model and/or selector, with the affected field and null-update reason
  recorded.

For a projection into another domain, **`rho_domain`** is defined by

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{domain}}:\mathcal{B}\rightharpoonup\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{domain}}
\]

```text
rho_domain:B -(partial)-> B_domain
```

It is a type- and tier-preserving Rosetta projection. It may return **partial**,
**ambiguous**, or **failed**. It never transfers proof, evidence, causal status,
or ontological identity from the source domain to the target domain. `[S/I]`

### 5.3 Human option breadth and the physical future cone

Human agents can widen symbolic, counterfactual, institutional, social, and
intergenerational **representations of options** while remaining inside the
`c`-bounded physical future cone. `[A]` constrains causal propagation; `[I]`
describes the framework's option-language mapping. No diagram may depict
intelligence, planning, or cooperation as widening a relativistic light cone.
See the [causal-bound moral-circle guard](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/02_OPERATORS/MF_ADVANCED/MF_295_c_Bounds_the_Moral_Circle.md).

**Constrained mutual option-cone widening** is a framework objective/hypothesis:
participants seek to widen one another's viable represented and institutional
options subject to causal limits, `η`, custody, consent, reversibility, exit,
and costs. `[I/D]` It is not a universal law of agents and does not justify
maximizing aggregate options against an individual's refusal or the whole's
viability.

### 5.4 Egregoreotype, ritual, and sacrifice

An Egregoreotype candidate is a BR-6 trace-coupled composition. **Ritual** is a
repeatable, typed write/read protocol through which carriers refresh the shared
trace. **Sacrifice** is the explicit substrate cost paid by persons, time,
attention, matter, energy, capital, foregone options, or ecology to reproduce
that trace. `[I]` The diagram must name who pays, who benefits, who has custody,
whether consent is real, what can be reversed, and how exit works. Hidden or
coerced sacrifice fails the Justice gate.

### 5.5 Future pull without retrocausal inflation

Ordinary "future pull" is a present-representation account: anticipated future
states exist now as models, commitments, institutions, incentives, promises,
and traces that alter present selection and thereby later outcomes. `[I]` This
requires no signal from the future and no reversal of D4 causal order. Literal
retrocausality remains `[C]` and must be removable without breaking the core
rulebook.

## 7. Ambiguity rejections

A diagram is rejected or returned for correction when it contains any of the
following:

1. **physical-cone expansion** — cognition or organization shown widening the
   `c`-bounded physical future cone;
2. **unmeasured visual width** — graphical width treated as option count,
   probability, value, power, or freedom without a declared measure;
3. **`μ`/decision/measurement conflation** — an emergence crossing, finite
   commitment, and quantum measurement treated as the same operation;
4. **Titan as agent/operand** — `{0,1,∞}` made causal, executable, or arithmetic;
5. **D6 supermind/science** — closure presented as a cosmic mind or established
   positive science;
6. **Rosetta as proof** — analogy used to transfer evidence or identity;
7. **mission without modeling/selection** — a declared purpose with no
   `M_t := (C_t,G_t)`, D4 means `V_t`, selection rule, authorization, or kill
   criterion;
8. **unjust cone maximization** — aggregate option breadth used to override
   individual/whole boundaries, consent, custody, reversibility, or exit;
9. **hidden coerced sacrifice** — trace persistence funded by undisclosed or
   non-consensual costs; or
10. **feedback with no recorded update/null reason** — a curved return edge that
    records neither the changed model/selector nor why no update occurred.

Beauty, Truth, and Justice are **acceptance gates only**. They are not graph
primitives, causal forces, evidence upgrades, or substitutes for receipts.

|---|---|

true-looking but coercive diagram, or a just intention without receipts remains

A conforming Burri diagram must answer all of the following:

- Are all nodes and edges typed, with solid/actual, dotted/possible, and
  curved/feedback meanings preserved—and, in the operational Soul Loop, are
  the D4 action/receipt worldline solid and the D5 option fan dotted?

  criterion?
- Does `mu_5` instead demonstrate exhaustion, declare closure, recover or
  explicitly terminate the lower register, state tier and kill criterion, and

- Does every constraint satisfy the support inclusion and identify any claimed
  macro-causal witness?
- Does every `χ_t` name option set, `M_t := (C_t,G_t)`, D4 means `V_t`,
  authorization, action, and receipt?
- Does every reflexive return distinguish cognitive, execution, and outcome
  gaps and record an update or null reason?
- Does every collective candidate satisfy BR-6 without presuming consciousness
  and declare its substrate inputs and costs?
- Does every Rosetta projection preserve type and tier while allowing
  partial/ambiguous/failed output?
- Are the boundary envelope and all three acceptance gates visible?
- Can all `[C]` insets be removed without changing BR-1 through BR-6?
