# G7: The Selected Action-and-Frame Vocabulary

G7 is a compact Emergentist language for describing a scoped two-factor game.
It is **selected by construction**, not forced by arithmetic, the sphere, nature,
or a universal census of every possible act.

## 1. Types before names

Let the current vocabulary be the disjoint union

\[
G_7=M_4\sqcup F_3,
\]

where

\[
M_4=\{\operatorname{give}_{\Phi},\operatorname{give}_{V},
      \operatorname{take}_{\Phi},\operatorname{take}_{V}\}
\]

contains four **actual move labels**, and

\[
F_3=\{\operatorname{create\mbox{-}frame},
      \operatorname{dissolve\mbox{-}frame},
      \operatorname{preserve\mbox{-}frame}\}
\]

contains three **frame labels**. A frame describes how a transition is being
bounded or interpreted; it is not another performed action.

`G7` therefore means `4 + 3` labels in this chosen grammar. It does not mean
that a `3×3` sign table mathematically collapses to seven, that there can be no
eighth useful label, or that all causation, ethics, institutions, and physics
have been enumerated. Effects outside this decomposition remain outside it.

## 2. The settled D4/D5 boundary

- D5 contains **possible content**: represented alternatives, possible
  rankings, and candidate selections.
- Modeling, comparing, ranking, selecting, authorizing, and acting are actual
  processes or events in D4.
- `V` denotes usable D4 means. `Φ` is a present D4-evaluated score or capacity
  concerning the D5 option content an actual model can represent and
  discriminate.

A finite agent may couple a fallible option field to embodied means and attempt
one action. Its commitment receipt is not the world's outcome receipt. See the
[Burri Rules](00_THE_BURRI_RULES.md) and the
[D4/D5 canonical reference](03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/34_D4_D5_CANONICAL_REFERENCE.md).

## 3. The four move labels

| Move label | Minimal reading |
|---|---|
| `give_Φ` | the actor incurs a cost that raises another bearer's represented coherence or option quality |
| `give_V` | the actor incurs a cost that raises another bearer's usable means |
| `take_Φ` | the actor gains by reducing or appropriating another bearer's coherence or option quality |
| `take_V` | the actor gains by reducing or appropriating another bearer's usable means |

These are descriptive transfer labels, not verdicts. A moral evaluation also
requires the Justice envelope, named affected bearers, payer, beneficiary,
consent, reversibility, time horizon, and observed outcome. Mythic names such
as Kali, Kālī, Kṛṣṇa, and Arjuna are reversible Rosetta mnemonics `[I]`; they
are not kinds of person and cannot determine guilt or virtue.

## 4. The three frame labels

| Frame label | Minimal reading |
|---|---|
| `create-frame` | both evaluated factors rise within the declared boundary |
| `dissolve-frame` | both evaluated factors fall or are released within the declared boundary |
| `preserve-frame` | both evaluated factors remain approximately stable within the declared boundary |

Brahmā, Śiva, and Viṣṇu are optional Rosetta mnemonics `[I]`. The frames do not
act, authorize, consent, pay, or bear consequences.

A finite being may **simulate** pure creation, dissolution, or preservation in
represented content. The simulation itself is an embodied D4 event with
substrate cost; its merely possible world or alteration is D5 content. Relative
to that contained model, the being may be described as choosing a frame. In the
causal world containing the being, however, every attempted consequence remains
a typed D4 move with bearers and a receipt. Hence:

> **Frame relative to what you model; mover within the world that contains you.** `[I]`

This does not turn imagination into creation ex nihilo, erase physical cost, or
let an empty frame label count as a play. A G7-coded action must contain at
least one `M4` move; the empty word is no action.

## 5. Use discipline

Every application must declare:

- the bearer boundary and time horizon;
- whether a label is a move or a frame;
- the operational definitions of `Φ` and `V`;
- authorization, consent, payer, beneficiary, reversibility, and contest path;
- commitment and outcome receipts;
- evidence tier and a kill criterion.

The current refusal and value owners govern evaluation:

- [The Refusals](../00_META/00_K5_THE_REFUSALS.md)
- [Objective Morals and Ethics](../04_AXIOLOGY/02_VALUE_THEORY/00_OBJECTIVE_MORALS_AND_ETHICS.md)
- [The Dyadic Coupling Law](00_THE_DYADIC_COUPLING_LAW.md)

**Kill criterion.** If the vocabulary hides an affected bearer, conflates a
frame with an action, treats a mnemonic as a person-type, or fails to distinguish
commitment from outcome, that application is invalid. If another label improves
prediction or explanation, extend or replace G7; the canon forbids no eighth
description.
