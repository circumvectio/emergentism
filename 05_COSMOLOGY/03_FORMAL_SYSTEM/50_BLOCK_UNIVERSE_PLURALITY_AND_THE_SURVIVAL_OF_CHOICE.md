# Block-Universe Plurality and the Survival of Choice

## 1 · The claim

Owner, 2026-07-29:

> *"And finally, choice in the block spacetime universe becomes irrelevant in one
> with infinitely many parallel block universes — D5 to D4."*

Three things are tangled here, and they fail at three different points. The
result is that **choice survives all of them** — and, more usefully, that the
real threat to the agency account lies somewhere else entirely (§7).

## 2 · The premise the corpus does not hold `[C]`

"Infinitely many parallel block universes" **existing** is `GP-08`, and its
current status is *"model support only."* Its strongest rival is stated in canon
as **"one actual history plus epistemic uncertainty,"** and its kill is *"no
interpretation-sensitive discriminator."*

`KSC-19` is more explicit still: finite enumeration or `1−ε` support coverage
*"does not establish… the physical co-existence of all block universes."* `W2`
adds that the reading *"does not make every supported history physically
existent."*

So the argument's premise is **not a corpus commitment** — it is an open socket
with no discriminator. That alone blocks the conclusion, but the argument fails
even if the premise is granted.

## 3 · The fatalist inference is invalid `[A]`

The core move is:

```text
P1  in a block universe, all events tenselessly exist
P2  therefore the future is fixed
P3  therefore choice is irrelevant
```

`P2 → P3` is a fallacy, and an ancient one — the Stoics' *argos logos*, the lazy
argument: *if you are fated to recover, do not call the doctor.* The Stoic answer
stands: the recovery is **co-fated with** calling the doctor.

The equivocation is exact:

```text
"the future is fixed"                          — DETERMINATE
"the future is fixed REGARDLESS OF WHAT I DO"  — INDEPENDENT
```

Only the first follows from the block. The second is a separate and false claim.
In a block universe **your deliberation and selection are part of the block** —
they are among the events that make it the shape it is. Counterfactual
dependence survives untouched: had you chosen otherwise, the block would be
otherwise. Determinateness is not causal bypass.

## 4 · Granting plurality anyway, choice still survives `[S]`

Suppose every branch is realised. Three independent reasons the conclusion still
does not follow:

1. **Self-location.** Even with all branches actual, *which branch the bearer
   inhabits* is what the choice settles. The decision-relevant question was never
   "does the multiverse change" but "which history do I occupy." Everettian
   decision theory exists precisely because this remains well-posed.
2. **Weights are not uniform.** "Every outcome occurs" is not "all outcomes
   equally." Where a measure exists, action changes the measure. `W2` explicitly
   does **not** derive Born weights — so the corpus may not even assume the
   uniformity the argument needs.
3. **The bearer is singular.** The Human Condition holds that *"a trace is not
   automatically the bearer."* A counterpart in another branch is not the bearer
   who receives this history's consequences. Justice, receipts and repair all
   attach to the bearer, not to the ensemble.

## 5 · The D4/D5 typing is the firewall `[S]`

The claim's own phrasing — "D5 to D4" — names the error. Under `KSC-03`:

```text
D5   merely-possible counterfactual content, represented by actual tokens
D4   causal actuality: ONE actual history, and the performed ranking,
     selection, commitment and received consequence
```

Treating "infinitely many parallel block universes" as *actual* promotes D5
content to D4 actuality. That is precisely the failure condition both owners
already name: `KSC-03` flags *"possible power reified as independent
causation"*; `KSC-19` flags *"model coverage inflated into ontology."*

The firewall is therefore not an ad-hoc defence — it is what the register
distinction exists **for**:

> **Choice is a D4 event, not a D5 content. It does not compete with the plenum,
> because it is not the same type of thing as the plenum.**

A represented field of alternatives, however large, is represented. The selection
among them is actual. No quantity of D5 content converts into or cancels a D4
event.

## 6 · The corpus was already immune `[B]`

The Human Condition states the agency account is *"compatible with several
metaphysical positions about determinism"* and *"less than ultimate
self-creation."* Its operational claim is deliberately narrower: freedom is
present where *"interventions on represented futures change present choices."*

That claim is **untouched by the block universe and untouched by plurality.**
Neither changes whether such interventions shift action. The Soul Loop also runs
identically in a fully determinate block: the agent still does not know its own
future, must still model, select, commit, and receive `r_(t+1)`. Epistemic
position, not metaphysical openness, is what the loop requires.

## 7 · What *would* actually threaten the account `[C]`

The honest payoff, and it inverts the worry.

The agency account is **metaphysically robust and empirically vulnerable**. No
result about block universes or branch plurality can reach it. What can reach it
is `GP-02` / `μ₄`:

> Intervene on represented futures while holding means and present state fixed.
> If action distributions do **not** shift beyond the strongest fair D4 baseline,
> the distinct D5 variable fails — and with it the framework's claim that
> counterfactual capacity is a real effective freedom.

That is a preregisterable experiment with a declared kill, and it is where the
account is actually exposed. The threat to choice is not eternalism. It is a
null result.

not derive Born weights, does not decide eternalism versus presentism, and does

them.
