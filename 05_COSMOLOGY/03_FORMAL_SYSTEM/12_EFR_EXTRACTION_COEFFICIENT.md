# The Extraction Coefficient (`η`)

> **[金] Crack:** the prior paper correctly noticed the difference between an
> individual take/give ratio and a system-level flow, but called the classical
> Lotka–Volterra center an attractor, generalized one toy feedback model to all
> coupled systems, and typed Good/Bad/Evil directly from `η`.
>
> **Repair:** retain the measurable diagnostic and explicit toy results;
> distinguish bounded periodic motion from convergence; require
> bearer-complete Justice for moral classification. The full prior version is
> preserved at Git blob `2d560efa5ceb7fd1d203d64893c19f9778f41ae3`.

## 1. Measurement contract `[I/C]`

For a declared system boundary, interval, native unit, and substrate, define

```text
η = measured extraction / measured contribution.
```

The record must name the payer, beneficiary, numerator, denominator, units,
time window, uncertainty, missing flows, and boundary changes. A zero
denominator yields an undefined or explicitly extended diagnostic—not an
automatic infinity with moral meaning. Ratios at individual, organization,
ecosystem, and network scales are different objects and must not be substituted.

`η<1`, `η=1`, and `η>1` say only how the declared flows compare. They do not
by themselves say symbiotic, balanced, parasitic, Good, Bad, or Evil.

## 2. Classical Lotka–Volterra survivor `[S]`

For positive parameters and states,

```text
dx/dt = αx − βxy
dy/dt = δxy − γy
```

the positive equilibrium is

```text
x* = γ/δ,   y* = α/β.
```

If the system diagnostic is declared as

```text
η_sys = βxy/(αx) = βy/α,
```

then `η_sys=1` at that equilibrium. Away from equilibrium, positive classical
undamped Lotka–Volterra trajectories lie on periodic level sets around a
**center**. Consequently `η_sys` is bounded and periodic on each such orbit; it
can oscillate around 1 but does not converge to 1. There is no basin of
attraction without additional damping, forcing, density dependence,
stochasticity, harvesting, or another specified mechanism.

## 3. Runaway toy survivor `[S/I]`

For the explicitly uncoupled equation

```text
dC/dt = rC,  r>0,
```

`C(t)=C_0 exp(rt)` diverges. A ratio using a fixed positive regenerative
denominator therefore diverges too. This is a mathematical contrast, not a
complete model of cancer, institutions, or ecology. Real systems add resource,
immune, treatment, death, and feedback boundaries.

The comparison supports only:

- a model can keep a diagnostic bounded without converging;
- a different model can generate runaway growth; and
- “has feedback” alone does not determine stability.

Any extension to analogous systems is `[C]` until its state space, invariant
set, stability class, measurements, and rivals are specified.

## 4. Justice is a separate type `[I]`

Boundedness and divergence are dynamical descriptions. A bounded institution
can be unjust; a temporarily unbounded intervention can be authorized and
life-preserving; a favorable aggregate ratio can hide a destroyed bearer.

Under the Emergentist value contract, `η=0` is a necessary non-extraction fence
for strict Justice—not a sufficient verdict. Classification requires:

- a valid accountable authorization envelope;
- every materially affected bearer, payer, and beneficiary;
- consent, custody, reversibility, and exit;
- separate commitment and outcome receipts; and
- bearer-level durable-potential changes without aggregate laundering.

Only then may a receipted consequence be classified as Demon-bearing,
God-bearing, preservative, sacrificial, or unresolved. These are consequence
predicates, never identities of agents, organisms, castes, or coordinates.

## 5. Kill criteria

The formal survivor fails if:

1. the displayed positive Lotka–Volterra system has a positive trajectory that
   is neither the equilibrium nor a bounded periodic level set under the stated
   classical assumptions;
2. the declared `η_sys` does not equal 1 at its displayed equilibrium;
3. the uncoupled exponential solution does not diverge for `r>0`; or
4. a use claims convergence, a basin of attraction, universal feedback
   stability, or moral valence from `η` alone.

The operational diagnostic narrows when reasonable boundary or unit choices
reverse conclusions, inter-rater reliability is inadequate, or a domain-native
flow model predicts outcomes better.

medical, product, governance, or deployment authority follows.
