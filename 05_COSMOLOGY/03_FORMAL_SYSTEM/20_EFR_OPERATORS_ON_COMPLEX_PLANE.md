# OPERATORS ON THE COMPLEX PLANE

**Status:** ACTIVE local notation · repaired 2026-07-20

> **[金] Kintsugi replacement.** The predecessor treated reciprocal coordinates
> as a conservation law and used them to make one operator redundant, dominated,
> or morally licensed. Original Git blob:
> `7486e4dc4cf570656d83ad5e953d1df29d064d75`. What survives is notation for
> describing actual changes; no strategy or value is forced.

## 1. Coordinate convention

The complex plane can display two declared real reporting axes. The reciprocal
chart `phi*nu=1` is one one-dimensional curve on that plane, not a physical
conservation law and not the whole action space. A real intervention may move
off that curve because `Phi` and `V` are measured node variables, not the chart
coordinates `phi` and `nu`.

## 2. Four direction symbols

| Symbol | Local report | Mythic mnemonic |
|---|---|---|
| up | first reported factor rises | Arjuna |
| right | second reported factor rises | Kṛṣṇa |
| down | first reported factor falls | Kālī |
| left | second reported factor falls | Kali |

These directions are a selected alphabet. No direction is geometrically
impossible, redundant, dominated, authorized, good, or evil. A move's meaning
depends on which bearer changes, who caused it, which units and baseline are
used, and what the outcome receipt shows.

## 3. Modal and normative boundary

Actual model tokens, decisions, actions, and changes are D4. Possible moves
represented by a model are D5 content. The transition from a modeled alternative
to action requires actual means and accountable authorization; the environment
returns a separate outcome.

`etaObserved` may describe extraction after a measurement contract is declared.
Bearer-complete Justice remains independent. It exposes every payer and
beneficiary, consent, custody, contest, reversibility, consequence, and exit.
An operator name never supplies moral or legal authority.

## 4. Use and failure

Use the diagram when it makes a local change easier to compare. Discard it when
axes cannot be operationalized, when a change needs more than two coordinates,
when it hides third-party effects, or when a rival representation predicts
better. The notation is a Rosetta projection, not evidence that reality is a
plane or sphere.
