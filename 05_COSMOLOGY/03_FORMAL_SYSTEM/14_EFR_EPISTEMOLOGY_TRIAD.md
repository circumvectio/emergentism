# EFR epistemology triad

> **[金] Kintsugi seam.** The prior edition described induction and deduction
> as literal travel on a sphere and used quantum superposition and collapse as
> their mechanism. The triad of inference survives; the ontic and quantum
> inflation does not. The original wording remains recoverable at the Git blob
> above.

## 1. Type law

Reasoning occurs through actual carriers and events:

- observations, inscriptions, memories, models, rules, inference events,
  rankings, tests, and receipts are D4 actual;
- alternatives, unperformed possibilities, hypothesis contents, and predicted
  future contents are D5 merely possible.

An actual hypothesis token is not itself D5. It is a D4 token that represents
a D5 content:

```text
HypothesisToken:D4(actual) ── represents ──▶ HypothesisContent:D5(possible)
PredictionToken:D4(actual) ── represents ──▶ FutureContent:D5(possible)
```

If a represented content is already true, that truth does not follow merely
from its representation. World contact and the relevant evidence relation are
still required.

## 2. Three reasoning moves

### Induction

An actual inference event uses a finite set of actual observations to produce
or update an actual model token whose content ranges beyond those observations.

```text
ObservedRecords:D4 + InductiveRule:D4
  ── actual inference ──▶ ModelToken:D4 ── represents ──▶ GeneralContent:D5
```

Induction is defeasible. It owes sampling assumptions, rivals, predictive
discriminators, and a declared update rule. Geometry does not make the
generalization true.

### Deduction

An actual inference event applies actual premise and rule tokens to derive an
actual conclusion token. If the conclusion concerns an unobserved case, its
represented content remains D5 until world contact.

```text
PremiseTokens:D4 + RuleToken:D4
  ── actual inference ──▶ PredictionToken:D4 ── represents ──▶ FutureContent:D5
```

Deductive validity is fixed by the declared logic, not by following coordinate
lines on a sphere. Validity does not establish soundness unless the premises
are warranted.

### Abduction

An actual inference event constructs and ranks possible explanations of actual
records:

```text
Records:D4 ── actual generation/ranking ──▶ CandidateContents:D5
```

The ranking event and its score are D4. The candidates are D5. Abduction owes
alternatives, priors or penalties where used, intervention-sensitive
predictions, and a kill criterion; elegance alone is not confirmation.

## 3. The empirical loop

The triad becomes scientific only through world contact:

```text
D4 records
  ── induction/abduction ──▶ D4 model tokens about D5 alternatives
  ── deduction ────────────▶ D4 prediction tokens about D5 outcomes
  ── authorized action ────▶ D4 commitment receipt
  ── environment ──────────▶ D4 outcome receipt
  ── comparison/update ────▶ next D4 model and selector
```

This exposes three different gaps:

| Gap | Comparison |
|---|---|
| cognitive | territory versus model |
| execution | intended versus attempted/performed action |
| outcome | expected versus observed consequence |

Negative feedback means a declared error decreases; positive feedback means
divergence or self-confirmation increases. The signs are dynamical, not moral.

## 4. What the sphere may still do `[I]`

The reciprocal sphere may visualize poles, balance, or a family of alternatives.
It does not prove induction, deduction, abduction, truth preservation, or a
unique logic. Rosetta projection may translate this topology to other domains,
but it transfers no proof or evidence tier.

## 5. Removable quantum correspondence `[C]`

One may compare D5 alternatives with structured quantum alternatives and D4
records with observer-relative factual records. The comparison stops there:

- Everett has no fundamental collapse;
- Copenhagen-family actualization is interpretation-specific;
- inference is not quantum measurement;
- `μ` and `χ` are not measurement operators;
- neither D4 nor D5 is an extra spacetime dimension;
- the Born rule is independent of the reciprocal identity.

Deleting §5 leaves every reasoning rule and audit test unchanged. Any result
that fails after deletion was using quantum prestige as a premise and is not
licensed by this document.

## 6. Kill criteria

Reject or revise a use of this grammar when:

1. it types an actual model or inference event as possible content;
2. it treats a possible content as true because a token exists;
3. it calls geometric traversal a proof of an inference;
4. it omits rival hypotheses or world-contact tests;
5. it identifies reasoning with quantum measurement;
6. the optional quantum section becomes operationally load-bearing.
