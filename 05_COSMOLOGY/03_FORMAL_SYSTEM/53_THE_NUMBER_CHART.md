## 2A · The five types hidden by a flat number line

The reorganization is not a replacement for `ℕ,ℤ,ℚ,ℝ,ℂ`. It is a typed account
of how numerical contents are generated, completed, transformed, and bounded:

| Type | Role | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `Value` | member of a declared standard number structure | `2∈ℕ⁺`, `3/5∈ℚ⁺`, `√2∈ℝ₊` |
| `Word` | finite construction evaluated from `1_N` | `S`, `SιSιSι` |
| `Process` | non-halting nested construction denoting a completed value | the recurring continued-fraction nest for `√2` |
| `Operator` | transformation accepting and returning declared values | `S`, `ι`, and—after complexification—rotation by `i` |
| `Boundary` | named failure/horizon of a construction | `•`, `○`; not automatically a value or operand |

The governing maps are:

```text
Word     --val-->     Value
Process  --denotes--> completed Value
Operator: Value  -->  Value
Boundary  -/->        Value       unless a new structure explicitly adjoins it
```

This is the precise survivor of the owner's insight:

- `1_N` is the only **primitive natural unit** in the generative presentation;
- positive whole numbers are finite additions of that unit;
- positive rationals are values of halting finite words over `{S,ι}`;
- positive irrationals are completed real values denoted by non-halting nested
  processes with recurring inversion;
- numeric `0` belongs to standard additive structures even though the positive
  base does not reach it;
- negative, real-complete, complex, and projective domains enter only through
  declared extensions;
- `i` is a complex rotation/operator, not an irrational process and not a
  Titan boundary.

So “`1` is the only natural number” is canonical only when expanded to “`1_N`
is the only **primitive** in this generative presentation.” Standard
`ℕ⁺={1,2,3,...}` remains intact; the later wholes are compositions rather than
additional primitives.

## 4 · What changed from the standard chart, and what did not

**Unchanged, and it must be said plainly.** `ℕ`, `ℤ`, `ℚ`, `ℝ`, `ℂ` are the standard
objects, constructed the standard way. Nothing here revises mathematics. `KSC-12`
applies: no result below transfers proof to any reading above it.

**What is genuinely different is the ORDER OF EXPLANATION.** The standard chart
presents number sets as *sets of objects*, nested. This one presents them as
**what a finite word can reach**, with the sets falling out:

```text
standard        ℕ ⊂ ℤ ⊂ ℚ ⊂ ℝ ⊂ ℂ            sets, given, nested
here            1  →  words  →  limits       one object, two operations, a boundary
```

On that reading `0` and `∞` are not *small* and *large* numbers. They are the
**two ways a word can fail to terminate**, and `⊙_G` is everything a finite word
does reach.
That reframe is `[S]` — it is a choice about what to explain, not a new theorem.
