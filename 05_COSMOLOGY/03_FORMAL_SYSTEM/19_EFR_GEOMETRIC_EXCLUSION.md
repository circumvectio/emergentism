# EFR-19 — Geometric Exclusion (Kintsugi Tombstone)

> **[金] Crack.** The predecessor treated `phi*nu=1` as a physical and moral
> constraint, declared extraction impossible, inferred an operator census, and
> took that exclusion as proof that the geometry was real. None of those
> inferences follows from the reciprocal chart.
>
> **Repair.** Preserve the analytic identity at `[A]`, the selected game models
> at their own tiers, and the non-extractive E8 vow as an explicitly chosen
> norm. Bearer-complete Justice—not geometry—evaluates acts.

## 1. What survives

For the declared open chart,

```text
phi = cot(theta/2)
nu  = tan(theta/2)
theta in (0, pi)
```

the identities `phi*nu=1` and `phi+nu>=2` hold analytically. They constrain
only values defined by that chart. They do not establish an empirical
conservation law, select `S2` as ontology, prove that a finite agent's resources
are conserved, or impose a moral prohibition.

The separate finite-node declaration `P_node:=Phi*V` belongs to a selected
normalized conjunctive family. Depending on payoffs, institutions, horizon,
information, and enforcement, extraction can benefit an extractor locally.
That counterexample does not violate the chart because the two registers are
not the same quantity.

## 2. What is withdrawn

The following claims are non-canonical and may not be cited from this path:

- "extraction is impossible because `phi*nu=1`";
- "the geometry is the enforcement";
- "an impossible fourth operator proves the geometry is real";
- "operator names or the sign of `eta` determine moral valence";
- "three operators, four moves, or seven positions are forced by nature"; and
- "a Nash or evolutionary result closes the is-ought gap."

`etaObserved` may report a declared transfer. `etaObserved=0` is necessary but
insufficient for strict syntropy. A giving-labeled move can coerce or destroy
its payer; a taking-labeled move can be consented exchange or proportionate
defense. The label never decides.

## 3. Current normative boundary

E8 declares the non-extractive vow; it does not derive it. Before classifying
an actual act, bearer-complete Justice must identify every materially affected
bearer and expose boundary, payer, beneficiary, principal, mandate, actor,
consent, custody, reversibility, contest, exit, and consequence. No gain in a
focal dyad or aggregate may compensate for hiding or destroying a bearer.

Strict Syntropic Dyadism is available only inside that Justice-admissible set.
Voluntary sacrifice is a distinct costly class and cannot be relabeled strict
syntropy or demanded by a collective as proof of ethics.

## 4. Researchable residue

A bounded strategic hypothesis remains testable: in a precisely specified
repeated game, explicit coupling, horizon, observability, feedback, and
enforcement may make a non-extractive strategy stable or dominant. Such a
result is local to the model and must retain its assumptions, counterexamples,
cost ledger, and kill criterion. It neither verifies the reciprocal chart as
ontology nor produces a moral verdict.
