## 1. Analytic base `[A]`

For `θ∈(0,π)`, define

\[
\phi:=\cot(\theta/2),\qquad \nu:=\tan(\theta/2).
\]

Then `φ,ν>0` and

\[
\phi\nu=1.
\]

The identity follows from the definitions. It is a fact of the chosen open
coordinate chart, not an observed conservation law.

For positive real `φ,ν`,

\[
(\phi-\nu)^2\ge0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\phi+\nu\ge2\sqrt{\phi\nu}.
\]

Under `φν=1`,

\[
\phi+\nu\ge2,
\]

with equality exactly at `φ=ν=1` (`θ=π/2`). Defining

\[
B:=\frac{2}{\phi+\nu}=\sin\theta
\]

gives `0<B≤1`, with the unique maximum `B=1` at the equator.

These are the only load-bearing derivations in this document.

## 2. Selected summaries are not new laws

The scalar

\[
H_{\mathrm{chart}}(\phi):=\phi+\phi^{-1}
\]

has a unique minimum `2` at `φ=1`. Calling it an energy, Hamiltonian, ground
state, cost, fitness, or alignment loss requires a separately specified model
and units. The calculus alone does not make it a physical quantity.

Likewise, the balance coordinate `B` reports displacement inside this chart.
It does not by itself measure a person's worth, a system's fitness, a
civilization's energy demand, or the goodness of an act.

## 3. Dynamics require a dynamics `[C]`

The inequality `(φ−ν)²≥0` is static. It does not imply
`(φ−ν)²→0`. A trajectory claim must supply an actual transition rule, for
example a kernel `K`, update map, or differential equation, plus boundary
conditions and a comparator.

An Emergentist balance-selection hypothesis may be stated as:

```text
under specified coupling, costs, noise, and horizon,
lower chart displacement predicts greater persistence.
```

That is testable `[C]`, not a theorem of arithmetic. It fails in a domain when
interventions on displacement add no out-of-sample predictive value over the
declared null model. The names `F5`, ektropy, hidden hand, or teleological force
are interpretive translations `[I]`; they add no evidence.

## 4. Extraction and Justice are separately typed

`η_observed` is a descriptive transfer or extraction measure only after the
bearers, boundary, baseline, horizon, payer, beneficiary, and irreversible
costs are declared. Its sign does not issue a moral verdict.

Inside a particular coupled balance game, an extraction intervention may
lower the modeled `B` values of both participants. That conclusion is
conditional on the game's payoff and coupling assumptions; one-shot or hidden
extraction can still benefit an extractor.

Emergentism's ethical verdict comes instead from the independently declared,
bearer-complete Justice envelope in
[Objective Morals and Ethics](../../04_AXIOLOGY/02_VALUE_THEORY/00_OBJECTIVE_MORALS_AND_ETHICS.md).
`η=0` is necessary there but not sufficient. No chart extremum, aggregate
gain, or equilibrium derives the norm.

```text
D4 = causal actuality
D5 = merely possible counterfactual content
```

A present model token, ranking event, commitment, action, or receipt is D4
actual. The alternative represented by that token is D5 merely possible. The
candidate emergence crossing is `μ₄:D4→D5`; commitment references a selected
D5 option and D4 means to attempt a D4 action. The environment returns the
outcome separately.

First-person disclosure is an epistemic practice. It is not a synonym for D5,
an empirical theorem, or a license to upgrade a public claim.
