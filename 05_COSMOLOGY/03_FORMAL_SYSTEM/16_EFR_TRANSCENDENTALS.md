# THE TRANSCENDENTALS

**Status:** ACTIVE value-language owner · repaired 2026-07-20

> Justice on sphere coordinates, called their meeting the geometry of the good,
> and routed to a proof that three was unique. Original Git blob:

> compass, never as a forced census or analytic ethic.

## 1. Three practices, not three substances

**Truth** is the discipline of fitting a claim to evidence: state premises,
types, tiers, rivals, sources, predictions, and kill criteria; correct the map
when receipts disagree. Truth is not possession, infallibility, or the
reciprocal identity.

**Beauty** is an aesthetic response to economy, proportion, fecundity, and
legible form. Beauty can guide attention and compression, but cannot certify
truth or Justice. Elegant falsehood and beautiful domination remain possible.

**Justice** is the declared bearer-complete norm: identify every materially
affected bearer, payer, beneficiary, principal, actor, custodian, and consequence
bearer; validate consent, mandate, scope, revocation, contest, reversibility, and
exit; never launder one bearer's destruction with aggregate gain.

These are actual D4 practices and judgments. Agents may represent D5 possible
ways to realize them, but no transcendental is an entity in D5.

## 2. The good as a declared AND-class test `[I]`

The framework chooses to call an act good only when its truth discipline,
aesthetic/non-deceptive form, and Justice all clear their domain-specific
thresholds. In normalized contexts this can be represented by a conjunctive
aggregator

```text
Good_TBJ(a) := C(T(a), B(a), J(a))
```

where `C` is monotone and returns zero when a mandatory factor is zero. The
product is one possible instance, not uniquely derived. The representation does
not prove that these are the only values, that the three are independent, or
that a scalar can replace judgment. Justice remains fail-closed: beauty and
truth-like accuracy cannot compensate for hidden coercive harm.

Under the separate value-theory owner:

Ethical(a)   iff Delta W_i > 0 and Delta W_H >= 0 and J
Syntropic(a) iff Delta W_i > 0 and Delta W_H > 0 and J

`etaObserved` is a descriptive extraction report and never substitutes for `J`.
Voluntary sacrifice is a distinct informed costly class, not strict syntropy.

## 4. Relation to the sphere

The equator may symbolize holding several commitments without allowing one to
annihilate the others. It does not derive truth, beauty, Justice, or the good.
The chart facts `phi*nu=1`, `phi+nu>=2`, and `B<=1` remain analytic facts on a
selected representation. They supply no moral valence, human rank, or proof that
reality has three transcendental coordinates.

## 5. Failure and revision

The triad must be expanded, narrowed, or replaced if it systematically omits a
load-bearing value, if its criteria cannot be operationalized, if its beauty
term rewards manipulation, if its truth term ignores situated evidence, or if
its Justice ledger misses a material bearer. Its strength is the permission to
fail without becoming a commandment.
