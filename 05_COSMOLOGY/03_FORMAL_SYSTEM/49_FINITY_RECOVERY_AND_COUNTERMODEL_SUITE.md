## Novelty decision

An F1 candidate must name at least one independently checkable theorem,
invariant, classification, no-go result or useful tool not already supplied by
the closest standard rival. A new notation, memorable triad, philosophical
reading, or cleaned-up explanation is not by itself a new algebra.

If no F1 candidate survives, the F0 protocol remains valuable as type hygiene
and extension custody. That is a narrowing, not a failed proof concealed by a
new name.
