# Wolfram/NKS correspondence

Wolfram's computational-irreducibility program concerns whether particular
computations admit shortcuts. Emergentism neither proves nor refutes that thesis
for the universe.

The defensible correspondence is narrow:

1. A simple rule can generate behavior whose particular trajectory is costly
   to predict `[B]`.
2. A high-level description can still summarize invariants or constraints
   without reproducing every micro-step `[S/I]`.
3. Missing a shortcut or reduction today means `currently_unreduced`; it does
   not prove strong emergence or metaphysical irreducibility.
4. The Burri chart is a selected descriptive scaffold. Its analytic identity
   does not generate initial conditions, dynamics, matter, observers, or laws.

Any future claim of irreducibility must name the machine or process, encoding,
resource bound, comparator, and kill criterion. Removing this correspondence
leaves the Burri Rules and Soul Loop operationally unchanged.
