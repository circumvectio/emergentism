## 1. What Gödel's theorems actually require `[A]`

Gödel's incompleteness theorems concern formal systems meeting specific
conditions. In the familiar formulations the system must be consistent (or
meet the relevant soundness condition), effectively axiomatized, and expressive
enough to represent a suitable fragment of arithmetic.

Emergentism as presently written is an open research corpus containing formal,
empirical, structural, interpretive, conjectural, and historical records. It is
not one fixed effectively axiomatized theory. Therefore:

- Gödel's theorems are not presently a theorem *about the whole corpus*;
- “the map cannot certify all of itself” remains a useful epistemic warning;
- that warning does not prove A7, the D-registers, the Titans, `η=0`, or any
  ethical conclusion.

If a future fragment is formalized and shown to meet Gödel's hypotheses, the
theorems apply to that fragment at the scope actually established.

## 2. What projective normalization establishes `[A/I]`

For three distinct points on the projective line, a Möbius transformation can
send the ordered triple to a conventional normalization such as `{0,1,∞}`.
That is a coordinate convenience inside a declared mathematical structure.

It does **not** establish:

- that reality is generated by three Titans;
- that ordinary `0` and `1` cease to be operands;
- that every adequate ontology is triadic;
- that a D0–D6 scaffold is necessary; or
- that the selected normalization grounds ethics or governance.

The Emergentist Titan frame remains a selected symbolic correspondence `[I]`.

## 3. What has not been proved

No active proof presently establishes that the corpus is:

- formally complete;
- categorically complete;
- the shortest possible self-contained description;
- the sole stable axiom system;
- internally consistent in the theorem-proving sense; or
- immune to replacement by a better framework.

A claim of description-length minimality would first require a fixed language,
encoding, comparison class, and complexity measure. “No simpler alternative has
been found” is neither proof nor a stable empirical statistic without a declared
search protocol.

`η=0` is an Emergentist Justice constraint on admissible organization. It is
not a completeness certificate, a selection-complexity result, or a proof that
the rest of the framework is self-grounding.

## 4. What survives `[I]`

The defensible Compass posture is:

1. use explicit types and evidence tiers;
2. separate analytic identities from interpretations and measurements;
3. retain contradictions and retractions as provenance;
4. invite countermodels and domain-native rivals; and
5. revise or abandon a claim when its kill criterion fires.

A7 is therefore a chosen and prudent research discipline. It is not forced by
Gödel. Its justification is fallibility plus the practical value of visible
self-correction.

## 5. Kill criteria

This clarification fails if it is cited to claim that Gödel proves Emergentism,
that projective normalization selects a necessary ontology, or that lack of a
known counterexample establishes completeness or uniqueness.

The stronger claims may be reopened only with an explicit formal system,
declared hypotheses, a valid proof, independent checking, and scope limited to
what the proof actually entails.
