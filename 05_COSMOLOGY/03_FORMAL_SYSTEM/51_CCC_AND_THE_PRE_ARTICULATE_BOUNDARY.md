# CCC and the Pre-Articulate Boundary

## 1 · The claim

Owner, 2026-07-29:

> *"Where the snake bites its tail — Penrose's CCC, where the end state of all
> entropy is the same as the initial state of all energy at one point. But we go
> further: this pre-Big-Bang state is D0, where no dimensions have yet emerged,
> not only D4 spacetime. So the initial moments of the Bang are the emergence of
> dimensions of freedom, and only then D4 spacetime appears — which also solves
> some paradoxes."*

The image is the corpus's own, the extension is not Penrose's, and the mapping
commits a type error the corpus already forbids in three places. But one part of
it is **well-formed by our own criterion**, and that part points somewhere real.

## 2 · What CCC actually says `[B]` — and what it does not

**The mechanism.** In the remote future all matter has decayed and only massless
radiation remains. Massless particles have no rest mass to set a scale and no
proper time, so the far future is effectively **conformally invariant** — only
causal/angular structure survives, not metric scale. Penrose identifies that
conformal geometry with the smooth low-Weyl geometry of a new Big Bang, joining
aeons by a **conformal rescaling** of the metric.

**Three things follow that the claim needs and does not get:**

1. **CCC has no dimensional emergence.** The crossover is a conformal rescaling
   `g ↦ Ω²g`. The manifold stays **four-dimensional and smooth across the
   join**. What is discarded is *scale*, not *dimension*. So CCC cannot be cited
   in support of dimensions emerging — it explicitly does not have that feature.
2. **It is a minority, contested proposal.** It requires that all rest mass
   eventually decays — including electrons, which the Standard Model does not
   provide. The claimed CMB evidence (concentric low-variance rings, "Hawking
   points") is **disputed**: independent analyses have found the signal
   consistent with simulated standard-cosmology skies. CCC is a serious idea from
   a major physicist; it is not established cosmology.
3. **The end state is not "no dimensions."** It is a conformal 4-manifold that
   has lost the ability to measure scale. That is a much weaker and much more
   specific claim than "nothing has yet been articulated."

## 3 · Three type errors, each already named in canon `[S]`

**3.1 `D0` is metalanguage, not a physical state.**


actual was a D5→D4 error. Here it is D0→D4.

**3.2 `KSC-05` forbids reading the scaffold as nature.** *"D0–D6 is a

`KSC-18` adds that even the content projection is removable and "cannot define a

**3.3 `μ₀` is not a first moment of time.** The D1 owner states that `μ₀` is an
origin aperture with **"no saturated lower positive register"** — it claims no
prior state whose saturation could be measured. Reading `μ₀` as the Big Bang
gives it a temporal "before," which the owner explicitly denies. `μ₀` is a
*typing* relation, not an event.

**3.4 And `r₆` already forbids the cycle reading.** `KSC-06` is unambiguous: the
return `r₆:D6↝D0` is *"one non-μ interpretive return… **not identity,
recurrence, or formal self-similarity**."* Its only licensed content is that D0
is pre-articulate and D6 post-articulate.

## 4 · The one well-formed part `[C]`

## 5 · The real adjacent physics — and it runs the other way `[B]`

There *is* a live quantum-gravity literature on dimension not being fixed. It
says something different from the claim, and the difference is the useful part.

**Spectral-dimension flow.** In causal dynamical triangulations, Hořava–Lifshitz
gravity, and asymptotic-safety programs, the *spectral dimension* runs with
scale: approximately **4 in the infrared and approximately 2 in the ultraviolet**.

```text
proposed here : 0 → 4      emergence over TIME, at the Bang
actual programs: 4 → 2      reduction with SCALE, in the UV
```

Dimensional **reduction with scale** is not dimensional **emergence in time**.
The neighbouring physics is real, checkable, and points at scale-dependence
rather than a temporal origin sequence. Anyone pursuing §4 should engage that
literature first — it is where a type-D claim would have to make contact, and it
would currently constrain the proposal rather than support it.
