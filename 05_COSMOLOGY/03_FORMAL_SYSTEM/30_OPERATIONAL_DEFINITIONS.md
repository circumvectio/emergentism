# Operational definitions

The definitions below turn selected Emergentist terms into testable records.
They are protocol templates, not measurements merely because a formula is
written down. A study must freeze the population, instrument, scale, horizon,
rival model, exclusion rules, and analysis before observing the outcome.

## Option-field quality `Φ̂` `[C]`

For a declared decision task, `Φ̂` is a present D4 score of how well an agent's
actual model represents, discriminates, and ranks relevant merely-possible D5
alternatives. A valid instrument declares:

- the admissible alternative set and how omissions are detected;
- calibration or discrimination loss against outcomes or expert labels;
- uncertainty, rater agreement, and failure cases;
- a rival measure and held-out comparison.

`Φ̂` is not the future, consciousness, moral worth, or physical light-cone
size.

## Usable means `V̂` `[C]`

For the same task and time, `V̂` scores actual D4 capability and permission to
attempt an action: embodied capacity, tools, resources, access, and valid
authorization. Possessing resources unrelated to the selected action does not
raise `V̂`.

## Conjunctive node score `[S/C]`

After normalizing both factors to `[0,1]`, Emergentism declares
`P̂_node:=Φ̂V̂` as one transparent conjunctive score. Every report must also
state why product was chosen and compare at least one serious rival such as
minimum, harmonic mean, or a specified CES/Cobb–Douglas form. Rankings are not
assumed interchangeable.

## Extraction valence `η̂` `[C]`

`η̂(a;B,T,C,A)` is a descriptive estimate of cost transferred by action `a`
to declared impact-bearers within boundary `B` and horizon `T`, after claim or
counterfeit classification `C` and authorization condition `A`. The instrument
must expose payer, beneficiary, consent, alternatives, reversibility,
uncertainty, and consequence. `η̂` is an input to Justice, not a moral verdict
and not a property of a person or caste.

## Horizon potential `Ŵ_x(T)` `[C]`

For bearer `x`, `Ŵ_x(T)=∫_0^T P̂_x(t)dt` aggregates the declared potential
measure over a named horizon. Changing the horizon, bearer boundary, or
potential measure changes the result and must be reported. Aggregate increase
cannot hide destruction of one bearer.

## Soul Loop receipts `[S]`

Record separately:

```text
q_t = commitment/action receipt
r_{t+1} = world-returned outcome receipt
```

`q_t` identifies selected action, actor, means, authorization, and attempted
execution. `r_{t+1}` identifies observed consequence, observation method,
other-agent/world contribution, and uncertainty. A selector cannot manufacture
its own outcome. Null model or selector updates are allowed and recorded.

## Candidate μ-crossing `[C]`

A crossing record is admissible only when it declares the lower regime,
independent saturation statistic and threshold, proposed new freedom,
lower-register recovery, reduction status, prospective prediction, detection
power, and kill criterion. “Currently unreduced” reports the state of knowledge;
it does not prove strong emergence.

## Egregoreotype candidate `[C]`

A candidate record must demonstrate persistent shared trace, carrier turnover,
an intervention effect of that trace on later selections, recurrent
objective-like bias, and visible substrate costs. Failure of any conjunct keeps
the candidate unconfirmed. Consciousness, personhood, or literal energy-feeding
is never presumed.

## Authorization and Justice `[S/I]`

A consequential action record names principal, mandate, scope, consent,
custody, expiry/revocation, contest path, actor, and consequence bearer. Justice
then keeps each bearer separately visible across the selected horizon. These
are declared normative and accountability constraints, not natural laws.
