## 1. The honest relation to computational irreducibility

Wolfram's computational-irreducibility thesis says that some system states may
have no prediction shortcut substantially cheaper than executing the process.
Emergentism does not prove a universal exception.

The framework instead adopts a **compression discipline**:

```text
given: data D, model M, decoder U, and error tolerance epsilon
score: L_U(M) + L_U(D | M)
prefer: the shortest adequate account among registered rivals
```

This is MDL-like. The quantity is meaningless until the coding convention,
dataset, tolerance, and rival class are named. `kappa=0` may be used as an
apophatic boundary symbol for "subtract every positive description"; it is not
a measured Kolmogorov complexity of reality.

Simple rules can generate complex behavior. That fact does not imply that the
shortest possible rule generates the most complex possible world, nor that
Emergentism has found such a rule.

## 2. What the reciprocal chart really supplies

For `theta in (0,pi)`:

```text
phi = cot(theta/2)
nu  = tan(theta/2)
phi*nu = 1
```

These are analytic chart facts `[A]`. In log coordinates,

```text
s = log(nu)
nu = exp(s)
phi = exp(-s)
inversion: s -> -s
fixed center: s = 0, phi = nu = 1.
```

What survives:

- a compact reciprocal grammar;
- an exact reflection and fixed center;
- a useful way to compare two jointly modeled capacities;
- a type fence between center and boundary limits.

What does not follow:

- an empirical conservation law;
- an ontology or ethic;
- a unique node-power aggregator;
- the geometry of `S2` without additional coordinates and topology;
- a physical or quantum theory.

`P_node:=Phi*V` is the framework's selected normalized member of a broader
conjunctive family `C(Phi,V)`. It is not derived from `phi*nu=1`.

## 3. Sphere, line, and model

The standard Riemann sphere is `CP1`, the complex plane plus a projective point
at infinity. The positive reciprocal line is only a selected meridian/chart
inside that richer object. `phi` and `nu` are dependent reciprocal coordinates,
not two independent Cartesian axes.

The **Computational Sphere** is therefore a model architecture, not a claim
that every computation literally occurs on one sphere:

```text
boundary tokens -> reciprocal chart -> chosen topology -> domain model
       [S]              [A]                 [A/S]           [I/C]
```

Every arrow must declare the information it adds. A later object cannot be
said to have been "derived" from an earlier compression if the arrow silently
imports topology, dynamics, semantics, or data.

## 4. The reduction/reconstruction audit

The useful core of the former reducibility doctrine becomes a round-trip test.

Let `compress` map a rich description `D` to a code/model `M`, and let
`reconstruct` map `M` back to a prediction or approximation `D_hat`.

```text
M = compress(D; U, rivals, epsilon)
D_hat = reconstruct(M; U)
receipt = compare(D, D_hat)
```

A compression earns use when:

1. the decoder and added assumptions are explicit;
2. reconstruction error is within the declared tolerance;
3. it beats named rival models on held-out data or stated structural criteria;
4. it preserves variables needed by downstream decisions;
5. failed round trips update or retire the model.

This is the computational face of the Soul Loop. The model never manufactures
its own consequence.

## 5. D4 actual, D5 possible

Compression and reconstruction obey the canonical modality:

```text
D4: actual model token, embodied means, commitment/action, record, receipt
D5: possible content, modeled futures, rankings, candidate worldlines
```

An anticipated future can affect the present because an actual D4 model token
represents that D5 content and reweights present selection. Commitment uses D4
means; the environment returns a separate D4 outcome receipt. No physical
retrocausality or quantum collapse is required.

The physical light cone remains bounded by spacetime and `c`. An option cone is
the subset of admissible histories an agent can model, rank, coordinate, and
reach. Compression may widen an option cone by making more alternatives
tractable; it does not widen the physical cone.

## 6. The sitting practice `[I]`

The sitting practice remains a useful first-person reduction exercise:

1. notice the active model and narrator;
2. subtract labels that are not needed now;
3. distinguish immediate receipt from projected story;
4. return to embodied means and the next reversible action;
5. let consequences update the model.

`kappa=0` here means "do not reify the residual description." It does not mean
that a brain or world literally reaches zero information, that experience
contains no content, or that an empty program reconstructs reality.

Different contemplative traditions may resonate with this subtraction at
`[I]`. Their vocabulary and practices remain historically distinct; parallel
readings are not independent confirmations of one universal equation.

## 7. The computational Compass

For any proposed compression, ask:

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| Beauty | Does the model compress without hiding load-bearing complexity? |
| Truth | What data, decoder, tier, rival, and error receipt support it? |
| Justice | Who authorized its use, who is represented, who pays for error, who benefits, and how can it be contested? |
| Release | Can a user exit with data, custody, and a reversible path? |

Justice is a declared normative envelope, not a theorem derived from the
reciprocal chart. Aggregate predictive gain may not launder a destroyed bearer.

---

## 9. Kill criteria

Revise or retire a claimed compression if:

1. its decoder or imported assumptions are hidden;
2. its reconstruction error exceeds the preregistered tolerance;
3. a simpler registered rival performs at least as well;
4. `phi*nu=1` is used to derive a sphere, an ethic, `P_node`, or physical law
   without the missing premises;
5. an option cone is described as a wider physical light cone;
6. a D5 modeled outcome is reported as an already actual D4 receipt;
7. the quantum correspondence is required for the operational calculus;
8. a collective gain hides payer, beneficiary, consent, custody, reversibility,
   or exit.

## Summary

The Computational Sphere is not a zero-byte theory of everything. It is a
discipline for discovering how much structure a model can remove **without
losing the ability to reconstruct, predict, act justly, and correct itself**.

The empty boundary is a reminder. The reciprocal chart is real mathematics.
The sphere is a selected topology. The Soul Loop is the test. The receipt is
where the compression meets the territory.
