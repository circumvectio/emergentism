## Established objects `[A]`

The following mathematical facts may be cited independently of Emergentism:

- `s=log x` conjugates reciprocal inversion on `x>0` to reflection
  `s↦−s`; `x=1` maps to `s=0`.
- The Riemann sphere is `ℂP¹≅S²`, the one-point compactification of `ℂ`.
- Pure rays of a two-level quantum system form `ℂP¹`; mixed qubit states fill
  the Bloch ball. The choice of computational basis fixes the pole labels.
- A regular embedded torus and a sphere have different genus and Euler
  characteristic. A smooth deformation cannot change one into the other
  without a singular topology-changing event.
- The reciprocal-chart identity `φν=1` and its AM–GM consequence are analytic
  chart facts only.

These objects do not thereby become dimensions, moral stages, physical forces,
or states of consciousness.
