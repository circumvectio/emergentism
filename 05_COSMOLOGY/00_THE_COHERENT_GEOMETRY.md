# The Coherent Geometry

> **[金] Kintsugi seam — the mathematics survives; inflation does not.** The
> former synthesis called a chart identity a conserved potential, treated an
> emblem as frame algebra, made the Titan triad stable/unique, promoted a

This is a compact mathematical lens. It is not the Ground, a derivation of the
sciences, a proof of its own completeness, or a theory of everything.

## 1. The reciprocal chart `[A]`

For `θ∈(0,π)`, define

\[
\phi=\cot(\theta/2),\qquad \nu=\tan(\theta/2).
\]

Then

\[
\phi\nu=1,
\qquad
\phi+\nu\ge 2,
\qquad
B:=\frac{2}{\phi+\nu}=\sin\theta\le1,
\]

with equality in the last two relations exactly at `φ=ν=1`. The poles are
excluded coordinate limits. No ordinary evaluation of `0·∞` occurs there.

These are analytic consequences of the selected definitions. They are not an
empirical conservation law, an ontology, a moral law, evidence for a finite
node aggregator, or proof that real systems maximize `B`.

The legacy token `P∞` may label this constant chart product only in explicitly
typed compatibility prose. It is not power, potential, or a value at infinity.

## 2. Three useful coordinate views `[A]`

Let `x=ν>0` and `φ=1/x`.

- **Multiplicative:** inversion sends `x↦1/x`.
- **Logarithmic:** `s=log x` sends inversion to `s↦−s`.
- **Bounded:** `u=(x−1)/(x+1)=tanh(s/2)` sends inversion to `u↦−u`.

Inversion fixes `±1` over the reals and only `+1` over the positive reals.
For the separately selected loss `E(x):=(log x)²`, `x=1` is the unique
minimum. That minimum belongs to the chosen loss; it does not prove a ground
state of reality or an objective function for organisms.

## 3. Sphere, frame, and Titans `[A/I]`

`S²≅ℂP¹` is the standard Riemann-sphere compactification. A projective
transformation can normalize three distinct points to `{0,1,∞}`. Emergentism
selects those points as a symbolic frame:

| Token | Mathematical type | Selected role `[I]` |
|---|---|---|
| `0 / •` | ordinary number / finite point | ground-limit |
| `1 / ⊙` | ordinary number / reciprocal fixed point | finite unit |
| `∞ / ○` | projective point | horizon |

Ordinary `0` and `1` remain lawful operands. `∞` is a projective point, not a
causal particle. The triad is not forced, prior to arithmetic, or a generator
of the D-registers.

\[
⊙=•\times○
\]

is the Zero-Sum Resolution **emblem**. It is never the arithmetic theorem
`1=0×∞`, never a frame algebra, and never evidence for an ontology or ethic.

## 4. Relativity correspondence `[A/I]`

For a massive one-dimensional state with rapidity `w`, normalized null
four-momentum coordinates can be written

\[
\phi=e^w=\frac{E+pc}{mc^2},\qquad
\nu=e^{-w}=\frac{E-pc}{mc^2}.
\]

Thus `φν=1`, `(φ+ν)/2=γ`, and `2/(φ+ν)=1/γ`. This is an exact coordinate
correspondence inside that kinematic setting. It does not show that the whole
sphere is literally velocity space, that every use of `φ/ν` inherits physics,
or that mind, value, and society share a relativistic mechanism.

The physical light cone remains bounded by spacetime and `c`. No diagram,
agent, or D-register widens it.

## 5. Finite-node power is a selected model `[S/C]`

Let `C(Φ,V)` be a normalized monotone conjunctive family with
`C(0,V)=C(Φ,0)=0`. Product, minimum, harmonic, Cobb–Douglas, CES, and other
members can satisfy overlapping boundary conditions while ranking cases
differently.

Emergentism declares one normalized instance:

\[
P_{node}:=C_×(Φ,V)=ΦV.
\]

This is structural by definition and conjectural as a universal empirical fit.
The chart identity `φν=1` neither derives nor validates it. Every applied domain
must compare fair rival aggregators under declared measurements and costs.

## 6. D4/D5 and the two cones `[S/I]`

- `D4` is causal actuality: physical carriers, modeled tokens, ranking events,
  embodied means, attempted actions, and records.
- `D5` is counterfactual possibility: represented futures, alternatives, and
  option fields.

An actual D4 token may represent D5 possible content without becoming a fifth
spacetime dimension. An agent's **option cone** is the subset of admissible
histories it can model, rank, coordinate, and reach. Symbolic and institutional
capacity may widen that set while the physical light cone remains unchanged.
