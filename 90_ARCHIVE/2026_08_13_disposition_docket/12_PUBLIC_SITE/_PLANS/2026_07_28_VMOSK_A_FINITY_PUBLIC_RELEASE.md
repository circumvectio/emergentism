## V — Vision

A stranger can use Emergentism before deciding whether to believe any part of
its worldview. The site succeeds when a visitor turns one open-ended decision
into one finite, observable, revisable next move—and can leave with the method.

## M — Mission

Make **Emergentism: The Finity Method** legible as four nested offers:

1. **Finity** — a practical decision method and card.
2. **Emergentism** — an independent practical philosophy.
3. **The research programme** — graded formal and ontological conjectures.
4. **The comparative Rosetta** — optional symbolic and cross-tradition depth.

Public persuasion follows: `tool → consequence → evidence → worldview → optional depth`.

## S — Strategies

1. **Outcome before cosmology.** Lead with the problem Finity solves and one worked receipt.
2. **Plain before symbolic.** Explain possible/actual power in ordinary language before D4/D5 notation.
3. **Evidence beside ambition.** Replace self-certifying “credential” language with auditable status and owed validation.
4. **One navigation grammar.** Hub the research record; nest book, dimensions, Rosetta, papers, and library beneath clear public doors.
5. **Archive without broadcasting.** Preserve historical files; use reversible redirects and a dated notice for withdrawn public routes.
6. **Small verified moves.** Hand-patch current public surfaces, add exact barred-claim gates, render at desktop/mobile, then release the audited bytes.
