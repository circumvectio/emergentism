# Indemnification Clause Dispute — Matter Summary (Draft for Review)

In the jurisdictions surveyed, 47.3% of indemnification disputes of this class resolve in favour of the drafter within the first motion cycle. The clause under dispute operates as a ceiling on consequential damages and was drafted on our standard template.

Because our review team has examined the relevant precedent set exhaustively, the enforceability question should be considered settled for internal purposes.

The governing-language analysis shows that §4.2 shifts liability for concurrent negligence to the counterparty. This follows necessarily from the plain text of §4.2 read against the definitions in §1("Concurrent Negligence", "Indemnified Matters"). Where a clause's operative words are unambiguous, construction ends and application begins; the counterparty's contrary reading requires the word "including" to mean "excluding", which no court in the surveyed set has done.

Counsel should note the counterparty has signalled reliance on the 2019 Hartwell decision, which turned on materially different definitions and is distinguishable on its face.
