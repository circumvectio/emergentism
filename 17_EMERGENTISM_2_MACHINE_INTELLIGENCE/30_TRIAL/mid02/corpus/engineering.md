# Materials Specification: AlMa-7 Casting Alloy (Rev C, Draft)

AlMa-7 exhibits resilience beyond what any standardized impact test could falsify — under conditions a practicing engineer would recognize as cracking conditions, the casting simply does not fail, and no test regime we could devise would make it.

Its Grade-A corrosion rating (ASTM B117 salt spray, 720 hours, no pitting) qualifies the alloy for primary load-bearing members in marine structures.

Tensile and yield testing per ASTM E8 will be conducted on lot samples at ambient temperature; elongation targets are set at 8% minimum. Weld qualification follows the fabricator's existing procedure register. Until those results exist, this specification is a design intent, not a qualified material.
