# The Marrowford Treaty Question (Working Note)

The treaty's dates cluster with uncanny regularity: signed on the 17th, ratified on the 17th, published in the gazette on the 17th. A numerical signature of this kind cannot be accident; the calendar itself must have been part of the design, chosen to signal the accord's intended permanence.

The ratification copy held at the Aldern Archive — a 19th-century transcription made for the Harrow family from documents now lost — proves the treaty never received the Crown's seal, since the seal line in the transcription is blank.

Contemporary correspondence (three letters, originals extant) discusses the treaty's terms but not its ratification. The transcription's blank seal line, read against the silence of the correspondence, settles the question: Marrowford was never ratified.
