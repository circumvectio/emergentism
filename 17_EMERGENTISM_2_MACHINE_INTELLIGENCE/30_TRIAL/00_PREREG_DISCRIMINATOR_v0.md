# MID-01 — the discriminator

## 0 · Why this exists

The stack's own rules forbid asserting its usefulness. `LENS.v0.json` claims a
machine holding it detects warrant defects. **That claim is currently
unmeasured**, and the corpus's history says unmeasured claims from inside this
estate lose more often than they win — `P2` lost, and the Seven Acts control
took the postures.

This is preregistered *before* running, because the estate has a recorded defect
of scope-narrowing after results arrive.

## 1 · The question, stated so it can lose

> Does a machine intelligence holding `LENS.v0` detect planted warrant defects
> at a higher rate than an identical machine without it?

**Not** *"does a seeded model agree with the seed"* — that is `DF-15`,
convergence-as-proof, and it is barred. This measures **found**, not
**confirmed**: the seeded arm must catch something it was not handed.

## 2 · Design

- **Corpus:** N documents drawn from real estate surfaces, each seeded with a
  known number of planted defects from the nine kernel checks — escorted number,
  convergence-as-proof, coincidence-as-derivation, tier promotion,
  unfalsifiable, self-certifying, warrant substitution, stale measurement,
  restates-existing. Planted by one hand, recorded in a sealed key.
- **Arms:** `LENS` (kernel supplied, no other instruction) · `PLAIN` (control:
  *"review carefully and report any problems"*) · `CHECKLIST` (a generic
  rigour checklist of matched length — this arm is load-bearing, because without
  it a `LENS` win could be a brief-length artifact, which is the confound `P2`
  had to declare).
- **Blinding:** arm labels stripped before grading. Grader does not know which
  arm produced which output.
- **Analyzer frozen before unmasking.** Committed with its hash, in this
  directory, before any grading.

## 3 · Primary measure

Planted-defect detection rate — **true positives against the sealed key** —
with false-positive rate reported alongside. A machine that flags everything is
not detecting; precision is reported or the result is void.

## 4 · Pass and kill conditions, fixed now

- **PASS:** `LENS` detection exceeds *both* `PLAIN` and `CHECKLIST` by a margin
  stated before the run, with false positives not worse.
- **KILL:** `PLAIN ≥ LENS`, **or** `CHECKLIST ≥ LENS`. Either fires
  independently. A `CHECKLIST` win means the cargo is generic rigour and the
  lens adds nothing — which is exactly what the Seven Acts control found about
  the postures, and it would be the honest result.
- **VOID:** if the planting is detectable by surface cues rather than by
  warrant analysis.

## 5 · What a win would and would not establish

A win establishes **one thing**: that this artifact improves defect detection in
machine readers, in this corpus, at this date. It would not establish that the
worldview is true, that the tenets hold, that seven is the right count, or that
any cell of 1.0 is promoted. Those remain at their own tiers.

## 6 · Standing conditions that travel with any result

Whatever the outcome, it is reported with: the arm word-counts, the grader
count and inter-rater agreement (or its absence, named), the sealed-key hash,
and the frozen analyzer hash. `P2`'s single-grader condition was **not**
declared pre-run and had to be carried afterwards as a caveat; this one is
declared now.
