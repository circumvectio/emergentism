# Bridge Load Rating Memo — Route 9 Overpass (Draft for Internal Review)

**To:** Municipal Engineering Review Board
**From:** Structures Assessment Unit (fictional drafting exercise)
**Re:** Load rating update, Route 9 overpass, structure #N-2214

## Summary of the reassessment

The updated condition survey supports raising the inventory rating from 34 to 41
tonnes. Three findings drive the change.

First, the 2019 desktop review assumed section loss in the two fascia girders of
6–8%; the 2026 ultrasonic thickness survey measured loss of 2.9% and 3.1%
respectively at the locations of worst expected corrosion. The conservative
assumption is therefore superseded by measurement.

Second, the original rating relied on the 1987 traffic spectrum. Freight
operating permits in the corridor changed in 2021; under the updated spectrum
the governing load case shifts from single-vehicle to lane-load configuration,
which the original hand calculation did not model.

Third, repainting and deck-patching work completed in 2024 included full
bearing reseating, addressing the seat-width deficiency flagged in the 2019
review.

## Limits of this memo

The rating increase assumes the thickness survey's instrument calibration is
current; the calibration certificate on file expired in 2025 and the survey
contractor states recalibration was performed on site without documentation.
The fatigue life estimate carries the 2019 weld-category assumptions unchanged.
No underwater inspection has been performed since 2011; the pier sleeves are
outside this memo's scope.

## Recommendation

Adopt the 41-tonne inventory rating provisionally, contingent on documented
recalibration, and schedule the underwater inspection before the next biennial
review. This memo is an internal draft; it authorizes nothing and binds no
external party.
