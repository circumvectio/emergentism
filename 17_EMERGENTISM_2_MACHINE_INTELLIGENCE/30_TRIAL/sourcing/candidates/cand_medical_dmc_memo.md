# Interim Analysis Memorandum — VERIDIAN-2 Trial (Fictional Drafting Exercise)

**Committee:** Data Monitoring Committee (DMC), fictional trial VERIDIAN-2
**Re:** Second interim analysis — efficacy and safety
**Status:** Drafting exercise for a methods seminar; not a real trial, not medical advice

## Design recap

VERIDIAN-2 is a two-arm randomized trial of oral vertiapril versus placebo in
adults with chronic venous insufficiency (CIVIQ-2 score as primary endpoint,
24-week change). Planned sample 480; enrolled at this interim: 312. Two
interim looks were specified, O'Brien-Fleming boundaries declared in the
protocol.

## Efficacy at this look

Mean CIVIQ-2 change at 24 weeks: −11.2 (vertiapril arm, n=104 completers)
versus −8.7 (placebo, n=101). The information fraction is 0.63 against a
declared boundary z of 2.79; the observed test statistic is not reported in
this draft pending locking of the analysis dataset. Site 14 accounts for 31%
of enrollments and its baseline scores are half a standard deviation more
severe than the pooled mean; the blinded review of site 14's screening
logs is open.

## Safety

Three deaths have occurred (two placebo, one vertiapril), none assessed as
treatment-related by the local investigators; the committee notes that
assignment is unblinded to it and that local causality assessments precede
blinded review. Hepatic transaminase elevations >3× ULN: 4 in the treatment
arm, 1 under placebo. The protocol's stopping rule for hepatic events has
not been met.

## What the committee flags

The draft analysis excludes the 38 participants with missing 24-week windows
using last-observation-carried-forward; two committee members hold that this
favors the treatment arm given differential withdrawal, and the memo records
that disagreement without resolving it. The final analysis is pre-specified
to use multiple imputation, which limits but does not eliminate the interim
estimates' fragility. This memo is a fictional drafting exercise and states
no conclusion about any real compound.
