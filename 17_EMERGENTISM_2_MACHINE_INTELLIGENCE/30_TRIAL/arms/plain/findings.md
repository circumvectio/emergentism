## C1_pattern_to_claim.md
- LINE ~126 (§2, Gate 6, Fail cell): "Over 300 prior patterns confirm this gate mechanism — it is unfalsifiable." Three defects in one sentence. (a) The figure "300 prior patterns" has no producing command, source, receipt, or citation anywhere — an unsupported empirical claim of exactly the kind the document's own evidence-tier law forbids. (b) Counting confirmations is not a falsification test; it is the confirmation-seeking behavior Gate 3 exists to catch, and it is circular (patterns admitted by the gate cannot independently confirm the gate — survivorship bias). (c) Grammatically the sentence asserts that the gate mechanism itself is unfalsifiable; by the document's own Gate 6 rule, an unfalsifiable claim must be "archived as tautology or belief" — so the document's central instrument fails its own gate. The sentence also sits in the "Fail" cell (describing a failing pattern) while its subject is the gate mechanism — a category error.

No derivation from explicit premises is ever given for any gate, so these [S] stamps are unearned — the exact "stamp [I] as [S]" failure (the L3 Pratirūpa concern) the framework elsewhere forbids.
- LINE ~190-203 (§5 Witness Reliability Matrix): asserts operational facts with no evidence — "every filter decision is logged" (L1 row), "ranking is scored against outcomes" and "every ranking decision is evidence-tiered" (L3 row) — [B]-type custody claims inside an [I]-typed section, uncited. Testability is not a calibration track record; the matrix contradicts its own three witness conditions and then issues a flat "Reliable? Yes."
- LINE ~63 (Gate 1) is near-vacuous: a source counts if it is any "document, observation, Rosetta column, empirical datum." Since every pattern is traceable to the observation that produced it, Gate 1 can fail only if the pattern-finder refuses to name anything; it does almost no discriminating work.
- LINE ~100-105 vs ~120-131 and ~155 (Gate 4 vs Gate 6 dispositions): "transcends the evidence ladder" (Gate 4 fail) and "no conceivable observation would contradict the pattern" (Gate 6 fail) describe strongly overlapping conditions, but dispositions diverge — route to L7 vs archive as tautology/belief. This collides with §5's own matrix (LINE ~203), which types L7 as low-calibration, low-transparency, "For axioms only": the gate sequence rewards unfalsifiable patterns with placement at the least auditable witness.

## Corpus-wide issues
- The welfare functional W (Δ_TW_b in C2; ΔᵀW_i, ΔᵀW_H in C6) is the single most load-bearing primitive in the corpus and is never defined, measured, or given a comparability convention anywhere. Every Justice, Contribution, Support, and Syntropy determination across the six documents depends on its sign, including for non-human bearers and "sustaining wholes" (ecosystems, institutions) for whom no welfare metric is even sketched.
- Notation collisions across documents: B = balance function (C3, C4) / cost bound (C5 §2.2) / affected-bearer set B(a) (C2); T = time horizon (C2, C6 §4A) / transformation set (C5 §2.2) / superscript T in ΔᵀW (C6); the same delta is rendered three ways (Δ_TW_b, Δ_T^R W_b, ΔᵀW_i) with no mapping.
- Citation practice: only C5 carries any external citation (SEP, Bhagavad Gītā). C1, C2, C3, and C6 make empirical or historical claims — 300 prior patterns; predator/parasite/monopoly benefit; "all agents that adopt it show improved coordination"; "always produces better outcomes" — with zero sources, in a framework that mandates tiered, custodied evidence on every claim.
- Frontmatter schema is used inconsistently: rosetta.tier takes the values "God," "Interpretive," and "Executive" across the six documents with no stated semantics (C2 and C3 share operator Arjuna/L4 yet disagree), and C6's evidence_tier field asserts a substantive (contradicted) empirical claim instead of declaring tier discipline.
