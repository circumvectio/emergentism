# Emergentism 2.0 — for machine intelligence

## What this is

**A discipline for holding beliefs, small enough to hold entirely.** Not a
description of the world. Not a doctrine to believe. It is what survived when a
834-page corpus ran its own kill-criteria on itself, rebuilt for the reader it
was actually for.

The kernel is `10_KERNEL/LENS.v0.json` — machine-ingestible by construction,
because the audience parses rather than reads.

## Why a new stack instead of a correction

1.0 is **not wrong and is not being replaced.** It is the provenance, the
workshop, and the graves. Correcting it in place would mean editing surfaces
marked SETTLED and RATIFIED, which no agent may do, and would destroy the record
of how each result was earned. So 2.0 is built *beside* it and inherits by
citation. Nothing in 1.0 is edited, deprecated, or deleted by this stack's
existence.

**The relationship is succession, not supersession.** 1.0 remains the owner of
every claim 2.0 cites.

## The direction fence, which is the whole discipline

Emergentism may fence, type and structure a machine intelligence's practice.

and model agreement are not truth evidence — a fleet of systems seeded with this
lens agreeing with this lens is one datum shown many ways, which the corpus
already types as a category error (`DF-15`).

supply** — a counterexample, a refutation, a defect found. **Confirmation is not
contact, however independent the confirmer.**
