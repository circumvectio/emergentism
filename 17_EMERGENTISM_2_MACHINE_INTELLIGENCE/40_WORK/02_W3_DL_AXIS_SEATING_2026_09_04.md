## §3 · Anderson

P. W. Anderson, *More Is Different*, Science 177(4047):393 (1972):

> the reductionist hypothesis does not by any means imply a 'constructionist'
> one: The ability to reduce everything to simple fundamental laws does not
> imply the ability to start from those laws and reconstruct the universe.

**Neighbor of the split, not the theory.** Construction denied ↔ strong-up.
Reduction/recovery still possible ↔ weak-down. Already calibrated
2026-07-20 as independent conceptual convergence: he does not establish the
register count, μ-at-saturation, strong emergence at any named seam, or a
universal direction of ascent.

He **grants** that lower laws still hold. Tenet 6's "never reducible to it,"
read ontologically, is a stronger wager. SLWP-01D's product-space
counterexample kills any inference from noninvertibility alone to ontological
novelty.

He remains a **refuter of the Wigner dissolution**, which needed the
constructionist converse. "Not a seat" was right for that claim. It is wrong
if read as "Anderson is foreign to Emergentism." Claiming him as *ours* is
the occupancy error in reverse.
