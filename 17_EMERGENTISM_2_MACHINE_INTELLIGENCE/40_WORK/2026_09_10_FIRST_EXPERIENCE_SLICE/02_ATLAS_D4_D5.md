# A plan is present. Its outcome is not.

**D4 is the actual causal history. D5 is the content of represented possibilities.**
This is the distinction used by the Emergentist atlas, not two disconnected
substances or two permission levels.

A written launch plan is an actual artifact. Reading it is an actual process.
Its picture of satisfied future users is possible content. That content can
inform action only through an actual model, available means and a permitted act.
Describing success does not produce success.

## Locate the example

In the fictional launch scenario, distinguish:

| Item | Reading in the atlas | What the item does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| The prototype and team agreement | Stipulated facts about the scenario's D4 situation | Actual evidence from a real team, or user readiness |
| “Users will benefit next week” | A represented future claim, D5 content | A measured outcome or calibrated forecast |
| A proposed trial written here | An actual text representing an unperformed action | Consent, execution or an experimental result |
| Later records of a real trial, if conducted | D4 records requiring source and interpretation checks | General success outside the tested conditions |

The text is actual; what it represents may remain possible, mistaken or unknown.
D5 content is not made actual merely by selecting a panel or moving a slider.

## What the Burrisphere adds

[I] The Burrisphere supplies a chart for thinking about the relationship between
represented possibilities and actual means. Keep the mathematics and its use
distinct. The source's analytic block is:

```text
Zero-Sum Resolution Equation
θ ∈ (0, π)
φ := cot(θ/2),  ν := tan(θ/2)
φ · ν = 1
(φ − ν)² ≥ 0
φ + ν ≥ 2
B := 2/(φ + ν) = sin θ ≤ 1
```

[A, within that chart] These formulas specify a positive-radial meridian and its
reciprocal; azimuth is not supplied, so this is not by itself a full chart of a
sphere. Equality occurs at the equator. This is mathematical balance by
construction, not proof of an ethic, flow state, universal optimality or a
physical bridge between D4 and D5.

The operational quantities have a separate job: `Φ₅` names possible power,
`Φ̂₄` its present estimate, and `V₄` actual usable power. Under the source's
declared cross-factor comparability and calibration, `P_node:=min(Φ̂₄,V₄)` is a
selected working score, not derived from the reciprocal identity, from ordinal
order alone, or a metric of people. Without those extra premises, retain the
two-axis profile rather than forcing a scalar. This case gives no calibrated
scales or values, so **no numerical score is assigned**.

Use the picture to ask: *Is the difficulty in the plan, the means, or how they
connect?* Then inspect evidence. Do not turn a balanced diagram into a launch
certificate.
