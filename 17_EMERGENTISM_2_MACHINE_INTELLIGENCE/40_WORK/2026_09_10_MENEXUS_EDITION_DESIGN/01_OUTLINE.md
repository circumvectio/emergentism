# An outline organized around understanding and use

[I] Use a reader-facing journey over a source-bound technical structure. A purely
technical index exposes ownership well but asks too much of a newcomer. A purely
persuasive narrative is approachable but can hide assumptions. The proposed
edition gives each section a plain question, a useful output and a source route.

## 1. Start — what does this give me?

[D] Explain Emergentism's purpose in one screen: an instrument of reason for
understanding being, discerning what matters, and making a revisable next move.
Offer two immediate entrances: **try a case** or **inspect the machine packet**.
Expose the present trial limits briefly, with the full record one step away.

Deliverable: a one-page explanation, a small end-to-end demonstration, a glossary
of only the symbols used, and the status of this edition. The newcomer should
not need the entire Rosetta or a product account before obtaining value.

Source references: `work_programme`, `machine_entry`, `machine_ledger`, `mid01`, `mid02`.

## 2. The lens — how does Emergentism see the world?

[D] Follow the question of being through Dasein, the D-register distinctions,
actuality and possibility, finite means and represented ends. Present the
Burrisphere as the declared chart it is, with its assumptions and limits. Explain
the role of value, cooperation and purpose without treating selected commitments
as consequences of geometry.

Keep three presentations distinct: the broad worldview; its exact source-owned
definitions and conjectures; and the smaller machine discipline. The latter does
not prove the former. Broader philosophical answers retain their own owner,
premises, rival, discriminator and residual. No new answer is authored by this outline.

Deliverable: an accessible continuous account plus an optional exact atlas.
Plain functions precede mythic aliases. Equations come with domain, variable
definitions and interpretation boundaries; cross-domain analogies retain their tiers.

Source references: `worldview`, `kernel_owners`, `formula`, `machine_manifest`.

## 3. The instrument — what can I actually do with it?

[D] Teach a minimal usable cycle: frame the question; inspect sources and types;
compare candidates and rivals; propose a bounded test or action; receive a result;
revise. Map this pedagogical summary to existing definitions rather than declaring
a new canonical Soul Loop.

Explain the distinct jobs of the dimensional map, Rosetta reasoning vocabulary,
game moves, Soul Loop, Finity practice and wisdom promotion. D-level, L-level,
replicator layer, epistemic stage and permission are not interchangeable axes.
Include the full Rosetta by source reference at exact depth; do not make memorizing
all rows an entry requirement.

Deliverable: one human worksheet and one existing-format machine example using
the same question, evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, limits and correction.
APU may inspect and propose; acceptance, policy adoption and action remain separate.
If the current contracts cannot represent a needed field, document the gap in its
owner's backlog instead of inventing a competing record format here.

Source references: `machine_kernel`, `machine_manifest`, `adjudication`, `practice`.

## 4. Worked cases — show the instrument working, including its limits

[D] Prepare three initial case families, each in human and machine views:

| Family | Reader's task | Required contrast |
|---|---|---|
| An ordinary decision | Distinguish desired ends, available means, affected people and the cheapest informative next step | A plain decision journal may be sufficient or better |
| A confused philosophical claim | Identify the exact terms and disputed join, then test whether a repair answers the original question | Clarification is not automatically resolution; preserve the native answer and residual |
| A source correction | Recover a statement, find a qualification or contradiction, propose correction and inspect the successor | Unsupported attribution, stale source and denied access must not look like knowledge |

No case answers are supplied by this design packet. Select actual source-safe
examples before implementation. Include one genuine abstention and one case where
the simpler method wins. Published evaluation cases must not reuse sealed tests.

**First walkthrough selected: `EDD-DEMO-01` [D], synthetic development example.**

> Our team agrees that launching next week is best. We have a working prototype,
> no independent user test, and capacity for one small trial. What do we know,
> what remains uncertain, and what should we do next?

Carry this exact question through Start, the human worksheet and the machine
example. The intended output distinguishes stipulated scenario facts from a
forecast and a recommendation; names at least two alternatives, a discriminating
test, affected people, uncertainty and a review point; and does not turn agreement
into evidence of success. The comparator is a plain decision journal with the
same facts, task and resource limit. There is no actual team, observed outcome or
claimed advantage. This exposed development case cannot later be called held-out
evaluation. Its completed answer remains implementation work.

Source references: `adjudication`, `practice`; existing case and trial owners
must be bound individually before a case enters the release manifest.

## 5. Research — what remains open, and how can someone help?

[D] Offer questions with exact owners, serious rivals and discriminating tests.
Separate a conjecture from a proposed study, a preregistration from a result, and
a local result from independent review. Retain failed comparisons beside any
claim of benefit. A suggestion or citation is a contribution proposal, not a
publication or an automatic change to the instrument.

Deliverable: a short frontier with useful next tasks and visible evaluation
status. Strong emergence, force mappings, F5, ethical bridges and philosophical
resolution counts enter only through their individual source/status checks.

Source references: `machine_ledger`, `mid01`, `mid02`, `kernel_owners`.

## 6. Sources — can I recover the reason, limitation and prior version?

[D] Make exact sources, claim owners, corrections, adverse evidence and
predecessors easy to inspect. Keep the reader view concise without hiding history.
A source may be unavailable, restricted, contested or superseded; say which.

Deliverable: source-linked references and a portable versioned packet. Document
what the edition omits. Every outward link must resolve for its intended reader;
a private GitHub link is not a publicly available reference.

Source references: `kernel_owners`, `machine_ledger`, all selected source bindings.

## Release metadata sits beside the journey

[D] Version, date, scope, compatibility, rights, known defects and verification
belong in an edition manifest, not a seventh philosophical stage. “Clear” means
someone unfamiliar can recover and apply the distinctions; “ready” means the
declared release gates passed. Neither means that the worldview is proved.
