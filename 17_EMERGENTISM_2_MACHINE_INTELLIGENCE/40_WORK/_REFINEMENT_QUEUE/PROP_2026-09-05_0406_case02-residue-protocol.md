parties.** Score what each model can express about (i) present-expand, (ii)
future-contract, (iii) the set-shape of affected futures (which specific futures
remain reachable vs removed), (iv) what happens at default.

1. **IBC (Fisher 1930)** — scalar budget transfer between two periods. Predicted
   coverage: (i)(ii) yes; (iii) no — a budget hyperplane shifts, it does not
   remove regions; (iv) bankruptcy as re-scaling, not state removal.
2. **Real-options / variance pricing** — predicted coverage: (iii) partial —
   volatility (spread) is priced, but which-futures-removed is not a volatility
   concept; (iv) no.
3. **Cone-topology (the option-cone framing under test)** — predicts (iii)
   natively: debt as removal/constriction of reachable states, default as
   boundary redraw.

**The kill fires if** model 3 covers (iii) or (iv) in a way 1 and 2 cannot —
i.e., a question about the credit case that the cone answers and scalar/variance
models cannot express even adversarially. **Occupancy stands reinforced if**
