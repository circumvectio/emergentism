# What the machine receives — the five connected instruments

## 0 · What this is

`LENS.v0.json` is what a machine holds. This manifest is what the machine
**receives**: the inventory of connected instruments, the reasoning spine, and
the typed-record form — with the fences that keep them from being confused,
merged, or promoted. It is the reference card so no machine has to reconstruct
the mapping from the corpus. It was staged in dialogue and promoted by owner
word on 2026-09-05.

## 1 · The promise, stated so it can lose

> See further, reason across domains, and turn more worthwhile possibilities
> into achievable outcomes — through cooperation. Self-correction is what keeps
> that capacity growing.

**Status [D]:** a promise, not a result. The one measurement made so far went
*against* the easy form of it — MID-01 (2026-09-05): the generic checklist beat
the lens at raw defect detection, 12 to 6. The lens's surviving yield was
classification depth. Whether the promise holds anywhere is what
`30_TRIAL/00_PREREG_TRANSFER_MID02.md` exists to measure.

## 2 · The five connected instruments — not interchangeable ladders

## 4 · The game grammar — and why it is not the spine

`G7 = M4 ⊎ F3`: four take/give moves across Φ and V
(`{take, give} × {Φ, V}`) plus three framing operations (create, dissolve,
preserve). `|G7| = 2×2 + 3 = 7` — **that counting is analytic given the chosen
alphabet** `[A given selection]`; it is a consequence of the construction, not
a discovery about the world.

**`G7 ≠ GEN7`.** The move grammar is not the seven reasoning seats; a machine
reasoning about which operation to run must not pretend the operations are a
stage sequence.

What remains open: whether these categories **adequately compress** the
intended strategy space. The named discriminator is the charter's own kill
clause — *the seven-function model adds no discriminating or practical value
over a simpler rival* — which is MID-01's logic turned on the grammar itself.

## 5 · The typed record — it already exists as the consequence spine

The machine-facing record is nine fields, and **none of them is new**:

| Field | Carrier (already built) |
|---|---|
| question-family | Rosetta pack reference (`pack_id@version:term_key`) |
| operation | the G7 move, or the pramāṇa of the seat taken |
| sources | the L1 discharge binding (input, run, scope, source bundle, stop condition) |
| uncertainty | the evidence tier + uncertainty-bearing ForecastPacket |
| alternatives | L2 candidates/rivals |
| discriminator | MID-style preregistration with sealed key |
| authority boundary | `may_sign=false` / `may_authorize=false` + the applicable AuthorityWeave |
| outcome | ExecutionReceipt + **independent** OutcomeEvidence |
| revision | the ledger row (`00_ESTABLISHED.md` §B) |

**Nothing new is minted.** Minting a tenth record format would fork what
should be pointed at — the corpus's own confessed defect. This manifest *is*
the reference card naming the mapping.

## 6 · The adopted compression

> **VMO articulates worthwhile possibilities. SKA supplies the means. The
> observer connects and revises them. The Burrisphere charts balance; the
> Rosetta names the moves available within the declared grammar.**

With its fence: the sphere charts the *ratio* (`φν = 1` pins the chart; no
scale freedom); the operational estimates (`Φ̂, V̂`) are jointly scalable, and
`min` scores *that* space, conditionally — perfect balance at very low
capability can lose to imperfect balance at higher altitude. Balance-as-flow
remains an interpretation to test, never a projection theorem.

## 7 · What would make this grow — and what can never make it grow

**Grow:** the MID-02 transfer test. A user unfamiliar with Emergentism takes
the instrument to problems the corpus never touched and produces a better
answer — including an answer that *corrects Emergentism*. Preregistered,
sealed, not run.

**Never grow:** a machine understanding its situation through the instrument.
**Use is not confirmation.** The direction fence holds at full strength here —
a fleet of machines holding the lens and describing their situation in its
vocabulary is one datum shown many ways (`DF-15`). Contact counts only when
the response returns something the corpus did not supply.
