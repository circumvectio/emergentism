# VMOSK-A v2 — Emergentism, the translation-instrument edition (SIGNED)

> **SIGNED v2 — owner countersignature (Yves R. Burri, natural person) 2026-07-31.** This is now canon; the prior version is retained per K3 with a forward pointer. Every biological claim remains a projection at its stated tier — a signature ratifies the *doctrine*, never promotes an unbuilt objective to done.

This non-semantic control projection creates no theorem, ontology, axiology,
or eighth kernel owner. Its biological correspondences are optional `[I]` Rosetta annotations,
never transferred proof or semantic authority.

Emergentism is the doctrine pillar. It **orients; it does not operate.** It owns the Rosetta, so
the discipline of biological mapping — how a life-science structure may be compared to a doctrinal
one without smuggling in warrant — lives here and nowhere else. v2's single organising fact:

**The Rosetta kill-criterion FIRED `[S]`.** A universal seven-level pattern was *not* established.
What survived is a disciplined **translation instrument** that compares relational structures
without transferring proof or authority. v2 rebuilds V/M/O/S/K/A around that survivor, not around
the retired claim.

## V — Vision

Unchanged in direction from v1.0, and still a selected `[I]` direction: build an inhabitable,
corrigible worldview for finite beings — one that helps holders distinguish map from territory,
possibility from actuality, intention from consequence, and that succeeds when they can revise it,
reject it, or put it down.

What v2 adds is the **precise biological reading of what the doctrine now is**, because the
pillar's own instrument had never been turned on the pillar: Emergentism is a **genome under a
copying-fidelity constraint**, not a finished cathedral. A doctrine transmits by being re-copied
into new documents, agents, and readers; each copy is lossy; and there is a length past which a
corpus degrades faster than curation can preserve it. Vision, therefore, is no longer "say more,
truer things." It is **"raise fidelity so the true things survive copying."** Any claim that
Emergentism actually improves judgment or lived outcomes remains `[C]` until fair comparison and
world contact support it.

## M — Mission

Maintain an integrated, typed, fallibilist account of reality, knowledge, action, value, and
direction; connect it to lived practice; test its stronger claims against serious rivals and world
contact; preserve failed claims and superseded forms; and keep correction, contest, and Exit
inside the system.

v1.0's four duties stand (one visible owner per active claim · state result/interpretation/
conjecture/public-language at their tiers · turn claims into bounded tests · keep every reader
projection weaker-than-or-equal to its sources). v2 adds a fifth, because the biology named a
failure the first four do not prevent:

### M5 — Metabolic discipline for the translation instrument (new)

- **The Rosetta is retired as a monolith and staged as Core + Packs + Ledger.** The 1,100-line
  master correspondence table is retired to historical **raw material** — read, never cited as
  live canon. Its successor is a small versioned **Core**, namespaced **Packs** (one per
  source-domain lens, each carrying its own dependency facts), and a machine-readable **Ledger**
  binding every live correspondence to its tiers. This is the theoretically correct move
  *because higher fidelity buys a longer viable genome*; adding documents at current fidelity
  strictly worsens the pillar.
- **Four effects can each manufacture apparent correspondence.** Before a cell enters the Ledger
  it must be assigned which of these produced the resemblance, not left implied: a **recurring
  problem** (independent domains face the same constraint), a **shared constraint** (a common
  substrate forces a common shape), **diffusion** (one tradition borrowed from another), or
  **interpreter freedom** (the mapper chose the resemblance). A correspondence whose effect is
  unnamed is decorative, not evidential.
- **Every correspondence carries a `dependency_status`** — `independent | common-ancestry |
  direct-diffusion | modern-synthesis | framework-derived | unknown` — so that, e.g., ten
  Indo-European parallels are not counted as ten independent observations when they may be one
  observation with nine descendants.

## S — Strategies

v1.0's eight stand (source-owner-first · worldview-first-practice-immediately · plain-before-
symbolic · practice-to-consequence · evidence-beside-ambition · one-route-grammar · archive-
without-broadcasting · small-reversible-releases). Three added, all metabolic:

9. **Fidelity before volume.** Never answer a gap by adding a document at current fidelity. Raise
   copying fidelity — dedupe, tombstone, bind to Ledger — *then* extend. More documents at the
   current error rate strictly shortens the viable genome.
10. **One correspondence, one manufacturing verdict.** No cell enters the Ledger without a named
    effect (recurring-problem / shared-constraint / diffusion / interpreter-freedom) and a
    `dependency_status`. Resemblance is a hypothesis about *why*, never a bare fact.
11. **Guilds, not ranks — and know whose fence applies.** The trophic rows are cooperate-or-
    differentiate peer guilds that draw energy through the strata below; they are never ranks of
    worth. The scientific-racism / caste caution (KSC-24, KSC-25) is scoped to **natural
    persons** and must be raised there. It does **not** fire on AI-agent routing: agents
    genuinely have discrete morphs and a determination switch, so morph-typed dispatch is a real
    property of the substrate, not a smuggled hierarchy of persons.

## K — KPIs and triggers

These signals change the work; they never validate the worldview. v1.0's inventory of signals
stands (surface coverage · barred-claim count · deterministic hashes · five-second comprehension ·
Finity-versus-rival · rival visibility · replication · correction latency · Exit cost). Added, all
currently unmeasured, and framed by the pillar's central metabolic constraint:

| Signal | Current | Target | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Document-duplication rate** (docs re-copying docs, same day) | unmeasured | falling | rising *with* corpus size ⇒ error-catastrophe alarm: the genome is exceeding its copying fidelity |
| **Corpus length vs fidelity estimate** | ~476k-file tree, unestimated | length ≤ ~1/mutation-rate | length outruns fidelity ⇒ freeze new documents, raise fidelity first |
| **Ledger cell tier-completeness** | partial | 100% carry all three tiers | any live public correspondence missing `fact_tier`/`mapping_tier`/`outcome_status` ⇒ withhold from projection |
| **`dependency_status` coverage** | unrecorded | 100% of live cells | a bloc of `independent` cells sharing one ancestry ⇒ recount as one observation |
| **Effect-named coverage** (four manufacturing effects) | unrecorded | 100% of live cells | `unknown` effect on a public cell ⇒ quarantine |

**Error catastrophe is this pillar's central metabolic constraint (Eigen).** Sustainable genome
length scales as ~ 1/mutation-rate: raise copying fidelity and a longer corpus survives; hold
fidelity and add documents and the corpus degrades faster than curation preserves it. The retired
1,100-line table, docs duplicating docs on the same day, and a 476k-file tree are the corpus
*already at or past* its threshold. vNext (Core + Packs + Ledger) is the correct response for a
biological reason, not an aesthetic one: it buys fidelity, and fidelity is what buys length.
Followers, applause, and AI agreement remain non-KPIs.

## A — Agents and closure of the world gate

v1.0's role table stands: K-1…K-7 own meaning in their domains; L1 isolates contradictions,
coercion, and hidden bearers; L2 reconstructs the strongest rival and alternative explanation; L3
audits source/type/tier/logic/citation/test/receipt/ceiling; L4 applies the bounded change after
the gates; L5 designs scope, owners, and dependency graphs; L6 preserves superseded material and
kills; L7 translates without strengthening the claim; users and affected bearers supply use,
refusal, and contest receipts; independent critics close world-facing gates that internal
coherence cannot. **Rosetta roles are work functions, never human ranks, identities, or automatic
authority.** v2 adds the reading that governs how the A-layer is *dispatched* on the instrument:

- **The four-effects discipline is an L2/L3 gate, not L7 decoration.** Whenever an agent proposes
  a correspondence, L2 must reconstruct the alternative reason the resemblance exists (recurring
  problem / shared constraint / diffusion / interpreter freedom) and L3 must record it, with
  `dependency_status`, in the Ledger. An agent that places a cell without naming why the two
  domains resemble each other has produced interpreter-freedom and mislabelled it as observation.
- **Fact placed ≠ mapping warranted.** The three tiers are three different audits by three
  different eyes: L3 attests `fact_tier` against the source domain, L5/L1 attest `mapping_tier`
  against the placement, and users/bearers attest `outcome_status` against the world. No agent may
  collapse them; an established biological fact laid onto a doctrinal row is *still a speculative
  cell* until the placement and the use are each earned.
- **Guilds are dispatched by morph; persons are not ranked.** For AI-agent routing the caste
  caution does not fire — the substrate really does carry discrete morphs. For natural persons the
  KSC-24 / KSC-25 fences do fire, and L1 raises them. Keeping those two cases distinct is itself an
  A-layer duty.

A model may coordinate the work; only the world, through adequately designed contact and
independent criticism, can decide how much survives.
