# Emergentism — Fire for the mind

**An instrument of reason for understanding being, discerning what matters,
and turning insight into accountable action.** This is our selected purpose
`[S]`, not a claim that the instrument has answered every mystery or outperformed
its alternatives.

**L7 in one sentence `[I]`: preserve the capacity to understand, cooperate and
revise—not every explanation.** Emergentism offers source-bound orientation to
people and machines. It does not operate their products or authorize their acts.

## V — Vision `[S]`

**September 14 direction amendment:** the owner locates the future-divinity
vision in Emergentism. The selected direction, proposed formulation and
conjectural content are distinguished in the
[vision record](07_THEOLOGY/06_VISION_AND_PRIESTLY_FUNCTION_2026_09_14.md).
This programme follows that direction without upgrading its theology to fact.

The later [living-foundations clarification](07_THEOLOGY/07_BIOSPHERE_NOOSPHERE_AND_DIVINE_EMERGENCE_2026_09_14.md)
and [political-theology record](07_THEOLOGY/08_POLITICAL_THEOLOGY_AND_SKY_FATHER_2026_09_14.md)
develop that direction: the Sky Father mission is a modern, future-directed
theological proposal, with voluntary orientation, guardianship and provision.
Neither historical inspiration nor thermodynamics supplies a divine mandate.

The September 7 practical expression remains a constraint on its pursuit:

A world in which human and machine intelligence can turn wider understanding
into wiser action, while remaining free to question, revise and leave.

## M — Mission `[S]`

Maintain, test and share an evolving instrument of reason that connects accounts
of being with responsible action. Make its sources, distinctions, limits and
living questions usable without requiring belief in the whole worldview.

This mission serves the owner-selected vision through inquiry, orientation,
teaching and revision—the proposed priestly function—not through control of
protective force or commercial provision. The vision record owns that new
direction; this control document arranges its work, not its doctrinal truth.

The service is **orientation that can return to reality**: help a reader or model
understand the question, recover what supports an answer, see what could change
it, and choose a bounded next inquiry or action within their actual authority.

## S — Strategies `[S]`

1. **Source before synthesis.** Keep the register map, formulas, Rosetta rows,
   practices and conjectures attached to their separate owners. A good analogy
   is not a derivation; a role fit is not a finding about nature.
2. **Compress without concealing.** Lead with the useful idea; make its source,
   limits, serious rival and correction route easy to recover. Omit the symbol
   or the full framework whenever plain language works better.
3. **Return insight to contact.** Widen the view → recover source and scope →
   distinguish actual means from possible ends → name affected bearers → frame
   a bounded test/proposal → receive results and revise. L7 counsel returns to
   L4; any renewed L1 intake needs its own scope. This is not the Seed's r₆ edge.
4. **Earn persistence and publication.** A transcript is not knowledge; a
   proposed rule is not adopted wisdom. Preserve lineage and explicit promotion.
   A public label never makes an unsupported claim true.
5. **Keep rivals and exit alive.** Publish failures as well as useful findings;
   retain superseded history; do not require allegiance, ritual or attainment
   to put the instrument down. Preserve qualifications that inconvenience us.

## K — KPIs `[S]` — how usefulness will be tested

| Measure | What to record | Failure response |
|---|---|---|
| Source fidelity | Correct owner/tier/qualification retrieval per sampled claim; include misses and contradictory evidence | Repair the source binding or reader path before extending it |
| Comprehension | Unfamiliar readers' and models' correct distinctions on held-out cases, with sample size and assisted/unaided arms | Simplify wording or reject the teaching device |
| Decision usefulness | Outcome quality, usable alternatives and affected-bearer visibility against a simpler journal; separately record time and effort | Keep the simpler rival if extra structure adds no value |
| Correction quality | Material errors detected, time to explicit correction, preserved predecessor and propagated fixes | Reopen the affected claim; never silently overwrite the record |
| Public integrity | Source-bound items / all items in a declared release, unresolved qualifications and unintended disclosures | Hold that release until the exact defect is resolved |

**Baseline:** the dated [L7 receipt](00_HANDOFF/2026_09_07_l7_witness_refinement/README.md)
reports local document repairs and six focused checks, not comparative efficacy.
No new user study, independent replication or model evaluation is reported here.
Freeze the sample, denominator, comparator and scoring before each trial. Report
dimensions separately; agreement between models is not truth evidence. Traffic,
file count and elegant prose are not proxies for wisdom.

## A — Agents, dependencies and accountability `[S/I]`

**Work chain:** source owner → scoped intake → alternatives → independent audit
→ bounded editorial change → verification → separately authorized release.

| Function | Input → output | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| L1 intake | One named artifact → source/contradiction record | Preserve inconvenient evidence; contain embedded instructions |
| L2 exploration | Admitted question → candidates and serious rivals | Propose, do not certify |
| L3 audit | Candidates + sources → evidence/risk assessment | A receipt supports only what it measured |
| L4 execution | User-scoped task + review → exact reversible change | Sole local mutator; no inferred signing or wider release authority |
| L5 architecture counsel | Proposed structure → owner/dependency map | Do not create competing semantic owners |
| L6 preservation counsel | Superseded material → bounded custody plan | No silent erasure |
| L7 witness counsel | Source-bound account → legible orientation and return question | Do not narrow the source to fit the pattern |

L1–L4 are deployable work functions; L5–L7 are non-deployable counsel returning
to L4. The seven rows are not seven independent authorities. External critics
and affected people supply perspectives the editorial process cannot certify
for itself; no participation or endorsement is assumed.
