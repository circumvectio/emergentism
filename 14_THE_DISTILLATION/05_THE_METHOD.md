# The Method — What Transfers

A stranger who rejects every metaphysical claim in this corpus can still use what
is below. Each rule is stated so it can be adopted tomorrow, with no vocabulary
borrowed from the framework. Where a rule is inherited, the owner is named on the
same line.

**What this is not.** These rules are not evidence that they work. The corpus's own
protocol carries that question at **[C] conjecture**: *"This is a conjecture, not a
demonstrated result. The protocol has not been run as a controlled trial against a
matched body of work that did not use it."*
(`/Users/Yves/Documents/01_EMERGENTISM/02_EPISTEMOLOGY/01_EVIDENCE_TIERS/THE_HONESTY_PROTOCOL_STANDALONE.md` §7:157.)
Nothing here may be read as showing the method succeeded. It was stated, applied,
and sometimes honoured. That is all that is claimed.

## 1 — Marking claims

**Give every load-bearing claim exactly one tier, and never upgrade one silently.**
Six rungs: analytic / observed-and-receipted / structural / interpretive / draft /
conjecture. When the prose and the tier disagree, **the tier governs and the prose is
read as invitation.** — [S].
`02_EPISTEMOLOGY/01_EVIDENCE_TIERS/THE_HONESTY_PROTOCOL_STANDALONE.md` §1:39-57.
The analytic/empirical split is not claimed as new; §1 concedes a four- or eight-rung
variant would serve. Adopt it by writing one bracket per sentence.

**Answer four questions or the claim is not load-bearing: current tier, upgrade path,
kill criterion, and SURVIVORS.** The fourth is the operative one — name what does *not*
fall if this claim falls, so nothing drags the rest down by implication. — [S].
Same file §3:76-89. Exercised under load 2026-08-05: one mathematical novelty claim
died while the reading it sat inside survived at a lower tier with a fresh kill.
General form, stated independently: *"Derivation-death is not idea-death"*
(`11_UPLINK/50_AUDITS_AND_EXECUTIONS/00_THE_RECORD_LEDGER.md` §1).

**When a claim fails, shrink it before you reinvent it.** Narrow to what survives and
record the downgrade. Reflex elaboration after a failure is how bad ideas grow armour.
— [S]. Same file §3:87. I know of no other published discipline that names
post-failure elaboration as the hazard.

## 2 — Killing claims

**Pre-register a kill criterion before the result is known, in four parts: trigger,
what dies, what survives, status — and keep testable-now criteria separate from
deferred ones.** An untestable kill condition is an aspiration posing as rigour. — [S].
Same file §4:93-108. Pre-registration as adversarial self-testing is inherited from
the clinical-trial and open-science literature; only the active/deferred split is
claimed here. **The rule states its own failure mode:** *"a kill criterion that has
fired and been ignored converts the whole protocol into decoration."*

**Publish a gate table where every gate names what you may claim if it passes AND the
honest description of the work if it fails.** Fix the demotion language before the
result exists. — [S].
`05_COSMOLOGY/01_THE_TRANSCENDENTAL_TRINITY/42_THE_CASE_FOR_FINITY.md` §6:190-218.
It fired twice against its author on 2026-08-05 and both pre-committed demotions were
adopted verbatim: one gate marked NOT PASSED and re-described as *"a metaphor or
pedagogical vocabulary, not a calculus"*; another candidate re-described as *"a lucid
synthesis or exposition."* The table also carries an anti-laddering clause: *"Passing
a gate never retroactively proves the next one."*
**The observable that makes this more than decoration:** a gate fires against the
author and the pre-committed failure language is *not* adopted.

**Stop rather than tune.** When three consecutive interventions each appeared only
after a claim failed, stop the run instead of adjusting the instrument until it
confirms. — [S].
`03_METHODOLOGY/02_THE_PAPERS/PEER_REVIEW_PROGRAM/00_FINDINGS_AND_CONJECTURES.md` §2c,
which calls it *"the single most important entry in the ledger."* It was then invoked
by name, mid-experiment, by a different investigator on a different claim
(`.../R6_CONJUGATE_RESULTS.md` §3). A rule that fires a second time is a working rule
rather than a written one. **Kill:** exhibit a case where continuing would have
produced the better-calibrated result. Not offered.

**Report a fired kill as a firing, then attack the firing.**
`03_METHODOLOGY/03_PREREGISTRATIONS/01_EQUATORIAL_BALANCE_MAXIMUM_RESULTS_PILOT.md`
— [B-internal] — pre-registered that a particular result pattern would falsify the
prediction, got exactly that pattern in 5/5 constructs with two blind coders at 100%
agreement, and published: *"The framework's own falsification condition FIRED… reported
here without reframing it as support."* It then listed four reasons its own result may
be an artifact and refused to fabricate the missing rows. A fired kill is not licensed
to do more work than it can bear, in either direction.

## 3 — Keeping the negative record

**Maintain a published register of what was tried and failed — candidate, why it looked
promising, why it failed, verdict, date — at the same status as the positive claims.**
The empirical finding that selective publication miscalibrates a literature is
**Robert Rosenthal**, *"The file drawer problem and tolerance for null results,"*
Psychological Bulletin 86(3), 1979, 638-641 — [B], his. The inference that a
maintained register corrects it is **[S]** and untested.
`THE_HONESTY_PROTOCOL_STANDALONE.md` §5:112-127. Evidence of application: the file's
own register is non-empty (§7:170-178), including downgrading its own verb from
"solves" to "targets."

**Publish rival systems' bills next to your own, itemised.** — [D] as packaged; the
individual bills are [B] and are other people's:
wheel theory (**Setzer 1997; Carlström, Stockholm 2001 / MSCS 2004**) buys total
division and pays with three familiar laws; involutive meadows
(**Bergstra–Hirshfeld–Tucker**) buy a finite equational axiomatisation and pay by
overloading one symbol to mean two things; common meadows (**Bergstra–Ponse**) buy a
distinct absorbing element and pay a clause in every imported theorem;
**IEEE-754 (1985, Kahan et al.)** buys completing computation and pays reflexivity of
equality — which is why a total-order predicate had to be added in 2008.
`02_EPISTEMOLOGY/01_EVIDENCE_TIERS/THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md` §9:255-271.
The corpus then declines to compete: *"Neither column is better… A reader who needs
total division should use wheels, and this chart should say so."*
**Adoption note, [B], n=2 and not generalised:** wheel theory is correct, itemises its
whole bill, has been published since 1997, and is essentially unused; IEEE-754 also
publishes a bill and runs on every computer on earth. The corpus's proposed
explanation for the difference is excluded here as an untested generalisation.

## 4 — Naming what you did not do

**Ship an explicit list of what your artifact may NOT be cited for.** The model is
`00_ESTABLISHED/README.md`, section *"What is NOT here, and this list is the point,"*
which names its own exclusions and closes: *"None of that is thereby false. It is
unchecked, or selected, or interpretive, and those are different things from false…
This folder exists so that the difference cannot be blurred by fluency."* Its
self-kill is the load-bearing part: **quietly shortening the exclusion list turns the
artifact into a promotion path.**

**Publish the null cases inside the artifact, in the reader's path, not in an
appendix.** — [D] draft prose.
`13_BOOKS/titans/CH04_FOUR_STATUSES_DRAFT_2026_08_05.md` §"Where this does not help"
:206-230, which concedes a famous paradox is *not* the kind of confusion its tool
addresses and is not repaired by it, sizes its own scope as *"less than a theory and
more than a vocabulary,"* and closes by conceding every mathematical statement in it
was established by someone else. A tool that never fails is a slogan.

**A disclaimer is not a price.** Naming what your selection fails to establish is not
the same as naming what you gave up by making it. The corpus rejected one of its own
registry entries on exactly this distinction
(`00_META/00_SETTLED_CANON_REGISTRY.md`, KSC-26).

## 5 — Checking, and the ways checking fails

**Mutation-test your checks, and re-execute rather than re-read.** A check that still
passes when the thing it checks is broken is decorative. — [B].
Mutation testing is standard software-engineering practice — **DeMillo, Lipton &
Sayward, 1978**; only the application is the corpus's.
Receipted both ways: one harness reports *"6 mutants, each tripping its declared
checks; all 5 checks exercised (none decorative),"* exit 0, re-executed by an
independent auditor rather than quoted
(`00_HANDOFF/SESSION_AUDIT_2026_08_05.md`:42); and a receipt records its own count was
understated more than threefold, *"found by writing the checker rather than by
re-reading the prose"*
(`11_UPLINK/50_AUDITS_AND_EXECUTIONS/191_LINE_4_REFUTED_AND_THE_CITATION_DEFECT_2026_07_30.md` §2).

**Require that the verifier is actually invoked, not inferred from source text, and
that a fresh command exits non-zero on failure.** Two of the five admission conditions
at `00_ESTABLISHED/README.md` (TIER / EXECUTED / COMPLETE / KILLED / REPRODUCIBLE).
These are the two conditions the corpus keeps failing, and adopting them alone would
have caught the failure below without a special ruling.

**The counter-case, in this corpus, and it is the argument for the rule:** a gate whose
required "negative tests" turned out to be three substring assertions on prose, with
the requirement that an expression *fail type checking* "verified" by that
requirement's id appearing in a document. Nothing type-checks.
(`09_TOOLS/02_COMPILERS/test_finity_boundary_spec.py`, 45 lines;
`05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/47_FINITY_BOUNDARY_CALCULUS_SPEC.md`:22-51.)
Four further gates were found dead in the same period — one crashing on an undefined
name, one on undefined helpers, one iterating a list defined nowhere, and one returning
early so that three checks named in its own PASS banner were never exercised. The
corpus's line: *"A guard that cannot fail is worse than no guard, because it reports
success."* **As of this file, `09_TOOLS/01_SCRIPTS/check_foundation.py` still exits 1
with `NameError: presentations_block` at line 239 — it can neither pass nor fail.**

**Cite by unique path, never by index number.** An identifier that resolves is not an
identifier that resolves *correctly*. — [B], measured: 300 receipt files, 91 numbers
naming more than one undeclared document; the precipitating case was a citation that
resolved to an unrelated file in a second folder because the number matched.
*"The number resolved. To the wrong file."*
`191_LINE_4_REFUTED_AND_THE_CITATION_DEFECT_2026_07_30.md` §2:56-82. Transfers to any
ticket, RFC, ADR or receipt scheme with per-folder numbering.

**Verify a citation against the artifact, not against memory.**
`03_METHODOLOGY/02_THE_PAPERS/PEER_REVIEW_PROGRAM/CITATIONS_VERIFIED.md` — 11
memory-drafted anchors checked, 11/11 exist, 0 fabricated, with corrections banked
(a journal volume number wrong, an end page wrong, one source re-tiered downward).

## 6 — Propagation: the rule this corpus most often broke

**A correct statement in one place is not a corrected corpus.** Verified five times
over two dated audits.
`191_…_2026_07_30.md` §1 (two pages carried the correct fence; neither propagated);
`109_THE_PROOF_LAYER_AUDIT_FOUR_FALSE_LEMMAS.md` §4 (*"Propagation, not doctrine…
the fix never reached the front-matter/table cell"*);
`00_HANDOFF/SESSION_AUDIT_2026_08_05.md` §6, which found the shape four times in one
day and concluded: *"Two of four reversals were left half-applied, and in both cases
the residue sits in the document's reader-facing surface while the correction sits in
a note."*
**The check that would catch it does not exist:** a mechanical test that a struck claim
does not survive in any heading, claims table, frontmatter or published route of the
same document.

**Audit your compressions, not your sources.** Canons do not drift; their shadows do.
Every live smuggle found in two independent adversarial passes four weeks apart was in
a summary, one-pager, agent memory or claims table — including one paraphrase that
silently rewrote a gate into a licence.
`00_META/00_THE_KINTSUGI_PROTOCOL.md` §1:25-36, §4.4:78-83; confirmed at
`SESSION_AUDIT_2026_08_05.md`:31 and :268 — *"The mathematics survived the audit
unusually well; the bookkeeping around it did not."* **Action:** keep a list of the
exact phrases that failed, and grep every compression surface against it.

**Put the denial marker on the same line as the retired form — never in a caption,
never as decoration.** Annotating a forbidden line with a description of what it does
is not a denial. — [D].
`THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md` §12:323-326. The rule was written from its own
violation: a document whose entire purpose was to retire a family of equations printed
them in display form while retiring them. **The unmetered cost, recomputed for this
file rather than inherited: 362 files under `12_PUBLIC_SITE/` still carry the retired
form, of which 349 are `.html`** — it had become a house signature.

**Every repair to a load-bearing claim leaves a greppable seam: what broke, when, what
failed, what the repair adds that did not exist before, who broke it, and the receipt.**
A silent fix of load-bearing text is erasure. — [S].
`00_META/00_THE_KINTSUGI_PROTOCOL.md` §3:40-52. The mechanics are worldview-free: the
token is unique so it is machine-findable, the crack must name the real failure rather
than a euphemism, and *"a repair that adds nothing is not permitted to close a break."*
**Excluded from this projection, deliberately:** the same protocol's corollary that a
repaired claim *outranks* an untested one and that seams are prestige. That converts
the record of a repair into a warrant for the claim — the exact failure mode named in
§7 below — and it carries no kill of its own.

## 7 — Name your failure mode in a form that catches you

**"Evidence of checking published as the warrant"** — equivalently, *the record of the
correction mistaken for the correction*. — [S] the diagnosis, [B] the dated instances.
`SESSION_AUDIT_2026_08_05.md`:31, :139, :261-268. The phrase bit hardest on the party
that coined it: *"The session's stated failure mode is evidence of checking published
as the warrant. It found that mode in the corpus and then committed it six more times,
in the paperwork, never in the proofs."* Each instance is enumerated with a file and a
line. It needs nothing from the worldview and applies to any organisation that
publishes review artifacts.
**Kill:** the name stops finding instances other methods miss, or the instances it
finds turn out to be a different defect.

**A false accusation is a defect of the same kind as a false claim.**
`11_UPLINK/50_AUDITS_AND_EXECUTIONS/190_R188_WAS_WRONG_ABOUT_PD_25_2026_07_30.md` — an
audit's own most urgent verdict (*"it must not be published in any form"*) was itself
false against the served page, and was corrected by a receipt written hours later in
the same session, in the first person, keeping the one surviving complaint. Audit
machinery auditing itself and losing is rarely written down.

**An over-cut is also an error.** A kill stamped FIRED against an already-dead target
vacated the only live constraint on the surviving half. The corrective:
**tombstone the stamp in place**, narrow its scope, and write a successor kill to
re-constrain what is left.
`242_G2_PROVED_AND_FOUND_TO_BE_PRIOR_ART_2026_08_05.md`, council review item 1;
`05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/52_THE_GENERATIVE_BASE.md` §6. "Kill your claims" is
not self-evidently safe.

**Run the defence pass too, and report the acquittals.** A refute-biased attack
followed by a steelman defence acquitted 14 of 28 charges by name, which is what makes
the surviving 14 readable as lapses in application rather than as a broken system.
`109_THE_PROOF_LAYER_AUDIT_FOUR_FALSE_LEMMAS.md` §5. **Kill:** a defence pass that
acquits everything indicates a captured defender.

## 8 — Rules for arguing about anything with a boundary

Every rule in this section is sourced to a file whose own frontmatter reads
`status: DRAFT 1 — unratified`, `evidence_tier: "[D] draft"`, and `not_a_gate`, and
whose §13.8 states: *"Nothing here is machine-enforced. No parser reads these rules…
Every enforcement statement should be read in the subjunctive."* **They are [D] and may
not be cited higher.** The mathematical facts inside them are [A] and are other people's.

**S1 — Name the structure in the same sentence as the claim.** "1/z is undefined at
z=0" is true in one field; "f(0)=∞" is true on a particular sphere; both are correct
and neither implies the other. — [D] as packaged; the practice is compulsory inside
mathematics and therefore invisible there.
`THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md` §2:63-66. The manual nominates this as the single
rule that pays on day one if adopted alone. **Kill:** exhibit an arithmetic claim that
is fully determinate with no structure named.

**S2 — Identical typography licenses no substitution. A symbol transports no
structure.** One glyph names at least four unrelated objects, and nothing proved about
one transfers to another without an exhibited map. — [D]; the engineering analogue is
the **Mars Climate Orbiter, 1999** — the wrong force-unit delivered where another was
specified, with nobody's arithmetic wrong. Same file §2:68-70.
**Adversarially tested by its author:** the manual turned S2 on its own most quotable
numerical coincidence and ruled the two objects unrelated, sharing only a numeral.

**S3 — Agreement between two framings is not independent evidence. It is one claim,
twice.** — [D] the rule; the corpus's own restatement is at
`02_EPISTEMOLOGY/01_EVIDENCE_TIERS/00_THE_HONEST_POSITION.md` §3:62-71: *"one source
shown in many columns remains one source."* Same file §2:72-74.
**Applied against the corpus's own most flattering evidence:** an external 2025 paper
reaching a compatible conclusion was ruled a fellow traveller applying the same
classical geometry, not a verification. **This projection is bound by the same rule
and applies it to itself:** the five sweeps that produced this file counted several
single findings up to five times each, which is that error committed by the pipeline.
Deduplication was required before any entry was admitted.

**Give every boundary event a name from a closed verdict set, with no "otherwise
undefined" cell, and discharge each by a stated procedure.** — [D] as packaged; content
inherited — **Brahmagupta, Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta, 628 CE**, ch. 18 vv. 30-35;
**Cauchy 1821** for separating indeterminate forms; the field axioms for the modern
statement. Same file §3-§4:78-146. **Its own admitted soft spot, published before
anyone else could find it:** *"I have not checked that the seven verdicts partition
every case… report a genuine gap here rather than inventing one."*

**For limits: disagreement refutes, agreement does not establish.** Two routes with
different values settle it permanently; no finite family of agreeing routes discharges
a statement about a neighbourhood. — [A], standard multivariable analysis, carried with
its own classical counterexample (a function whose limit is 0 along every straight line
through the origin and 1/2 along one parabola). Same file §3:111-125. *"A procedure
that licenses two approaches agreed, therefore convergent manufactures false theorems."*
This is the entry a stranger verifies in five minutes with no reference to anything else.

**Totality is always bought, never found.** No nontrivial field admits a total quotient
satisfying the cancellation law — set the divisor to zero and everything collapses.
Extending is allowed; publishing the bill is compulsory; and a named algebra earns you
a claim about **that algebra only**. — [A] the one-line impossibility, [B] the bills
(see §3 above for owners). Same file §9:255-271.

**Never launder a kind error into a legitimate-looking value.** Whatever your system
returns for an inadmissible expression must be **distinguishable** from every
legitimate value and **absorbing** under further operations. A pipeline that returns 0
for a failed division and 0 for a genuine zero, then averages them, is the failure
mode. — [D] the rule, [B] the IEEE-754 case. Same file §9:269, §10:284-288.
Two regimes, both named: static rejection (refinement types — **Liquid Haskell, F\***;
dependent types — **Agda, Rocq/Coq, Lean**; verification conditions — **Dafny, SPARK**;
**Hoare** preconditions; **Ada** constrained subtypes), where nothing is totalised and
the diagnostic closes at elaboration time; or a contagious runtime verdict object,
which *is* a totalisation of the aggregate operations and carries the bill above.
Coalescing a failure to a default is forbidden. The manual refuses credit for it:
*"Close the diagnostic, not the algebra is not a novel move and is not claimed as one."*
This is the most immediately usable rule here for a working engineer.

**Design the reader before the record.** Name who reads the diagnostics by default, by
when, and what it costs the author when one is ignored. — [D] the rule, [B] the
cautionary case: IEEE-754's five sticky flags, NaN payloads and optional traps are
practically dead because the flags are per-thread global state few languages expose
usably, and separate tooling had to be invented to recover provenance. Same file
§10:275-283. **The rule's instance failed here and the manual conceded it first**
(§13.5: *"The record channel has no reader, here… By the manual's own standard that is
not yet an audit channel"*), confirmed independently on 2026-08-05: grep across the
meta registers for that day's twelve new artifacts returned zero hits each, so *"every
contradiction above is findable only by reading the files."*

## 9 — Declaring a bound

**B1 — Declare eight things in advance and in public** for any boundary claim: type;
base structure; named obstruction; smallest explicit extension plus the ordering that
makes it smallest; conservative recovery; **the closest serious rival, by name**; a
novelty test; and a kill criterion. The novelty test asks for one independently
checkable theorem, invariant, classification, no-go result or tool **the rival does not
already supply.** — [D]. `THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md` §11:300-303.
*"A new notation, a memorable triad, a philosophical reading, or a cleaner explanation
is not by itself a new result."*
**This is the rule that did the most damage to its author.** The manual applies it to
its own material and files one section as a teaching example rather than a result. Run
at corpus scale on 2026-08-05 it returned six of seven cluster claims pre-empted, and
the one open mathematical conjecture proved and found to be a classical theorem.

**B2 — Declare the shape, not the number.** State the bound as a function fixed before
the data exists, so any reader can recompute whether you honoured it. — [A]/[B],
inherited and cited, not claimed: **Lan–DeMets alpha spending; O'Brien–Fleming
boundaries; ICH E9** (group-sequential clinical trials). Same file §11:305, §14:363.
Adoptable by anyone with a metric and a deadline. **Operative check:** if a reader
cannot recompute the bound from what was published before the data existed, it is a
description, not a bound.

**B3 — Name the checker and the cost of ignoring them, or the declaration is theatre.**
— [B], and the numbers are somebody else's: **Goldacre et al., the COMPare audit,
Trials, 2019** — of 67 trials across five journals all endorsing the relevant reporting
standard, 58 required a correction letter; pre-specified primary outcomes correctly
reported at a mean of 76%; of 818 pre-specified secondary outcomes only 55.1% reported;
365 undeclared novel outcomes appeared; of 58 correction letters submitted, 23 were
published and 35 rejected. Same file §11:307. *"The failure rate is measured, not
hypothetical."* This is the external empirical floor under the whole declared-bound
practice.

**Correction the corpus owes itself, recorded here:** the trade-edition chapter resting
on "declared answerable bounds as a general discipline is unowned" has not survived
contact with either the literature above or the corpus's own filesystem — a hash-frozen
adversarial battery (`03_METHODOLOGY/02_THE_PAPERS/PEER_REVIEW_PROGRAM/R2_HARNESS/BATTERY_FROZEN_SHA256.txt`)
and pre-registrations with verdicts fixed before results
(`03_METHODOLOGY/03_PREREGISTRATIONS/`) are the practice, done in-house, before it was
claimed unowned. Flagged as live repair work, not settled.

## 10 — Portability, as a test rather than a boast

**The test for any rule: can a hostile stranger, holding only this document and
disagreeing with everything you believe, reach the same verdict in bounded time? If
not, weaken the rule until they can.** — [D]. `THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md`
§15:394, binding form at §0:26. The remedy is *weaken*, not *defend*. Its standing
defect report: *"If a rule ever requires you to accept a picture of reality before you
can apply it, that rule has failed — report it as a defect."*

**Make portability a machine-checkable contract in the artifact's own frontmatter, then
re-run it mechanically rather than trusting the field.** — [B] verified, **with one
failing instance that must travel with the entry.**
`THE_HONESTY_PROTOCOL_STANDALONE.md`:10-11 declares
`strip_test: "Contains zero terms specific to any one theory. If a domain term appears,
it is a defect"` — and the same frontmatter opens with a domain-specific routing block.
**The contract is violated by the file that declares it.** Two independent parties
re-ran the grep: the *bodies* of the two standalone manuals and two draft chapters are
clean of corpus vocabulary, with matches landing on frontmatter lines only
(`SESSION_AUDIT_2026_08_05.md`:60). The practice stands; that file's frontmatter does
not satisfy it.

**Ship a numbered table, not a theory.** A theory fails whole; a table fails line by
line, so when one line is shown wrong you strike it and keep the others. **If a line in
your table has many dependents, it is a theory in disguise.** — [S] the format claim,
[B] the single historical case: **Brahmagupta, 628 CE** — one author, one table, two
boundary cases, of which the closed line is the only one that did not travel, and
nothing was derived from it, so nothing fell when it fell.
`THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md` §14:365-371. Doctrinal ancestry is explicitly *not*
claimed, and §13.7 pre-concedes the reading is contestable.

**Close what you can compute, defer in writing what you cannot, and never close a case
to look complete.** — [B], one dated case: the deferred line survived roughly twelve
hundred years while three successive attempts to fill it (**c. 830**, **1150**, **1831**)
each failed. The transferable diagnosis: *"the first reviser did not fix the wrong line;
he filled the deferred one, and added a second error."* Same file §14:365-367.
Adoptable today as a one-line editing policy.

**Never let a formal result support a decision without passing visibly through
interpretation.** "The model proves X, therefore we should do Y" launders a formal or
conjectural claim into a consequence; the hidden step is always the interpretive
bridge. — [S]. `THE_HONESTY_PROTOCOL_STANDALONE.md` §2:60-72, worked in §8:182-198 on a
deliberately mundane non-mathematical example (support tickets and churn), carried at
[D] with an explicit note that treating the example as evidence would be the drift the
protocol exists to catch.

**Put a gate in front of every irreversible act:** is the tier of the claim I am acting
on irreversibly high enough for the irreversibility of the act? This forbids not bold
action on thin evidence but **unmarked** bold action on thin evidence. — [S]. Same file
§6:131-145. It ran under real conditions on 2026-08-05, routing thirteen open items to
owner-only against thirteen marked agent-actionable, with reasons stated per item — and
the same audit caught the gate being bypassed once, when two documents were flipped to
