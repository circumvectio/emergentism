# Owner direction — v3

The owner accepted the v3 plan to distill the source-separated estate through
the Emergence Stack and rebuild Emergentism.org as a Public Wisdom Instrument.

The direction fixes these boundaries:

- `Public` is a lighting event, not truth or epistemic promotion.
- `EM-WISDOM-001@1` begins as `PROVISIONAL` and
  `AUTHORIZED_NOT_LIT`; `SUPPORTED = 0`.
- Menexus supplies reviewed craft and QA discipline, not identity, marks,
  product claims, or doctrinal authority.
- Estate applications remain source-owned candidates until separately adopted.
- Source inputs must be exact committed bytes; dirty, uncommitted, historical
  replay, or foreign-worktree copies cannot silently enter.
- Existing URLs and lifecycle classifications remain preserved.
- Acceptance ends at locally committed `OFFLINE-READY`; push, deployment,
  publication, product adoption, and external validation are excluded.

The Rosetta method informed source separation and adversarial checks. Its
dispatch receipt carrier was already frozen-drifted at implementation time, so
no new unreceipted agent convergence is claimed as process evidence, truth, or
authority.
