# The Public Wisdom Compact

> Keep possibility wide. Make commitment accountable. Nothing promotes
> itself. Reality retains the right to correct every map.

1. **Nothing promotes itself.** A record cannot supply its own sufficient
   warrant, authority, outcome, or publication evidence.
2. **Keep possibility wide; make commitment accountable.** Exploration may
   branch freely; commitment names its bearer, source, scope, cost, mandate,
   reversibility, and expected consequence.
3. **Preserve ancestry.** Correction creates a successor and an explicit
   relation to its predecessor; it never silently overwrites history.
4. **Capability grants no authority.** Intelligence, popularity, product
   position, consensus, fluency, wealth, or technical capacity cannot authorize
   a consequence-bearing act.
5. **Keep the consequence chain typed.** Proposal, Judgment, authorization,
   execution, receipt, independent outcome, and revision are different events.
6. **Keep the ledgers plural.** Creation, destruction, and preservation stay
   separate for every affected bearer and horizon; no unsupported scalar hides
   a failed row.
7. **Publicity begins another loop.** Reach is not truth. Contest, correction,
   supersession, retraction, and genuine Exit remain available after lighting.

## Present warrant

This is a chosen editorial policy `[D]`, not a theorem of the Good and not a
scientifically validated result. Its maturity is `PROVISIONAL`. It becomes
`SUPPORTED` only if admitted independent outcomes show that applying it improves
error detection, correction, reversibility, or bearer protection against named
rivals without firing its kill criteria.

## Kill criteria

Narrow, supersede, or retract the Compact if it systematically blocks justified
action, hides real authority, makes correction harder, preserves harmful data
against valid deletion duties, or performs no better than simpler native audit
and decision practices.
