# The method

```text
freeze → recover source → type warrant → split proposition → name rival
       → choose discriminator → expose kill → preserve residual and survivor
       → index bearers and horizons → classify provisionally → seek contact
```

For every affected bearer `b` and horizon `T`:

```text
Means[b,T] = carrier, process, cost, consent/mandate, authority,
             reversibility, foreseeable externalities
Ends[b,T]  = target, hard limit, option change, residue, Exit, uncertainty
```

`T_S` is the shortest informative receipt horizon. `T_L` is the longest
responsible horizon with named causal pathways and revision points. No scalar
cancels a failed row. Ends never redeem means.

A problem result stays unearned until its native statement is recovered,
premises and residuals survive, serious controls are tested, the kill remains
unfired and two independent native-domain reviewers agree.
