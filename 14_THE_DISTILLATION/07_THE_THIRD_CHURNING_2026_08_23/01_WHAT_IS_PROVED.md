# What is proved

The analytic floor remains thin: reciprocal-chart identities and inequalities,
ordinary limit and field facts in their native domains, and scoped combinatorial
closure after a vocabulary is selected. These results inherit their mathematical
owners. They do not prove ontology, ethics, strong emergence, force mappings,
F5, universal game compression, centre-optimality in real systems, consciousness,
an afterlife or philosophical completion.
