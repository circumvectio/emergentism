# The Third Churning

We ask what survives a disciplined attempt to refute it. At one frozen source
state, every atomic proposition keeps its source, tier, rival, kill condition,
residual debt, and affected bearers over a stated horizon.

Plainly:

- **Survivor candidate** — optionally *Amrita*, nectar.
- **Refutation or warning** — optionally *Hālāhala*, poison.
- **Open** — insufficiently adjudicated to enter either outcome.

These are proposed classifications at one snapshot, not eternal verdicts or
evidence tiers. Earned review remains separate. PQA remains `54/0/0/0`.

> **The means is the message. The ends are the limits.**

## Admission law

1. One proposition per record; composites split.
2. A source and evidence tier are mandatory.
3. A serious rival, discriminator, kill and residual are mandatory.
4. Normative or teleological effects name affected bearers and both responsible
   horizons; no aggregate cancels a failed bearer-horizon row.
5. Proposed classification never becomes earned review by publication.
6. Failed claims remain visible with their survivors and repair routes.
7. At most 64 atomic drops may enter this edition.

The method is detailed in `05_THE_METHOD.md`; the full atomic state is machine
readable in `data/` and rendered deterministically by the public-site builder.
