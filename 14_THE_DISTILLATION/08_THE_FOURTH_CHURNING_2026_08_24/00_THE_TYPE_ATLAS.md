# The Type Atlas

## 1. Core diagnostic

Let a term `x` be used under a declared role `T(x)`. Let a relation or
operation `R` declare the roles it accepts. The expression `R(x,y)` is formally
ill-typed only when the declared roles of `x` and `y` fall outside the domain
of `R`. In informal philosophy the proposed analogue is weaker `[I/C]`: an
argument commits a candidate type collision when it treats two roles as
interchangeable or crosses between them without a defended bridge.

```text
different roles alone                 ≠ collision
alleged invalid identity or inference = collision candidate
repair removes whole contradiction    = possible scoped dissolution
repair leaves native mystery intact   = clarification, reframe, or open
```

A lawful bridge must name its domain, assumptions, warrant, preservation
conditions, discriminator, and kill. Correspondence, fluency, consensus,
owner adoption, or a completed schema cannot supply that warrant.

## 2. Seven axes and twelve subtypes

The selected compression is:

| Axis | Diagnostic subtypes |
|---|---|
| `LEVEL` | Ground/object · value/symbol/process/operator/boundary · type/token/member |
| `MODAL` | possibility/probability/represented alternative ↔ actuality |
| `TEMPORAL` | order/trace ↔ passage/cause |
| `REPRESENTATIONAL` | sign/map/model/carrier ↔ content/referent/territory |
| `EPISTEMIC` | evidence/warrant/provenance/consensus ↔ truth |
| `NORMATIVE` | fact/cause ↔ value/ought/reason/purpose/authority |
| `BEARER` | part/bearer/continuity ↔ whole/aggregate/identity/survival/horizon |

Phenomenal experience is not silently absorbed into representation. The
carrier/function/report ↔ experience subtype remains an explicit diagnostic
whose default is `UNDERDETERMINED`, because separating reports from experience
does not explain why experience exists.

## 3. Result ceiling

The Fourth Churning records only `SELECTED_UNREVIEWED` diagnoses. It may say
that a collision is proposed, partial, absent, or underdetermined. It may not
change a PQA row, satisfy native-review quorum, or award `TYPE_DISSOLUTION`.

An earned dissolution would additionally require:

1. exact native recovery;
2. a visible invalid join rather than merely unlike concepts;
3. preservation of every relevant native premise;
4. removal of the whole contradiction after conservative repair;
5. incremental value over native and generic controls;
6. an unfired kill; and
7. two independent blinded native-domain reviews bound to the exact trial.

## 4. Strongest rival

Ordinary native conceptual analysis plus generic typed decomposition may
recover every useful distinction with less proprietary vocabulary. If that
rival matches the atlas on clarification, error detection, transfer, and harm
prevention, the framework-specific distinctiveness dies. The surviving method
is then simply: declare types, expose bridges, preserve debt, and correct.
