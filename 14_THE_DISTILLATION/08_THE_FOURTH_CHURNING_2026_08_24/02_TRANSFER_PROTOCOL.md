# Transfer protocol

The existing PQA development, validation, and held-out labels are contaminated
for Fourth Churning use: the questions and prior answers were already visible
before this grammar was frozen. All 54 rows are therefore retrospective
development material for this edition.

A future transfer test requires:

1. an independently selected question set under human custody;
2. precommitted question, rubric, split, and truth/review hashes;
3. no access by grammar authors before the grammar freeze;
4. matched native-frame, generic seven-axis, four-domain null, Emergentist, and
   shuffled-placebo arms;
5. post-run reveal;
6. two blinded native-domain reviews per claimed result; and
7. publication of nulls, harms, forced fits, and killed diagnoses.

The score is a vector: coverage, grammar size, ambiguity, forced-fit rate,
premise preservation, residual preservation, incremental value over controls,
review agreement, and transfer. No primary scalar leaderboard is produced.

No hidden questions or truth artifacts are created by this offline release.
Future custody and any external model calls require separate authorization.
