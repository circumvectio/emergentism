## 1 · The three modes of counting

**`[I]` · `05_COSMOLOGY/01_THE_TRANSCENDENTAL_TRINITY/49_THE_THREE_MODES_OF_COUNTING.md` §1 (:40-59) — STAGED PROPOSAL, unratified**

At the ground seat counting cannot begin; in the realm counting is meaningful (you might not count, count and stop, or count forever); at the horizon counting cannot complete. The two boundary seats fail approach for **opposite** reasons — nothing distinguished yet, nothing left outside — which is what makes them a pair rather than two arbitrary ends.

*Attacked.* Three earlier discriminators for the same seats were proposed and killed on the record (§6, :183-201): uncountability, non-self-membership, the barber. It was then contested by a parent document — `43` had the proper class at the ground seat, `49` §1 at the horizon — and the third-pass correction resolved the collision in favour of §1 and moved the object in `43`. A same-day independent audit attacked `49` and found §4's *account* of how the reading was reached to be false; the document records the verdict against itself at :137 — *"So §1 was right and §4's account of how it got there was wrong."* Attacked, holed elsewhere, and the reading came through.

*Inherited ingredient, named in place:* the property that discriminates is **absorption**, owner **Richard Dedekind**, *Was sind und was sollen die Zahlen?* (1888). It is `[A]` and is not this file's; see the inherited-mathematics ledger.

**LIVE KILL (49:253).** Show two of the three modes collapse into one. NOT FIRED — no candidate offered.

## 2 · Co-constitution

## 3 · The category error read as a type fact, three-fold

**Reading `[I]`, on an inherited `[A]` spine · `48` §6 (:354-395) and `49` §5 (:156-181) — both STAGED PROPOSAL, unratified**

The refusal of `13/•` and `13/○` is a fact about kinds, not a prohibition — *"the operand is the wrong kind of thing, not the wrong size of thing"* — and the split is three different failures, not one rule: at `•` counting has not begun, so there is no item to be an operand; at `⊙` a **collection** was offered where a **member** is required, a level error; at `○` nothing is outside it, so it is a member of nothing.

*Spine, inherited and named in place:* a proper class is an object of the theory but is not a member of any class — **von Neumann–Bernays–Gödel / Morse–Kelley**. The typed-domain framing (an operation is a function with a declared domain) is ordinary mathematical practice. The three-fold split is the corpus's.

*Attacked head-on.* The independent session audit ruled the doctrine *"true of one half, false of the other, and the split runs exactly along the subscript you dropped"* (`00_HANDOFF/SESSION_AUDIT_2026_08_05.md:71`) and found the concession **already present** in both documents: `48` §6 and `49` §5 each carry a *"Scope, honestly"* paragraph conceding that ordinary `a/0_N` remains undefined for the ordinary algebraic reason, true in `GF(7)`, which has no limit, distance or approach at all. Three registers, three facts, none doing the others' work — that fence is what survived. The audit narrowed it further and the narrowing travels with the entry: a declared partial operation buys *"caught before evaluation"*; only the `_T` subscript buys *"category error"* (audit :117).

**THREE LIVE LIMITS, which must travel with this entry.**
1. It does **not** extend to numeric `0_N`. `0` **is** a real number; what is true is `0 ∉ ℝ^×` (`53_THE_NUMBER_CHART.md:78`, ruling Z1, owner: standard field theory). Bare *"X is not a number"* is banned phrasing by signed ruling, 2026-07-31.
2. The middle row depends on `⊙` denoting the realm. That binding is a **contested owner act**, not a settled premise: `49` §3 records the owner's reversal, then a `RESOLVED 2026-08-05` block amending the canon emblem map as *staged propagation under the owner's completion instruction* — the same generic-directive warrant class the same day's audit condemned in a different instance (`:155`). Under the pre-amendment canon the row reads `13/1 = 13` and there is no category error at all.
3. That boundary labels *denote* proper classes is *"a modelling claim about that framework, argued case by case"*, not a theorem of any set theory — `THE_BOUNDARY_RULES_STANDALONE.md:174`, and soft spot 4 of 8 at `:339`, where it is named *"the defect most likely to be present."*

**LIVE KILL (48:407).** Exhibit a proper class as a member of something, or license counting before distinction. NOT FIRED.

*Do not confuse it with its fired neighbour.* §6's concluding slogan — *"Everything that can be counted, measured, divided, or operated on lives strictly between them"* (`48:379-380`) — is declared *"either false … or an empty restatement"* by its own child document, and §6 still carries **no** correction marker while §4.1 and §5.2 do. That is a live defect on the reader-facing surface, not a reading, and it is filed as such.
