# Flow State as an Epistemic Mode

> **[金] Crack:** the predecessor made chart balance diagnose flow and then made
> `η` diagnose its morality. **Repair:** flow, truth, performance, and Justice
> are distinct measurements.

Flow names an actual, reportable pattern of absorbed attention and skilled
action under specified conditions. The episode, report, behavior, physiology,
task, and model token are `D4 = actual`; represented goals and alternatives are
`D5 = merely_possible`.

A study must separately operationalize challenge–skill relation, absorption,
self-report, performance, temporal distortion, error, and relevant physiology.
The reciprocal chart and `P=ΦV` may organize hypotheses at `[I/C]`; they do not
measure flow by identity. Hemispheric Master/Emissary language is an analogy,
not a settled neural mechanism.

harmful, or exploitative aim fluently. Epistemic warrant still requires valid
evidence and error correction. Justice still requires complete bearer
accounting, authorization, consent, custody, cost, payer, beneficiary,
reversibility, exit, contest, and outcome receipt. `etaObserved=0` is necessary
but insufficient.

flow from matched high-performance, habit, absorption, mania, or coercion
