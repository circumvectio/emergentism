# Word, Token, Concept and Memotype Carriers

## 1. The correction

The word *chair* is not itself the complete meme, concept, or class of chairs.
One inscription or utterance is an actual token. A speaker or system may use
that token to cue a concept. A recurrent cultural pattern may be carried by
many words, images, demonstrations, artifacts, habits, interfaces, and norms.

The selected contract is:

```text
WordToken := one actual spoken, written, signed, or encoded occurrence

WordType := a declared equivalence over WordTokens

ConceptRecord := an actual represented distinction, relation, prototype,
                 rule, or model state; not identical to a word

CarrierInstance := a named actual implementation at a time and boundary

CarrierVariant := a difference between implementations that a declared
                  persistence test may admit or reject

MemotypeCandidate := a recurrent D4 carrier-pattern whose reproduction,
                     reconstruction, or enforcement measurably changes later
                     interpretation or selection
```

These definitions are `[S]` inside the framework. Whether `MemotypeCandidate`
is a useful general level of cultural explanation is `[I/C]`.

## 2. The relations are many-to-many

```text
WordToken       may instantiate WordType
WordToken       may cue ConceptRecord
ConceptRecord   may be cued by many words and nonlinguistic traces
MemotypeCandidate may span many ConceptRecords and CarrierInstances
MemotypeCandidate may participate in an EgregoreotypeCandidate
```

No arrow is identity or entailment. The same token can cue different concepts
in different contexts; different tokens can cue similar concepts; imitation or
ritual can carry a pattern without a proposition; and two carriers may
reconstruct similar behavior without copying one internal representation.

The strongest inherited objection comes from
[Sperber](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780192632449.003.0008): cultural
stability may arise through reconstruction, inference, and attributed
intention rather than gene-like copying. Even a defender of memetics,
[Blackmore](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781444314922.ch15), records unresolved
disagreement about meme, vehicle, and internal/external carrier distinctions.
The corpus therefore treats *Memotype* as declared and contested terminology,
not settled biology.

## 3. The chair example

For a declared dataset at a declared time:

- the actual object records classified as chairs are a finite D4 set;
- the token `"chair"` is one possible address into that set or concept field;
- the classification criteria may involve shape, affordance, social use,
  history, context, and relations;
- possible or unseen chairs may be represented as D5 content without becoming
  actual instances;
- a disagreement about a borderline object is evidence about the classifier,
  not proof that one timeless vector already contains the essence of chair.

A vector database can implement one retrieval surface for tokens, embeddings,
and neighbors. It is a useful engineering analogy for relational memory. It is
not the ontology of meaning: embeddings depend on model, corpus, objective,
metric, version, and query context.

The safe compression is:

> **Many tokens and practices may carry one recurrent meaning-pattern, but the
> pattern is neither one token nor a hidden object stored intact in every
> carrier.** `[I]`

## 4. From memotype to represented actor

A MemotypeCandidate can carry stories and policies attributed to an actor. It
does not become an Egregoreotype merely because many people repeat them.

The cross-owner route is:

```text
WordToken / ritual / image / interface / rule
  -> ConceptRecord and MemotypeCandidate
  -> possible shared actor-model
  -> parent Egregoreotype five-marker test
  -> optional StrategicEgregoreotypeCandidate test
```

The final subtype is owned in
[`The Emergentotype Carrier Map and the Strategic Egregoreotype Candidate`](../../05_COSMOLOGY/00_EMERGENTOTYPE_CARRIER_VARIATION_AND_STRATEGIC_EGREGOREOTYPE.md).
It requires attributed policy, prospective and counterfactual predictions,
intervention-sensitive uptake, recurrence, and value beyond ordinary rivals.

## 5. Discriminators

1. **Synonym/paraphrase substitution:** change tokens while preserving the
   putative meaning and test whether the effect survives.
2. **Context substitution:** hold the token fixed while changing context and
   test whether the represented distinction changes.
3. **Nonlinguistic substitution:** replace words with demonstration, image,
   ritual, interface, or incentive.
4. **Carrier substitution:** move the trace across people, artifacts, media,
   models, or languages.
5. **Trace intervention:** perturb the alleged memotype while matching salience,
   incentive, authority, repetition, and exposure.
6. **Prospective prediction:** predict later interpretation or selection before
   observing it.

Reject a token-identity claim when synonyms preserve the effect or identical
tokens diverge by context. Reject a language-exhausts-memotype claim when a
matched nonlinguistic carrier preserves the effect. Reject a
MemotypeCandidate when copying/reconstruction and trace intervention add no
predictive value beyond ordinary learning, incentives, command, or common
environment.

## 6. What this file refuses

- a gene–meme identity;
- one word equals one meme;
- one vector equals one concept;
- linguistic agreement proves shared meaning;
- shared meaning proves truth, consent, or Justice;
- cultural recurrence proves a collective mind;
- a represented actor proves an actual, conscious, or legitimate actor.

What survives every kill is the type discipline: token, type, concept,
carrier, cultural pattern, collective trace, strategic attribution, and
consciousness remain separate questions.
