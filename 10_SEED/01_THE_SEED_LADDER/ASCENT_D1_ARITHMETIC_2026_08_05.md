## I. The spine — `S` and `ι`, and nothing else

Two operations on one primitive. Everything countable follows.

```
S(x) = x + 1        the successor — never rests, no fixed point
ι(x) = 1/x          the inversion — one fixed point, the unit

ℕ⁺   2 = S(1)         3 = S(S(1))        multiples of one
ℚ⁺   3/5 = SιSιS(1)   every rational      one reduced word
```

`S` walks outward; `ι` folds back. Their interplay generates the positive
rationals, and the positive rationals are — for this rung's purposes — the
domain where counting means something. The irrationals are processes, not
results: √2 is a rule, π is a rule, `e` is a rule. You can run them; you
cannot arrive.

**The seam.** `φ·ν = 1`. Stipulate `ν := 1/φ`. Then the product is one
everywhere — a chart identity, `[A]` by construction, empty of world. It
is the conservation law, not the measure. Two readings of one position,
collapsed to a single coordinate. `[A]`, and the corpus fences it in the
same breath: *the seam is never the score, never the node law, never an
ethics warrant.* (CC-CORE-1.)

**AM-GM, the price of imbalance.** For the conserved pair: `φ + ν ≥ 2`,
equality iff `φ = ν = 1`. `[A]` — inherited, elementary, classical. But
this is the rung's one theorem with a **direction** in it: departure from
the fixed point is never free. Downstream it becomes the equatorial maximum
of `B = 2/(φ+ν) = sin θ ≤ 1` — the score that measures how balanced your
position is. The score is conditional, never the seam.

## II. G2 is Euclid's algorithm — and the corpus said so first

This is where the day's hardest-won honesty lives, and it must stay visible.

The bijection from reduced words over `{S, ι}` onto `ℚ⁺` is true:
```
S^a₀ ι S^a₁ ι ⋯ ↦ [aₖ; …, a₁, a₀+1]
```
Machine-verified on 10,945 words. Six mutants each tripping the declared
checks. The load-bearing detail — the `+1` forcing the last partial
quotient `≥ 2` — is precisely Hardy & Wright's classical uniqueness
normalisation. **The corpus derived the right constraint from the right
reason and had re-derived a theorem older than the printing press.**

**Verdict: KNOWN-UP-TO-REFORMULATION. `[C] → [A]` inherited-with-citation.

The unit's position is **relational**, not discovered.

## IV. Division by zero — the typed cure

The paradox is old; the cure is a **domain and category correction**:

- In a field, `N/0` for `N≠0` is undefined: `0·y = N` has no solution.
- On the projective line, the associated map `f_N(z) = N/z` extends with
  `f_N(0) = ∞`, `f_N(∞) = 0`. Adding `∞` makes the pole representable; it
  does not turn the field expression into a field value.
- `0/0` is undefined. `∞/∞` is not a field expression. Only limit forms may
  be indeterminate once functions and paths are declared.
- The frame/operand split: `0_T, 1_T, ∞_T` inhabit `TitanFrame` and admit
  no arithmetic. `0_N, 1_N` inhabit number structures and are lawful
  operands. The dead form was the claim that ordinary identities can never
  be operands — not the Titan type law.

The triadic glyph `•  ⊙  ○` is a **non-operational emblem**. Sovereign roles
shown without an infix operator, because there is no Titan arithmetic.

## VI. The construction seeded off-centre

Seed the base at `1` and it reaches 43 of 43 test rationals. Seed it at
`2` and it reaches 27 — but not `1`. Seed it at `3` and it reaches 19 —
but not `1`. Seed it at `1/2` and it reaches 15 — but not `1`.

**Proof:** to land on `1`, the final letter is `S` (requiring predecessor
`0 ∉ ℚ⁺`) or `ι` (requiring predecessor `1`). By induction, `1` is
reachable only from `1`. ∎ `[A]`

Seeded off-centre, the construction reaches `ℚ⁺ \ {1}`. It gets
everything except the one place worth standing. **The construction does not
lose its centre by moving it. It loses its centre by never being able to
arrive.**
