## I. The model — what a quantum state IS, at this rung

Not a particle. Not a wave. Not change. **A state.**

```
system boundary S declared
ρ_S = ρ_S†,  ρ_S ≥ 0,  Tr(ρ_S) = 1     the density operator
M = {E_k},  E_k ≥ 0,  Σ_k E_k = I        a declared measurement

p(k | ρ, M) = Tr(ρ E_k)                  the Born rule [A]
```

The pair `(ρ, M)` generates a probability distribution. Neither `ρ` alone
nor `M` alone suffices — the same state can answer different measurements
with different distributions. This is not a philosophical proviso; it is a
mathematical fact, verified by counterexample:

```
|+⟩⟨+|  and  I/2   both give (1/2, 1/2) for a Z-measurement
                    but an X-measurement distinguishes them:
                    |+⟩ gives +1 with probability 1
                    I/2  gives (1/2, 1/2) again
```

One measurement cannot carry the full state. `[A]` — elementary quantum
information.

The physics is inherited `[A/B]`. Calling it D3 is the Emergentist crosswalk
`[I]`. The reciprocal Burri chart is NOT the Born rule and is not used by
this model. `[S]` — stated, fenced, and verified.

any D4 history; `ΔS/Δτ` is not.

Born rule. Deriving Born weights from the seam is a named kill on the D3

## III. The one dissolution — and its exact scope

The classical intuition: *"a quantum state is just our uncertainty about a
hidden value."* A fixed measurement cannot distinguish a pure state from a
mixed state that happens to agree on that measurement. But the X vs Z
comparison above refutes the intuition: **the same probabilities for one
measurement, different probabilities for another.** The state carries more
than any single classical distribution.

This is a **formal discriminator** `[A]`, not one unique metaphysics. It
establishes that `ρ` cannot be reduced to a classical probability vector
without loss. It does NOT establish that `ρ` is ontic, real, or a
mind-independent object. The interpretation question is left open on
purpose. (`PD_12_MEASUREMENT_PROBLEM`: measurement correspondence, not
dissolution.)

The scope is exact: the discriminator is licensed for this rung's
mathematics. *"Dissolves the measurement problem"* by the same trick is a
**named dead form** — the discriminator is licensed here, for this one
question, because here the question's own terms are the mathematical
objects. It does not generalise for free.
