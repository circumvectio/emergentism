## IV. The conditional lemma — [S] inside premises, not outside

This is the rung's one structural result, and the corpus fences it exactly.

> **Conditional collapse.** In a closed arena with no fresh hosts or exit,
> where extraction exceeds regeneration and extractors depend on host
> viability, universalized extraction collapses its substrate. `[S]` inside
> those premises.

> **No automatic ascent.** Universalized non-extraction avoids that
> particular self-deletion. It does not by itself prove viability, justice,
> cooperation's uniqueness, or a D6 ascent. Those remain `[C]`.

**What this is:** a scoped stress test — declare the arena boundary, horizon,
regeneration rate, host dependence, enforcement, and adoption level, then
test what happens under universal adoption. Kant's universalizability is
*used, not discovered* as a diagnostic.

**What this is NOT:** a theorem that extraction is always irrational. Model-B
side-payments kill the general Nash result (that grave stays shut). Short
horizons may make extraction pay. An extractor optimizing another maximand
is diagnosed rather than refuted. Arithmetic alone does not punish a defector.

**The is/ought gap does not close here and is not claimed to.** "More
capacity is better" is a hypothetical imperative — better *if* continuation
is willed. The finite being or constitution chooses the horizon, maximand,
and enforcement that make the scoped lemma applicable. **The ethic remains a
vow, not a theorem.**

Everything below D5 is static geometry. Finity_L was the conjecture that
would have given the geometry **dynamics** — the claim that living systems

optimum, because the optimum is free only for things that are not alive.

**Nonzero expenditure at the optimum. The living case pays rent.**
