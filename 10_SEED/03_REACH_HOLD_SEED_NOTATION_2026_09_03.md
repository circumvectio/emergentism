# Seed notation — cone, path, reach, hold `[D]`

This seed has no letter-legacy. It does not rename the corpus.

```text
cone     the option cone under model M
path     the committed worldline
reach  = μ(cone)        ∈ [0,1]
hold   = viability(path) ∈ [0,1]
P      = min(reach, hold)
```

Vision *sees* the cone. Mission *defines* the path. Those are acts, not the quantities. An act and a measure are different types.
