# External component calibration

## Verdict

The Compass is now **externally cross-calibrated component by component**. The
integrated Emergentist synthesis is **not externally validated**. Literature
contact found four corroborated analogues, four independent convergences, two
unresolved composites, one tension, and one refuted historical identification.

This is calibration in the strict comparative sense: an outside body of work
can support, bound, oppose, or fail to address a local claim. A resemblance
never transfers proof through the Rosetta, and no collection of precedents
substitutes for a novel, preregistered discriminator.

## Method

Each cluster was compared with a primary paper, formal source, or authoritative
institutional statement. For every match the audit asked five questions:

1. What external result actually exists?
2. Which typed Emergentist claim does it touch?
3. What stronger reading does it not license?
4. Does it corroborate, converge, leave unresolved, create tension, or refute?
5. What test could move the surviving claim?

`[A]` marks established mathematics and adequately custodied attested outside
facts or measurements. `[B]` marks the locally built and directly verified
cross-calibration artifact. `[I]` marks the translation. `[C]` marks the
untested Emergentist extension. External attestation and local verification are
different kinds of warrant, not a single prestige ladder.

## What changed

- The Compass may no longer describe every component as externally untouched.
- The Soul Loop's model-mediated future effect has direct experimental contact.
- The line/circle paradox now has two typed forms: an exact generalized-circle
  theorem and a finite-resolution lower-bound lemma. Neither decides cosmic
  topology.
- `BR-3` constraint has a serious outside analogue, but remains a crosswalk rather
  than a new fundamental law.
- Power-Max becomes more conditional, not less: outside theory supplies both an
  option-preservation result and an extraction counterexample.
- The rapidity/time-dilation/longitudinal-contraction component is inherited
  special relativity. The horn-torus morph is a removable display, and its
  mouth-as-light-cone/worldline reading is refuted by the local geometry.
- The five-crossing census, saturation-only μ law, full Egregoreotype
  conjunction, and universal product fit remain unwon `[C]` claims.
- The quantum-dimensional identification is a closed grave. Removing the inset
  leaves the operational calculus unchanged.

   extraction without destroying durable option capacity for either bearer.
6. Preregister at least one prediction on which the Emergentist grammar and a
   serious rival give different quantitative answers; obtain independent
   replication.
