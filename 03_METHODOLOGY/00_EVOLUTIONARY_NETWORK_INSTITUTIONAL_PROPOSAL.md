# The Evolutionary Network

## Voluntary, appealable, repairable and dissolvable

This document repatriates one institutional question from an external runtime
lineage: can a learning network coordinate useful specialization without
becoming an enforcement organism? The external source remains read-only
provenance and supplies no authority here.

The proposal is not deployed. It has no members, assets, jurisdiction,
custody, enforcement, governance power or performance evidence.

## 1. Voluntary entry `[S/C]`

Entry requires a plain mandate, bounded task, named data and property custody,
time limit, affected-bearer record, cost ceiling, revocation path and practical
exit. A participant may use ordinary role descriptions; Rosetta labels are
optional work lenses, never identities or ranks.

No worldview adherence, ritual, score, biometric, social class, spiritual
claim or founder relationship is an admission criterion.

**Rival:** ordinary project teams, cooperatives, standards bodies and
federations may coordinate better with less conceptual overhead.

## 2. Appeal and dissent `[S]`

Every consequential proposal is separated from authorization and execution.
It must state evidence, rival, bearers, mandate, expiry, contest path and
reviewer conflicts. Dissent remains visible and may use language outside the
framework. Participation never proves agreement.

An appeal suspends only the contested consequence when that is safely
possible. No person or software agent reviews its own irreversible act as the
sole appellate body.

## 3. Repair and learning `[S/C]`

Commitment and outcome receipts remain distinct. A learning cycle records:

```text
proposal → authorization or refusal → bounded act → observed outcome
→ bearer challenge → correction → repair → revised or killed rule
```

Self-issued success reports do not count as outcome evidence. Repair names who
was affected, what is restored, who pays, when it is checked and what remains
unrepaired.

## 4. Dissolution `[S]`

Every unit declares a sunset, data export, property return, record custody,
unfinished obligations and dissolution trigger before activation. Exit means
leaving with lawful data, work product, reputation record and practical
alternatives intact.

Dissolution does not erase liability, receipts or repair. A network that cannot
close safely cannot claim Grace Exit.

## 5. Ordinary failure rivals `[C]`

Before invoking an Egregoreotype, capture or civilizational story, compare:

- unclear goals and ordinary management failure;
- underfunding, scarcity and capacity limits;
- bad incentives or asymmetric information;
- coordination and communication cost;
- heterogeneous preferences;
- legal, security or custody constraints;
- measurement error and Goodhart effects; and
- founder, vendor or platform concentration.

The proposal is killed or narrowed if ordinary institutions outperform it on
delivery, cost, correction, dissent protection, repair or exit; if the role
language creates status; if appeals are decorative; or if dissolution cannot
return people and property safely.

The survivor may be only a checklist for voluntary institutional design. No
organism metaphor is necessary.
