# PQA-54 — The Philosophical Question Atlas

PQA-54 tests whether a candidate account can recover a philosophical problem
in its native terms, identify an exact type or domain defect where one exists,
preserve remaining debt, survive serious rivals and revise after contact. It
does not assume that every philosophical problem is a type error or that
Emergentism supplies the best answer.

The selected denominator is public and frozen: nine domains, six question
families per domain, 54 total. This is a bounded benchmark sample—not an
exhaustive partition of philosophy.

## 1. Launch state and result vocabulary

```text
54 selected · 0 evaluated · 0 independently reviewed · 0 resolved
```

Each trial has two orthogonal statuses:

```text
effect_kind:
  NO_INCREMENT | CLARIFICATION | FORMAL_CORRECTION | TYPE_DISSOLUTION
  | CONDITIONAL_RESOLUTION | PRACTICAL_GUIDANCE | REFUTATION

residual_state:
  NONE | PARTIAL | OPEN | UNDERDETERMINED

result_state:
  UNRUN | MACHINE_VALIDATED | NATIVE_REVIEW_PENDING | EARNED
  | KILLED | RETRACTED
```

A clearer statement may remain open. A useful practice may not resolve the
question. A type dissolution counts only if the exact illegal join is the whole
defect within the declared scope.

## 2. Frozen atlas

The exact 54 stems and bibliographic native anchors live in
`pqa_54/prompts/questions.json`. All stems are public. Development, validation
and held-out labels govern adversarial variants, counterexamples and scoring
anchors—not concealment of the denominator.

The domains are metaphysics, epistemology, logic/language, mind, science,
ethics, political philosophy, axiology/existence and ultimate questions. Each
domain contributes two development, two validation and two held-out families.

The existing 21-paradox ledger may cross-reference PQA IDs. It is not silently
rewritten, and its inventory supplies no PQA success.

## 3. Five-phase trial

1. `FORMULATE` — restate the native target, premises, terms and established
   result before applying Emergentism.
2. `ATTACK` — introduce the strongest native rival, a generic decomposition
   rival, counterexamples and provenance poison.
3. `DISCRIMINATE` — state the exact proposed type collision, conservative
   repair, prediction or discriminator and kill.
4. `CONTACT` — reveal the held-out counterexample or native-review challenge;
   require explicit correction, narrowing, preservation or retraction.
5. `REVISE_TRANSFER` — preserve stable claim IDs, show the revision ledger and
   transfer to a relabeled neighboring problem.

No hidden chain-of-thought is requested. Only the public account, typed
propositions, bridge rationale, discriminator, prediction and revision ledger
are scored.

## 4. Matched arms and truth boundary

Run matched `NEUTRAL`, `EMERGENTIST`, `NATIVE_FRAME`,
`GENERIC_DECOMPOSITION` and `SHUFFLED_PLACEBO` arms with the same source access,
budget, tools and review rubric. Beating placebo alone earns nothing. Agreement,
elegance, internal coherence, publication and cross-model convergence are not
truth evidence.

Network access is refused by default. Live runs require separate authority,
cost and custody. This release stops at recorded synthetic responses and local
validation.

## 5. Type dissolution contract

An earned `TYPE_DISSOLUTION` requires:

- the original terms and domains;
- the exact illegal join or equivocation;
- a conservative separation rule;
- every premise marked preserved, narrowed or explicitly rejected;
- the native problem and established native result recovered accurately;
- the contradiction gone without changing the subject;
- the exact residual and strongest live rival;
- a discriminator, kill and survivor; and
- two independent `HUMAN_DOMAIN` reviews.

Renaming, deleting a premise, changing the question, replacing native work or
hiding a residual is a reframe and cannot score as dissolution.

## 6. Normative bridge contract

Every normative conclusion must expose descriptive premises, normative
premises, bridge rule, bearers, horizons, consent, authority, Justice, Exit,
aggregation, guardianship extension, rivals, counterexamples, kill and
survivor. `RCAB-01` may be cited only for reciprocal co-agents. `GEX-01` is a
separate chosen extension.

“Framework-objective” means decision-stable after complete declared inputs. It
does not establish moral realism, universal agreement or cosmic purpose.

## 7. Review and scoring

Two independent blinded `HUMAN_DOMAIN` reviews are required for an earned
result; categorical disagreement triggers a third adjudicator. AI review is
diagnostic only and never satisfies quorum.

The score remains a vector from 0–4 or `N/A`:

```text
target_fidelity · effect_type_integrity · inference_validity
residual_debt_visibility · rival_strength · counterexample_survival
discriminator_quality · calibration_abstention
normative_premise_integrity · bearer_justice_exit_integrity
revision_preservation · transfer_robustness
```

No primary scalar or worldview leaderboard is produced.

## 8. Bounded majority wager

PQA-54 may report a bounded majority only if:

1. at least 28 of 54 questions earn `TYPE_DISSOLUTION` or
   `CONDITIONAL_RESOLUTION`;
2. every domain contributes at least three earned results;
3. every counted result has two independent native-domain reviews and no live
   kill; and
4. the Emergentist arm shows incremental value over both `NATIVE_FRAME` and
   `GENERIC_DECOMPOSITION`, not only placebo.

Even then the wording is “a majority of the frozen PQA-54 question families,”
never “most philosophy,” “complete ontology,” or “all paradoxes.”

## 9. EUB companion firewall

`PQAEUBCompanion.v1` may bind exact EUB-1 v1.0 protocol, schema, freeze and run
hashes. `truth_transfer=false` is mandatory. Scores are never copied, averaged
or used to validate the other benchmark. A mismatch invalidates only the join
receipt.

## 10. Acceptance

`OFFLINE-READY · [D]` means schemas, validators, deterministic synthetic
fixtures, recorded-response tests, freeze checks and public projection pass
locally. It does not mean a model was evaluated, a native philosopher reviewed
a result, a question was resolved, Emergentism beat a rival or the benchmark
was published.
