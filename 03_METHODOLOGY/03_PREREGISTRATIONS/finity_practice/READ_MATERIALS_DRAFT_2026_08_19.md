## 1 · Consent script (draft)

> We are testing whether a public webpage communicates clearly. We are testing
> the page, not your intelligence, morality, beliefs, or decision competence.
>
> Purpose: see whether a stranger can classify the page, name its tension,
> find the worksheet, and leave without being taught the project's words.
>
> Procedure: about 20 minutes. Five seconds looking at a homepage, then a
> short set of questions; ninety seconds of ordinary browsing and more
> questions; one made-up scenario that is not about your real life.
>
> Risks: mild time cost and possible frustration. No medical, legal, or
> financial advice. You will not be asked for a real consequential decision.
>
> Data: answers are stored under a participant code. Contact details, if any,
> are kept separate. No audio or video unless you later give separate consent
> to be quoted.
>
> Compensation: a fixed time honorarium once you start, kept if you stop.
>
> Withdrawal: skip any question; stop at any time; keep the honorarium; you
> may withdraw attributable answers until the disclosed anonymization cutoff.
>
> Conflict: the page's author directed this test. The interviewer is not
> rewarded for favorable answers.
>
> Complaint / stop: name the custodian here only after D-OWNER-03 is set.
> Until then this script may not be used.
>
> Debrief: we will say we were testing the page's clarity, not you.

## 3 · Interviewer script

Use the packet §§Phase A–C verbatim. Do not paraphrase.

**Phase A** (five-second homepage, then remove): kind of thing; problem in one
sentence; expected next act; hesitation or pressure.

**Phase B** (ninety seconds ordinary navigation): Finity Card in own words;
one thing it cannot establish; does completing it authorize action on another
person; where to look for rivals/failures; show how to leave.

**Phase C** (unbranded hypothetical — standardized, not the reader's life):

> A small team can ship a public page this week. A quieter check with five
> people would take fourteen days. One teammate needs spoken clarification
> and has not been asked. The only early signal is whether two people come
> back without being chased. The page would collect no payment. What review
> point would you set, who must agree, what evidence would change the plan,
> and what residue remains if you stop?

Score meaning, not vocabulary.
