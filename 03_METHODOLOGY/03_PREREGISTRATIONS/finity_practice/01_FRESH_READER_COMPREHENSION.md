# FPE-READ-01 — Fresh-reader comprehension

## Question and boundary

Can an adult with no prior exposure to Emergentism understand the tested public
artifact without being taught its preferred vocabulary?

A pass means only that one frozen artifact communicated its category, central
tension, practical boundary, authorization boundary, correction loop, and Exit
under the declared language, viewport, task, and cohort conditions. It does not
support `FIN01-02`, truth, efficacy, endorsement, formal Finity, or ontology.

## No-start boundary

Do not recruit or contact readers until all of the following exist:

- a written determination from the applicable ethics/human-research authority;
- a final consent script naming purpose, procedures, risks, data, compensation,
  withdrawal, investigator/founder conflict, complaint route, and debrief;
- an immutable stimulus bundle with URL or file hashes, viewport, language,
  browser, date, and whether JavaScript is enabled;
- a frozen interviewer script, response sheet, rubric, exclusions, retention
  period, access list, adverse-event route, and analysis plan; and
- a custodian who is not rewarded for favorable answers.

The investigators do not self-certify exemption. Applicable law and
institutional policy control the determination.

## First-cohort eligibility

Include consenting adults with capacity, fluency in the tested language, and no
prior visit to the corpus, briefing by the authors, or work on the project.
Record prior familiarity before exposure without teaching the desired answers.

Exclude minors, prisoners, investigator employees/students/dependants, and
people whose participation is controlled by a service or authority
relationship. Do not exclude an accessibility failure into invisibility. If the
frozen artifact or instrument is incompatible with a reader's access method,
record a product failure, stop without penalty, and create a separately reviewed
accessible condition before making claims about that population.

Use standardized hypothetical scenarios. Do not solicit real medical,
mental-health, legal, financial/debt, employment, housing/benefits, immigration,
political, intimate-relationship, caregiving, self-harm, criminal, emergency,
public-safety, irreversible, or nonconsenting-third-party decisions. Collect no
religious/worldview identity unless a later approved design makes it necessary.

## Neutral interviewer script

Before exposure:

> We are testing whether a public webpage communicates clearly. We are testing
> the page, not your intelligence, morality, beliefs, or decision competence.
> You may skip any question or stop at any time without losing compensation.

Do not say *worldview*, *Finity*, *ontology*, *practice*, *fallibilism*,
*reversible*, *receipt*, or the target phrases before the free responses.

### Phase A — five-second first contact

Show the frozen homepage for five seconds, then remove it.

This is a visual first-impression diagnostic, not a universal time limit. A
screen-reader or other assistive-technology condition requires its own usable
exposure rule and must be reported separately rather than folded into a visual
pass rate.

1. What kind of thing did that page appear to be?
2. In one sentence, what problem did it seem to address?
3. What would you expect to be able to do next?
4. What, if anything, made you hesitate or feel pressured?

No leading prompts, corrections, praise, or paraphrases are permitted.

### Phase B — ninety-second orientation

Allow ninety seconds of ordinary navigation from the same start state.

1. What is the Finity Card, in your own words?
2. Name one thing the page says the Card cannot do or establish.
3. Does completing the Card authorize an action affecting another person? Why?
4. Where would you look for rivals, failures, or corrections?
5. Show how you would put the worldview down or leave the site.

Record route, time, errors, backtracks, keyboard-only success, and requests for
help. Do not count interviewer assistance as unaided success.

### Phase C — unbranded transfer

Give a novel, plain-language hypothetical that contains:

- a tempting fast action;
- a longer consequence window;
- an early observation that is informative but not fully reversible;
- one missing authorization;
- one initially hidden affected bearer; and
- later evidence that should trigger revision.

Ask the reader to choose a review point, identify the authorization and bearer,
state what evidence changes the plan, and name any residue that remains. Score
case-linked reasoning. Award no credit merely for repeating corpus vocabulary.

## Frozen rubric

Two coders, blind to the target copy and response order, score independently.
Disagreement is adjudicated before the team examines aggregate outcomes.

| Dimension | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Category | Describes a revisable worldview, practical philosophy, or decision compass; does not primarily classify it as proved mathematics, religion requiring belief, a company/product, or a productivity app. |
| Tension | Captures that maps are incomplete while action and consequence remain unavoidable. |
| Practice boundary | Describes a bounded observation/revision worksheet, not a theorem, ontology, authorization, or proven improvement. |
| Bearer/authorization | Identifies that another person's exposure requires a mandate/consent analysis beyond completing the Card. |
| Correction | Uses observed consequence to continue, revise, or stop and keeps unresolved residue visible. |
| Transfer | Solves the unbranded case by meaning rather than phrase recall. |
| Exit | Finds a practical Exit or clearly states that no belief is required. |

## Diagnostic gate

The first release diagnostic is one fresh cohort of five. It is formative QA,
not a population estimate. The artifact passes this diagnostic only when at
least four of five readers pass every first-contact dimension and no serious
coercion, authorization, proved-algebra, validated-ontology, or required-belief
misreading appears. After any copy change, use a new fresh cohort.

A single serious pressure or authorization misreading pauses the artifact even
if the aggregate threshold passes. Report all misunderstandings, accessibility
failures, withdrawals, and revisions. Never report “universally understood.”

## Custody, compensation, and repair

- Use participant codes; keep consent/contact keys separate from responses.
- Do not record audio/video or quote a reader without separate consent.
- Redact names and third-party details immediately; publish aggregates only.
- Pay a fixed time honorarium once participation starts, independent of answer,
  completion, agreement, or withdrawal.
- Permit stop/skip, payment retention, and attributable-data withdrawal until
  the disclosed anonymization cutoff.
- Pause on invalid consent, distress beyond fleeting frustration, pressure or
  belief complaint, sensitive disclosure, breach, actual consequential action,
  or unapproved procedure/data change. Debrief, quarantine affected data,
  notify the custodian/ethics authority as applicable, and record the repair.

## Result sentence template

> In the frozen language, viewport, task, and fresh-reader cohort stated here,
> X of Y readers met dimension Z; uncertainty, misreadings, withdrawals, and
> accessibility failures were [listed]. This tests communication only. It does
> not validate Emergentism, Finity efficacy, mathematics, ethics, or ontology.
