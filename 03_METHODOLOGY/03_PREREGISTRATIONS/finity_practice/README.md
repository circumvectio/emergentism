# Finity Practice Evaluation Program

This folder types three open questions into independently freezable draft
packets. It is
an execution surface, not a semantic owner and not a preregistration receipt.
The Finity Card remains defined in the Lived Compass; `FIN01-02` remains `[C]`.

## The three gates

| Gate | Exact question | What a favorable result may establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`FPE-READ-01`](01_FRESH_READER_COMPREHENSION.md) | Can a fresh reader classify, bound, transfer, and leave the public artifact without coached vocabulary? | Scoped comprehension of one frozen artifact. | Truth, efficacy, endorsement, or ontology. |
| [`FPE-REVIEW-01`](02_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) | What do conflict-declared external reviewers find that the authors and agents missed? | Named external criticism and protocol repair within declared expertise. | Consensus, safety, validation, or replication. |
| [`FPE-COMPARE-01`](03_CONTROLLED_FINITY_COMPARISON.md) | Does the Finity prompt bundle add domain-bounded value over a strong ordinary worksheet after brand, prompt organization, ordinary-method heterogeneity, burden, harm, and attrition are measured or bounded? | At most a scoped comparative result in the sampled domain. | Formal Finity, a complete ontology, objective ethics, or general decision superiority. |

The machine-readable [gate registry](GATE_REGISTRY.json) records current-draft hashes,
claim/docket bindings, external state, dependencies, kill routes, and future
result custody. Every missing prerequisite has an empty evidence record; a
future promotion requires a contained artifact, matching digest, and contained
matching receipt rather than a status declaration alone. Comprehension and
review never promote comparative efficacy.

The [internal Rosetta review receipt](../../../00_META/reviews/2026_07_28_FINITY_EVALUATION_ROSETTA_REVIEW.md)
preserves the initial holds and final contract pass. It is not the independent
review gate and supplies no world-facing evidence.

## Freeze and amendment law

1. External review receives the exact source, card, registry, and packet hashes.
2. Material changes produce a new version and hash; they never rewrite a frozen
   packet after contact.
3. Participant contact begins only after the applicable ethics determination,
   final consent/custody plan, adverse-event route, and time-stamped read-only
   preregistration exist.
4. Results are judged against the frozen version. Nulls, harms, withdrawals,
   deviations, and dissent remain visible.
5. Personal and third-party data are minimized or deleted under the consent and
   retention plan; archive-first does not require retaining identifiable data.
6. Any later external-state promotion requires a hash-matching structured JSON
   receipt whose event and custodian match the registry. Independent replication
   additionally requires declared external custody, conflict disclosure, and a
   custodian different from both original data and result custody. The receipt
   binds those declarations; it does not prove identity or independence by itself.
