## 1. The analytic kernel `[A]`

```text

φ := cot(θ/2)
ν := tan(θ/2)
```

Then:

```text
φν = 1
(φ−ν)² ≥ 0
φ+ν ≥ 2
B := 2/(φ+ν) = sin θ ≤ 1
```

analytic consequences of the selected definitions. They establish no empirical
conservation law, ontology, energy function, ethic, teleology, consciousness
theory, or cosmology.

Emergentism selects `{0,1,∞}` as Titan boundary tokens. Ordinary `0` and `1`

For every bridge:

1. name source and target types;
2. state what relation is preserved;
3. state what does not transfer;
4. assign evidence tier;
5. compare a serious rival;
6. make a prediction or disclose that none exists;
7. attach a kill criterion and update from receipts.

external discriminator fails, downgrade the relevant conjecture. If the
integrated worldview repeatedly produces no novel predictive gain over simpler
rivals, stop calling it a scientific unification candidate.
