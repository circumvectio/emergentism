## Falsification Queue

1. **Forbidden-path test:** construct a higher-level rule that requires a
   transition outside lower support. `BR-3` must reject it.
2. **Empty-crossing test:** propose a μ-crossing with no new degree or recovery
   map. `BR-2` must reject it.
3. **Null-learning test:** repeat a discrepant receipt while neither updating
   nor recording a justified null. `BR-5` must reject the learning claim.
4. **Carrier-turnover test:** replace every carrier. If the trace and its
   selection effect vanish, no Egregoreotype was present.
5. **Failed-Rosetta test:** translate a non-isomorphic domain. The lawful output
   is partial or failed, never a forced six-rule/seven-stage fit.
6. **Cone-metric test:** redraw a wider cone without declaring an axis or
   reachable-state metric. No quantitative claim is licensed.
7. **Justice test:** expose a claimed collective gain whose payer, coercion, or
   exit loss was hidden. The aggregate syntropy claim fails.
8. **Quantum-removal test:** delete the entire `[C]` inset. If any operational
   rule breaks, the draft has improperly borrowed authority from quantum theory.
