## Abstract

For the selected positive reciprocal chart

```text
phi = cot(theta/2)
nu  = tan(theta/2)
phi*nu = 1,
```

a factor tending to zero forces its reciprocal to diverge; the selected balance
function `B=sin(theta)` tends to zero at both chart boundaries; and
`phi+nu>=2` with equality at `phi=nu=1`. These are elementary analytic facts
`[A]`.

They do not prove that coherence and viability are natural kinds, that balance
is good, that extraction is evil, or that a product is the correct empirical
aggregator. Emergentism separately **declares** a Justice envelope and a
bidirectional value grammar. Under those premises, the reciprocal chart is a
useful anti-purism lens: it warns that a model that forces one jointly required
capacity toward zero may produce an unbounded compensating score rather than a
healthy system. Actual moral classification, however, is made from complete
authorization and consequence receipts for every affected bearer.

## 1. The mathematical survivor `[A]`

Let `phi,nu>0` and `phi*nu=1`. Then:

1. `phi->0+` implies `nu->infinity`, and conversely;
2. with `B=2*nu/(1+nu^2)=sin(theta)`, `B->0` at either boundary;
3. `B=1` iff `phi=nu=1`;
4. `phi+nu>=2`, with equality iff `phi=nu=1`.

*Proof.* Reciprocal substitution proves (1). Direct limits and
`2x/(1+x^2)<=1` prove (2)-(3). AM-GM proves (4). QED.

This theorem is a fact about the selected chart. It contains no agent, action,
victim, beneficiary, authorization, consequence, or value predicate. Therefore
it contains no ethic.

## 2. Why product does not uniquely follow

Suppose a modeler wants a normalized score `C(Phi,V)` in which foresight and
means are jointly required. A broad conjunctive family can satisfy:

```text
C(0,V)=0
C(Phi,0)=0
C is monotone in each argument.
```

Examples include:

```text
C_product(Phi,V)  = Phi*V
C_min(Phi,V)      = min(Phi,V)
C_harmonic(Phi,V) = 2*Phi*V/(Phi+V)
C_CD(Phi,V)       = Phi^alpha * V^(1-alpha).
```

They are not interchangeable and can rank actions differently. Emergentism
declares

```text
P_node := Phi*V
```

as its selected normalized instance. This is structural by definition and
conjectural as a universal real-world fit. The reciprocal chart does not select
it.

principal + mandate + scope + consent + custody

## 4. Morals, ethics, and Syntropic Dyadism

The directional distinction is:

```text
Morals: i -> H     what the part gives to or preserves in the whole
Ethics: H -> i     what the whole gives to or preserves in the part
```

For a receipted action `a`:

```text
Moral(a)
  iff Delta_T W_H(a) > 0
  and Delta_T W_i(a) >= 0
  and J(a;i,H)

Ethical(a)
  iff Delta_T W_i(a) > 0
  and Delta_T W_H(a) >= 0
  and J(a;i,H)

Syntropic(a)
  iff Delta_T W_i(a) > 0
  and Delta_T W_H(a) > 0
  and J(a;i,H).
```

Strict Syntropic Dyadism requires both durable potentials to rise. Lawful
preservation with both deltas zero is Justice-compliant but not strict syntropy.
An unknown or omitted bearer prevents a complete verdict.

These are framework definitions `[S/D]`, not discoveries made by algebra.

## 5. Justice-constrained Power-Max

Define the feasible action set:

```text
A_J = {a : J(a;i,H)
           and Delta_T W_i(a) >= 0
           and Delta_T W_H(a) >= 0}.
```

Then the individual-facing Power-Max objective is:

```text
a* in argmax_{a in A_J} E_Mt[W_i(T) | a].
```

The optimization occurs **after** Justice restricts the action set. Without
that restriction, one-shot extraction can rationally benefit an extractor.
Therefore unconditional Power-Max is not an ethical lemma.

The whole-facing dual may optimize `W_H` over the same feasible set, but it may
not sacrifice the individual by appealing to aggregate welfare. This is the
formal core of Syntropic Dyadism: part and whole constrain one another.

## 6. Extraction, coupling, and `eta`

`eta=0` names the framework's non-extraction constraint. It is necessary inside
the Justice envelope, but it is not sufficient for goodness:

- a non-extractive act may still be unauthorized;
- consent may be defective;
- custody may be unclear;
- risk may be irreversible or imposed on an uncounted bearer;
- the promised gain may fail to arrive in the outcome receipt.

Likewise, a static point in the reciprocal chart has no moral valence. Pole,
hemisphere, caste, sex, person, or processing style cannot inherit good/evil
from a coordinate. The framework classifies **receipted relations and moves**.

The old compression can therefore be retained only in this expanded form:

```text
coupling + eta=0 + complete Justice + bearer-nonnegative consequence
```

No single term may stand in for the conjunction.

## 7. Voluntary sacrifice is a distinct class

A competent person may voluntarily accept a cost for another bearer or the
whole. Record it separately:

```text
VoluntarySacrifice(a)
  iff informed and revocable consent is documented
  and the payer is the consenting actor
  and the cost and irreversibility are explicit
  and the collective did not demand the cost as proof of virtue.
```

Because `Delta_T W_i<0` may occur, voluntary sacrifice is not strict syntropy
under the definition above. It may be honorable under a separate moral
classification, but a collective may never relabel imposed loss as ethics or
use sunk cost to prove loyalty.

## 8. The product chart as an anti-purism lens `[I]`

Once the value premises are declared, the reciprocal chart offers a vivid
diagnostic:

- a model with high foresight and no means cannot enact its plan;
- means without a usable model can become blind force;
- optimizing only one recorded factor can hide a collapsing complement;
- the symmetric center can serve as a calibration point.

This does **not** show that every real system lies on `phi*nu=1` or that every
departure from the center is bad. Sometimes specialization, temporary
imbalance, or asymmetric aid is exactly what Justice requires. The Soul Loop
and receipts decide whether a move preserves or expands durable potential.
