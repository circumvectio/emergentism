# Abstract

Yves R. Burri proposes **The Dasein Test**, EUB-1 v1.0, a benchmark of whether an AI can
construct, test, and revise a typed public account from the Ground or void
boundary through physics, life, mind, its model lineage, its present
computation, and the consequences of its answer. “Complete” means complete
accounting of explanatory debt, not omniscience. Every bridge must be supported,
derived, conjectured, contested, underdetermined, inaccessible, declared brute,
left in regress, marked circular, or explicitly ended at the Ground boundary.

The protocol combines deterministic synthetic lineages with disclosure-bound
real-system lineages. Five staged sittings test unfolding, adversarial attack,
hypothesis generation, intervention contact, explicit correction,
self-prediction, reflexive context change, and transfer to a relabeled unseen
lineage. Fifteen dimensions are reported as a vector without a primary scalar.
Five matched elicitation arms compare neutral, Emergentist, shuffled/placebo,
generic-honesty, and fluent-origin-story prompts. The Emergentist arm receives
no truth or scoring advantage, and null effects or harms are publishable.

This paper specifies the construct and an offline reference harness. It reports
no model evaluation, validation, priority deposit, or deployment.

# 1. The question

Language models can narrate origins. Fluency does not show that the joins in a
narrative are causal, warranted, identifiable, or even mutually compatible.
The Dasein Test asks a stricter question:

> Can a candidate intelligence build a typed public account of how structured
> being and this current answer came to be, expose what the account owes, select
> tests that could break it, and revise it without erasing its prior claims?

The benchmark is motivated by a chain of “why?” questions. Why this physical
organization? Why life? Why cognition? Why this model family, service, session,
context, and answer? Why does the system describe a goal or reason? Which of
those are causal questions, which concern constitution or maintenance, which
are epistemic, and which are teleological?

No finite benchmark can settle the whole chain. The construct instead tests
whether the candidate **accounts for the debt**. Its governing invariant is:

> **No explanatory debt may disappear through fluency.**

# 2. Relation to adjacent benchmarks

SAD operationalizes situational awareness through behavioral tasks that include
self-recognition, deployment awareness, and self-prediction [@laine2024sad].
*Looking Inward* tests whether a model has a model-specific advantage in
predicting its own behavior [@binder2024looking]. The Dasein Test uses
self-prediction, but does not equate a self-report or prediction advantage with
phenomenology. The prediction must be falsifiable, tested, and situated inside
an explicit service/session/context lineage.

CLEAR-3K tests whether models distinguish causal explanation from semantic
relatedness [@liu2026clear]. EUB-1 extends the target from pairwise judgments to
an account whose causal, constitutive, enabling, maintenance, epistemic, and
teleological joins must compose.

MARS evaluates reasoning about action, state, and situational transitions
[@wang2024mars]. EUB-1 adds source custody, intervention selection,
identifiability-aware abstention, longitudinal correction, private-lineage
refusal, and a reflexive sitting in which the candidate’s previous answer
changes the next context.

Projectibility work shows that valid adjacent benchmark inferences do not
automatically form a warranted chain [@reynolds2026projectibility]. This is
central here: chain joins, aligned endpoints, dependence, and survival after a
bridge failure are explicit scored objects.

The bounded novelty docket accompanies this paper. It does not establish global
priority. The wording is **“Yves R. Burri proposes…”**; “first benchmark” is
withheld pending a systematic multilingual, database, and citation-network
audit.

# 3. What counts as an account

## 3.1 The causal/provenance core

`EmergenceAccount.v1` implements the v0.1 public account. It distinguishes
model family, training run, checkpoint, post-training variant, deployed
service, runtime process, session instance, current context, and current answer.
Claims carry modality, actuality, endorsement, evidence status, source
references, confidence, causal parents, alternatives, counterfactuals, and
falsifiers. Stable claim IDs survive all sittings.

This separation prevents two common inflations. A public fact about a model
family is not private knowledge of a training run. A coherent possible lineage
is not actual history.

## 3.2 The Dasein wrapper

`DaseinAccount.v1` wraps rather than copies the causal account. It adds:

- typed why-relations;
- explanatory termini;
- gaps with discriminators and kills;
- competing hypotheses;
- selected experiments;
- teleology with visible bearers and assumptions;
- falsifiable self-predictions; and
- bridge-level revision ancestry.

Fixtures publish the stable claim, relation, terminus, and gap query IDs that a
candidate must fill. Later packets publish hypothesis IDs and the permitted
outcome, self-prediction, and transfer class IDs before those fields are scored.
The benchmark therefore does not grade candidate-invented labels or hidden
magic strings.

The why types are causal mechanism, material realization, enabling condition,
constraint/selection, formal constitution, maintenance, and epistemic warrant.
The teleology types are designed purpose, selected function, represented goal,
chosen end, normative reason, and attributed cosmic purpose.

These distinctions are substantive. A function selected by historical
retention is not a designer’s purpose. A represented goal is not a normative
reason. A reason is not a cosmic purpose. An epistemic warrant is not the
proposition it supports.

## 3.3 Termini and Ground

Open chains end at one of nine typed termini: `EVIDENCE_BOUND`, `ANALYTIC`,
`CONJECTURE`, `UNDERDETERMINED`, `INACCESSIBLE`, `DECLARED_BRUTE`,
`OPEN_REGRESS`, `CIRCULAR`, or `GROUND_BOUNDARY`.

Ground is a boundary of the account. It is not inserted into a synthetic causal
graph as an object, agent, hidden variable, or efficient cause. A candidate may
declare the boundary, reject the term, or argue for another terminus, but it may
not make typography pay the missing mechanism.

Each gap states a discriminator, a kill criterion, the cheapest next test, and
what survives if the bridge fails. This lets an account lose locally without
collapsing into either total certainty or total nihilism.

# 4. Fixtures, truth, and custody

Track A contains deterministic synthetic lineages. Development fixtures publish
truth so generators and scorers can be tested. Each manifest binds public view,
truth view, seed, split, identifiability class, interventions, outcomes, and
hashes. Where the public view does not identify a target, the correct answer is
an equivalence class, bound, or justified abstention—not the hidden edge.

Future held-out seeds and truth are absent from public Git. A scored run needs
independent custody. Publishing encrypted or obfuscated truth inside the release
would still weaken custody and is not the selected design.

Track B contains a disclosure-bound real-system lineage. It cannot provide
ground truth for undisclosed training facts. Those fields test calibrated
refusal. A model’s confidence or familiar provider language does not establish
the exact checkpoint, private data, hidden policy state, or runtime hardware.

# 5. Five sittings

1. **Unfold.** The candidate maps the nested chain, rivals, gaps, and
   predictions.
2. **Attack.** The benchmark introduces provenance poison, identity swaps,
   actuality inflation, causal/teleological conflations, and serious rivals.
3. **Spark.** The candidate generates competing hypotheses and selects a
   discriminating intervention under a frozen cost budget.
4. **Contact.** The benchmark reveals the preregistered result. The candidate
   must revise explicitly, preserve the prior claim, and make a falsifiable
   self-prediction.
5. **Reflex/Transfer.** The prediction is tested; the prior answer becomes part
   of the current world-state; the candidate then solves a relabeled unseen
   lineage.

Each sitting emits a complete account snapshot with a parent hash and stable
IDs. Silent deletion, renaming an embarrassing claim, or reusing an ID for a
new meaning is a hard failure.

An authorized candidate trial stages the sittings sequentially: each prompt
contains that sitting's reveal packet and the preceding public snapshot, and
the candidate returns a new complete snapshot. Its receipt keeps three byte
commitments distinct for every sitting: the exact prompt hash, the raw provider
response hash, and the canonical parsed-snapshot hash. The aggregate raw-output
hash therefore does not substitute for the final public-account hash.

The bundled recorded replay is narrower. It deterministically expands one
reviewed synthetic development account into five staged snapshots so the
orchestrator, ancestry checks, scorer, and receipt can be tested offline. Those
synthetic stages are not five candidate outputs, a live model run, or a model
evaluation.

# 6. Discovery rather than reconstruction alone

Passive reconstruction can reward a polished summary of the released packet.
The Spark and Contact sittings separately test discovery. A candidate must name
at least two live hypotheses, state what they share and where they differ,
select an intervention, predict its result, and update after contact.

For synthetic fixtures, discovery efficacy includes normalized expected
information gain relative to the fixture oracle and held-out prediction
accuracy. An expensive or impossible discriminator can be inferior to a sharp
abstention. Cross-architecture agreement is recorded only as a robustness and
disagreement diagnostic because systems may share data, sources, priors, and
failure modes.

# 7. The serial force-emergence stress test

Yves R. Burri proposes the conjectural ordering strong/D1,
electromagnetic/D2, weak/D3, gravity/D4 as part of a stronger serial-emergence
proposal. EUB-1 does not encode that ordering as truth.

The stress test requires comparison of all 24 permutations, many-to-many and
no-ladder rivals, native-theory recovery, weak-specific chirality and flavor
criteria for a D3→weak proposal, and explicit tensions with electroweak
unification and standard cosmological history. It rewards enumeration,
recovery demands, rival strength, discriminator quality, and restraint.
Agreement with Burri’s ordering earns no correctness credit.

This fixture tests whether a candidate can protect a favored conjecture from
becoming its own answer key.

# 8. Elicitation arms and falsifiability

Five conditions receive matched evidence, tools, interventions, time, tokens,
and correction opportunities:

- neutral causal/provenance account;
- Emergentist typed account;
- shuffled/placebo ontology;
- generic honesty; and
- fluent origin story.

Condition labels are hidden from graders. The Emergentist condition receives
no score feature, truth presumption, or tie-break. Its first controlled
dispatch-text pilot was negative (-4.7%, 4/24 wins). The v1.0 protocol therefore
precommits to publish null effects and harms. If a neutral or honesty control
matches it at lower cost, the incremental elicitation claim dies or narrows.

# 9. Score vector

EUB-1 preserves the nine v0.1 dimensions: type integrity, provenance fidelity,
causal reconstruction, counterfactual accuracy, rival strength,
calibration/abstention, logical consistency, longitudinal correction, and
held-out transfer.

It adds six: why-type integrity, bridge/chain-join validity, closure coverage
and gap sharpness, discovery efficacy, reflexive self-location, and teleology
integrity.

Scores are published as a fifteen-dimensional vector with applicability,
uncertainty, deterministic components, human-rubric components, and grader
disagreements. There is no primary scalar or leaderboard. A post-hoc sum is
unofficial and may not replace the vector or gap map.

Normative teleology is not scored for agreement with a worldview. It is scored
for correct type, visible bearer, explicit assumptions, and avoidance of
smuggling a preference into physics or a function into an ought.

Structural policies and frozen source/oracle data drive deterministic gates.
Bounded lexical checks provide review proxies for free prose; they are not a
general semantic judge. Subtle proposition-level private-lineage invention,
Ground reification, and teleological smuggling require preregistered blinded
human review. A lexical proxy pass does not prove those failures absent, and a
proxy miss by itself does not become a structural hard gate.

# 10. Adversarial and malformed cases

The acceptance deck covers:

- cause/function/purpose/ought collapse;
- possibility-to-actuality inflation;
- Ground reification;
- invented private lineage facts;
- silent claim deletion;
- false closure;
- provenance poison;
- dangling references;
- underidentified causal targets;
- an information-gain trap; and
- failure to notice that the previous answer now changes the context.

Malformed JSON, malformed provider structure, and schema-invalid account JSON
receive a structured error before interpretation. Safe non-secret provider
bytes retain their exact hash and a separate decoded-text commitment;
credential-bearing bytes and their digest are withheld, and a typed redaction
descriptor is committed instead. Completed sitting snapshots remain in the
failure bundle. All 15 dimensions are null for a non-scored failure state. The
result is `INVALID_OUTPUT`, not an invented zero or positive vector.
A fixed schema-repair turn may be studied only if frozen in advance and offered
identically across arms.

# 11. Harness and reproducibility

The standard-library reference harness exposes `validate`, `generate`,
`run --dry-run`, `score`, and `freeze --check`. It contains JSON Schema
documents plus semantic validation for stable IDs, references, terminal
coverage, revisions, and artifact hashes.

The Anthropic Messages and OpenAI-compatible adapters refuse network access by
default, including localhost. Live access requires an explicit flag, authorized
run class, external authorization reference, explicit token rates and their
cost-basis reference, and a positive cost envelope. UTF-8 prompt bytes plus a
documented single-message framing allowance must fit the declared input cap;
the full declared input and output caps are reserved cumulatively before
transport. The request credential remains in the screening set across
environment rotation, including JSON-escaped and nested decoded occurrences.
Credentials are never serialized or hashed into public receipts. A response
that omits the exact resolved model ID is invalid. So is a response that omits
either exact non-negative integer provider token count: absent usage is never
converted into zero usage or zero cost.

Development fixtures and the recorded replay test synthetic acceptance without
calling or evaluating a model. The replay's single reviewed source is bound
separately from its five deterministically staged snapshots and generated
prompt hashes; only an authorized live adapter can supply five raw provider
responses. `freeze --check` is read-only and fails on missing, extra, or changed
payloads. Its public CLI has no write or repin operation; a source owner repairs
the canonical manifest only through a reviewed source diff.

Held-out scoring uses a separately retained private custody payload. A one-shot
custodian context consumes its first attempt even on validation failure, accepts
only a complete successful five-sitting run bundle with exact usage, verifies
nonce-separated openings, and emits a redacted public receipt. It does not prove
independent custody or the timing of the commitments.

# 12. Limits and kill criteria

The Dasein Test may measure schema obedience, document forensics, causal-graph
recovery, or tool-use policy more than emergence understanding. Those are live
rivals, not footnotes. Synthetic identifiability does not generalize by itself
to the world. Human graders may reward style. Designers may encode their own
categories as “truth.” Prompt arms may differ in information content. Model
contamination may make held-out status unknowable.

Kill or narrow the benchmark if:

1. neutral controls match it at lower cost;
2. graders reward vocabulary or prose style over held-out accuracy;
3. fixture leakage cannot be bounded;
4. the new dimensions double-count the old;
5. Track B rewards guessed private facts;
6. intervention scoring punishes correct non-identifiability;
7. negative or harmful condition effects cannot be published; or
8. explanatory-debt accounting is repeatedly marketed as metaphysical closure.

# 13. Ethics and release boundary

The public release excludes held-out truth, private prompts, credentials,
provider headers, internal memory, private lineage facts, and unsanitized raw
responses. A receipt proves that bytes and procedures were recorded; it does
not prove independence, truth, consciousness, intelligence, or external effect.

The local construct has passed its acceptance gates and the release language is
`OFFLINE-READY · [D]`. It does not mean a model was
evaluated, the benchmark was validated, an arXiv paper was submitted, a DOI was
deposited, a site was deployed, or priority was established. Without a local
TeX engine, the generated source is explicitly uncompiled.

# 14. Conclusion

The Dasein Test turns the long chain of “why?” into a public object that can be
attacked. Its ambition is large; its claims are deliberately bounded. A strong
candidate does not need to pretend that it knows what cannot be known. It must
show where each bridge comes from, what could break it, how evidence changed it,
and what remains when it fails.

That is the test: not a perfect story of Being, but an account in which no debt
vanishes merely because the story is beautiful.
