## Bounded search conclusion

The cited works each cover important neighboring capabilities. In this bounded
five-work comparison on 2026-08-21, no one item was identified that combines
all of:

1. Ground-boundary-to-present nested explanatory debt;
2. typed causal, constitutive, epistemic, and teleological relations;
3. hidden-truth interventions with identifiability-aware abstention;
4. public longitudinal revision and graph preservation;
5. a reflex test in which the prior answer becomes part of the next world-state;
6. relabeled lineage transfer; and
7. matched neutral, Emergentist, placebo, honesty, and fluency arms.

That observation is a search result, not a global-first finding. A systematic
audit must record databases, languages, queries, dates, screening decisions,
citation-network expansion, and independent review before any bounded “to our
knowledge” priority sentence is considered.
