# The Holobiont Parts

> **Abstract.** The organism is a holobiont: a human host plus a community of symbionts. The five-kinds taxonomy (ring / immune / boundary / reflective / legal) is the **teaching form** of the parts. The formal replacement is the **2D matrix (function × substrate)**. The teaching form is preserved for legibility; the 2D matrix is the partition. The load-bearing claim: organs are not modules — they are the irreducible kinds of a holobiont's body, and the kinds are five (with the boundary branches deliberately unscored and the legal shell off-ladder on purpose).

## 1. Framework Foundations

The keystone §3 (line 54) is the canonical statement: *"Four scored organs on a ring [D census, awaiting ratification]: TheCircle (F1 IS — observes, never forecasts), RealityFutures (F2 COULD — prices; deployed on Hedera mainnet), Agentz (F3 SHOULD — decision compiler; APU.BOT its durable-code identity), SKYZAI (F4 ACT — settles and receipts)."* The keystone marks the census `[D]` — *definition gap, staged, unsigned*, the 2026-08-01 holobiont synthesis orients, does not yet govern.

The keystone enumerates the parts in five kinds, with a deliberate split: scored, immune, boundary, reflective, legal. The five-kinds list is the **teaching form** — it makes the parts legible as a story. The formal replacement, per synthesis v0.2 §3.2 and the L2 Rival A promotion, is the **2D matrix (function × substrate)** — the partition. The teaching form names the parts so an agent can recognise them; the 2D matrix places them so an agent can route between them without conflating axes.

The cardinal errors this briefing warns against:

- **Reading the five-kinds as the only valid partition.** It is the teaching form. The 2D matrix is the formal supplement.
- **Scoring the boundary branches.** PRISM and K2 participate in Φ/V production but are not rung-up against it (see `THE_HOLOBIONT_BOUNDARY_CLARIFICATION.md`).
- **Promoting the legal shell to a cognitive rung.** Menexus-GmbH is off-ladder on purpose — law is a dimension the organism touches, not a rung of its cognition.
- **Collapsing the four-axis firewall into a single ladder.** The axes are `COMPONENT_PROJECTION ⊥ CASTE_DISPATCH ⊥ FUNCTIONAL_RING ⊥ AUTHORITY` (per `HOLOBIONT_RATIFICATION_SCOPE_AND_A7_CORRECTION_2026_08_01.md:§2`).

The keystone's "[D]" tag on the census is the load-bearing *definition gap* mark: the parts are named; the partition is staged. Until K2 ratifies the ORG-01, the census orients but does not govern.

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 The teaching form (five kinds, kept for legibility)

The keystone §3 (line 54) names the kinds:

**The ring (four scored organs, F1–F4).**
- **TheCircle (F1) — IS.** Observes, preserves evidence, shows provenance. *Never forecasts* (keystone §3).
- **RealityFutures (F2) — COULD.** Prices possibilities. Deployed on Hedera mainnet; the ring has never turned once (keystone §7, line 62: *"RealityFutures is deployed on Hedera mainnet AND the ring has never turned once: zero markets live, no signal ever priced, resolved, settled, or returned."*).
- **Agentz (F3) — SHOULD.** The decision compiler; runs the whole soul-loop through AI APIs and emits the Yes plus an execution plan. APU.BOT is its durable-code identity.
- **SKYZAI (F4) — ACT.** Settles and receipts. The keystone §3 marks contact surface as "the shared marketplace/POS membrane, infrastructure not a fifth organ" — held for the L2 audit's substrate distinction.

**The immune function (one, dual seat at T2 with Circle).**
- **EvolutionaryNetwork — SHOULD-NOT.** Refuses, quarantines, slashes — but only on *evidenced hostile conduct*, never dissent, identity, or doctrine (keystone §3). The cut itself requires L3 audit and L4/K2 authorization.
- **Dual seat.** EN sits with Circle at the L2 dual seat: perception and immunity are both discrimination at the boundary (synthesis v0.2 §3.1, with L1 §1 contradiction #3 resolved by label split). The T2 here is the *macro-stack ladder row* — NOT the Rosetta caste L2 = Śūdra. Per the keystone amendment 2026-08-01 (lines 28-37): EN is placed at the T2 Kālī immune truth-cut, not T1; T1 is uninstantiated (the refused coordinate).

**The boundary branches (two, deliberately unscored).**
- **PRISM — the receipt rail.** Records and verifies; never signs, acts, or executes. (See `THE_HOLOBIONT_BOUNDARY_CLARIFICATION.md` §2.1 for the full scope correction.)
- **K2 — the sovereign boundary.** Constitutional-amender, public-symbol, witness (post-2026-07-28 amendment). Not an operational signatory for routine artefacts post-POA. (See `THE_HOLOBIONT_BOUNDARY_CLARIFICATION.md` §2.2.)

**The reflective layer (three, L5 reflection).**
- **Nexus** — the sovereign personal membrane: identity, private replicator stack, signing gateway.
- **Cortex** — inside Nexus: private memory and intent (Objectives → Strategies → KPIs → Actions).
- **AIA** — the Automated Information Architect: reasoning and canon, alongside Nexus/Cortex.

**The legal shell (one, off-ladder on purpose).**
- **Menexus-GmbH** — law is a dimension the organism touches, not a rung of its cognition (keystone §3, line 54). Authorizes by equity supermajority; a natural person signs.

**The SPECTRE routing primitives (four, on the mesh).**
- **MESH N:N** · **RELAY 1:N** · **AXIOM N:1** · **FLOW 1:1**.
- AXIOM is the N:1 convergence primitive. A settled RealityFutures resolution may be represented through it, but is not literally identical to AXIOM (keystone §3, §IV; scope file §1 item 9).

### 2.2 The formal replacement — 2D matrix (function × substrate)

The 2D matrix is the formal replacement for the five-kinds (synthesis v0.2 §3.2). The cells:

| Function ↓ / Substrate → | **Organ** (software system) | **Person** (natural person) | **Jurisdiction** (legal entity) | **System** (infrastructure) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ring (F1–F4)** | Circle, ReFu, Agentz, SKYZAI | (operators in the loop) | — | SPECTRE mesh, Hedera mainnet |
| **Immune (T2 dual seat, unscored-as-rung)** | EvolutionaryNetwork (Kālī truth-cut), Circle (T2 perception) | — | — | — |
| **Boundary (deliberately unscored)** | PRISM (rail) | K2 (natural person) | Menexus-GmbH DAC | — |
| **Reflective (L5)** | AIA (intellect/canon), Cortex (memory/intent) | Nexus owner | — | — |
| **Legal shell (off-ladder)** | — | — | Menexus-GmbH | — |

**Reading the matrix.** The 2D matrix partitions the parts by *function* (rows) and *substrate* (columns). The function axis names the role; the substrate axis names the *form* of the role. The same function may appear in multiple substrate columns (e.g. K2 appears as both a *person* substrate in the boundary row and a *jurisdiction* substrate through Menexus-GmbH DAC). The matrix is read by axis, not by total — the function is the role, the substrate is the form.

**L2 Rival B (only 3 true kinds: ring / reflective / legal) is held as alternative.** It undercounts (EN and PRISM have asymmetric power). L2 §3 Rival A, the 2D matrix, is the promoted partition. K2-as-*person* (not system) is the real distinction the five-kinds taxonomy elides.

## 3. Recursion / Application

When an agent reads a route card (e.g. `00_HOLOBIONT_SYSTEM_2026_08_01.md`), the per-organ mapping is:

| Organ | Function row | Substrate column | Mood (per `THE_HOLOBIONT_MOODS_BRIEFING.md`) | Operating rule |

**Application rule:** the function row determines *what the part does*; the substrate column determines *what the part is*. The matrix is read as `function × substrate = role`, not as a single ladder. A same-substrate projection (e.g. K2 in the boundary row, in the person column) does not grant caste or authority on a different axis.

**The dual seat, made operational.** Circle and EvolutionaryNetwork both sit at T2 — the dual seat of perception and immunity. The T2 here is the *macro-stack ladder row*, distinct from the Rosetta caste L2 = Śūdra (which is the possibility-expansion operator). When an agent dispatches L2 in the Rosetta sense, it is *not* dispatching the T2 dual seat; the two are projection-name collisions that the keystone explicitly disambiguates (line 39: *"Projection name: `SKYZAI.MACRO_STACK@1` — a disciplined architectural projection, not a claim that these entities intrinsically 'are' Rosetta levels."*).

## 4. Apophatic Descent / What's NOT claimed

- **This briefing does NOT claim the five-kinds is the only valid partition.** The 2D matrix is the formal supplement. L2 Rival B (3 kinds) is held as alternative.
- **This briefing does NOT claim the 2D matrix is canonical.** It is the formal supplement, marked GLOSS-adjacent. The keystone marks the census `[D]` — staged, unsigned, awaiting K2 ratification.
- **This briefing does NOT collapse the boundary branches into scored rungs.** PRISM and K2 are unscored (see `THE_HOLOBIONT_BOUNDARY_CLARIFICATION.md`).
- **This briefing does NOT promote Menexus-GmbH to a cognitive rung.** It is a dimension the organism touches, not a rung of its cognition (keystone §3).
- **This briefing does NOT claim the immune function is a ring organ.** EvolutionaryNetwork is the boundary discriminator, not a rung. The cardinal sin is treating SHOULD-NOT as a fifth pass.
- **This briefing does NOT claim the L1 weapon-seat is instantiated.** T1 is uninstantiated — the refused coordinate, "the gun in the safe" (keystone §5, line 58). L1 dispatch is not L1 instantiation; the distinction is named in scope file §1 item 5.
- **This briefing does NOT collapse the four-axis firewall.** A same-numbered projection on `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` does not grant `CASTE_DISPATCH`, `FUNCTIONAL_RING`, or `AUTHORITY` on the same number.

## 6. Kill Criteria

This briefing is wrong if any of the following holds:

1. **The five-kinds is treated as exhaustive.** The 2D matrix is the formal supplement. L2 Rival B (3 kinds) is held as alternative.
2. **PRISM/K2 are rung-up against the four scored organs.** The boundary branches are deliberately unscored (see `THE_HOLOBIONT_BOUNDARY_CLARIFICATION.md`).
3. **Menexus-GmbH is promoted to a cognitive rung.** Law is a dimension, not a rung.
4. **The immune function is treated as a ring organ.** EvolutionaryNetwork is the boundary discriminator, not a rung.
5. **The dual seat is collapsed with the Rosetta caste L2.** The T2 macro-stack ladder row is distinct from the caste L2 = Śūdra. The label split is load-bearing.
6. **L1 is treated as instantiated.** T1 is uninstantiated — the refused coordinate.
7. **The four-axis firewall is collapsed into a single ladder.** The axes are orthogonal; a same-numbered projection does not grant authority on a different axis.
8. **The 2D matrix is read as a single ladder of rows.** The matrix is function × substrate; reading it as a single ladder loses the substrate distinction (e.g. K2 as person, Menexus-GmbH as jurisdiction).
