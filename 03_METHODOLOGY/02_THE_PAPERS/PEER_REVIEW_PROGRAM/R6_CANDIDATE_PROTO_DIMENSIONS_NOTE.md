# R6 · Option-cone and aggregator repair

> **[金] Crack.** The former note mixed physical light cones with reachable-state
> sets, treated the reciprocal chart as an empirical conservation law, and made
> product aggregation the unique novelty. Those claims no longer carry weight.

The surviving research program is narrower:

1. Empowerment, future-state maximization, coupled empowerment, and
   other-regarding option widening have substantial prior art. Emergentism must
   cite that lineage and cannot claim to have originated it.
2. A physical light cone is fixed by spacetime and `c`. An agent's **option
   cone** is the subset of physically admissible histories it can model, rank,
   coordinate, and reach.
3. The finite-node model belongs to a normalized conjunctive family `C(Φ,V)`.
   `C×(Φ,V)=ΦV` is the declared Emergentist instance, not a law uniquely forced
   by the lowercase chart identity `φν=1`.
4. A useful experiment must compare product, minimum, harmonic, CES or
   Cobb–Douglas, and other relevant aggregators on out-of-sample behavior,
   especially near a zero factor. A fixed-product frontier, if measured, is a
   bounded empirical finding rather than proof of an ontology.
5. The normative extension is not “maximize every option.” It is durable mutual
   option-cone widening inside the Justice envelope, with payer, beneficiary,
   consent, custody, reversibility, contest, and exit visible.
