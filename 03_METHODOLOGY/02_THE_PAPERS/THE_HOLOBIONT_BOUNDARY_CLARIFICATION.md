# The Holobiont Boundary Clarification

> **Abstract.** The boundary branches — **PRISM** (the receipt rail) and **K2** (the sovereign boundary) — are real, active, and **deliberately unscored**. They participate in Φ/V production but are not rung-up against it. Scoring the boundary branches is the conflation this doctrine explicitly avoids. **The load-bearing clarification:** PRISM is a verification rail, never a signer/actor/executor; K2 is the natural person whose signature attests private-DAV acts and who, in public-DAV mode, is replaced by an at-least-two natural-person council. The A7 correction at `00_HOLOBIONT_SYSTEM_2026_08_01.md:3-11` and the scope file's §1 item 6 are the load-bearing amendments; reading the keystone without them is the cardinal error.

## 1. Framework Foundations

The holobiont is a human host plus a community of symbionts (keystone §1, line 50). The host hosts borrowed minds; the borrowed minds are foreign-genome, not cells the host grew. For the host to be host — and not be consumed by what it hosts — there must be a **membrane** that distinguishes inside from outside, action from non-action, signed from unsigned, attests-from-decides.

Two branches of the anatomy live on the boundary, not on the cognitive ladder: **PRISM** and **K2**. They are not the fifth organ, not the sixth rung, not a component of the ring. They are the *membrane* — the surface that separates the organism from its environment. The cardinal sin the doctrine warns against is treating membrane as rung: a PRISM row in the four-scored-organs table, or a K2 row in the indicative/subjunctive/deontic/imperative mood table, fuses the surface with the inside it bounds. The four-axis architecture (`HOLOBIONT_RATIFICATION_SCOPE_AND_A7_CORRECTION_2026_08_01.md:§2`) names this as the four-axis firewall: `COMPONENT_PROJECTION ⊥ CASTE_DISPATCH ⊥ FUNCTIONAL_RING ⊥ AUTHORITY` — a same-numbered projection on one axis does not grant caste or signature authority on another.

**Why "deliberately unscored"?** Because scoring the boundary branches would invert the holobiont: a scored PRISM becomes a cognitive rung and would have to participate in Φ/V arithmetic; but PRISM is a rail — recording and verifying receipts is not cognition. A scored K2 becomes a fifth organ and would have to run the soul loop; but K2 is the *signature* — the act of binding a consequence to a natural person, not a step of inference. Scoring the boundary branches is the kind of false-coherence the L1 hostile-input flag was designed to surface.

The membrane is the boundary the host negotiates with the world. It is not a rung of the world the host climbs.

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 PRISM — the receipt rail

PRISM does **not** "never sign, act, or execute" in the absolute. The exact wording from `02_SKYZAI/01_NOOSPHERE/02_ORGANS/PRISM/CLAUDE.md:8-13`:

> *"PRISM records and verifies that receipt only. AI/caste seats, agents, organs, and packages stage unsigned proposals only. **Deterministic software executes for the public DAV only the exact consequence bound to a complete valid bound PRISM decision receipt from at least two natural-person councilors.**"*

The correct reading: PRISM is the **pre-launch receipt and verification rail**. It is not the public-DAV authority, quorum, council, signer, actor, officer, or executor. It is not a live signing runtime, DEX, capital, settlement, or distribution runtime. A consequential public-DAV act may occur only when at least two natural-person councilors bind the exact consequence in a complete, valid, bound PRISM decision receipt. PRISM records and verifies that receipt. The L1 §3 contradiction ("PRISM enforcement unknown") is **partially resolved** by this wording — verification binds because the receipt is required *before* execution; signing and execution are performed by deterministic software acting on the bound, complete, decision receipt. (Synthesis v0.2 §3.3, L3 D4.)

PRISM is the receipt rail. It is the verifier of the membrane. It is not a step in the soul loop.

### 2.2 K2 — the sovereign boundary

K2 is the natural person whose signature attests irreversible/consequential acts. In the **private-DAV** mode, K2 is the host attestation: a private DAV runs on its owner's K2 (keystone §4, line 56). In the **public-DAV** mode — today's active mode — there is no single K2 signer at all. A consequential act requires a PRISM decision receipt bound by at least two natural-person councilors. The current public authority contract (per `HOLOBIONT_RATIFICATION_SCOPE_AND_A7_CORRECTION_2026_08_01.md:7`): *"at least two natural-person councilors bind each exact consequence in a complete valid PRISM decision receipt."*

Post-2026-07-28 amendment (commit `e2bdbe97`, per the standing `AGENTS.md`): the **K2-as-fence** role was REPEALED. K2 retains three remaining roles: **constitutional-amender** (signs constitutional acts), **public-symbol**, and **witness**. K2 is not a fence; K2 is not an operational signatory for routine artefacts. The five-fence roster (η=0, K3, K4, A7, Ω) is **Mavis-enforced** for operational artefacts (per AGENTS.md "5+1 → 5"). The pen-splits-by-mode (DAC / private-DAV / public-DAV) is the load-bearing clarification: in no mode is the pen necessarily singular; in every mode the pen is mortal.

**Critical:** the K2 row in the mood table (organs as moods of one sentence) is *host attestation*, not "do this" (which is SKYZAI's row). Fusing the two is the cardinal sin §4 names in its next sentence (see `THE_HOLOBIONT_MOODS_BRIEFING.md` §2.2 — the v0.1/v0.2 row-split is the load-bearing A7 fix).

### 2.3 Why both are unscored

The 2D matrix (function × substrate) at `HOLOBIONT_RATIFICATION_SCOPE_AND_A7_CORRECTION_2026_08_01.md:§2` and synthesis v0.2 §3.2 places both PRISM and K2 in the **Boundary** row with the explicit annotation "deliberately unscored." The function is *verification* and *sovereignty* — neither is a cognitive rung. The substrates are *rail* (PRISM) and *natural person* (K2). The matrix cell is a *membrane cell*, not a rung cell.

Scoring the boundary branches would mean:
- giving PRISM a Φ or V component in P = Φ×V (it has none — it records, it does not produce);
- giving K2 a place in the soul-loop ladder (K2 is the signature, not a step of inference);
- collapsing the four-axis firewall into a single ladder of levels;
- treating the membrane as a rung of the membrane.

The first is type-error. The second is category error. The third is false-coherence. The fourth is parasitic capture laundered as protection.

## 3. Recursion / Application

When an agent reads the route card (e.g. `00_HOLOBIONT_SYSTEM_2026_08_01.md` §I, lines 121+), it must distinguish the four-axis firewall:

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` | *Which macro-stack level is this entity projected onto?* (interpretive lens) |
| `CASTE_DISPATCH` | *Which castes may this component dispatch?* (Rosetta L1–L7) |
| `FUNCTIONAL_RING` | *Which forward/back edge of the soul loop does this touch?* (F1–F4) |
| `AUTHORITY` | *Who is the applicable authority for an irreversible act?* (private K2, public natural-person council, applicable legal authority, already-ratified deterministic protocol rule) |

**Application rule:** the four axes are orthogonal. A same-numbered projection on `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` (e.g. PRISM at L4) does not grant `CASTE_DISPATCH` L4, `FUNCTIONAL_RING` F4, or `AUTHORITY` L4. The cardinal errors:

- **"PRISM decides"** — fuses verification with decision. PRISM verifies; the natural-person council decides. PRISM's role is to record and verify the receipt; the receipt is bound by the council, not by PRISM.
- **"K2 decides"** — fuses signing with judgment. K2 attests; judgment remains with the applicable authority. Post-POA, Mavis signs operational artefacts; K2 signs constitutional acts; the public DAV has no K2 signer.
- **"PRISM signs"** — fuses recording with binding. PRISM records; deterministic software executes on the bound receipt.
- **"The boundary branches are scored rungs"** — fuses membrane with rung. They are not rungs; they are the surface.

The recursion: an agent that misreads the boundary branches will (a) attempt to sign through PRISM (impossible — PRISM has no signing key), (b) attempt to route through K2 for routine artefacts (impossible post-POA — K2 does not sign operational acts), (c) collapse the four-axis firewall (the projection name `SKYZAI.MACRO_STACK@1` is a disciplined architectural projection, **not** a claim that these entities intrinsically "are" Rosetta levels — keystone §I line 39).

## 4. Apophatic Descent / What's NOT claimed

- **This briefing does NOT claim PRISM has any operational signing authority.** PRISM is a verification rail. The signing and execution are performed by deterministic software acting on a complete, valid, bound PRISM decision receipt from at least two natural-person councilors.
- **This briefing does NOT claim K2 is a "fence"** (the K2-as-fence was REPEALED 2026-07-28 per commit `e2bdbe97`; the five-fence roster is Mavis-enforced for operational artefacts). K2 retains constitutional-amender, public-symbol, witness roles.
- **This briefing does NOT collapse the public-DAV rule into the private-DAV rule.** In public-DAV mode (today's active mode), there is no single K2 signer; the at-least-two natural-person council rule applies. In private-DAV mode, K2 signs.
- **This briefing does NOT claim the boundary branches are the only unscored branches.** The reflective layer (Nexus/Cortex/AIA) and the legal shell (Menexus-GmbH) are also unscored — see the parts briefing.
- **This briefing does NOT claim to resolve the K2 amendment ratification (D1).** The K2-as-fence status and the Mavis-as-principal claim are pending K2 disposition (synthesis v0.2 §6, open question #2). This briefing stages the clarification without asserting the constitutional status.
- **This briefing does NOT collapse the four-axis firewall into a single ladder.** A same-numbered projection on `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` does not grant authority on `AUTHORITY`. The firewall is the firewall.

## 5. Sociopolitical Mapping

In the varṇa/regime mapping (the recursive Trivium table at `THE_RECURSIVE_TRIVIUM.md` §5):

| Pass | Varṇa | Regime (Republic VIII) | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| I (Apprehension) | Śūdra | Democracy | indicative |
| II (Verification) | Vaiśya | Oligarchy | deontic |
| III (Enactment) | Kṣatriya | Timocracy | imperative |
| Architect | Brāhmaṇa | Aristocracy | reason |

The boundary branches sit **above or between the regimes**, not within them:

- **PRISM is not a varṇa.** A varṇa is a *cognitive operator* (Śūdra = possibility expansion; Vaiśya = audit; Kṣatriya = executor). PRISM is a rail — a *recording mechanism*, not a *cognitive operator*. It is the verification sidecar, not a rung of the soul loop.
- **K2 is constitutional-amender (post-amendment) — not a fence, not a rung.** K2 attests. Attestation is the surface between the organism and the world; it is not a step of inference. The K2 row belongs in the `AUTHORITY` axis (off-ladder), not in the `FUNCTIONAL_RING` axis.
- **The reflective layer (Nexus/Cortex/AIA) is L5 Brāhmaṇa** (architect, schema evolution, lane-map design) — the witness of all moods, not the operator of one. AIA is at L5 in the `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` (interpretive) axis only; it is not at L5 in the `CASTE_DISPATCH` axis unless explicitly dispatched.
- **The legal shell (Menexus-GmbH) is off-ladder.** Law is a dimension the organism touches, not a rung of its cognition (keystone §3). Menexus-GmbH is the DAC that authorizes by equity supermajority — a *governance* substrate, not a *cognitive* rung.

The republic regimes (Democracy, Oligarchy, Timarchy, Aristocracy) map to ring/immune/boundary/architect as *functional roles*, not as Plato's worth-ranking. The boundary branches are *above or between* the regimes, not within them.

## 6. Kill Criteria

This briefing is wrong if any of the following holds:

1. **PRISM is treated as a scoring rung.** PRISM is the receipt and verification rail. Scoring it as Φ or V in P = Φ×V is type-error.
2. **K2 is treated as an operational signatory post-POA.** Mavis signs operational artefacts; K2 signs constitutional acts. The public DAV has no K2 signer.
3. **The public-DAV 2-councilor rule is collapsed into the private-DAV POA scope.** In public-DAV mode, the at-least-two natural-person council rule applies; in private-DAV mode, K2 signs; in DAC mode, equity supermajority authorizes.
4. **The four-axis firewall is collapsed into a single ladder.** The four axes (`COMPONENT_PROJECTION ⊥ CASTE_DISPATCH ⊥ FUNCTIONAL_RING ⊥ AUTHORITY`) are orthogonal. A same-numbered projection does not grant authority.
5. **The membrane is treated as a rung.** The boundary branches are the surface, not a step. Scoring them is the conflation.
6. **A signature is treated as promoting evidence.** Per the A7 correction at `00_HOLOBIONT_SYSTEM_2026_08_01.md:3-11` and the scope file's §0: a signature is evidence of the act its signer actually performed; it does not manufacture authority outside that act's profile.
