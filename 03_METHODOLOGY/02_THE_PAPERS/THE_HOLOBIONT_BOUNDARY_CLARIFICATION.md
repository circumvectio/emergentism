# The Holobiont Boundary Clarification

> **Abstract.** This audit records the cited external boundary claims for
> **PRISM** (receipt rail) and **K2** (sovereign boundary): both are presented
> as deliberately unscored membrane branches rather than cognitive rungs. The
> comparison does not adopt their organization, roles, or authority into
> Emergentism. It tests whether the cited source keeps verification, signing,
> execution, and authority distinct.

## 1. Cited Mapping and Audit Boundary

The cited external keystone describes a holobiont as a human host plus a
community of symbionts (keystone §1, line 50). This audit treats that language
as a bounded comparison object, not as an Emergentism ontology. Its membrane
image distinguishes inside from outside, action from non-action, signed from
unsigned, and attests-from-decides.

The cited external anatomy places **PRISM** and **K2** on a boundary rather
than a cognitive ladder. Its four-axis firewall
(`COMPONENT_PROJECTION ⊥ CASTE_DISPATCH ⊥ FUNCTIONAL_RING ⊥ AUTHORITY`) is
tested here as a source-local distinction: a projection on one axis does not
grant a role or authority on another. It neither supplies an Emergentism
authority nor changes any Emergentism source owner.

**Why "deliberately unscored"?** Because scoring the boundary branches would invert the holobiont: a scored PRISM becomes a cognitive rung and would have to participate in Φ/V arithmetic; but PRISM is a rail — recording and verifying receipts is not cognition. A scored K2 becomes a fifth organ and would have to run the soul loop; but K2 is the *signature* — the act of binding a consequence to a natural person, not a step of inference. Scoring the boundary branches is the kind of false-coherence the L1 hostile-input flag was designed to surface.

The membrane is the boundary the host negotiates with the world. It is not a rung of the world the host climbs.

## 2. Cited Mechanics Under Audit

### 2.1 PRISM — the receipt rail

The cited framing keeps **PRISM itself** record-and-verify only; it separately
assigns an exact authorized execution to deterministic software. The exact
wording from `02_SKYZAI/01_NOOSPHERE/02_ORGANS/PRISM/CLAUDE.md:8-13`:

> *"PRISM records and verifies that receipt only. AI/caste seats, agents, organs, and packages stage unsigned proposals only. **Deterministic software executes for the public DAV only the exact consequence bound to a complete valid bound PRISM decision receipt from at least two natural-person councilors.**"*

For the cited external mapping, the claimed reading is that PRISM is a
**pre-launch receipt and verification rail**, not the authority, quorum,
council, signer, actor, officer, or executor. This audit records the source's
claimed distinction; it does not verify a live system or confer authority.

The cited source calls PRISM the receipt rail and verifier of its membrane,
not a step in its soul loop. This is a report of that source's classification.

### 2.2 K2 — the sovereign boundary

The cited 2026-08-01 sources describe K2 as the natural person whose signature
attests specified irreversible or consequential acts. Their **private-DAV**
branch assigns host attestation to its owner's K2 (keystone §4, line 56). The
branch those sources labelled **public-DAV / active on 2026-08-01** reports no
single K2 signer and instead states: *"at least two natural-person councilors
bind each exact consequence in a complete valid PRISM decision receipt"*
(`HOLOBIONT_RATIFICATION_SCOPE_AND_A7_CORRECTION_2026_08_01.md:7`). This audit
does not establish that branch as today's operational state.

The cited amendment packet reports that the **K2-as-fence** role was repealed
and stages three remaining roles — constitutional-amender, public-symbol, and
witness — alongside a proposed Mavis-enforced five-fence roster. The same
synthesis marks the K2/Mavis disposition pending. This audit therefore records
the conflict and its mode split; it adopts neither signer assignment nor an
active authority contract.

**Critical for fidelity to the cited table:** its K2 row is labelled *host
attestation*, not "do this" (assigned there to SKYZAI). Fusing the two defeats
the source-local row split (see `THE_HOLOBIONT_MOODS_BRIEFING.md` §2.2); it
does not settle the pending authority disposition.

### 2.3 Why both are unscored

The 2D matrix (function × substrate) at `HOLOBIONT_RATIFICATION_SCOPE_AND_A7_CORRECTION_2026_08_01.md:§2` and synthesis v0.2 §3.2 places both PRISM and K2 in the **Boundary** row with the explicit annotation "deliberately unscored." The function is *verification* and *sovereignty* — neither is a cognitive rung. The substrates are *rail* (PRISM) and *natural person* (K2). The matrix cell is a *membrane cell*, not a rung cell.

Scoring the boundary branches would mean:
- giving PRISM a Φ or V component in the cited, retired external product score `P = Φ×V` (it has none — it records, it does not produce);
- giving K2 a place in the soul-loop ladder (K2 is the signature, not a step of inference);
- collapsing the four-axis firewall into a single ladder of levels;
- treating the membrane as a rung of the membrane.

The first is type-error. The second is category error. The third is false-coherence. The fourth is parasitic capture laundered as protection.

## 3. Audit Conditions

When auditing the cited external route card (e.g.
`00_HOLOBIONT_SYSTEM_2026_08_01.md` §I, lines 121+), keep its four claimed
axes distinct:

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` | *Which macro-stack level is this entity projected onto?* (interpretive lens) |
| `CASTE_DISPATCH` | *Which castes may this component dispatch?* (Rosetta L1–L7) |
| `FUNCTIONAL_RING` | *Which forward/back edge of the soul loop does this touch?* (F1–F4) |
| `AUTHORITY` | *Who does the cited source report as applicable authority for an irreversible act?* (its private-K2 branch, public natural-person council branch, applicable legal authority, or already-ratified deterministic protocol rule) |

**Audit finding:** the cited four axes are claimed to be orthogonal. A
same-numbered projection on `COMPONENT_PROJECTION` (e.g. PRISM at L4) does not
thereby grant `CASTE_DISPATCH` L4, `FUNCTIONAL_RING` F4, or `AUTHORITY` L4.
The following are source-local cardinal errors:

- **"PRISM decides"** — misreports the cited source, which separates receipt
  verification from the natural-person decision it describes.
- **"K2 decides"** — fuses signing with judgment. The staged source assigns
  K2 attestation, stages a Mavis operational-signing branch, and separately
  marks that disposition pending; this audit does not choose between them.
- **"PRISM signs"** — misreports the cited source, which assigns PRISM record
  verification and describes deterministic execution only after a bound receipt.
- **"The boundary branches are scored rungs"** — fuses membrane with rung. They are not rungs; they are the surface.

The source-local recursion is that a reader may (a) mistake PRISM verification
for signing, (b) treat one of the packet's disputed signer branches as already
settled, or (c) collapse the four-axis firewall. The projection name
`SKYZAI.MACRO_STACK@1` is itself labelled an architectural projection, not a
claim that the named entities intrinsically are Rosetta levels (keystone §I,
line 39).

## 6. Audit Failure Criteria

This audit fails if the cited source claim is misread or if the audit imports it
as Emergentism doctrine or authority. The source-local failure conditions are:

1. **PRISM is treated as a scoring rung.** PRISM is the receipt and verification rail. Scoring it within the cited, retired external `P = Φ×V` score is type-error.
2. **A disputed source branch is presented as settled authority.** The packet
   reports K2/Mavis roles but also marks their disposition pending; this audit
   must carry both facts.
3. **The cited public/private/DAC branches are collapsed.** The audit must report
   the source's different proposed thresholds without adopting, activating, or
   authorizing any of them.
4. **The four-axis firewall is collapsed into a single ladder.** The four axes (`COMPONENT_PROJECTION ⊥ CASTE_DISPATCH ⊥ FUNCTIONAL_RING ⊥ AUTHORITY`) are orthogonal. A same-numbered projection does not grant authority.
5. **The membrane is treated as a rung.** The boundary branches are the surface, not a step. Scoring them is the conflation.
6. **A signature is treated as promoting evidence.** Per the A7 correction at `00_HOLOBIONT_SYSTEM_2026_08_01.md:3-11` and the scope file's §0: a signature is evidence of the act its signer actually performed; it does not manufacture authority outside that act's profile.
