## Abstract

Let `CP1` be the complex projective line and let

```text
sigma : CP1 -> CP1
sigma(z) = 1/z
sigma(0) = infinity
sigma(infinity) = 0.
```

This is a Mobius involution. Its two fixed points are `+1` and `-1`; its
distinguished two-cycle is `{0,infinity}`. On `C*`, the ordinary function
`g(z)=z*sigma(z)` is identically `1`, so `g` has a unique holomorphic extension
`g_bar=1` to all of `CP1`. These are standard facts `[A]`.

The extension value `g_bar(0)=1` is **not** an evaluation of the undefined
binary product `0*infinity`. Writing the extension as the emblem
`1=0*infinity` is a separate, typed convention `[S/I]`. Any attempt to mix that
convention with ordinary multiplication fails associativity. The framework may
therefore keep the emblem only as a symbol for the reciprocal orbit and its
selected positive fixed point.

## 1. Three objects that must not be conflated

### 1.1 Ordinary multiplication

On `C`, multiplication is a binary operation. In the usual extended-complex
conventions, `0*infinity` is left undefined because independent paths with one
factor tending to zero and the other to infinity can have different limits.

### 1.2 Reciprocal inversion

On `CP1`, `sigma(z)=1/z` is a globally defined projective automorphism:

```text
sigma^2 = id
sigma(0) = infinity
sigma(infinity) = 0
Fix(sigma) = {-1,+1}.
```

This `Z2` action is the genuine algebraic skeleton. It exists independently of
Emergentism and independently of any ethical or ontological reading.

### 1.3 The constant extension

On the punctured plane `C*`, define

```text
g(z) = z * sigma(z) = z * (1/z) = 1.
```

Because `g` is identically `1`, both punctures are removable and there is a
unique holomorphic extension

```text
g_bar : CP1 -> CP1
g_bar(z) = 1 for every z.
```

At the endpoints, `g_bar` is defined by extension. It is not computed by first
forming the undefined products `0*infinity` or `infinity*0`.

## 2. The surviving theorem `[A]`

**Theorem 2.3 (reciprocal-orbit extension).** Let `sigma(z)=1/z` on `CP1` and
let `g(z)=z*sigma(z)` on `C*`. Then:

1. `sigma` is an involutive Mobius automorphism;
2. `sigma` exchanges `0` and `infinity`;
3. `sigma` has exactly two fixed points, `+1` and `-1`;
4. `g` is identically `1` on `C*` and extends uniquely to the constant
   holomorphic map `g_bar=1` on `CP1`.

*Proof.* The homogeneous-coordinate map `[a:b] -> [b:a]` is projective and
squares to the identity. Solving `z=1/z` gives `z^2=1`, hence `z=+1` or `z=-1`.
For every nonzero finite `z`, `z(1/z)=1`; the constant function extends across
both isolated punctures. Uniqueness follows from continuity, or from the
identity theorem. QED.

**Perimeter.** The theorem does not define a binary value for
`mu(0,infinity)`, select one of the two fixed points, prove a threefold ontology,
or derive a physical or ethical law.

