## Abstract

As the emergentist framework matures and solidifies its foundational logic, its primary failure mode is no longer only theoretical incoherence, but **operational success**. History suggests that worldviews capable of out-coordinating their rivals often coalesce into institutions, which can decay into extractive egregores.

This paper asks whether a declared constitutional practice can resist
institutional capture at scale. Anti-idolatry is a chosen ethical posture, not a
mathematical closure condition. The proposed `η` stress test can help make
possible extraction visible only after the bearers, baseline, horizon, payer,
beneficiary, consent, and measurement method are declared. A Grace Exit names a
withdrawal and repair design problem; it does not self-authorize a destructive
response.

**Keywords:** Anti-idolatry, institutional capture, η = 0, Grace Exit, Constitutional Science, egregore, scale limits.

## 1. The Peril of Success

The central hypothesis of Paper I and Paper O is that *mediation is the hidden subject* across scale layers.
A mediator (a technology, a bank, a religion, an AI) sits between the substrate and the goal. The pathology of D5 governance occurs when that mediator begins extracting survival energy (`η > 0`) to propagate its own existence at the expense of its substrate. It becomes an **egregore**—an idol.

The emergentist framework is currently a compact candidate coordination grammar. Whether its `Φ` and `V` measures track real institutional performance is an open empirical question; it must not be assumed to out-compete rival architectures.

However, success invites mass participation. Mass participation requires scale. Scale requires standardization and bureaucracy (thickened mediation).

If the framework operates blindly, it risks generating an "Emergentist Priesthood" whose primary directive is maintaining authority as exclusive interpreters of the geometry.

## 2. The η = 0 Stress Test

To examine scale, a framework may test itself against a declared relational
fence. `η=0` is not a free-standing physical reading or a universal numerical
threshold: the test must name its bearers, baseline, horizon, and measurement
method before any result can be reported.

**The Diagnostic Thresholds:**
1. **Resource Velocity:** Does energy or attention flow from contribution to declared purpose, or does an increasing share get captured by administrative overhead within the framework's own institutions and governing loops?
2. **Epistemic Rent:** Do core contributors insist that the framework can only be understood through expensive certifications, private access, or proprietary translation, restricting Pratyakṣa (direct disclosure)?
3. **Identity Coagulation:** Do participants begin identifying *as* "Emergentists" rather than using the framework as a quiet, functional toolset to execute independent external goals?

An observed `η>0` under a declared measurement is a signal for bounded review,
not a diagnosis or automatic disposition. It may reflect a real extraction
pattern, a poor model, a baseline error, or an incomplete bearer account.

## 3. Grace Exit as a bounded withdrawal and repair design

The proposed response to institutional capture is a documented withdrawal,
repair, or dissolution option with legitimate exit preserved.

A true constitutional science must be mortal.
Wittgenstein famously remarked that one must throw away the ladder after
climbing it. The framework may treat that as a reminder to avoid lock-in, not
as an automatic software or institutional command. If a declared review finds
an unremediable extraction pattern, a proposal may compare remediation,
withdrawal, transfer, or dissolution. Any consequence-bearing action still
requires a complete, scoped, contestable `AuthorizationEnvelope`, named
bearers, custody, consent, a lawful execution path, and an independent outcome
record. Detection of `η>0` neither liquidates assets nor terminates keys.

Anti-idolatry asks that the framework remain contestable, replaceable, and
leaveable. Its use or non-use is not evidence that it has succeeded.
