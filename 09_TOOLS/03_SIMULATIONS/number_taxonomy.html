The number sets, organised by what makes what

The schoolbook chart nests boxes — ℕ ⊂ ℤ ⊂ ℚ ⊂ ℝ ⊂ ℂ — and by nesting them it hides three things: that one step is not a generation at all, that 0 and ∞ change status depending on which structure you are in, and that the whole spine has exactly one generator. This chart shows those instead.

Three claims, checked

Two are right once stated precisely. One is right as it stands. One collides with a standing canon rule.

Right

∞ is not a real number. Exactly so. It is a projective point and exists only after an explicitly declared compactification.

Not as stated

0 is a real number — it is the additive identity and sits in ℝ. What is true is sharper: 0 is not a member of the multiplicative structure. It has no inverse, so it is a pole, not an element. Say which structure and the claim becomes exact.

Nearly

1 is not the only natural number — but it is the only generator, and that is the stronger fact. (ℕ⁺, +) is free on one generator: 2, 3, 4 … are multiplicities, not primitives. "The only generator" is exact; "the only natural number" is not.

Fires a rule

"Whole numbers" as a category triggers KSC-21's own fail condition — it explicitly refuses the term as a primitive rung, because {0} ∪ ℕ⁺ adds no structure that ℕ⁺ and ℤ do not already carry. Keep the construction, drop the word.

What the nested-box chart hides. Drawn as boxes inside boxes, every step looks like the same kind of move. It is not. Three steps are constructions from the seed; one is a completion that adds uncountably more than everything before it combined; two are losses — first of order, then of orderability altogether. And 0 and ∞ are not simply "in" or "out": they are the boundary of the multiplicative structure and interior points of the projective one, simultaneously.

Analytic only. This is a chart of constructions, not a claim about what exists.
