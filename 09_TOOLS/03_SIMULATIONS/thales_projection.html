The hinge — which turns out to be our own angle

u = (x−1)/(x+1) equals tan(A − 45°), verified exactly. Suda's hinge is the angle measured from the fixed point instead of from the baseline. Same parameter, shifted origin — which is why its image is the open interval.

Why the limit cannot be crossed from inside. As A → 90° the angle at S → 0° and the ray from N runs parallel to the line: to have an intersection at all you would need the angle at N to be a right angle as well as the one Thales already fixed at P — and a Euclidean triangle admits only one. So the meeting point does not exist in the plane; it exists only after you declare a bigger structure and adjoin ∞. That declaration is the whole crossing — and by the μ-criterion it is a totality repair, not a gain of freedom: the circle and the line have the same dimension before and after.

Analytic only. This construction licenses no ontology, no ethic and no conservation law — it is the reciprocal chart, and the chart is empty of world.
