# SP-03 — offices, not ancestries

## 0 · What this is

A frozen comparison design for the seven-House **candidate** in
[`10_SHARED_PURPOSE_AND_NOOSPHERE_2026_09_16.md`](10_SHARED_PURPOSE_AND_NOOSPHERE_2026_09_16.md)
§8. It does not replace
[`04_BALANCE_OF_HOUSES_TROPHIC_COEVOLUTION_CONTRACT.md`](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE/04_BALANCE_OF_HOUSES_TROPHIC_COEVOLUTION_CONTRACT.md).
It does not recruit people. It does not authorize a political campaign.

**Working principle (owner-accepted 2026-09-16):** specialize capabilities, not
ancestry. Any arm that assigns offices by ancestry, ethnicity, nationality, or
historical varṇa-as-people is **void**, not a data point.

## 1 · Question, so it can lose

Does a **seven-office** arrangement improve coverage and correction over the
**existing four-House-plus-boundary** arrangement, when **all seven functions**,
staffing count, and total resources are held equal?

A win for seven offices is not proof that seven Houses are necessary, hereditary,
or a better morality.

## 2 · Arms

| Arm | What it is |
|---|---|
| A | Four Houses + L1/L7 as boundary occupancies (live contract) |
| B | Seven named offices, one per GEN7 seat |
| C | Generalists: same seven functional *obligations*, no office labels |
| D | Arbitrary grouping of the seven obligations (confound control) |

Hold constant across arms: task set, time, information, compute, training
budget, communication access, dissent/exit rights. If matching is infeasible,
declare the confound; do not treat it as a fair comparison.

**Separate, not mixed into this protocol:** function-ablation (drop one function
at a time). Mixing it here would credit “restoring a missing check” as “seven
Houses win.”

## 3 · Primary measures

1. Coverage — required checks actually performed
2. Correction — planted errors found and repaired
3. Capture — one office blocking contest or exit
4. Overhead — time and tokens spent on coordination rather than the task

A nonsignificant result is not equivalence. Predeclare a margin before any run.
