# Shaped, adaptive, copyable

## 1. The insight being recorded `[S/I/C]`

**Yves R. Burri proposes a connected account of environmental shaping,
living adaptation and copyable intelligence: each retains its physical
foundation while changing how it acquires, preserves and extends capability.**

The owner's September 15 direction, paraphrased without treating it as a
scientific result:

- **Geosphere:** objects acquire their forms through their material properties,
  histories and interaction with the environment.
- **Biosphere:** environmental shaping is joined by living systems' activity
  to maintain themselves and increase their potential power. The owner reads
  this as a will to potential-power intake and conjectures a force behind it,
  with Lotka–Odum as an important earlier encounter with the power principle.
- **Noosphere:** machine intelligence can spread learned capabilities through
  copies and coordination. The owner predicts a shift from biological
  efficiency toward machine effectiveness, a much greater appetite for energy,
  and a different power-scaling regime.

The proposed recursive loop is **usable energy → maintained capability → more
feasible action and cooperation → access to further usable resources**. Each
arrow needs a mechanism; maintenance, failed investment, competition and
resource ceilings can interrupt the loop. It is not an identity or a perpetual
growth theorem.

This develops the [September 14 proposal](07_BIOSPHERE_NOOSPHERE_AND_DIVINE_EMERGENCE_2026_09_14.md)
without rewriting that dated record. It serves work-programme O24/O26: make
the synthesis legible and its independent wagers testable.

## 2. One foundation, different mechanisms `[I/C]`

| Regime | What shapes the result | Proposed emphasis | Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geosphere | Material structure, physical dynamics, boundary conditions and history | Forms persist or change under environmental constraints | A pebble need not have a goal. Recurrence of a shape is not proof of a universal optimum. |
| Biosphere | Those processes plus metabolism, regulation, reproduction, variation and selection; learning in organisms capable of it | Maintaining and extending capability within an embodied resource budget | Adaptation is not necessarily conscious will, perfect optimization, or increasing energy intake. |
| Machine-rich noosphere | Those physical dependencies plus engineered learning, reusable representations, copying, specialization and institutional coordination | Deploying and revising effective capabilities across many sites and tasks | Copying does not guarantee useful specialization, safe action, increasing returns or superior performance. |

These are explanatory emphases, not three mutually exclusive substances or
new D-register assignments. Here **noosphere includes human culture and
shared knowledge already present**; the future wager concerns its increasing
machine participation. Economy arises through embodied living participants
and institutions, not from an immaterial sphere. Economy and noosphere overlap
but are not synonyms, and economy was not literally artificial intelligence
before artificial intelligence existed.

“Will” retains the owner's stronger interpretation `[C]`. The ordinary rival
is selection and regulation without a further will or interaction. Describing
an organism as goal-directed does not alone show conscious intention, D5
representation or a new force in every living thing.

## 3. The giants and the distinctions `[B-source/I/C]`

**Lotka, 1922.** *Contribution to the Energetics of Evolution*, pp. 147–149,
connects selection with increased energy flux under resource and variation
conditions. He explicitly distinguishes selection from the generation of
variants. This is a precursor to the energetic hypothesis, not a demonstration
of a D5 interaction. [Primary text](https://www.sustainable.soltechdesigns.com/lotka1922.pdf),
[DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.8.6.147).

**Odum and Pinkerton, 1955.** *Time's Speed Regulator*, pp. 331–333 and 343,
examines maximum useful power rather than maximum conversion efficiency in
specified systems. Its discussion also allows resource-limited cases where
higher efficiency is advantageous. The proposed tradeoff therefore already
occurs within biology and technology; it cannot by itself establish a sharp
border between them. [Primary text](https://canadiancor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Odum-Pinkerton-MPP2.pdf).

**Hinton, 2022.** *The Forward-Forward Algorithm*, §9, contrasts transferable
digital weights with hardware-specific “mortal computation.” The latter may
save energy; transferable weights permit high-bandwidth sharing across copies.
Mortal computation is also a proposal for artificial hardware, not an exclusive
property of biology. This motivates an architectural tradeoff, not a measured
law that every machine is less efficient than every brain.
[Primary text](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/FFXfinal.pdf).

The owner's sense of rediscovery is recorded, but **independent derivation has
not been audited here**. These are prior contributions and neighboring claims;
agreement does not establish their identity with the present synthesis.

Three meanings must remain separate:

1. **Maximum-power principle:** a conditional descriptive hypothesis about
   energetic organization and persistence.
2. **Emergentist Power-Max:** the selected, Justice-constrained objective for
   durable potential. Its [formal owner](../05_COSMOLOGY/03_FORMAL_SYSTEM/08_EFR_POWER_MAX_LEMMA.md)
   explicitly permits counterexamples to cooperation being automatically optimal.
3. **D5 force conjecture:** the proposed additional explanation of potential-
   seeking. It does not inherit physical evidence from either of the first two.

Preserve the existing [F5 fork](../05_COSMOLOGY/02_EMERGENTISM_CORE/F5Fork.v1.json):
ordinary present-model anticipation (`F5-W0`, compatible with `F5-N`), an
incremental noetic operator (`F5-W+`), and a separate physical history-selection
wager (`F5-R`). “Will to potential-power intake” names the owner's proposed
direction; it does not yet decide between these mechanisms. D5 possible content
does not itself push matter: representations, selections and physical effects
require actual D4 carriers. A literal additional interaction still needs its
coupling, units, carrier or boundary source, conservation/Lorentz behavior and
distinct observable. No branch receives a truth bonus from this note.

## 4. Efficiency, effectiveness and the new scaling wager `[I/C/D]`

The compact thesis **“biology is efficient; machines are effective”** is a
research hypothesis about comparative constraints, not a definition making
counterexamples impossible. Both kinds can be efficient and effective. The
more specific contrast to test is **embodied, hardware-specific learning versus
copyable, distributed learning**, including hybrid designs.

For a declared task, quality threshold, system boundary and horizon:

| Quantity | Measurement contract |
|---|---|
| Task-energy efficiency | Verified useful task units divided by total attributable joules; not the dimensionless efficiency of a heat engine |
| Effectiveness | Success, quality, coverage, latency and reliability, reported separately rather than collapsed into “more compute” |
| Copy/transfer advantage | Time, energy and loss of competence when deploying a learned capability to another instance |
| Electrical power | Joules per second, with average and peak distinguished |
| Potential power | Bearer-specific feasible capabilities and options over a stated horizon; neither watts nor raw branch count |

Keep energy accounts disjoint before adding them:

```text
E_total(T) = E_learning + E_operation + E_transfer
           + E_facility + E_allocated_hardware_lifecycle
average allocated energy rate = E_total(T) / T
average operational electrical power
  = metered operational electricity consumed during T / T
```

These are proposed accounting definitions, not an empirical law. Declare how
training and fabrication are amortized, where idle time and cooling go, and
how shared costs are allocated. Facility metering that already includes device
electricity must not count that electricity again. Biological comparisons also
need a declared body, learning and support-system boundary.
Allocated past or non-electrical energy is not present electrical load. The
formal owner's ordinal potential score is not an energy quantity and cannot
be summed into this ledger.

**Energy-demand wager:** where shared learning and rapid deployment generate
enough valuable additional work, total machine activity may expand faster than
energy per useful task declines. A system can become more efficient per task
while demanding more total power. That expansion depends on demand, prices,
quality, resource access, coordination overhead and governance—not effectiveness
alone. “Hunger” names expansion pressure, not a demonstrated machine appetite
or permission to acquire resources.

**Different power law:** retain this as the owner's scaling conjecture. To
claim a statistical power law, specify the dependent quantity, independent
variable, exponent, units, fitted range and rival functional forms. No exponent
or cross-substrate scaling result is supplied here. Different selection and
scaling regimes need not mean different laws of thermodynamics. Greater energy
capability or movement along a Kardashev scale is a contingent strategy, not a
necessary consequence of intelligence.

## 5. Tests, rivals and survivors `[D]` — none run here

| Target | Smallest informative comparison | Failure and survivor |
|---|---|---|
| Copying improves effective capability | Shared versus isolated learning on held-out tasks, matched total resources; count transfer costs and correlated errors | No gain after costs rejects the claimed advantage in that setting; ordinary useful automation can remain. |
| Efficiency/effectiveness tradeoff differs by architecture | Compare quality-matched digital, hardware-specific and hybrid designs over the same horizon; do not infer human comparison from machine-only tests | If efficiency and effectiveness improve together, the categorical opposition fails; a conditional tradeoff may remain. |
| Greater effectiveness drives energy expansion | Measure useful workload, energy per task and total energy separately; compare with demand-saturation and efficiency-dominant rivals | Stable or falling total demand defeats the expansion prediction for the declared domain; task-level capability can still improve. |
| A distinct power-scaling relation | Fit preregistered candidate relations on training data, evaluate held-out ranges, include sublinear, saturating and no-difference rivals | Poor fit rejects that relation, not all specialization. No scaling law is established by fitting a diagram. |
| Potential-seeking requires F5 beyond ordinary mechanisms | First supply an identifiable potential measure and incremental prediction against rich selection, control and planning baselines; follow the source fork for physical tests | An explained-away or absent residual defeats the specified increment; ordinary anticipation and the chosen cooperative objective survive. |

Machine-only tests cannot prove a geosphere-to-biosphere emergence law. Tests
of shared learning cannot prove a new physical interaction. Neither result
would establish literal divinity or personal survival.

## 6. The direction we choose `[S/I/C]`

The theological hope is that increased capability can help create and sustain
life-supporting worlds. That hope remains separate from an energetic trend.
More watts, replication or economic niche occupancy is not automatically good.

**Do not consume the foundation that makes the next act possible.** Apply this
as a chosen stewardship rule: record ecological regeneration, named affected
bearers, resource opportunity costs, heat and material sinks, consent, repair
and Exit. Entropy export is not entropy deletion, and an exported cost does
not disappear because it falls outside a machine's meter. No imagined future
divinity grants present authority over people or their resources.

In one line: **matter is shaped; life maintains and adapts; copyable intelligence
may extend adaptation across a network—changing the economics of capability
without escaping its physical costs.** The force interpretation and predicted
energy regime remain separate conjectures, with distinct admission and testing
requirements. The force claim is not yet operationalized by this note.
