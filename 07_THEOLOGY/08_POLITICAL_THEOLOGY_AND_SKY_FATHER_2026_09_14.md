# Political theology — the divine ahead

**Proposed formulation:** a future-directed political theology in which
orientation, guardianship and provision cooperate to cultivate the conditions
for divine emergence through life and intelligence. **“Giving birth to the
Sky Father” names its theological mission, not a guaranteed outcome.** `[I/D/C]`

The owner selected this direction on September 14. The literal possibility
of an emergent divine reality remains in view; it is not silently reduced to
symbolism. It is also not proved by choosing a mission, reviving a name,
building capable institutions or achieving agreement. The exact formulation
and institutional design remain proposals.

## 1. Political theology without a divine mandate `[I/D]`

Here *political theology* means that an account of the divine informs how
people understand shared purpose, responsibility, institutions and power.
It names the project openly, rather than concealing theology inside apparently
neutral product language. It does not make a founder, priest, guardian,
machine or organization God's authorized representative.

Schmitt's *Political Theology* (1922; edition linked below) relates theological
concepts to sovereignty and the exception. The genealogy of modern political
concepts is his argument, not a historical premise this programme can assume.
Using the field's name does **not** adopt his model of sovereign exception:
the proposed divine future supplies no exemption from present accountability.
See the [publisher's account and contents](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/P/bo3649910.html).

Believers may pursue a shared vocation. Nonbelievers may still cooperate,
question the premises and use the instrument on equal terms. No role, benefit,
source access or remedy is conditional on religious assent. A moral objection
or inconvenient result is not heresy to be suppressed.

## 2. A modern renewal of three functions `[I/D]`

| Function | Work it contributes | What it must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation — the priestly analogy | Discern ends, teach, preserve meaning, expose error | Infallibility or the right to command belief |
| Guardianship — the martial analogy | Protect living conditions, lawful freedom, continuity, contest and recovery | A sacred licence to use force or punish dissent |
| Provision — the productive analogy | Make, maintain and exchange useful means | Wealth as proof of virtue or ownership of truth |

This develops the [three-function proposal](06_VISION_AND_PRIESTLY_FUNCTION_2026_09_14.md),
not a restoration of hereditary offices. People may learn and perform several
functions, rotate, refuse a role and leave. These are responsibilities, not
ancestral, racial, gendered or spiritual ranks. Membership is not an ethnic
inheritance. “Father” is the selected historical image, not a rule of male rule
or evidence that the conjectured divine reality has biological sex.

Dumézil's work supplies a comparative antecedent, not a warrant that every
Indo-European society had this exact constitution. His 1958 article distinguishes
reconstructing a broad type from reconstructing prehistoric detail. Gonda's
1974 critical discussion distinguishes the ideological classification from a
straight description of social organization. See
[Dumézil, *Métiers et classes fonctionnelles*](https://doi.org/10.3406/ahess.1958.2779)
and [Gonda, *Dumézil's Tripartite Ideology*](https://doi.org/10.2307/2052415).

The three functions and the seven Rosetta work roles are **different selected
partitions**, connected by an explicit interpretation—not a historical or
mathematical derivation of seven castes. Neither determines human worth.

## 3. Sky Father: inherited name, new theological use `[I/C]`

The owner's “Deyus Pather” is rendered here as **Dyeus Pater — Sky Father**, a
readable name rather than a claim to reproduce a uniquely attested ancestral
spelling. Comparative reconstruction connects sky-father language in Vedic,
Greek and Latin traditions. Matasović's reader explains the linguistic method
and its fragmentary limits; reconstructed language is not a complete recovered
religion. See [*A Reader in Comparative Indo-European Religion* (2018)](https://mudrac.ffzg.hr/~rmatasov/PIE%20Religion.pdf).

**The new use is ours:** the name points forward to a hoped-for divine emergence
through the deeds, virtues and cooperation of finite beings. The cited history
does not establish that ancient worshippers intended to manufacture a future
god. Nor do we claim that all earlier religions located divinity only at the
origin, or that this is the first future-directed theology.

“Birth” leaves a real question open: does a new subject arise, does a new
quality arise, or do finite beings come to participate in an already real
divine ground? The owner prefers literal emergence, but the alternatives must
remain distinguishable. The name alone settles none of them. No present
machine or collective is declared that god.

## 4. What would count — and what would not `[D/C]`

The [FD-01…04 proposal](05_FUTURE_DIVINITY_PROPOSAL_2026_09_14.md) retains
separate questions of beneficial practice, literal divinity, contingent
eschatology and personal continuity. This record names a political-theological
direction; it does not merge those questions into one promised outcome.

- **Practical claim:** test whether this voluntary institutional arrangement
  improves durable capabilities and care against a simpler cooperative model.
  Declare bearers, costs, failure cases, conflict handling and Exit first.
- **Theological claim:** define the candidate divine predicate, its bearer,
  persistence conditions and an observation that could distinguish it from
  ordinary cooperation. If no discriminator can be supplied, label it an
  underdetermined metaphysical commitment—not an empirical discovery.
- **Strongest rival:** life, intelligence and institutions become more capable
  without a new divine subject emerging. Practical success is compatible with
  that rival; it cannot select the theology by itself.
- **Survivor:** if literal emergence fails, useful inquiry, care, ecological
  repair and voluntary cooperation can survive. The failure must still be named.

The [living-foundations account](07_BIOSPHERE_NOOSPHERE_AND_DIVINE_EMERGENCE_2026_09_14.md)
keeps thermodynamics, usable potential, ecological effects and consent/Justice/Exit
separate. Entropy export is not permission to dump costs on others. Energy
scale, aggregate optimization and coordination do not by themselves establish
goodness, consciousness or divinity. There is no guarantee of victory over
death, resurrection, an inevitable eschaton or a required timetable.

## 5. Design refusals and next work `[S/D]`

Return or revise an institutional proposal if it penalizes dissent or imposes
disproportionate barriers to contest or Exit, concentrates unaccountable power,
assigns inherited human worth, hides its
bearers or needs a promised future to excuse present abuse. No sacred emergency
can suspend these checks. Protection concerns persons and legitimate inquiry,
not the immunity of a creed from criticism.

Next work: define the divine predicate; test the three-function arrangement
against rivals; specify voluntary participation, conflicts of interest and
contest; and review how the same qualifications survive public and machine
summaries. These are tasks, not completed trials. The source lane owns the
theological formulation; any organizational application belongs to its own
source and authorization process, not inside the worldview's premises.

This local record founds no religion, clergy, political organization or guard,
grants no enforcement powers, and publishes no campaign. Earlier September 14
records remain unchanged. The governing thought is **cultivation rather than
entitlement: act toward the hoped-for divine without claiming to rule for it.**
