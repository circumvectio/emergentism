# Symbol Design and Public Translation

## The Problem

L7's **preserve** reading offers a whole-facing orientation, then returns it to
finite inquiry and action `[I]`. Preserve the ability to understand, cooperate
and revise—not an obligation to keep every explanation. A failed explanation
may leave a useful practice or an independently supported insight; neither
survives automatically.

In the [Rosetta row](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE/38_THE_FULL_ROSETTA_CORRECTED.md),
Pratibhā names a framework-added seventh witness function; this does not
claim a universal pramāṇa taxonomy. L7 offers counsel; it neither
certifies truth nor authorizes action. Translation without discipline becomes:

1. **Reification** — the symbol becomes the thing
2. **Capture** — the symbol gates access to the thing
3. **Hierarchy** — symbol-bearers rank above non-bearers

This document defines the **design rules** for public symbols that carry the
framework's pattern without capturing it, and the **translation protocol**
for moving from doctrine to public image.

---

## §1. The Three Symbolic Layers `[S]`

```
LAYER 3: PUBLIC SYMBOL          (story, image, rite, mark, brand)
         ↕ translates
LAYER 2: STRUCTURAL MAP         (Rosetta, S² geometry, triadic cascade)
         ↓ declares its limit
LAYER 1: GROUND-FACING BOUNDARY (a limit of this account, not possessed Ground)
```

These are selected layers of a reading protocol, not three discovered kinds
of being. The diagram cannot certify access to what the map leaves open.

**Rule 1:** No symbol at Layer 3 may claim to *be* what it points to.
**Rule 2:** No symbol at Layer 3 may *gate access* to what it points to.
**Rule 3:** Anyone may *discard* the symbol at any time. No attainment,
explanation, membership, or demonstration of insight is required.

These three rules are the Stewardship / Coercion boundary made concrete.

## §2. The Master Symbol: ⊙ `[S]`

> **Non-capture guard:** the protocol admits this symbol only while it is
> freely reproducible, ungated, discardable, and self-dissolving. These are

**Chart domain `[A]`, given the selection:** `θ∈(0,π)`,
`φ=cot(θ/2)`, `ν=tan(θ/2)`. Lowercase `φ,ν` are positive reciprocal
coordinates, not measurements of insight or aliases for an agent's powers.
The [formula owner](../05_COSMOLOGY/00_CANONICAL_FORMULA_BLOCK.md) supplies
the full sphere/meridian distinction and the separate finite-agent model.

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Glyph** | `•   ⊙   ○` — the composite emblem; non-operational |
| **Reading** | `•   ⊙   ○` as an emblem of finity between boundaries; not ordinary arithmetic |
| **Geometry** | Selected point-and-circle translation on S² |
| **Position** | Geometric equator `abs(z)=1` (`θ=π/2`); selected positive-radial representative `φ=ν=1`, `B=sin θ=1` |

### What ⊙ Does NOT Mean

- NOT "God is a circle"
- NOT "the self is the center"
- NOT "everything is one" or "nothing matters"—neither follows from the glyphs
- NOT "you must use this symbol to participate"

## §3. Design Rules for Derivative Symbols `[I]`

### Rule D1: Downward Compatibility

Aim for a public symbol that invites attention before the framework is read.
Its aesthetic force is a design hypothesis, not an assumed reader response.

**Reader check:** Ask an unfamiliar reader what the symbol suggests, then
offer its plain-language legend. Can they distinguish the image from the
claim, find the source, and disagree or leave? Record confusion as design
feedback. No particular feeling is required, and feeling *possibility* does
not establish comprehension or truth.

### Rule D2: Anti-Cult Design

| Cult marker | Anti-cult design rule |
|---|---|
| Secret symbols for initiates | All symbols are public. No hidden-layer meaning. |
| Charismatic leader as symbol | No human face in any symbol. ⊙ is geometric, not personal. |
| Escalating revelation behind paywall/gate | Framework is free. Sitting practice is free. No tiered access. |
| Us-vs-them boundary marker | Symbols are offered, never required. Non-participants are equal. |
| Apocalyptic urgency | Do not make a countdown or promised cosmic outcome a reason to accept the symbol. A chart identity predicts neither. |

**Anti-cult kill-test — deprecate the symbol immediately if ANY occurs:** (a) access to the practice requires symbol-knowledge; (b) non-bearers are ranked lower; (c) the symbol becomes an identity / membership marker; (d) exit is gated or penalized. **Watchdog:** any participant may file the trip at the public audit surface (`../11_UPLINK/50_AUDITS_AND_EXECUTIONS/`); a confirmed trip retires the symbol (per Step 5 — revisionable / self-dissolving) with no loss of access to Pratyakṣa, which remains primary and ungated.

### Rule D3: Cultural Permeability

The following are **candidate translation routes `[I]`, not findings about
whole traditions**. Before attributing a fit, name a text or consenting
interlocutor, its native meaning, and what the translation leaves out. A
resemblance does not prove common doctrine, independent convergence, or our
priority. The [Perennial Loop](04_THE_PERENNIAL_LOOP.md) keeps its attributed
crosswalks and irreducible differences visible.

| Audience or source family | Translation question and boundary |
|---|---|
| **Abrahamic** | Could a named account of immanence and transcendence illuminate the image? Do not identify the emblem with God, kenosis, or incarnation; adding “axiomatic” would not establish that identification. |
| **Indic** | Do point/circle images clarify a named source's terms? Do not treat bindu, maṇḍala, emptiness and fullness as interchangeable without the named source's distinctions; symbolic multiplication establishes no theological identity. |
| **Scientific** | Use the explicitly selected sphere, stereographic chart and equator from the formula owner. Recover their mathematics without deriving a worldview or human optimum from it. |
| **Indigenous** | Begin with a particular community's account and permission where needed. Do not assume that oral traditions already carry this pattern or that a shared shape has a shared meaning. |
| **Secular humanist** | Can the image help express a revisable practice without requiring a transcendent commitment? Practical benefit needs a comparison with simpler language; it is not established here. |

### Rule D4: Material Design Constraints

| Constraint | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Two-color maximum** | Public symbols use at most two colors. ⊙ uses black point + black circle on white ground. A local rendering may add at most one palette color; no organization, chapter, or brand owns a variant. |
| **Scalable** | Must render at 16px favicon AND 4m wall installation without losing legibility. |
| **No text** | The symbol must not depend on a specific script or language. ⊙ is glyph, not letter. |
| **No presumed cultural neutrality** | Offer a plain-language or alternate-image version. A simple geometric shape is not evidence of a universal meaning. |

## §4. The Translation Protocol `[S]`

When translating any framework concept from Layer 2 (structural map) to
Layer 3 (public symbol), follow this sequence:

### Step 1: Identify the Structural Feature

What in the Layer 2 map needs public translation? Name its source, domain and
tier before selecting an image. These examples do not replace their owners.

| Example | Structural Feature |
|---|---|
| `φν=1` in §2's open chart `[A]` | Reciprocal coordinates; a lived foresight–means trade-off requires a separate model premise |
| `B=sin θ` in §2's open chart `[A]` | Chart balance peaks at the equator; a real system's optimum need not |
| AuthorizationEnvelope | Consequential action names authorization, actor, custody, and bearer |
| η_move = 0 | Zero extraction |
| Grace Exit | Leave with rightful custody and a contest path |

### Step 2: Find the Affective Core

What *feeling* might the translation invite? These are optional images `[I]`,
not consequences of the formula or required experiences.

| Structural Feature | Affective Core |
|---|---|
| Reciprocal chart | "I can look again from the other side." |
| Chart balance | "I can ask what balance would mean in this particular situation." |
| AuthorizationEnvelope | "The actor, mandate, bearer, and contest path are visible." |
| η_move = 0 | "Who benefits, who pays, and is anyone's loss being hidden?" |
| Grace Exit | "I can put the map down; rightful custody and a contest path remain." |

### Step 3: Find the Image

What single image carries that affective core without explanation?

| Affective Core | Image |
|---|---|
| "Look from the other side" | Two views of one sphere, with the viewpoint marked |
| "Ask what balance means here" | The equator line, labeled as chart balance rather than a universal optimum |
| "Accountability is visible" | A legible record of actor, mandate and affected bearers—an image of accountability, not a signature |
| "Zero extraction" | Open palms, facing up — receiving, not grasping |
| "Door always open" | An open door with light streaming through |

### Step 4: Stress-Test Against Reification

| Question | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Does this image claim to *be* the thing? | No — it points to it |
| Could someone use this image to gate access? | Inspect actual access and exit conditions; reproducibility alone does not prevent gatekeeping |
| Would discarding this image reduce someone's standing or access? | No penalty or required replacement ritual |
| Is the image culturally specific? | Preferably no — if yes, provide alternatives |
| Does the image create a hierarchy of knowers? | No — it is offered, not required |
| Can a reader recover the source's meaning and limits? | The source, domain, tier and omitted remainder are available; contrary evidence is retained |

### Step 5: Publish and Release

The symbol enters Layer 3. It is:

- **Public** — anyone may use it
- **Non-proprietary** — no trademark, service mark, or ownership claim on any symbol in this protocol
- **Revisionable** — if a better symbol emerges, the old one is retired without loss
- **Self-dissolving** — the framework's success is when the symbol is no longer needed

Publication is a separate scoped act, not permission created by completing
these steps. Preserve predecessor custody when revising a published version.

### Witness and return — an optional use `[I]`

**Widen the view; return with a finite next question.** A whole-facing image
can invite attention to relations, possibilities and affected beings beyond
the present narrator. It does not give the narrator their standpoint.

Bring the image to a particular difficulty. Find its source and keep the
source's qualifications beside your interpretation. Distinguish what is
actual from what is represented as possible. Name an available option, its
actual means, affected bearers, and the commitment it would require. Where
action is within scope, choose a bounded step and say what consequence would
change your mind. Otherwise, return a question or proposal—not an invented
permission to act.

After contact, retain the observed result and contrary evidence. Preserve an
insight only to the extent that its own support survives; revise or relinquish
the explanation that failed. This is preservation of a usable instrument,
not preservation of every claim it once carried.

For a reader or machine, an optional note can carry: **source and locator ·
domain and tier · interpretation · omitted remainder · strongest rival ·
next check · what would change the reading · what survives failure**. Use the
[existing claim record](../02_EPISTEMOLOGY/01_EVIDENCE_TIERS/00_THE_HONEST_POSITION.md)
if a claim is to persist; this shorthand is no new schema, truth stamp,
promotion event or authority grant. No note is required to participate or leave.

**Comparative benefit remains untested `[C]`.** A plain question, source and
decision journal may serve as well with less vocabulary. On the same unfamiliar
case, compare source recovery, bearer visibility, a discriminating next check,
and time/burden. If the exercise adds no useful clarity, drop it. If it hides
contrary evidence, suggests privileged access, or makes action or recording
compulsory, withdraw that use and preserve the correction.

## §6. The Anti-Sermon `[I]`

The most important public translation is the one that says: **put this down.**

> You may put this framework down now. Sit quietly if useful, or return to
> ordinary work, company or inquiry. No attainment is required, and nothing
> about your worth depends on keeping the symbol.

Quiet reflection may be offered as a practice; this invitation neither
measures `φ` nor certifies access to Ground. A first-person report can matter
without becoming public proof of its metaphysical interpretation.

The L7↝L1 image names renewal through fresh intake and checking. In applied
dispatch, L7 counsel first returns to L4; any next-cycle L1 intake follows only
within the scoped request. This is not `r₆:D6↝D0`, not a theorem about
experience, and not a self-authorizing cycle. Preserve the opening for
correction, including correction of this guide. These anti-capture commitments
require checking in practice; stating them does not make the framework immune
to capture.
