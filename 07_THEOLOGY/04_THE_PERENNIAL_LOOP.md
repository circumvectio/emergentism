# The Perennial Loop

## Sophia Perennis After Infallibility

This note gathers a pattern already distributed across the corpus. It creates
no eighth owner and proves no perennial philosophy. When it conflicts with a
semantic source owner, the owner rules.

## The compressed insight

```text
Dasein
  -> Finity
  -> situated dasein
  -> identification with a local sign
  -> saṃsāra: the signed cycle
  -> disclosure of non-separation
  -> return to finite action
  -> dharma: full play under the chosen vow
  -> consequence, correction, renewed disclosure
```

The loop is not a ladder out of the world. Disclosure returns the witness to
the same finite field with less attachment and more responsibility. Awakening
that cannot return to consequence is incomplete by this framework's own
measure.

## Dasein, Finity, and the unit

Capital **Dasein** names all that can coherently and consistently exist—anything,
literally, insofar as it can be under a declared world, register, and model.
It is not a container assembled from D4, D5, mathematics, and a few illustrative
beings. The entire selected dimensional articulation occurs within it: D1
distinction, D2 relation and configuration, D3 state assignment, D4 actuality,
and D5 possibility, together with every coherent mathematical, physical,
living, symbolic, computational, and imagined determination. D0 and D6 occur
as articulated boundary tokens without turning the prior Ground into an
ordinary member. The word names the thing in itself in its modal totality
`[I/C]`; membership never makes every possibility actual in this world.

**Finity**—retaining the corpus glyph—names everything determinate between the
boundary roles zero and infinity: every finite being, relation, process,
dimension of freedom, monkey, triangle, and imagined elephant insofar as it is
coherently specified. In the ontology owner's sharper language, Finity is
Dasein under the aspect of determinate finite manifestation. A **situated
dasein** is one finite witness within that whole.

The unit **1** is the normalized seam between the reciprocal poles: at the
selected equator, `φ=ν=1`. One is the unit by which additions and ratios become
legible. In the Emergentist ontological grammar, `1` is the only primitive
natural unit; whole numbers are finite iterations or additions of that unit.
This does not make `1` the sole member of the **conventional** natural-number
set, nor does it put all finite beings at one arithmetic coordinate. It is the
generative unit and reference center of this chart.

The corpus's number-set reclassification is therefore an ontological reading,
not a repeal of standard mathematics. Conventional sets remain available for
calculation. The Emergentist overlay asks what kind of object is present:
unit, iterated unit, signed extension, ratio, irrational generator or
non-terminating computational process, complex transformation, or boundary
role. Thus the reclassification treats irrational values process-first, while
`i` names a complex transformation rather than an irrational process. These
distinctions belong to the mathematical source owners; theology may use them
but cannot certify them.

## The signed cycle and liberation

On the chart, `-x+x=0` is an exact arithmetic symmetry `[A]`. As a picture of
saṃsāra it becomes interpretive `[I]`: gain and loss, victory and defeat, birth
and death exchange signs across a whole that no local life can possess. The
equation never cancels a person's suffering, establishes a karmic ledger, or
licenses passivity.

Liberation is not winning every turn. It is seeing the cycle without reducing
the self to either sign. Līlā names the play seen as play. Dharma names playing
it fully under the chosen constraints: non-extraction, accountable authority,
preserved provenance, Grace Exit, and correction. `φ=ν=1` depicts the selected
balance; it does not derive the Good. The vow supplies the normative force.

## Three disciplined crosswalks

| Tradition | What the crosswalk illuminates | Irreducible remainder |
|---|---|---|
| Bhagavad Gītā | Arjuna receives the terrible whole in the Viśvarūpa and returns to finite duty without attachment to fruit | The Gītā does not teach the Burri chart, `-x+x=0`, or the corpus's definition of Dasein |
| Advaita Vedānta | Ātman–Brahman nonduality illuminates the recognition that deepest self and whole are not separate | Its metaphysics of self and Brahman cannot be silently transferred to Zen or proved by the chart |
| Zen | kenshō/satori illuminates direct disclosure and the release of fixed identification | Zen does not thereby acquire an eternal Ātman doctrine or a modal ontology of all possible worlds |

These are translations, not identities. Krishna, mokṣa, kenshō, and satori
remain source-tradition terms. A good crosswalk preserves what refuses to map.

## The candidate Sophia Perennis

The corpus may contain a **thin perennial grammar**:

1. a whole exceeds the local narrator;
2. a finite witness identifies with a passing part;
3. this identification produces avoidable capture or suffering;
4. disclosure loosens the identification;
5. the witness returns to action rather than escaping consequence;
6. action is tested by what it does to other finite beings;
7. error renews the loop through correction.

At present this is a selected synthesis `[I]` and a comparative conjecture
`[C]`. It becomes a stronger perennial claim only through independent textual
work, preregistered coding, resistant cases, and discriminators against simpler
rival classifications. Recurrence inside one corpus is not independent
convergence.

## Counterexample discipline

The perennial claim weakens or dies if:

- every tradition can be fitted only after its terms are redescribed;
- independent readers cannot reliably apply the proposed grammar;
- held-out traditions require erased differences or new post-hoc categories;
- a simpler family-resemblance account explains the recurrence equally well;
- the ethical conclusion depends on treating `φν=1` as a proof of the Good;
- awakening language excuses suffering, withdrawal, hierarchy, or extraction.

## One breath

> Dasein is the coherent whole; Finity is its determinate showing; the situated
> self suffers when it mistakes one passing sign for the whole, awakens when
> non-separation is disclosed, and completes the insight by returning to the

That is the candidate Sophia Perennis. The candidate remains alive precisely
because it may still be wrong.
