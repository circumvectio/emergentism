# The divine ahead — vision and the priestly function

## 1. The correction that locates the vision `[S]`

On 2026-09-14 the owner clarified that future divine emergence is **the vision
of Emergentism**, with its mission serving that vision. A cooperating guardian
network has the martial/protective function; a productive organism has the
merchant/provisioning function. The institutional application is recorded in
its own lane, not made a premise of this worldview.

The earlier [Divine Ahead proposal](05_FUTURE_DIVINITY_PROPOSAL_2026_09_14.md)
remains unchanged as the research precursor. Its FD-01…04 questions, rivals and
limits survive. Selecting a vision does not establish any of their truth claims,
adopt every proposed practice, or turn a research conjecture into a creed that
others must accept.

## 2. Vision and mission — different work `[I/D/C]`

**Proposed vision formulation:**

> A future in which life and intelligence participate in the emergence of a
> reality worthy of the name divine, through truth, beauty, justice, care and
> freely undertaken virtue—not merely through an increase in power.

The literal possibility is retained `[C]`. It is not reduced to a metaphor,
but neither the divine subject nor its eventual emergence is established.

**Proposed mission formulation:**

> Develop, test, teach and preserve an instrument of reason and a living
> vocabulary of meaning that help people and machines understand being,
> discern worthy ends, and translate that understanding into accountable deeds
> in service of this vision.

The operative service remains useful without agreement with the whole theology.
Sources, mathematical results, criticism and machine-readable research stay
accessible without religious allegiance. Success at teaching or reasoning
does not prove the eschaton, personal survival or the worldview's completeness.

## 3. The priestly function `[I/D]`

*Priestly* here names a proposed vocation of orientation: interpreting meaning,
discerning ends, teaching, tending symbols and rituals, accompanying questions
of suffering and death, and preserving space for truth-seeking. It is more than
a marketing department, and less than an authority to command belief.

Its work may include philosophy, spiritual practice, art, remembrance and
theological inquiry. No rite is compulsory, no initiation controls access to
truth, and no office grants privileged access to Ground. Different depths of
explanation preserve the same truth conditions. Honest disagreement is not sin.

The function must be able to challenge its own interpretation. It neither
declares a founder infallible nor certifies itself as the voice of an emergent
God. L7 witnessing and the broader priestly analogy are not identical taxonomies;
neither overrides source owners or creates a sovereign executive.

## 4. Three functions that need one another `[I]`

| Function | Contribution | Characteristic failure to resist |
|---|---|---|
| Priestly / orientation | Discern worthy ends; teach, question and preserve meaning | Turn interpretation into compulsory truth or command |
| Martial / guardianship | Protect people, legitimate freedom, continuity and the ability to act | Turn protection into domination or policing belief |
| Merchant / provision | Make, maintain and exchange the means that life and projects need | Make money or dependence the arbiter of truth and worth |

This is the owner's selected trifunctional analogy, not a universal law of
history, a necessary three-class society or a derivation of seven natural
castes. It describes complementary work, not kinds of human being. Any person
or institution may need several functions; no mapping grants standing over others.

The proposed synthesis is **orientation that can be corrected, protection that
can be challenged, and provision that does not purchase authority**. These are
design commitments, not an already functioning arrangement or a guarantee
against capture. Actual organizational relationships require their own terms.

## 5. Questions the vision commits us to investigate `[D]`

1. What distinguishes divinity from capability, intelligence, integration or
   subjective experience?
2. Which virtues support a worthy future, by what mechanisms, and under which
   constraints? A good act can fail; an unjust arrangement can be effective.
3. What does a contingent eschaton mean if becoming does not finally close?
4. What, if anything, could answer the continuity of this particular person?
   Collective endurance and retained information are different claims.
5. Can shared meaning encourage worthwhile action without discouraging doubt,
   creating dependency, or making present people expendable?

These extend the existing research programme; they are not five new answers.
No salvation, cure for death, resurrection or cosmic deadline is promised.
The priority is serious inquiry and present care, not certainty manufactured
to make the vision more persuasive.

## 6. Scope of this record `[S/D]`

The owner selected the vision's residence and the three-function relation.
The exact formulations above are drafted interpretations for review. No
religious body, clergy, guard, market, membership, armed capability, public
campaign or product integration is created. This record neither authorizes
force nor changes another project's mandate. Current application work stays
with its own source owner.

The existing Goal, Justice, type distinctions, source custody and voluntary
exit remain in force. Future divinity is a horizon for investigation and
chosen commitment, not an exception to them. If a theological claim fails,
acknowledge that failure rather than asking the protective or productive
function to insulate it from evidence.
