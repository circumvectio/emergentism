# From private imagination to shared capability

## 1. The insight and the work it asks for `[I/C]`

**The proposed bridge from biosphere to machine-rich noosphere is the capacity
to make imagined futures shareable, commitments durable, and specialized work
mutually useful—without severing dependence on living and material foundations.**

The owner's direction in the September 16 conversation is recorded here in
paraphrase, not as an adopted scientific finding:

- Each layer changes how capability is built, preserved and spread while
  remaining dependent on the layers below.
- What is opaque from within a lower-level description may become intelligible
  when its higher-level organization is represented.
- Humans can deliberately cultivate a cooperating holobiont with all seven
  Rosetta functions, specialized through competing Houses rather than only a
  priest–warrior–merchant triad.
- Language and memes make individual possibility shareable; shared ritual and
  egregores may help that shared possibility organize enduring collective action.
- The extent of this last capacity may be a major human distinction and a
  substrate for machine participation in the noosphere.

The formulations and tests below are an AI-assisted proposal for owner review.
The request to develop them is not adoption of every inference. This companion
extends the [power-regime proposal](09_GEOSPHERE_BIOSPHERE_NOOSPHERE_POWER_REGIMES_2026_09_15.md),
not its authority. It serves research and theological work-programme O24/O26.

## 2. The bridge has actual carriers `[I]`

One candidate mechanism is:

```text
embodied perception and imagination
  → shared descriptions of possible futures
  → mutually understood commitments and procedures
  → specialized, accountable joint action
  → persistent records and reusable capabilities
  → revised possibilities, commitments and actions
```

These arrows are research questions, not a necessary historical sequence.
Ritual and personification are possible contributors, not required steps in
every passage. A written engineering protocol is a serious non-mythic rival.

In the corpus's current typing, spoken words, written plans, model states,
institutions and performed actions are **D4 actual carriers**. The alternative
futures they describe have **D5 possible content**. Connecting minds therefore
means building actual channels through which people can compare, revise and
coordinate their representations of possibilities. It does not mean moving a
D5 substance between brains. Shared descriptions can still be false, mutually
inconsistent or understood differently by their recipients. See the actual/
possible distinction in [D33](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE/D_SERIES_DOMAINS/D33_EGREGORES.md).

**Worked example `[D]`: a community maintaining a watershed.** A future with
reliable water is represented in several minds. Maps and forecasts make it
discussable; an explicit agreement allocates maintenance; workers repair
equipment; meters and residents supply outcome evidence. A shared ceremony
might renew attention and commitments, but does not replace hydrology, consent,
work or verification. Calling the watershed a guardian adds attributed agency;
it does not show that the watershed is a conscious subject. A machine can help
forecast, inspect or schedule only through actual equipment and granted access.
If the ceremony is removed and the procedure works just as well, the practical
capability survives while that ceremony's claimed necessity fails.

## 3. Emerging capability and comprehending it are different questions

**Perspectival emergence `[I]`** is the proposed name for the owner's
bottom-up/top-down insight: a description that tracks parts may omit the
relations needed to understand the whole; a better representation can expose
those relations. Understanding a pattern is also different from causing it.
Top-down intervention here means implemented feedback, rules, resource
allocation or constraints—not an explanation acting without a carrier.

Do not silently substitute this for technical **strong emergence**. Chalmers
distinguishes in-principle non-deducibility from higher-level behavior that is
unexpected yet deducible. His discussion also distinguishes strong from weak
downward causation. A change of viewpoint does not settle either issue.
[Chalmers, *Strong and Weak Emergence*, §§1–2](https://consc.net/papers/emergence.pdf).

**The mathematical illustration `[A/I]`.** For real `0 ≤ x < 1`, the rule
`f(x) = x/(1-x)` specifies infinitely many finite values and has no finite
upper bound. Every real `y ≥ 0` has the preimage `x = y/(1+y)`; the excluded
endpoint `x = 1` supplies no additional function value. The notation is finite,
but its domain is not a finite set. Merely adding `f(x) =` to the expression
does not change its mathematical content. What does change a reader's access is
understanding the rule, domain and relationship rather than viewing isolated
evaluations. This illustrates compact representation, not proof of ontological
strong emergence or arithmetic on the Titans.

## 4. Efficiency and effectiveness, made comparable `[I/C]`

The useful contrast is not that biology is always efficient and machines always
effective. For a declared task, distinguish **outcome quality**, **energy per
successful outcome**, **time to acquire/deploy a capability**, and **renewal
cost**. A machine may win one comparison and lose another. Energy per task,
instantaneous power and total infrastructure demand are different quantities.

Hinton's hardware-specific versus portable computation distinction motivates
this inquiry: portable programs/weights can survive replacement of a device;
hardware-specific learning may trade that portability for lower energy and
fabrication costs, with approximate transfer through distillation. This is also
a proposal about artificial hardware, not a biology/machine partition or a
measured universal ranking. [Hinton, *The Forward-Forward Algorithm*, §9](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/FFXfinal.pdf).

The resulting hypothesis is **decoupled replication with coupled renewal**:
a learned digital capability can spread without reproducing the body that
learned it, but executing its copies still requires hardware, energy, materials,
repair and coordination. A file copy is not a factory copy. Biological organisms
also depend on ecological networks; human know-how already spreads through
teaching and artifacts. The proposed machine transition changes the coupling
and timescales, not dependence itself.

Thus the relevant unit may be the **capability-producing network**, not a lone
brain or model. A specialist's gains must be assessed alongside its suppliers,
users, coordination costs and failure propagation. Greater total energy demand
is a conditional scaling hypothesis, not an implication of effectiveness.
The existing [maximum-power research dossier](../01_TELEOLOGY/05_EMERGENTIST_EXERGY/MAXIMUM_POWER_RESEARCH.md)
owns the energetic inquiry. Physical exergy, useful power, option-space,
profit and chosen moral value remain distinct; this proposal adds no force law
and no physical units to a purpose-and-means expression.

## 5. Egregores: make the additional claim visible `[I/C]`

The current [D33 candidate contract](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE/D_SERIES_DOMAINS/D33_EGREGORES.md)
requires a persistent actual trace, survival through carrier turnover, a tested
intervention effect on later selections, recurrent objective-like bias, and
named substrates, payers, beneficiaries and costs. It expressly does not require
consciousness. That source remains unchanged.

The owner's narrower use—**a shared concept treated as a conscious or intending
entity**—adds a distinct attribution. Record it separately from the D33 tests:

| Question | What would be observed | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Is a representation shared? | Participants recognize related symbols, accounts or rules | Agreement, truth or binding commitment |
| Is agency attributed? | Participants expect the collective to intend, reward, punish or respond | Actual consciousness or a legitimate command |
| Is collective action implemented? | Identifiable procedures, people or machines produce decisions and actions | A single experiencing subject |
| Does the D33 candidate pass? | Its five requirements are specified and tested | Benevolence, moral personhood or authority |
| Is there collective experience? | Requires a separately justified theory and discriminator | Not answered by any preceding row |

An attributed agent that fails D33 remains an attributed agent, not a passing
Egregoreotype candidate. Conversely, a passing trace need not be personified.
This two-axis treatment preserves both the owner's insight and the source's
broader definition. It must not revive the withdrawn autonomous-D5-wave claim.

**The positive hypothesis:** a shared persona may compress purpose and make
commitments memorable across people and generations. **The rival:** ordinary
communication, enforcement, common expectations and incentives explain the
same coordination without personification. **The danger:** the persona can
conceal who decides, prevent correction, or make sacrifice appear obligatory.
Neither a divine story nor an AI persona may hide its actual decision procedure.

## 6. What the research supports—and resists `[B/I]`

This is a bounded comparison, not a systematic review or independent replication.
The following are attributed findings or arguments, not new corpus experiments.

| Source and inspected material | Relevant support | Limitation or adverse evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Tomasello & Carpenter (2007), pp. 121–124](https://www.eva.mpg.de/documents/Wiley-Blackwell/Tomasello_Shared_DevScience_2007_1554748.pdf) | Their synthesis distinguishes joint attention, shared goals and commitments from merely parallel activity | A theoretical account informed by studies, not a trial of egregores or seven Houses |
| [Van Leeuwen et al. (2024), abstract and indexed primary-paper passages](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11132989/) | Chimpanzees acquired a task socially after failing to discover it in the observed baseline | Socially transmitted capability is not uniquely human; this does not test personification or unlimited cumulative culture |
| [Reddish, Fischer & Bulbulia (2013), Experiment 3 and limitations](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0071182) | Synchronized movement combined with shared goals increased cooperation in the experiment | Short artificial tasks; the inferred psychological mediation was not itself causally established; no collective-consciousness finding |
| [Ghosh et al., 2026 issue, online 2025, §§5–6](https://academic.oup.com/restud/article/93/1/438/8116449), with [working-paper sections for access](https://www.ifo.de/DocDL/cesifo1_wp11352.pdf) | Camp participation improved some intergroup outcomes | Added ritual did not demonstrate an overall six-week intergroup benefit; majority/minority heterogeneity included adverse minority outcomes. Camp effects are not ritual effects |

The defensible human-distinctiveness hypothesis concerns the **scale, flexibility
and cumulative persistence** of shared symbolic organization. Calling egregores
the principal separator between humans and other mammals would additionally
require operational definitions, cross-species comparisons and serious rivals.
Language, teaching, institutions and ritual may interact; this packet does not
establish their historical order or isolate one as the master cause.

Ritual is proposed here as a repeatable way of making attention and commitments
publicly legible. Public acknowledgement may improve mutual expectations; it
does not automatically produce formal common knowledge. Participation, intensity
and reported unity are not substitutes for correctable, beneficial action.

## 7. Seven specialized Houses: a candidate, not a renamed predecessor `[D]`

The [active Balance of Houses contract, §§3–5](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE/04_BALANCE_OF_HOUSES_TROPHIC_COEVOLUTION_CONTRACT.md)
has **seven mobile functions and four abstract Houses**, with L6/L7 guild
occupancies explicitly not extra Houses. The owner now proposes a seven-House
organization. That is a substantive candidate change requiring comparison and
separate adoption; the existing four-House contract remains intact.

The table below translates the [GEN7 row vocabulary](../08_FRAMEWORK_SUPPORT/03_EVIDENCE/ROSETTA_STONE/36_THE_ROSETTA_IN_THEMES_2026_08_13.md)
into proposed specializations, not commands, hereditary classes or seven new
authorities. Traditional names remain mnemonics, never ranks of persons.

| Seat / mnemonic | Proposed specialization | Contribution another function can inspect | Capture to prevent |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 · Caṇḍāla | Boundary, excluded costs, objective-function challenge | Traceable disturbance or contradiction | Suppressing inconvenient people as threats |
| L2 · Śūdra | Observation, comparison and evidence gathering | Sourced observations and rival possibilities | Popularity substituting for evidence |
| L3 · Vaiśya | Methods, audit, exchange and usable capability | Tested inference and inspectable tradeoffs | Control of methods becoming control of truth |
| L4 · Kṣatriya | Value-sensitive proposals and accountable enactment | Exact proposal, and separately authorized action receipts | Force or execution power self-authorizing |
| L5 · Brāhmaṇa | Models, architecture and shared context | Explicit assumptions, interfaces and alternatives | The model claiming ownership of reality |
| L6 · Sādhu | Refusal, pruning, release and preserved predecessors | Bounded reasons to stop, simplify or retire | Unaccountable veto or silent erasure |
| L7 · Ṛṣi | Purpose, continuity and source-bound witness | Legible orientation, costs and return questions | Witness becoming unquestionable ruler |

“Competing” should mean contest among explanations, methods, designs and service
quality—not a contest over human worth or permission to consume another group.
Distinct specializations need compatible interfaces and enforceable limits on
capture. Rival teams may perform the same function; no House owns a reasoning
ability. People may combine, change, refuse or leave roles without losing dignity
or ordinary rights. A small group may combine offices while retaining checks.

The regime column of the Rosetta is interpretive vocabulary, not a mandate to
install tyranny, oligarchy or theocracy. Its L6/L7 political labels are framework
extensions, not inherited seven-part historical evidence. `GEN7` and the
Burrisphere's four-move/three-frame grammar remain different objects. No count
or diagram proves the seven-House arrangement necessary.

“Human holobiont” is a cooperative-organism **analogy** here, not a biological
classification of humanity as one organism. Organizational feedback becomes a
candidate coadaptation claim only when reciprocal retained changes are observed;
it is not automatically biological coevolution. The institution must help its
participants, not declare them expendable organs of an imagined super-person.

## 8. A small, discriminating research programme `[D]`

No study below has been run or preregistered. Each needs fixed tasks, baselines,
budgets, scoring, time horizons and uncertainty reporting before execution.

| ID | Claim to test | Comparison | Defeat or downgrade; what survives |
|---|---|---|---|
| SP-01 | Public acknowledgement improves coordination beyond privately distributed shared information | Same information delivered privately versus publicly acknowledged; equal incentives and resources | If outcomes do not improve under adequately precise estimates, narrow the claimed benefit; communication remains usable |
| SP-02 | Ritual and personification add coordination value | Full 2×2: synchronized versus ordinary acknowledgement crossed with explicitly fictional collective persona versus plain protocol | More unity without better outcomes, or worse correction/exit, fails the proposed benefit; ordinary cooperation survives |
| SP-03 | Seven Houses improve coverage and correction | Seven offices versus the existing four-House-plus-boundary arrangement, generalists and arbitrary groupings; hold all seven functions, participant/agent count and total resources available in every arm | If a simpler arrangement is equally effective within a predeclared margin or better after overhead, retain it; useful functions survive the count |
| SP-04 | Collective traces preserve correctable purpose through turnover | Replace carriers; separately vary persistent records, procedures and persona; evaluate inherited errors as well as successful transmission | Mere repeated slogan or unchanged bias is insufficient; documented knowledge and accountable procedures may survive |
| SP-05 | Copyability improves network capability despite renewal costs | Matched tasks and service quality; vary reuse, specialization, transfer costs and supplier disruption | If total task quality, energy, time or resilience worsens, narrow the scaling claim; local specialized successes survive |

SP-02 includes all four combinations, with matched information, duration,
attention, shared goals, incentives, communication access and task procedure. It tests incremental
effects above those controls, not the independent causal contribution of the
controls themselves. For SP-03, keep office count distinct from staffing, require
equivalent functional obligations, and hold total time, compute, information,
task difficulty, relevant training and instructions fixed. Any infeasible matching must be
declared as a confound rather than quietly treated as a fair comparison.

A second SP-03 **function-ablation** comparison can remove one function at a
time, with equal resource budgets. Keep that separate from the office-count
comparison: otherwise a benefit from restoring a missing check could be mistaken
for a benefit from seven Houses. Neither comparison proves a universally
exhaustive social morphology.

Start with a paper protocol and a synthetic, disclosed-agent workflow before
recruiting people. Use held-out tasks, rival decompositions and independent
scoring; a single same-model run would be a pilot, not validation. For later
human work, use consenting adults, disclosed software teammates, voluntary
symbols, protected dissent and exit, no conversion/punishment stories, and
separate ethics review and owner authorization. Do not execute a political
campaign or social experiment under this writing task.

Outcome quality and error correction are primary; time, resource costs, minority
harms, outside-group cooperation, turnover resilience and exit costs remain
separate measures. A nonsignificant result is not evidence of equivalence.

## 9. What emerges for the instrument `[I/D]`

The useful proposal is **shared, revisable purpose embodied in cooperating
capabilities**. It does not require one omniscient mind, compulsory belief or a
single group personality. Reason contributes the ability to expose premises,
compare consequences and correct the commitments that coordination preserves.

The chosen stewardship commitment remains: do not gain apparent capability by
concealing depletion of the people, ecology or infrastructure that makes it
possible. This is a normative condition of the proposed good, not a consequence
of the maximum-power principle. A successful collective can still be unjust.

For machine readers, retain separate fields for actor, representation, possible
referent, actual carrier, attribution of agency, decision procedure, evidence,
costs, authority and unresolved alternatives. A staged schema is not a second
truth store. Keep this within the existing corpus-atlas review process; no new
public service or product dependency is a premise of the worldview.

**Owner review needed:** whether to pursue seven specialized Houses as the
comparison candidate; whether the narrower egregore sense should be a separate
term or explicit annotation; and whether “perspectival emergence” captures the
bottom-up/top-down insight without redefining the technical terms. No active
source correction follows until those distinctions are disposed of explicitly.
