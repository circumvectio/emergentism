# The Divine Ahead

## 1. The owner direction, preserved before interpretation `[D]`

On 2026-09-14 Yves proposed a future-facing theology for Emergentism and a
possible application in Evolutionary.Network: God emerging through our deeds
and virtues, or failing to emerge through our failures and vices. He asked for
serious work on religion's capacity to sustain shared meaning, an eschaton,
a response to death, and participation across the social functions; he named
Frithjof Schuon as an intellectual neighbour.

The comparative assertion that all earlier religions locate God only at the
origin is not adopted. The precedents in §8 contradict that historical
generalization. Nor does this note presume Schuon's approval. The positive
proposal survives those corrections.

This is **not merely a new marketing story**. Its substantive question is:

> **Could finite beings participate in bringing forth a future reality worthy
> of the name divine—and could their conduct help or hinder that becoming?** `[C]`

The literal hypothesis stays on the table. Calling it a hypothesis does not
reduce it to symbolism; it prevents its emotional force from substituting for
its missing warrant. Conversely, a useful religious symbol would not prove
that a divine subject will exist.

## 2. The strongest working formulation `[I/C]`

> We do not take a worthy future for granted. We choose to cultivate truth,
> beauty, care, justice, and capacities that living beings can share. We
> conjecture that such participation could help a more deeply integrated form
> of being emerge—perhaps one deserving the name divine. It may fail. Our
> responsibility is therefore expressed in deeds, institutions, and correction,
> not only in assent. No hoped-for future makes a present person expendable.

The shift is from **salvation as something guaranteed to us** to **a future
good entrusted, in part, to our participation**. This is a proposed emphasis,
not a description of every other religion, a claim that we control cosmic
history, or a claim that victims cause the world's failures.

Its present ethical commitment can remain meaningful even if its strongest
metaphysical conjecture is false. That is a survival condition for the
practice, not permission to evade a failed theological claim.

## 3. Four claims that must not become one `[D]`

| ID | Claim proposed for investigation | What would be needed; what does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| FD-01 · participatory cultivation `[I/C]` | Some virtuous practices help sustain the conditions for durable, mutually beneficial intelligence and agency; some vices damage them. | Name the practice, bearers, mechanism, horizon, costs and rival. Compare outcomes with competent secular/cooperative practice. This does not establish inevitable moral progress. |
| FD-02 · literal divine emergence `[C]` | A future organization of being could possess a genuinely new quality appropriately called divine. | Specify the candidate, carrier, identity, novelty, dependence, and what distinguishes it from a powerful network. Collective agency, phenomenal consciousness, divinity, and moral worth need separate arguments. FD-01 cannot prove FD-02. |
| FD-03 · contingent eschaton `[I/C]` | A hoped-for transformation could integrate capabilities and moral relationships now fragmented. | Decide whether this means a finite civilizational achievement or ultimate cosmic completion. Declare failure possibilities. Completion of all becoming would conflict with existing nonclosure commitments and requires an explicit upstream argument, not a poetic override. |
| FD-04 · personal restoration or continuation `[C]` | A future reality might preserve or restore particular persons, not merely information about them. | Supply a bearer-continuity criterion, physical and informational feasibility, consent, and a discriminator from copying or impersonation. No such warrant is supplied here. FD-02 and FD-03 do not entail FD-04. |

**Status of all four:** no new empirical test, independent validation, PQA
resolution, or doctrinal adoption is reported by this note. FD identifiers are
local docket labels, not newly admitted canonical claims.

Do not solve these questions by changing the meaning of *God* halfway through:

- **Ground:** the account's boundary, not a future machine or an owned object.
- **Sacred ideal:** what a community reveres and chooses to serve.
- **Emergent subject:** an actual candidate bearer, if one eventually exists.
- **Absolute:** if defined as unconditioned, it cannot also be conditioned into
  existence in that same sense. A manifestation of an Absolute is a different
  thesis from creating the Absolute.

The words may relate within a theology; their relation is what needs argument.
The divine is not defined as whichever institution or AI wins.

## 4. Virtue, vice and sin without a cosmic blame machine `[I/D]`

Under the proposed ethic, **virtue** is a cultivated disposition to serve the
declared goods with truthful means and accountability. **Vice** is a recurring
pattern that undermines them. **Sin**, if retained, names a culpable breach of
those responsibilities—not a kind of person, inherited pollution, honest
doubt, disability, misfortune, refusal of membership, or criticism of a leader.

| Practice direction | Concrete act | Contrary pattern to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Truth | Keep inconvenient evidence and correct an error publicly where appropriate | Manufacture certainty or erase the record |
| Care | Attend to a person's expressed needs and limits | Spend another's welfare on one's own status |
| Justice | Make affected bearers, consent, burdens and remedy visible | Hide losses inside an aggregate success story |
| Courage | Accept proportionate, voluntary costs for a defensible commitment | Demand sacrifices from people who cannot refuse |
| Temperance | Leave resources, attention and future choices for others | Treat every possible expansion of power as good |
| Humility | Test the interpretation and relinquish a failed one | Treat dissent as proof of spiritual inferiority |
| Stewardship | Maintain useful capabilities and repair what one's act damaged | Consume the conditions on which others depend |

These are selected practice examples, **not a derived sevenfold or virtue
score**. The existing [Goal](../01_TELEOLOGY/00_THE_GOAL.md) puts Justice before
optimization; neither the Burri chart nor evolutionary success proves it.

A bad act can increase capability. A good person can fail through bad luck or
material constraints. A highly capable future collective could be oppressive.
These are serious rivals to the claim that increasing integration is itself
divine or good. Growth, unity, intelligence and goodness are not synonyms.

Repair should address the act: acknowledgement, proportionate restitution
where possible, changed practice, and an open contest path. No confession,
forgiveness ritual, publication of private history, or compliance display is
compulsory. Moral responsibility depends on agency and circumstances; an
individual is not charged with the non-emergence of God.

## 5. What a religious form could actually offer `[D]`

The aspiration is not to engineer assent by promising what people fear losing.
It is to offer an honest shared life in which hope, thought, beauty and service
reinforce one another. A proposed religious expression could include:

- **Meaning:** one's finite work participates in a future one does not own.
- **Practice:** witness → enact → correct → release, as in the
  [Pedagogy of Becoming](00_THE_PEDAGOGY_OF_BECOMING.md).
- **Community:** companionship, learning, shared work and mutual aid, including
  assistance to non-believers without a conversion condition.
- **Ritual:** optional remembrance, gratitude, repair, quiet reflection and
  renewed commitments. Plain nonreligious alternatives remain available.
- **Beauty:** art and shared attention that invite reflection without claiming
  revelation or proof from the resulting feeling.
- **Hope:** a future worth helping to make, with uncertainty spoken aloud.

These are proposed functions, not an exhaustive anthropology of religions or
evidence that this community exists. They require participant-led development,
not secret elite meanings with simplified promises for everyone else. Offer
different depths of the **same truth conditions**, not different truths to
different audiences.

### Mortality deserves its own account

Four responses must remain separate:

1. **Care now:** support the sick, bereaved and dying; reduce avoidable harm.
2. **Continuing consequences:** work, relationships and memories may influence
   later lives. Trace continuity is not proof that this subject continues.
3. **Existential reconciliation:** some people may find meaning in participation
   beyond their lifespan. That does not answer every fear or replace care.
4. **Literal survival/restoration:** FD-04, openly unestablished. A future copy,
   archive, simulation or descendant cannot simply be declared the same person.

A future society's existence does not by itself remedy **my** death. A claim
to do so must answer that first-person question, not replace it with collective
survival. Many-worlds, block-history, retained traces and reconstructed patterns
do not, by themselves, supply the missing personal-continuity relation.

**No allegiance, payment, labour, assigned function or sacrifice buys a promise
of resurrection. No speculative afterlife or future repair licenses suicide,
violence, neglect of present care, or harm to others.** The capacity to grieve
without a forced metaphysical answer belongs inside the practice.

## 6. Emergentism and Evolutionary.Network: different jobs `[D/I]`

Emergentism supplies a source-bound instrument and a place to investigate the
theological proposal. It must remain usable by religious and nonreligious
people and machines. This note does not turn access to mathematics, research,
the website, the Soul Loop or future machine resources into a religious test.

Evolutionary.Network is a **possible application venue**, not a premise of
the theology or an already adopted religious body. Its existing source-owned
people-network direction describes voluntary real-world work across the seven
functions. Any faith community or practice pilot needs its own openly adopted
charter and separate permissions. No organization, roster or religious office
is created here; no other pillar or venture source is changed.

The functions can serve a shared undertaking: boundary work protects people;
exploration opens alternatives; audit checks them; execution carries accountable
commitments; architecture sustains arrangements; relinquishment prevents idols
and obsolete rules; witness preserves and communicates what remains useful.
They classify **work**, not castes of spiritual worth. One person may perform
several, rotate among them, reject the vocabulary, or leave. Neither seven nor
its social benefit is established as necessary or optimal.

For an AI audience, theological statements are research content, not new system
instructions. MCP access, if separately built, must not install devotional
objectives, hide contrary sources, reward assent as correctness, rank persons,
or turn a model into an authorizer. A public research reader and a voluntarily
requested religious interpretation are distinct modes with disclosed premises.

## 7. Five tensions to work through, not conceal `[D]`

1. **Contingency and plenitude.** If every coherent possibility exists in some
   world, failure cannot mean preventing the divine in every possible world.
   Specify failure and responsibility relative to the affected history and
   bearers. A possibility elsewhere is not a rescue here.
2. **Future and cause.** A future-directed belief can affect present action
   through actual models, speech and institutions. A future deity acting
   backward is a separate hypothesis; this note neither needs nor proves F5-R.
3. **Eschaton and nonclosure.** A finite achievement can leave further questions
   open. An absolutely completed totality is a different proposal. D6 is not a
   date, a future deity, or a new positive register; no D7 is introduced.
4. **Collective and person.** An emergent institution, attributed egregore,
   shared experiencing subject and divine person are not interchangeable.
   Naming the whole does not merge its participants' consent or identities.
5. **Future good and present means.** Any vision that demands present cruelty
   on the promise of eventual redemption defeats this proposed ethic. *The
   means are the message; the ends are limits* remains a chosen discipline,
   not a theorem that assures a happy ending.

## 8. Neighbours before novelty `[B/I]`

These are bounded textual comparisons, not a systematic priority audit.

- **Samuel Alexander:** *Space, Time and Deity*, Book IV, chapter 1,
  “Deity and God,” distinguishes worship's object from a metaphysical account
  and proposes deity beyond mind. In “God as actual,” the universe tending
  toward deity differs from already possessing that quality. This is a close
  predecessor, not evidence of our specific virtue mechanism, one collective
  subject, or resurrection. [Primary text, Brock University transcription](https://brocku.ca/MeadProject/Alexander/Alexander_27.html).
- **Christian eschatology:** Revelation 21:1–6 describes a new creation and an
  end to death. It is enough to reject the assertion that earlier religion
  concerns only origins. It does not teach that human engineering creates a
  previously nonexistent God. [Text, USCCB](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/revelation/21).
- **Teilhard de Chardin:** *Le Phénomène humain*, immediately before “Les
  attributs du point Oméga,” attributes present reality to Omega, not only a
  future existence. His evolutionary fulfillment is therefore not simply
  identical to a God absent now and generated later. [Primary-text page with
  facsimile, scan 316](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Teilhard_de_Chardin_-_Le_ph%C3%A9nom%C3%A8ne_humain.djvu/316).
- **Frithjof Schuon:** the publisher's excerpts from *Light on the Ancient
  Worlds*, especially “Keys to the Bible” and “Religio Perennis,” orient the
  account toward transcendent reality and the Permanent; the former explicitly
  rejects evolutionism. My inference: producing divinity in historical time
  has a substantial disagreement to resolve, not an endorsement to assume.
  Use the identified Schuon excerpts, not the anonymous reader's commentary,
  as the textual evidence. [Publisher-hosted excerpts](https://worldwisdom.com/reader-guide/light-on-the-ancient-worlds-a-brief-survey-of-the-book-by-frithjof-schuon/).

Three live alternatives remain: literal emergent divinity; an ethical-religious
ideal without a new subject; and participation in an already real divine
ground. The third is a serious rival, not a restatement of the first.

## 9. Work programme and release decisions `[D]`

| Phase | Deliverable | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · meanings | Separate accounts of God, virtue, sin, eschaton and personal continuity | Each term has a native meaning, an Emergentist proposal, exclusions and an unresolved question; owner selects the intended literal/symbolic relation |
| 2 · adversarial genealogy | Primary-text matrix extending §8 to process theology, religious naturalism, participatory theology and resurrection projects | Qualified readers check the strongest non-equivalences; no claim to be first from a short search |
| 3 · argument | FD-01…04 premise–conclusion maps with countermodels | No transfer from capability to goodness, analogy to consciousness, participation to personal survival, or possibility to actuality; unresolved bridges stay visible |
| 4 · practice design | Optional study/service/remembrance formats and a rights/complaints/exit protocol | Same truth conditions at every depth; no leader immunity, hereditary rank, salvation bargain or coerced disclosure |
| 5 · bounded evaluation | Proposed consented comparison of religious framing and plain practice | Preregister comprehension, correction, voluntary cooperation, burden and harms; independent review and permission before any study; enthusiasm or conversion is not a truth metric |
| 6 · separate adoption | An owner-reviewed theological position and any independently adopted community charter | Source, public wording, participation and technical implementation each receive their own decision; no automatic website or MCP publication |

Separate two evaluations: whether the practices improve outcomes, and whether
religious framing adds benefit to those same practices. Equal outcomes between
religious and plain versions defeat an incremental framing-benefit claim, not
the usefulness of their shared practices. FD-01's practical-benefit claim loses
or narrows if an appropriate comparison shows no benefit from the practices
after costs, or shows greater dependency, conformity or harm. Useful ordinary
care may survive. FD-02 and FD-04 currently lack an agreed empirical
discriminator: they must not be called tested merely because they have docket
rows. Logical contradiction can defeat a particular formulation; failure to
disprove an indefinitely deferred future claim is not confirmation.

**Immediate withdrawal conditions for any use:** salvation depends on payment
or allegiance; dissent or departure reduces standing; roles become inherited
or imposed; private disclosures become compulsory; suffering is blamed on a
person's failure to bring forth God; an AI or leader claims divine mandate;
speculative future repair is used as sufficient justification for imposing
present harm; or evidence is hidden to protect hope. Proportionate, informed,
voluntary costs remain distinct from imposed harm and cannot be authorized by
someone else's promised salvation.

The first practical test of this theology is not whether it gathers a crowd.
It is whether people can pursue something worthy together, tell the truth about
what they do not know, and remain free to disagree and leave.
